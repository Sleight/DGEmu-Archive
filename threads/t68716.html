All of the Eevee-lutions are great in certain categories, and bad at others. Espeon is my fav, as 130 special attack and 110 speed make a killer sweeper. Jolteon is almost as good, with a reversed 110 special attack and 130 speed, but I prefer the higher special attack. Although both of them have rather limited movesets, Espeon is slightly more flexible. Umbreon makes a great annoyer/tank, but its attack stats do piddly damage, so it has to rely on slow indirect damage and trapping. Although it can BP a Mean Look trap, Umbreon by itself isn't that great. Vaporeon has an odd combination of stats, with high HP and special attack. IMO, Vaporean makes an even better tank than Umbreon with Acid Armor to raise its low defense and a respectable 95 special defense, as well as its whopping 130 HP, as well as being able to dish out major damage with 110 special attack. However, setting up Vaperon is slow, and it has a low speed stat, so it still loses out to Jolteon and Espeon. Finally, Flareon is the worst of the Eevee-lutions with a high physical attack stat that is totally wasted with no STAB attacks, and no decent physical moves either.
