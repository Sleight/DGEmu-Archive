CChrystalleneJan 31 2006, 05:17 PM
IBRPG Version Chrys.0Finally done and approved by other team mates... Sorry for the delay. These are the things that we have decided on to become the IBRPG Version Chrys.0. Unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A, there will be many new features that will be added and removed. We would like to hear some comments and critics from you as early as possible so we may adjust the system as early as possible. Please tell me if I forgot to include something or verifications.As of now, we would like to thank Mas for allowing us to use his work as basis for our IBRPG and Dragons Master for allowing us to return something back to the site.Character Properties-------------------------------------------------Name: This is your unique character name. You may change it later, however, you must make sure that it is still unique.Gender: You will have to choose between Male and Female. Decide carefully as it may have some effects in the future. You may not change this once you have chosen.Element: Decide which elemental alignment you prefer. Take note that elemental alignment may have some effects in the future.Side: Decide which side you prefer. Take note that being good, neutral, or evil may have some effects in the future.Starting State: Decide whether to take a tutorial, skip tutorial and start normally, or start normally as a level 10 Citizen. Quality: Decide if you want the perfer more pictures or would text be okay. This can be changed anytime in the Character's Information.Starting with the tutorial allows the player to have initial equipment, gils, and other items. Skipping the tutorial allows the player to have initial equipment and gils. Starting as a level 10 Citizen allows the player to start with basic armor only.Avatar's URL: This is the picture that will be displayed in your character photo area.Maximum Avatar's width & height: You may choose whichever size you desire as long it does not exceed the 100x100 pixel limit.All characters will start as a Citizens as contrast to the Novice class of IBRPG ver 2.5.A.Character Statistics-------------------------------------------------LF: Life Force are your standard HP (Hit Points), MP (Mana/Magic Points), and Stamina combined. Each action will consumme LF (this includes forging, usage of skills and magic, movement in dimention among other things).STR: Strength refers to your physical strength. This affects the amount of base damage that will be inflicted when using physical attacks. Furthermore, this will affect which equipment is advisable to wear.DEF: Defense refers to the amount of physical damage that can be withstood. This affects the amount of base damage that will incurred when attacked physically.MAG: Magic refers to how effective spells can be casted by the caster. This affects the amount of base damage that will be inflicted to the target when using magical attacks.MDF: Magical Defense refers to the amount of magical damage that can be withstood. This affects the amount of base damage that will incurred when attacked magically.LUK: Luck refers to the amount of damage that can be inflicted/incurred due to attacks. Although this does not affect the base damage, it does add or subtract from the total damage in the end.Town-------------------------------------------------Smithy Shop: This is where players bring materials and the smith forges normal equipment for a fee. Unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A, players must bring the materials so that equipment may be forged.Potions Shop: This is where players bring materials and the potions maker mixes a potion for a fee. Unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A, players must bring the materials so that potions may be mixed.Town Treasury: This is where clerks are will to take a hold of gils and items to send it to someone else for a fee. To be able to use this feature, a player must be at least level 15. This is very much like Money Transfer and Item Transfer of IBRPG ver 2.5.A.Town Hall: This is where the different statistical data of registered players can be seen. Only players of level 15 and above are listed. Also, this is the location where players can change their class for a fee. This is in response to the problem of using scrolls in IBRPG ver 2.5.A. Furthermore, unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A, players may change their names for a fee.Hotel: This is where players sleep to recover LF. Paying for a good room allows the player to recover LF faster. However, for those who are not willing to pay are allowed to sleep on the floor for free. This will allow the player to recover LF at a normal rate. This is the same as the Hotel of IBRPG ver 2.5.A.Barracks: This is the place where different clans are given headquarters. Anyone is allowed to visit a clan headquarters. This is the same as the Clan List of IBRPG ver 2.5.A with a slight twist. User shops are merged with clans.Quest Guild: This is where players are sent to a quest for certain items. Being able to successfully do them, people are rewarded. Unlike IBRPG ver 1.4, quest will require items too to accomplish which will be taken away once the quest is done.Sanctuary: This is where players may donate gils. Level 15 and lower players who visit this location may take the gils from this location. This is a new feature.Town Square: This is competitions occur. Unlike the Battle Arena of IBRPG ver 2.5.A, Not only will you be able to fight other players, you may also participate in other tournaments like "Rock, Paper, and Scissors" and Slot Machines.Personal Status-------------------------------------------------Item Creation: This is where you can create your own equipment, items, and other things. Unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A, players do not have to be of a certain class or wearing a certain scroll to be able to use this feature. However, there will be certain requirements to be able to fully use this function.Weapon Strengthening: This is where players may strengthen weapons. Unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A where there is a 50% (one star)/40% (two star)/30% (three star)/20% (four star)/10% (five star) chance of upgrading a weapon, all players will have a new skill. The very first time players strengthen weapons, players will have a 10% success rate. After successfully strengthening a weapon, the success rate of strengthening a weapon is increased by 1%. The maximum is 100%. Furthermore, to strengthen a weapon, a player will consumme materials and LF.Equipment List: This is where players may equip/remove their equipment. Unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A, there will be an unequip all function. Furthermore, players are allowed to wear 1 head gear, 1 armor, 2 hand gears (weapons and/or shield), 1 pair of boots, and 3 accessories.Item List: This is where players may use items the heal themselves or remove status effects. This is very much like IBRPG ver 2.5.A.Ability List: This is where players can check out the mastery of skills and spells.Character's Information: This is where players can change your avatar/graphics settings. Players may see their statistics here.Mail Box: This is where players can read your mail. This is the same as IBRPG ver 2.5.A.Character's Status: This is where players may check their own statistics. This is pretty much the same as IBRPG ver 2.5.A.Team Formation: This is a new feature. This is where players may invite two other players to join them in exploring dimentions and fighting in tournaments. Players in a team who do not act (attack, use skills, use items, and other things) in a certain time limit will not act for that turn. A player that does not act for 7 turns will be removed from the team. A player that dies during battle and is not revived during the battle will be removed from the team. Players may leave the team any time. If the player who formed the team gets removed from the team, the team is disbanded. If the entire team dies, the team is disbanded. Player in a team may target their team mates (useful when healing). Combination attack is possible due to this feature. Movement is decided by the team leader.Characters-------------------------------------------------Citizen: Classless player. Begin here.Standard: The following are the basic classes. Visit Town Hall to change for a fee.Blacksmith: They forge normal weapons and armor out of metals.Herbalist: They mix herbs to make potions and food.Knight: They learn new skills from old ones.Mage: They are adept at using offensive magic.Priest: They are adept at using defensive magic.Summoner: They are able to call monsters from monster cards to attack for one turn.Special: Master three specific classes change into these special classes.Assassin: They forge weapons that induce different status effects. Must master being a Knight, Blacksmith, and Herbalist.Creator: They craft light and dark equipments. Must master being a Blacksmith, Herbalist, and Priest.Shaman: They summon monsters that are enhanced with magic. Must master being a Herbalist, Priest, and Summoner.Warlock: They will double cast spells just to show off. Must master being a Priest, Summoner, and Mage.Magic Knight: They draw their power from equiped monster cards. Must master being a Summoner, Mage, and Knight.Element Forger: They forge weapons with elemental properties. Must master being a Mage, Knight, and Blacksmith.Secret: The following can only be attained by doing something radical.Archer: They are masters of the bow. Skills: Magic Missile - a normal attack that has 0.5% chance of dealing leathal damage. Counter - allows them to dodge and react to any attack.Berserker: They ignore any damage delt to it. However, each hit to them will cost 1/2 of their remaining LF (rounded up). They do not lose LF for actions. They can not be healed. Skills: NONE.Cursed Beast: They are able to regenerate (think werewolf/catgirl regeneration rate) and use unlimited rage for a short span of time (first three turns). Skills: Rush - attack a single monster thrice.Frozen Angel: They have fully mastered all Water Spells and have swords that ignores the Defense of enemies. Skills: Frozen Light - attacks all enemies in the field while inflicting frozen effect.Hell Master: They have all of the most powerful magical spells but are not willing to cast weaker spells. However, he may put any amount of LF into the spell and thus amplifying the spell. Spells cost him 1/2. Skills: Forbidden - attacks all enemies in the field while ignoring their Magical DefenseLasher: They are able to attack all enemies in battle and has a chance of paralizing them (tie them up). Skills: Whip Lash - attack a single monster thrice.Reaper: They are able to make wide slashes that may kill one random monster as long as the monster is weak. Skills: Drain - attack a single monster and a percentage of the damage dealt to the monster.Successor: They are empowered by the number of races that worship them. Each race that worship them will raise their stats. Skills: Banish - remove a monster from combat.Cards-------------------------------------------------Specific Card: Collect all 108. Use them at the same time in the sanctuary and get a chance to become the new MVP.Monster Card: Card gotten from a monster. Summoners may summon the monster in the card. Magic Knights may strengthen their weapons using these.Equipments-------------------------------------------------There will be level/class requirement for equipments. However, if the player wears the equipment and the player is not strong enough (strength does not reach the advised strength requirement), the player will lose more LF than usual when acting.Normal Equipments: All weapons that come from the Smithy Shop are normal equipments. The stats of each equipment will be affect by the type of equipment that will be made (base stats of the equipement) and the ore that was used to create the equipment (added stats of the equipement).Strengthened Weapons: These weapons are strengthened. They have higher stats in comparison to their normal counter parts.Cartriage Weapons: These weapons have cartriage slots. These are loaded with magical cartriages that can be reloaded anytime. Each time a player attacks using a cartriage, a cartriage will be used and the effects of the magic in the cartriage will be released. The cartriage is consummed never to be used again. Each reload will cost one turn. Only those who mastered Creator and/or Element Forger may make weapons with cartriage slots.Cartriages: Only those who mastered Creator and/or Element Forger may put magic into cartriages. However, anyone may have the smithy shop make them cartriages without magical effects.Arrows: Unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A, to use the bow, players must use arrows too. If there are no arrows left, the player may not attack.Clans-------------------------------------------------There will be changes to clans in accordance to the requests of some players. For one, user shops of IBRPG ver 2.5.A will be merged with clans. Each clan will be able to sell 7 different items. The earnings will go to the clan value (similar to the clan bank of IBRPG ver 2.5.A). The clan leader will have the option to boot out any member of the clan. Furthermore, clan logs will have time and date. Unfortunately, stat boosts from clans will be removed. It also has been decided that there will be three different types of clans:Purist Clans: This clan has only one type of characters in them. All knights, all mages... all of one type alone. They have a discount on specific items depending on what type of clan they are. Players from this clan may not change classes as long as they are in the clan.Mixed Clan: This clan is composed of mixed characters.Secret Clan: This clan must contain at least one of the following as members of the clan: Archer, Berserker, Cursed Beast, Frozen Angel, Hell Master, Lasher, and Reaper. Members from this clan will get stat boosts.Exploration-------------------------------------------------Here you will be able to see six different dimentions.Four of the six contains different types of monsters. Unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A, monsters in the four dimentions get stronger as get closer to the boss. Bosses have a fixed location depending on the dimention. Also, after defeating the boss of a dimention, monsters of the said dimention change and they get stronger. The boss of the dimention also changes. The changes lasts only for the duration of a player's stay in the dimention.One of the dimentions is a training field. The monsters in the training field adjust according to the level of the player. However, monsters in this training field contains all of the monsters that can be found in the other four dimentions.The last dimention is the dimention of the goddess which is guarded by the MVP. This can be entered only through the sanctuary.Battle Sequence-------------------------------------------------Unlike IBRPG ver 2.5.A, damage is not dealt simultanously. Damage dealing is divided into three phases: Quick Act Phase (quick attacks are done here), Normal Act Phase (attacks that are done with normal speed are completed here), and Slow Act Phase (slow charged attacks are completed here). When running away from enemies, it will be an assurance that the player who ran will get hit since running will be consided to be a Slow Act.MVP Battle-------------------------------------------------The MVP is the Most Versitile Player in the entire IBRPG ver Chrys.0. When a team encounters an MVP, the team is disbanded and the MVP is faced by the team members individually. If the MVP is defeated, the MVP copies the stats of the last player to defeat it. Afterwards, the player will immediatly have to face the goddess.Experience-------------------------------------------------Experience is decided by the following:If in a team, base experience received by each player will be divided evenly among the team members.If survived the battle with critical LF (10% or less) left, the actual experience gained is 200% of the base experience recieved.If survived the battle without dying, the actual experience gained is 100% of the base experience recieved.If survived the battle due to revival, the actual experience gained is 50% of the base experience recieved.If did not survive the battle, the actual experience gained is 25% of the base experience recieved.If ran away from battle, the actual experience gained is 5% of the base experience recieved.Learning Abilites-------------------------------------------------Unlike IBRPG 2.5.A, abilities will level up after using them for a certain number of times. Skills and spells could be learned through the following:Quest rewardsUsing a certain chain of attacks, skills and/or spellsChanging classes
