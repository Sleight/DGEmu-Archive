But after Diamond and Pearl came out, and now that I've played Diamond all the way through (heck, I knew even an hour in even without getting the Pokétch yet), I can safely say Diamond and Pearl are probably the best they've done thus far.I know people will go on and on about G/S/C, and that was all well and good when the only thing you had to compare it to was Ruby and Sapphire, which had a comparatively weak PokéNav (next to the superior PokéGear), as well as a lack of day/night cycles, with nothing more than a few clock features such as berries, tides, and when to (eventually) evolve Eevee into Espeon or Umbreon. And then comparing that to FireRed and LeafGreen, it still seems better, as they have no clocks.But the graphics were weak (Color vs. Advance - not a difficult decision), the storyline was lacking (even for a Pokémon game), and the extra region felt kinda tacked on. You couldn't even go to the Safari Zone, and Viridian Forest was reduced to a small maze! Even Mt. Moon was much smaller, with the only up-side being that you could see Clefairy dance on Monday nights.Compare the new features of G/S/C to the new features of R/S/E, and compare the storylines. So what if you can go to Kanto in G/S/C? So what if you see old characters 3 years after the fact? The fact remains that compared to where the games are now, they seem... well... primitive.Even the NES "Legend of Zelda" held up better than Pokémon Gold, Silver, and Crystal.And I'm not EVEN bringing in Diamond and Pearl into it. Compared to D/P, G/S look pathetic. We're only given Sinnoh and a silly black-and-white digital watch, and we don't have quite as many weekly events... but even so, there is SO much more to do than I've ever had in Gold and Silver. Even after beating the Elite Four, parts of Sinnoh were opened up. It wasn't a matter of filling up the Pokédex with things we've already seen, we get even MORE new Pokémon, plenty of Mewtwo-like Legendaries to look for in various previously-hidden areas... we can still do Contests if we hadn't already, and we have the BATTLE TOWER.Seriously, I started playing on April 22, managed to beat the Pokémon League on May 9, and I'm STILL enjoying myself.On top of the game itself, we now have WiFi to play around with. Sure, the GTS demands can be very odd sometimes, but I've still had successful trades, and very fun battles with my friends. I enjoy the new Pokémon just fine (I actually completely forgot about them being new VERY early), and I feel more comfortable about the possibility of a battle when I'm out in public, now that I don't have to drag along a link cable with me....You heard me.And I'm sorry, but the remnants of a simple criminal organization are nothing compared to a sinister cult bent on the destruction of the world. Team Galactic make even Aqua and Magma look like environmentalist Hippies.(Then again, they kinda were.)So um... yeah... long story short, D/P FTW.
