Actually, that's another card game. My friends and I play it, but we call it A Thousand Blank White Cards. You take note cards (for our purposes, cut in half), and you distribute them to each of the players. Say, 15 each. Then, each person takes a pen or pencil and draws the rules on each card. The specifications are that each card must have a title, image, and explanation of what the card does (though it is possible to have a card that does nothing, with an explanation of what the card is instead). Once everyone is done, the cards are put into a deck along with some blank cards to be made during the course of the game. Then, it's played like any generic card game, with each player drawing a hand and playing a card at a time. The craziness is that, like the game Fluxx, cards can alter the gameplay. Basically, you're only limited by the imagination of the people playing. You can keep points, but they usually don't really mean anything beyond giving people a reason to screw each other over. Anyway, good cards are kept each game and worked into future decks, so over time you have a house deck that everyone has contributed to - hence the name A Thousand Blank White Cards.I remember, last time we played seriously, I implemented a bunch of cards that were basically like a monster-battle trading card game within the whole game, complete with elements and consumable items.
