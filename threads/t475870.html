PCSX2By far the best ps2 emulator. It will take a pretty powerful computer to make games run at a decent speed, but the compatibility is pretty high. First look up if your computer can run games decently here: http://forums.pcsx2.net/Thread-Sticky-Will-PCSX2-run-fast-on-my-computer (you can also ask in that particular thread if your pc is decent enough). I'd also suggest reading this guide to set up pcsc2 as it goes pretty in-depth: http://forums.pcsx2.net/Thread-Official-English-PCSX2-configuration-guide-v0-9-8side note: Although the guide says not to use speedhacks or fixes, just use it. They only say that because people may use the wrong settings and complain on their forums. While there's not much wrong with that, the speedhacks can speed up games by A LOT, the downside is that the settings might be incompatible with the game, but you can change them in real-time, or save state just before altering the hacks.
