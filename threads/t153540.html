DDhaosAug 19 2006, 04:40 PM
Well, much like any game review it should be detailed. Minimum requirement is 100 word (just use MS Word or wtv to count them, don't do it by hand). Use of the word "best" is not recommended because contrary to popular belief, not every game is the best or one of the best. I was told to automatically reject reviews that contain the phrase "This is the best game ever" or something to that extent. Obviously reviews that are like "Gameplay was good, sound was good, etc." Aren't really helpful at all so just decline those. As mentioned, plaigerized reviews do get accepted because they are great reviews, we just don't reward them with points because they didn't write it. Plaigerized reviews get declined and deduct the 10 DPs for the attempt at trying to pass off an amazing review as their own. For anyone who doesn't know a plaigerized review can often be recognized by its length, language, and of course when you paste a sentence in google it pops up as usually the first result. Aside from that not sure if we can really write an FAQ on review inspecting because its pretty much YOUR best judgement that decides whether they're accepted or not. Chances are that you'll see LOTS of reviews that are 1 sentence/1 paragraph long, I usually just automatically decline those because they're garbage. Anyways, I brought with me an example of a good review that got rewarded 10 DPs, as you will see it detailed everypart of a game:http://www.dgemu.com/n64-review-604----2.htmlHope that helped.Also, in the SS mail we often get mail from people asking "How do I review a game" because I'm not sure, but I've never seen it explained on a site, so my response usually is as follows (copy and pasted because we get quite a few SS mails as such):
someone wrote:
1. Click the rom you'd like to review2. Click the review tab near the top3. Click the "write a review" button near the left4. Write your review ans ubmit itNote: Just because you write a review DOES NOT mean it will be accepted and even if it does get accepted it DOES NOT mean you will receive DPs for it. Within 24 hours of submitting your review you will know if it was accepted or not (by checking the review tab). If it does not get accepted it is because it did not meet our expectations.
That's about it.
