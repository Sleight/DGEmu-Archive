Any suggestions would be greatly appreciated. The Ultima games were some of my fave RPG's and I would love to know your guys' suggestions of RPG's like them or of their calibur that had the well thought out puzzles in their dungeons and stuff like U6 and 7

, the kinds of immersion they had in their games. Probably isn't a big deal nowadays but the ultima games had a lot you could do in it, and a lot in terms of making an immersive world. All the NPC's had their own personal schedules by how they go about their day, they don't just stand there all day and all night waiting to interact with you, they actually did stuff, like they sleep, wake up, eat, do whatever tasks they have to do(ie. blacksmiths forge and weld stuff, and shopowners set up shop as well as close shop at appointed times, ect. ect.), and then go to sleep at whatever time they go sleep. On top of that, the majority of NPC's had unique, well thought out personalities very different to each other(except maybe town guards who most share a generic personality, and most enemies don't talk)

 

They also had a fairly straightforward yet engaging story(nothing fancy like Planescape: Torment, or some of the Final Fantasy games, but still pretty interesting), as well as an emphasis on exploration to discover what to do next and to find treasures or secrets(something you don't really see in new school games, this emphasis on exploring.).  

 

I would love to hear suggestions of games that are similar in playstyle and immersion to the Ultima games, or even just has the depth that compares to Ultima games. I'd prefer PC games but if you want you can list Console RPG's too
