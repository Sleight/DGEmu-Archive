Oo.o!Dec 01 2006, 08:18 PM
Let me start off by saying that I am an incredibly huge fan of both Final Fantasy and Kingdom Hearts. Both are among the greatest series I've ever played.Final Fantasy has been going forever and a day. Each game adds a new twist on old features that keep fans coming back for more. Each story's major setting seems to jump around from the previous game -- going from futuristic to medieval to somewhere in between. With the release of Final Fantasy XII, I was thoroughly disappointed. The writers are becoming lackluster, and absolutely murdering some of the features that once made Final Fantasy an enjoyable title. They are really focusing on everything else except plot depth which will lead to ( possibly ) the actual Final Fantasy. In my opinion, Final Fantasy VI was the very best. It had a plot that was all but displeasing, and characters in which we could find easy favorites in. Everything from there seemed to decline.Kingdom Hearts astounded me right off the bat with its beautiful opening. Soon after I was delighted in seeing three of my favorite Final Fantasy characters mixed in with a couple other originals. This delight was furthered upon seeing more popular Final Fantasy characters throughout the game. The Disney characters and levels were a bit disconcerting, however; Disney seems to be our do-good uncle who is always watching over our shoulder. That was one of very few characteristics about the game that I found displeasing. The plot was incredibly deep, albeit a bit vague. We learned more and more as the story progressed, and were left with a great cliffhanger at the end. With the release of CoM we are introduced to a new threat greater than what we previously faced. ( Some would say that this threat is the idiotic gameplay coupled with the retarded battle system. I vouch for that... ) Organization XIII becomes more and more apparent as you move throughout Castle Oblivion, and we meet new characters upon new characters. This game was a filler for the next release. ( Which took too long to come out, in my opinion. It left me bored. ) When Kingdom Hearts II was finally released I was displeased at the fact that we had to play repeats of the sequels to all the Disney movies we've watched. We're introduced to yet again NEW characters, and soon see some old ones. The plot is still thick as ever, and we find out more about the origins of the keyblade and the darkness. The fighting was way too easy in this sequel but still retained the delightful freedom of movement which escaped Final Fantasy's sometimes boring ATB system.Overall, I would have to vote for Kingdom Hearts.
