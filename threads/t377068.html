Synmuh Zurh' post='3871837 wrote on Jan 18 2008, 11:32 PM:
Its a self titled poem "Synmuh Zurh"Hope you like it.. ^^,------------------------------------------------------------The one that can be bad just for the good of allThe one that will sacrifice himself just to save the worldA one who listens when times are silentA one that cries without knowing he's violentI'm a one man army in some casesA leader under any random circumstancesA wolf that roams in the nightA problem that's difficult to be rightBe wary of what you think of meI maybe just like this for you to seeBut i thank those people whom i meetThat change my world quit a bit
This was overall a good poem. However, I feel that you're trying to be powerful, but just not quite getting there. Rather than a roar, you're just shouting. You need to fully abstract if you want to do things this way and punch your lines out effectively. I think that you should consider changing the first part to One that can be bad.... etcOne that will sacrifice... etcThis punches and gets people interested, and then you go on about 'I' and people realise you're talking about yourself in the first part. Not just some idealism... and it makes it flow better, rather than being disjointed from the rest of the poem. A one who listens when times are silentA one that cries without knowing he's violentI think you should chop off the 'A's' here too. Just have "One" to keep with what I said before. And consider putting those two stanzas together. It makes your point a lot punchier. A few things on spelling and grammar too:Be wary of what you think of meI maybe just like this for you to seeShould be I may be (with a space)That change my world quit a bitSpelling: quit = quiteAnd one last thing:I would consider taking out the conjunctions in the poem. The "I'm" and "he's". This is more colloquial written language, and if you want it give it punch and power, keeping it formal by writing the whole thing will do that. However, if you're going for more personal - doing the Personality - this is Me thing, then keep them. Or put more in, where you can to make it a prevalent theme. All in all, I know I've said a lot but I believe in constructive criticism, I liked your poem. I liked the theme and what you're trying to represent. I'd love to read some more o.O
