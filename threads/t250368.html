I personally like both. Mortal Kombat has an advantage if you like gore, realism (to a certain extent), or consistent quality. Street Fighter, on the other hand, can be absolutely INSANE when it gets right down to it, especially if by 'Street Fighter' you include Marvel vs. Capcom or, perhaps, Darkstalkers. The series has been kind of hit and miss, unfortunately, but the fact that the various incarnations of Street Fighter 2 still sell like hotcakes whenever they come out really speaks for what people want. Mortal Kombat might let you rip out people's spines or throw them into spike pits, which adds a certain bloody beauty to it, but the characters in Street Fighter actually emote, and have distinct personalities to their movement, something not quite as obvious in MK, though still present to a lesser extent. In a way, sticking to 2D with Street Fighter, similar to SNK, has allowed Capcom to evolve the series in a completely different way than MK will ever go. More extreme moves, bigger Hadokens, and even crazier characters, versus the Mortal Kombat Universe's complex fighting styles, advanced graphics, and more strategy based fighting. They can exist in perfect harmony at opposing ends of the Fighting Game spectrum.
