Right. It's not so black-and-white when you pit two characters who are very even. For example, Fox versus Peach isn't so easy. Granted, a very technical Fox player is likely to have the upper hand, but most players can't use Fox well enough to rush a Peach.Now in this example, both characters can exploit each others' weaknesses. Peach is easily killed vertically, something that Fox can do very well considering he has arguably the best U-air in the game and the second strongest U-smash. On the flip side, Fox is a fast faller, making him susceptible to chain throws which Peach is capable of. Peach's D-smash is also more effective against Fox due to his fall speed.There's more to it than that, but you can see that with two characters close to each other on the tier list, it's not so clear cut.But when you compare characters that aren't close together on the tier list, that's a different story. The tier list isn't 100% accurate, but it's accurate enough to show who can compete with who when played on the same skill level.
