You just need DP(Download Points). You can get those pretty easily. You can either wait for the daily DP, where you get DP each day until it reaches a certain point, which is 100 DP(max). Or you can post often here in the forums, like taking part in quizzes and contests. Helping the site. If you post a review for games, emulation, ect. Sometimes It will be rewarded by Mods. Making donations to the site, in which the money will also go towards your DP. You can also convert your GP's into DP, or use the DP loan system.

GP's you get by posting. You start with 100 GP's. You get 10 for every post, (minus in places you can't like the SC) Posting a topic will give you 15 GP's. You can also earn GP's through GP rewarding quizzes, or in the Debate section. Here . Moderators can also reward members GP's for helpful advise or reviews made in topics/threads/ect. You can also use your GP's for buying items at the http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?app=ibEconomy&tab=shop

Be careful though. You may lose GP's or DP at any time if you break the sites rules.

I hope that helped. If not, you can read the rules and guidlines for yourself http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php/topic/417167-forum-rules-and-guidelines/ :)
