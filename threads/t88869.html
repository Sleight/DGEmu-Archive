someone wrote:
.in "theme" sigs u can't just put effects on text or make nything outta of it cuz text is "secondry" stuff and the "theme" is wat u should b lookin at...
That solely depends on your point of view - for some people textis not secondary because it is very important for the overall composition of a picture - others in turn are rating sigs by theirtechnical aspects ( how they were made and if the respective techniques were put to good use ) - so, of course, they will also look at how well the text is incorporated. Most of us unconsciously apply a hybrid approach - looking at the picture itself (the colors, "flow", IF and how it appeals to the eye ), but also the techniques used ( because we've used them ourselves and can therefore judge and rate their implementation due to our"expertise" ).There are no rules like "the text is secondary in theme sigs"Text can even ADD to a theme if used correctly.Even if you're good - you obviously still have a lot to learn.So, in a nutshell : "Beauty lies in the Eye of the Beholder" (not the D&D one :D )
