Ok, now that I've gotten your attention, I'll tell you how to easily earn DPs.The best (and fastest) way to get DPs for our site is to donate. $10 will get you 500 DPs that you can use on any system, and will stay with you until they are used. You can either donate with Paypal or with e-Gold.If you don't want to donate money to the site, that's fine. There are plenty of other ways to earn DPs.One method to get larger amounts of Global DPs is via the DP Loan system. Once you have 50 posts, you can use this to exchange a few days of points to get it at one time. This is great for people who need a large game, such as Zelda: Phantom Hourglass. The points will be given to you after the first Global DP reset (which occurs at 12 midnight GMT+2). You have 24 hours after that reset to spend your points, before you have to "repay" your loan, by having your Global DP at 0 DPs for however long you took the loan out for.Don't have 50 posts? Still need more points? Then make an excellent review for our site! Those people who can make excellent reviews will earn 10 DPs in the points that match whatever system the game was for. Reviewed a GBA game? Then you get 10 NDS/GBx points. A Sega Genesis game? 10 Sega DPs is your reward. Not that these are for EXCELLENT reviews...not all reviews will get them. For tips on how to get that excellent review, read the review FAQ.Not a good writer? No biggie! There's another way. Posting! Note that this DOES NOT earn you DPs directly. You instead earn something called GPs. For every post you make, you get 6 GPs. Every thread you create earns you 10. These points do not expire, unless they are used (either by conversion or in the DG Store), or your points are deducted by a staff member because you broke the rules.Once you have enough GPs, you can convert them to DPs. You can make them either Global DPs, or for a slightly higher cost, make them for a specific DP type. Remember that Global DPs ALWAYS RESET AT 12 MIDNIGHT GMT+2. It doesn't matter if you have 2 DPs or 200. It WILL go back to 12.5 DPs! If you lose points because of the reset...they're lost. Remember this when converting.Why don't we just make the Global DP reset higher? Because bandwidth isn't cheap. At over 800,000 members, if we raised everybody's DP count 1 point, that would be an extra 0.8TB of bandwidth. That's a lot of bandwidth, and a lot of cost.Hate the DP method? Your last method is to head over to the Other ROM Sites forum, and look for other locations to download ROMs. Some may be good, some may stink. But they're alternatives to us.So, now that you've read this, DO NOT ever ask for higher DP counts, earn more GPs per post/thread, or something of a similar nature. You will have your GPs deducted, and possibly receive a warn (which makes your GP to DP conversion rate worse!), so don't do it. You've got plenty of great methods here - use them.For further info, check out our rules forum, where a large amount of great info awaits you.
