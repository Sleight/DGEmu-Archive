Wait.I've been seing lots of ANN- or Animenfo-like genre descriptions.I do not approve of them. We don't need "magic" or "supernatural"- I simply call it fantasy. And if it's some ESP stuff, it's probably sf anyway. An anime should preferably have no more than four genres and there should only be a few of them.Basic, or "setting" genres are: sf, fantasy, school, sports, history ...andthat's it. They are ALWAYS named first. There can be combinationslike "fantasy, history" or "sf, fantasy". You don't HAVE to name asetting if it doesn't fit into any of those. "real life"(with subgenres school) and "games"(only Hikaru no Go so far) are getting trashed, because they are either too general and require subgenres or, in the case of "games" are only used for one series.We do have established genres like "magical girl" or "sf/mecha",but "shounen" and "shoujo" is way too general. Here are two examples how to make it more detailed:Shounen = Action, Adventure, Comedy Shoujo = Romance, Drama, AdventureAlso, the order of the genres is important:"Action, Adventure, Comedy" would mean that the anime is fixed on fighting/chasing or the like, involves exploration/travelling/etc. and has some funny scenes (or scenes that are supposed to be funny<_<),while "Comedy, Action" would be an anime that makes fun of fightingor has the characters competing in ridiculous ways (like Ranma 1/2 - although there is also romance involved in this case).There is a certain amount of flexibility involved, but that's to be decided for each case individually.
