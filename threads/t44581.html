Bbooyah_boyApr 11 2005, 09:10 AM
Follow these steps:I. Decide upon what you would like and create such using any graphics program you have available (MSPaint, PhotoShop, Paint Shop Pro, etcetera, etcetera, etcetera), or else request such of someone else (many on this forum will create a signature according to your input and desires for a fee, generally in GP's).II. Host such on any free image hosting server (Photobucket for example).III. Apply such by sticking the appropriate hyperlink your host will supply you with into the "Signature" box located in the "Edit Signature" area, which may be found by clicking on "My Controls" button located at the upper-right of each page.IV. Sit back and enjoy seeing your signature displayed at the bottom of each post you make!The End.Best of luck...
