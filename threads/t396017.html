Staff Discussion ManagementThe purpose of these discussions is to Develop, and, Organize DGEMU and the DGNetwork. If you have an idea that is within reason, and, is something you could in some way help implement then by all means start a topic with the appropriate code. All new ideas must get the approval of the majority of the Staff. Once the approval has been obtained a final Council vote will be taken. The purpose of creating this is so bmods and support staff may be able to crank out some idea's to the majority of the staff. Discussion Rules and GuidelinesDo NOT go Off-topic. A little chicanery is ok so long as you don't over do it and stay on topic.Do NOT use foul language, flame another staff members idea, or, ask any foolish questions. Show you are worthy of your status. If you have an issue with their idea simply explain the problem with it in an intellectual manner.Give informative titles. Do NOT make other staff members guess what your topic pertains to.Do NOT duplicate topics. If you have something new to add or a change that needs to be made then Edit the topic. If you are unable to, have someone who can do so for you. Those who can edit should do so even for those who cannot.Check the Discussions at least once a month. Although it is better to check it weekly. Keep this place productive and develop your ideas further.Read what others say, even if it's a bit long. Every staff members opinion counts (even if you don't like that particular staff member). Unless everything you are going write is important, try making your replies shorter and more to the point.Do NOT pin your topic. Unless your topic is a truly important message. A topic which has received no replies for a month will count as a closed discussion and whoever is in charge of the implementation may start work on it. Providing it got the approval of councils final say.The Council may decide on a closure of a certain discussion when they wish to rush implementation due to importance, and, if they feel they and the rest of the staff have received enough feedback on the subject.Do NOT delete any staff discussions. Omission of a staff discussion is up to the discretion of the Admins.These rules and guidelines are subject to change with out notice. It would be wise to check them for changes regularly. Not acknowledging that the rules have changed is no excuse.- Dragons Master & Leer und Namenlose and the DGEmu Administration
