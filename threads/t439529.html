This wonderful game serves as a sequel to the 1982 movie which was simply titled Tron. You play as a man who gets digitized and sent inside a computer to fight viruses and data corruption. You will end up traveling from system to system in your fight. Everything from lowly PDAs to mainframes.
While I'm putting it here in the 3D shooters section the game should almost be classified as an RPG first and a shooter second. As you perform various game tasks or find build notes your version number will slowly increase. For every full version (1.0.0 2.0.0 etc) you get to add points to stats for health, energy, file transfer speed, weapon efficiency, and processing speed. You can find file archives containing security permissions, e-mails, and new programs/subroutines. All equipment is presented as programs and subroutines for them that begin as an alpha version and can be upgraded to beta and then gold versions. You will have a different amount of equipable slots depending on the memory of the system you are in and equipable spaces can be damaged and will have to be defragged before they can be used again. When battling viruses you can also find you sub routines getting infected reducing their effectiveness or even completely disabling then until you run anti virus on them.
Oh, and everyone simply must play a game where a sniper rifle is named the LOL.
