I'm pretty sure Classic is only in-browser. It being free, you don't have the ability to save your progress, so there isn't really any point in having it offline. If you did have an account and a paid copy, you would be able to play single player or multiplayer. Whether you play single player through the browser or through a stand-alone client does not matter so long as it's the same computer - the levels are saved on your hard drive, so they are the same in either instance. And yes, it would be terrible of you to grab a free copy of a wonderful game by an independent developer. Actually paying for the game encourages the developer to continue making more games. One could argue that plenty of people have already bought the game, so one more illegal download wouldn't make a big difference, but this is not an ethical argument because, if everyone thought the same thing, sales would drop to zero.It's like Amnesia, another great game by an independent Swedish developer. Within the first month of sales, the company reached its goal, triggering them to decide to make continue making games. They lost quite a few sales, however, to illegal downloads. Given that they made such a wonderful game while operating at below minimum wage for all involved, it seems a travesty that they should earn so little while making something so wonderful. And unfortunately for Frictional Games, the developers of Amnesia, fewer people who pirated the game are likely to pay for it later as compared to Minecraft, given the replay values.Also, guys, this person wants to join our server.
