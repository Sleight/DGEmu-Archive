CCat in the HatORIGINAL POSTJan 09 2012, 10:28 AM
Fire Emblem 8-Finally decided to read the story, use items, do supports, and not whore out the tower thing. It was fun, though not difficult. Some of the characters clashed with the backstory I had invented for them, but overall it was a good FE game. 7 is still the standout of course, though I prefer having options when upgrading units. There's not a whole lot to say about it... Gears of War 3-Enjoyed it a lot more than I thought I would. The battles were all suitably epic and cool and I felt a lot more like a badass than I ever did in the first two. The story was pretty crappy, though compared to other FPS games, it was good. It was no RPG, obviously, so I guess it did what it could. The bosses were all pretty great except for the final one. As is usual in games today, that was pretty disappointing. I really had fun with it, though the online experience is where it kind of went awry. The game doesn't seem to like to match you with people near your skill level, and so I just kept getting stuck with people that were much higher level and (usually) much better than me, which took out all the fun. Most of the time I'd be dead before I could do anything, and it really mucked up the experience for me. It was alright though. Much better than GoW 1 or 2. No horrible lag and the guns feel decently fair, though the shotgun is still random and way overpowered compared to all the non-power weapons. Uncharted 2-Really loved this one, though it felt like it went on forever towards the end, and the final chapter was a bit of a letdown just because the enemies were so annoying, and the final boss was just strange, but fun once you figure out how to run around in such a tiny space. Of course the platforming parts were epic all around, and it was gorgeous to look at. The combat is what I didn't like from the first game, and I really didn't like it here either. It was alright, just doesn't seem to have as much of an impact as other shooters (such as Gears of War). All the guns feel insignificant for some reason (not much rumble?). But the game was brilliant overall, especially the final part where you run across a bridge. My heart was pounding. Can't wait to play the third one. Didn't play multiplayer, so no clue how that goes. Games I attempted to beat but didn't Bayonetta-I don't even know how to describe my experiene with this one...****ed up? Vanquish-It was fun for awhile, but for a game that is supposed to be so fast paced, you sure do spend a lot of time behind cover. Towards the end of the game the battles just get ridiculous and you spend so much time behind cover that it's not even fun anymore. The enemies take way too long to die and the whole game falls apart. Until then it's a blast though. Story and characters are awful though. Skip the cutscenes. They don't make any sense anyways.
