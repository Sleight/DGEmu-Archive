Here's my opinion on RPG for GCN:Baten Kaitos was fun at first, then the combat system got boring and so did the drawn out conversations.I couldn't put Paper Mario: the Thousand Year Door down until I beat it. It's turn based, but has action buttons like the first one.Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles was a fun twist to the FF serries. It is more fun with more people, but you can still have a lot of fun just playing by yourself.Evolution: World was pretty fun. It has a decent amount of strategy to the battles and provides a decent challenge for many gamers. If you want an extreamly serious game, I wouldn't reccomend playing this because it does have more of a "child" appeal.Skies of Arcania: Legends was pretty good. It allowed you to focus on a particular character or level them up evenly. I would have liked more variety in the battle system, but the storyline more than makes up for that.Lord of the Rings: The Third Age added a new perspective to the LotR serries. I'm not sure if you would enjoy it to the fullest unless you liked the storyline of the books/movies.I have no comment on the Pokemon games, for I have not played them.The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventure, Star Fox: Adventures, and The Legend of Zelda: the WindWaker are not RPGs but are adventure games. All the Fire Emblem games are strategy games, although they do have levels and a bit of an RPG experience.I probably missed some in there, so those probably aren't the only choices there are out there.
