RRyuukaApr 05 2006, 05:44 AM
Consonent sounds are more prominent than vowel sounds. Also, each syllable is weighted equally in Japanese. In Sakura's case, the 'k' overshadows the 'u' sound in the second syllable, and the 'ku' syllable has to have the same length as the others, so they compact syllables with longer vowel sounds. This is also why "Sasuke" is heard as Saske, the 's' just overshadows the 'u' sound and the whole 'su' syllable is quickened to have the same weight as the other syllables. So in the end, the proper pronunciations are still "Sa-Ku-Ra" and "Sa-Su-Ke." They just say the 2nd syllable faster and the 's' sound happens to be more prominent.
