Celebrating Rockman/Megaman's 25th anniversary, I've decided to compile this ultimate theme song compilation! This contains pretty much all the official songs (I could find / own) that have lyrics in them. There is no fan-made songs in here; all tracks were either used officially by Capcom or released in an album that Capcom published. A total of 63 tracks for your enjoyment!

This compilation is split into 4 sections:

  • Tracks 1 - 25: Music that were used in the games.
  • Tracks 26 - 38: Music that were used in official Rockman albums.
  • Tracks 39 - 43: Music that were used in Rockman game commercials in Japan.
  • Tracks 44 - 63: Music that were used in the anime and cartoon series.

The tracks vary between Japanese, Chinese, English, French, and German lyrics. I know, I'm missing Reika Morishita's 2012 remixed tracks of Another Sun and Anata no Kaze ga Fuku Kara. If someone is able to help me source them, I would be really happy! ^.^

To help you guys follow, here's a list of the Japanese titles with their English equivalents for the titles that are not obvious:

  • Rockman DASH: Megaman Legends
  • Tron ni Kobun: The Misadventures of Tron Bonne
  • Irregular Hunter X: Megaman Maverick Hunter X
  • Rockman.EXE: Megaman Battle Network (for the games) or Megaman NT Warrior (for the anime)
  • Ryuusei no Rockman: Megaman Star Force (for the games) or Shooting Star Megaman (for the anime, a litteral translation)

Enjoy the compilation!
