Ccelticwannabe2Mar 05 2011, 11:38 PM
Absolutely games should be considered art! "Art is the product or process of deliberately arranging items (often with symbolic significance) in a way that influences and affects one or more of the senses, emotions, and intellect." This is Wikipedia's definition of art. There are several ways in which games can fall into this category. How many of us have been effected emotionally by the death of a beloved character in a video game? How many of us have found ourselves secretly urging them on to victory, sometimes without realizing we are doing it? Symbolism is rampant in video games, and while sometimes it is simplistic and can be understood on the instinctual level, often it is rather obscure and must be understood through the intellect. While video games currently do not influence our senses of smell or touch, they are unarguably interacting with our sense of sight and sound. If music on a CD can be considered an art form, why can't music designed for video games?http://www.gamespy.com/articles/110/1103298p1.htmlThis link is to GameSpy's article detailing the fact that even Roger Ebert, the acclaimed and widely respected film critic, admits that at least for some video games can be art.My last point I would like to make is that video games as a form should, perhaps, be considered more of an art form than something such as paintings or sculpture due to one simple facet, their interactivity. Art that involves you directly and lets you help to decide how it plays out should be given more respect than art that does not take the viewer into consideration as anything more than a passive observer. at least that is my opinion.
