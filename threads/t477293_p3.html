and it's very simple

each attack consumes stamina

you start with 7 stamina points (i think)

each attack has an x% chance to hit, which increases after every successful attack

you have, from least likely to land a hit to the most likely: a strong attack, which consumes 3 points of stamina, a normal attack which consumes 2 points, and a light attack, which consumes just 1 point

elemental attacks must be set on your element grid before battle. grids have tiers of magic/element, each element has a minimum tier requirement but can be set on a higher tier to increase its power, so, you can have fireball, a tier 1 element, in the 7th level of your grid. but!!!1 the stamina consumed by the element is the same as the tier level it is set on, so, doing this is generally a bad idea.

elements are both weak and strong against their opposite element, so, if serge (white element) attacks lynx (black element) with one of his techniques (kind of like elemental attacks, except, not really), it will cause extra damage and may inflict the fatigue status! but! the same applies if lynx attacks him with a black element!!1

consumables must be set on the element grid, and i'm pretty sure they can't be put in a higher tier. but remember! they are consumables, make sure you don't run out of them!

also, there's the field change mechanic, which means that the more you use a single element color, the more effective this element becomes! this is useful for summons, but remember, summons use stars, and stars are a very finite resource in this game, so, don't waste them


see? verry easy

disclaimer: i haven't played this game in over 2 years, so, this info may or may not be exactly accurate
