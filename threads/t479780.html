SshadowmanwkpOct 09 2013, 07:23 AM
You can use no$gba in conjunction with no$zoomer or myzoom to achieve this effect. Alternatively you can use DeSmuME which does not feature a true fullscreen option, but you can resize the screen and apply some filters so that the graphics look a bit better. DeSmuME also is in active development, while no$gba's updates are sporadic at best.
XXtmgearOct 17 2013, 12:56 PM

Download WinDS PRO. It comes with a lot  of special adds for NO$GBA. Some of them allows you to use the emulator at full screen, like No$Zoomer for example.  If you have a fast computer you can also use Desmume wich has a full screen option, but it won't work very good if your PC is slow.
