In any case, there's a bazillion things to discuss/theorize on, but I should probably begin with laying out the time jump sequence in a linear path, the convoluted diagram from the in-game explanation is pretty confusing.



1) Sigma's normal life
2) Jumps from Christmas 2028 to January 25, 2074, caused by swapping with much later version of his conciousness
3) Experiences the Nonary game for 36 hours (Phi End)
4) Jumps to 2029, caused by Akane's trigger (End or Beginning)
5) Lives 45 years, engineers the AB Plan, Nonary game, Gaulems, etc
6) Reaches the moment when he wakes up in the A/B room at the start of the game, jumps to Christmas 2028 from swapping with his earlier consciousness
7) Meets Phi, events of Nevada test site occur, loses arms and eye, fails to stop outbreak
8) Lives 3 months until reactor explosions in 2029
9) Swaps with his consciousness at 4), is now in 2074 at the end of the Nonary game
10) (Another Time End), then unknown

Also Phi makes all the same jumps, except she is in cold sleep during 5) and unconscious during 6). So her actual experiences would omit those 2 points



With this representation its much simpler, and brings up a couple of points:

-The events of VLR covers only 2-4, everything after that has not yet been 'observed' and therefore not guaranteed to occur that way. Notably 7, which I assume will be covered in the sequel

-(Another Time End) is the exception, it occurs at 10. However, the way I understand it, this occurs in a different timeline where Sigma and Phi fail to avert the crisis, and is therefore irrelevant to the 'true path' that the player is supposed to be following. This explains why the Sigma and Phi here know about the Nevada test site events, yet cannot speak about them. It also brings up a whole slew of questions on the identity of of the conscious you experience in this segment, but that's a different matter...

Remember that this game uses the concept of multiple realities, ala world lines from Steins;Gate, aka quantam theory.
The thing is that you can jump back to any point in time and diverge the timeline, but that doesn't stop the other timeline for existing...thus the Sigma that came from a timeline where he failed to stop the outbreak jumps back and stops the outbreak, creating a divergence (point E in the in-game diagram)

Sure he's required to fail once in order to allow the existence of his current self, but causality and all that doesn't matter for any individual timeline, cuz you can just say he got his consciousness from a different one.
