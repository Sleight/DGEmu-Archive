SSkeleton JellyApr 13 2008, 08:36 PM
Sony built the POPS emulator into the PSP firmware at 3.02 (POPS is the name for Sony's PSX emulator.)You can mess with the controls, but normally L2 and R2 are controlled by the analog stick (Left is L2, right is R2, up is L2+R2)You are limited though... If you use an official firmware, you can only use games you buy from the Playstation Store.If you have custom firmware, you can rip your own games, and play every PSX game.
