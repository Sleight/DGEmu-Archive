If it has multiple platforms, like Samus' level, it's a terribly risky weapon. The inability to jump will doom you, and unless your close enough to someone, or they are distracted, they can easily wait you out if they can see it coming in time.The pokeballs are pretty powerful, but awfully random and unpredictible. It can either be life-saving, or worthless. Not to mention they have a bit of start-up that can screw you over in close quarters.The proximity mines are one of my favorites I have to admit. I'd have to say the best time I have ever had with this, is when I chained like 2 or 3 mines in a row, then watched metal mario get totally screwed. ^_^Weak, but not too useless:The laser blaster is pretty weak, though it does good at annoying your enemies at long range. Very fun to piss them off with. ^^The Fire flower is, in a word, lame. The only time it does good, and it does very good, is when you manage to fight your opponent into a corner at melee range. It's great for racking up immense damage and breaking sheilds, but the odds of your opponent being dumb enough to fall into a corner at melee range is minimal, you litterally have t beat them into that corner, and hope they can't use the roll command and escape from your firey corner of doom.The inviciblity star normally does'nt last long enough for me to rack up any damage of much significance most of the time. It, much like the hammer, gives a clear, "Stay the **** away from me if you value your life" feeling to all your oppoents, making it that much harder for you to damage them more then once or twice. At least the hammer made sure if you ever got close enough, they would'nt be able to do anything but get knocked off the screen, but by the time you catch up to any opponent, you won't be able to deal nearly as much damage as with the hammer(Unless you have your combo all planned out). Thankfully, this does let you jump however. Sucky weapons:I've never had much luck with the bat, I've mostly always thrown it away as soon as I got it, preferring normal melee attacks over the bat as universally more reliable.Bombomb just walks far too slowly, and is way to easy to spot. And since it keeps moving, you can't count on it as a stationary "Knock enemy into this point for sweet kill" area, so It's really only ule if you knock your enemy along the ground, or you throw this thing at them. It might sound pretty useful, but for some reason, I never get much use out of this thing. and the rest of the time this thing comes back to always bite me in the @ss.The fan has no throwing power. And though it is quick, it does'nt have much snap to it, meaning that pulling off a 20-hit combo, which normally would dish out 200-300 damage, would now only deal out about 80. Thoguh you can get it out quicker, Speed is mostly irrelevant because it lacks much power behind it's strike, and this game releies greatly on just that one good smack, that can send your opponent to their doom.

Im gonna write an Item List, so everybody can pick their own favorite with some info about the item:Beam Sword: It has a very mighty Smash Attack, and if someone begins to use the normal attacks all the time you just can't get near the guy who is using it, because you'll get hit and sent back.Home-Run Bat: Swing and Away! It's good for one hit KO, but its slow man.Proximity Mine: It's very funny, because if you conceal it well, it'll go off suddenly and you'll make people go mad.Bob-Omb: An excellent proyectile. The bad thing is that if it begins to walk when you're trying to pick it up you're gonna hate it.Fan: It's a very fast weapon, and it shouldn't be underestimated by two reasons. One is it's speed. If you're fast enough, your enemy will not be able to set free and you can manage to do huge damage. Two is it's special skill to break the shields of the enemy. With the Fan you can break an enemy's shield in 3 or 4 normal attacks or in 1 Smash Attack.Pokeball: A very good proyectile with a surprise in it. A direct hit can severely wound an enemy.Bumper: It's kinda useless (1 point of damage per hit), but it makes you bump far away. Useful only when tossed at enemies trying to get back on the ground.Shell: A very good proyectile that does good damage and tosses you very far away. It can be used more than one time if its a green one (and if it doesn't breaks).Hammer: Good weapon, but overestimated. Two hits of this can kill a fully healed player, but this item is not able to be tossed, blocks your second and third jump skills, and if you fall of the platform with this item on, you're dead. This item is good for large maps and with no little platforms, such as Hyrule Castle or Sector Z. However, you mustn't be over confident, and think 2I got a Hammer and im gonna kill them all and they just can't stop me" because that is false. A skilled player can take out a Hammer easily in different ways (Example:DK's Punch to toss him out of the platform), or try to steal it (Example: Using Kirby's Brick on a player with a Hammer can make the player drop the Hammer, and then Kirby just picks it up and sends the player to outer space).StarRod: Smash Attack releases a star. It does good damage, and it's a good proyectile as well, because anyone hit by this rod is sent far down and back, so it's useful to toss enemies trying to get back.RayGun: Kinda useless, because of it's little damage, but it's funny because you can use it to trap somebody, and if you're a good shooter, he can't release himself until you run out of ammo.Star: Gives invulnerability for some time.Fire Flower: Very useless, since the only way you can use it to make good damage is when you trap somebody against a wall, and i don't think people get cornered so easily. Also, if you manage to corner somebody, it'll be a miracle if someone doesn't take the chance to kick you away while you're busy cornering the guy, so i don't recommend it. Maxim Tomato: Heals 100 points of damage.Heart: Heals all damage.Boxes: Slow but effective proyectile weapons. May explode or release items after it is broken.Capsule: Light proyectile weapon. May explode or release items after it is broken.I think these are all items, and i hope this list is useful to someone. My personal favorites are the Sword, Bat, PMine and Pokeball.
