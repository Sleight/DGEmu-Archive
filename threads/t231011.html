BBowleyForeverMay 03 2007, 11:57 PM
Just so everyone knows, Pokémon Jade and the fake Diamond for the GBC were hacks of Keitai Denjuu Telefang, a similar monster RPG developed by a company called Smilesoft in the year 2000. Like Pokémon, KDT was also split into two versions, one being Power, the other Speed. Since Pokémon was really popular beyond anyone's wildest dreams back then, some bootleggers decided to take advantage of the hype and turned the KDT games into "Pokémon" games, replacing the title screens and boxart with fake images and slapping the Pokémon logo on top, and even attempting to translate the entire games' text, with hilariously poor results. For more information, check out the Telefang Wikipedia article.
