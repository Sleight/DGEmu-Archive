I was browsing around the gamecube and Wii areas and noticed very little information on the emulator Dolphin and what I could dig up was very out of date.I think at the time, Gamecube emulation was primitive and Wii emulation was in its very early stages. As some of you who chat with me on MSN know, Dolphin has come a very long way ever since SVN R4000+ and several of the most popular games are fully playable at very good framerates too.I was wondering if you all think it's time to enlighten our members here on how to set up the emulator properly. Perhaps some of you should play around with it too as you'd be surprised what now works. For the time being, I updated Atomicknight's old thread and pinned it but as I said, I think we still need a guide because the emulator does need to be set up correctly or else it won't work to its full capacity. It would also make sense to have a copy of it in the gamecube emulation section too since this emulator does cover both systems (compatibility is about equal on both platforms surprisingly).So here's where I've posted the link.http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=435650You can download the emulator from that site to try out for yourselves.My recommendations are for you to use the Direct3D9 plugin for video (it's MUCH faster) and keep the simple keyboard plugin active if you have an xbox 360 controller. Otherwise, use Njoy. The WiiMote functionality has its own plugin you can configure to map buttons and motions to triggers, axis, etc. Should you have the right bluetooth device, you can use a real WiiMote (although I cannot experiment with this so perhaps someone else can). In the latest of SVN's multiple WiiMote support was added making it possible to play games like New Super Mario Bros Wii with other people.The beauty of certain Wii games is how they support the gamecube controller. That will work to our advantage here since you don't have to set up motions for those (e.g. Mario Kart Wii and Super Smash Brothers Brawl). For First Person Shooter Wii games, you can potentially set up WASD+Mouse control which is excellent for those games on the PC platform.
