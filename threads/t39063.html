Read the rules and then have fun. (Anything marked with a star is a guideline.)Regarding the title just put who's in the battle and yah. If you wanna add a description of the type of battle go ahead. Now to the rules.- A STAFF MEMBER'S INTERVENTION IN A BATTLE TOPIC HAS ALL PRIORITY OVER THE RULES SET. DO NOT CHALLENGE A STAFF MEMBER'S DECISION IN A BATTLE TOPIC UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES. THIS IS A RULE AND BREAKING IT MAY RESULT IN WHATEVER PUNISHMENT DEEMED NECESSARY. - A margin of at least two votes is needed to decide a winner. If a battle is locked up at 6 all then somebody must get two more votes to decide a winner. Example 6-6 a winner is decided when the votes are 8-6, 9-7 etc.- Here are the set guidelinesSig Type: Stipulation:Time Limit:Victory Conditions:- Only people that cannot vote are the participants.- Your post cannot be edited. Once you have submitted a sig that is it. You cannot change your first entry no matter what. We don't want cheaters now do we.- You are only allowed to enter one battle at a time.- You must give detailed explanation when voting for a sig. Failure to do so will result in your vote being disqualified. Stuff like "better brushing" and "I think its better" don't count. - A disqualification results in a loss so all rules applying to losses are in effect- Do not post at the start of a battle asking "when can i vote" or telling the participants to hurry up, also remember to stay on topic!Pengu- edit:- You must be a contestant in any battle topic you open. In addition, the number of participants in the topic must not exceed three ( a three-way). - If you, for a reason like a tourney or otherwise, wish to "get around" these rules, you must PM me, and ask permission. Once given, you can proceed with whatever tourney you might have had planned.
