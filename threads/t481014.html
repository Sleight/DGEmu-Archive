KKirie MotobaMay 11 2014, 07:41 AM

When you get stuck with a warning on C5, that's usually the hard disk is trying to allocate data, but because some fuck up occurred, it was never able to allocate it properly, thus resulting in data loss and corruption. This may mean it was trying to allocate it away because of bad sectors, but the bad sector count isn't up. Since you have 8 counts of that, that means this happened quite often.

 

The trick to salvage the drive from this though: backup all your stuff onto a separate drive and have Windows boot from it.

Install Windows on that other drive with HD Tune Pro.

In Windows' disk management, delete all the partition from the drive.

Launch HD Tune Pro, go to the Erase tab, perform a Zero Fill erase (basically a low level format, resetting the drive to the factory state it was delivered minus the SMART counters that will be left intact to avoid frauding health statuses).

Once that completes successfully, you should have a clean drive with no C5 count, but let's cross fingers you don't have instead warning on 05 and C4.

If your drive checks out clean now, just initialize it through disk management and then create your partitions and format to use them again.

I saved a 250gb Seagate drive before by doing this. Since then, working fine no problems.
