DQ3 has been translated to english for SNES.I've been playing it non stop for days and am almost complete.The patch is near perfect except for two things I noticed.The first is when you use certain items,(wing of wyvern,invisible herb) causes all sprites to disappear,but it fixes it self when you enter a door/town.The second is in the battle screen sometimes your character's levels display get a little messed up,but it does not affect gameplay.I highly recommend getting this patch.Just make sure you remove the header from the rom before patching.I would say SNEStool works the best for this,because I couldn't get ZSNES to patch it correctly.Some new features over the original are the addition of small medals,animated enemies,and backrounds for the battles.IMO it is far better than the GBC version for obvious reasons.So go download and enjoy,and tell me how you liked it in this post.
