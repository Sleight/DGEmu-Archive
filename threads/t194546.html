Well there is one main problem with that and that's the fact we "stream" the file, it's not a binary and so jumping 20 megs will jump to nothing 'cuz at that moment it wasn't read..Trough we can fseek to the point where they downloaded, but the problem is we cannot determine if they want a fresh download or if they want resume - so we can resume someone's download and he'll get just the other part and have a broken download instead of the whole file again..And that's not even the largest problem - the largest problem is that we cannot save the state of the download of the members... since downloads die forcefully (not through a nice button that let's us know how much left) we cannot keep anywhere the amount we need to go forward in the file and start our stream from.. even if we could, that would mean updating records of stream point every few miliseconds for every download of every user and that would bombard our database..In short, in the way we work now, which is the safe way (people can't download without going through our system, since the binaries are inaccessible) we cannot support such features..Sorry, - DM
