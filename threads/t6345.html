This topic answers how to obtain certain posting statuses, which are displayed to the left of your posts.

Posting Ranks
If you haven't noticed, under your avatar or username, theres a word, and underneath that, there is a little symbol (henceforth called 'pips') that look like . That is the rank you're on.
So, I proudly present you, the posting ranks.
All new members start off at 1 pip, as they have 0-49 posts. Another pip is added for each rank you achieve.

Egg: 0
Hatchling: 50
Serpent: 150
Winged Serpent: 300
Dragon: 500
Elite Dragon: 750
Holy Dragon: 1000
Reaper Dragon: 1500
Ancient Dragon: 2000
Master Dragon: 3000
Tiamat: 5000
Bahamut: 10000

You need to POST in sections that increase post count in order to increase your posting rank.
All Staff members (Support Staff, mods, admins) do not have any pips, rather a badge as a sign of their position on the staff.
You can also customise this title to whatever you want. Just buy the "Custom Member Title" (and fill in the text box according to what you want) in the DG Store.
