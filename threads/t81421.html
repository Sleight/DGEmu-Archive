DDr. QSep 03 2008, 10:27 PM
OK, since I have destroyed this game, through use of gamefaqs, may they forever be praised, I believe that I can shed some light on the awesome-ness of this game, or lack thereof.For those who don't really know what the hell is going on, this game plays a tiny bit like the movie X-Men 2. No, really! You know how in X-Men 2, The good guy mutants and the bad guy mutants had to work together to defeat the greater evil? Well, that totally is what happens in X-Men Legends 2. You can play as good guys or bad guys, or both.It is a party-based, real-time, beat-em-up type RPG, which makes me very happy. I dislike RPGs where you stand in a line and take turns attacking. I prefer the kind where you can run up to an enemy and pummel him into the air, freeze him when he lands, then switch characters and punch him into next October. And for that, this game excels.However, this can get very tedious. OK, so what RPG doesn't get tedious? At least you don't have to grind. *grumble* Bloody MMORPGs *grumble* Anyway, what make a tedious RPG really good is a great story, and this story is severely lacking. Now, considering all the stories that Marvel has written previously in comics books and such, there isn't much that has already been said. It has a comic-book feel to it, which is probably how they wanted to go, but doesn't lend itself to an RPG story, which should contain twists, turns, and awesome dialogue. Instead, we get maybe one twist (OMG Beast is brainwashed holy crap!), no turns (Apocalypse is the bad guy. A-DURRRRRR!), and some comic-book quality lines.The characters are a lot of fun, especially the unlockables. I am a huge fan of Deadpool. Let me list off his awesome. Gun. Swords. Teleportation. Psychoses. If you have read any of the Deadpool comics (and I have), you will know that the guy is certifiable. At times, he thinks to himself in yellow thought boxes, then people will respond, and he will think "I need to fix my thought boxes." He's a nut! And hilarious!Not all characters are this much fun. For instance, once you start using a few characters for a long time, they level up much faster than the rest, who still get XP, but not nearly as much. So, you get stuck with those four.An important first four is essential to the game. My first four, after some tinkering, have been narrowed to Storm, Juggernaut, Bishop, and Nightcrawler. Storm can fly and is the token female of the team. Juggernaut is huge, hits things, and can tank like nobody's business. Bishop has guns. Nightcrawler has swords and teleports and is an elf. After you beat the game, you get Deadpool, so he doesn't count.However, for beating the game, if you plan right, this combination can absolutely destroy everything. I mean everything. With these four people, you have four unstoppable forces. One of Storm's super mutant abilities causes your party to be invincible for a time. Awesome. Juggernaut has two moves that work together. One is passive and has a chance to restore mutant energy based on damage done, and the other is a punch that increases Juggernauts attack strength and size with every hit. Awesome. Bishop can level up his energy regeneration so that he can fire guns constantly, or use a super ability that cause no energy loss over the party for a time. Awesome. Nightcrawler has a special ability that is, essentially, 'porting from enemy to enemy, doing damage. Now, if the only enemy around is the boss, he attacks the boss, say a dozen times in a few seconds. Awesome.To sum it up, the game is ridiculously broken. If you want to play a fun RPG that will only take 10-15 hours to beat, pick it up. If you want a really fun RPG, get Earthbound.
