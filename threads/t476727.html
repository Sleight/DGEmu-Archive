KKirie MotobaJun 08 2012, 09:54 PM
There is a memory leak since after version 2.

Just open your task manager and open Firefox 13.
The first thing you will see is Firefox already gobbling up around 100mb of RAM (only only have the ad blocker and download status bar with a skin on my firefox)

Now use that single window and tab and surf around the Internet, Youtube, DGEmu, 4chan, Gamespot, etc. And leave that window open for 1 week straight.
You will notice that the RAM Firefox is eating will go from 100 to probably 250MB by that time or so.
Now continue surfing and try again a few days later, you will see that the RAM usage will just keep going up.

Now open the well known MS browser, IE9. You will notice 2 tasks created, one for main application, one for the tab. Adding both together, it comes to about 35MB of RAM consumed (it consumes 1/3 of what Firefox takes!)
Now do the exact same operation with IE9 and leave it open for a week. You will notice that RAM will probably consume about 50MB by that time. Again, repeat usage and leave open for a few days, it will actually stay stable!

IE9 is an inferior browser, yet, it manages its own memory a lot better than Firefox.

I'm a massive user, and I can say this:
I usually leave about 4 to 5 Firefox windows open with about 3 to 8 tabs each windows, and after a week, Firefox is eating a whole 1GB of my RAM.
