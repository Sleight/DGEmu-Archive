Yupp, I second the DSTWO and the Acekard, too. The Supercard DSTWO is, like, 30 bucks, and it's the best flashcart of them all, in every respect, by far. Totally worth your money.

The Acekard 2i is the one to get if you would rather not spend the 30 bucks. It's cheap ($12+ according to shoptemp), highly reliable, and often-updated to allow you to play all the latest roms. It doesn't have as many features as the Supercard, but it'll def serve you well for some time.

Not too sure about the R4i SDHC, thoughh! Sure it's dirt-cheap (about $6 plus), and reliable enough to play all the latest roms. However, it's still made by a company which had to borrow the R4 name - these companies almost never maintain support for their products as long as a good company/brand like the Supercard or the Acekard will. And when support is gone, not only will potential AP updates be gone, but so will the updates which will allow it to work after your 3DS updates. So I'd still stick to the Acekard, if I were you!
