EenkaneiFeb 26 2014, 05:53 PM

Angel Densetsu. Excellent school-life story with charming characters who never fail to interact in hilarious ways. A great comedy read. Written by the author of Claymore (this was his previous work), but the art is a bit off at first. I recommend skipping to the third volume or so if you just can't get over the art (I have no problem with this, but some people say they do). Personally I've come back to Angel Densetsu and reread it four or five times by now.

 

I'd also like to mention Mx0, which aside from its abrupt end (axed), is another great read.

 

Both are less than 100 chapters, so check them out if you have time.
