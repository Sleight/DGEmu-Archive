easy, i'll show you. </MARQUEE> - put this at the end of the marquee.This goes before what you want to be in the marquee: <MARQUEE bgcolor="#58AA38" loop="-1" scrollamount="5" width="100%">This contols the colour of the marquee background: #58AA38This controls how fast it scrolls, higher=faster, lower=slower scrollamount="5" This controlls the size of the marquee: width="100%"And that pretty much all you need to know.
