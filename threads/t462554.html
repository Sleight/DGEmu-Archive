Please say if the explanations make any sense. I can't be objective at this point.The scene: a maintenance closet/officeThe players: one C. Luther, one Det. S. Shane.The time: one hall, two floors of stairs, a left, and one quarter hall later.A muffled yell. Two shots from the hallway. Something screeched. Shane burst into closet.She slammed the door behind Calvin, and braced herself against it. Calvin grabbed the chair and propped it under the handle. Shane and he slid to the floor, shaking.“Oh God... oh God...”“What... what was that...”“A night.... mare.”“That's... that's what it's called? A nightmare?”“No. Night. Mare. Separate the two. Mare like a horse.”“That looked nothing like a horse.”“I know.”Calvin crawled to the janitor's desk. He searched and found a bottle of cheap whiskey.“That looked nothing like anything.”“I know.”He took a mouthful, and passed the bottle to Shane.“What the hell is it doing here? What's going on?”“I told you I'd explain.”“Right now.”“Right now?”“Yes. Right the hell now. What is going on. What is with those pictures. Everything.”“Fine. Where do you want me to start?”“The pictures.”“Okay. Those pictures. They're pictures of an eye.”“Whose eye?”“That's a loaded question. Let's not talk about 'who.'”“Why not?”“There's... things. Out there. You don't want to think about them. Or talk about them, directly.”“Why not?”“It's... complicated.”“Everything's complicated with you. Why don't I want to talk about them?”“Words have power. Knowledge has power.”“Power. What kind of power?”“It's not easy to define. I guess that's part of it. You give something a name, you define what it is, you give it shape and form. There's things out there, they use that. They use the names they are given, it gives them access to this world. Back in the old days, that's what gave the idols and gods their power, I think.”“I don't understand.”“It's like. It's like you create a vacuum. Something has to fill it. It's just that that something is some sort of horrible demon thing that thirsts for blood.”“In no way does that explain it.”“I'm sorry. It's not an exact science.”“So. That mare thing. It didn't exist until you named it?”“No. It's not one of those idols or whatever they are. It's... I don't know. Almost self-defined. It doesn't need us to make it up, but that means it has to take a pre-defined form. It can't be a god, so it becomes a servant.”“It's a servant.”“Or a herald. It comes before, serves the will of it's master. It's weaker, so it can tear into reality a little quicker.”“Okay. You're going to have to slow down. Its master. We're talking about that Eye?”“That would be its master.”“And it's weaker.”“Yes.”“And it's tearing into reality... it's not from this world, or dimension, or whatever?”“No. No it's not.”“You said earlier, that it wasn't very smart yet.”“That's because it's coming in bits and pieces. Not all at once. It'll get smarter.”“Why hasn't it found us? Why isn't it tearing this door down?”“Exact science. This isn't one. I don't know.”“Okay, back to that eye. What is it?”“It's a perversion of all that's pure and holy. It rapes reality by its very presence.”“It's just an eye. Not even a real eye, just a drawing of one.”“That's like giving it a name, it's like a token. Or a boarding pass. Or something along those lines. Drawing it gives it a pass into reality.”“How was it drawn in the first place? If you said it wasn't real?”“The Meuller brothers. Richard and Georg.”“Yeah, what about them?”“The Meuller family... it's an old family. Very deeply involved in the occult.”“They... I don't know, they made it up? On like a whim?”“Maybe. I've followed the Meuller's for a while. Read some of their journals. As far as I can tell, Richard had a nightmare, not like, you know, the thing that tried to kill us, a normal one. The eye was in it. He felt compelled to draw it. Georg saw it, and made a copy. They both killed themselves.”“Let me guess. They stuck stuff in their eyes.”“More or less. I'm not going into details, but it was more than just the eyes.”“Oof.”“Yeah.”“So where do you fit into all of this?”“Oh. Me? You don't want to know about me.”“Oh, yes. Yes I do.”“You know those things, that you aren't supposed to think about?”“Yes.”Calvin smiled. “I'm what happens when they start thinking about us.”Once upon a time, there was this young man. He was an angry young man. He picked fights he couldn't finish. He used harsh words. He was a son of a bitch, and he wanted you to know it.His name was Calvin.There was a girl he took a fancy to. I forget her name. I'm sure it was something flowery, though, like Rose, or Lily, or what have you.This young lady, this beautiful flower, she died.He didn't like that. Not one bit.There was a chemist, in the little burg our love birds lived in. Calvin took her corpse to him, and told him, the chemist, to keep her body fresh and undecayed. Calvin would go into the world, and find a cure to death. Then he would come back, and claim his bride.The chemist thought that Calvin was creepy as shit, but money is money.So, Calvin went off into the wild yonder. He searched far and wide. He visited monasteries, libraries, temples, looking for something.He finally did. A man in a hut in a forest said he could bring back the dead, but only for a price. Calvin was perfectly fine with that. The man would revive flower girl, Calvin would throw the man off a cliff, and everyone would live happily ever after. Well, Calvin would, and that was good enough for him.So, they came back. They went to the chemist's shop, found her where Calvin had left her. The man did his thing, some sort of ritual. I don't know. Calvin got behind him, with a club. Possibly whistling. Ready to brain this guy the moment she woke up.She didn't wake up.Something woke up, but It wasn't her.He didn't know what It was.He didn't want to know.Calvin saw her body get up. Her hands ripped the man's throat out. They filled her mouth with his flesh. Her tongue licked her hands.Then her eyes looked at Calvin.And Calvin swung at her head. Hit her head with everything he had.Her body was knocked to the floor. His club was broken, and her head was too. Maggoty things were waving from her burst neck. He kicked at her body. Kicked her ribs in. Stomped her hands into a bloody pulp. He took a knife from the chemist's lab, and he cut her up. He took the man, the summoner's clothes, and wrapped the pieces of her into it.And he burned the shop down.And every moment of the beating and the burning, he heard Something screaming in his head.It was the Thing that possessed his flower's body.And It was angry.Every night, he had a nightmare. Her body, bloodied and hanging together by sinews. Pointing a finger at him. Blaming him. For everything.So Calvin went back to the plot of land where the chemist's shop had been. He wanted to face his demons, metaphorically if not literally. Instead, he found the body of the man, the man who brought that Thing into the world, standing there. Throat gone, and all. He wasn't alive, no more than Calvin's flower had been, but somehow possessed by the same Presence.Perhaps It was expecting to exact some revenge. Perhaps.It certainly wasn't expecting Calvin to have a hatchet. And some kerosene.Calvin was such an angry young man.“How long ago was this?”Calvin didn't answer.“Luther? How old are you?”“I don't remember.”“You don't remember. You don't remember a lot.”“I think it's punishment.”“What?”“My memory.”“You can't remember things because you did something wrong?”“Sort of.”“Can you guess? How old you are, I mean?”“At least two hundred years old.”“At least- holy shit.”“Yeah.”“I... wow. That's a long time.”“I'm sure it is. I'm sure it'd feel longer if I could remember more than bits and pieces.”“Why can't you remember?”“I can speculate.”“Go ahead.”“Okay, so remember how I said that paying attention to things gives them power?”“The things. Before we go any further, I need to know. What are they?”“I guess that's part of what makes me... me. So sure. What they are isn't something that I can really explain.”“Try. Just try.”“Look. There's dimensions right? I'm not talking about parallel dimensions, like sci-fi writers talk about. I'm talking about width, length, time, all that.”“Okay.”“These... things. They don't exist in those dimensions. Not without some help. They exist outside of space, outside of time. Do you get what I'm saying?”“I... think so?”“Don't try too hard. It's not like it'll help you any. They're minds, is what they are. Consciousnesses, living in pure chaos.”“Where did they come from?”“I don't know. I was raised Christian, so my guess is that they're demons. That's as good a guess as anything else.”“And they need a body. A host.”“Host is better.”“Why do they kill people? Isn't that killing potential hosts?”“That's why I like my demon theory. They're evil, for evil's sake. Everything's nice and simple.”“Okay. Back to your age. What do they have to do with it?”“I killed- well, that's not quite right. I defied one of them. I defied one of them in a very clear, very insulting manner. I killed a host, and then I had the balls to not roll over and die to make It feel better.”“Okay.”“Well, I'm sure that I became the talk of the town over in demonland. I was a human not acting like a human should.”“Because you were acting like an angry young man. An angry, stupid young man.”“Something like that. So, they started thinking about me. A lot.”“Flattering.”“I guess. I think, and this is just speculation, that all this attention started doing for me what it did for them. Maybe they blew what I'd done out of proportion. Maybe they made Chuck Norris jokes about me. Maybe, and this is a big maybe, they kind of sort of unhitched me a little.”“Unhitched?”“That's the best I could come up with.”“Unhitched you from what?”“Time. I don't feel it like you do. It passes me by. I don't age. I don't remember. Well, I remember, obviously. I still know who I am. I know how to tie a knot. I can drive a car. But anything that isn't related to muscle-memory, things like people and names and places, those I forget. And most of them, I forget completely. I don't remember anything about flower girl. Other than her name was a flower, and that she existed because I tried to bring her back to life and I failed and I mutilated her corpse.”“But you remember the rest of that story.”“Because I wrote that down in a journal when I realized just how much I was forgetting.”“Why don't you write everything down, then? Who you are, what you've done, where you've been?”“Because when I wrote down that story, the book tried to break my head open.”“Oh.”“Yeah.”“So.”“What?”“Where's that night mare?”“The nigh- oh.”“Oh?”He got up. “I can't believe I forgot about that thing. Shit.”“What's wrong?”“We've been giving it time to... shit.”“It had to shit?”“What? Oh, I see what you did there.”“Thank you.”“Very clever.”“I try.”“Who else was in the station? When this started?”“Um. The two officers who made your arrest-”“Where are they?”“Probably in records, doing paper work.”“Okay. Who else?”“The officer who was supposed to be watching us. He should've been on the other side of the mirror. Was his body...?”“If he died in there, I didn't see it.”“He might be in the bathroom, then. And the captain. Frank Gold. He'd be in the detective's station.”“Where your desk is.”“Yes.”“Where the pictures are.”“Yes.”“That's possibly the worst news you could have given me.”“I'm sorry?”“Okay. Well, at this juncture, we've only got one choice. Get up.”“What is it? What do we do?”“We open this door.”“What, and let the night mare kill us?”“If it wanted us dead, if its Master wanted us dead, it would have knocked this door down by now. I'm guessing we're about to have a nice chat.”“With a night mare? Or with some pictures?”“I'm sure it'll be nothing so nice. Just keep your gun handy.”He opened the door.The night mare was standing in the doorway.“Good morning, Mr. Luther, Detective,” it said. Its head nodded to each of them.“Morning,” he answered.She said nothing.“My Master would speak with you. If you would follow me...?” It gestured down the hall.Calvin offered Shane his arm. “Shall we?”She took it. “Are we going to die?”“We aren't dead yet. The future will work itself out.”
