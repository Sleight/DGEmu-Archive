SshadowmanwkpORIGINAL POSTSep 26 2012, 07:42 AM

Recently an exploit has been exposed on samsung smartphones that is able to wipe your whole phone (including data, apps, etc.). The worrying part is that this exploit can be started by simply loading a website. If your phone loads a single line of specific code, your phone can be wiped. It seems that not only samsung smartphones are affected, but other android smartphone manufacturers as well. Please use extreme caution while surfing the web with your smartphone.

To check whether or not your phone is vulnerable, you can go to this link: http://dylanreeve.com/phone.php

If your phone displays your IMEI-number, you are vulnerable to this exploit.
