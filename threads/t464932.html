It's hard to say, because the standard role of a point guard is distribute and get people open looks at the basket. In my opinion that player is Rajon Rondo. Then again players can do that while still being able to put up a lot of points. That player to me is Chris Paul. But an ideal point guard is the kind of point guard that makes the entire team. What I mean by that is he is a player that can turn a bunch of par-level or even sub-par (for NBA standards) into solid good, or even great, players. That player is Chris Paul of the New Orleans Hornets. He can take a group of players that nobody has heard of before and turn them into a championship contender. Another player who defines that ideal point guard mold would be Mike Conley of Memphis. Yes he is not the best point guard but he does what a point guard is supposed to do, and that is make his teammates better players. Granted he gets a lot of help from Zach Randolph and Rudy Gay, but that team wouldn't be much if it was not fro Mike Conley. Overall I would say that they are two of the better PGs in the NBA.
