There are numerous theories as to what the role of the Media is in society and whether or not it is neutral. One view holds that the media acts as societies security dog and is an important check and balance in a functioning democratic society. Another holds that as the people presenting the news come from a specific background, this influences the way they not only perceive the news they are reporting but subsequently how they report it, thus making the news biased. There is the other view that in a capitalist society, as they are trying to create a profit, they need to try and attract and appease sponsers in order to maintain profitable. There is also the view pushed in the 'Propaganda model' by Chomsky and Hermen, who believe the mass media is controlled through 5 means. So are the mass media biased and why or why not?
