DdibDec 07 2004, 12:31 PM
Ever since I first started playing SNES games through emulation that had otherwise been unavailable to me, I have attempted to understand why people rave about Earthbound. And to this day it continues to elude me on every possible point.I'll just reprint an older post that I wrote elsewhere regarding it:
someone wrote:
What the hell is the attraction to this game? Every place that I see it rated and reviewed, like GameFAQs.com for example, has people singing praises from their rooftops over it. They go on and on about how it's one of the greatest games ever for the SNES. To suggest something like this really means a lot. Consider that just some of the games the SNES has going for it just in the RPG department, including: Final Fantasy 4 Final Fantasy 5 Final Fantasy 6 Terranigma Tales of Phantasia Star Ocean Ogre Battle Front Mission Chrono Trigger Secret of Mana Lufia & the Fortress of Doom Each and every one of those games has many, many things working out for them. Great music, beautiful graphics, innovative elements, brilliant story lines, etc. But when I tried Earthbound, I could not find one single redeeming quality to the game. Not even one, honestly. The graphics were an eyesore (even for a SNES game of that time). The laughable attempt at isometric design produces graphics that are so cartoony that they couldn't even be considered good by being fake looking, in the way as a Paper Mario for example where it's intentional. You could draw a better game in MS Paint, and I sincerely believe that's exactly what they did. The controls are abysmal. They are unresponsive, tedious, and slow, and feel like you're playing an outdated NES game a'la Final Fantasy 1 (and I love FF1, by the way). You can just feel the precious seconds, accumulating into minutes then hours, slipping away from your life as you press controls then wait for something to happen as a result. All that can be said for the sound effects and music, is that they were present. Really, that's it. There was stuff coming out of my speakers while playing the game. I didn't play it for too long, maybe a few hours at most, but I didn't see anything interesting happening in the story. There was a spaceship, and I beat up some gang of bullies at an arcade, and that was about it. Not a single interesting thing. Final Fantasy 6 captivated me from the very first seconds I turned on the game. This game had me looking forward more to not playing it than to continue playing and see what happens next. Probably worst of all is the shamefully horrid combat system. It was like playing Dragon Warrior 1 on the NES, only without the one redemptive quality that it would be 1990 and so I couldn't possibly expect any better. An example of a fight was: "You encounter three enemies. I hit, they hit, I hit, they hit. Heal. I hit, they hit." It had no ingenuity whatsoever. I know that turn based combat isn't much more, in and of itself, but you can tell the difference between turn based combat in a game like Final Fantasy 4 and Dragon Warrior 1, and I would take DW1 over Earthbound any day. So there is Earthbound for you, and yet people still swear by it. They say it is a great game. There are only three possibilities behind this declaration that I can conceive of: 1) I am really missing something, and this game actually has some hidden factor that could ever make it entertaining or even worth playing. 2) People love it purely for nostalgic purposes, but despise it on every other level. Sort of like the original Ghostbusters computer game (which also had a NES port). It's an immensely crappy game, and yet you just can't stop playing it. 3) People are stupid, and the support behind Earthbound explains exactly why a game like Deer Hunter 3D can outsell a game like Black & White. So if anybody can shed some light on this, please do so.
I will take Final Fantasy Mystic Quest over Earthbound any day. I will take any NES RPG over Earthbound any day. I will even play any given Game Gear game over Earthbound, and considering just how much I despise the Game Gear that says a lot.To say that Earthbound is a "hit or miss" game is a gross mistatement. This would suggest that the people who claim it to be a "hit" aren't drooling morons who deserve to be shunned from society for the sole fact that they consider Earthbound even mildly entertaining, and that they continue to do so to this day would constitute a clear sign of brain damage on any number of levels. I can honestly say that I have played some of the worst games in the history of humanity, and the fact that Earthbound gets these inexplicably rave fan reviews qualifies it as the single most overrated game of all time.
