[Wow, I was kinda surprised to see a Bakuman thread on an emulator site :P (But I'm new to the forum scene)]Nanamine's flaw is that he is the only one benefiting and there was no strict incentive for his helpers to maintain their role nor their secrecy (as we saw later).But at it's root form (multiple ideas offered by a group to a given artist/group of artists to produce) I think it's easily a viable entertainment medium, but not for a Manga like jump.We see it to varying extents on the internet already. The example that comes to mind is with the webcomic Drow Tales. They're side stories (which I never personally got into much when they were free, but I think at this point require some sort of membership) have a section that is fan directed. Now this is only a polling system (I think how it goes is the artists offer a few options for what the characters can do and the audience votes from amongst those options, but to the artists' credit, I think they offer a variety of options that would be likely to be voted on (that particular section of the site is predominantly focused on fan-service lol).Something that you see to a lesser extent as a profiting entertainment medium (but a few significant exceptions exist) is where you have users directly input into story formation in the way of roleplaying with a varying levels of regulation and control (i.e. group storying writing, D&D, and I think there was some Ninja game like this that was based around the Naruto universe? Don't know much about it).But in those cases you end up with the potential for a lot of trash (admittedly a subjective call) that can be slipped into a story by your random trolls and less creatively inclined users.For something like Nanamine's group to work, I'd prefer a medium where users' ideas were judged by other fans (perhaps with the author's discretion to limit the pool of applicants) and then the top rankers' work could be added with limited editing for continuity.In some ways, this is how shirt.woot.com contests work- artists produce designs based of a premise. Buyers vote amongst the designs, and the top 3 are sold with profits to the artist and the company. (And oh how awesome some of the shirts are :) )
