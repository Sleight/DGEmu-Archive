KKirie MotobaNov 23 2009, 07:11 AM
Just to give you a small idea of what you're investing into:Both original and slim PS3 can have their hard disks swapped out.I personally bought an 80gb model off someone and changed the hard disk to a 250gb one myself without hassle.For those interesting in installing Linux on their PS3, stay away from the slim model as they completely stripped out that function, along with any chance of playing PS2 games.Also, for those that lack space and want to make their console stand vertically, you have to buy a separate vertical stand for the slim model, versus the original was large enough to stand by itself.Also, they removed a few more USB ports from the device, along as the introduction of a noisier Blu-Ray drive (but works very well though)The main advantage of the slim device is that it consumes a lot less power than the old PS3, which is great.Though it won't justify me getting a second PS3, it might be an option for me if my current machine fails.
