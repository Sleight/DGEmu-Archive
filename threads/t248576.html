SSir VGMay 25 2007, 06:09 AM
If you can't burn DVDs, you're pretty much not gonna be able to play 95% of PS2 games. While there are a few CD games (ICO being one), pretty much every PS2 game out there is a DVD sized game. So you'll need to get yourself a DVD burner and install that on your computer.As for finding PS2 ISOs, if you get a torrent from a place like Mininova, The Pirate Bay, or Demonoid, you simply download it and you have the ISO (occassionally it's zipped or rarred and you'll need Winzip, Winrar, or 7-zip to unzip it).If you're using Swap Magic, you'll also need the slide tool if you're using the PS2 (the fat model). I'm not sure on the PSTwo (slim model)...somebody else will have to answer that one.Oh, and a good place to get DVD burners is at NewEgg. You can also order DVD+Rs from here (you don't need RWs and I find +R works great in my PS2). TDK is a good brand to get.
