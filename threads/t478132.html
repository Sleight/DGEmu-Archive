I disagree. The Vita is a handheld console. It has Skype, that's as far as it needs to go. Found a Wi-Fi hotspot? You're good to go.

Turning the Vita into a phone seems impossible. Even the Go is a bit too big to be a phone. What about the touchscreen? The Vita has a good size for a handheld console, but is too big for a phone. Conversely, a smartphone (let's take the Sony Ericsson Xperia Arc S, for example) is too small to provide serious gaming experience.

The best we've got as far as combining phones and handhelds goes is the Xperia Play. It has the necessary interface and hardware. If we could get something just as small but more powerful, and thusly capable of emulating PSP titles, that would be the next step up, as modern smartphones can emulate the GBA and even PSX. Maybe more, I haven't delved into this just yet. Point is, these hybrids would be one generation behind current handhelds. At best.

However, before we look into anything else, we need to focus on battery life first and foremost. Personally, I'm waiting for Sony to release a new version of the Vita, perhaps smaller, but definitely longer-lasting.

This is all pure conjecture, of course, so I'm completely open to other ideas. Though, I would like to hear some argumentation in addition to opinions.
