Alright, ladies and gentlemen, what you're now a part of is one of the most ambishious and important undertakings in DG history. You thought the Download Center was big? You don't know big. My motto is go big or don't go at all. So naturally, you can expect this little introduction/instruction post to be quite lengthy and in depth. Anyway, onto the meat of it.As you're well aware, the idea behind this Recommended DS Games project is a simple one: to procure a list of games in an organized format with objective ratings to create a sort of checklist for essential and must-play Nintendo DS games. It is our job to swoop in to crutch the stupidity of the member base residing here and stave off their zombie-like groping for a DS game recommendation. Think you can handle that?A list is the preferred format, which can be discussed further in detail here, but the main things to be included are the game title, a rating, and a short, concise description extending no longer than one line that encapsulates the game.Ideally, each of you on the reviewing panel would be able to choose a game of your liking, and then rate it accordingly. However, it would also be nice if we could get multiple people to rate each game, and then average the scores for a more solid, objective-based rating without bias. More on this later.----------So how do I rate a game?There are two ways you can go about doing this: the long way or the short way. Obviously we prefer the long way, but I know that can be a bit too much to ask, so we're giving you the option to eithera) Write a long-winded, in-depth review, give it a rating, and a one-linerb) Merely give the game a score and a one-linerIf you do choose to write a full review, it'll be submitted to the reviews database and linked from the Recommended DS Games topic.The following criteria have been agreed upon and you should provide a score for each category, then average the scores for an accurate final rating. Ratings for each area will be on a 1-10 scale, integers only.Format:Genre: ______1. Premise (_/10) This is a vague descriptor, but was the best word we could come up with. Aspects of the game that fall under this category are things like story, plot, writing, general premise of the game itself (is it overly ridiculous, badass, silly, pointless, etc.), production values, etc. Basically anything that doesn't belong in the other categories.2. Gameplay (_/10) Self-explanatory. However, you must try to avoid talking about what you actually do in the game, and more about how what you do is implemented well or poorly by the game's designers. Facets of interest include: controls, game mechanics, computer AI, difficulty, learning curve, level designs, multiplayer, and anything about how the game plays. This category is the most important and should be given careful consideration.3. Visual (_/10) The game's aesthetic value. Bear in mind that the term “visual” does not equate to “graphics” and the reviewer must avoid rating this category based on how it compares to other games or even other systems. This is a Nintendo DS, and obviously the graphics aren't going to be spectacular, especially in 3D. It's a matter of how well you thought the game was designed visually, and how it affected your gaming experience. A quote from Bish: “One could argue that 2D sprites are old and outdated, but if they're good sprites with detailed backgrounds, I don't think that should be given a lower score, than a terrible 3D attempt.” Something to consider.4. Audio (_/10) Same applies to this category as the Visual, although not as much: rate the game on how the audio fit the game and enhanced your gaming experience. Mega-complex symphonies aren't always the best music, and if the game can get away with 8-bit beeping, rate accordingly. Things to consider include: sound effects, background music, attempts at voiceovers, and anything else that falls under the auditory criterion.5. Appeal (_/10) Similar to Premise, but a good way to close out a review. This is basically a summation of the game, and includes: addictive quality, replay value, closing thoughts/comments, etc.6. Overall (_/10) Average the above scores and give a precise rating to one decimal here.7. One-liner(Not to be included in the review) Your one-liner for posting on the main games list. The purpose of this is to provide a BRIEF description of the game and the overwhelmingly redeeming qualities. Basically, if you're recommending this game, what can you say to really sell it?Again, try to be as objective as possible and rate these categories fairly. If you don't want to write out a full review, scores in each criterion will suffice. It would also be ideal to review games you would recommend, since this is a recommendation topic. However, if you want to rate a particularly shitty game that has been hyped up or considered popular, go for it. This list can also serve as a “what not to play” list.As previously mentioned, it is encouraged for multiple people to rate games.----------READREADREADOnce you've completed an analysis of a game, create a new topic, with the following format:
