I need people to critique my writting or at least tell me areas to improve on. And mind you, I have copy rights to all this, so don't do anything you'll regret.A Future For EveryoneByJake “Swaski” Bachmeier Anain They said that history would repeat itself. Thousands just shrugged it off saying, “not in my lifetime.” Anain was one of those people. He was seated in a 986 Defa Cargo Plane called NightWolf. He was one of many. They were heading back to Europe, for the III world war. “Why am I here?” he muttered to himself. The plane gave an upward jerk, signaling that they were moving to a higher altitude. They were still to low for their chute to open if they jumped. This was his first time being shipped to the front lines. He thought about it, realizing that it has only been 17 weeks since he joined. The first week was spent waiting in anticipation to find out if the military thought that he had what it takes to make a difference. The last 16 were spent training. He felt the plane start to even out, and saw that everyone was attaching their main line to the jump bar. Looking around, he saw only two soldiers that he had met in his time of training. One was in his front, Tanden, the other towards the back, Jack. Tanden was a noob also; he started the same day as Anain did. Jack, on the other hand, was just finishing his second time on the training course, due to the fact that he was stupid enough to run in a shelling field to save one of his friends. Sergeant thought that he could use a second course to straighten him out. The council stated the fact that, no man is to risk his live to save another, no matter their rank or relationship. He then noticed the new guy that came in two days before their trip to Europe started. Nobody knew anything about him, except for that he does not talk, and he keeps to himself. There was just something mysterious about him, but Anain couldn’t think why. He was the only one equipped with long ranged weaponry, showing that he spent three years at least in sniper school. But why did his squad need a sniper if they were only to cover the town they were heading to. The town couldn’t have been more than 2 square miles. It seemed uncalled for to have non-close range armaments in a place like this. If anything that Anain learned from his sergeant, it was that being unprepared in a situation such as having close range combat with a long range firing weapon was always deadly. An alarm went off in the background, then a red light appeared. Readying himself, Anain checked that he had his M16 in the ready position, and that his main line was secure. Sergeant came out of the cabin in his new urban camouflage uniform; his last one singed from years of firing ammo. “Everyone is to jump 3 seconds after the man in front of them,” he hollered, “this is to prevent you from becoming tangled in the others chute.” “On the count of 12, jump.” he told the first man in line. The seconds ticked by slowly, almost unnaturally slow, and then a green light appeared. “ Go, go, go” yelled the sergeant. His voice was almost inaudible from the air rushing past the open door at high speeds. 3 seconds passed before the next man jumped, then Anain saw that Tanden was next. It took 5 seconds before Tanden mustered up the courage to jump. There was 2 more jumpers before him. Time seemed to slow as he peered out the door to see how far he could fall if something were to happen. He stepped back and swallowed. “I can do this,” he thought to himself. Suddenly he was shove through the door. He looked up and saw the plane was torn in half from the flak guns. He realized then that someone was clinging to him. He rolled over to get a better look. He saw the new guy, with one arm wrapped around him while his other hand held a knife in it. He was sawing away at his main line that was tangled in Anain’s chute. He was successful, but he now faced a new problem. Anain’s chute was open, thus keeping them from falling to their deaths. Now, though, the new guy was free falling. In unnatural swiftness, the new guy reached down and pulled his reserve chute with such calmness, that you would have thought that he did it a million times before. Anain looked down to see how much farther he had until he touched down. He estimated about another 10 seconds. He then looked up to see how the new guy was doing. As he watched, he started to think. How did the new guy know to get off the plane? He was another 5 men behind Jack. He knew that the guy could not have seen the pattern of the Flak guns moving towards them. So how did he know? Snapping out of his thoughts, Anain realized that he had to get into the landing position so he would not break his legs. He touched down awkwardly, causing him to stumble over. He got up just in time to see the new guy land with such finesse, that it again, showed that he has experience. Tanden All Tanden could see was darkness. All he can hear was a faint, steady beep. Then he heard some whispers coming from wherever he was. He had a pounding headache and felt a tingling sensation in his right leg. Tanden decided to open his eyes to find out where he was and what was on his leg. His eyes opened to reveal a blinding light coming from above him. His ears picked up a slight shuffling noise. He looked down and opened his eyes one more time. He saw a young, tall man gazing at him. The man looked quite shocked when Tanden opened his eyes. Then he started yelling for someone named Josef to come immediately.“Where am I?” Tanden asked with more of an authoritive voice. Just then a dark colored man came in the room and grabbed his eyelids to hold them open. He pulled out a flashlight and turned it on then shone it in Tanden’s pupils.“Do you know where you are?” he questioned.“No” was his reply.“Do you remember what happened to bring you to the ER?”“No, why what happened?”“You where in a terrible accident. You are lucky Swaski was there to stop the Shreil from finishing you off.” Tanden remembered the tenacious species Shreil. They were bi-kedal like dogs with no fur and hollow fangs that, if you were to get bitten, they would inject you with a corrosive solvent. You would have been a puddle within ten minutes after injection.“Who is Swaski?”“Why, he is the one that brought you here. Don’t you know him?”“I never met anyone by the name of Swaski.”“Really?” Tanden woke with a start as he was a light sleeper. He could hear someone in the background talking to Josef, asking how his patient was doing. The doctor said that Tanden was going to make a full recovery in a few days. He closed his eyes and pretended to be asleep when someone walked into his room.“I know you are not asleep. Your breathing has quickened, plus the heart monitor has a faster heart beat on it. So you can stop fooling around now.” said a shadowy and evasive voice.“I presume you are Swaski, am I correct?” Tanden asked.“You are indeed correct, my young, foolish friend. You are very lucky that I showed up when I did, otherwise the janitor would have cleaned you up with a sponge.”“So, are you going to tell me what the hell happened, or are you just going to sit there?”“I will tell you in do time. But now you must rest.” Suddenly, Tanden blacked out.Copy right 2007-2011

Read through the quote of your post. I made changes to your original post. Mostly just simple spelling mistakes and grammatical errors such as "They were heading back to Europe, for the III world war." instead should be "They were heading back to Europe, for the third world war." And other things like capitalisation and punctuation.Note: Never use numbers in your writing, always type the number out in full such as 17 should be seventeen. Always write numbers in full.Note2: When naming something like aplane like NightWolf. Either put the name in Italics or with quotation marks.Note3: Don't use slang like noob unless it is a character talking. You're writing from a third person perspective so you need to use proper english, so I changed noob to novice. Same with saying that someone was "stupid enough" to do something.Note4: Unsure about this line, "The council stated the fact that, no man is to risk his life to save another, no matter their rank or relationship." America's policy is no man left behind, try and alter it a little to make it reference more to the training course only.Note5: Removed the line "There was just something mysterious about him, but Anain couldn’t think why." Cause after stating that the guy doesn't talk, of course he's going to be mysterious.Note6: Always start new paragraph after someone speaks.Note7: Rethink this logic: "But why did his squad need a sniper if they were only to cover the town they were heading to? The town couldn’t have been more than 2 square miles. It seemed uncalled for to have non-close range armaments in a place like this. If anything that Anain learned from his sergeant, it was that being unprepared in a situation such as having close range combat with a long range firing weapon was always deadly." Snipers can support a strike team regardless of how big or small a town is. Having a company of only close range weapons without long range support would be far more deadly.Note8: Right action for the right object. A light doesn't appear, it flicks on or lights up.Is good so far.Note9: Give the Seargeant a name. Remember 3rd person perspective. You aren't actually in it, you're watching it all happen. Referring to the Sergeant as just sergeant implies that its a character talking about someone he knows which is suited for first person perspective. Note10: In the paragraph where the sergeant explains how to jump out, consider altering it to make it that you, the narrator, explains. In the military, you're always taught before you actually do it, therefore won't need it explained to you ten seconds before you actually do it.Note11: "He was successful, but he now faced a new problem. Anain’s chute was open, thus keeping them from falling to their deaths. Now, though, the new guy was free falling. In unnatural swiftness, the new guy reached down and pulled his reserve chute with such calmness, that you would have thought that he did it a million times before." The new guy had one arm wrapped around Anain. He never let go therefore wouldn't have free falled after cutting the chute.Note 12: "He touched down awkwardly, causing him to stumble over. He got up just in time to see the new guy land with such finesse, that it again, showed that he has experience." If the new guy free falled enough distance that he could open his chute without becoming entangled with Anain again, he would therefore land first.Note13: "“Really?”Tanden woke with a start as he was a light sleeper. He could hear someone in the background talking to Josef, asking how his patient was doing. The doctor said that Tanden was going to make a full recovery in a few days. He closed his eyes and pretended to be asleep when someone walked into his room."After Josef says "Really?" Tanden is suddenly asleep and waking up while Josef is elsewhere. There is no inbetween. Need to describe more are Josef says "Really?" to show Tanden going to sleep.Note14: General elaboration. Elaborate more on a scene. Describe the area. Describe what the people look like. Just like Note 13, Tanden awakes, has a few short words with Swaski and then blacks out again. More elaboration and extension of scenes. It seems like a pointless scene to me, why have Tanden black out again when Swaski can just explain it all then. Or, why have Tanden wake up and interact with Swaski if nothing to help the story move forward happens.BTW I am actually an aspiring writer with several works of my own, if you'd like to collaborate and chat just PM me and I can give you my email.
