Since this thread is on the DS board, I'm assuming the focus is on DS games. If that is the case, there's some pretty simple reasons that most of the good DS games are Japanese.For starters, the manufacturer of the system is based in Japan, and so will have a large focus on winning over Japanese developers. It's difficult for even a large business to get developers to make games for them when they are over-seas, and also speak a different language. The opposite happens to Microsoft trying to get Japanese developers. They're more likely to sign contracts with Nintendo or Sony.Also, consider the hand-held game market in general. Most of the market for handheld systems and games is in Japan or Europe, and, to my knowledge, there's not many European Developers (that I'm aware of anyway). Because the market for the games is overseas, not many north american developers hop onto the handheld bandwagon, except to do tie-ins for the games they are developing for the console systems. And at that, they are usually pretty dumbed down, and low quality games by handheld standards.The game industry is a unique one, but it's still a business. When looking at things like where all the good games come from, sometimes you have to follow the money trail, too.
