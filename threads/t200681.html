The main question at hand is whether this is correct way to get the desired result. Some of the content of your proposed rules do seem hypocritical or paradoxical. I'll go through each one individually:
someone wrote:
Advertising, spamming and trolling are not allowed. This includes using the forum email and Personal Message system to spam other members.
This does seem quite contradictory in light of the forum's name being "Spam Center," does it not? It'd probably be a good idea to disassociate these rules with the posting rules since that completely contradicts the notice that states that posting rules are not enforced in the Spam Center. If specific rules against advertising and what not are required, then add those as rules specific to the forum (or I'll just move them to General posting rules).
someone wrote:
Multiple or repeated posting, whether or not in order to increase your post or point count is strictly prohibited.
Not especially applicable now since the chances of that happening are extremely slim. Plus that really doesn't seem fitting for a place like the Spam Center anyway.
someone wrote:
Do not post meaningless posts or threads, especially threads with no content.
This one's a bit of a gray area. On the one hand, random letters and what not definitely should not be tolerated (actually, they should be deleted directly). But certainly the Spam Center offers a haven for people to ask random questions or what not that are not especially serious. I mean, restrictions are necessary, but to what extent?
someone wrote:
Don't use hurting expresions such as "go away", "n00b", "you sux", etc.
Suggestion for this one. Rather than stating it in that sense, something like "Treat others as you would like to be treated" (as suggested by Duce) would be better. Honestly, that rule just sounds awfully childish (although I suppose one could easily make a case that the behavior of the members in question can also be considered as such), but it has a good intention.
someone wrote:
Don't use vulgar expressions.
I believe this can be combined with the "bad word filter" rule.Anyhow, enforcement seems to be much more of an issue than the rules itself. I seriously believe that most of the members in question will change their behavior if someone higher up asks them to do so. Really, we could really do away with this new rules proposition and just say something like "Use some common sense. The fact that you don't like someone doesn't mean that you have the right to flame that person. Be decent or consequences will result." That's seriously all it takes.
