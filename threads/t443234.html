I thought the story was great, have no idea what people were complaining about. It was simple and easy to follow. Everything story wise is explained clearly throughout the film, a hell of a lot easier to follow than the fragmented and crazy story in The Imaginarium Of Doctor Parnassus (which is a brilliant film, great story, it just takes some time before you're fully aware of how everything connects and unfolds).I never agreed with the whole racism argument because let me put it this way Skidz and Mudflap were both voiced by white guys (one of which is the voice of Spongebob Squarepants) and it wasn't the movie that suggested that they were "black" it was newspapers and critics. Michael Bay and the writers never intended them to be viewed as "black," they just wanted two transformers that were idiotic and comedic, so who was the racist, the filmmakers or the critics and media who implied it? Jazz in the first film said things like "this looks like a cool place to kick it" and was voiced by a "black" guy, but no one said anything about him being a racist transformer.Other than that I thought it was a great film, my only issue is with Leo and other characters that needed less screentime, Sam's college roommate cause honestly I thought they could've made the film without him, he was an unnecessary character who was god awfully annoying. Skidz and Mudflap were funny, but there was too much of them and when it came to the fighting they did absolutely nothing, so Bay should've either made them really good fighters despite being childish, or reduced their screentime, and put a decent transformer on scene when Devastator appeared.One more thing Bay needs, diversity. Bay needs to expand a lot more when it comes to the Alt Modes of certain Transformers. Grindor's helicopter mode is exactly the same as Blackout from the first film, Demolisher from the opening scene of the film, his alt mode is then later seen as a part of Devastator, as well as many others like Long Haul, Scrapper, etc. all the Constructicons necessary for Devastator seemed to be at two places at once. One group forming Devastator, while exact replicas were chasing Sam and fighting the military. Even Bonecrusher from the first film made another appearance. Bay needs to amp up the diversity of Transformers and not repeat the same vehicles over and over, despite that this is possible cause two or three transformers can scan the same vehicle, it just makes it a confusing mess for the audience.My last complaint is that Bay needs to learn balance between the Transformers. The first film gave all the transformers close to equal screen time, with bumblebee of course receiving the most attention, but Jazz, Ironhide and Ratchet had roughly the same number of lines, whereas in this one, all the dialogue goes to Optimus, Ironhide, Megatron, Starscream, Jetfire, Skidz and Mudflap while Ratchet, Arcee and Sideswipe (the two new additions) got barely any screen time at all. I could understand why Jolt barely had any screen time and only made a few appearances but that's because he was a late addition to the film.Other than that, If Bay and the writers can improve on balance between the Transformers and removing pointless characters, the third film should be phenomenal. Regardless of my complaints, ROTF gets a 3-1/2 out of 5 and I can't wait for the DVD release in Australia this week.
