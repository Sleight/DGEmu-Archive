SshadowmanwkpApr 03 2012, 07:35 AM
The original R4 will not work with the 3ds. The reason is pretty simple: nintendo noticed that people were using flashcarts and went on to blocking them since the DSi. After some time workarounds were found and some flashcarts that could run on the dsi could work on the 3ds as well after an update. 3ds flashcarts are not really an all too familiar territory for me to be honest (old-school gray ds owner here), but there are a few flashcarts that work on the 3ds in various price ranges.HOWEVER, flashcarts that were made for the original ds cannot be used for the DSi or 3DS in any way. There are no firmware updates, be to bypass it, because the security is probably hardware related. So in short it means that you need to buy a new flashcart if you want to play roms on your 3DS.Note: Although some flashcarts can be used on the 3DS, they will not play 3ds games (yet), they can only play ds roms and homebrew for the ds, it's going to take a long while before we see a fully-featured 3ds flashcart that can play 3ds roms.
