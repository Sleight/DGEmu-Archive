Hmm, Like LizasLilProtege said “Which Nintendo consoles are you considering to be retro?”.When I think of “retro” in gaming consoles (and computers) I think of the old 8-bit systems, so to me that leaves two choices in the Nintendo category; the NES and the GameBoy. And out of those two choices I would pick the NES.Now if you are trying to define the older Nintendo systems from the 80’s and 90’s; NES, SNES, N64, GameBoy, and GameBoy Color. Well I would still choose the NES, with the SNES coming in second place.While the SNES was bigger and better compared to the NES, the NES was what I grew up on and was first exposed to plus the NES had allot of great original games on it that are still great today.While the N64 also has allot of good titles, unfortunately I find the early days of 3D consoles to be a bit of a turn off with their ugly geometry and poor texturing.
