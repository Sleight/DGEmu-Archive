1. Posting Rules
All forum rules apply in the Competition sub-forum, especially:
Don't make overly short posts even if the answer needs only one word.
Don't flame others because their questions/answers are silly or not in your taste.
Don't go off topic.
Don't post in all caps.
No silly guessing

2. Rules for Quiz Makers
Here are the set of rules to follow when you want to run a quiz:
Before starting a new quiz, please make sure the subject has not been done before.
Don't pick a subject that is covered by a more general one, that is don't create a sub-quiz of existing ones.
Make sure to include a score board.
Quizzes are meant to be long running so that make sure to update them regularly.
If there has been no reply in a quiz for more than three months, then it is considered as dead and can be overtaken by others wishing to continue it.
If you want to continue a "dead" quiz, PM a moderator (me or Channel28) to ask for their permission. If you wish to do so read the Adopt a Quiz thread.
Do not create a quiz and then abandon it immediately.
Include a list of guidelines for contestants to follow. (Rules, such as point values, grading system, misc.)

3. Rules for Contestants
For members who answer quiz questions, please follow the following rules:
Answer the current question, not the first.
Don't reply with the same answer that a previous post already stated.
If you don't agree with an answer, PM the quiz runner.
Make sure to stay on topic.
Don't ask the quiz owner what the next question is. Be patient and wait for the next one.
No copying answers from websites such as wikipedia word for word.
Do not post questions of your own. Allow the quiz runner to ask the questions unless otherwise specified.
Do not reply with just "Is it (answer)?" The same goes for answers like "It is (answer)" or "His name was Bob". Give a few words explaining why you answered what you did.

Awarding Quizzes
Well, due to inactivity of quiz makers lately, there are very few GP award quizzes out there. The GP awarding quiz still exists but as for the others it varies. Very few staff members award GP for quizzes let alone participate in them. If you want GPs awarded in your quiz pm a staff member for help. Anyone who doesn't follow the previous set of rules are subject to point deduction and warning.

Thanks for your attention.
