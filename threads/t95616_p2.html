NOTHING can beat ocarina of time! not wind waker, not roacle of seasons, not twilight princess, not ANYTHING!
... I sincerely hope that twilight princess does beat OoT. While having our grand giants like Ocarina of Time set the stage, they have to bow down at some point. Besides, TP looks cool so far. Don't you want to play it? I damned as hell do.But back to topic. According to my usual philosophy of babbling on, I shall attempt to explain my personal views...Perhaps, although my sight is skewed on this matter, in the matters of plot OoT holds a major advantage: it sets the entire scene, i.e. the beginning. At least, we can suppose it is the beginning. WW is just another adventure of one of the many Links down the river - perhaps one of the more peculiar adventures - but OoT is the start of it all. Of course, release-wise it's the 4th or so (or 5th? someone clear that up for me later) but plot-wise, the ground has been set. We have our Gerudos (go tanned ladies with red hair!), Zoras, Gorons, Kokiri (they should appear more often - I like them), Hylians and Sheikah - plus Deku Babas and miscellaneous. While we can assume there is some evolution that goes on into WW, the background has beeen set in OoT.In terms of controls I have to give it to WW - parry, steal weapon, spin like mad, sweet! It's an enhancement of the OoT system, so it would naturally be better. Any designer that makes a follow up even worse deserves to be fired. Other than the battle scene both hold fairly level with me, so its thumbs up to WW there.Graphics... eh, it's a moot point. Same with music, because right now I can't remember any WW music (leaving a VERY heavy bias towards OoT - Saria's Song! Dancing Darunia!). While I liked OoT's graphics, I didn't like adult Link's face. And WW has a nice variety of facial expressions. It's actually pretty tough to make cel-shading, you know, so I got to give my hands to that. But as a traditionalist I prefer our gloomy doomy semi-realistic 3D, so all of them tend to flatten out.What else is there...? Character development? In both of them Link doesn't speak much, so that's left up to you whether he's the surly broody type (unlikely) or our daring conquistador (just as unlikely) or our confused hero that does what he's told (which irks me). Both have personalities fleshed out decently - although I find it easier to imagine what Tetra would say and do in comparison to OoT's Princess Zelda - but I'm a Saria fan. And WW has no Saria. Damnit, they left out one important selling point. Peh. I guess it goes to WW there, but I miss Saria. One character I could actually go "Aw, isn't that sweet..."Difficulty and challenge has to go to OoT, hands down. Granted, my perception may be flawed due to my most recent experience is Master Quest (which, I must say, felt easier than the original OoT), but the item limits in WW is just a tad... unlimiting. At least when fighting Ganondorf in OoT you could go "Oh hell no, I got no more arrows left! What the hell am I meant to knock him down with now?" but the same can't really be said for WW. In terms of innovation... well, that requires a comparison against other games of that time, so my knowledge of that will be flawed. I'd say that OoT would win in terms of popularity given how it awakened the world to the wonders of Zelda (or Link, if you want to be picky), but WW had a few neat tricks of its own. I just can't remember what went out along with WW back then though... Argh. Since OoT gave Nintendo that popularity push I'd go with it still.Replay value? This is classic Zelda - if you want to make it harder, play it again at 3 hearts and watch as a Iron Knuckle kills you in one hit. Since I only played WW once, to OoT around 3 times (I'm sad, I know), I'm bending towards OoT.So, yeah. Final verdict. Hopefully no one got bored and left in the middle of my drab. OoT it is then.
