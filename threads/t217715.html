The reason why it is done at the next reset is that taking out a DP loan simply changes your reset amount, which if everybody took out loans at various intervals and the server had to do them all immediately, it would cause other problems. That's why the focus on 1 time.Plus there's the chance that if you take the loan, get your points immediately, that the server will reset your global DPs back to 0 a few minutes later, depending on when you take out the loan. If you want them fast, learn the server reset time (12 midnight GMT+2) where it falls on your time, then just take it out a short time before that...like 30-60 minutes prior.
