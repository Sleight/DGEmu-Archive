Needlessly slow games are a huge pet peeve of mine, but even if LoD went at a good, reasonable pace, it still would've been absolutely unplayable. I see people calling it a classic all over the place, and I'm just left speechless, 'cuz I see absolutely nothing remotely redeemable about it. Actually, scratch that, the aforementioned music is good, and all of Dart's misogynist grumblings toward Shana are so hilariously badly written and cheesy. But yeah, remove the unintentional comedy in a few bits of B-movie level dialogue, and the game is very likely the most disappointing RPG I've ever encountered. Mediocrity given form.
