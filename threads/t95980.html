Fable's a great game, and I've finished it as good and evil at least twice, but I do have some problems with it. First off, the good and evil aspects are kind of shallow. It's always a good or evil choice with no middle ground, and you're either good with a halo and everybody (and I mean everybody, even the guys) falls in love with you, or you're evil and you've got HUGE horns growing out of your head and everybody's afraid of you, but you're still a 'hero'. What's up with that? It's also too short and too easy. It's really easy to just buy tons and tons of potions for fighting the final boss, and while he may take a while to die, you can survive for as long as you like. Also, it's easy to find little bugs in the game, like killing Rock Trolls without having them attack you once, or getting your combat multiplier super high (my max is 121, before I got bored). Having said all this, it's a really fun game and well worth a play. I loved it and am currently playing as evil. Once I destroyed everybody in Knothole Glade. The town was deserted for weeks afterwards before people were brave enough to come back.
