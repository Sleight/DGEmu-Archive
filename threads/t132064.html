WwavehawkJun 04 2006, 07:06 AM
I prefer 1st person, because with most 3rd person shooters, I find that the character I play almost always hides whatever is directly in front of me. Seriously.I think FPS are better on PC than console, while with consoles, 3rd person shooters are better. This is mostly due to control; I'm more comfy with a 1st-person shooter if it's with a mouse and keyboard, but with 3rd-person shooters, a console controller works fine for me. Case in point: I'm awful when playing KillZone on PS2 or Halo on XBoX, but Kill.Switch is a breeze for me. Conversely, I loved Halo for PC and obviously the PC Half-Life 1. And ESPECIALLY the PC Ghost Recon (the first one) and Rainbow Six games.I guess it's mainly orientation. If I'm playing an FPS, I feel more immersed into the game, and it seems unnatural for me to aim and fire with analog sticks. On the other hand, 3rd person shooters also have little tricks that make it different from FPS, like the blindfire and cover options in Kill.Switch, so moving about with the controller's analog sticks isn't difficult for me.
