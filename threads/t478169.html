"I Put a Spell on You" by Screamin' J. Hawkins
Chardrcheze
It just ain't Halloween without it. This song has always been the perfect Halloween party song.

"If There Is Something" by Roxy Music
Kula Diamond
It starts with a cheerful upbeat and a... Youthful! look at things, our guy in his early youth surrounded by girls and can't make up his mind as to which one he like, nor is he trying to, just enjoying life. In the second part the tone suddenly change, becoming deeper with a hint of sadness, our guy is more mature now, and like the song, it hit him quickly it seem. He's now deep in love, and desperately so, despite being with the girl he love("I would put roses round our door") and I would like to think that "growing potatoes" symbolize him willing to have kids and grow them up with her. I love the instrument in this part, it's one of those that haunt you for a long time after hearing it, can make you feel a lump in your throat, it's that good(sad). The instrumental that start in the second part continue for a long time and the tone start slowing/mellowing, the story continue after many years have passed, our guy is now old, not as passionate as he was but still seems happy or satisfied at least, remembering and longing the old times with his girl, how everything was better when they were young. I love this song. Also ponytails are adorable.

"Burn" by the Sisters of Mercy
Sakamoto-san
The melody of the song, slow and weird. The voice of the singer once he starts, sounds demonic? Though he always sings like that... A theme of despair in the song. What can you ask more?

Resident Evil main title theme by Marilyn Manson
Mephiles the Dark
I believe this song is a good choice. To me, this song (back when i first heard it) gave me chills. I first heard it near the end of the first Resident Evil movie, but iirc it's also in the beginning. Anyway, this song is 'horror' themed but not classic horror. I believe this would be considered a type of "new age" horror. Like every horror flick that's been made sometime after 2005. Whenever i hear this song i try to picture myself in a certain scenario in which this eerie music might surround me. The setting reminds me of being trapped with no way out, a similar situation existing in Resident Evil, Silent Hill or Left 4 Dead. To be honest, only i can picture what i've said, but i hope you too can picture that when you hear this song.

"Isolated" by Chiasm
Le Loup-Garou
This song is played in the crazy sister nightclub in Vampire the Masquerade: Bloodlines, where you'll get your first 10 storyline quests or something. Anyway, you spend a lot of time there.

"Underworld Cats" by Dead United
Genevance
A horror punk gem. Will send shivers down your spine.

"Panic" by Caravan Palace
mouse
They took a snippet from a love ballad (Chet Baker's "You're Mine, You" - "I will never free you") and made it into something that, to me, comes off as pretty creepy. It's an upbeat song, but that just makes it creepier.

"BRAINS!" by Voltaire
PK Overdrive
For its Halloween-y feel. I didn't want anything straight up horror genre. I can take out the WH40k OST or hell, even Dark Souls' OST and find me a good, long orchestral track.

"When You're Evil" by Voltaire
PK Overdrive
Voltaire has his own take on music. He knows just how seriously to take himself. When You're Evil isn't exactly cheesy, the singer narrates the thoughts and intentions of the supposed madman with a touch of humor.

"Dance While the Sky Crashes Down" by Jason Webley
BR
I'm too much of a bum to write a sentence or two about how I feel regarding this song.

"Katrinah Joesphina" by Universal Hall Pass
mouse
Sure, I've got a total crush on Melissa Kaplan's voice, but this song just whispers "ghost story" to me. It's haunting.

"My Old Friend" by Emilie Simon
Sakamoto-san
French artist. A unique slow beat, her voice adds to the special feeling of the song. If you add it along a bunch of songs with errie feeling, this one would fit right in, even if the lyrics are nothing horror-like.

"Magus Castle" from Chrono Trigger OST
Hentai K
Anyone who played chrono trigger for the first time will know how creepy magus castle is, especially while this playing. A dark foreboding atmosphere can be felt while some distant insane laughter really kicks up the creepy factor.

"The Dementors" from Harry Potter Prisoners of Azkaban (GBA)
Hentai K
The incredible amount of tension felt through this song while fighting the dementors is almost enough to induce a heart attack. The inhuman screaming all adds to the unnatural atmosphere.

Lavender Town theme from Pokemon
casabrah
According to what I have read, it's a combination of the Pokemon's cries. And there's a creepypasta going around if you haven't heard about. Not saying I believe it haha, but it is pretty cool and creepy.
