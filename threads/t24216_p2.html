Legend of Dragoon not recognized by the media? Where were you guys a few years ago? That game had so much hype and press thrown it at, it was like the second coming of Final Fantasy VII (which Sony probably dearly hoped it would be).Personally, I was totally underwhelmed with the game. Granted, it had a neat fighting system that was so unforgiving at times it hurt, but the story was just so... BLAH. And Dart... what kind of name was Dart?In all fairness, there were some really nice cinematics in it- but that's not what I bought it for!Reading through the lists in the previous posts, I had to toss my lot comments into the ring-Azure Dreams- absolutely underrated! Nobody likes dungeon crawlers anymore it seems!Silhouette Mirage- one of Treasure's most bizarre and surreal games, but a total blast, even though Working Designs decided to muck with the difficulty a bit.Silent Bomber- a cross between an action game and Bomberman. Run around setting off explosives for the good of the people! You could set bomb patterns, and trigger everything remotely, with the explosions getting bigger and bigger as the bombs went off. It also had sticky bombs you could hurl at the enemies and watch them go boom as well.TEAM BUDDIES- a game that absolutely floored me, considering I got it for 10 bucks when it was new. Armies of happy little bean guys, with floating limbs a la Rayman, get guns and tanks and slaughter each other in a super happy cartoon land, all the while screaming and yelling with thick English and drill sergeant accents! You could build all sorts of junk to fight with- tanks, guns, bombers, jeeps, flame throwers... and there were tons of different skins to unlock for your buddies. They even had a rudimentary squad system in place so you could gang up on the computer or 3 other players! A true gem of a game!This is all off the top of my head- there's no telling what I can find if I dig out my collection!
