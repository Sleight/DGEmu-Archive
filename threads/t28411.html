Stay by the right edge during opposing attacks, even when Master Hand dies. Double-jump and dodge diagonally upward to negate the effects of most attacks. Even the Over Slap (the slap with wind blasts) can be easily jumped over. The only real exception is the Snatch; to avoid it, lure the attacking Hand upward as he starts and fall back down as he tries to grab. Use the airdodge above the Master Hand immediately before the Fist Catch Combo. Ah, yes, and try not to rely on the shield. It runs out quickly.

Well, let's start with the basics... the Hand's attacks!Grapple (Both) - The hand will wiggle their fingers and home in on you. Best way to evade it I found was either jumping in the opposite direction of the hand attempting to grab you, or rolling out of the way in the opposite direction. Should one of them grab you, wriggle the control stick to break free. If not, they're squeeze some damage into you three times before letting go! Crazy Hand will also inflict status effects on your character, the more noticible is a flower on the head, which constantly increases your damage meter so long as it's there on the head!Laser Fire (Both) - The hand will fire four sets of lasers to go around the stage. Fortunately, they have a blind spot directly beneath them. So long as you're there as it starts up, the hand can't hit you. Not to mention that they must complete the move even if you're there! So that means you can rack up the damage while they waste their time hitting nothing! This attack doesn't make for a good smash.Heavy Fire (Both) - The hand will follow your character's vertical position for a while before unleashing three heavy bullets of... whatever. It's pretty obvious when they're about to use them. And the best way to evade it is jumping over, or ducking under the three shots! It can very easily be blocked with the shield as well though, so... no fears there!Heavy Slap (Both) - The hand will sweep the ground with one large slap. Definitely painful, and a decent smash! Perfectly simple to evade by jumping. Blocking stops it from doing anything as well!Thrice Slap (Both) - The hand will sweep the ground with three slaps. Jumping makes an excellent way of evading it. Rolling towards where the hand would normally be will also be sufficient. Ahhh, how the hands love to attack someone who isn't there!Hand Drill (Both) - The hand will launch into the air like a rocket filled with smoke, and promptly comes back down to pretend to be a drill! This does MASSIVE damage, and is better off avoided at all costs. Fortunately, a simple matter of rolling, or promptly running to the other end of the field is good enough to evade the whole thing. Just don't try blocking... the shield will more likely break before it's done!Aerial Fist (Both) - The hand will launch into the air, and then head towards the ground of the stage from the background with a fist! It's painful. And it will send your character flying into the air if you're not careful. Blocking could cause the shield to break, fortunately, I believe jumping completely evades the move.Airplane Combo (Both) - The hand will launch into the air, do some aerial aerobatic in the background, and then slam onto the stage almost similar to the Aerial Fist. Except afterwards, the hand will then come back from the side of the stage it's usually on and land back onto the stage... almost like a flying airplane landing on an airstrip. Strange, don't you think. However, only the Crazy Hand doing this move will hurt you on the 'landing' part. As far as I'm aware, the Master Hand 'landing' does nothing. Either way, evading is all about jumping and rolling!Smack-down (Both) - The hand flies up to the top of the stage, hover for a milli-second, flatten up the said hand, and then slam into the stage falm first! This can be a good smash move. Jumping won't aid evasion whatsoever, so you'll have to go with running or rolling. Prefrably running because the hand doesn't follow you for very far, and it first must flatten out its hand, which buys you some time!Smash-down (Both) - The hand flies up to the top of the stage, hover for a few seconds while following your position. And then, almost suddenly, the hand smashes into the stage like a fist! The attack is blockable, and likely evadeable via Rolling, but blocking is the way I do it...Two-Finger Walk (Both) - The hand does a flip, and then proceeds to walk from one side of the stage to the other using two of its fingers acting as legs. If you get hit, it will sometimes follow suit by flicking you away. It can be painful, and although blockable. If you can jump right over the hand itself, you should be able to evade it completely.Three-way Prod (Both) - More of severe prodding than just normal prods! The hand follows you around for a little bit with a finger pointed at you. And after a brief moment, it will poke at the spot you were at before it started three times, each one more painful than the last! Rolling, jumping away, walking away... anything works providing you don't just stand there!Flail (Crazy Hand) - The Crazy Hand lays on the ground for a little bit, before flailing around the ground causing a minor earthquake. Being in the immediate area is not an option! Jump, or get to the other end of the stage!Bomb Rain (Crazy Hand) - The Crazy Hand hovers above the stage raining bombs on the stage, which explode upon contact of the said stage. Staying on either end of the stage makes for an effective way of evading the whole attack! Blocking is not really an option here, the attack takes too long!Team Attack: Applause - Both hands approach the ground of the stage, closing the gap until the middle of the stage after a brief release of power (Which is believed to cause your character to go to sleep if you stand around idley...), and then they start applauding, which is pretty much constant slapping along the ground of the stage. My way of evading is done via jumping above them. However, it is possible that you can hang over the edge to evade the whole thing, although I'm not certain on that. Blocking is no option here mate!Team Attack: Fist to Palm - Both hands play hard to fight! The Master Hand waves the Crazy Hand over to it, in which is does... via a fist! Master Hand catches the Crazy Hand with its palm... and that's about it. Very damaging, yet easily evadable. Jumping is your friend! Don't waste it...Team Attack: Explosive Impact - Both hands go to each end of their stage as fists, and meet with each other like fists. It hurts, it smashes... and you generally don't want to be on the stage floor when they do it! Jump!!!Now, the few things you may want to know...- When both hands are idle, the Master Hand is always on the right side of the stage, Crazy Hand always goes to the left side.- Eliminating one of the hands stops the team attacks! That's how essential it is...- The Crazy Hand is the one who will initiate the need to do a team attack. The two will only do a team attack when both of them are idle!- Crazy Hand emits multi-color fireworks of sorts around the hand, which is the visual aid that a team attack is coming. (You'll know what I mean once you see it...)- During the time of this, the Crazy Hand will remain idle, waiting for Master Hand to stop doing whatever so it can become idle too. Free hits!- Almost all of Crazy Hand's attacks causes status alignments, even if very minor.- Crazy Hand is faster than Master Hand.On the character aspect of it, it could theoretically be a simple matter of taste. Although people do suggest mainly Yoshi (Down+A while in the air), Ganondorf (Up+A Smash while on the ground) and (Young) Link (Down+A while in the air). But, may I also suggest Kirby? Kirby has the 5-jumps in order to bring him back to the stage via a side-smash, but he is also able to use his Stone ability, which will prevent all damage and damage recoil happening to Kirby while he's in that state. If they're planning for something nasty, change to stone and wait it out! Not to mention that his 5-jumps allows him to get in some effective aerial combos before he needs to land to jump again. It's up to you though, every character has a way of beating those two hands, you just need to use who you want to use!
