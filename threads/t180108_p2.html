I'll sum it up a bit. its a bit untidy at the moment. Harry and Voldemort share wand cores, interestingly from Dumbledore's pet phoenix Fawkes. Now when two wands share cores, the wands will not willingly do battle against each other. Yet if the owners force them to, an effect, thank you RzmmDX, is called Priori Incantatem, which takes place when spells collide. (thanks spamma)When this takes place, a "battle of takes place" where the spirit of each wizard is pitted against the other. A beam will be formed between the wands, and each wizard attempts to move "beads of light" into the others wand.This all takes place in some kinda dome of light. Eventually, one wizard will cause the wand of the other wizard to regurgitate and seemingly undo all the previous spells in reverse order, manifesting an ethereal image of that which was made or destroyed. Such as Harry's parents. NB, although JK Rowling did screw up the order of Harry's parents, since it should have been his mother then his father.
