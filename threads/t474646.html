The proper way to cook eggs is to mix them in the pan, not a bowl then pour into a pan. But I guess that mainly works for scrambled eggs. Anyways this is is how I make an omelette:

1. Mix eggs in bowl with milk and a little black pepper. No salt, salt + raw eggs = broken down, runny eggs.
2. Place paper towel in center of frying pan, pour a little olive oil in the center, use oily paper towel to lightly oil pan (could be replaced with cooking spray I guess. or butter if you're a fat )
3.Heat pan as high as possible on burner
4. Pour eggs
5. Take pan off of burner, let residual heat set the bottom of the egg.
6. Once bottom half of egg is cooked, put ingredients/seasoning on half of egg.
7. Flip over.
8. Either enjoy slightly moist omelette or brown the outside/form a crust and eat a drier less texturally pleasing omelette.

I like mine with a soft exterior but some won't eat it without the browned crust on the outside

I don't eat breakfast either.

My daily weekday routine:
I get up, prep myself, go to work. Once I get to work, I take 1 to 2 coffees in the morning and eat lunch.
Afternoon, I take 1 to 3 more coffees or tea (usually tea), then get home, eat, and drink a ton of water or juice.

Even though I don't eat much breakfast, I still got my own way of making it awesome when I do, Chinese style because of MSG!! XD

I just simply call it water eggs, very simple to do.
1. Take a large frying pan, not those small pans often used to eggs.
2. Put in enough Canola oil to at least turn it around in the pan and have most of the surface covered with a thin layer.
3. Pre-heat the pan.
4. While pan is pre-heating, break 2 eggs into a bowl.
5. Add about 20ml of water into it, add a pinch of MSG, a pinch of salt, and a pinch of black pepper.
6. Use a fork and quickly stir the content.
7. By the time you're done mixing the egg, the pan should be ready.
8. Pour the whole content into the pan. You will see that most of the bottom will nearly instantly get cooked within seconds.
9. Continuously push the cooked part together, the raw part will also start to quickly cook up.
10. Within less than a minute, your egg should all be ready, quickly remove the content into your plate.
11. There you go, already flavored and very very moist scrambled eggs to go with that dry as hell toast.

Also, to enhance that toast, screw the toaster. You can reuse that same already hot frying pan, add a table spoon of garlic butter into it. Within a few instants, the butter will melt away.
Add your toasts into the pan. While holding the bread down against the bottom of the pan with whatever you use to cook, just move it all around the pan. Turn it around, repeat the process.
You'll almost instantly get crunchy on the outside, soft on the inside toast with garlic taste.

And to that, I just add salad, tomatoes, and dressing. No bacon, no breakfast sausage, none of that fatty stuff as the toast itself with the garlic butter is already enough to bring up your cholesterol.
