Heya all,

I don't know if any of you DGEmu members are in the Montreal area, but as you know, the upcoming weekend (August 22 to 24 2014) is the Otakuthon anime convention in Montreal. This year, we have a lot in store for participants of the con. Asides from the usual panels, market place, and other awesome events related to anime and Japanese culture, we also got a few fine guests from Japan coming in for interview sessions and signature, such as Nobuo Uematsu, composer of some great tracks in Final Fantasy games, and Yui Ishikawa, voice actress in many popular anime series such as Attack of the Titans, Gundam Build Fighters, and more.

This year, however, is special because with a few dedicated fans from Montreal, we have a nice sub-event going on called "Hakurei Shrine Festival", basically a sub-event focused solely on Touhou Project and many of its aspects!
