SSinkhanJun 22 2007, 10:36 PM
For reference everyone, laser and optical mice are NOT the same things.As you all know, optical mice use a red light (LED) that we've come to know and be occasionally blinded by when we got curious. Most of these mice work by detecting movement relative to the surface they are working on by checking the light reflected off it.Laser mice use an infrared (invisible to the naked eye) laser that works in sort of the same way, but since they're lasers and not just plain old light, they can bring up to 20 times more sensitivity. They're typically best for uber crazy insane gamers who need that edge in a game.
