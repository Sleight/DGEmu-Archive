I think there is no real need to open a council for that since it is more or less the formalization of existing practises. However, there are now more strict procedures if it is accepted.Following the Murtagh case that caused some trouble and disagreement in the staff, I think it would be a good idea to draw a complete set of rules for ban and unban. The rules below are a proposition and everyone is invited to discuss them. The resulting consensus should be considered as the definitive rules every staff with the banning power has to follow. All comments in red will be removed.____________________1. Banning ConditionsAre eligible for ban the following cases ONLY:(1) Mass spamming (or flooding): the definition of this term is given by atomicknight and we fix the number of spam topics/posts at 10. That means if a member posts 10 posts/topics with "dfgkjhk" like content, he/she is eligible for ban. However, 10 is just a suggested number to give an idea, use your own judgement.(2) Dupe accounts: ban ONLY the dupe accounts and leave the original or main account valid. Comments on this item:- Give one positive one per account and report all warns from every dupe account.- Report also the GPs under 100 from all the other accounts (GPs over 100 can be left aside).- If there are many dupes, reporting all the warns should make the member eligible for ban under the rule (3).- If one of the accounts was banned under the rules (1) or (4), then ban all accounts. *banned under rule (3) is actually already implied: if one of the accounts has a high warn level, reporting the warns should lead to a new ban.I said nothing about banned under rule (2): if a member was banned under the previous dupe account rule, then if he/she recreate another account, just give a warn per account and report the previous warns*(3) High warning level: generally, members with a warning level greater than 50% are eligible for ban. However, again use your judgment, this is just a suggested number.(4) Other cases: for example, sometimes, there are members who registered just for advertizing. It can be easily recognized when you see a list of links leading to pornographic or pharmaceutical sites. Same policy for members posting pornographic pictures and Bugmenot accounts.2. Unbanning ConditionsAre eligible for unban the following cases ONLY:(1) Members banned under previous rules: this concerns mainly members banned for dupe accounts. If there is another rule update, this item should remain still valid.(2) Special reason: any staff member may propose banned member for unban if he/she can provide some reasonable grounds for this.3. Procedure- When banning or unbanning a member, always add a line in the warn log stating the reason why he/she is banned. If the warn log is full, delete a previous warn and add the last. It should help identifying why a member has been banned. *Also if scripters can make a log for who banned who, that would be helpful*- When you want to unban a member, open a poll in the banning subforum and state your reason. Every staff member is welcome to vote and post the reason of the choice. The majority decides the outcome.4. Duties and Power- Support Staff and Forum Assistants: report for banning anyone who is eligible under the rules (1), (2), (3) and (4). Please include the link to the profile(s).- Forum Guards: ban anyone eligible under the rules (1) and (2) ONLY. Report for banning anyone who is eligible under the rules (3) and (4). Any situation in which the circumstances are not crystal clear should be reported as a ban report rather than handled directly.- Forum Sentinels and Administrators: ban anyone eligible under the rules (1), (2), (3) and (4).- Everyone is authorized to open a poll for unban.5. Report for BanPlease use the following formats when reporting a member for ban:- Topic title: member username- Content: The post should include the following items.State the reason.member usernameIf the member has dupe accounts, please insert the following line in the post:[DUPE=IP]Wee this is an IP with lots of guys to ban [/DUPE]Thanks for your attentionDGEmu AdministrationCredits: Ody, Firebat, DJ Night Force 9, atomicknight, qcarachato, Falconer.
