KKHRSFeb 06 2014, 12:22 PM

Since I've been enjoying reading manga on my tablet a lot recently I decided to re-read Swweeet. This is a really interesting though short manga that I think anyone who enjoys psychological plot twist-y stuff will like. I won't spoil it but if you DO decide to read it, read it in its entirety. The first half of the manga is really misleading as to the actual tone of the manga. I won't spoil anything, but it starts off with a sort of slice of life feel to it and is overly cartoon-y, but once the story picks up things start o get a lot more serious and darker. Some of the light hearted and silly things from the beginning take on a whole new light once you start getting into the nitty gritty of it.

 

I read this a couple of years ago but it's one of those things that's still great the second time you read it because you can pick up on some of the subtleties and etc a bit easier the 2nd time around.I especially like this story because throughout the entire story they're leaving well-hidden hints as to what the big "mystery" of the story is and it isn't until the end that you're able to really piece things together. It's really a short read, I read in a little under an hour. I know if anyone might be into this, it'd be Mouse.

 

Also, fun fact: The author killed himself and had a lot of mental issues, so you know a "psychological" story written by a crazy guy is bound to be entertaining.
