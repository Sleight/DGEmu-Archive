To be honest I think this whole concept is quite silly. It may be true that sometimes it seems that when we wish for something good it really happens but we also need to evaluate each case. You reallllllyyy wanted some new game and somehow you got it? Not that suprising, someone who knew you wanted it probably bought it for you. Sure there are a lot of times when it seems like the law works but we as humans look for patterns and things that we want to be true we somehow make true in our minds. Think of all the horoscopes that people listen to and claim as real, they look for that one aspect of the reading that they can twist into being true and so continue to believe it, day after day. It's probably the same for people who believe in this, they start out already wanting to believe it and so somehow twist every thing that happens into something that came about from this law of attraction. In life stuff inevitably happens, and so if you just happened to be wishing something like it would happen, you'd naturally blame it on this law. But what about all the other people who had stuff happen to them with no one wishing anything on them. To blame life on some law is a bit of a cop out. "Sorry man, I crashed into your car because in my mind I imagined it happening", the courts would certainly buy that one!There is a difference between this "law of attraction" and attitude. Having the right attitude about anything can change your life around. Hence all the people who are dying of cancer but pull through because they were tough. I've seen scientific studies that suggest attitude affects what hormones and antibodies your body puts out. There are also those who just lose their will to live and allow their bodies to shut down. This is different to willing something to happen, it's a natural outcome of you brain controlling your body.And if you need evidence of this law not working, go into any casino. Every body there is wishing for 21 or for the ball to land on the right hole. And yet... the house always wins! :-)
