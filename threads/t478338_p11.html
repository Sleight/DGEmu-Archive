Man, Magi has been a trip. I think it's by far one of the most interesting shounen manga around right now. The problem is the dialogue, jesus fuck, I've read plot-based manga with less text than this shit, some chapters feel like reading a straight up book. It's worth it though because all of the lore and everything helps to paint the picture of this really interesting world, something that manga like one piece and nardo had the potential to explore but ultimately dropped the ball on. One piece had the potential to have a really interesting world and lore but after the skypeia arc they just kind of gave up on creating interesting areas and only focus on the characters now which is ok but there haven't been any particularly amazing characters. Punk Hazard was cool at first but the intrigue tied to it fizzled out really fast. OP's worlds and lores are all like this because they're episodic and based on the corresponding arc (like the dolls in in the current arc and the fishmen land in the fishman arc). It's hard to care about OP's lore when a new one is presented every year that has nothing to do with any previous lore and will have nothing to do with future lore.

 

Magi also handles pacing really well, it has timeskips in appropriate places, shows what needs to be seen and what doesn't and doesn't waste too much time. There's only been a few instances in which Magi uses an unnecessary amount of time to describe certain characters (I felt the Alma Torran arc went on for way too long and had way too much dialogue, almost made me consider skipping/dropping it).

 

The art is good, nothing amazing but not bad. Some of the battle scenes are hard to follow as they look really busy and jumbled, the anime solves this problem.

 

Really the only complaint I have about Magi is the writing, there's just too much being presented at one time and it gets annoying to read. It really has the presence of a light novel than a manga. It really doesn't have an emphasis on fighting that you would expect from a shounen manga. I wish the anime would continue as it would work a lot better in that medium than manga, it's supposed to be very colorful which is impossible to convey in black and white. I first watched the anime and enjoyed it a lot more visually than the manga.
