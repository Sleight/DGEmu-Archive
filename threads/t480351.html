If you're talking about master table, the nodes glow when you unlock class potentials, and there's one for normal/veteran/elite. The high potentials are unlocked when you get both adjacent nodes, and the master potentials unlock when you master 4 classes. (Master means learn all the potentials, not just unlock elite)

The useful ones IMO are
Double Action - Scout/Shock/Gunner/Fencer
Invincible - Lancer/Tech/Sniper/Gunner

Even using emulator though it takes quite a while to master 4 classes so its better to just stick to the same ~10 units you want to use throughout the game, much like FE.

As for who you should use, definitely use the 3 main characters and Aliasse (password unlock) since their special skills are really strong and you need Kurt to use orders.

The rest of the starting 422 Squad are pretty suck though since they all have low accuracy, except for Serge and Gloria who are really good. For the most part all the characters that join later are pretty good also (except Zahar lol)
