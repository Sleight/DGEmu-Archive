Basically just that, however, i'd like to add a little more to that general idea. You need to keep a sort-of happier disposition, because when you're somewhat happy and content with life's circumstances then you can most certainly spread that optimism around and it'll come back to you. There's no real "key" to happiness as we all go through a certain level of "tough" times and accumulate stress. Though we can prolong it by keeping ourselves happy, you know never letting someone else's problems or attitude affect how we feel (or should) feel about our own life. Also, just do things that make you happy personally. Do not pay any mind to how others try to persuade you with their outlook on life, because i'm sure it's much different than yours.

Above all else keep seeing your future in a better and more positive way.

To add something that I don't think has been said: Counter-productive as it may seem, one of the keys to happiness is to let go of the idea that you should be happy all day, erry'day. Life can be pretty damn crappy sometimes, regardless of what you do. And if you want to accomplish much of value, you have to be willing to take risks, make mistakes, and just generally open yourself up to the possibility of being hurt. Under such circumstances, the thing to do is be proud of yourself for getting through the bad times. There are different kinds of happiness, after all.
