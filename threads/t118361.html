CCoerulAug 13 2006, 10:01 PM
It depends on what you're using to draw hair, your style, etc.In a graphics program like Photoshop, I usually just use a small brush to smudge highlights and shadows with. I color in the hair with a base color, then make a new layer for highlights, smudge with a small brush, varying pressure. I make another layer for shadows and do the same. You can burn and dodge for major highlights and shadows later. :3When I'm drawing something on paper, I just make the important lines in the hair to show where it is and if there are any chunks. If it's really detailed, realistic, I draw each hair individually with short or long strokes. *depends on hair length*
