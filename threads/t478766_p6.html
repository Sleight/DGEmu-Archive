AaioliMar 09 2013, 08:25 PM

Ashe is basically an all-kite perma-slow bitch. I absolutely love playing as her and even though she seems pretty easy to handle at first, unleashing her full potential is a bit more complex.

 

Her Q (Frost Shot) is a toggle. Whenever it's on, all her basic attacks will inflict a slow effect to the opponent. This means that if you land consecutive hits successfully, you can perma-slow an enemy champ. Each arrow fired when her Q is on will cost mana however, and this combined with her W (Volley) will quickly empty her mana gauge early game, so you can't spam either of them endlessly.

 

Her E (Hawkshot) is an ability that gives you vision for a few seconds. I'll usually fire it over gank points in the jungle to see if someone's coming when I'm feeling a little over-extended. It has a fairly long cooldown for a non-ult, so I won't usually use it to check the lane bushes (I use her W for that).

 

Her R is a pretty powerful tool but is best use to initiate or end a chase. What I mean by that is if you use in the heat of battle, it will be a lot less effective since the enemy champs can see it coming and it will probably not stun them for long if you're standing right next to them. However, if you shoot it from a bush or a concealed spot and stun an unsuspecting enemy, you have the element of surprise working in your favor and it will isolate the target, giving your teammates time to react. It works well against escaping enemies as well if they're low on health. Since they're escaping, they will most likely bee-line in order to put as much distance between themselves and your team (unless they go hide in the jungle). This gives you a clear shot to hit them in the back and stop them dead in their tracks. Even if it doesn't kill them, it will stun them and give a chasing teammate time to catch up and get the kill. 

 

Now keep in mind that I am only level 15 and all of this advice might be complete horse crap, but this is how I have been using her and I've had success so far against low-level players like me.

There is no reason, unless you play Shaco, for you to not get a leash for the jungle.

Stick around for a few hits and you miss out on a few minions from mid lane. Stick around for longer and the top laner misses a few minions (due to distance travelled).
