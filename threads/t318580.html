I'm just thinking of a Tekken game with an advanced campaign mode...Normally in the story mode you would just select a character, say Jin, Lei, Eddy or whatever, then you would go on to battle other characters and fight the bosses in the end. The recent few editions to the series included special cut scenes. The story mode is supposed to represent the King of Ironfist tournament, but it seems that we could only catch a glimpse of a small part of the tournament, which seems to be only the back part as there are only the main characters left.What I'm thinking is a Tekken that has a story mode that encompasses the entire King of Ironfist tournament. So how is it different? Before every match starts, there would be a chart representing the progress of the tournament, in the form of a Single-elimination tournament. See here if you don't know what it means: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-elimination_tournamentAnd that's not all. To make the King of Ironfist tournament complete, the player would have to face off with a number of random fighters in the beginning, usually fighters of the same origin as the fighter the player chose. For example, if the player chooses Eddy, he would have to fight against Capoeira fighters coming from the same region, school or master as him (Though Eddy never did enrole in any Capoeira academy). Another example: Say if you choose Yoshimitsu, you would need to fight against ninjas coming from your own clan first then the rival ninja clans. (I love giving examples) Yet another example: If you choose Lei, you would have to fight against other Wushu fighters in Hong Kong, including other policemen and even criminals looking to enter the competition.The random fighters would be, of course, randomly generated, everything from their clothes, equipment if there's any, to their face and complexion, and most importantly, their skills and moves. The period in which the player fights against these random fighters would be called the selection phase, in which the champion of each region (There could be several as some of the main characters came from the same area) would move on to the main part of the tournament which you guys should be familiar with in the other Tekken games. At the start of the selection phase, the random fighters would be mostly easy to defeat, but as tournament progresses, the last fighter would be difficult to handle.During the selection phase, premade fighters could also be made to be seeded into the selection phase. These fighters would be put into the selection phase to form a story. For example, when you choose King, you would have fight against other Mexican Luchadors, and some premade ones would form a sideplot- The certain Luchadors could be anything from rivals to fighters seeking to destroy King's orphanages.As the game moves on to the rest of the campaign, which is the part where all the main characters clash, there would still be a single elimination tournament chart for you to refer to. It could also be possible for some of the random fighters to make it into the main part of the campaign. Yes, the entire course of the tournament is not premade. Only key characters to the plot of the fighter you chose would be programmed to clash with you though.
