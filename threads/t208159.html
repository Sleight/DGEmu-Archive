I disagree with the topic. It's strange that I'm actually reading this, since most people I've seen have been whining that Final Fantasy is changing TOO MUCH. If you think about it, other than the title, the similarities of Final Fantasy are mainly in name and superficial elements. Sure, there are some common factors, but if you think about it that way, shouldn't Square Enix just rename all their RPGs Final Fantasy since they follow these factors as well (such as saving the world, villains having a back story, overly cute girls, orphans as heroes). The point I'm trying to make is that the Final Fantasy Series doesn't really have a connection with each other, and that you can't really view them in a linear manner.I believe that the title of "Final Fantasy" is just a symbolic icon for RPG fans to rally under, even if you don't particularly like the series; it's just something to identify with. To Square Enix, the title is just there to flag whichever project they think they're putting the most resources in, as a signal to fans of their work.I'm not saying the series is all great, there are lots of things I hated in the series.
