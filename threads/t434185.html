55hoursMar 16 2009, 10:53 PM
If you like AC:WW, you'll love this game as well.After playing AC:WW for some time, it gets quite repetitive and maybe a bit boring,since after paying off your debt and finishing the museum, there isn't much else to do. (Apart from wifi-ing)Well, Enchanted Folk takes all the good stuff from AC:WW, and adds some really neat stuff to it.Some highlights:- You can take classes where you will learn magic spells or incantations, which you can use in the game and during wifi.All sort of things: you can either obtain items, change the weather and atmosphere or let events happen that involve your neighbours.. - When you have learned some songs, you can either sing them or play them on an instrument. You can jam together with your neighbours or with your friends during wifi.- Your relationships/friendships with the neighbours can really grow. From time to time they will ask you if you like them enough to be your friend.. and neighbours will react to this, ask you if you are really close with your friend etc. There is even a 'love shrine'.. so, what do I need to say more? (You can even go on dates ;p)- The 'land?' is alot bigger though, not only is there a school, but also a dorm, a city with several shops, a dragon/mushroom cave, a haunted house, an island... it's amazing!- One really cool thing are the 'mystery times'.. the atmosphere will completely change, new insects/fish, mushrooms etc will appear, and a special character will appear, who you have to help. (There are around 50 of these events!)And best of it: YOU CAN FLY ON A BROOMSTICK. Hehe.Oh, and one funny thing I came across, I won't really spoil it though, but remember Resetti from AC:WW?Well, try not saving your game twice in a row, and see what happens. I was amazed by this neat feature, haha.So, there you have it. I know I wrote way too much, but I was anticipating this game for so long, and for now, I'm not dissapointed!
