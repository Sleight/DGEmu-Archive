AarrestedgamingORIGINAL POSTApr 05 2013, 03:39 AM

We all know about those classic games like Mario, Zelda, Metroid, Final Fantasy, Castlevania and the like, but what about all those other great games out there that never got the attention they deserved? What are some of your favorite lesser known games, or just games that had interesting ideas or concepts?

 

One of my personal favorites was Sunset Riders for the SNES. Contra style gameplay set in the wild west. Not much more to be said about this great game.

 

Another game of note to me was Metal Walker for the GBC. A RPG were you control a robot, collecting items to transform said robot into a better fighter, and to gain abilities outside of battle to help you progress through the game, and a battle system that plays similar to Pool. Pick your angle, stop the power bar where you want, and ram your opponent into bad pick ups, or ricochet around them to pick up that thunder pick up they're weak to. Not the greatest game, but definitely one of the more interesting. 

 

Any one else have some lesser known games they'd like to share?
