85 Vehicles (including 4 motorcycles), 120 different events to compete in, hundreds of items to smash throughout the city, and races where you can pick your route instead of hitting invisible walls and giant floating arrows. Oh, and you are encouraged to wreck your opponents. The game also features a rather large soundtrack with some nice variety to it but for some reason the PC version doesn't include the ability to play your own songs stored on your PC. The game has both offline and online multiplayer but I didn't try playing it online and I'm really not sure anybody should try.
The game is broken into 3 chunks for download. Decompress with any program that can handle a multi part 7z file and you will have an ISO. Either mount it on a virtual drive or burn to a DVD. On the disc you will find a crack folder with a keygen, the latest patch, and the cracked exe. Install the game (using the keygen when it wants the product key), then install the patch, and finall copy the cracked exe to the install directory.
Have fun.
