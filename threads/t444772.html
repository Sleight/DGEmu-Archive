TThe Flying DutchmanDec 16 2009, 08:47 AM
Gaming companies always create collectors and special editions for the consoles, moreso than they do computer games, because of piracy. Computer games can easily be pirated, console games however are not so easy. Therefore they assume that due to piracy, computer sales of a game are going to be drastically lower than sales of console games. If console games are where all the money is, obviously thats where they'll put the SE's or CE's.It's also the case of demand. Most gamers will use consoles. I, being one of them. I cannot remember the last time I played a computer game. The only games I'll play on a computer are games like Command And Conquer because I don't think they translate well to console control systems. I prefer mouse and keyboard for C&C. Other than that, all other games will be on my console.Its simply smart marketing. Don't invest too much money on computer based gaming when sales shows all sales of the same game are more popular on a console. Hence why you often see games released on console weeks before they appear on PC.
