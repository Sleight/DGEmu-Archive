Welcome to the madness that is the Screenshotters Blitz, Fall Edition!For anybody wanting to help out DGEmu, here's your big chance to do so and score some major DPs!During the month of October, those signed up for the Blitz will work to complete sets in order to obtain the grand prize of 500DPs! Yeah, you heard right. FIVE HUNDRED DPs.Signups will begin now and last until September 29th, 11:59PM CST. After this time, entries will not be allowed. Starting October 1st, sets will be assigned to all contestants. To complete a set, you have to complete the following:1) Take a good title shot of each game in the set.2) Take a good in-game shot of each game in the set.3) Upload both shots properly and add genre and emulator listings.Three simple steps per game. Complete a set, grab another. Away you go!At the end of the month (October 31st 11:59PM CST), the contest will end and tallies totaled. Here's what the winners get!First Place: 500 DPs (1 DP system of your choice)Second Place: 250 DPs (1 DP system of your choice)Third Place: 125 DPs (1 DP system of your choice)Fourth Place: 75 DPs (1 DP system of your choice)All runners up: 50 DPs (To NDS/GBx DPs only.)In the event of a draw, each person in the tie will split even the prize for each of the positions.Ex: 3 people qualify for 1st place. Thus, they will split the 500, 250, and 125 prizes equally amongst them (each will get 291 points)Ex: 2 people qualify for 2nd place. Thus, they will split the 250 and 125 prizes equally amongst them (each will get 187 points)Ex: 5 people quality for 1st place. Thus, they will split the 500, 250, 125, and 75 prizes (as well as 1 50-point runner up prize) (each will get 190 points, as well as 10 additional Nintendo Handheld points)(Note: In case of partial points from these prizes, DP totals are rounded down. So the 2nd example would not give 187.5, but 187.0 for the sake of simplicity.)Judges rules:1) All snapshots must be good quality snaps. We reserve the right to reject snaps that are not of good quality.2) All snapshots must follow the Screenshotting standard. This standard will be listed at the beginning of the contest. Please read it throughly. Any snaps not following this standard will be rejected.3) In order to classify as a “runner-up”, 1 full set must be completed. If this is not done, a runners-up award will not be awarded.4) Judges reserve the right to kick anybody out of the contest for purposely making a mess of the screenshotting project, whether it's uploading screenshots improperly, breaking other rules, etc.5) All decisions by the judges are FINAL. Anybody contesting, complaining, or whining to the judges or anywhere on DGEmu Forum will automatically be disqualified from receiving any award, and possibly other penalties for breaking forum rules.6) Those disqualified from the contest run the risk of being barred from future Screenshotting contests.7) During certain points of the month, we do checks on progress. Those who have shown no signs of work will be immediately dismissed, even though we state 'a set by the end of the month'. There will be an extraordinary push this month on this, due to the sheer number of people in this competition. Don't feel you can slack off until October 31st and think you can get your work done then (though I don't expect you to have your set done in a day. I'm not that mean). We don't want that.8) Judges can amend the rules if necessary at any time.To apply for the contest, please submit your post within this thread. You may also ask questions, but don't worry about what to do...everything will be explained once the blitz starts.All off-topic posts (aka SPAM) will cause the poster to suffer a warn, unless you are a staff member.(Seriously, don't push your luck. Applications or questions only.)Thank you for your participation in this contest. We, the judges, thank everybody for participating. Except those that screw up royally.Sir VG, Blitz Master
