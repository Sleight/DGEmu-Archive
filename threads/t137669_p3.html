Out of Moltres, Zapdos, and Articuno, I would vote for Zapdos. Why? Let me get a little technical.Let's start with Moltres. It's type setup is Fire/Flying. Doesn't sound too bad? Well, Rock moves do 4x the damage to it. While Fire, in this case, doesn't have to worry about the Ground weakness since Flying negates that, Water will still do 2x the damage, but Rock is the biggest thing to worry about. In addition, its moveset from level-ups isn't that great. You can't get Fire Spin on original RBY, you have to go to GSC to get it. And even then, you catch Moltres and it's next best Fire move will be Flamethrower at 49. On the Flying side of things, it gets Peck, Wing Attack and then Sky Attack at 73 (GSC) or 60 (RBY). Not exactly the best of things when you have to spend money on a "Legendary" in order to make them any sense of useful. And what's this? In RBY at L51 you get Leer. That's right. Not a cool or powerful move. You get Leer.Articuno. Ice/Flying. 4x the damage from Rock. Will take 2x damage from Fire and Steel (if you are playing a version with that type). In my opinion, this doesn't look good. For an Ice/Flying Pokemon, what good is it to have a secondary type of Flying when you don't get any Flying type moves except for those it already learned? Articuno gets two Flying type moves, Peck or Gust, depending on the version. You get Peck in RBY and Gust in GSC. The only other way to get a Flying type move is to "waste" a TM on it. I say "waste" in that fashion because it seems pointless to me and a few others that you should have to combine a TM with a so-called Legendary Pokemon just to make the Pokemon just a basic worthwhile fighter.Zapdos is last in the line for getting taken apart by my thought-process, but there's a reason. Let's take a look. Zapdos is Electric/Flying. Rock does 2x the damage. Okay, fine. Still gives you "super effective", but at least it's better than both Moltres and Articuno's 4x the damage from Rock. Flying is weak to Electric, but since Electric does 50% damage against Electric, the damage gets negated down to just 1x instead of 2x (2x cut in half, basically). Ice will still get through at 2x. And still, Ground has no effect. Zapdos gets the ultimate Electric power, Thunder, at L51 in RBY and L73 in GSC. Although in RBY all three Legendaries needed a bit of help to make them live up to their names, Zapdos is by far the most useful. Electric and Flying moves combined are super-effective against 5 different types (Water, Flying, Grass, Fighting, and Bug). Fire and Flying moves from Moltres chalk up 4 types (Grass, Ice, Bug, Fighting). Articuno's Ice and Flying give 6 (Grass, Fighting, Ground, Flying, Bug, and Dragon), however, what is the ratio of fighting Bug and Dragon Pokemon in a battle against battles not involving them? Actually, go back into the full 376 Pokedex from all games and find the ratio of Pokemon with either Bug or Dragon as one of their two types, and you'll see that Ice/Flying does more damage than good. In GSC Zapdos also gets Drill Peck, and at least starts out with Peck and Thundershock, two of the staple attacks from its arsenal.
