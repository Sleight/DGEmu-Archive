The game is very fun to me, but it is far from perfect. There are more obvious glitches (20 hours before doors open/able to loot corpses after combat on occasion? O_O ) and stuff that should not occur than I can list (ranger pet easily tanking high dragons for example). I also dislike only being able to set 6 spells/abilities - I find myself pausing all the time when I play, especially on Nightmare where sometimes the tactics don't seem to kick in when I need them most. If your PC is a mage, you are severely limited by only having 6 spells/abilities ready to cast from the hotbar. People go on and on about the graphics being sub-par as well, but I only really noticed that if I stopped playing and actually took a long glance around the area. Honestly though, we are spoiled indeed today to complain about graphics such as these when it is the gameplay and story that should be considered foremost. I thought the story was compelling but also generic to most other fantasy RPGs, which is okay to me because it is an awfully difficult trend to break.
