They are still being developed. Some of the most notable homebrew are dsx86, a pc emulator that can run windows 3 (although limited, but old dos games work like a charm). There's also a port of minecraft in full 3D and is quite the talk right now on gbatemp. Not really a new homebrew, but nesds also received several updates a month or so ago, adding button shortcuts and adding single card multi-player (uses a cartridge swap method).There are probably quite some that I missed, but if you check the homepage and the ds homebrew section of gbatemp you can pretty much stay up to date with the ds homebrew that is released. The console might be near-dead, but there are still interesting developments in the homebrew scene.
