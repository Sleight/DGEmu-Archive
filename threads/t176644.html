just bought Carbon:own the City for DS.Quite good,considering that i expected worse graphics for the DS.Cars arent easilly recognizable unless you really squint hard at the body.(i couldnt differentiate the RX7 from the RX8 at first glance,a testament to the poor details)and apparently EA HAS to squeeze in some kind of story with every game nowadays.here's a beirf rundown:(spoiler)You are racing your brother 6 months ago when a nasty accident kills him on the spot and puts you in hospotal for a long time.And now you want to reclaim the streets to find out who set up the freak accident.New features include a racing crew to assist you in your races,and the DS exclusive allows you to customize the dashboard which is on the bottom screen.The dashboard feature is quite a welcome addition to the game considering how it feels as if you're really looking at a car dashboard.that aside,its pretty much the same stuff:arcadey controls which isnt helped much by the tight confines of the tiny DS. >.<to make up for it,you can press the down button together with the the left or right button for deep steering,and up + left/right for gentle steering in addition to the noormal left/right steering.sounds good,but really,iyou wont bother with it at all.Music is quite good but the lack of tracks makes it dull after awhile.Controls are a mixed bag:The car seems to handle a LITTLE bit more realistic than Most Wanted (but still a far cry from Underground).The braking is horrendous though:the brakes and the handbrakes seem to feel the same,at times too strong for its own good.(if this is an attempt to make the game semi-real-simulator,its a failure :D )that said,its still a bundle of fun.Trust me,it'll take you a decent amount of time to get though it,definitly value for your money. ^^
