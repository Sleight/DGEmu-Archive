EEastern PaladinFeb 28 2013, 06:28 PM

 I own the game and I've put a lot of time into it so far. The graphics, game-play, story, and voice acting are great. The story and voice acting are often hilarious too, the plot is insane and most of the characters are way over the top. It's one of the first games I bought after I purchased my 3DS and I still play it. I highly recommend it.

 The game does have some minor problems concerning the controls and the voice acting distracting the player but it's not so bad and the pros far out weigh the cons.

 

The single player side of the game has enough content to keep you going for a good 30+ hours and for those that enjoy muti-player you won't be disappointed with this game, it's lots of fun and can really extend the life of this already lengthy adventure.

 

BUY IT, and while you're at it download 3D Classics Kid Icarus and Kid Icarus: Of Myths and Monsters (both from the E-Shop) :P

They're both great classics and deserve your consideration.
