When the installation completes, you're now ready to patch the game to the current version.From your start menu, mouse over All Programs -> Riot Games -> League of Legends -> Play League of Legends. Once the game finishes any patches that need to be applied, click the orange "Play" button in the lower right corner. This will bring up the PVP.net Client at the Login screen.At the Login Page, type in your Summoner's Username (which is only used for logging into the website or the PVP.net Client) and your password, then click "Log In" or hit "Enter". If this is your first time logging into the client, it will ask you several questions, including the Summoner Name you wish to use. The Summoner Name is what anyone on the website, game, or PVP.net client will see you as. The other questions are merely for helping you get started comfortably. After you input your Summoner Name, you're ready to play!
