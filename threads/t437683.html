poliop' post='4652406 wrote on Jul 8 2009, 07:42 AM:
Is it better for your hardware to leave your pc on and how does that work??
Mechanical wear and tear combined with power surges. It takes less power to keep things running than to start them repeatedly. Inertia plays a big part with optical drives, hard drives, and fans. It is better to leave a machine running throughout that day than to repeatedly have parts having to spin up.
