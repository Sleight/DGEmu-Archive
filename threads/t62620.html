..luchaduceJul 19 2005, 09:55 AM
ok make your sig as normal. dont colour it yet. Add the text and place all of it in a set (helps to move everything easier, if you don't know how its the little folder in the layers window). Create a new canvas as big as you want. try 400*230px and make it transparent. move your set into that new canvas, and arrange it so it lines up with the canvas. Add your render do what ever effects and then do your colouring. Once its complete save as .png and click interlaced. That'll save it with transparency. Not that hard :lol:
