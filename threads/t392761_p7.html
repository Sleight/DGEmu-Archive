Harvest Moon! As much as I enjoy Pokémon, Harvest Moon has more that appeals to me. Pokémon games are fun when you're new to them; however having played since the release of Yellow, I'm getting tired of the same old mechanics and gameplay. To me, it plays like a very basic RPG. Four moves; an item option; a change Pokémon option, and a flee option. The gameplay doesn't really go beyond that. The game consists of you the player trudging/cycling/flying around different continents, fighting everyone and everything you see. Aside from that, there's just the exploration. Perhaps it's because I'm growing up, but all the different Pokémon continents feel like the same place re-mapped to look a little different. One other nit-pick you could say I'd have due to my age, is that recent generations of Pokémon got more generic and silly after the Silver/Gold generation (Rotom? Really?). It's cleverly disguised to hide the fact that you're basically just running around battling everyone, but it's an easy guise to see through when the nostalgia of the game wears off.Harvest Moon is an amazing series with many more elements to it than Pokémon (especially if you consider the Rune Factory spin-off). The characters are more interesting, the plot has more depth and the gameplay mechanics are more varied (farming, fighting, marrying, exploring, trading, customising, creating etc.). There's more to it! In retrospect, I remember preferring Pokémon to Harvest Moon about 4 years ago. Maybe it's got to do with growing up?
