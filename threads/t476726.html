It is originally a brand of "lovable, huggable" soft toys in the 1980s but it became an animated cartoon by Hanna-Barbera Productions. Talking of which, I have seen the cartoons back in that decade (there was also a movie entitled Pound Puppies: The Legend of Big Paw released after the original cartoons were shown) and they were great in its time.

A few months ago, I watched the new version of the show that was broadcast on Boomerang but it was completely different to the 80s version and it was totally awesome! The dogs often plan different techniques on giving away different puppies to their owners.

About the characters from the show, I liked Strudel and Rebound because one of those mentioned dogs (Strudel) was intelligent whilst the other (Rebound) was extremely playful. Listening to Niblet's voice reminds me of the Futurama character named Bender. Lucky and Squirt are OK but Cookie looks quite tough. Another fact about the characters are two of them (ie. Strudel and Cookie) are named after food items and a small dog who is Niblet's sister (ie. Rebound) is named after a basketball term describing a capture of the ball after a failed shot attempt in the US sport.

Which characters from the new version do you guys like best and what episodes (also from the new version) have you seen?
