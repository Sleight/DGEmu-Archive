There's not going to be a fourth one, that's for sure: Michael Bay and Shia LaBouef(sp?) have left, so without them these movies won't be going any longer than a trilogy. But there's still a lot of money to be made from this franchise, so we'll probably get a reboot eventually.In that case, I guess there technically WILL be a fourth one. Huh.Anyway, speaking as a pretty avid TF fan, I liked the movie, but it had what I like to call a very high "Fanwank Factor": the Decepticon "big four" (Megatron, Starscream, Soundwave, and Shockwave) are in it, the Wreckers are in it, so on and so forth. There's even cameos by minor transformers from the previous movies in the background during the big final battle. As a TF fan I couldn't help but like it, really, even though I'll tell you upfront that it really isn't a very good movie. So I guess if you're a Transformers (or even if you just like robots blowing stuff up), you'll enjoy it. If not, you probably want to skip it. It's more of the same, really, only not quite as mind-numbingly stupid as the second one and somewhat more eventful than the first one.
