A month 'til release!
You can find compiled info on gen6 at Serebii: http://serebii.net/xy/
Couple of new features so far that look interesting...


Mega Evolutions -
-temporary in-battle evolutions enabled via held item
-gives base stat boost, new ability, and sometimes new typing
-option to evolve is manually chosen in battle and can be used the same turn as a move
-mega evolution can only be done once per battle, doesn't seem like it will revert until battle ends

Interesting concept since the mega is not revealed until you evolve it, there are various potential uses to that such as faking a lower speed tier or evolving something to deal with its counters. Some of the megas seem even to be designed for this, such as Venusaur who gets Thick Fat and loses its two main weakness (at the cost of Chloropyll) and Ampharos who gets Electric/Dragon typage. Also a mechanic that makes old 3rd evolutions possibly viable again.


Choose 2 starters - you get one from gen6 and one from gen1. For nostalgia's sake I guess...also all the old starters seem to have gotten mega forms.

Fairy type - Super effective against Dragon, for balancing purposes, like Dark was made to balance Psychic in gen2. Still no info on most of its other type matchups...there are a bunch of older pokemon getting the conversion to Fairy type though, it'll pobably turn out to be a recovery focused type with a couple of strong attacks to anti-Dragon.

Super Training - touchscreen minigame that raises pokemon's EVs. Also has a visual graph that shows the EV spread. Sounds like an awesome features that lets you EV train while playing the game normally and not have to worry about stray points. Also Gamefreak demonstrating they are willing to give some insight to some of the 'hidden' mechanics, maybe at some point in the future there'll be an in-game way to check IVs and maybe even change them...one can dream

Bank - apparently to be released in December, with a 1 month free trial. If there are no alternate methods to transfer pokemon, it could mean that no one has access to previous gens or perfect pokemon for at least 2 months...
