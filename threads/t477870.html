KKirie MotobaSep 26 2012, 02:39 PM
You mean your TV seems to be reacting faster than your monitor to the output?

It's usually a case of response time between the monitor and TV.
Most cases though, the monitor response time is faster than a TV, depending on which kind.

For example, I have 3 Asus screens at home, they're all rated at 5ms response time. Although they might not all necessarily refresh at the same time while booting up, inside Windows, everything is fine.

However, if you plugged a screen with a 2ms response time vs a 5ms, or 8ms, you will notice that one seems to receive the input a lot slower than the other.
