Ughhh... OK, I'll try to explain it. First, find the picture you want. If it's online, right click it, and click "Copy". Then go to Photoshop, and go to File>New. Click OK without making any changes. Go to Edit>Paste. This will put your image in its own file. Remove the bg (if you wish). Open up your background file. Go to the file with your picture, grab the Move tool, and 'drag' the picture onto the background file. You can just PM me if you have anymore questions XD
