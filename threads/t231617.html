Most of you guys seem to be missing one critical point about deducting points: there's no net loss of GPs when you get points deducted for a duplicate topic. All the deduction says is that the topic already exists and that you should go post in the other one instead.Allowing duplicate topics isn't going to make things easier. What's the point of starting a new topic when there's another one covering the exact subject and has several hundred replies already? And the problem wasn't with too many people using the search - it was with too many people not knowing how to use it correctly and running searches that returned thousands of results. DM has already limited the search results, so that's not an issue anymore.But certainly, stop posting "Search next time" and use something like "Read the pinned topics" (which tends to be the case) or just link to the correct thread without telling the topic starter to search.Topics certainly should be judged on quality, although duplicate topics almost invariably are of low quality to begin with. I always go around closing random topics that really don't lead anywhere, especially with all those random "favorite" topics. If a good discussion is formed in a duplicate topic for some reason or another, just merge it with the original.
