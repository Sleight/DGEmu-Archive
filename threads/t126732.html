ZZennalathasJun 12 2006, 04:04 PM
I don't think you can say that Final Fantasy isn't a role playing game. It's fairer to say that the RPG genre has spawned out several sub-genres. I agree, RPG's should allow you to make key decisions (At the least, if not allow specific character development found in Elder Scrolls or Fable) but this is not necessarily the definition of the genre. In Final Fantasy, you do play the role of the character and complete specific quests tailored to that character's life. You play his/her role. So, where Fable is much more immersive, FF results in a movie feel and separates you from the game. This detachment from the game lends the genre a stale game play that's been copied far too many times. FFX tried to steer away from this with it's character trees, but still, the characters are what the story requires them to be, instead of the story being what the characters require. It's a different view on gaming, and sadly a very re-hashed, over-down take on things. The true RPG would be Fable, where FF must fall into the movie/cinematic sub-genre. In this respect, Fable brought more innovation to the table than any FF game (excluding, of course, FFI and Tactics), and therefore is a superior game. Final Fantasy is a dead series; there's nothing left for them to milk. Square Enix needs a new title, and a new idea.
