Right now I am reading four books at once. Well, not literally... They are:

 

The Magic Mountain by Thomas Mann

Treatise of Human Nature by David Hume

The American Language by H.L. Mencken

Dubliners by James Joyce

 

I would totally recommend The Magic Mountain. The original text is German, but the translators did a wonderful job on my edition.

 

Treatise of Human Nature is pretty good. Hume states that Causes do not necessarily cause certain effects and goes on to prove it with arguments that are pretty good.

 

The American Language is really interesting.

 

Dubliners is okay, I would probably give it a 4/5. Joyce is really good with words.
