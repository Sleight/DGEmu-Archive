Kula Diamond' timestamp='1332395977' post='5002759 wrote:
I thought it would be fun to fly one of those, so I bought one that I think looks exactly like the smallest one in that picture. It was fun for like a few minutes, most of which were used to learn how to fly the thing(It actually helped that I like tank control Resi games). I imagine the bigger ones are a lot more fun.
The tiny pink and silver heli? That one is really nothing more than a toy anyone could fly, but it help did with learning the sticks. It only has 3 channels: throttle (up and down), yaw/rudder (controlled by adjusting the speed of the counter-rotating main blades rather than a tail fan), and forward and backward (elevator, controlled by a fan on the tail rather than a swashplate and main blades). Like I said, anyone could fly that thing, it'll hover on spot with absolutely no user input. The giant black and red heli is just a bigger version of the same thing, but it's large enough to handle a little wind outdoors.Small black heli is actually a proper RC model. Has 4 channels (throttle, rudder, elevator and aileron), directional movement is done by tilting the main blades with the use of a swashplate, and yaw is controlled by a tail fan which gives much quicker response. Having only one set of main blades it isn't as stable and takes a lot of small inputs to keep it in place, but it's a ton more fun and man it can move.Yellow heli is a step up again, 6 channels (extra channels are for collective pitch control), rather than having a fixed pitch blade (stuck at one angle) these can change angle while flying which is how it generates lift. Rather than increasing the throttle to climb the throttle (in stunt mode) is constantly at near full speed, and to go up or down you change the angle of the blades - this generally gives you more stable flight and is less taxing on the gyro as the torque is fairly constant so its not having to keep the tail in line all the time. You can actually angle them the other way and fly upside down as well, which is cool, but I'm not very good at it.The planes are a much more recent edition that I'm still learning. Muuuuuch easier than the helis but scary to learn because they need to be moving fast to stay in the air. The black white and red one will easily do 60kph at full throttle so it gets out of view quickly.
