RRickstilwellORIGINAL POSTFeb 23 2006, 07:18 AM
I just found out that if you have an iPod you can charge it on your PS2. This is convenient if you go somewhere that does not have a computer with usb ports but you have your Playstation 2. Just connect the link cable that comes with your iPod into your iPod unit and plug the usb connection into either of the usb ports on your playstation 2. The charging symbol will show up on your iPod and you can just play your games as it charges.
