KKrool_KingJun 17 2011, 04:40 PM
I find the best way to study is as follows:First, the most important thing you can do is pay attention during classes and try to follow the thought-chain the teacher is following when explaining the things to you. Always check yourself: Did I understand what this is about? How did he get from here to here? Where does this fit in with what we did last time? And if you don't know the answer, just ask.Second, post-prepare. This means that, as soon as you get home, ask yourself what you learned that day and reconstruct it in your mind. That way, when you're actually studying, it's going to be much easier to go through the subject matter systematically.Third, try to learn with a friend or two. If one of you doesn't get it then someone else in the group who did will explain it.Don't leave your Maths-notes to rot until just before a test and don't start learning for, say, History, by memorising as many events as possible. For things to be well assimilated, they need time, and that means you have to be in a state of constant learning.Last but not least, don't forget to relax. Separate your study-time from your leisure-time rigurously and don't ever study half-assedly (it's really better to put off studying if you don't feel like it).That was my high-school experience talking, and it's even more invaluable advice for college later on.
