Is this the greatest racing game ever? Not by a long shot. Is it realistic? No. Is it frustrating at times? Yes. Despite all of this is it still fun? Yes it is.
What we have here is a game where the only cars you can drive have the name Ford on them. You might be thinking that this would mean you have a very narrow range of vehicles to drive. Amazingly enough you are wrong. There are 55 vehicles in the game and most of them you have to unlock in order to use. Here is a lovely list of all the vehicles you can drive with links to what their real world version look like.

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Racing_3

And here are some screen shots from the game.

http://www.gamefaqs.com/computer/doswin/image/921160.html

Like I said. This isn't an A list game but their is certainly some fun to be had if you don't have too high of expectations.
