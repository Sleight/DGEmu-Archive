Quick summary to note, for those that didn't watch the whole thing:

- 3DS version will be coming out in summer 2014, WiiU version will come in winter 2014.

- Online mode will have more features, quite long to explain.

Renge Miyauchi wrote on Apr 09 2014, 02:03 PM:

- Online mode will have more features, quite long to explain.

 

Not really, too long, but I will explain this.

 

-Online will have two modes For Glory (Ranked) and For Fun (Unranked, but wins are tallied).

-FG mode will be No Items on Final Destination stage only, but the stage skin can be changed to look like other stages.

-FF will have all other stages and all items on always.

-An option to play with friends is available, and you can customize rules however you like with them.

 

-Lastly, a Metriodvania-esque mode called Smash Run will be added to the 3DS version.

--You and other players play on the map defeating enemies to collect boost for your characters (speed, power, defense, etc.)

--Once 5 minutes have passed, you will face all the other players on the map in an all out brawl with the boost applied.

--It is not known whether or not this is online, local, or both.
