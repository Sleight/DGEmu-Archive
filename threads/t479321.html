Hello there!

I would like to listen to your opinion about the idea of a gaming browser (for casual gamers), which is a project that me and my team are currently working at.

 

Our main idea was to create a PC/Windows application from where one can access a collection of online games (mostly flash). We though of it as a more compact thing than a website, and maybe even more fun. We also thought on adding an inner social network for members to communicate with each other while playing.

 

It all started a little more than a year ago, and now we have this Joyvy app, which has a games' catalog, as well as a social component. It is installed on a user's PC, and requires neither additional downloads, nor a browser for playing any games. As for the games, we get them from public feeds (but select them manually). There are also a few developers we are collaborating with at the moment.
