DDragons MasterORIGINAL POSTJul 14 2010, 09:11 PM
Hello Members and Guests,Following the downloads improvements we've recently added and a bunch of staff conversation on the subject, we've decided to alter the download points concept a little bit, making it a lot more user-friendly, and way more user-benefiting:Only 2 Download Points TypesWe used to have a billion different currencies - One for system genre (Atari, Retro, Nintendo consoles, Nintendo handhelds, etc..) and another global one that reset everyday to 25 - we've merged all the system genres ones into one DP currency - Static DPs.So we now have 2 Download Points Currencies: - Global DPs - Reset everyday to 25 - Static DPs - Never reset, can be gained by either converting gps to dps or donating.Oh yeah - please comment with good (great) new names for these DPs currencies - Global DPs is kinda meaningless now because Static DPs are also global (can be used to download all the games on the site).Global DPs used first..Downloads now use your Global DPs first and only when you're out of it, your Static DPs will be used - that is how things should happen, that saves DPs to people who use not only the global ones (donators, converters, etc..) and makes a whole lot more sense than what was going on until now.Cheers, - Ben aka Dragons Master
