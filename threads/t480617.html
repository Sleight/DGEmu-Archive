KKenAug 31 2014, 03:12 PM

I've no clue, this plays kinda like MMZ but not quite because you don't shoot things dead, you shoot them (which does minimal damage) and then you zap them dead, and that makes all the difference in how you approach certain groups of enemies and especially boss battles, you have to manage your electricity wisely because it's used to repel enemy attacks, double jump and zap them (and use prevasion which is dodging attacks while standing still). There's no special weapons to be gained though you get skills limited in their use and different guns.

 

The platforming is solid because you use the electricity to fall slowly, move platforms, repel certain projectile attacks, illuminate dark areas, jump higher in certain zones, destroy certain objects or platforms...

 

It's up to you I guess. I think 15 USD is an alright price since you can replay stages like in Kid Icarus: Uprising to get better scores.

 

Btw, True Ending requirements which you can fulfill only after you've finished the game once:

Don't read unless you've finished the game once...Get the 7 jewels (one hidden in each boss stage + Copen's) and give them to Joule, then wear the necklace she makes through the whole last stage (equip it before entering the stage). Have fun against Nova without prevasion!
