If anyone is in the market for a GPU and doesn't want to spend a lot of money... go for the HD 4870 1GB which has been way down in price since the release of the other cards. It can be had for as low as $130-150. It's definitely the best bang for the buck card currently since it handles both lower resolutions and higher resolutions with ease. Otherwise I'd say get a HD 4830 at a $60-80 price point or a HD 4850 at a $100 price point. Obviously this was all from a gaming perspective.If it's HTPC needs that you need a card for... get the HD 4670. It is hands down THE best discrete video card to buy for an HTPC gauged computer. It has great performance on 1080p playback... but it also handles SD materials with near perfection also (something that you wouldn't think was hard to do... but really is). The card also sports 7.1 LCPM audio over HDMI along with the video signal.If you're just doing a basic run of the mill web browsing/processing computer build... the GA-MA74GM-S2 is the motherboard to get. It has decent onboard video (HD3100 I believe), runs cool, very energy efficient, and will get the basic video processing done. The reason I suggest this motherboard is because it is only $50-55 and is perfectly capable of modern CPU's and 4GB's of ram with plenty of other features let alone the video portion.
