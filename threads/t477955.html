A quick thing you can always do next time it happens:
Shut your system down. Remove battery and unplug it from wall. Press and hold the power button for about 10 seconds to make sure you drain it from any charge.
Put back the battery, turn your laptop back on with battery only.

Also, I would recommend looking for a possible bios flash. It might sometimes help resolve hardware issues. Make sure that you're plugged in with a fully charged battery though: a screw up flashing your bios = your laptop is screwed.
