You mean you can compile a custom rom yourself and flash over ADB, or you're just going to unlucky unlock the bootloader, root, install custom recovery, and flash someone else's zip? Because there's not a lot of risk if it is the latter and the rom has been tested over on XDA.

Plus, as long as you can get into download mode or custom recovery, you're good. It's super hard to hard brick an android device. You'd have to manage to get locked out of Download Mode, which is nearly impossible.

If this thing can already sideload everything you want, there's no point in rooting, Unlocking, and flashing though. That's not really going to help game performance. Flashing is more for helping memory management for multitasking and UI stuttering. Plus the features that come with custom roms, which would be almost universally pointless on that stick. Unless there's something you can't install (like Plex) on the stock firmware, it's not going to be worth the trouble.
