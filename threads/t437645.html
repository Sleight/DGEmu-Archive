I don't see why you'd want to own 2 PS3s.While you can do a lot with the PS3, most of the time, it involves media sharing. For example, one PS3 as the server, and the other as the media centre. So having 1 PS3 as the media centre is already great, with your PC streaming media to your living room.As far as I know, there are no additional functionalities that appear when you have two PS3s. On top of that, all DLCs will be specific to your PSN ID, which means if you want to play the same stuff, you'll have to download twice.Furthermore, if you wish, you can partition your HDD on your PS3, and install Linux. However, it is still easier to have your PC partition the HDD and install Linux, than your PS3.There are, however, some interesting functions when you also own a PSP. For example, if you own a PSP Resistance game, and Resistance 2 on the PS3, you can "infect" your machines, giving you additional features. You can also stream any media on your PS3 to your PSP, provided that you have WiFi access for your PSP. To reiterate: No to 2 PS3s, but yes to PSP + PS3.
