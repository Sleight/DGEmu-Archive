The answer to this question is obviously Articuno. While it is weak to both Fire and Electric attacks, it's a spc. def. tank and can withstand enough Thunders and Fire Blasts to be able to unleash its deadly Mind Reader/Sheer Cold combo. It's greatest weakness is Rock attacks, since they do 4 times normal damage but that's the same as Moltres. While this seems like it gives Zapdos an advantage, most Pokémon that learn Rock attacks naturally have a part Ground type, which Ice attacks are super-effective against and Electric attacks are useless against. Of course, there are always exceptions, but for most cases, Articuno is the best.Apparently there's a misconception here that Ice is super-effective against Rock and the truth is the exact opposite. The only reason Ice attacks are super-effective against Pokémon like Golem and Rhydon is because they are part Ground type.
