MMo­lh.Jul 28 2012, 08:43 AM
Platinum was followed by Pokemon Black/White and its sequel, Pokemon Black 2 / White 2, which is currently only available in Japanese (though a great translation patch can be found here). This "Generation V" features totally new pokemon, and a totally new region (Unova). In B/W only these new pokemon were available; in B2/W2 pokemon from other generations are available too. Personally I quite enjoyed playing them! You can read more, including details about storyline and gameplay changes, right here: http://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_V

Besides that, Pokemon Conquest was also released. It's an interesting cross between Pokemon, and the Nobunaga's Ambition series of turn-based strategy games. I don't know much about it, having not tried it yet, but you can apparently learn more about it here: http://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Conquest

Hope that's what you were looking for! :)
