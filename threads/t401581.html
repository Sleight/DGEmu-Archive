This thread will compile a bunch of good tools to help you do various things on your Wii, in other areas besides Virtual Console and WiiWare.Wii Parental Control Password ResetterForget your code? Get a second hand Wii? Well, use this to reset the parental control password.http://wii.marcansoft.com/parental.pspDirections:Go into the Parental controls itself, then Click I forgot on the Pin entry page, and I forgot on the Secret Answer entry page. Your 8 digit confirmation code will be on the next page. (The one that tells you to call Nintendo.) Enter this code into the link and choose the date based upon your time zone.
