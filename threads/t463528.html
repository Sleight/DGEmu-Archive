CCorlelSep 11 2011, 01:21 AM
Post-plot (the main one, that is) is when you unlock the ability to evolve your pokemon. Some will require stones or other items to evolve, just like in the main games. For instance, Onix would require a metal coat in the inventory before going into the cave to evolve to Steelix. These items can be found in various dungeons, often in Kecleon shops. The cave will mention that you cannot have a partner with you upon entering. This problem is solved at the get-go, as your main partner from the beginning will go to its Friend Area.
