Well, the RPGs I really like are Xenosaga (one and two) and Shadow Hearts: Covenant. Xenosaga has a lot of cinema cutscenes but great sound and a very extensive plot that takes place thousands of years in the future. You battle versus Gnosis, an alien lifeform, and corrupt governments and secret organizations. It is turn based battle system but with very unique level up system where you gain points after each battle where you can use them to increase stats, Tech attacks, and Ether (like magic) spells.Shadow Hearts has a more conventional level up system but is really cool because it takes place between WW1 and WW2 and there are a lot of historical element interwoven in it. The battle system is one of a kind using a Ring system that is based on good timing to increase effectiveness of attacks and magic.Both are very long extensive games. I would check them out.
