MMORPGs (Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Game) have worlds that tend to keep on evolving and changing while the player is offline and not playing. An MMORPG is a subset of an MMOG (Massively Multiplayer Online Game). In other words GTA4 can be both an MMORPG and a MMOG. In addition, since it's only mentioned as a possible MMOG there's the possibility it might drift into the realm of MMOFPS (Massively Multiplayer Online First Person Shooter).Almost forgot: It's coming out for the 360 at the same time October 16, 2007. And the 360 version might have exclsive material the PS3 won't have, since the material might depend on the XBox Live.Everquest is the inspiration for the online model of GTA4. In addition, the setting (the city) might actually be based on a city in a foreign country. Previous settings were Liberty City (New York City), Vice City (Miami), Los Santos (Los Angeles), San Fierro (San Francisco), and Las Venturas (Las Vegas).
