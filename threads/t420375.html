There is a way to get mew on yellow without having to go to a Nintendo event. I'll give you a step by step tutorial on how to do it. Please note that this is a glitch, so it may screw up your game, and don't blame me for it.1. Make sure you did not fight the gambler facing the house between Lavender Town and Saffron City and the 2nd youngester past nugget bridge to the left, who is facing upwards and has a slowpoke. Also, make sure you have a pokemon that can fly. Start in the entrance to the underground path to Lavendar town in Celadon City.2. Make sure to save in front of the nearest entrance around the gambler and do not save again because it may undo the glitch.3. Step in front of the gambler(One step forward from the underground entrance) and immediately press start and the menu should come up before he notices you.4. DON'T exit the menu when you do step 5, instead go to the pokemon that I told you you should have for flying anf fly to Cerulean city.5. The gambler should notice you but you will have flown away, but your A and B = your start buttons shouldn't work.6. Beat the youngester I mentioned in Step 1, and your A and B buttons should work againalong with your start key.7. Fly back to Lavendar town and start walking towards Saffron City. 8. Along the way (Route 8), your menu should randomly pop up, hit B button to exit and walla! You should immediately afterwards fight a lvl. 7 Mew.That is how you catch a Mew in yellow version!
