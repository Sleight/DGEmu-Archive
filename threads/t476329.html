Leaked snapshot from BlockBuster’s internal systems have revealed the upcoming games on the Wii U console. There’s no way to verifying the veracity of these leaks, so do take them with a pinch of salt.

Aliens Colonia Marines
Assassin’s Creed
Batman: Arkham City
Darksiders II

And then there’s Splinter Cell 6 and Ninja Gaiden 3, which is already on pre-order online.

Now that’s quite a list to reckon with. The reaction from the gaming community however has been somewhat less than enthusiastic. But that is probably because there are no big surprises on this list. All of these titles were already expected to turn up on Wii U. Especially Just Dance 4, given its history on the console.

However, gamers cannot deny being excited about having games like Batman, Darksiders and Assassin’s Creed to work with on the upcoming console. Especially with the completely redesigned console that is going to be the Wii U. Nintendo is expected to show off a lot at the upcoming E3 gaming convention but we have some more info to share before that happens, thanks to some of the patents that have been granted to the company.

The patents that were filed by the company last summer have finally been published with one full month to go before the E3 convention. This gives us a chance for closer examination of the features that are known to be and could be on the new console.
There’s mention of a 5inch screen on the patents, which has since been changed. The LCD display on the controller is now 6.2inches officially. The idea of using existing Wii Remotes with the handheld touchscreen controller has already been demonstrated at E3 last year.

The older remotes will be smoothly fused in to the new set up, if the drawings are anything to go by. So those who have invested in the existing Wii, will have the option of combining the new and the old during their gaming sessions.

The controller will of course be motion sensitive and will come loaded with a gyroscope, accelerometer and also a camera. Over all, it takes advantage of all the latest innovations in mobile technology and mobile gaming and puts them in the hands of Wii U owners. The second screen on the console will add to the over all experience of the game by proving secondary information like maps and it will also act as a HUD in certain situations. If Nintendo can pull this off properly, gamers are bound to love this new concept.
