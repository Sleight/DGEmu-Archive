AalethiometerJul 06 2011, 11:42 PM
Overally, they are not too bad considering they are still amongst your first attempts. Each has it's strengths and weaknesses, but I'd say your best is the third sig, with the penguin. The placement of the render is good, as are the blending effects and overall colour scheme. Unfortunately, I'd say the text on this one lets it down a little bit, and the background feels a little bit empty. I feel by utilising your brushes a bit better you could vastly improve this sig. Try creating some sort of flow between the brushes and the render, especially in this case considering how active the render looks. Finally, this sense of direction could be reinforced by cleverly positioning the focal point of the light.Other than that I'd say your second sig and the sixth sig were the next best. With the sixth sig again, is feels a little empty considering how busy the render itself looks - plus, the render is a little pixelated at the edges and appears to still have a white background left on it around the edges. The text on this sig is better, as it is blended a bit better.Overall, I'd say your sigs do appear to be improving - your text looks better, the backgrounds seem better, and right from the start your colour schemes have been bang on. Stick at it, and perhaps investigate some tutorials on blending your renders as well as your text.
