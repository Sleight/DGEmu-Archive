KKula DiamondNov 08 2011, 03:42 PM
It's hard at first, but you could get used to it pretty fast. It's even much easier to learn high level play here than CoD. Also, lvl 50 has as much health as you do, his weapons are far better though. Don't waste time with aiming, best way to fight at low level is by running and shooting(blind fire). Avoid encounters in wide open areas, always run in closed places so most of your gun fights will be at close range(more like fistycuffs range). Your choice of weapon at the start should be the pistol. when you see someone start running toward him but don't start shooting until you're in a range where your blind fire will hit him easily, for the pistol that's about 3 or 4 steps away from the target(you will get a better feel for this after a few tries). Now, when you're the right range shoot four our five bullets then melee him. This is the easiest way to get kills at low levels. Keep doing that, shouldn't take long before you can buy the Blind Fire Accuracy improvement for your pistol. Which should be enough until you unlock M4. (the Uzi is pretty nice for blind firing too but it's more risky than the pistol). As for your boosters, start with Power Hunter, it will allow you see the location of power weapons, which essential since they're much better than anything you can start with even at max level, the shotgun is the best weapon in the game for blind firing, grenade launcher and RPG are pretty lethal too. One last tip, though not as important. Learn to use your grenades, when someone suprises you at close range and you know you're going to die roll and drop a grenade while doing so, you will be amazed how many kills doing so will earn you.Also this is how to blind fire with the shotgun:http://www.youtube.com/watch?NR=1&v=kFRgt6p_Cr4
