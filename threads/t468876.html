Hey mate. Welcome to the site I suppose. I'm going to be honest from the outset and say that although I've been a member for a very long time, I haven't been an active, contributing member. With that in mind, I think it wouldn't be a far stretch to say - like with anything - that you'll only get out what you put in. I tend to use the site to find out information about ROM's but usually just to download them but I think you may have already deduced that from my low post count. I have other interest areas and I don't often game, so when I do I come here to read up on what active members are saying and download the ROM's.The site is really good and from what I've observed there's a great culture especially with the involvement of the staff and the members and there's always hundreds of people logged on reading and occasionally contributing. The ROM's are the best quality you can find and I think I am in a position to attest to that.The site is kind of a one-stop-shop for everything as well.
