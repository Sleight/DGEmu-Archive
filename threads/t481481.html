Firstly, in order to "do horribly" enough so that the GRift only advances by 1 rank, you have to finish the rift within the last 2:30.  So you clear up to the Rift Guardian, go back to town and do bounties or w/e, then go back and finish the rift at 12:30.  Personally I find that the 'low-level Grift' trick is not that lucrative though, the legendary/hour drop rate might be higher but it also scales with GRift level, and clearing a bunch of low-level GRifts while wasting time to keep it advancing slowly isn't necessarily more efficient than just doing T1 Rifts.  Repeatedly doing higher GRifts (30+) is great for drops but that requires that you can already easily farm T6 Rifts, much more endgame stuff.   

 

As for getting more GRift keys...its random.  Higher chance to get them for clearing rifts at higher Torment levels, 100% drop at T6
