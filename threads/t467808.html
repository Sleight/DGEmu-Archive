A Single Moment Of Sincerity. Even as I see you... knowing what may come, placed upon your stairs- stalling with my laces blue, hearing your music- as my heart beats to the drum, anxiously I'm walking until past your door I see, you're perfectly holding, a picture of me. Oh such an angel sitting on the bed, sad feelings race my head, and everything's been said, I assume- we're done, because of my shameless stupid fun, tears burn my eye, then you look up and start to cry, Dear Christ I beg of you please just let me die, the silence is broken- as I hear your faint "sigh", what can I give, to make this right. This is not how it was meant to be! I still want to make it work- why do I have to be such a **** jerk! your hands... they seem very cold- as you I gently hold, brushing back your hair, from your hazel eyes, sincerely they look of hurt- and have never told lies. Oh how I'm sorry. how I am ashamed, I still deeply love you, honest it's true, I carefully... lean over and give to you my kiss, and that's when my heart stops! that's when I know pain! as I glance at your wrists and the white carpet blood stain! Now! I am sorry, Now! I feel guilt! Now! I'm ashamed at the monster I became, though my love for you will always remain the same. This is the part where I truly die, as with your faint voice, you reply "Listen we had a good try, but now it's goodbye, these oceans are far between our hearts, and bleeding has got too hard" and that was the painful end, as you dropped the broken glass shard. These tears blind my eyes, as I gasp for air between the cries, filled with hate by my lies, and without fair goodbyes. I regret now for not gazing in your hazel eyes- and saying an honest farewell, just like this, before I go to hell "I truly love you I want you to know that, I'm so stupid please forgive me. my heart betrays me for your last first kiss goodbye, take care -Aurevoir- my Love." Later as I cleanse my eyes for clarity- I remember, A Single Moment Of Sincerity.
