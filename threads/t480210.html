Rule of Thumb is that OLDER consoles do NOT play newer console games ..... the only exceptions are those consoles that are not really a step up but a to the side, like the Gameboy Advance SP = Gameboy Advance, 2DS = 3DS, etc.

 

eg.

XBox does not play XBox 360 games

XBox 360 does not play XBox One games

GBA does not play NDS games

NDS does not play 3DS games
