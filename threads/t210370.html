HhackerxFeb 17 2009, 08:28 AM
If you don't like the sound then mute it. Bring up the home menu and point at the bottom for the controller settings.Yes out of necessity it is a small speaker but that isn't the only reason for the sound quality. The other catch is limited bluetooth bandwidth. Seriously think about all the data going back and forth between the controller and the system. Positional data from an IR camera tracking multiple IR points, accelerometer data reporting multiple axes many times per second, control signals for lights and motors, data being sent back and forth between the system and the flash memory in the controller, and depending on what is plugged into it either a lot of buttons or more accelerometer data. All of that in addition to the audio being sent to the controller. I'm not sure what bluetooth revision it is for certain but based on the development cycle of the system and the release date the highest revision of the bluetooth protocol they can be using in these things is 2.0 and it very well may be 1.2 or 1.1 which means at best the total data throughput is a theoretical 2.1MBps and it could be well below 721kbps. The fact that they can transmit all that data with a connection with that low of a throughput is amazing in and of itself. But if their is interference causing a drop in bandwidth for the connection guess which function is the least critical to gameplay and thus the first to have data packets dropped to make sure the rest of the data isn't interrupted? If you guess the audio you are correct. Most people have absolutely no appreciation for just how amazing of an engineering feat the Wii controller is.
