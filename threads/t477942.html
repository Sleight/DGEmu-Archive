Synching your Pokemon game to the Pokemon Global Link
- Make sure that your wireless is enabled.
- After passing the title screen, choose "Game Syn Settings" in the main menu. This will connect you to Nintendo WFC.
- Once it prompts you to create data to play Game Sync, choose Yes. Wait a few moments until the data creation is complete.
- Once you receive your Game Sync ID, go to Pokemon Global Link on your computer: http://en.pokemon-gl.com/
- Login to your account, and then click on "Add Game Card"
- Enter your Game Sync ID into the box and click on "Register"
- Accept the registration and you're done!
Note: If you registered more than 1 game, you must "swtich your game card" with the proper game version in order to get the promotions.
