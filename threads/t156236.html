EelektrykeyeSep 13 2006, 10:58 PM
Nintendogs is probably not a good choice for him. It requires speaking to train the dogs. Not all deaf people speak. If he is verbal and has the training to pronounce words (even if they're not pronounced the same as you and I, they need to be distinguishable in some way so that the game doesn't think he's saying the same word) then he might be able to enjoy Nintendogs. In general though, I would not recommend the game to my deaf friends for that reason.If he enjoys Super Mario Bros, then he will probably enjoy most of the side-scroller platformers like Megaman, Super Princess Peach, Sonic Rush or even Castlevania (I played a lot of that without paying attention to the story at all). I also recommend Mario Kart and Tetris because they are very easy to pick-up-and-play. If he likes golf, True Swing Golf is a really great game.
