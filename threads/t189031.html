MMr.WitecatDec 06 2006, 05:11 AM
Your best bets for mass zobie removal in Dead Rising are any long weapons (IE: Potted Plants, Baseball Bats, Lead Pipes, Park benches). Pipes and Bats can be stored, but the other two must be dropped if you switch items. If you are going the fist approach, wait until you level up enough to get the football tackle, which allows you to bounce off zombies left and right if you time the attack right. And if you really want to level up fast, save civilians! Save as many people as you can and you get a nice experience boost from it. Even if they bite the dust on the way to the security room, you still get points just by having them join you.
