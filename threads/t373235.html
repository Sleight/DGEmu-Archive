I just finished Super Mario Galaxy yesterday. (thats 120/120 stars) Honestly, I'd toss it in the 8 to 8.5 range. It's definitely a solid title, but far from a perfect ten. It is grossly flawed in many ways. Foremost among these is the difficulty curve. There are probably about three legitimately challenging stars in the entire game. Just as you start to reach stars you might actually die once or twice tryng to get, the game is already over. Even in the final few galaxies, there are still stars they practically throw in your face. It didn't feel like the classic platforming of Mario 64, where death actually mattered and they didn't throw fifty free lives at you every time you managed to walk five feet without tripping over yourself. It felt like charity. The spherical world concept was somewhat novel, although neither revolutionary nor entirely original, and for that I give it some credit, but it was wholly under-utilized. There were some mechanics I felt would be a given that were conspicuously missing. The controls and core gameplay are as solid as any Mario game to date, but the game is a bit more shallow than I'd prefer.
