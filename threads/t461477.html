There isn't really a way to go back to it immediately. But, unless you go to some random dungeon ONLY to train, it's best to stay near to the start of it to train, or go to the end or another teleporter. This way you'll surely get enough time and EXP to train them all, also... I used this method to train my digimon: when I was defeated by a certain digimon (because I was advancing in the game too fast and not training them enough) I found out a realy interesting thing. I just went back to the start and ran trough each and every dungeon I have ever been to and opened every chest. This way I got many different items, as well as my digimon got leveled pretty much. If you have about 3-4 dungeons to run trough and REALY stay patient, believe me, you can gain from 5-10 levels per digimon. ;)
