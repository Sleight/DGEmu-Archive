theres an entire list of brush sites in the dream thread. My recommendations are sigsource.tk and deviantart.com/For deviantart go to http://www.deviantart.comthen click browse. You will see a drop down menu. Click it and find resources, a second one should appear. Select Photoshop brushes. Make the settings, Top Favourite, 24, and well I forgot the other one but yeah it should give you a screen with a whole heap of previews. Choose some, download them, unzip them if you have to. Now to use them close photoshop, then go to my computer>>>C:/>>>Program Files>>>Adobe>>>Photoshop 7.0>>>Presets>>>Brushesplace them in the last folder. Start up photoshop, create your canvas, fill it with clouds or black or what ever and press B, right click in the canvas and then click the arrow in the top right hand corner, select display large thumbnails or what ever and click what ever brush set you want. It'll prompt you and you should choose replace or what ever it says.
