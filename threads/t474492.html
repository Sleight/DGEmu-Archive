Overview:pSX Emulator, also known as psxfin or simply pSX, is one of the most easiest to use Playstation 1 emulator.The emulator has a high compatibility and is very user friendly.This guide will help you setup the basics in order to play games. What you will need:- pSX emulator: http://psxemulator.gazaxian.com/- PS1 Starter Kit (contains bios and blank memory card): *To be uploaded*- Microsoft DirectX Runtime: http://www.microsoft.com/download/en/details.aspx?displaylang=en&id=35- 7-zip: http://www.7-zip.org/ Game formats:Playstation games are distributed under different file formats.
