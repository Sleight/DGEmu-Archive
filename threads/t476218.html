Folders and what goes in:
ISOs and CSOs
H:\ISO\game1.cso
H:\ISO\game2.iso

For bought games from the PSN:
H:\PSP\GAME\PSNgame1\eboot.pbp

For homebrew, Read the instructions. There's usually a bit more to it than just copy-paste. Although They're usually in
H:\PSP\GAME\Homebrew\eboot.php

Certain older homebrew need to go in a GAME150 folder ( On my 3k with 5.03 anyway )
Note: My PSP is H when connected to my PC. Substitute that with whatever letter yours is.
