SShidou MariyaOct 06 2010, 05:53 AM
You should put a modified version of my sig. That would be nice.In all seriousness though, there might be something awkward going on.First step, do the uninstall, and by luck, try plugging it to a different USB port.If it still doesn't work, try to see if there are conflicting hidden devices or a copy or your device when not plugged.In Windows XP, open up your start menu and click on "Run..."Type in "cmd" without the quotes and hit enter.In the command prompt, type in "set devmgr_show_nonpresent_devices=1" without the quote and hit enter.Then, type in "devmgmt.msc" without the quotes.*In your Device Manager window, click on View > Show hidden devicesNow you should see a whole new bunch of grayed out devices everywhere and a new section called "Non-Plug and Play Drivers" and other such.Try to filter through them and eradicate anything that could be related to your EasyCap thing.Restart and reinstall, hoping it works.If you're on Windows Vista or Windows 7 (can also be done in XP)Right click on "Computer" in your start menu and hit "Properties"Click on "Advanced System Settings", then click on "Environment Variables"In the "System variables" section, click "New..."Variable name: devmgr_show_nonpresent_devicesVariable value: 1Click OK until you're out, log off your session and log back in.Right click on "Computer" again and hit "Properties"Then, click on "Device Manager".Continue from the asterisk from the previous section.Wow, I had the Window on quick reply for a few hours since I got caught up installing a new heatsink and set of fans into the tower, and didn't see your message confirming your OS pass by...
