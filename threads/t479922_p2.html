One of the downsides of the 3 in 1 though is that it doesn't offer any kind of medium grit sharpening surface, just a medium-fine whetstone, extra fine finishing rods and then a carbide sharpener which is complete garbage. As for the sharpening solution that comes with the one you linked, you can get away with just using either water or windex, just something to keep things lubricated. It comes with an angle guide but I heard someone mention that you can just angle your knife's edge on a quarter or matchbook or something to get a 45 degree angle if you're learning how to free hand sharpen.

if you're looking for kitchen knife maintenance get the one I linked

if you're hella rich then get both as they compliment each other really well
