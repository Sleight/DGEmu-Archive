1. Don't use the blur tool to blend your renders - create a "show all" mask on the render layer and use feather after selecting the empty space around it (right click on the selection to access it) 2. Not only that but create a curves adjustment layer and experiment with its settings after brushing on multiple layers and changing their blending modes to your liking3. You can use the same color while brushing and then change each layer's color by adjustments > color balance (select colorize and don't take too much brightness or you will only see white).Now that's what I call advice :)
