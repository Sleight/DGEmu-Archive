UundefinableAug 10 2009, 08:54 AM
I think closed source software is perfectly fine in it's own right. Companies spend billions of dollars just to develop their software. They also spend billions more on continually improving upon the closed source software they do create. The fact that it is closed source protects these companies and their products from being manipulated and updated before their own programmers are able to produce the same changes to make a new version. If they allowed their programs to be open source, they'd lose a lot of money from those computer programming geniuses who are able to do this. Not only would random geniuses hurt them but, geniuses that are employed by a software company's competitors would be able to analyze the program to make it better and sell it under a different name which could potentially be devastating to the company that originally came up with the software.Open source software however, that has been lisenced under the General Public License (GPL) is awesome! I commend those that create all these programs and plugins and such for anyone to use. I think it is very generous of them even though they technically could have sold it for a price especially if it was in high demand. While working with Wordpress, I found that most of the plugins are licensed under the GPL and am very glad for it. Since someone was wondering how people make any money off of open source software, many of the programmers that create these plugins have donation links on their main sites that allow you to donate to them if you really like their programs or if they are of very good use to you and you appreciate them enough for making it. Of course they probably don't make that much money, but I suppose that it has to be enough to where they continue to make these open source programs. Anyway, just as I voted, I think both closed and open source software have their own benefits. I do think that large companies do have a right to keep their software as closed source... especially if it is the only type of software like it available. I also think open source software is more for more simpler programs that are made by people instead of companies (which is more common) just so it can be available to others to work on and improve for the benefit of the community as a whole instead of letting large corporations in on its workings.
