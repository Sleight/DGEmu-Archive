BBendilinORIGINAL POSTMar 22 2009, 01:18 AM
My roommate and I are making our own RPG, but we're not going o try to market it in any way so we're mostly just editing/using sprites and music from other games for it. However, we're hoping that once we're done this project (Currently called "Legend Project/Touched by a Hero") we'll work on an actual, legitimate, legal RPG that we're hoping to get on Xbox Live Arcade along with three of our other friends for programming/the music.It probably looks like trash right now, but here's a screenshot or two of what we have so far of the first area in the game.A lot of it looks empty and boring at the moment, but we're really flooding this game with events and cutscenes which only trigger when you accomplish certain feats/talk to certain people at the correct time in the game.We're not claiming this game is "revolutionary" or "has multiple paths that gives no two players the same experience" (like most game developers like to BS these days), it's mostly just amusing and interesting fluff that makes the game and its inhabitants come more alive. Think along the lines of Majora's Mask, where what you did at certain points had consequences on later events, except not being part of the plot.I started working with sprites circa 11 years ago, but haven't done much within the past few years outside of a forum signature or two. Like I said, for this specific game we're just messing around and having fun. We're looking to have the final game as a SNES rom so it can be played on SNES emulators (and portable devices that can play SNES emulators).
