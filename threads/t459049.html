HHighravenJul 30 2010, 12:30 PM
The game is awesome!I've put over 60 hours into it and I'm still playing. After you beat the final boss, you can farm treasure maps for the best gear in the game.The class system is nice too. Unlike Dragon Warrior 3, when you change classes you become pretty much a new hero, plus any abilities and + stats you got via the other vocations you've put points into. For example, my mage has stats that give him an additional 30 mana which carries between every class. Spells, sadly, don't carry over between jobs (vocations). You can change classes at anytime at Alltrades Abby after you've unlocked them. There's 12 in total, the 6 starting classes and 6 to unlock.
