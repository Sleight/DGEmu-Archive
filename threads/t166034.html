Final Fantasy XI is an MMORPG catered to a certain audience. More specifically, it's been made for the people who strive to complete games 100%, meaning getting all the best weapons, completing all the side quests, maxing out all the character levels, etc. That's the kind of game Final Fantasy XI is. There are weapons that take an immense amount of gil to create (relic weapons), sometimes taking over two years to make. There are tons of side quests, most of which aren't significant, but many have their own little story which is pretty neat. Your one character can change jobs whenever you have access to your Mog House. Each job has their own independent level, meaning maxing out the level on, say, warrior will not affect the level of ninja and vice versa. The game also emphasizes cooperation. There are many things you cannot do in the game without the help of other players. For most jobs, that includes leveling.
