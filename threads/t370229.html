SSelzarionMay 25 2008, 03:36 PM
I do believe that to a great extent, our lives are determined by the media. The media carries information - Information which we use to build how we see the world for the sole reason that we can't experience all of that by ourselves. Not being there first hand, we're forced to take information from second hand sources. I would think that the majority of sources have some sort of slant, no matter how subtle, because every piece of information put forth to the masses comes with some sort of underlying motive. For example, let's take a newspaper agency. They have a set of underlying guidelines for their reporters to follow. All reporters can only report on certain things and put them forward in a certain light. There are things they throw the spotlight on for months and there are things, though real and factual, which are just taboo. What's on the first page carries a lot of weight because it's the paper's way of bringing the agenda to the people. They want the public to think what they think is important, is important. Even though there may be more pressing matters reported only in later pages, or possibly not at all. So in this sense, our view of the world is determined by the media.However, our view of ourselves is also probably determined by what we see in the media. Things like trends; the games we play because of the ads we see, the fashion we follow because of the stars we've seen in it. The media shows us how we should be living, the products we should be consuming, and the ideas attached to those products. In a sense, it's a desire to keep up and in the end, I don't think it's something we could walk away from easily. I don't think I could spend a day without the internet, my phone, the TV, my PSP or my NDS. :PBut yes, I do agree that in the end it's all up to the user. The thing about today's media is knowing that what comes to us from different sources may not be completely right.
