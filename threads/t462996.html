well i know everybody's gon say metroid prime pinball, which is undoubtedly the best for ds. another lesser known title that's pretty good is flipper critters. flipper critters is a pinball game that has a quest-like story mode. it's decent, colorful, full of cute looking cartoon animals, and great music. all in all, it's not too bad, but compared to metroid prime pinball it's a bit on the stale side. the only reason i'm keeping the game is cuz of the music (i really give props to this game regarding its music). however, if you enjoy pinball games then you'll find something worthwhile. i spent a couple hours on it, and i'm not really a fan of the pinball genre in the first place. o, and another thing to mention is that the game is a bit challenging cuz the story mode only gives you 4 or 5 lives and i have yet to unlock any extra. but ay like i said i ain't a pinball pro so i dunno what a true fan would expect
