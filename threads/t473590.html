Oh, for what it's worth, I added three new jobs to the Jobs plugin for a total of nine, and I set it so players can have up to two jobs at a time.The new jobs are:Netherknight - Gains money and job experience from mining blocks found in the Nether and killing Nether mobs.Engineer - Gains money and job experience from placing anything and everything related to redstone circuitry.Designer - Gains money and job experience from placing decorative objects such as paintings, wool and cobwebs.I also made it so that the Builder, Engineer and Designer job classes lose an equal amount of money and experience for breaking the same blocks they're paid to place. Oh, I also set up a survivor kit for Zombieland (full leather armor and a wooden sword) that can be obtained once every 10 minutes by typing "/kit survivor". Also also, job money and experience are earned only on the main world. Also also also, each world now has an independent inventory folder, so you can't carry items from one to the other, but when you return to a world, you will have the same inventory there as when you left.I think that's all the changes I have made so far today.
