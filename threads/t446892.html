In D/P/Pl where you get the PokéRadar, every pokémon you battle in shaking grass consecutively increases the chances of a Shiny pokémon appearing, where it maxes out at 40 pokémon fought. if you see a glowing and shaking patch of grass, there's a shiny. In D/P/Pl/HG/SS If you breed two pokémon of different Regions (Say, a Pikachu from a Japanese cart and a Pikachu from an EU cart) the chance of the resulting egg pokémon being shiny is increased to 1 in 2,048. In Black and White, the increased chance is enhanced even further, to an INCREDIBLE 1 in 1,365. Just thought I'd share for you all.
