What that is is that for each time you write data to your card, your card will also write to this Backup FAT area of what you recently wrote, which is, as you may have guessed, a backup. This is mainly for restoring the card in the event your card becomes corrupted. The drawback of having a backup FAT is, as you also may have guessed, slower card performance.We all know the main functions of a SD card in a flash cart is reading games and writing save data. Granted, it doesn't constantly save every 5 minutes (unless your the type that does save every 5 minute2 in Advance Wars), I see no need for having a Backup FAT on a SD card in a flashcart. So far the only devices I know that can format cards without a backup FAT are PocketPC's, for some odd reason formatting in cellphones dont work either as it is worse, cell phones format with a low cluster size(slow write speed) and with a backup FAT. In my experience, and I have a hell lot of experience with SD cards, is that the more you read and write to your card, with a backup FAT, the more slower your card performance will be.
