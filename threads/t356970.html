I'll add more to my post once I've played the game.I'm going make this the official UT3 discussion thread since the game is out now and the other threads are outdated and only talk about the Pre-release demos.EDIT:Ok I finally played it so here's my mini-review since I didn't play too far into it.I have to say that it's a huge improvement graphically over UT 2003 and 2004 and it seems to be more closely involved with online play as well. All the familiar weapons are present too which is a nice touch and they also changed the default keyboard mapping to match other new FPS games like Crysis and Bioshock (A S D W to move, E to interact, etc).The one thing that's worth mentioning the most is the absence of FMVs and I really wish Crysis did this too. Graphics are most certainly good and advanced enough to the point that full motion video is no longer needed and that's what this game does. All cut-scenes are rendered in real time and wouldn't look much better if done in FMV from what I could judge. The only FMVs in this game are the logos in the intro but that's perfectly normal (or who knows, those could be rendered too).Overall, I'd say this is a fine replacement for its predecessor. If you have a fairly modern PC, give it a whirl >.< .
