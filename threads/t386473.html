Okay I tried to give this question the fullest and most complete response I could. However there is A Ton of material to sort through. So I will just provide certain instances that I am aware of of the Hulk being defeated. While none of these are considered "weaknesses" in the way that Green Kryptonite is a weakness of Superman, they are ways in which the Hulk can be temporarily mastered.In the original comic series the Hulk could be transformed back into Banner after being exposed to massive doses of gamma rays. This is the closest he has come to have a true weakness. However, this was written out of his character by the 1970's.The next few things depend strongly on who was responsible for writing the Hulk character at the time.Hulk has been temporarily bested by The Ringmaster, using hypnosis to control the Hulk.In the animated Avengers show, the hulk was defeated by Thor after he went rogue. In the comics he was also beaten to a stalemate by The Sentry, causing him to revert back to Bruce Banner.The Hulk was able to beat a corrupted version of himself from the future by transporting him back in time to the center of the explosion that originally created the Hulk. Therefore we can assume that the Hulk himself could be defeated by a blast of the same magnitude.Other posts have mentioned relaxants, sedatives or lack of air being able to render the Hulk incapacitated. I cannot corroborate these, but they seem likely to have happened at some point amongst the vast lore that contains the Hulk.The most important thing to remember is that the Hulks weakness or vulnerabilities are highly dependent on the writer at the time. While this is not a completely comprehensive list, it is the best that I can provide. If you seek further reading I would suggest The Incredible Hulk OnlineHope this helps!
