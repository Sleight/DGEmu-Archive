Game Gear was way ahead of its time - very much like virtua boy from Nintendo. The major problem with the console was not the console itself, but support from 3rd party manufacturers, and limitations on certain parts of the machine - like battery life. Game Gear was a technological innovation because it was the first handheld to be backwards compatable (master system) while still providing rock solid games to play. The demise of the system was sad, but we were left with a series of good games that can still be played today, and with recharagable batteries, life is no longer an issue. Did you know game gear was one of the first handhelds with recharagable battery option. How about the TV tuner? First handheld in color that was backwards compatable? It is too bad it died, but even worse was the Nomad. The Nomad is my favorite all time handheld console.
