Overview
This guide will talk about the confirmed working 3DS flashcarts currently available on the market. Note that all these card currently use an exploit on the Nintendo 3DS with firmware versions 4.1 to 4.5. Any 3DS firmware later than those version have been patched, rendering the following flashcarts useless.

 

Notes
Each one of these cards have a specific list of supported ROMs. These ROMs are avaiable for download from our 3DS section of our site found here.

 

 

The Flashcarts

Gateway 3DS (Average retail price of 80$)

This card was the original card that started it all. It has received continuous updates and has evolved quite a lot since its release.

 

Features:

- Package comes with 2 flashcarts: 1 for supporting DS games, the other for 3DS games

- Save backup

- Local multiplayer

- Region free ROM loading as of firmware 1.1a

- Firmware spoofing as of firmware 1.1a

 

Issues:

- No online play

- 1 single 3DS ROM can be loaded at a time via software

- No automatic saving. You are required to close your game and return to the home menu, or else your save will be lost

- No homebrew support

Conclusion

More and more options are being released to the consumers, all based on the technology that the Gateway 3DS has used since their release. Thanks to this, the prices have dropped drastically since the beginning. Keep in mind that the technology is not 100% perfect yet, and will require you to have a 3DS console with a really old firmware of 4.5 and lower.

TtbbtbbApr 07 2014, 11:01 PM
my_coal wrote on Mar 04 2014, 02:18 AM:

Do you know of the DSTWO will play 3ds games and allow you to do it online? I am currently on firmware 7.1.0-16 so does this mean i cant utilize the flash cartS?

 

DSTWO will not play 3DS games, it only plays DS games, but it will let you play DS games on 3DS. With firmware 7.1.0 you cannot use any of the current 3DS flashcarts (you need 4.5 or earlier firmware). You can use a few DS flashcarts, like the DSTWO and a few others, but most are blocked. 

 

my_coal wrote on Mar 12 2014, 01:49 AM:

So does this mean that everytime they update firmware i have to buy a new DSTWO? Sorry if my questions are stupid, its been a while since ive used an emulator in my old NDS.

 

No, you should never have to buy a new DSTWO unless you lose or somehow break it. Whenever Nintendo releases new firmware that blocks the DSTWO, they are very quick to put out a new DSTWO firmware that lets it work again. 

 

However, it's best to update the DSTWO before updating the 3DS, otherwise you have to either use an older system like a DS or DSlite to update the DSTWO or use a slightly tricky alternate method to update it on the 3DS that is blocking it.

 

Since you're already on 7.1, it's possible that if you buy a DSTWO it won't work on your system until after the DSTWO has been updated, so keep that in mind.
