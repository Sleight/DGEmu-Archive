Whether you have Photoshop, GIMP, or even Paint, all artists are welcome. :P PrizesThe Winner of each round will receive two Prizes. One, a Free Download of the game they designed as well as 1000 GP. Wins will be tallied and the member with most wins will receive 10,000 GP. The Runner Up will receive 5,000 GP. RoundsIn each round, a game will be given to design. Depending on activity from entrant's, the amount of rounds will differ. Five Rounds are planned.EliminationsWhen each round is completed, a new topic will be created and a public vote will be hosted. Judges will also give their critique. The first to five will be the winner. The person with the least votes will be eliminated. If more than one Entrant's have zero votes, depending on the number of contestant's remaining, they will be eliminated. JudgesJudges have the final say in what happens and who is eliminated.
