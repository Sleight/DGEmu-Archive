SShadowApr 25 2011, 05:10 PM
I agree with cg. I have Grado SR-80i's and Head-Direct RE-ZEROs played through a Creative (ugh) X-Fi XtremeGamer, and with neither one can I tell the difference between MP3 320 and FLAC. I can, however, tell the difference between MP3 256 and MP3 320 with the Grados in some songs that are recorded well.What I like even more than MP3 320, though, is MP3 V0. Variable bitrate is more economical with space than constant; V0 is the highest quality variable and it lets it go up to 320kbps if it needs to, but encodes at lower bitrates for less complex portions of the song.With regular cheapie headphones though, don't waste space; just use the common MP3 192.Or even better, if your music player supports it, go with Ogg Vorbis! Higher maximum bitrate and better compression! Also, open source. :)
