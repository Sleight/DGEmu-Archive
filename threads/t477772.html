http://www.feedbooks.com/
They have ebooks in epub, mobi, and pdf formats. They also publish original ebooks by new authors including fan-fiction. This site is formatted in a way that makes downloading straight to your e-reader via its web browser a cinch.



http://manybooks.net/
This site is known for offering almost every format imaginable including audiobooks in .mp3 or .ogg.



http://www.gutenberg.org/
Project Gutenberg is more than just ebooks. They have several hundred audiobooks, some read by humans and others computer-generated. There is an expanding sheet music section. They are best known, though, for being the first major site to offer a database of free e-books.



http://www.baenebooks.com/c-1-free-library.aspx
They specialize in publishing science fiction/fantasy titles submitted by independent authors. They offer the books for sale in e-book format and in print, but they do offer some of the books for free in e-book format linked to above.



http://www.free-ebooks.net/
They offer mobi and pdf formats for ebooks, but also include textbooks and academic articles. They also have apps for Android and iOS.



http://www.mobipocket.com/freebooks/Default.aspx
They exclusively offer mobi formatted ebooks for Kindle devices. They have an ebook creator program and also mobi readers for phones and PC. They also have several hundred foreign language ebooks, such as 700 French titles, 170 Dutch, 100 German, 100 Spanish, 275 Finnish, and more.



http://www.ipl.org/
A public service organization founded at the University of Michigan School of Information. You can find a ton of reference books and education material from the IPL. You can also find magazines and newspaper articles from across the globe.



http://www.freetechbooks.com/
They specialize in free computer science and mathematics ebooks. They also have free tech books like computer programming and engineering, physics, marketing, business etc.


OverDrive
https://www.overdrive.com/
This site allows you to "check out" e-books and audiobooks by logging in with your local library card.
