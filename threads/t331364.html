SSyko ClownJul 25 2009, 08:50 PM
Need for speed pro street was one of my first PS3 games. Kept me entertained. The story is lacking, but controls and steering are solid. The graphics were okay, and the car had some real weight. The physics made the driving a dream. The drag mode was good, and the top speeds were amazing in some of the better cars maxed out with upgrades. It's all you want from a racer, even if its not a meg-hit of the NFS franchise. But it has everything you want. This isn't the typical need for speed, through the city with cops in tow, racer. This game has your classic circuit, drift, time attack, checkpoint to checkpoint, and drag races. But also includes two neat top speed modes. Recording: 1)the highest speed possible, and 2) recording speeds at checkpoints in a checkpoint race and tallying them at the end. Granted some of these are road races but because the story takes place inside a "legal" racing circuit you don't have cops plowing into you sirens blazing while you're drifting around a dude for first place with only a lap left. It's fun, it's fast paced, and it's just good. If you got a PS3 it's worth $20.
