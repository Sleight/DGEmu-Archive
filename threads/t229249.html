Support and GuidelinesThis guide is intended for all Support Staff (SS). It covers the description of the current work for SS as well as new missions. Please read carefully so as to avoid making mistakes.Firstly here is a thread, Tools and Utilities to Help you Mod and more, that has a few neat tool you can use to assist you in your duties as Support Staff, as well if you have not done so already we recommend you use Firefox as your browser.1. Primary Mission: Answering Emailsa. BasicsAddress: http://webmail.dgemu.com/Login: supportIf you do not know the password, PM myself, Ish or another Sentinel or Admin (but not Ben (DM), or PS) to obtain it.Once you get in the mailbox, click on 'Mailbox" and a list of unanswered emails should be displayed. Remember to check the mailbox AT LEAST once a day: don't let a mail stay unanswered more than 24 hours!NOTE: See below for more details about Support Emails, '5. Support Emails'.b. Answer FormatThe general attitude when replying an email is to be as proper and polite as possible. Remember that you are speaking in the name of Dgemu and therefore, you should NEVER insult or make fun of the person who sent the email. When a member begins to rant and insult you, just ignore it. Do not reply to provocation.Suggested format for a reply:
someone wrote:
Dear Member,[Content of the email]RegardsDGEmu Support
c. Common Questions and AnswersFor all question covered by the site FAQ: redirect them to the Site FAQ (So read it first)For all question covered by the "Support and Suggestion" FAQ: redirect them to the Forum FAQ, the OLD S&S FAQ or the OLD, OLD S&S FAQ (So read it aslo)The member forgot his/her username: retrieve it here by using his/her email address, if you have trouble finding the name you should also use the Member Search via the forums or the Member Search Staff Utility (the Staff Utility is limited to finding one name, but it allows you to search usernames, the Member Search function on the forums only allows you to search the Display Names but it comes with a list of names instead, so use both depending on the circumstances.The member wishes to change his/her email address: do it hereThe member forgot his/her password: tell him/her to go on the Login Page and retrieve it by himself/herself.The member cannot validate himself/herself: after verifying that it is not a dupe account, do it for him/her hereFor ROM problems: if you know the solution, answer it directly, otherwise, either leave it to other SS or suggest the member to post on the forum after making a search for similar topics.For donation questions: if you know the solution, answer. Otherwise PM PlaySwiss.Other common questions can be found here.These points covered the primary job of an SS and should be followed by ALL SS without exception.2. Moderating SupportSince SS are the most likely to become the baby moderators in the future, you should begin to support the moderation of this forum (if you are not doing this already). Here is how you can help:Report rule breaking posts.Find the dupe threads on the forum and post the link to the original topic.Provide technical support to members on the forum.Report mass spammers and dupe accounts for banning.3. Othersa. Inspecting ReviewsAs part of the staff, SS have check the Review Center regularly to decide whether the reviews submitted by members are worth being accepted. The Review Center is here.Make sure the review you are inspecting meets the requirements that are in the Reviews FAQ/Old Reviews FAQ.For all reviews, except those that are just spam, be sure to Google to make sure the Review has not been plagiarized, you can do this most of the time by selecting one sentence that you think will be suitable (NOTE: Choose a sentence that has NO grammar or anything in it, as Google will not notice them, especially apostrophes (') << that thing.), paste it in the Google search and the include quotation marks ("Example") at the beginning and end so it searches for the exact phrase, if there is a match then it has been plagiarized and you should select 'Decline This Review And Deduct 10 DPs for plagiarizing/spamming'. Before pressing this though you should report this review in the Review Inspection Notes thread.If the review is not plagiarized or is spam then, you may use the following as Guidelines when inspecting views (these are not exactly the same as the ones in Reviews FAQ/Old Reviews FAQ, they are just ones I personally do ... I have a high standard, it really is up to YOU, you can make your own requirements if you want, the decision is left for you o make:If the review is only a few lines long, less then 100 words then simply 'Decline This Review!'.If the review is over 100 words but isn't at least 4 paragraphs and doesn't contain a scoring (eg. 10/10) for different categories (eg. Sound, Graphics, Story, etc.) then chose 'Approve This Review!'.If the review is well over 100 words is about or more then 4 paragraphs long and does include scoring and categories as mentioned above then chose 'Approve This Review And Reward Member with 10 DPs!'.Remember the above is just a guideline, you don't have to follow it directly. Also be aware that SS are not the only staff who inspect reviews, all other staff members are permitted to inspect the reviews as well.If you come across any reviews that have already been submitted that are spam, plagiarized, etc. you can report them in the Accepted Plagiarized, Bad or Wrong Game Reviews thread.b. Welcoming MembersSince members that take time to Introduce Themselves are generally willing to become part of this community, it is a really good idea to answer their good will. The rule of thumb is to ensure every thread has at least one welcoming reply. When doing so, do not forget to remind them to read and follow the rules (providing a link would be helpful too).c. Report MembersAs well as reporting members posts in the forum as a staff member you now have access to reporting members for other things as described below:If you come across a member with a rude or inappropriate display name you can report them in This ThreadUsing the IP Finder you can find out if a member has more then one account or not, among a few other details; Log In name, Display Name, Email Address, Date they Joined, Date they were Last Active and their current Warning level, the last one, IP Ban can not be used by SS.If you are sure about whether someone is a dupe then report them in the Bannings ForumIf you are not sure about whether someone is a dupe or not then Report suspected duplicates here! thread.4.
