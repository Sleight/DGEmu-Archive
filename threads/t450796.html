Tenchi Gakuen is introduced as a prestigious girls' school, unique in its Sword Scholarship program in which teams, known as "shinyuu", battle each other for bragging rights and the hefty monetary rewards given to victorious teams. Enter Kurogane Hayate, trying to impersonate her twin sister Nagi in the school until Nagi is out of rehabilitation. Initially disinterested in anything outside retaining her sister's scholarship, Hayate resolves to actively take part in battling when she finds out that their old orphanage is 8 million yen in debt with moneylenders. Seeking out the surly Mudou Ayana, who had fallen from the upper ranks after abandoning battling in general, Hayate embarks on her mission, encountering a cast full of idiocy in the process.
Introduction adapted from the TVTropes page. There's also an excellent character page over at that link.Seven Seas has released 5 out of the 8 volumes they had permission for, last I heard. Part of the chapters can be found on Mangafox too.I've read up to the latest chapters in Ultra Jump, and things just get better and better -- by which I mean the awesomeness level gets cranked up even higher ALL THE TIME, against expectations. Hayashiya's sense of comedy is quite possibly the best I've had the chance to see, and all most of the characters are well-defined. Those which are not have their own quirky traits that make them quickly memorable.Have you read it yet, or plan to? What are your thoughts?

I like the series and have been following it in mangafox. I think it got a decent story with a lot of comedy moments inserted even during actions parts. The relationship between Kurogane and Ayana is certainly fun to watch with Kurogane always play the foolish/ playful partner and Ayana being the responsilble one and tend to not show her feeling (it's obvious she cares about Kurogane more than she show). I do hope Hayashira would show Hayate's real abilities though, I mean she usually acts like some useless slacker and all, but she does have amazing skills as shown in some of the fights. Seeing her using her full abilities might be fun (although I do enjoy seeing the goofy side of her).
