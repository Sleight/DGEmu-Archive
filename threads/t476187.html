When you edit posts, do remember to be respectful to the post and the guys who made the posts. Don't edit the post if you don't have to, and try not to add or remove content in the post unless you are really sure that it is appropriate. Posts are the main form of communication in these forums, afterall - and communication is the lifeblood of any forum or community, online or otherwise.

Little jokes here and there are okay of course, particularly in 'chatty' places like the Spam Centre. We're not a very straitlaced community, really. Be sure to clearly distinguish the joke from the original post, though, and to make only jokes which are good-natured! Aim to make the original poster laugh WITH you, don't aim to laugh AT him. Accordingly, do not remove or add stuff which will confuse or anger the original poster, and/or those around him. While that may be good for a cheap laugh, it is also highly annoying. It is also a clear case of power abuse.

Just thought I should clarify, after I witnessed some confusion about this in the SC. Don't let that spoil the mood, though - you guys have an exciting and important job ahead of you, something the whole community will be gratefully relying upon. BEFORE YOU EDIT!
Click the edit button, in the top left of the post box there is a light switch:

Click this to view the TEXT ONLY (no formatting, this will copy the BBCode, etc), now select all in the post, copy it, go to the bottom, put about a dozen enters and paste (so you have a duplicate, one untouched and one you can edit freely, this will allow for errors, if you make a mistake you will have a backup copy!

BEFORE completing your edit (Save Changes) I recommend to select all and copy once again, then hit Save Changes, I only suggest this as on the rare occasion the BBCode will convert to HTML instead, if this happens you will notice:
Recovery Staff Assignments
If that happens just edit it again, paste (so you will override the new HTML coverted text to the BBCode text you copied before) and Save Changes, that should fix it. If not post in the Recovery Staff Operations thread and (just in case) put the copied text in a Code Tag:
[ code]
Text in here
[/ code]
One of the senior staff will have a look and fix it, even if they have to convert the HTML to BBCode themselves!

If you don't feel 100% sure if you have done it right you can put the untouched copy in spoiler tags:

Text in here

and have ShadowMan or Channel28 take a look at it, if everything is fine they will delete the untouched copy in the spoiler tags and that thread will be marked as complete.

HANDY TOOL!!
'CoLT' ('FireFox' Extension) - Adds an extra item to the right-click menu bar which allows you to copy a link in several ways, BBCode (this is what you will mostly use), HTML Link, etc. This extension is VERY useful linking a person to a topic, etc in the forums. Instead of typing out:
'iMacros for Firefox'
you right click the link, select 'Copy Link Text and Location As' > 'BBCode'. Now go to where you want to paste it and go Ctrl+V (Paste) and it inserts the BBCode for you.

If like me you don't think you need the other options and just want BBCode you can get rid of the other options, in 'FireFox' go to 'Tools' and select 'Add-Ons'. Find 'CoLT' in the list and press the 'Options' button. Select the option you want to get rid of and click the 'Remove' button, or if you still want the other options you can use the 'Move Up' and 'Move Down' buttons to change their order in the menu.
