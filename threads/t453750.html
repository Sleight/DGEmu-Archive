NOTE: I looked through all six pages of topics and couldn't find a topic quite like this one. Correct me if I'm wrong, however, because sometimes I tend to miss the obvious. And it's not a typical "FAVORITE BAND" thread.- - - - -One of the most important things, for me, when getting into a new band, is their lyrics. Not only do I have to like the instruments, but I have to be able to really feel the lyrics. So I'm curious, what's everyone's favorite band in terms of their lyrics and meaning?Now, I'm going to be a dork and say that my favorite band, Alesana, is my favorite.Mainly because that is the REASON I love them so much!They're classified widely as emo/screamo, but they're much more than that, and the label disgraces what they really are, in a way. Are there screaming vocals? Yes, and the band wouldn't be the same without Dennis. But Shawn has a beautiful singing voice (I don't care who says he sounds "feminine", he speaks like a guy just has a higher singing voice). The band members THEMSELVES clarify several times that their songs are not about depression and relationships!On Frail Wings Of Vanity And Wax -- is based mainly off of Greek mythology. Ambrosia is based on Midas, Nero's Decay on the fall of Rome, Third Temptation of Paris on Helen of Troy, ect.Where Myth Fades to Legend -- is based mainly off the Tales of the Brothers Grimm. Seduction is about the Maid Maleen, A Most Profound Quiet about the Robber Bridegroom, ect.The Emptiness -- it's based off a short story that the band wrote themselves. Many people speculate it's about the poem by Annabel Lee, but it isn't, they named the female character after her as a tribute to Poe, Shawn Milke said it himself.So yes, to conclude this rather long post, I really respect their lyrics and the meaning behind their songs.What are your favorite bands, in terms of these factors?
