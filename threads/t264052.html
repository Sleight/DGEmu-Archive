[center]Ever wondered how they made those sigs that looks like scanners? Well heres the Torturial for it. Our end result will look like:Step 1:First we’ll start off by making a new image with the dimensions of height: 2 pixels width: 1 with a transparent background. (Depending on how much space you desire between each line, you may want to add to the height, but its suggested you keep it at these dimensions because its simple and it works.) It should look like this:Step 2:Next press the 'D' button your keyboard and select the pencil tool. Click on the arrow that's pointing down beside 'Brush' in the options bar. Now adjust the size of the pencil by setting the master diameter to 1px and make sure the Mode is set to normal. It should look somewhat very similar to this:Step 3:Now zoom in as much as possible by continuously pressing "ctrl" and "+" together on your keyboard until you can’t zoom in any more. With your pencil tool, draw 1 pixel at the very top of the image. Like this: Step 4:Next, go to Edit->define pattern from your top menu and in the text box that will come up, type in 'scan lines' for the name and click OK. Then open up the image you would like to incorporate the scan lines in and create a new layer and select the paint bucket tool. Now go the options and click on the drop down menu and select 'Pattern'. (Note: Make sure its the mode is 'Normal'.)It should look similar to this:Step 5:Now click on the arrow beside 'Pattern' and select the scan lines pattern. Just click anywhere on your image and voila! Instant scan lines. Now add some of your own creativity by blending it in and experimenting.Now you just finished your first Scanned Sig =)[/center]Credit goes to my big brother Daniel. Comments please.
