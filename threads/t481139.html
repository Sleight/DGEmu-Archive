There's that Shin-chan game, Wario Blast, and KOF95 on the Game Boy. So much good times back when I was young.

 

I was poor, and only had a Game Boy Pocket at the period when the GBA was coming out... Quite sad. XD

 

But the games I used to play, so many good ones. I had Megaman I and II, and my friends lent me Wario Blast and a few others I'm trying to remember.

And during a bus trip to the East of Canada, I remember spending so much time in the bus just playing games. There was an Azn kid that came from Hong Kong with a GB also, so we exchanged games during the trip. That's where I played the Shin-chan game, Rockman World 4 (which back then I didn't even know it existed), and KOF96.

 

The hours spent looking into that dark screen, playing with the contrast, and finding a light source to see what the hell was going on the screen. That and constantly running out of AAA battery and buying new ones during the trip.

good times.
