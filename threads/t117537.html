Installing a modchip onto your PSX console won't affect the lifespan itself, as the modchip's purpose is simply to make the PSX think the security on the disc is present, when in reality it isn't.What could damage your PSX lens is basically how you treat it.Depending on the blank media you choose to copy your games on, low quality ones simply causes more strain on the laser itself to read the disc, thus reducing its life span. Same thing goes to scratched discs.Using the boot disc method to play backups is just simply straining the bay door of the PSX, since you're always opening and closing it, unless you mod it to keep it always open.Using the swap method, the drive's motor lifespan would basically be reduced, since everytime you're pulling out the disc forcibly right after reading the security layer of a game disc causes a stall on the turning, and the motor forces itself to attempt to turn the disc.
