A permanent CFW (hereafter referred to as just "CFW") is always better than a HEN, not least because a CFW stays after you reboot it, but a HEN (and the stuff you load with it) only stays in the RAM, so if you reboot it you got to load it again. More info on HENs vs CFW: http://wololo.net/wagic/tn-hen-faq/Also CFW Pro 6.35 is a real CFW. What gave you the impression otherwise?Edit: See correction in my post below. Sorry.
