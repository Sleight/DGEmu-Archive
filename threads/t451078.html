Just picked up the new 30 Seconds To Mars album "This Is War" yesterday, and I have not stopped listening to it since. It has got to be one of the most brilliant albums of the year. I'm a huge fan of 30STM but this to me has definitely got to be their best work to date. There is not a single song on this album that I don't enjoy but I will admit that my stand out favourites are "Night Of The Hunter," "This Is War," "Closer To The Edge," "Vox Populi," and "Search and Destroy." I say this because this album to me fits perfectly to the story I am currently working on, for those of you who don't know me I'm an aspiring author from NSW Australia, and "Night Of The Hunter," for me, has become the new theme to my current project.This album, it's meaningful lyrics and the brilliant music will never get old for me and so I give this album 5/5 stars.Holler out and send your opinions and respect to what I believe to be one of the best bands and best albums of the year.Let your opinion on this album be heard, whether it be good or bad, that is the beauty of free speech and opinion.
