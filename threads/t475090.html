I have heard a lot recently about how much stress it puts on a family to have to take care of a severley disabled child their whole lives, especially when the parents get to the point where they need care themselves but cannot afford it / can't afford someone else to take care of their child. In the cases where doctors know the child is going to be born with just a brain stem, or so disabled that they will be wheel chair bound and only able to gurgle (with no evidence that the child will develop normal mental understanding of their situation or the world around them), would you abort the child? or consider it a burden of life that one must overcome? I guess this can be seen as a pro life vs pro choice question, but I'd like to focus more on the question of how much suffering we feel obligated to take upon ourselves for keeping up appearances of being moral/sacrificing people, and even though a parent may love their child no matter what is it fair to the child to live a long life of nothingness (confirmed medically by brain scan, not saying the handicapped can't lead fulfilling lives) while the family suffers?
