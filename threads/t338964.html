SSkullgrinMay 14 2009, 08:54 AM
The Legacy of Kain series is one of my all time favorite game series. I might be biased due to my love of Gothic Horror, but the games were ahead of their time. The plot can be very hard to follow if you pick up a game out of order. The reason is that time travel plays a heavy part in the games. And events are constantly shuffling in the time lines to the game due to time travel. The 5 games as a whole represent one of the most rich and intriguing stories in video games.You start out as Kain, an arrogant noble, who gets murdered pretty close to the opening of the first game. While in what I assumed was Hell he's approached by a Necromancer who offers him a chance to kill his captors if he serves the Necromancer. Kain agrees and sacrifices his humanity to become a Vampire. It's not long after that you encounter the band of thugs that killed you, and it's not a challenge to put them down. It's not that satisfying to kill them and that's the point. After that Kain is bound to the whims of the Necromancer, who turns out to be one of the Guardians of the Pillars. One of the Guardians has been murdered, and her lover (who is also a guardian) is the Guardian of the Mind. He slowly goes insane, and his psychic connection to the other Guardians drives them mad as well. Kain is tasked with restoring the Pillars by killing the Guardians and returning their item to the Pillar it represents to repair it. Over the course of the game you'll find numerous special items, weapons, and abilities to aid in your quest. By the game's end you're faced with an impossible choice. One that sets the stage for the sequels.Soul Reaver introduces us to Nosgoth after Kain has enslaved it. Lording over the remaining humans and his vampire Kingdom, Kain is worshiped more like a God then a King. His generals are his vampire "sons". Chosen by him and brought back to life as his vampire "children". Every so often Kain would go into a metamorphosis and emerge with a new power or evolution. However, nature plays a cruel trick on his favorite "son" Raziel. Poor Raziel evolves wings before his master. For his treason and blasphemy Kain rips the wings from his back and casts him into the Lake of the Dead. The water slowly burns Raziel for what seems like ages. He awakens in the Underworld to the Psychic voice of the Elder God. It tasks him with killing his brothers and Kain, along with other vampires to return their souls to the Wheel of Fate. So that the process of re-incarnation can take place again. Life has also stagnated to a point as Kain has polluted the skys to permanetly cloud the sun to an overcast type condition. Occasional beams of sunlight litter the country side. They're among the few ways to kill the vampire enemies in the game. Combat is a bit tough because you must kill the vampires to defeat them. Just beating them will only dizzy them long enough for you to use a killing blow. You can kill them with fire, impalment, or sunlight. Water also works but it's hard to throw things accurately. At the end of the game you use an abandoned time machine left by the Time Guardian. When you walk out of it you're greated by someone who should be dead... The Time Guardian himself.Soul Reaver 2 focuses on the fact that Raziel is in the past. He's being manipulated throughout the game. There are no boss battles. Personally I found this to be dissappointing since for the most part the puzzles weren't that hard. You have all of your earned powers from Soul Reaver, and a few elemental tricks for the Reaver. The basic storyline to the game is Raziel is being manipulated by both Kain and the Time Guardian. As you uncover more of the past of the Legacy of Kain games you'll find out some shocking facts. Kain never considered Raziel his enemy. He was instead guiding him towards fulfilling a destiny. At the game's conclusion you're faced with one of the biggest shocks in my video gaming experience. As you find out exactly who killed Raziel to begin with. And it's not who you think. The game ends with the timeline being shuffled and Raziel and Kain becoming dismayed when they learn that they were manipulated.Blood Omen 2 takes place in the new timeline established at the end of Soul Reaver 2. In it several changes have happened, most notably Nupraptor escaped his death in Legacy of Kain. In the new timeline Kain is viewed as the Vampire's Messiah and last hope. Humans are hunting the vampires to death, and several have jumped sides hoping to be left alone after their brethern has been killed. The humans are the pawns of a race of demonlike creatures mentioned in Soul Reaver 2. The combat is pretty fun, it allows for a fair number of weapons to be picked up. Usually from enemies, but some can be found in certain areas. There's a fair number of stealth kills which are pretty fun to pull off. The final battle pits Kain against the leader of the demons. Apon his defeat I can't remember if the timeline shuffles or not.Defiance was the last game. And as previously stated you play as both Kain and Raziel. Both play slightly different. Kain is a bit more powerful and has better telekinesis at first. Raziel is faster and can combo faster. The combat system is incredibly fun and using all of your abilities makes for some wicked combinations. I used to love grabbing soliders with my TK and throwing them into candles to set them on fire and then grabbing them with it and throwing them at their buddies. Everyone gets ignited and runs around flailing. Good times. Even more plot and backstory for the game is revealed and some loose ends are tied up. For example the missing boss Turrel from Soul Reaver is found by Raziel. His absence was explained by the plot of this game and Raziel fights him to the death. In Soul Reaver instead of fighting him you fight a Turrelian vampire (who's incredibly easy to kill). Rumor has it they ran out of space in that game and dumped Turrel. Anyway, the basic plot of the last game is Kain and Raziel are both exploring Nosgoth fighting the demons while trying to figure out how to change things so the demons won't have won. Raziel comes to find out quite a bit about the history of the Vampires and the Demons. At one point the Vampires were actually Angels who had fought the Demons. But they were cursed when the Demons were defeated changing them into Vampires. Also you find out who the Vampire Messiah truly is and how Raziel and Kain's destinies are intertwined. The final battle is pitted against one of the biggest names in the series, and at it's conclusion Kain remarks that for the first time in his existence he's filled with hope.Sadly there was a rumor of a 6th game, but I've not heard anything about it. I hope I didn't get too wordy. I just love the games is all. P
