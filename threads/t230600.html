[center][/center]Name: Xbox 360Release Dates: North America (United States & Canada)- November 22, 2005 Japan- December 10, 2005 Europe- December 2, 2005 Australia/New Zealand- March 23, 2006General SummaryThe Xbox 360 is one of the latest video game consoles and is part of the seventh generation, a position it shares with Sony's Playstation 3 and Nintendo's Wii. It is most noted for Xbox Live, which allows player-to-player communication as well as online game playing. Like the PS3, the Xbox 360 comes in different system configurations: Core, Premium, and Elite. The Core system is the cheapest and includes a wired gamepad, composite cables, and an Xbox Live Silver service (more on this later). The Premium system includes a 20GB detachable hard drive, a wireless gamepad, an Ethernet cable, an Xbox Media Remote, component cables, composite cables, Xbox Live Silver service, a month of Xbox Live Gold service, and an Xbox Live headset. The last configuration is the most expensive, the Elite. It includes a 120GB detachable hard drive, a wireless gamepad, an Ethernet cable, an HDMI Out/Cable, component cables, composite cables, Xbox Live Silver service, a month of Xbox Live Gold service, and an Xbox Live headset. The Core system costs Xbox 360 Core 279.99 USD and has a matte white exterior. The Core is no longer in production. It has been replaced by the Arcade, which costs the same as the Core and has the same exterior. The Premium system costs 349.99 USD and has a matte white and chrome exterior. The Elite system has a matte black and chrome exterior and costs 449.99 USD. two special editions of the console came out promoting Halo 3 and The Simpsons Movie.One of the most popular and well-known of the 360's features is the Xbox Live service. It is available in 37 countries and territories worldwide with Hungary, Brazil, and Poland to be added in the first quarter of 2007. The Xbox Live went under a major change with the launch on the Xbox 360. The most visible change is the inclusion of Xbox Live Silver and Gold services. The Xbox Silver service allows the use of most of the Live features excluding online game play capabilities whereas the Gold service includes all of the Xbox Live features.Xbox Live FeaturesGamertag- the user's name for use on the Live account, allows for messaging and locating.Xbox Live Marketplace- Contains Xbox Live Arcade (playable titles and rankings for those games), Video Marketplace (distributes television shows and videos to users in the U.S. only), and Xbox Live Pipeline (website that lets users view content that they downloaded via their accounts; currently redirects to standard Xbox site).Gamerscore- Directly linked to the amount of 'Achievement' points a user has earned in Xbox Live matches.Gamercard- Mini-profile that displays gamertag, gamerscore, reputation, and Gamer Zone.Xbox Live Diamond Card- Real-life discount card to be used in certain stores (only available to American 12-month subscribers).TrueSkill- Skill-ranking system that measures your 'skill' through ranked games.Xbox Live Vision Camera- Allows gamer pictures, video chatting (in and out of games), and still pictures.Xbox 360 Wireless Headset- Allows voice chatting (in and out of games), audio (for video chatting), and voice recognition for certain games.Games for Windows - LIVE- Lets non-Xbox platforms to connect to Xbox Live, though with less functions.Game with Fame- Connects Live users to celebrities who play on Live for some announced time.The Xbox 360 allows backwards compatibility for a number of the Xbox games. These games are played through use of a hard drive and an emulation hard drive.
