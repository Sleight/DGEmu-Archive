im curious, personaly i love sports games... mario golf, madden, nhl, nba live, theyre all great, but people r always saying how they hate sports games. :( jus curious y :(
Well, it's easy to see how someone could hate sports games. I mean, there's a new re-iteration for each sport every year, that generally isn't all that different, and as soon as the newest sports game is out the one that you got last year (or maybe even last month) would now get more worth if it were used as a frisby as compared to the value that a game store would give for it on a trade in. :( lol The responsibility really lies in the hands of the developers. They could very easily just push out another piece of junk every year because the hardcore fans would always buy it, or, they could really put some effort into it, and maybe, just maybe, people other than hardcore sports fans would buy it, and therefore, new sports fans could be born. This really goes on beyond just sports games. Games in every genre can be criticized with this same scrutiny. Are developers just going to stick Final Fantasy on a pile of dead fish and sell it because they know that their loyalists will buy it, or are they really going to put some effort into it and create something that all gamers will agree is a great gaming experience. This just gets stuck on sports games more often since there is generally less that can be changed in a sports game from year to year than can be changed in a RPG, FPS, RTS, etc. from year to year. I like sports games, but I generally prefer "fake" sports games (i.e. Mario Kart, F-Zero, Mario Golf, Mario Tennis, etc.). However, there are plenty of great "real" sports games out there too. I think many people just tire of developers who keep putting out junk year after year, and game retailers who rip us off on trade-ins. Also, as has been said by other posters, generally, if people don't enjoy something in real life (like sports), they won't enjoy it in a video game (unless it's something that can't be done in real life. :( lol). But that is where the developers have to step in and make those games that really redefine the boundaries of the genre. Hope this was informative, and not to long. :( In Christ's Love, Cody Alan Rushing
