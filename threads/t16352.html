It all depends on how serious you are about the game. The actual storyline is definitely easy - all you have to do is spend hours and hours leveling up your Pokemon and take advantage of type weaknesses to win against in-game opponents.Hardcore battling is a whole different story, since it requires comprehensive knowledge of the game mechanics, of which there are quite a number. When you're making a team, you need to choose the Pokemon, their movesets, and also coordinate their statistics and natures. Doing any of that can take a long time and requires quite a bit of planning (just search up kuisanagi's posts and look at everyone she's written on the matter - it's guaranteed to blow you away).So yeah, it depends on whether you believe the point of the game is simply to get through the storyline or to create a team for battling.
