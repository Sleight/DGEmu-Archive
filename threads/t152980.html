AatomicknightAug 30 2006, 07:53 PM
Liberate a mission? Do you mean liberate a panel? Oh well, here goes.The idea is to liberate all the Dark holes, which will then allow you to fight the boss(es) of the level. Once you do that, the liberation mission is complete and you may get a reward based on how quickly you finished the mission.In order to liberate a panel, you use one of your liberation team members and press A on a "Dark panel." That then launches you into a fight against a virus (or against a miniboss or combination of the two depending on what squre you choose to liberate). If you defeat the enemies within 3 turns, you "liberate" the panel and it becomes a normal panel that everyone can walk on. If you liberate in 1 turn, you liberate not only the panel that was selected, but every panel touching the team member used to liberate the panel. Depending on how many panels are around the team member, your space may be limited, expanded, or changed (if you there is a Dark panel behind you also, you get enemies on both sides). If you attempt to liberate a panel with a miniboss on it, you will fight only the miniboss. If you attempt to liberate a Dark hole, you will fight both a miniboss and other viruses. Liberating a Dark hole will also liberate all the panels around it.There are also item panels (for traps, items, zenny), bonuses (almost the same as items except with other good and bad possibilities), and keys (that can be used to unlock gates).Different team members have different abilities, which you will need in order to finish each mission.
