How to enhance your render quality.This tutorial will show you how to enhance the quality of the render you are using. This will make your renders just look overall better than when you started out with. This tutorial is for someone who has just started to use photoshop and already knows soem of the basic terms. But a person who has never used photoshop may be able to use this tutorial.Place the Render on your canvashttp://i105.photobucket.com/albums/m234/en...rendertut01.gifNext duplicate the render layer by pressing control+J.http://i105.photobucket.com/albums/m234/en...rendertut02.gifThen we must go to Filter>Blur>Gaussian Blur and set the blur to somewhere between 2.2 and 2.5.http://i105.photobucket.com/albums/m234/en...rendertut03.gifNow you should have something that looks like this.http://i105.photobucket.com/albums/m234/en...rendertut04.gifNow lets add an outer glow to your duplicate render layer.Double-click the duplicate render layer on the layers pallete.This will open the later styles, check mark outer glow and press ok.http://i105.photobucket.com/albums/m234/en...rendertut05.gifNow you will have something that looks like this.'http://i105.photobucket.com/albums/m234/en...rendertut06.gifDoesn't look that great yet does it? Well this last easy step will pull everything togethor. You must reduce the opacity of the duplicate render layer to about 20-40 percent depending on the render. This is mostly your own preference.Finished resulthttp://i105.photobucket.com/albums/m234/en...rendertut07.gif
