Ok, so we all hear about the Megaman X's and battle network and all that good stuff, but what im curious about, does anyone remember the Megaman Legends series?There was "The Misadventures of Tron Bonne." Where you played as Tron, a pirate trying to save her family from a loan shark at first but it turns into so much more as you uncover the true evil plot and do missions earning money through stealing, theiving, "digging", hijacking, all the while driving your robot the Gustaf, equipped with a cannon, bazooka, or gatling gun, and with 6 small servebots by your side while your out in the field, all of which can have different abilities that help both on or off of the battle field, (There were 40 servebots in total).Then it was continued in "Megaman Legends", Where you crash land on Kattalox Island and search for parts to fix your broken airship all the while digging deeper and deeper into the islands secrets reguarding it's ancient treasure, "the motherlode" and investigating the supposed disaster that would befall the island if it was ever unearthed. All the while protecting the island from Pirates, the same pirates from "The Misadventures of Tron Bonne" The eldest Brother Tiesel Bonne, the middle sister Tron Bonne, and the baby brother Bon Bonne, (who was really more like a giant robot.)And finally "Megaman Legends 2" the rarest of the series, which i unfortunitly have never played before but currently have one of the last copies of the game on order as soon as it's available.So basically, i hear about all these other Megaman games, but who remembers this small side step in the series? To me, I thought these three were the best to ever come from Capcom as far as the Megaman name was concerned.What do the rest of you think?
