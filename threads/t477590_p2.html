The fifth one is the golden armor that you acquire in Doppler's stage 1.

All sub-tanks, all heart tanks.

 

Doppler stage 1: Beat the mini boss. Once beaten and just exiting the mini-boss door, you need to reach the capsule for the golden armor with full health (but believe me, that section is a nightmare with tons of assholes shooting at you). There's a ride armor option at the complete top right of the screen. That will let you get to the capsule easily without having X take any damage.

Trick to get it is dash jump towards the right doing a leap of faith, but as you are about to jump fire a charged shot. If timed correctly, you should kill off the enemy hanging on the wall where you're going to land and let you climb up (or else, you will get hit and fall all the way to the bottom and need to refill your energy).

 

The capsule location is in the section under where the giant spike balls roll. At the first pit that leads to death, if you drop down and slide on the left side, you'll eventually land into a secret area with a capsule.

 

Golden armor is now yours:

It basically gives you all of the 4 optional chips' abilities in 1, and if you stand still, it will gradually heal X over time.

 

 

Also, if you want to obtain Zero's saber, rendering the game extremely easy:

During the game, make sure to go visit the optional section where you fight Vile and actually kill him off (basically last him with any special weapon and not the X-buster, causing him to explode instead of teleporting out).

 

Once you reach Doppler's stage 2, right after the water section, you will encounter a mini-boss door. Switch to Zero before entering (I would also recommend to charge Zero up to level 3 and waste your first 2 charged shots before entering the door).

Enter and fight the mini-boss with Zero. If you followed my recommendation, landing a blade slash should 1 hit kill it.

Instead of the mini-boss dying, he'll inch its way to Zero and blow up on him. X will come in and Zero will give you his blade. You can now charge to level 3 and throw Zero's blade.

 

Zero's blade with X damage guide:

- If you slash the enemy at close range, it's an automatic half HP taken off on any boss.

- If you slash from far and the blade lands on the enemy, the blade will do an extra double slash, dealing a total of 66% damage to any boss.
