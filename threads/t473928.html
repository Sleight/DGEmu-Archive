MMysterieMay 18 2013, 07:14 AM

NOT a good idea, you might want to stop using glass cleaner before it's too late. Glass cleaner strips the protective covering from discs, and can permanently damage a disc. Instead of glass cleaner, use clean, lukewarm water and the soft glasses cloth.

 

If this does not work, check the disc for scratches. Minor, barely-there ones shouldn't cause too much of a problem for the reader, but if the scratches are easily noticeable to your eyes, they could be what's disrupting the lens from reading it. If this is the case, then look for something called Scratch Out!. It's a disc repair solution that works the scratches out of the disc so that it becomes readable again. I'd suggest using a glasses-cleaning cloth for applying it, to avoid lint. If done right, it can work miracles.

 

However, if there is a scratch that goes so deep it damages the silvering in the center, then I fear you will have to get a new disc. the silvering is where the data is stored, and the slightest amount of damage to this lining can destroy a disc.

Good luck with your game, I hope you can fix it!
