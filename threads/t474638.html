KKuisanagiDec 16 2011, 09:46 AM
IIRC saving after beating the game takes you back to the save point right before the boss, and you can use a transporter that takes you back to Pulse. The battle system really opens up when you max out the Crystarium (it doesn't really take that long), which means unlocking 6 ATBs skills and all of the classes. This allows you make interesting Optima setups and switching them often to control what your party does...in fact, the endgame gameplay is basically constantly switching Optimas, and occasionally setting your awesome super-skill, and winning battles is pretty much always based around timing things right. Also buffing and debuffing is really crucial, as is healing, which is something other RPGs seem to never get right... Needless to say, the endgame content is also alot harder than the story stuff...maxing Crystarium and equips doesn't actually make you overpowered, but just barely qualified to take on the cool enemies and bosses. That's one thing that FFs do often get right =)
