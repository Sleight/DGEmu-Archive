KkharonalphaJun 25 2010, 08:16 PM
Square: Final Fantasy V & Secret of EvermoreI never got to play the original FF as a kid with an NES, so its Nintendo Hard difficulty ramp, at least to me, isn't compensated for by a nostalgia filter, and, in fact, my first exposure to a FF games was FFVII. It's just a shame that VII was, to be honest, kinda cruddy in terms of storyline and characters, and so while it was fun to play, it left me wanting more. So I found emulation and found the SNES FF games, and I fell in love with FFV as soon as the job system appeared, because it let me really, truly, play with my team exactly how I wanted it without having to take characters I didn't like just because of their abilities. I recognize FFVII was similar in that any character could use any ability, but FFV gave me the feeling of a classic RPG in terms of class choice, with the storytelling of an SNES-era console RPG. To this day, I still think FFV is the best Final Fantasy game, hands down.Secret of Evermore is a totally different style of RPG, and I just loved it for the flavor it had. Playing off the mechanics of Secret of Mana, you travel between 4 movie-history themed worlds as a huge B-movie nerd who only has his dog to back him up. Also the power of Alchemy! The game is just so much fun that I can't help but load it up every now and then.Enix: ActRaiser & E.V.O. The Search for EdenWith amazing games like Actraiser, E.V.O. Search for Eden, the Gaia trilogy, Dragon Quest, and many more, I have no easy time picking a favorite from the Enix catalog. This is only compounded by the fact that so many of them have so much promise, but then fail to deliver in some form or another. That's why, after all else, I have to give this one another tie, with ActRaiser and EVO both holding special places in my heart for giving gamers a very traditional style of game, namely they're both Platform games, and adding a twist to it: in ActRaiser, the god game mode tied in with the platforming, and in EVO the RPG-like evolution system allowed you to build the creature you wanted, even if said creature wasn't the coolest option in the game.
