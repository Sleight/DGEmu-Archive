EEnhasaJan 03 2007, 01:03 AM
There are so many awesome SNES games that it's hard to choose ten, but here are my favorites:1. Chrono Trigger: This is my favorite game of all time. The time travel concept is really well done, and all of the characters are interesting and useful. The graphics and sound are among the best on the Super Nintendo, and the game has very high replay value due to the New Game + feature and the existence of multiple endings.2. Final Fantasy IV: This is the first true RPG I played -- not counting the primitive NES RPGs like Dragon Warrior and Final Fantasy -- and it blew me away. The classes were permanent, but they really matched the personalities of the characters. FF4 is also one of the only games that ever made me cry.3. Final Fantasy VI: I was obsessed with this game when it first came out, and it's still a very enjoyable game. The only real downside is that with such a huge cast, it's hard to become attached to everyone.4. Breath of Fire II: I almost didn't buy this game after my experience with the mediocre Breath of Fire, but I'm glad I didn't make that mistake. This is still one of my favorite RPGs, even though the music and graphics are nothing special. I always liked the concept of the different anthropomorphic races, and the ability to combine people with Shaman was a nice touch.5. Earthbound: This is another classic that I almost missed. I saw the reviews for it in Nintendo Power and thought: "A modern RPG with crappy graphics? No way!" But despite my first impression, this game was really fun. If you want a break from serious games, this is the game to play. I also recommend the prequel (Mother) on the NES, if you can find a translated version.6. Final Fantasy V: I first played this game on an emulator a few years before it was released as part of Final Fantasy Anthology. The ability to customize characters is unparalleled, but it still retains the class system that I find so essential in a good Final Fantasy game. The only downside is the not-so-good story.7. Seiken Densetsu 3: I first played this game a few years ago, but I remember when Square first promised (and then reneged) to release this as a sequel to Secret of Mana. When I called, they said: "Play Secret of Evermore. It's better." They were wrong.8. Secret of Mana: I remember reading reviews for this game in Nintendo Power, and I knew I had to have it. The two years that it took Squaresoft to release Secret of Mana felt like an eternity. It was the second real RPG I'd played on the Super Nintendo -- not counting that piece of tripe known as Final Fantasy Mystic Quest. The real-time combat, colorful graphics, and brilliant soundtrack were a refreshing change. 9. The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past: I don't really count this as an RPG, but nonetheless, it ranks high on my list of SNES experiences. It was the third Zelda game I'd played. With this game, Nintendo went back to the format of Zelda 1 but improved and built upon it.10. Mega Man X: Ever since the first NES games, I've always been a fan of the Mega Man series, but this game was truly awesome. The power-up items littered throughout the levels kept you on your toes, and the soundtrack was great for a Mega Man game. Sometimes, I still find myself humming the tune from Spark Mandrill's stage.

NNathanderAug 18 2011, 08:34 PM
In no particular order1. Final Fantasy III/VIThe game that really helped get me back into RPGs. I was actually one of the people that played Final Fantasy VII before I played VI (and I didn't have a PS at the time, so I played Eidos' PC port of VII), and I still find VI to be the superior game. A lot of this is due to more diverse characters with their own unique skill sets on top of being able to learn magic, as well as the fact that Kefka is one of my favorite villains of all time.2. Donkey Kong Country 2The may not have been the greatest platformers, but I still love the DKC series. 2 happens to be my favorite, and a lot of that is also due to nostalgia reasons.3. Super Mario RPG: Legend of The Seven StarsAnother game I have a lot of nostalgic memories associated with (a friend and I beat it during Christmas vacation). It's an easy, easy RPG but also one of my favorites. I can't imagine living without that game.4. Earthbound As you can tell, there's probably going to be a lot of RPGs on this list since the SNES was a fantastic system for them. I wasn't one of the people who got to play Earthbound as a kid, but one who would go on to try it out later due to word of mouth. And it doesn't fail to deliver exactly what it promises: it's a quirky, fun, and often times strange look at an RPG, and one of my favorite games of all time.5. Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's IslandOne of my favorite Mario games of all time, in part because of how odd it is, or was, when it released at the time, as it moved away quite a bit from the standard Mario formula. 6. Legend of Zelda: A Link To The PastWhile it isn't my favorite Zelda game, you can't really debate that it's a classic and one of the best games on the SNES, as well as the Zelda series in general. The use of having two different worlds to expand the quest was neat, and the amount of items and sidequests are incredible. 7. ActraiserI'm not a big fan of sim-games, but in this game, where you play as a god helping to rebuild the world/pave the way for mankind and has platformer sections interspersed with it, it's an incredibly fun, enjoyable game.8. Dragon Quest VDragon Quest is one of my favorite RPG series, and V is my favorite in the series as a whole. It has some of the most fleshed-out and endearing characters, some utterly heartbreaking scenes, an incredible soundtrack, and a wonderful storyline. To me, it's a must play for any fan of RPGs.9. Contra 3I don't play too many shoot 'em ups, but Contra 3 is a classic and a must play. It has heavy and enjoyable combat, and the soundtrack and design is fantastic.10. Kirby Super StarI've always loved Kirby games, and this one is by far my favorite, a big reason for this being the selection there is in this collection.
