Let's observe the grammer in this sentence.'HI THIS PLACE IS ROCK THE WORLD'.First, you will note that the user typed in all capitol letters, making him not only seem like shouting, but like he believes the motto 'Caps lock is cruise control for cool.'It is not.This is a simple mistake that most newbies make on forums of MMOs. Typing in all caps is considered rude and unnessicary online, unless you are yelling.'Hi this place is rock the world.'Second off, you will notice the absence of punctuation that slows the sentence down. . The comma indicates that the speaker is taking a slight pause in the sentence, to help others digest the contents. For example, when you say 'Hey, look out!', you give a slight pause after the word 'hey', because that is the way normal people talk. In this case, we would put a comma after the 'Hi', to make it seem like we aren't trying to confuse someone.Now, some might argue that this is actually two sentences; 'Hi', and 'This place is rock the world'. However, because 'Hi' does not contain a subject and a verb, we must assume that it is not it's own proper sentence, and that I will conclude it is part of the original sentence. I acknowledge, however, that it seems misplaced.'Hi, this place is rock the world.'Now, you will notice that the poster has called 'this place' (which we will assume to be DGEmu.com) to be (is\be) 'rock the world'. Now, of course, something cannot *be* rock the world, because that means that you are being an action. When was the last time you tried to be a jump, for example? This is silly; you *can* jump, but you do not morph *into* the jump during the cycle of the leap. Therefore, we should change the wording of the sentence from the word 'is', and put an 's' after the word 'rock', to make it seem as if DGEmu.com is doing an action.Remember, kids; a sentence must have a noun and a verb. In this example, the poster simply has two subjects.'Hi, this place rocks the world.'Finally, we note that the sentence ends with a period instead of an exclimation. This detonates that the poster is using it in normal speech, in a slow and deliberate conversation. However, the all-caps from before detonate that he is yelling this in an exclamatory style. Therefore, we must put proper punctuation on the sentence in order to make it seem excited and exclaimed.'Hi, this place rocks the world!'Now that we have corrected the sentence, we may add something to give it an added flair, like the [ u ] and [/ u ] tags to underline the 'rocks the world' area, in order to make it stand out more.'Hi, this place rocks the world!'And now, we have a completed sentence.
