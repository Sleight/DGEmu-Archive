Ok, so here's my thinking on this topic.In December James Cameron's film Avatar will be released and just recently we saw what was supposed to be an preview of the finished product and story in the new theatrical trailer. James Cameron has made numerous claims saying that the digital 3D technology used in this film will be revolutionary and that it will be the best film of the year that it will be the most original story line in ages. I watched the trailer myself and honestly, I'm not impressed. I think it's unoriginal with a recycled story and the CGI used is no more impressive than the incredibly detailed characters in Transformers, or the 97% CGI Star Wars Episode 3.So what do you think? Will Avatar revolutionise cinema or is James Cameron a blowhard reject director who doesn't have an original thought.Personally I believe that James Cameron is an egotistical idiot. I enjoyed Terminator, True Lies and Titanic amongst others, but when I hear behind the scenes stories, James Cameron is an egotistical prick. When Arnold Schwarzeneggar tried to go for a role in Terminator, James Cameron (rather than audition him) took him out to lunch and tried to pick a fight with him and when Cameron realised that Arnie wanted to be the Terminator (and not Kyle Reese) Cameron said ok, left and made Arnie pay for the lunch.Then, as we know M Night Shymalan is working on The Last Airbender based on Avatar: The Last Airbender. And despite his film being based off of a source material that was using the name Avatar long before Cameron created his idea, Cameron has the hide to say to Shymalan "I will sue you if you use the name Avatar." Hence the reason why it's called The Last Airbender.Now for the story of Avatar, the idea is that a paraplegic soldier is given the chance to have his mind transferred to an Avatar body of a native species to an alien world, so he can find a diplomatic solution for getting rid of them so humans can harvest a precious mineral. This results in soldier falling in love with a native and inevitably fighting with them against the humans.And Cameron claims this to be an original story XDHow about Battle For Terra? Humans invade alien planet to take control. A lost human becomes friends with a native and ends up fighting with the aliens to defeat the humans.So far, Avatar is looking to be a bigger more bloated version of the same movie.And yes, I've heard the arguments about the fact that there is a soldier rolling around in a wheelchair. The scientists have enough technology to transfer minds from one body to another but can't fix a broken spine??It is said in the trailer "Complete your mission, and I'll make sure you get your legs back."What a great military? Instead of giving him his legs back right after the accident, they make him work for it because the military needs more paraplegics in the front lines showing that with a great amount of work you can get your legs working again at the small cost of destroying a native race :oMy vote goes to saying that Avatar will be mildly entertaining, with a ripped off storyline, not only from Battle For Terra but numerous other stories, CGI graphics that are no more impressive than any other movie coming out this year. I'm sure that with a budget of $200 million+ you can pay a visual effects company to make anything for you. The Na'vi look about as realistic as Gollum from the Lord of the Rings.In my opinion, Avatar won't be breaking any ground any time soon, it'll just be following the lead of every other film made over the last decade while Cameron's been on his break from feature films.
