Well, what do you know? This one is an English teaching game. Basically, you play as a typical loser, having a crush on a classmate who is going to attend Todai (Tokyo University). So desperate to get in Todai that our protagonist come to pray at a Shinto shrine and, after grudgingly donate 100 yen (his entire saves), got one kami-sama to accept to help him. The next day, Hajime woke up with three girls on his side, who turn out to be English teaching angels sent by kami-sama to be his home tutor (that's katekyoshi in Japanese => sounds like fun). I haven't met the blue hair one yet but apparently the blonde one will teach you words, the green idioms and the red grammar. Oh, and practically every sentence in the game is voiced, hence the big size, and it has a story mode too. You read through a chapter the way you read a visual novel and new English words, idioms and grammars will be available to learn. After you passed the test on those subjects, a new chapter will be unlocked. It's not that great of a game, but it's kind of fun, and since there's even a henshin sequence right at the first chapter; I guess it's worth the while.
