KKichiku Senshi RanceJun 30 2009, 11:09 AM
It might be some BS like SecuROM, too. Some of them mess with your DVD drive functionality, especially if you try to burn copy-protected games you torrented. Also, make sure (from Device Manager) that your drive is running in UDMA mode, not PIO. Once, for some reason, my drive went to PIO mode and my PC behaved in the exact same way your computer is right now. Is read speed just slow or is it even giving errors at times? If it's giving errors, then your drive might indeed be screwed... To check if any drive is PIO, Check all ATA controllers and make sure that in the Advanced Settings tab of every single ATA channel, any devices attached to any ATA ports are on Ultra DMA mode. If any device is PIO, and its type is ATAPI Cdrom, then that might be the problem. First try enabling UDMA and restarting, and if that fails, then uninstall your drive, then restart. After restarting, Windows will automatically detect your drive, reinstall it and if everything's OK it SHOULD start working in UDMA mode (full speed). My screenshots are from Win7, but they should look similar on both XP and Vista. EDIT: For XP, the controllers show up as "Primary IDE Channel", "Secondary IDE Channel" etc., and in the Advanced Settings tab, you're supposed to select "DMA if available" in Transfer Mode.
