The Shin Megami Tensei series, as opposed to Persona 3 (somewhat) and 4 also tend to have darker stories with focus placed mainly on the story and background. P3's dark atmoshphere and story somewhat resembles more of the SMT games rather than P4 which is has a more cheerier story and background. P3 and P4 have more emphasis on character development and interaction than the SMT games.

 

I also agree with OP in that the SMT games are way more difficult in terms of strategy than the P3 and P4 games with its challenging bosses. Even with the right setup in SMT battles in particular bosses can be tough to beat. In the P3 and P4 games if a team is done correctly (fusing personas with the right abilities, etc.) battles can be a breeze unless the characters are underleveled.
