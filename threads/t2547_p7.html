EEndymion_MallornOct 04 2014, 10:41 PM

Can I go back to the classics here?  Sorry, but I started on the Atari and the TRS-80, so I have two clear candidates.

 

First is of course, E.T. - the game that nearly killed the US game industry.  And after that, Pac-Man for the 2600.  Custer's Revenge usually gets mentioned too, but honestly?  It was meant to be awful, and stupid, so I'll let it take a pass.

 

Going up from there... it gets very hard for me to pick the stinkers out from the middle-ground.  See, I want to say Odell Down Under (since "Odell Lake" was actually based on the Odell Lake, and Down Under was based on some fever dream about the Great Barrier Reef), but I still love the graphics of that game.  It's a horrible game, of course.  But I love the look.

 

I'm going to also go for a few other low-hanging fruit here.  TMNT - the SNES one was bugged (I've seen a patch), but the DOS one was nearly impossible to beat.  As a fan of the TMNT, I couldn't stand it.

 

But now I'm going to go for a big one that I know people love.  Colonization.  Look.  Civ as a series is pretty good.  Some ups, some downs.  Colonization, on the other hand... just doesn't hold up to Civ.

 

Oh, and there are more, there are truckloads of stinkers out there.  But I think those are the worst overall.
