LLangilleSep 07 2008, 02:44 PM
It really depends on what you want in the game.With Age of Empires the places where you can place buildings is static, the land is quite rough graphically and it's fun to build defenses in it (because of the static placement of buildings). You also get a good historical game if you play the campaigns.With Age of Mythology, you get to control mythological units (such as hydra or Pegasus), the building placement is not static (you can place the buildings almost anywhere in any way on the map) and the land is very smooth graphically (which I personally don't like because I like having solid and obvious cliffs, like in AoE). Another interesting addition to this game is god powers, they can be quite fun to play with at times, but it can get annoying when you suffer sudden meteor strikes or hurricanes when you're just starting to build a lot of stuff. Finally, with AoM, you get to (as the name implies) play in mythological times, the game (loosely) follows the mythos of different countries in the (then) known world.
