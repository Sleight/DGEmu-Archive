The term "interactive stories" is a broad term for what can be considered contemporary games, i.e. stories in which you can play. A VN ironically is not really an "interactive story." Most VNs follow a strict storyline path with occasional divergent story paths depending on what it is. Phoenix Wright is a Visual Novel with Adventure Game mechanics, and 999 is an adventure game with Visual Novel mechanics. A real "visual novel" is exactly what it sounds like, it's a picture book written as a full 500 odd page novel. Those types of games are more like Fate/Stay Night, Higurashi no Naku Koro ni, and Clannad. Visual Novels don't really have you play anything beyond some story flags. Most "Visual Novels" are eroges (erotic games) and the appeal of this format is that you can go through most of the text to get to the hanky panky. These games are usually released under independent game companies and almost never get released outside of japan. The only real Visual Novel that I've played on a handheld that's been adapted in english by a well known company is Disgaea Infinite and honestly it's not all that great.
