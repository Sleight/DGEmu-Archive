KKirie MotobaAug 09 2012, 11:25 PM
That case, your DS is faulty then, probably an issue with the mainboard or a connection somewhere.

I salvaged a DS from my old job that someone threw in the garbage.
You could turn on the DS, but it freezes with a black screen at the top and white screen at the bottom.
That DS was also dropped into water (power connector had green gunk from chlorine drying)

Took it back home, and started swapping part with my other DS that had a broken hinge.

Turns out the DS I picked up had a defective top screen and a non-responsive touch panel.
The chlorine gathering was cleaned off easily with a Q-Tip and a bit of friction alcohol.
Ordered replacement parts, replaced, and everything was in perfect working order.

Before confirming that your mainboard is defective though...

Do as BR said.

Open the console, pull out the battery (obviously while unplugged from the wall).
Hold the power switch to on for about 5 to 10 seconds.

Put back the battery and check again.
