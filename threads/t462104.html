AanaiiraOct 29 2010, 05:07 PM
Now this may just be from personal experience, but homework has never been mandatory. Doing homework is encouraged, but not mandatory. It is generally accepted that you do homework to get good grades. You don't care about your grades? You don't have to do homework. What I'm basically saying is, any complaint about how homework is impacting your free time is moot. You have a choice to do homework. Neither the government or your teacher will hold a gun to your head and tell you to do it or else.As far as legality goes, the real question should be whether a government should have such blatant intrusions on civil rights. And the answer is no. Why? The government's duty is to protect citizens from harm. The only warrant for such an intrusion is if homework has a devastating effect on the majority of the population that there is no recourse but to ban it altogether, or restrict it such that only a few people have access.
