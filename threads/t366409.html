SspiralbondApr 25 2009, 09:33 AM
I seriously think that maybe you are being slightly too elitist for your own good when you say that superhero comics have nothing but filler and pretty drawings. Firstly, what's wrong with having these dramatic fight scenes inside superhero comics? Superhero comics can be related to the concept of romanticism, where the things that are drawn just appeal to the imagination of the reader. The raw epic feel a reader can get from these pages that you criticise provide a form of escapism from real life. I may be crossing a few boundaries by seeming so dramatic about my point of view, but it's the only way I can get my idea across. Also, it must be very bad coincidence that the comics you've read only show the more "shallow" side associated with superhero comics. There is actually depth in many superhero comics. I'm just going to draw a random example out of the air; X-men, which is quite popular among fans not only because of the powers they exhibit, but also because of the discrimination that the characters face, which can be seen as metaphorical for minority groups. Also, the "Ultimate" version of X-men has been compared to a superhero soap opera, in a good way, where the main focus is the character development. I'm not saying that they are all great though, some superhero comics do overdo things. I'm able to appreciate all kinds of comics, superhero or not, and if you actually start looking harder, you may find a superhero comic that you enjoy.
