Final Fantasy doesn't really have great villains.Zeromus (FF4) was a smart villain, and definitely evil... but because you don't find out about him until nearly the end of the game, you never really see him in action. Although he does get props for using Big Bang three times before you get to move in Hardtype, thus becoming one of the only difficult FF final bosses ever.Ex-Death (FF5) is so cartoony as to be laughable. He has no real motivations, no real personality... he's just evil. Boooooring.Kefka (FF6) has got some truly dastardly deeds under his belt, including successfully destroying the world, and he's got a great laugh. He's a really easy guy to hate, and that makes him a good villain. The problem is that his character development is limited to "he's just plain nuts", thus keeping him from being a great villain. That and thanks to the Quick-Vanish-Doom trick he's the only FF final boss you can slaughter in a single action without levelling for hours.Sephiroth (FF7) is way, way overrated. All he's got a big sword and mommy issues. Instead of having no development, his character development just makes no sense ("I just found out I may not be human, so I think I'm going to slaughter all my friends and develop an Oedipus complex"). Angsty bishies belong in anime, NOT in my video games!Edea (FF8) is... well, what the heck was she trying to accomplish anyway? All she does is provide drama for the Squall/Rinoa relationship. Rule number one of being a good villain: do not allow yourself to become a plot device.Kuja (FF9) I can't say much about, since I never finished FF9. But from what I saw he's got... cross-dressing. And that's pretty much it.Seymour (FF10) is standard FF fare... vaguely creepy, nihilistic, bare-bones development, and meets his end in a ridiculously easy boss fight.I don't play FF11, so I don't know who the villain is. But who cares about FF11? It sucks anyway.Shuuyin (FF10-2) is kinda creepy, especially in that one Crimson Sphere ("I told you to turn that thing off!"). To get any development for him, though, you need to engage in a whole lot of silly side-quests. He's still kinda dull, but I somehow doubt you're playing this game for the storyline anyway.Bottom line? FF villains kinda suck.
