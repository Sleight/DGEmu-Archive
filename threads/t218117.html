It's hard at first, but when you figure out his attack patterns, it is pretty simple. All i need was the skill that lets you stabs the ground and unleash energy on the ground and elixirs/potions. Unlimited combo and trinity were not necessary for me. After i block his charge and slash attack, I immediately slash him 4x times and then use the stab the ground skill. The great thing about that skill is that it knocked him into the air while delivering massive damage. It also causes you to become airborne, which is a good thing in my case, because that's when I utilized air combos until he teleports. When he tries to slash me into the air, I use the recovery skill to stand upright and when he teleports to do air combos on me. I repeatedly slashed which caused him to run into my slashes. :) There are two ways to get around "Heartless Angel":1) let it hit and use elixir/potion and repeatedly press triangle because he will charge and slash right after Heartless Angel. This is one of the bug in the game...when you use elixir/potion and there is a reaction button, if you press the reaction button, the elixir/potion will be canceled, but the effects remains. In other words, you can use elixir/potion and block Sephiroth's reaction attacks easily. 2) have double jump and high jump and get to Sephiroth before he unleash Heartless Angel and use air combos.I'm sure that almost everybody did the same for Sin Harvest Angel AKA Meteor: RUN LIKE HELL. XD After Sephiroth uses Meteor, he will repeat some of his patterns from the beginning. (reaction slash, teleport, slash you into the air, etc)However, if anyone is interested in different ways to beat Sephiroth, just look for it on youtube.com. XD
