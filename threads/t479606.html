If your TV really has no Composite input, then most probably your Component input is a hybrid. (please refer to your TV instruction manual)

 

If it is the case, then you can plug the red and white into the respective ports of the component, and the yellow would go into the green port.

This is an image from Nintendo's troubleshooting guide for the Wii on that scenario:

 

Again, make sure with your instruction manual that the green will take the yellow for composite, not all TVs support it.
