BBishFeb 10 2013, 03:50 PM

It depends. It has its purposes.

For example: You're mid-lane against a champion whose intention it is to push the lane, push you back to your turret, and then go roaming into other lanes. To adapt to that if that strategy was giving your team trouble, you'd probably want to max out your E first because it's your only form of AoE clear without your ultimate. That way you clear a wave faster than you normally would, making them come back to defend, one would hope.

 

There are two ways to utilize Ryze's passive, which, for anyone who doesn't know, reduces your cooldown on all your other spells by one second when you cast a spell. So cooldowns and spell order are very important to manage on him.

Spamming your spells at once: QWEQ is a very effective burst combination on squishies.

But for sustained damage; tanks and the like, you want to be weaving in other spells between Q casts. Q-W/E-Q-E/W-Q-R-Q-W/E-Q-etc. R as approproate; if the enemy team is in a clump, Q-R-Q-W/E-etc, pop that whilst the first Q is in flight and it'll gain the added effect of your Ulti. W/E as appropriate. Hopefully do the E first because it reduces their magic resist meaning they'll take more damage from you, but W for the snare.



tl;dr  when in doubt, just mash your QWER keys.

It still works.

I'm not too knowledgeable on the ins and outs of Udyr but, as far as I can tell:

Phoenix is typically preferred.

Tiger benefits greatly from building AD, but the problem is that a lot of Udyrs don't build straight AD damage. Their role in team fights is usually to run around their team stunning them. For that they need to be in the middle of their team and therefore be tanky.

Phoenix is more suitable to this role while you're out of Bear and Turtle stance. You get AoE damage. AP and AD boost. Plus some of the items you would build on Udyr (Wit's End for example) provide attack speed, which synergises with Phoenix nicely, in that that third strike you do that'll do the extra damage will come faster.

Man, my thought on Teemo huh.

I used to play Teemo a lot. Though this is a while back so all I can do is base it on past experiences and theorycraft his current state.

Let's run through his stats and skills.

Q - Blinding shot. Causes enemy Autoattacks to miss for up to 2.5 seconds depending on skill level. Scales off of AP. Great anti-carry ability, good utility.

W - Move quick. He... moves... quick.

E - Toxic Shot. Passive poison on all his Autoattacks. Scales off of AP.

R - Noxious Traps (AKA 'shrooms). Stealth traps that explode and slow when run over. Gives a tiny amount of vision, but can provide great map control. Shrooms will NOT STACK. They only refresh the DoT duration, so spread them out.

Nothing exceptional about his stats, except that his base Attack Speed is the highest in the game, IIRC.

Quick tip: Q resets his AA animation, so if you AA, Q then AA you can get decent damage off.

 

AP:

  • Q scales well and is your main nuking ability. Will chunk a massive amount of the enemy carry's health.
  • Depending on how you strategise your mushroom placement, deals great damage and has great utility.
  • Liandry's Torment does synergise well. An AA into a Q will DoT them heavily (but will not proc off of Toxic Shot), as will them running into a shroom.
  • But I feel like the problem with AP Teemo is his mushrooms. If you're a wizard at placing them, you're going basically be able to remote-poke the team down to nothing health. If you can engage on them after that, great. If you can't, what do you do in teamfights? Run into 5 of them, plop a mushroom down, and then run away? Sure, if you want to die horribly. Teemo is squishy, so you have to skirt around the edge of battle Q'ing and auto-attacking who you can. If you can bait them into your shrooms, great, if not, you've just lost one of your 3 major damage dealing abilities.

 

AD:

  • Not the greatest AD carry, but Q is pretty good to make them useless if they're heavy AA'ers like Vayne.
  • Toxic Shot is nice, but nothing to rely on.
  • 'shrooms still provide good damage against squishies and good utility.
  • AD carry build works.
  • But one of the greatest uses for AD Teemo is split pushing. He has Stealth, he's fast, he basically has free wards, so it's hard to pin him down. If the enemy doesn't have the best wave clearing abilities, this is a very effective strategy.

 

There's pros and cons to either build; it just depends on what you need him for.

Personally, I don't like AP Teemo. When you're good, you're great. When you're not, it's really obvious how useless you are. I like great AP Teemo play, but you just so rarely see it.
