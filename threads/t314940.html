For those of you who don't know me, (almost all of you mods), I come from a different message board. The rules are much stricter there, particularly on those one would call 'eggs'. This is done in three ways:1) Every time someone registers and verifies their e-mail account, they are allowed a 'provincial', or temporary, account. This account is limited to ten posts a day and only one topic. After forty-eight hours (two days) of registering, they are allowed to post more, but not an unlimited amount. (see two)2) They are then declared a 'new user', and are upgraded to thirty posts, three topics a day. Over time, they gain 'karma' at a rate of once a day, although moderations/reports reduce karma by three, five, or ten, depending on the severity of the offense. The more karma they have, the more they can post, to the point where, at seventy-five karma, they gain unlimited posting.3) Eggs are, in fact, treated as nothing. Upon registering, I once grew angry at the moderators, and attempted to start an uprising at ten karma. My first attempt got me banned for a month (known as Purgatory), my second for life. Later, at two-hundred karma, I did the same thing three times. I only lost eight karma and a week off the site (known as a warning).Based on the fact that the site has been successful, for the most part (the mods are, unlike here, supreme overlords who abuse their power at a whim, but they do keep the forums intelligent), I would suggest implanting rule one and three in this forum.So why would you justify these kinds off rules?First of all, this site's main board is in terrible condition, in my opinion. Spammers who break the rules within minutes of registration lose GPs; so what? If they don't want to follow the rules to get their download, they sure as hell won't want to start following them after they have to make an additional two-dozen topics to get what they want. Better to immediately let their accounts cool out for two days than to have to keep deleting their topics. And if that doesn't solve anything, just ban them. It's clean, efficient, and simple; a dozen people register for these forums every day, one or two more leaving shouldn't matter.Second of all, I'd like to make a personal complaint about topics. I do not wish to use me as an example, but about half the posts in my Iraq War topic are totally negligent of my initial argument. That said, most of the people ignoring my point were eggs. I'm not saying we should ban people for fleeing debates, or for not responding correctly, or for using Ad hominem attacks that border on flaming, but someone who totally ignores the initial part of the topic, they should get reported for spamming. Finally, something that has much less to do with this, I'd like to comment that certain topics on the General Boards are becoming uncounted de facto polls rather than actual topics. For example, the 'Snow versus Hail topic, I only recorded that less than five percent of the posts were responses to other users. Not ranting about that, but isn't that a disturbing trend? One hundred and two posts, and only five people who don't automatically add their answers?
