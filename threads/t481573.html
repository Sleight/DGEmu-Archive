Eureka Seven is my all time favorite anime. It has mecha battles, well developed characters and a cute romance story with the two MCs. It was the first anime I ever really got into and it remains one my favorites. Other anime in my top favorite list are: 

- The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya

- Seitokai Yakuindomo

- High School Dxd

- The World God Only Knows

- Kore wa Zombie Desu ka?

 

For manga, Monster Musume no Iru Nichijou is among my favorites list. Pretty obvious if you see my Sig. It does have a lot of adult content, but the comedy and the way they treat the whole "Monster Girls being integrated into human society" is just plain fun. The anime adaptation of MonMusume is going to be released next season which I'm looking forward to. I used to enjoy Naruto, One Piece and Bleach till I started branching out into more different manga. Other manga that I enjoy are Franken Fran, Molester Man, Densha Otoko, Renai Shimasen Ka? and One Punch Man.
