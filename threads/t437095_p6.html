KM.' timestamp='1338093842' post='5017437 wrote:


... Anyone have any good indie recommendations? Anything is fine as long as it's not crappy radio-friendly pop tunes.


You might need to get a little more specific. Technically, indie isn't so much a genre as it is an indication of the music's origin, and it isn't really fair to describe a group as "indie" and expect a definite sound. For instance, I could technically link you to a group that plays Megaman-inspired rock operas or some street performers who sing barbershop-style tunes while pretending to be robots - or an electro swing group, to keep the robot theme going - and all of those acts are technically indie. Then there's subsets that get more genre-y, like indie rock (though that's really more indie pop rock) or indie blues played by a Japanese guy on a cello.

Granted, one could argue that the "indie" label is often a guarantee for something that doesn't peddle to the tried-and-true (read: safe and boring) tropes of mainstream music, but even so, there's a lot of music that can fit under that umbrella.
