Broswers on the ds are atrocious. They need that ram expansion to render the pages a bit properly. You might want to try the browser built into dsorganize. It is the best you can get with only a slot-1 car, but it is still bad.So far there has been only one yahoo messenger for the ds. However, it was very buggy and most of the links are pulled, because it contained a bug that got it stuck at the loading screen... If you have an msn account, you might want to try beup. I haven't tried it in a while, but when I tried it a few years back, it worked pretty well.I don't really have tried voip homebrew but I do know that some exist. Do note though that because of the bad microphone and the disastrous wi-fi speeds of the ds, the quality will be quite bad.
