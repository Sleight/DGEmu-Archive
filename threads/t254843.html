FFloyd5642Oct 02 2015, 02:46 PM

When I was young, I had a Nintendo Entertainment System. There a few games that scared me, and these spring to mind. Keep in mind; I was poor, Super Nintendo was out, most of my games came from resellers like Funco Land. Here is a list of games that scared me and a reason. Names may not be accurate.

  • Sesame Street Counting Adventure Explosion: This game had this angry moon and scary music that would berate you if you got a question wrong.
  • Dr. Chaos: I didn't even have instructions for this one, so the basic controls eluded me. For instance, it is a menu game that you play by selecting different options and finding items in rooms. I didn't know how to change the menu options, so I would get chased by these indestructible monsters after you turn around in a room five times. It's sort of like Goonies II.
  • Dash Galaxy in the Alien Asylum: The cover looked kind of cool, but this game is actually kind of bad. I don't know if there is an ultimate goal to this game, and I don't really care. It certainly made me cry...
  • Startropics: Now this game is quite a gem, but I was seven, and was unskilled. So what happened was; the music change when you reach the cobras really freaked me out. I never reached the end of this one, but I did get to the chapter with Captain Bell's ship.

As for games that elicited an emotional response, I guess I can try to think of a few, but I may not have played them when they came out.

  • Final Fantasy VII: AERIS DIES!!! I guess that would have made me sad had I played it, but I didn't have a Playstation.
  • The Legend of Zelda Majora's Mask: "Duuuuude, did you see the moon? Gonna crush everyone in three days." That sort of thing with the inevitability of destruction really got to me, and the atmospheric music didn't really alleviate my feelings. Final Hours a.k.a. the song that plays during the final 6 hours of game time is really haunting.
  • Donkey Kong Country 2 Diddy's Kong Quest: Fantastic game. This one made me laugh once I finally got through Toxic Tower. I was laughing at the fact that Donkey Kong was strung up and getting shot at and stuff. I guess I was into Schadenfreude as a kid.
  • Donkey Kong 64: When I was running out of steam on that game, I starting cursing the day I bought it. It's just too much stuff to collect.
  • Mystical Ninja Starring Goemon a.k.a. Goemon 5: This game... THIS GAME. Man it made me laugh.
