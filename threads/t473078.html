KKula DiamondORIGINAL POSTJun 26 2011, 06:22 PM
Someone here mentioned this game few months ago, so I thought there might be some interest in it, that and the fact that words of this game awesomeness need to be spread.I liked the idea of the original(Alice/madness), but was never a fan of the game, control ruined the poor thing and I never got to finish it. Still, I had hope for Madness Returns. Picked it up last week and the first thing to noticed is the wonderful art direction, few steps after the opening cutscene and I realized that this is much like a Tim Burton movie, no matter how bad it turn out to be, I wouldn't regret watching it.Been playing it for sometime now, and things only kept getting better. Solid and fun 3D platforming full of hidden things invisible routes and secrets, that's extremely rare outside of Nintendo games nowadays. Had no problems with the camera while free(it usually is during combat and always free in platforming sections), when you look-on, it's the same z lock-on from Ocarina of Time, so it has some weird placements at times. The game looks long(took me forever to reach chapter 3, out of 5 chapters I think), which is good for a single player only game. Combat is varied enough once you get your full gear, with the variety between enemies and the frequency in which new ones are introduced, it only gets better. Didn't expect "decent" combat in a 3D platformer.Also the way Alice hair moves(thanks to NVIDIA's PhysX) is mesmerizing:<object width="425" height="355"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/dzCMnss0Tco&rel=1"></param><param name="wmode" value="transparent"></param><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/dzCMnss0Tco&rel=1" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" wmode="transparent" width="425" height="355"></embed></object>I spent quit some time bouncing her around and staring at the hair, I'm starting to grow a hair physics fetish. The various beautiful dresses never fail to amaze too. Over all I loved the game, it's not the best of it's kind but it have been a while since I played a game with nice none on-rail 3D platforming(I'm looking at you Uncharted and post Core Design Tomb Raider).As for the reason for making this topic, looks like most major gaming sites have been badmouthing this game. Maybe its classic 90s platforming design proved to be too much for those used to their hands being held throughout the game. That and it not being made by Bioware and called Dragon Age 2 is the reason why it's getting bad reviews. Seriously, though, it's irritating how a game with a critics metascore in the 60s(360 version has the most reviews so far) and get an average user score in the 90s(PC version which is obviously what you should play this game on... If you have a PhysX enabled vga card).So... yea, IF YOU EVER HAD AN INTEREST IN THIS GAME, DON'T LET BAD REVIEWS HOLD YOU FROM GETTING IT, THE GAME IS EVERYTHING I THOUGHT IT WOULD BE AND MORE.
