(all versions of ez flash play ds games, but i am only posting guides for IV and V.EZFlash IV TutorialThere are currently 3 EZ flash available, the EZ Flash IV Phat, EZ Flash IV Compact, Ex Flash IV deluxe, 1. Plug in your microSD card to your microSD->SD adapter (Typically included with microSD), and insert the card into your SD card reader on your computer. Make note of the drive letter windows gives to the drive. (For example, mine is set to X:\.)2. Open "My Computer" on your desktop or start menu, and right click on your card drive. Click 'Format'. Choose FAT (not FAT32) Formatting and click OK.3. Download the newest EZ4 Kernel. Kernel PageYou'll need to extract the "ezfla_up.bin" file from this archive.4. Copy the EZ4 Kernel file, "ezfla_up.bin" to the root (top) directory of your microSD card. Do not use the EZ4 Client to do this.5. Remove the microSD card and insert into your EZFlash IV. Insert into your DSLite *without* the EZPass3.6. Hold the R trigger as you turn on your DSLite. If you have your DS set to go to the menu before running a game, you'll need to choose 'Start GBA Game'. Hold the R button down until you see the kernel updating process begin. Do not turn the power off until the update process reaches 100%. It should reset by itself when finished.7. Reinsert your microSD card to your computer, and delete the "ezfla_up.bin" file from the card.8. Download the EZ4Client software.Download Extract the .zip to any location. Then you will run into a problem, the ez flash team is busy on the ez flash v, and the client is outdated and to play newer roms you will need to update it manually, inside the zip you just download is a folder called sysbin, inside that folder is a txt document named romlistds, you need to replace it with this, ftp://killerbong.hd.free.fr/romlistds.txt, simply go to this website click on save as txt document and then simply replace it with the one in your sysbin folder that you unzipped.9. Open the EZ4 software, click 'Config', and change the "Send Path" to your microSD Card's path. (e.g, "X:\DS\")10. Click 'Open' to select your NDS file.11. Click 'Send' to write it to your card. It is necessary to move the roms/programs to the card in this manner so that EZ4 Client may patch it accordingly. Do not just drag and drop backups from your computer to the card. They will not work properly. 12. Remove the microSD card from your PC and insert it into your EZFlash IV cart, and insert the cart into the GBA side of your NDS.13. insert the NoPass into the DS side of your NDS14. Power on the DS! It will show the NDS logo and warning screen, and then will boot to your EZFlash IV. From there, just select the game or file you wish to play.\Extra GBA Union.1. Write the GBA game which you wanna to link to minisD/microSD with EZ4Client (for exsample: 0140_-_Gyakuten_Saiban_(J).gba)2. Write the NDS game which correspond the GBA game to miniSD/microSD with EZ4Client, option " Link with GBA game" must be checked. ( for exsample:0186 - Gyakuten Saiban - Yomigaeru Gyakuten (JP) (M2).zip)3. Enter the GBA mode, format 256M/384M Norflash by press Start then A.4. Enter the SD card in GBA mode, choose the GBA game which you want to link, press Select then A, wait the write procedure finish.5. Restart DS in to NDS mode and excute the NDS game. if the link works, you can free choose any chapter at the beginning.Point: 1. NDS game which need to link must check the "Link with GBA game" option. 2. the GBA game which need to link must be wroten on the first position of 256M/384M norflash.EZFlash VFormat your MicroSDIt's always a good idea to start with a freshly formatted MicroSD card: * Insert your MicroSD into your PC. * Double-click "My Computer". * Right-click your MicroSD drive and choose "Format". * Under the "File system", select FAT or FAT32. * Click the "Start" button. The EZ-V ShellExample of MicroSD card containing Shell folder.EnlargeExample of MicroSD card containing Shell folder.The Shell is part of the EZ-V Operating System and is required for it to run.Included in the Shell are: * Graphic files (*.bmp) that make up look and feel of the user interface. * Configuration files (*.ini) that change the way the user interface works. * Save List file (ezsave.lst) - a database that tells the EZ-V what savegame settings to use for each game. The Shell and the Save List file are regularly updated, so check back every so often to make sure you are running the latest version.[edit]Installing the Shell * Visit the EZ-V software update discussion Here * Download the latest version of the Shell. Currently there are two user interface themes available - Standard and Vista. * Unzip to the root of your MicroSD card. Your MicroSD should now contain a single folder called "Shell". * Download the latest version of the Save List * Unzip "ezsave.lst" into the "Shell" folder on your MicroSD. Overwrite the existing one if prompted. The EZ-V LoaderThe Loader is the EZ-V Operating System. It's a good idea to update to the latest Loader for new features and bug fixes.[edit]Installing and updating the LoaderExample of MicroSD card containing Shell folder and Loader update fileEnlargeExample of MicroSD card containing Shell folder and Loader update file * Visit the EZ-V software update discussion Here * Download the latest version of the Loader * Unzip "ez5upldr.bin" to the root of your MicroSD card * Insert your MicroSD into your EZ-V * Insert your EZ-V into your DS and turn it on * Touch the health screen warning * In the DS Main Menu, while holding down R, launch the EZ-V * When the EZ-V splash screen appears, press R again to update the Loader * Wait until it finishes updating then press A to continue. * Your Loader is now updated After updating, you can delete "ez5upldr.bin" from your MicroSD Add games (ROMs) to your MicroSDThe EZ-V supports two types of ROMs - Clean and Hybrid.Clean ROMs are identical copies of the software found on the original cartridges.Hybrid ROMs are clean ROMs that have been modified by the EZ-V Hybrid Tool. This program is used to improve compatibility between ROMs and the EZ-V. It also adds additional features such as trimming (make file sizes smaller).As a rule of thumb, use clean ROMs. If for some reason it doesn't work, try using the EZ-V hybrid tool. Adding clean ROMs to your EZ-V * Insert your MicroSD into your PC * Drag and drop your "game.nds" file to your MicroSD Running gamesYou are now ready to play some games: * Insert your MicroSD card into your EZ-V * Insert your EZ-V into your DS * Touch the health screen warning to skip it * In the DS Main Menu, launch the EZ-V * In the EZ-V menu, highlight a game using the D-Pad and press A to launch! Image of MicroSD card containing save-game filesSavegame files are stored on the EZ-V and transferred to your MicroSD card next time you load the EZ-V menu. You will find them stored in a folder called "SAVE". It's a good idea to regularly backup your "SAVE" folder to your PC.[edit]EZ-V SettingsThere are a few extra items you should be aware of. These are accessible from the EZ-V menu:[edit]The Help ScreenAccess this by pressing START. It contains some simple instructions and the version number of your EZ-V Loader.[edit]The Settings ScreenAccess this by pressing L and R. Here you can change language and MicroSD speed settings.The MicroSD Speed setting sets how fast the EZ-V expects data to be read from your MicroSD. This affects a game's loading time and can alsoaffect a game's stability and compatibility. The speed setting can be changed from 1 (fastest) to 12 (slowest) or set to auto.Here are recommended speed settings for some brands of MicroSD cards: * PNY safety=4, fastest=3 * Speed setting based on 1GB Japanese manufactured version. Avoid the Taiwanese PNY 1GB version because it suffers stability problems. * Kingmax safety=8, fastest=6 * Sandisk safety=12, fastest=10 * Kingston *Taiwan* safety=7 fastest=5 * Kingston *Japan* safety=3 fastest=2 If a game doesn't load, try increasing the MicroSD speed setting. Remember that increasing it makes it slower.[edit]Manually configuring savegame settingsInside the Shell folder on your MicroSD is a file called "ezsave.lst". This is a database that tells the EZ-V what savegame settings to use for each game.Only adjust your savegame settings if the game is not saving correctly.If you play a game that is not defined in this database, you will need to manually adjust the savegame settings: * Highlight the ROM in the EZ-V menu * While holding down L, press X or Y to cycle through the various options * Run the ROM, test saving and loading. * If it doesn't work, reset and try a different savegame setting EZ-Flash V Triple-packmake sure your EZ-Flash V has firmware 1.36 or later installed.GBA ROM supportThe EZ-Flash V Triple-pack features 256mbit of internal memory (NOR) to store a single GBA ROM. Savegame information is stored on the EZ-Flash V Triple-pack and can be backed up to the EZ-Flash V microSD card from the EZ-Flash V loader. Once a ROM has been copied to the EZ-Flash V Triple-pack, it can be used as a standalone GBA cartridge. Adding a GBA ROM to the Triple-pack * Download a recent version of EZ4-Client and unzip it to your PC * Double-click EZ4_client.exe to start the program * Click the 'Configure' button * Change the send path to your SD card reader drive * Click 'OK' to exit the configuration screen * Click the 'Open' button. This will display the ROM file chooser window. * Highlight your GBA ROM and click the 'Open' button * Check the 'ROM Name' text field in the top left hand corner. Enter a name if this text field is blank. * Ensure the 'Add Save Patch' checkbox is ticked * Click the 'Send button'. The ROM will patched and copied to your MicroSD card. It will appear as 'ROM name.gba' * A folder called 'SAVER' will also appear on your MicroSD card. This can be safely deleted via explorer. * Remove the MicroSD from your PC and insert it into your EZ-Flash V * Insert the EZ-Flash V and EZ-Flash V triple-pack into your Nintendo DS and turn it on. * Skip the health warning and launch the EZ-Flash V menu * Highlight 'ROM name.gba', hold down R and press SELECT. A dialogue box will appear saying "Write ROM? Y/N". * Press A to confirm or press B to cancel. Wait about a minute for the ROM to be written to the Triple-pack. * Once complete, hold down R and press A to start the game! Once a GBA ROM has been written to the Triple-pack, it will remain until a different GBA ROM is transferred.You can now also run the GBA ROM from the DS main menu by choosing 'Start GBA game' Backing up GBA savegames to the EZ-Flash V MicroSD * Load the EZ-Flash V main menu * Hold down R and press X. A dialogue box will appear saying "Backup Saver? Y/N" * Press A to confirm or press B to cancel * A directory called 'GBASAVE' will be created on the MicroSD card and your GBA savegame will be copied into it. Note that firmware 1.36 contains a bug where GBA savegame backups do not automatically have the .SAV extension added to the end of the filename. You will need to manually add '.SAV' to your savegame file if you want to copy it back to the EZ-Flash V Triple-pack. Writing GBA savegames to the EZ-Flash V Triple-pack * Load the EZ-Flash V main menu * Highlight a GBA savegame (usually stored in the 'GBASAVE' directory) * Hold down R and press Y. A dialogue box will appear saying "Load Saver? Y/N" * Press A to confirm or press B to cancel * The savegame will be copied to the EZ-Flash V Triple-pack Note that firmware 1.36 contains a bug where GBA savegame backups do not automatically have the .SAV extension added to the end of the filename. You cannot send a savegame to the EZ-Flash V Triple-pack until you have added '.sav' to the end of the filename. Keys for Triple-pack * R+A: Launch Triple-pack GBA mode (req. firmware 1.36 or greater and EZ-Flash V Triple-pack) * R+SELECT: Copy GBA ROM to Triple-pack (req. firmware 1.36 or greater and EZ-Flash V Triple-pack) * R+X: Backup GBA savegame from Triple-pack to MicroSD (req. firmware 1.36 or greater and EZ-Flash V Triple-pack) * R+Y: Copy GBA savegame from MicroSD to Triple-pack. [edit]Keys while running backups * L+START+SELECT: Return to EZ-Flash V main menu (req. firmware 1.36 or greater, hybrid ROM with reset patch enabled)
