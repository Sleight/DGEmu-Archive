Games only, pretty much. Sure, i do also read books, and watch a movie or two once in a while, but apart from that, the PSP performs the other functions pretty poorly. The music player is pretty rudimentary, and while it has some kickass visualization, the rest of the interface is quite lacking (i mean, you can't even scroll through artists or albums! That's basic if it is to replace a dedicated music player. Hell, even crappy cell phones do better). Also, big as it is, the PSP is just way to inconvenient to have in your pocket with a pair of headphones sticking out the side of it. The browser is also quite crappy, and of much lower quality than a cell phone browser. The clunky scrolling controls (why analog AND d-pad? Simply analog would've been perfect) together with the fact that it only works with wi-fi (while a cell phone with gprs/edge/3g has internet access everywhere) spell total disaster for anyone who wants to regularly use the PSP for web browsing (and don't even get me started with the text input method!). And i don't use the photo storage function cuz it has no built in camera, it's too much trouble to take the pics on one device and them transfer them to another.Also, i'd like to point out, despite all this, i still feel the PSP is an amazing device. The functions it's supposed to perform (i.e. PLAYING GAMES!) it does magnificently. Failure of these add-ons really shouldn't be considered at all.
