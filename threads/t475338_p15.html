So I played a bunch of Xover, and it really is a TCG.

 

The whole part people were complaining about it ruining Megaman, the run and jump and shoot scene + skip button at a boss, and that guy that said the game can be made in 2 hours with his Flash game, that's only a small portion of the game.

Reality is that all those sections are just there to remove the monotonous clicking of a TCG game such as Ayakashi, and give it somewhat some sort of gameplay.

 

You collect cards with different rarity levels, level up your cards by merging them, and use those cards to power up Xover. The cards have elements, which are stronger / weaker to others just like in Megaman games.

The cards you collect are content from all Megaman series: Classic, Classic Game Boy, X, Battle Network, Zero, ZX, Star Force, Legends.

 

Each Area, once you clear all 4 stages + boss stage, you get a new armor. First and second area are the bosses from X, Area 3 is classic Megaman bosses (just beat Cutman). My Xover is at level 11, need to continue leveling him up. He can 1 shot area 1 bosses though, which is cool.

 

And then you join with friends to gain daily Zennies, and participate in battle arenas against other players or team battles with others. It really is a social game in the end, just like other games.
