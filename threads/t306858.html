kikeq' post='3906128 wrote on Jan 29 2008, 12:57 PM:
I dont know what mass effect is, can somebody explain me, please?
A futuristic space Shooter RPG. Sometime in the future, humans found some ruins of an ancient race while excavating Mars. This lead to the discovery of the Mass Relay system, which are basically terminals that can shoot a spaceship 100's & 1,000's of light years faster than light (the game calls it FTL, Faster-Than]-Light travel.)You are (eventually) the commander of an advanced space warship flying around the entire Milky Way Galaxy trying to stop a rogue Spectre (person/alien the Intergalactic Government gives unlimited power to take care of matters than can't be handled diplomatically.)You can literally explore dozens of worlds around the galaxy. You can choose a male or female character, and the face is fully customizable (like Elder Scrolls games, but just a human race, no alien species.) You can choose to be proficient in guns, techniques, or magic (called Biotics.)Mass Effect is kinda like KOTOR, in that you can choose to be good, evil, or somewhere in between. Different options in dialogs/alignments give different responses (and a different ending, from what I've heard. Got the red-ring-of-death on my 2nd play-through being evil. o.O )Hope this helps you, You should definitely look into getting this game... I HIGHLY recommend it. ^_^
