That really depends on the game.Normally PopCap releases games in a genre known as "casual" which appeal to those who aren't really into mainstream gaming but want to play a video game or two for relaxation. A typical casual game has low system requirements, is easy to pick up and play, and generally has a very cheery or even childish atmosphere (or sometimes educational too such as bookworm deluxe).Now Plants Vs. Zombies was definitely something special in that it somehow appealed to ALL gamers. Even I bought myself a copy off of steam as did my friends. Of course I am putting aside the stereotype that gamers will buy ANYTHING if it has zombies or ninjas in them. I was impressed at how it got such high ratings on all the major review sites like IGN and Gamespot (I have never seen that before).On a side note, your teacher didn't actually have to give away his/her serial number either. Everyone can download the full game themselves and play it free for 60 minutes (and then beg their parents to buy it for them if they like it).
