With the way that most of these clone consoles are I would say that the timing of when the n64 clones start coming would depend on when exactly the patent expires. From there it could be days or months depending on if they have the device ready to rock ahead of time and are just waiting for the patent to expire for release.

On a different note most of these new clones are based on ARM processors instead of the original hardware So they are basically emulator set top boxes that take legit carts. (key word here is legit. No flash carts from what I read) Or in the case of the Atari flashback consoles (after flashback 3), you have all the games on a rom chip (not hard considering you wouldn't need more than maybe 4 MB to have nearly all the games that were released and then some) So a clone 64 built that way would be no better than just running the game in an emulator on the computer. So... graphic glitches, game breaking errors, slowdowns, crashing... doesn't sound like fun to me. I'd rather find an old 64 and buy an everdrive.

Edit: http://www.freepatentsonline.com/y2001/0016517.html 2001 >.> so 2021, not 2015. Patents expire after 20 years.

Edit the second: Some of the parts involved in the N64 however were patented in 1995 for the use in the console, it's just the console design that was patented in the US in 2001 kinda odd really considering that the console was around since I think 1996 in the states...must have been a patent pending thing...
