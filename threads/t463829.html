DDaft PunkDec 06 2010, 05:48 AM
Make this!• 1Tbs butter• 10g butter• 1 clove garlic - chopped• 4 brown shallots - chopped• 2 small red Thai chilli - chopped• 1Tbs lemongrass - chopped (about 1/3~1/2 a stalk?)• 2 1/2C chicken stock• 1 1/2C coconut milk• 4C (~1kg) pumpkin - peeled and diced• Fresh basil leaves to serveHeat oil and butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Cook garlic, shallots, chilli and lemongrass for 5 minutes or until onion softens. Stir in chicken stock, coconut milk and pumpkin; bring to the boil. Cook, covered, for 15 minutes or until pumpkin softens.Cool soup slightly and blend with an immersion blender. Reheat (don't boil), garnish with basil leaves and serve.Bonus points if you make your own chicken stock.
