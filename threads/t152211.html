It's really not that hard. Just jump and the moment you leave the ground, immediately press the L-button and down/right (or left). You're essentially dodging into the ground. CChessmastaJun 22 2010, 07:08 PM
Lemme try and describe the benefits of wavedashing.First, a definition. Wavedashing is jumping, and immediately air dodging diagonally into the ground to allow your character to slide a certain distance, while still being in his or her standing animation. the distance that you slide is different from character to character. Luigi's is the longest, Marth, Ice Climbers, and Samus are characters whose wavedashes are pretty long, off the top of my head.First of all, with Samus, it's plain as day why you need to wavedash. Your roll is so slow, you need a better method of moving around. Wavedashing is a good answer.Say you're at the right place for Marth to tipper smash you, and you understand this fact. By simply wavedashing back, Marth's smash will whiff, and hit air. Then, you can wavedash back forward twice, and hit him with your forward smash while he's trapped in his lag frames.This is the concept of spacing. You want to keep at the very edge of your opponent's attack range, and using wavedashes to move around is a very efficient way of keeping the right distance to your opponent.Also, for characters like Ice Climbers, wavedashing makes you move much faster than you would normally while dashing. This is also true of Luigi.
