Chicken Fried SpamIngredients1 Can o' spam1 eggFew dashes of hot sauceFlour (for breading)Finely crushed breadcrumbs (for breading)Seasoning salt (basically salt mixed with a bit of sugar and paprika)Oil for cooking1 tbs (15 mL) butterSlice of bacon (optional)1 tbs (15 mL) flour1 cup (500 mL) milkBlack pepper and salt to tasteSlice the can o' spam into 1/2 inch (1 cm) thick slices. Get out 3 bowls, add flour to the first bowl, add the egg (beaten) with hot sauce to the second, and add the breadcrumbs with some seasoning salt to the third bowl. Heat the oil in a frying pan. Once the oil gets to tempurature, dip the spam slices in the flour, egg, then breadcrumbs, then add to the pan. Let the slice cook for a few minutes until the coating becomes golden brown, then flip the slice and cook the second side. For the gravy, heat the butter (anc chopped up bacon if you have it) in a small sauce pan until melted. Add the flour, stir to combine, and let cook a minute or two to get rid of the raw flour taste. Add the milk, salt, and pepper, and cook until it thickens. Serve with the spam fried steaks.
