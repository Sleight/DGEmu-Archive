Announcing the DGEmu Monthly Mixtape

2 posts · page 1 of 1 · 9 views
MmouseORIGINAL POSTOct 03 2012, 12:17 AM
Hey there!

We're going to try something new this month as a service to music lovers. Starting this month, we will hold a monthly contest where you all can submit your favorite songs to be included in a digital mixtape!

Participation in the contest will net you GPs, but there's a catch: The mixtape will have a different theme each month, and your song submissions must match that theme.

Given that October ends with Halloween, the theme for this first mixtape is horror. Spooky!

For more information on the contest and details on how to participate, head over to this topic in the Digital subforum. The deadline for submissions is Friday, Oct. 26.

On top of song submissions, we're also seeking artists to submit designs for the album cover! That contest also comes with a GP prize, and details can be found at the same topic.

We're hoping to make this contest an ongoing thing, as long as enough people show interest, so don't hesitate to participate!
