We are talking about Tomba2 The evil swine return right? there are no 4 masks, the goal of the game is to resuce tabby who is a friend of Tomba's. but before he can find her these things called evil pigs (8 of them) come and basically curse the land, altering everything. your job is now to capture the evil pigs, how you say, simple. for each of the evil pigs there is a corresponding pig bag that you must first find and then find the pig door which will only relveal itself after you have brought the corresponding pig bag near it, then you have to fight the evil pig in its domain and catch it and throw it into the bag 3X to capture it. I will agree to the fact it is a game in 3D but it retains alot of similarities of its predecessor Tomba in the 2D department. There are plenty of extra options of things that you can do. The game is based similar to FFT and FFTA, or in other words missions. there are missions that are required and there are extras. the extras get you really cool stuff and all. you have items and equipment that you can use and there are areas of non linear movement. for its time the graphics are great, the customization is decent and the gameplay and storyline are great too. I have had the game for 6-7 years I think, beat it around 4 times got my time down to about 2 hrs total, of course I had memorized the whole thing, and there are also additional missions that you can do if you also have Tomba, the first one. All in all I give the game a 4.5 out of 5 and would recommend anyone that wants to try something different from the mainstream of fighting, racing, and rpg especially considering it has all of them in it, with a twist...
