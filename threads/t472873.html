As announced at Nintendo's press conference at E3 (2011), the predecessor of the "Wii" is called the "Wii U".Controller SpecsI'm not the kind of guy who cares about console specs. It's not really that big. The controller on the other hand (Especially when it comes to Nintendo):- Button Arrangement: Two Circle Pads, + Control Pad, XYAB, L and R, ZL and ZR, Home, "-", "+".- Forward facing camera- Gyroscope and Accelerometer- 6.2 Inch Touch Screen- Microphone and Headphone jackLooks like an iPad, but with buttons. The back of the top half of the controller (no sorry, I mean tablet), is slightly thicker, probably for receivers and battery. It looks considerably thin.This controller allows you to play games on the touch screen, by streaming the image from the console. If someone wants to use the TV for news and soaps or whatever, you can continue playing with the system in your hands. You don't need to turn it off or whatever.The system is also able to use Wii Remotes. Although the controller will no doubt be expensive, you can use Wii Remote setups instead if you wanted. Oh, that said, the Wii U is also backwards compatible with Wii games. The Wii U controller can also hook onto the Wii Zapper. From that demonstration, the tablet appears to be very light.UPDATE: Apparently, the Wii U can only connect to one tablet. Sounds weird, but it does mean that we won't have to pay for more tablets if true.GamesOf course, games. Why do we buy game machines... for the games. It appears that developers are really gunning for this system, as it has opened new doors of interactivity for them. At E3 2011, an EA rep was a special guest at the Nintendo conference, which was a surprise.So, some of the known titles for the Wii U include:- New Super Mario Bros. Mii- Darksiders II- Aliens: Colonial Marines- Ninja Gaiden 3: Razor's Edge.- Tekken Wii Successor- Batman: Arkham City- Ghost Recon Online- Assassin's Creed- DIRT- Metro Last Night- Super Smash Bros. (4)- Pikmin 3 (Confirmed via Nintendo's 2011 developer talk. Originally, was supposed to be a Wii title. It was also very close to completion. However, Miyamoto decided to release the game in HD, and therefore is another known title to come to Wii U.)Basically, Wii U has titles that look like they will really appeal to hardcore gamers. In fact, nothing looked kiddy at all. Ghost Recon is a given online game, it's says so in the title. The same developer, Ubisoft, is making a multiplayer Assassin's Creed for Wii U as well. I think Ninja Gaiden 3 has online co-op, if I remember correctly, otherwise it's just standard splitscreen... or not! SSB would no doubt also have online, as will probably Tekken. Oh, and I would say, with titles like Aliens and Ninja Gaiden, there's gore.... lots and lots and lots of gore!Wouldn't be surprised if most of the Wii U games are hardcore, because it already has the massive Casual library of the Wii. Although, I do sense a Wii Sports HD or something like that appearing.Since DGEmu lacks a Wii U forum as of yet, I implore people to post in this one for the sake of neatness.Links
