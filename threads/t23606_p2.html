I'd recommend RuneScape. I know many of you've probably heard of it, maybe used to play it. Now the popular statement is that RS sucks.

 

I recommend it because for about half a year it's completely free-to-play, so to speak. Basically they've introduced in-game item called "Bond" that you can buy for gp and use to get 2 weeks of p2p time. when you first start getting the money to buy one may seem impossible, but give it a week and you should have decent stats to do some ok moneymaking and go P2P. After a while on P2P you should be powerful enough to afford Bonds easily :)

 

Now about the game itself. Ss it grindy? Yes, but what makes it different from most mmorpgs are quests. They are not your regular kill x of y type of quests. They actually have a plot, sometimes puzzles, sometimes tricky boss fights (by tricky I mean you can have the best gear and stats and die if you don't pay attention). They also make "your world" change, in that, say, some new areas get unlocked, some enemies gone.

 

Does it have microtransactions. Yes. 3 in fact: Bonds (the only way to add them into the economy is to buy them for irl cash first), SGS and TH. SGS handles cosmetics mostly, with some convenience like more bank space (doesn't matter much unless you're an absolute hoarder). TH is gamble, sort of. You get keys, open a chest and in it is a random reward. Most of the time it's minor thing like some logs, but if you're extreeeeeeeeeeeeemely lucky you may land some decent prizes like 200m gp - not HUGE amount, but could prove handy for a newbie.

 

Nothing else to say ... well, a lot to be honest, but it's best to ust experience for yourself.
