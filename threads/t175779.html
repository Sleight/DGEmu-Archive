MMoogleDeathORIGINAL POSTOct 26 2006, 06:40 PM
I did a search and didn't see any topics on Dark Crusade, so I figured I might as well start one. Anyone here been playing the new Dark Crusade expansion? It's the best RTS I've EVER played, and I've played quite a few of them in my days. It's just a shame how under-appreciated it is compared to games like Starcraft and Warcraft, when Relic has really come up with a fun and unique style of RTS gameplay that sets their games apart from others. The campaign mode in their latest expansion is a GREAT improvement over previous installments of the game, too, with a risk-like overworld where you conquer the planet one sector at a time, all 7 of the races with their own storylines involved, and two new races, the Necron and the Tau, just to give a bit of information about the game to those who may not have heard of it.So far I'm enjoying it quite a bit, but it's a very much different game than it was pre-Dark Crusade. The SM Grey Knights and the Ork's flash gitz are probably the best new units in the expansion for the existing races I've found so far. I haven't really played much chaos or eldar though, so I haven't gotten a chance to use the daemon prince or harlequen. I've tore some **** up pretty badly with the new Heavy Weapons Team the Imperial Guard just got, too. Their autocannon upgrade is amazing against certain races.I haven't played much Tau yet, but as far as the Necrons go, they're immensely powerful with a unique playstyle that sets them apart from the other races without making them too powerful or too weak.

If you're having trouble with the campaign, chances are you're rushing into things too fast. In single-player you will almost always be outnumbered (in the Orc stronghold base you're actually facing FIVE different sides, Eldar base 3 sides, Imperial Guard 4 sides, you get the idea) so rushing is pointless. (The Necron stronghold is impossible to rush due to TWO operation Necron fortresses guarding the final entrances.
