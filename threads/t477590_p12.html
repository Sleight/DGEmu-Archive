Pan Dimensional Being wrote on Feb 03 2014, 10:29 PM:

Emulators on chromecast maybe?

Maybe, but not like you think. The Chromecast works by your phone sending a URL to the device and then essentially telling the device to interact with that webpage. Someone did manage to get a GB emulator running, but it was not exceptionally easy from what I read, and that was done by reverse engineering an older version of the device. It may not even be possible now, and if it is, you'd basically be streaming a game from your device to your Chromecast which would stream it back to you. I'm not sure what the latency on input would be for that, but anyone that uses one for Netflix can tell you that playing and pausing isn't instantaneous, though no telling if that is a Netflix issue or a Chromecast issue or router or speed or what. Lots of variables. We may see something than can play turn based games soon, but I wouldn't hold my breath for action games.
