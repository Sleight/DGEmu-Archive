KKHRSMar 05 2014, 01:23 AM

Finally finished the game

 

[non-plot spoilers: chapters 1-5]

It really pissed me off how many times they forced you to re-do all of the crystals. It got so boring having to kill all the asterisk holders and release the crystals like 4 times. The only good part was in chapter 8 when the asterisk bearers started forming teams inside of eternia tower, that part was cool. Other than that, the end of this game just got wayyyy too repetitive. IMO the best part of the game was the first 2 chapters. I liked the themes of the first and second chapters, the first dealing with a corrupt government (I especially liked the thief+merchant+time mage triumvirate, it was a pretty cool sub plot) and the 2nd chapter involving what was more or less a metaphor for drugs was kind of cool and how the red mage's plan tied into the salve makers plan which they address in one of the different worlds where you have to fight the merchant+salve maker+red mage). I liked that they made all these intricate little subplots but I disliked how you had to go through ALL of those extra sub plots and battles JUST to see Khint's daughter or to find out that Holly used to date Victor. Chapters 3 and 4 were kind of generic and boring and then after that the game just got more and more stale. They started going into the meat of the plot but the true plot points were spread way too far apart by all the filler stuff that it just got boring.

 

[plot spoilers warning: end game + credits]

Regarding Airy. I honestly didn't expect Airy to be evil at first, but when they made the very first implication it was blatantly obvious that she was evil. I feel they could have done a better job conveying the idea that she was the "traitor"

 

Regarding Eternia. I liked how they tried to justify the Templar's actions because he was trying to save the world, but they never acknowledged the unbelievably massive amount of corruption within the Eternian ranks. Like, it's hard to ignore a corrupt king completely enslaving his own nation to work for him, a group spreading potentially lethal drugs within Florem, a man that was regularly kidnapping and raping random girls and a potions expert wiping out their own troops with poison WMDs. Then there's the matter of the prophecy. Like, trying to save the world is perfectly fine, but, I think the templar could have you know, not waited till he was almost dead and while Alternis was dying to reveal all the stuff they knew regarding the crystals and prophecy.

 

Regarding the shipping. Loved it.

 

Regarding the final boss. The final boss, even though he was just immediately shown without any real build up was a pretty epic fight (I almost crapped myself when I realized that the camera was recording my face in the background of the fight, lol. I just thought it was my reflection at first). It was a cool and creative fight (what with DeRosso fusing into Ouroboros, Ouroboros eating planets in between turns, etc.). I feel like Ouroboros's character could have used some more fleshing out, he had no real motive other than wanting to fuck shit up and just kind of showed up at the end. Arguably you can say he was a mysterious character which makes his appearance more... shocking, I guess? But I dunno, I don't like it when characters just show up out of nowhere especially in THIS game when every single fodder character has VA lines and a past, present and future.

 

The Reveals:

 

-Airy being bad. Like I said, it was kind of poorly done and was more or less blatantly told when it first became a plausible idea. There was no speculation, it was basically "Airy = bad." Edit: I left something out. When the game starts and Ringabel joins you, if you read Airy's profile in the D Journal, it states that she dislikes Ringabel. Initially it could be interpreted as her disliking him for being a perv, but with later information it could be seen as Airy disliking him because she knew he was from another world and could potentially out her. So they get bonus points for that though it's a bit obscure.

2nd Edit: Just looked up the Japanese title: Bravely Default: Flying Fairy. Take away the Fs and you get Bravely Default: Lying Airy. Bonus points for 2deep4u

Source: http://finalfantasy.wikia.com/wiki/Airy bottom of page

 

-Ringabel being an alternate version of Alternis (muh puns). This was actually well done. I read Alternis' journal as soon as I got it and it inserted Alternis into Ringabel's place before you recruit him (when you meet Ringabel it's mentioned that he helped the villagers in a hunt while Alternis' journal describes the hunt from Alternis' POV. Then there's the big reveal where you realize that Ringabel and 1st Alternis are identical but before you know that there are alternate timelines. This really confused me until it's explained that there are actual alternate realities in which case it starts to make sense and is only fully explained after Ringabel regains his memory (if Ringabel had already reached the end of the game then how come he starts off as lvl 1? :^))

 

-Eternia actually being good. Once again, poorly done because while they're working for the greater good they fucked up wayyy too many things.

 

-Tiz being a celestial thingy. Ok I'm not too sure on this one. I think it's all leading up to the sequel, I need to read up on it. Anyways, the first time you fight Mephilia (summoner asterisk) she mentions that Tiz has another soul or something inside of him. Ever since I saw this scene I wondered what the hell she was talking about. Then it's stated when you meet Ouroboros that Tiz has what is essentially a God inside of him. I was glad when they acknowledged this because it finally explained what Mephilia said. BUT THE SUPER MEGA LE EPIC TWIST was the end of the credits when Tiz goes to the graveyard. I'm not entirely sure what this scene is supposed to be and I think it's mainly up to individual interpretation until it's explicitly stated (most likely in the sequel) but the way it seemed to me was that a God was controlling Tiz's body the whole time and that "Tiz" didn't really have anything to do with the story. That's just how I interpret it but I think that would make a bretty good tweest.

 

-The Sequel trailer. Looks cool, looking forward to it.

 

Overall, the game had some faults but overall i think it was a good game. Glad I played it, looking forward to the sequel and uh.... what else... i liked almost all of the VA except for Agnes. The VA herself wasn't bad but that delivery was holy shit so bad. Favorite VAs were Kamiizumi and Artemia.
