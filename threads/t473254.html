Not really...Presented ourselves at a movie theater, got to see the new HP Touchpad running WebOS, though I already had hands on fun since 2 days at work with it.A quick presentation, I'm glad their bringing in their e-print function onto the cheap entry line printers also. That should make our lives much easier to print off things from mobile devices and such.Obviously, since HP is not the officially affiliated to Apple, all their printers will support the AirPrint also.Desktop selection hasn't changed, just the exterior design seems to have gotten slightly better, between Compaq, HP Pavilions, HP Elites, and HP AiO. The only extra is that they're introducing Beats audio to all their Pavilion and Elite models.On the laptop side, complete redesign on netbooks, G series, DV series, and Envy series. Everything will now be integrated with Beats audio, a nice plus to have better sound. Metallic DV models and Envy models will all have USB3 support from now on, a nice addition. And as always, HP ProBooks have gotten a new design also, and will be sold exclusively at Staples.Most of the focus was on the new HP TouchPad, running Palm's new revised WebOS. Functionality is really aimed towards tech noobs for once, simply register all your accounts (Facebook, Twitter, Hotmail, etc) with the tablet, and using their search feature, you can launch, reset, create mail, add status, and everything else directly without having to go through and open apps. Also, the device is fully cloud based, so everything and anything will be synched to everything. Pretty nice. Once again, Beats audio integrated on that HP table also, comparing sound on that versus the PlayBook, Asus Transformer, Iconia, and other such, it sounds much better.Apps availability, there's already quite a nice selection already available, even though the tablet is not released yet. Since the WebOS is Linux based also, porting them from Honeycomb's format is a breeze. And obviously, Angry Birds has already been ported.Flash, HTML5 support already on the device also, so no screwing around like the iPad and other such, and finally, a tablet that will have a 3G capable model without needing to bridge a phone or whatsoever with it.Lastly, for prizes, there were only 4 items: 2 portable HP printers and 2 HP TouchPads...
