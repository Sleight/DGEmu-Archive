SshadowmanwkpApr 03 2012, 07:46 AM
Most final fantasy games are set in their own universe, so you don't have to have played I-III to understand IV. However, IV might stump you a bit, because of the ATB system. Unlike usual RPGs, the battles in final fantasy IV are in real-time (and since then almost every final fantasy game IIRC). This means that enemies might attack you when you are selecting your commands. You can set the wait setting in the option menu, which will at least pause the battle if you go through the spell list. Other than that, IV is a pretty good game and it's not really hard for non-final fantasy gamers, but it does have a few tricks up it's sleeves.
