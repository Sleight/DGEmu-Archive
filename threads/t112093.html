This is about Gummi Ships people. Sheesh. Gummi Ships are required as part of travel to reach new worlds, however, collecting parts is unnecessary. The Kingdom ship can be used throughout the entire part. Going through and redoing them is another story. All you get is gummi parts from going back through. The action of the Gummi Ship is alot more intense now and hardly leaves room for breathing. You can also add small mini ships to help protect you in which you can add weapons to and lasers as well to aid you in battle. As you kill enemies, you collect medals and parts. Medals give you a better rank and getting a higher rank unlocks better gummi parts. There are mid-boss battles for the gummi levels as well. Hope that helps.
