I'm a bit lost. Are you talking about pen-and-paper RPG heroes? Because if so, you can pretty much create a character you feel is non-typical, because it all depends on the sort of RPG story you'll be going through. A character who's a typical hero in one story might turn up a fool, or worse, in another. I guess your best bet would be to create a character that feels natural. I used to play old pen-and-paper RPGs (GURPS, how I loved you), and I found that it's better to leave some elements of the character intentionally vague--and just roleplay them later on in the game. Most original characters seem to some out better this way. Like my martial artist who has a black belt and is always being pestered (at inopportune times) by a midget who goes "Boss! Boss! Da Plane, Boss! Da Plane!!" :P But if you're talking about PC RPGs, I'm afraid I can't help there.
