KKirie MotobaMay 30 2013, 05:35 AM

It's not stealth, but more a puzzler in the sense that you got melee weapons and guns.

 

Use melee weapons, you can take out enemies silently without alerting others.

Use guns, it makes noise, everyone comes at you.

 

In normal circumstances, you'd want to use melee weapons, but when a room has like 4 people armed with shotguns, you can't just barge in.

 

The timing is key also:

- Kicking open a door when enemy is nearby stuns them, which then you have to proceed into killing him before he's conscious and attacks you again.

- Dogs charge really quickly at you and chomp you down.

- Some enemies are flanked and hide, entry timing has to be calculated to kill them.

 

Enemies mostly die 1 shot to knife, katana, crowbar, etc.

Guns, depending on which, can splash, or whatever. Very limited ammo, you can't expect to take out a whole squad with a machine gun.
