After time you will remember all its functions J:*---represents a sub selection of the toolMarquee Tool: makes rectangular, elliptical, single row, and single column selections.Pointer: moves selections, layers, and guides.Lasso Tool: make freehand, polygonal (straight-edged), and magnetic (snap-to) selections.Magic Wand: selects similarly colored areas.Cropper: trims images.Slice Tool: creates slices.---Slice Select Tool: moves slicesHealing Brush: paints with a sample or pattern to repair imperfections in a image.---Patch Tool: repairs imperfections in a selected area of an image using a sample or pattern.---Color Replacement: replaces a selected color with a new color.Brush Tool: paints brush strokes.---Pencil Tool: paints hard-edged strokes.Pattern Stamp: paints with part of an image as a pattern.---Clone Stamp: paints with a sample of an image.Art History Brush: paints with stylized strokes that simulate the look of different paint styles, using a selected state or snapshot.---History Brush: paints a copy of the selected state or snapshot into the current image window.Eraser: erases pixels and restores parts of an image to a previously saved state.---Background Eraser: erases areas to transparency by dragging.---Magic Eraser: erases solid-colored areas to transparency with a single click.Paint Bucket: fills similarly colored areas with the foreground color.---Gradient Tool: creates straight-line, radial, angle, reflected, and diamond blends between colors.Blur Tool: blurs hard edges in an image.---Sharpen Tool: sharpens soft edges in an image.Smudge Tool: smudges data in an image.Dodge Tool: lightens areas in an image.---Sponge Tool: changes the color saturation of an area.---Burn Tool: darkens areas in an image.Path Selection: make shape or segment selections showing anchor points, direction lines, and direction points.Text Tool: create type on an image.---Text Mask Tool: create a selection in the shape of type.Pen Tool: lets you draw smooth-edged paths.---Custom Shape: makes customized shapes selected from a custom shape list.Annotation Tool: make notes and audio annotations that can be attached to an image.Eyedropper: samples colors in an image.---Measure Tool: measures distances, locations, and angles.Hand Tool: moves an image within its window.Zoom Tool: magnifies and reduces the view of an image.(more to come when I’m not so tired *yawns*)The Navigator ToolbarNavigator: controls the zoom level of the image. Press the Large Double mountain to enlarge the image and the single small mountain to shrink the image.Info: tells the coordinates of the cursor in X and Y positions and gives the hue of the certain spotHistogram: shows the level of saturation of different properties. (used VERY rarely.)Layer ToolbarLayers: This is the place where you will spend most of your time besides the sig itself. It allows you to fine-tune it, add color, and all sorts of neat things. I will delve deeper into this section in the next version. For now all you need to know is that when using Adobe Photoshop your actions are stored on different layers. These layers can be adjusted, sorted and set to different settings.---Layer Options (AKA the cursive F in the black circle): This is where you fine-tune each layer to perfection. You will spend most of your time here. So much time you will be able to memorize each functions screen. I know that sounds horrible but its great for your sigs!---Add a layer mask (Rectangle with a circle inside): (will be covered in advanced sig techniques)---Create a new set (Folder): Puts layers into a folder called a set. Very helpful when dealing with lots of layers, but not really needed for sig making.---Create a new fill or adjustment layer (half black half white circle): used to add color or warp the image. I will cover this in more detail when we make your first sig.---Create a new layer: self explanatory---Delete layer: self explanatoryChannels: Allows you to turn off Red Green and Blue channels. Also allows you to create new channels to run your sig throughPaths: Allows you to manage paths created by the pen tool. The pen tool in PS is virtually useless because of Illustrator’s better use of it. This is not needed for your sig making.EDIT2: nm
