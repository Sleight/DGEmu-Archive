It is not uncommon to see members getting points deducted from their posts. By far the most common reason for this, is a lack of development. As stated in the Debates forum rules (found here) there are certain expectations in regards to the quality of posts made in this forum. In light of this, I present a brief guideline to developing your posts. Not only will this help you avoid those nasty deductions, but it will also strengthen your arguments quite nicely too. Furthermore if you develop a post quite well and are consistently doing so, you will increase your chances of reward!

Developing Posts.
An easy way to approach this, is to think of how you would develop a paragraph in an essay. That is it should consist of a statement (what it is you are asserting), an example which demonstrates your statement and finally an explanation as to how and why it supports your statement. Ideally, I would like to see sources provided as well. However this is not strictly required. As I find examples can be useful, I will provide a few examples. Although be aware that these examples do not necessarily reflect my views.

For my first example I have randomly chosen the thread Women naturally smarter than men? through non-random means.
example one wrote:
I believe that men are smarter than women, because they are more logical.

This post, if made in a thread here would suffer a point deduction. While it makes an assertion it does not sufficiently develop it to the point where it has been supported in any way at all. To make an improvement to it, they could explain how men are more logical and/or what they mean by this.
example two']Woman are quite simply less intelligent than men. The reason for this being that they lack the logic required to be intelligent. This is relevant as logic is the ability to use reasoning. One only needs to look at the ratio of males to females in the natural sciences, a field very much dependent on logic and intelligence, to see men have a greater grasp over logic. Take this recent report from Japan for instance.<br />
While this example is not exactly going to win any awards for greatness, it does provide sufficient development. As such it would not suffer deductions.

A word on length (courtesy of Kiza19)
While the minimum word limit is 20 words you should not limit yourself with this. While it is possible for someone to make a developed post in only 20 words. More often we find that the posts are akin to those we find in the general discussion section. This is not sufficient here. If you are having trouble developing your points then try to find additional evidence online. Explaining your evidence and citing your sources can be a great way to fortify your case.

Debate of Week. (Courtesy of Finalage, 03/25/10)
The debate of the week is explained at a greater length here but I am placing this to give you a bit of an idea what it is about. A moderator will select a topic once a week, right now, it is me, every Monday, and pin it. (Or I may decide to create my own.) From then on, it is a debate of the week for that week. After a week has passed, the moderator will select a group of posts they believe is the best and create a pinned poll to be voted upon by the public. Whoever wins that poll at the end of that second week gets rewarded, generally, 250 GP's.
1st week - Pinned debate topic, post there to submit your entries.
2nd week - Best posts are picked and placed for voting, don't post in the poll topic, vote for your pick. (Don't chose "See Results" it will nullify your vote.)

Any questions, please direct them at me.
