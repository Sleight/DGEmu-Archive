What use could there have been of more than one type of finch or more than one type of apple tree? Evolution explains this by talking about mutations and organisms in falling in ecological niches.Mutation arises, it is selected for and that is passed on. This is a description of how evolution arises. It is not a prescription of how we should live. If it is so, then we should be jumping into nuclear reactors to gain as many mutations as possible and then selecting for our offspring that have the desired mutation.
