Bbooyah_boyORIGINAL POSTFeb 18 2006, 03:30 AM
When first I joined this site I held the greatest of ambitions for my future, however, I have learnt time and again that we so rarely fulfill our own desires, and so it goes that I must step down from my position as a moderator. I feel it to be the wisest course of action that I be removed, as, in all honesty, I can no longer pretend (not even to myself) that I remain the best suited for this role.Apart from my recent spells of inactivity, my health continues to wane and I know not how long I require to regain such, or, indeed, if I shall. Melodrama aside, I entered into this site's membership with a poor constitution and leave no better. I find that each day brings with it further pain, and each night leaves me more weary then that which came before. In short, I have not the energy to continue patrolling a site I have come to care for very much, and I no longer wish to harm it's many good members by failing to perform my tasks.This is not my final goodbye - I still have many obligations left to complete herein and projects left undone - yet I would like to conclude my ramblings with a few words of thanks: thank you to the administrators for the opportunity I have had to serve, not only them, but the members of this site; thank you to my fellow staff members (most notably mrcn, aoiblue, Parkos, Teck, PlaySwiss, Dragons Master, Falconer, Il Duce Vicious, and thievingmexican24) for the lessons your dedication and effort have imparted; and thank you all for what trust you have placed in me. I appreciate the latter more than any words may express.And so, to end as I began, I wish you all the best of luck...PostScript:At the risk of overstepping my bounds, I would like to submit a few members as candidates for my position...- djnforce9- mohl394- xenos219Each has proven - to me, at least - time and again their worth, and each has much yet to contribute.
