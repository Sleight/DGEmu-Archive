I think you got reputation and warning mixed up. reputation only reflect what other people think of you. A good reputation is always nice but either good or bad it does not affect your conversion points.

 

Warning on the other hand affects how much points you spend to converse into DP. Warning points 0 means your at neutral, you do not pay more or less points to get DP points.

 

Negative does not mean bad for warning. For example let say you have -10% warning. It means you spend 10% less . If it takes 3.00 to get points on 0 warning, It will only take you 2.70 to get a point on -10% warning. If  you get +50% warning it means you will spend 4:50 points.
