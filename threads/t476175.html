...how is it not a JRPG? It might look like Monster Hunter, but its not similiar at all mechanics-wise, you have target lock, auto-attacking, no dodging or anything, and an extensive skill/stat system. Its more like an MMO

As for tips&tricks, abuse being able to save anywhere. Save right before heart-to-hearts to redo them (giving different responses make the dialouge substantially different), save before opening rare chests to reset RNG, always save before gem crafting. Colors indicate level difference, don't worry about it unless its red. Sidequests kinda suck anyways because there are a bazillion of them if you're completionist, if you're not the only really important ones are skill tree unlocks, which you don't get until late in the game.

...and you don't have component cables? That's the first thing you should do when getting a Wii

Also you can control your party by settings their skills. Of course its not very strategic when you start out because you don't have many skills (like any RPG), but later on the system fleshes out, and there are tons of cool combos (and team setups) like setting up huge damage back attacks, double attack and critical % setups, topple lock strings, etc. Later on you'll be able to fill the party gauge way faster and do some elaborate stuff with chain combos. Also read the in-game tutorials, they do a good job of explaining how to hold aggro and using skills properly and stuff
