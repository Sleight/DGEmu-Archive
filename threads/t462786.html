MMo­lh.Nov 10 2010, 06:56 AM
The game publishers just like publishing the games in US and in Europe for different markets. The E version has more languages. Other than that there shouldn't be much difference. Any difference there is is because of the guys who publish the games, that's all.As for the ROM filesize difference, the extra languages will of course present themselves as extra data. I don't think the ROM release groups or ROM sites do anything much to change the filesize. Perhaps they use a rom trimmer to cut out any extra, useless data and hence give a different filesize, but I don't think they do that much. Or perhaps they pre-patched the ROM, but that happens very, very rarely (release groups like to release "clean" unpatched ROMs). Otherwise any difference in filesize will not be due to anything the ROM release groups have done.Neither are the different versions made by different guys. You can view the details of which companies made/published/etc the games on wikipedia (as well as on games sites of course), and they never say that the U and E versions are done by different people. At most they need someone to translate and localise the games (difference in language), but that's about it.

EEndymion_MallornDec 10 2010, 10:01 AM
To summarize; the difference in region depends on the cartridge in question. Some games are the same from start to finish from Japanese to all EU releases (with the understood necessity of translation). I think the best example might be the Super Mario Brothers Advance series, of that. Very little dialog, the gameplay is untouched, almost nothing changes. And then you have games like Gameboy Wars/Advance Wars; different sprites (did you know that in Japan, Nell doesn't wear sleeves?), but again - same gameplay. The Yugioh games suffered this as well - Yugioh V Expert 1 and Yugioh VI Expert 2 were released in U under different names, and there seemed to be almost nothing of the gameplay.The fourth Advance Wars game, following Dual Strike, was released to the EU and U translation teams separately. I think they did it deliberately to toy with us gamers. The name of the protagonist is different (In Japan and EU he's Ed; USA? Will), and each team uses a different name for the Captain (USA: Brenner / EU: O'Brian). Even the maybe-innocent girl you pick up is named after the two halves of the flower she was clutching (EU: Catleia / USA: Isabella - both after the Cattleya Isabella orchid). Heck, I think Lin is the only character in the game that gets out unscathed across all three regions.So... there you have it. It depends on the game. In practice there's no real need to worry about region if you're not linking your cartridges. If you are, just make sure you're using the same region - doesn't matter which. I always downloaded U roms from DGEmu, because they're just that smidge smaller - apparently, four languages do take up some space.
