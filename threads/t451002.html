AA Love SupremeDec 03 2009, 10:17 AM
Have fun with them. I recommend follow the tuts for 3 or so signatures and once you get a bit more confident try mixing in your own ideas. So on your fourth sig you might be following a tutorial but you might say "oh, what if I add in an extra layer and put a filter on it," etc.Too often people never improve because all they are doing is following instructions, and considering making sigs is all about creativity, blindly following tuts is a bit contrary to what you're ultimately aiming for. They can make great sigs from following other people's "style" but they never have built their own unique or interesting style.Following the tuts to get a few styles and techniques under your belt. And then see if you can mix them or chuck in your own "methods" or "techniques" based off your increased knowlegde of the functions of Photoshop.
