Ok, so this member, Endymion_Mallorn, PMed Ish about creating a posting FAQ, and the idea was forwarded to me. The member feels that it will be beneficial to members and will serve as a good supplement to the rules forum threads. I presume since he encourages discussion in his guide that he would prefer this to be in the Support & Suggestions section since the Rules & Guidelines threads don't warrant replies. Anyway, I'm not going to comment or give my opinion on the idea at this time since I don't want to sway anyone one way or the other. Instead, I'll just let you read the guide he wrote and PMed to me so that you can make your own decision:
Endymion_Mallorn'][font="impact"]POSTING FAQ[/font]Many people have asked why we might need a more in-depth posting guide, as the Posting Rules are already the first section of the Forum Rules. I believe that there is a very solid reason, in that our membership has been apparently struck by a plague of extremely short and, honestly, nonsensical posts. Let us begin by examining the rules first, shall we?[font="impact"]Part 2: What makes a "good post"?[/font]Now, we've talked about what you shouldn't do, about what makes a post be against the rules. Now, what makes a rules-abiding post be a bad post? Because we can all admit that we've seen really bad posts that somehow just aren't that bad yet. So, let's start with one thing I've noticed, that I find to be the biggest thing.
Ghostbusters II wrote:
Very good, Louis. Short, but pointless.-Egon Spengler (Harold Ramis)
In short, have a goal. Have a reason to post. Then explain your reasoning in the post. While the forum exists as a way to control the ROM site, many of us have a tendency to almost ignore the DGEmu ROM site for the excellent forums that lurk behind that front page. So, I understand the idea of posting, trolling, or bumping for GPs. But don't do it unless you have a good reason to post in that topic, because you'll get your butt banned quickly.Your posts should also be somewhat explanatory. A lot of topics lately are about, "what of these do you like" and somesuch. When you post up what you like, also talk about why. You're human, you have a reason.So let's quickly encapsulate everything we just said here. A good post has the following qualities:
someone wrote:
It's unique. Your post is your own, and when you talk about something, we want to know why you in specific are talking about it. We're not here to read something like "Me too!" spammed across the board. Also, even if you're posting in two or more threads on the same basic topic, say something new or different every time.Spelling and grammar are observed. Spelling in any language, grammar at least in the original language. I'm aware that not everyone here speaks English fluently. But this is an english-language board, and we ask that you respect that. We all understand that computerized translations are imperfect, but if you try to be as correct as possible in your native language, you have an increased hope of correct translation.It's an expression of your thoughts and feelings. As I said, don't just say, "Me too!" or "I like X!" Tell us why, or don't bother posting at all. If you're not obeservant of this, you will get reported for a short post, or worse.It's on-topic. Be careful of this. When everyone else is talking about something and you start to talk about something else, not even something else altogether, just something else, someone may take offense.The post has a reason for being. GPs are not a reason.Emphasis is placed in the proper places. Don't go overboard with color or font effects. No one will like it and you may get reported for a confusing and/or distracting post.
If anyone has any questions, please feel free to post them here, or PM me for help.
