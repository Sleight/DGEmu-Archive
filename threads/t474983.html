KKobato HasegawaORIGINAL POSTJan 26 2012, 04:19 PM
2 things I will start work on as I wait for test of staff utilities to come in:- DGEmu Arcade: a new page that is not based on the forums, will support GBX, NES, GG, and SMS games.- DGEmu Radio: a console music player base on NES sound files and other formats.Concepts detailed:DGEmu Arcade will open a new window with a new single page interface.It will take the concept of what we have in the forum: Load a Java emulator that will load a ROM.The advantage is that all the games will be centralized and choosable from say, a listbox or something.To prevent easy leeching, there will be an admin upload page that will take a rom, randomize the filename on the server, and save it to our database. That should prevent people from viewing the source and easily leech all the roms.I won't go into deep details such as box art, game manuals, and instructions for now, but that is something we can build upon.This is simply to have a nicer interface for the user and not rely on our forum interface for the Arcade.DGEmu Radio will open a new small window containing the base player interface.The page will consist, yet again, of list boxes or whatsoever that will let the user choose a game to load as a playlist into the player.Programming a custom playlist that loads from multiple files seems complicated for now, but we`ll keep that in mind.This is to allow users to enjoy classic game music while browsing through our forums and such, or just simply play game music on the fly while doing their homework and such.Why:Give an extra incentive to users to signup for DGEmu. We're known for our roms, we got people signing up to get games, but we want people to signup and make us their home for socializing also, a bit how Hongfire still revolves around their community right now. Increasing our members also gives us more opportunity to wheel in donations to help us maintain the servers.What we need in preparation:- A single page design that will fit with the current forum colors. Although I know how to work perfectly well how to code html4 compliant pages, my lack of artistic skills is the stick in the wheel.- A graphics designer for the logo.- Another web programmer that has some free time on hand. Experience with PHP, Javascript, and MySQL. If they know how to work with IPBWI, it'll be a great plus.To be honest, I haven't had too much experience with PHP, and it's been years since I worked with Javascript. The Staff Utilities page was something that forced me to research and relearn the curve again (I keep forgetting my ; at the end of each code line... Got to used to VB2010 and VBScript...). I'm actually surprised I actually got that far with that page without growing out white hair. XDAnyways, if any of you can contribute, or refer someone that would be interested in acquiring some personal experience, I welcome them with open hands, even if they aren't 2D girls. ;D
