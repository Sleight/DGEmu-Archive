Actually, all of them are built the same. The only differences lie in the cards available. Obviously, Synchros and Tuners do not appear in WC 2008 because they were yet to be released. So if you want the best YGO experience on the DS, I'd recommend Reverse of Acardia. Also, besides the updated and better card list, it has a somewhat better plot and other mini games to enjoy. The only downside I've seen with it is that it isn't as organized as what I've seen in past titles.
