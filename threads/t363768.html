If you are not willing to read these simple rules for the debate forum, you're probably not going to last very long here since you are likely going to be reading plenty of posts as long or longer than this one if you are going to be an active participant in the discussions. Keep in mind that all new threads in this forum warrant 20 GP and all replies warrant 10 GP. Because of the bonus GP we are more strict in regards to the rules, so if you spam or make a post that we deem 'stupid' then you're going to lose some serious GP and maybe get a warn, or even removed from this section; but the people who post here regularly know this and do an excellent job here, so follow their example.

The flip side to a harsher punishment, as said above, is that you get extra GP in this section, and will be given even more for outstanding posts and witty arguments.
When starting a thread, make sure that you have a good basis of discussion. Questions like "what is your favorite _____?" and the usual garbage that passes as a topic in places like General Discussions WILL NOT be tolerated here.
Make sure to sufficiently cover the background of whatever topic you choose in your opening statement so that anyone willing to read your post may be able to effectively participate.
When replying to a thread, make sure that you are caught up with the current discussion and that your post thoroughly addresses the topic with sufficient reasoning. Make your posts original and don't just recycle other posters' thoughts to make them sound like your own.
Do not type like an idiot. No 1337, txt spk or any of that nonsense.
Do not insult people meaninglessly or flame other members for no good reason. If you believe that a person's argument sucks, try to tear apart and shoot down the argument, not the person making it. A little snideness is fine, just don't be over the top with it.
Do not state your opinions as fact, or assume that everyone knows where you got your sources from. Try to have a source behind your arguments, even if it's only a wiki article.
Do not create strawmen. Related to that, do not twist another person's words or misquote them. (This goes for sources as well as other posters)
Spamming in this section or being an asshat will cause you to not be able to view Debates.

Now that you know what we expect of you, enjoy debating with your fellow members! :(
