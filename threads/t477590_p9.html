KKula DiamondNov 22 2013, 09:43 AM

One thing you have to try before giving up on the game is the abuse of moves shortcuts with the super long buffer window for special moves.
Go to training mode with Kyo and try this:

Huge Buffer Window 1: do a special move and keep holding the last button, you have ~17 frames in which if you go into a stat that allow the move to come out, it will come out. So no one frame links for super moves.
How to test it: Jump with Kyo and do am SRK+Punch on your way down, keep hold the "punch" and the move will come out the moment you land on the ground.
 
Huge Buffer Window 2: you can do the motion of the move you want, but only press the last punch/kick button many frames later, the move will still come out.
How to abuse this: with Kyo, make sure you have enough hyper meter(more than half). Do and SRK+High Punch,  then immediately hold the kick button. What will happen is when the dragon punch connect, the game will read the later half of your SRK as QCF and since you're holding the Kick now, the dragon punch will be canceled into Kyo's double kick move.
 
Command Shortcuts: Many moves have shortcuts, one example is QCB>HCF+Punch supers can be done as QCB>QCF+Punch.
How to abuse this: With Kyo, make the other character jump, juggle him using Kyo's QCF+HK, hit him with Kyo's Oboruguruma(backward SRK+Kick) and cancel into QCF+Punch(remember to keep holding the punch), as Oboruguruma conect, Kyo will cancel it into his super.

Abusing cancels, shortcuts and long ass buffer make KoFXIII combo system really fun and easy, it's my favorite fighter this gen, though this is useless if you can't find someone to play with.
