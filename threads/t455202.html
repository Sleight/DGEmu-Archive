I know it's a very old post but still I'll answer it here as someone else might come here looking for the same topic.
Auzzie Wingman' post='4812820 wrote on Mar 11 2010, 04:25 PM:
So, I wish to modify my PS2 so that I can:Copy the game to my computer so as to have backup. I mean 6 or so years of having them, you'd think they'd be able to manufacture dust.
You can backup PS2 games in your PC directly as PS2 games comes in CD or DVD media. You don't need modding to do that. All you need is a DVD copying software, you can find loads of them in Google.
Auzzie Wingman' post='4812820 wrote on Mar 11 2010, 04:25 PM:
Move the game to a USB hard drive which I can have my PS2 read it to play the game.
For that, you'll need a modchip. The best way to play PS2 games stored in HD is for those who own the "old fat" PS2, as you can mod it to attach a HD into the Network Adapter. In a PS2 slim, the only way to attach an HD is using the USB port, but since PS2 slim only have USB 1.1 ports, the reading speed for games will be slower than reading from DVD, so you'll have problems with cutscenes and streaming intensive games (they'll stutter).
