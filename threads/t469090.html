TTHE GRAND GALACTIC INQUISITORApr 05 2011, 12:42 AM
You run around dungeons and fight monsters. Collect materials and use them with recipes you buy to make better equipment. There are a few bosses deeper in the dungeons, too.As far as gameplay specifically, it's all real-time, so you move yourself around and the monsters move around, and you partake in a delicate dance of death. Weapon choices are swords (for close-range fighting and pushing enemies back), guns (for long-range, safe fighting, but with less dps than swords) and bombs (which are dumb). Weapons have power moves, some of which do unique things (like a sword that shoots a hadouken-ish ball of light). Also, in dungeons, you will often find vials that can either be thrown at enemies for status effects or used to heal yourself (or summon a roboknight to fight by your side and occasionally shock you).
