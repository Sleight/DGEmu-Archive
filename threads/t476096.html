Touhou takes many forms: games, music, videos, and even manzai!
What is manzai? Very simple: Japanese stand-up comedy.

To be able to understand many of the jokes being thrown in the following videos, you have to have an understanding on how the community perceives many of the characters. For example, Cirno is always labeled the most stupid character, Ran has a high affection for Chen, Nitori is always associated to cucumbers, etc.
You also need some understanding of Japanese products and cultures for some other references.
The rest is just mainly common sense. ;D
