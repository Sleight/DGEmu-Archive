Come on, no one in the game is "ultra useless," no matter how much you hate 'em. It's not FF6. You don't have a dozen interchangeable paper dolls. Everyone serves a specific purpose. Auron and Kimarhi kill armored things. Tidus kills fast things. Wakka kills flying things. Lulu kills slimes and elements. Yuna can heal, and Rikku has steal, mix, and is a replacement Tidus (but stronger, and not quite as fast, in my experience) if you can't get his stupid sigil.Anyone can kill the final bosses in two or three hits if you have the celestials and you've levelled up a respectable amount. It's a Final Fantasy. It's easy. Auron is awesome for most of the game, but he really only stands out in the end-game because his celestial weapon is the easiest to get. And even then, his speed is a weakness, he'll never be able to hit flying monsters reliably, and he doesn't have anything even half as good as the "useless" Wakka's attack reels. The only bad thing about Wakka, really, is that his overdrives and celestial weapon stuff takes forever to get. He only sucks for the first half or so of his grid. He starts getting big strength gains afterwards, and his grid leads right into Auron's, so he just gets stronger.And Kimahri can be a second anyone. Auron included, and it's not really that hard, it just takes a bit of patience and planning. Just send him to get Rikku's steal first chance you get, then store up his Sphere Levels until you get to Home. He'll be nothing special up to that point, but even in a state of pseudo-stasis, he shouldn't lag too far below Tidus. In Home, or just after it, you get a teleport sphere. Use that to send him backwards down Auron's path, then backwards down Wakka's path, and assuming everyone has been levelled an equal amount, he will beat every single attacker in every physical stat but speed and accuracy, both of which he'll still top Auron in. You could do it with anyone, really, but Kimarhi is the only one who has no one single essential ability that you'll miss out on by doing it with him. Even if you send him straight from his grid to Wakka's, he'll still be second only to Auron in power. I don't see how anyone could call him useless unless they didn't even bother to give him a chance in the first place.
