It's a short story really. I started adding more brush strokes until I uncovered a brush I had never used. So I used it on one side and the color was blood red. So I undid that and put it over towards the other side and then started moving around layers and deleting the blue brushes on the left side until I got that. And then for the text I just duplicated the text layer, changed the blue text to red, and then took out the last three letters in the red text, and the first three in the blue text. It was very simple.
