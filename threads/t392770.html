DDJ Night Force 9Apr 01 2008, 05:44 PM
Might as well give my input too:Like Sir VG said, sites like rapidshare and megaupload would hamper downloading even more. In fact, without a premium account, rapidshare puts a wait time between downloads even on smaller files (2 or 3MB). Now Ndash gets around this by mirroring the files on rapidshare, megaupload, and filefactory (allowing you to download a reasonable number of roms per day), but I doubt anyone here wants to spend time uploading all 4000+ GBA+NDS roms to three or four different hosting sites with the added cost of maintaining premiums for each so that the links don't expire. I suppose we could steal ndash's links but I doubt that site's admin will thank us for that. However, a few advantages that dgemu does provide which gets overlooked is convenience when downloading the roms. 1. Certain roms are huge and even exceed the upload limit on these file hosting sites. Imagine having to download "part 1" and then wait a whole day before you can download the second half of the rom. I suppose you could argue that it's either one day or many many days saving up DPs.2. The way the roms are stored on some of these other sites is just plain ridiculous (and Ndash is guilty of this too). Why can't you just extract one archive and get your rom? Instead, I've seen roms that have been packed into a part archive and then packed AGAIN into a single file (this would throw off anyone new to computers or who don't know about WinRaR yet especially when the files are in .001, .002, .003, etc (which winrar and 7zip won't recognize) instead of .r01, .r02, .r03, etc). Dgemu removes that annoyance and confusion for you: One .zip to deal with and there's your rom.3. Storing files off-site makes them difficult to maintain as said above. Sites like rapidshare do have a copyright policy making it easy for some idiot to "report" the hosted files and have them removed (or they are idle for 30 days and get purged like with my themed All-In-One bundles stored on mediafire). Then members have to wait for one of us staff to re-host.
