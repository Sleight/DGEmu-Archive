EErrandirMar 24 2012, 10:32 PM
Hmm. Well, let me think. Generally, any of the Hostelling International hostels were good. Not always the cheapest of the available options, and they didn't necessarily have the best extras, but I could never complain about cleanliness, friendliness, etc. They were never a gamble. Aside from that? The Frankfurt Hostel (or whatever it's technically called, you literally just walk straight out of the train station until you reach it) was one of the best (and I'm not just saying that because of the excellent free breakfast). The Sleep in Heaven hostel in Copenhagen was again very nice, very friendly, solid advice on things, free computer use even. Only complaint there was that depending on the room, it's a long long walk to the showers. Galway Hostel (again right outside the train station) was good, umm... I can't actually say that I've had a "bad" hostel experience. The one I stayed at in Dublin wasn't the best, but it was also the cheapest I've ever stayed at, so I really can't complain.
