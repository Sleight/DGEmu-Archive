Alternate Method(This method is for BMP snaps only!)First off, get a program called "Easy Thumbnails" from the following website: http://www.fookes.com/ezthumbs/ It's a little bigger than DM's program (Easy Thumbnails is a 1,025KB download) but will still be quick. And it's totally free and easy to install.(Click to Enlarge)First after loading up the program, you'll see a screen similar to the following. On the left will be where you're gonna direct the program to where your screenshots are, the right is where the image preview is.Find your screenshots via the Look In section. You'll see the folder listing below. If you want to keep the originals and the JPG files separate, create a blank folder and enter it into the Save In box (the ... box next to it will allow you to find the folder, or you can type it in).(Click to Enlarge)Click up on the Settings Tab on the top left and you'll see a host of new options. First off, change the Resize drop box to NO RESIZE. Given the the programs are almost always spitting out at the proper size, you shouldn't ever need to resize. Next, on the New name box, change it to ADD NOTHING. We go through our own renaming scheme later, so there's nothing the program can do for us. Last, under JPEG quality, you'll need to tweak the bar based upon the images. Once you're done, you can click "Make All" and it'll do a mass compression of every screenshot in the folder.Tips:1) Definitely put things into another folder.2) Start with 90% quality setting. We want good quality as well as staying within size limits.3) After you're done converting, go into your JPG folder. In Windows, if you choose View->Details on the menu at top, you'll get the list of JPG images as well as their file sizes. You can then sort by the file size and find out which ones are over the limit. If anything says 30KB or a higher number, you'll need to reconvert.(Click to Enlarge)Reconversion:You can select just the images you need to reconvert on the first tab (Files). Just hold CTRL as you click the images of your choice. Then after you chose what you need, go back to the settings tab and drop the quality bar down 5%. This time click MAKE (NOT MAKE ALL!) and the images you've selected will be reconverted. Repeat until all images are below 30KB.Notes:Try to have your emulator save your images in BMP if it gives you the option (for instance, ZSNES lets you choose between BMP and PNG). This program will NOT convert PNG to JPG.Questions about this method can be directed to Sir VG.
