Sorry if this topic has already appeared, the search function is currently not working.I loved the Twilight series. I enjoyed it. And then I read Breaking Dawn and thats when I thought to myself: "Stephenie Meyer is the biggest idiot in the writing world."Consider this:The thing that made Twilight so interesting in the first place was the idea that a Vampire found this new girl at school so completely irresistable because her blood had a unique smell and it was just the perfect sense of teenage angst with the threatening danger of the Nomads.New Moon spiced things up with Edward leaving due to an accident that put Bella in serious harms way, her friend Jacob is then discovered to be a werewolf, and upon Edwards return it brings the whole conflict of vampires vs. werewolves.Eclipse, my favourite, Victoria returns to cause trouble forcing the werewolves and vampires to work together and possibly even befriend each other, except for the one mistake that annoyed me. Edward, throughout the whole book was trying to convince Bella that she should be with Jacob. He's an idiot. The thing that made it so interesting was that he found her completely irresistable and couldn't stand to be away from her, then we find out that during his time away from her, he developed an "immunity" to the smell of her blood and can stand to be around her with absolutely no effort. Other than that, was great book, and in my opinion where it should've ended.Now, Breaking Dawn, I was fine with the wedding, I was fine with the pregnancy, I was fine with Bella turning into a vampire. But after that, thats when Stephenie Meyer destroyed a good franchise. She had Jacob imprint on Renesmee, incredibly stupid. There was the whole tension between Jacob and Leah that she was building up and then she tosses Leah out the window. Could've explained that a werewolf doesn't imprint on another werewolf, therefore will never imprint, is always a choice. Builds up the tension of a possible werewolf/vampire brawl which fizzles to nothing because Jacob imprints on a vampire baby. Then, everything seems fine until we get word that the Volturi are coming and you start to think things are gonna get interesting. We are then introduced to a dozen new vampires, some of which have awesome powers, one of them has the ability to control the environment, yet you never see them use these powers. Bella goes through a chapter or two of fight training and learning how to use her power. We then go to the battlefield. The Volturi have an army, the Cullens have an army, then as the tension builds, ready for a fight.... They talk. Then they talk some more. And more. And more. They call a vote. Then Alice arrives and saves the day and everything is resolved by talking! Everyone walks away happy and Bella and Edward live happily ever after. Theres no fight, theres no sacrifice or loss, there's just talking and an incredibly boring and anti-climatic ending to what could've been a great story.I hope to be a published writer one day and my philosophy is that a Great Ending can make a mediocre book good, but a bad ending only makes a great book bad. If it doesn't end well, the story is just terrible. As it says in my sig, a quote from the film Secret Window, "The ending is the most important part of the story" and if you don't get the ending write then its just a terrible story.Breaking Dawn is a terrible ending. I like to imagine that it doesn't exist and that the story ends with Eclipse. Stephenie Meyer's job as a writer is to tell stories and she was telling a great story until she ruined the end. The idea is not to write a story for you but for an audience, the purpose is to entertain, Stephenie Meyer was quoted as saying that the reason she didn't end the story with a fight was because she didn't want to kill any of the characters she created.So what? Who says you had to kill a prominent character? You could've had a fight, killed some of the background vampires, demonstrated some of the awesome powers that we only hear of and never get to see, and then have Alice come to the rescue after some blood has been shed.Thats just my opinion. Whats yours on the matter?
