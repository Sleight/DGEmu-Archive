SshadowmanwkpSep 14 2012, 05:40 AM
No$gba has some speedup options, it might make it will make the emulator skip some frames, but the speedups are pretty significant. Here's how to do it:

1. Load a ROM
2. Go to options --> emulation options
3. Look at the dropdown box in the upper-left that says "LCD refresh" change it to one of the unlimited MHz disaster versions. 100% gives a mild speedup, while 10% is the best playability and speedup mix that no$gba can offer.
4. If the speed still isn't to your liking, you can also change "Sound output mode" to mono or none and you can also lower the "sound desired sample rate"
5. Hit apply
6. Go to options --> save options to make the changes permanent

Other than that, there isn't really much no$gba can do in the sense of speedups. No$gba is widely regarded as the least demanding ds emulator, so there's not a really good alternative that will play ds games as well as no$gba will on your computer. The only advice if you REALLY want to play ds games is to upgrade your rig.
