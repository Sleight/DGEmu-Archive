Do Nots/Common Sense Stuff

Forum Problems and Guidelines Rules
Do not make any topic regarding any questions addressed in this FAQ or any of the other main FAQs or Rules forum threads in this site.
Do not post in a topic if you don't know the answer to a question or if it has already been answered, or give the standard (and rather annoying) "gee I'd like to help but I can't sry".
Do not make topics complaining about your warn level or point deductions. If you have an issue with an action a mod has taken with you or your posts, contact him/her privately.
Do not ask for emulators. Rather, look here or alternatively, ;)

Failure to follow any of these rules could lead to your points being deducted or a warn being issued.

Q: What are GPs/DPs and how do I get them?
A: Read This

Q: What other ways are there to get DPs other than posting?
A: We have a number of ways actually. There is the GP Rewarding Quiz, Signature of the Week, writing reviews, the DP Loan, the Screenshotters Team, and some moderators will reward "good" posts with Gps (so make great posts!).

Note: Some of these methods are available year-round and my depend on your (the members) interest in them, such as the Signature of the Week, so, If you want points that way, make sure to participate.

Q: What is the DP loan?
A: The DP loan is an easy way to get a lot of DPs. If you have more than 50 posts then you can click the DP Loan link on the homepage and decide how many points you want and how many days you are willing to wait for your Global DPs to reset again. Read the DP Loan guide for further details.

Q: Will a staff member give me GPs or can my friend give me some GPs?
A: Yes and no. A moderator will occasionally hand out some extra GP for high quality or helpful posts. However, transferring GPs between accounts will not be allowed here because of how easy it would be to create duplicates solely for the purpose of transferring.

Q: Which DPs are used first? (Daily or User)
A: Your Daily DPs are used first because they are the ones that increase everyday. We don't want to make you waste your Static DPs if you don't have to do so. ;)

Q: Why is my conversion rate higher than normal?
A: You got warned for breaking the rules. You can lower your warn level by helping the site. You can help the site by reporting or helping those in need. A mod will give you a negative warn if he/she believes that you deserve it. Read the Warn FAQ for further details.

Q: Why did my DPs get deducted if I didn't finish my download?
A: If you clicked the download link then the DPs are deducted even if it didn't finish.

Q: Why is the Search function down?
A: To ease the load on the server caused by the large number of searches made everyday. The resulting freed-up bandwidth can then be used for things like providing downloads for you. In place of the Search function please use Ctrl+F to search the relevant forum(s), e.g. for a topic to see if it has been done before, before making it.

Q: How do I change my display name/member title (egg)/etc?
A: Go to the DG Economy

Q: Can we have a shoutbox/foreign language forum/DP Reset Increase?
A: No. We had a shoutbox, and no one used it, we have a reasonable Global DP reset as it, and we are an English only forum.
