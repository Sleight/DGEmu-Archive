This is tricky question, but let us boil it down to something that can actually be discussed. What is good? What is evil? I will define good to be one who puts other people in front of himself, and evil as one who always puts himself in front of others. This definition itself may be controversial, so here is my reasoning. "Good" changes in different cultures, as does "Evil". We view good and evil from a view inside one culture. Good people follow the rules of the culture, bad people do not. However, when there are rules that define a culture it as about interactions between people. If the laws and rules were every man for themselves, rules would not be needed. As it happens, rules exist to tell people when they should do things for other people, like paying taxes, jury duty, not killing, not stealing: all instances of checking your natural drive to act only for yourself. That makes people who break these rules, whatever they happen to be bad, and people who do it knowing they are breaking the rules and take pleasure in breaking rules are called evil. What this boils down to is putting your own desires or the desires of others first. Now that this is sort of explained, we can continue the discussion. For a human to continue to exist, he has to find a mate and reproduce. In this process itself there is a contradiction. To attract a mate you need to sacrifice your own needs on occasion, but you are doing so for a inherently self-serving goal, namely to ensure your families survival. Neitzche says that all humans are self serving, but realize they need to interact with others to survive. According to this, people are naturally "evil", but pretend to be "good". So if someone is good enough at always being "good" whenever he is around people, there is no way to tell him from a really good person. At that point, the distinction is meaningless. So in accordance with Hobbes and Neitzche, I think all humans are at base self serving, but at the same time, do to rules, laws, and birth into a rigid society, do their best to be "good".
