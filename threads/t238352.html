AatomicknightMay 11 2007, 05:38 PM
Well, the main idea was that the increased bandwidth usage on one single day would be offset by the decreased bandwidth usage on other days. Restrictions on who can use the system and a discount rate for the number of DPs that can be obtained upfront were implemented to make sure that it isn't as easily abused. The usage by members is also quite low - the most I've seen is about 20 members using it on a given day (the usual number is less than 10). Multiply that by about 100 MB of bandwidth each and you get an increase of approximately 2000 MB - 250 MB = 1750 MB ~ 1.7 GB on that particular day. For the remaining two weeks though, you get a decrease of 14 * 250 = 3500 MB ~ 3.4 GB. True, this calculation doesn't take into account how often members download, but there seems to be plenty of leeway even if DPs are not used fully from day to day.Certainly, I can see why this could be a problem. If it does turn out to be a large problem, we can just remove it. But based on what has happened thus far, bandwidth issues on a day-to-day basis don't seem to be a critical factor.
