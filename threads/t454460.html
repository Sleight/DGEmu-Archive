Easy levels?Grass: I'm sorry to say Bulbasaur has a hard time of it until you get to Vermillion, at least as far as Pokemon that are weak against Grass go. Once there, however, just head a little east and try out all your Grass moves in Diglett's Cave. Nothing there but Digletts and the occasional Dugtrio. Later, you'll be able to use the Vs. Seeker to get some help from Hikers.Water: Squirtle catches a break earlier than Bulbasaur, with all the Geodudes that appear in Mt. Moon. In Fire Red, though, he doesn't have all the Sandshrews to feed on just west of Cerulean--it's all Ekans. Hang tough until you get to Diglett's Cave, then Water Gun those Digletts like there's no tomorrow. Later, you'll be able to use the Vs. Seeker to get some help from Hikers, and then even later than that, there's Mt. Ember with all its delicious Fire-types. Once Squirtle learns Bite, I'd recommend he hold on to it, as it is a Dark-type attack that helps against Psychic and Ghost.Fire: Charmander owns in Viridian Forest, with all those Bug-types to burn. North of Cerulean City is Nugget Bridge, with a few trainers using Grass and Bug types (interspersed with Poison, Flying and so on). Just west of here is a grassy area where higher-level Bugs, your version's first (or second) Grass-type, some Pidgeys and the occasional Abra. There's plenty of good experience in the Silence Bridge and the Celadon City Gym. Once you have Surf, you can Surf south of Pallet Town to a grassy area that's all Tangela.Electric: Pidgeys and Spearows abound in the early parts of the game and linger until very late, when Fearows start showing up. Cerulean City Gym is full of delicious experience. Later on, you'll be able to fight Bird Keepers with their Spearows, Fearows, Doduos and Dodrios. Put your Electric pokemon in the front whenever you're surfing.Psychic: At the start of Cycling Road, at the Celadon side (just after you catch that Snorlax), there are a bunch of Bikers and Cueballs with mostly Mankeys, Machops, Grimers and Koffings, which fall easily to Psychic moves. Also, the Defense EVs you get from these guys are great for counteracting the naturally low physical Defense of Psychic-types. Earlier in the game, however, your psychics can pick up decent levels wherever there are partial Poison types, such as that grassy area on the other side of the river from Nugget Bridge, where you first get Abra.Flying: Spearow starts out at an advantage since he has a Flying-type attack, Peck, right from the start. You have to level Pidgey up a bit before it learns Gust, but once it does, it too can take advantage of the easy early experience in the Viridian Forest. Later, you'll have a chance to glean some experience points from Cue Balls at the uphill side of Cycling Road (memorize which of those guys are Bikers and which are Cue Balls). The Silence Bridge Area abounds in Bugs and Plants, with the occasional Pidgey or Pidgeotto feeding on them just like you. Surf south of Pallet Town for an area exclusively populated by Tangela. There's also loads of delicious experience at the Fighting Dojo in Saffron and Erika's Gym in Celadon.Ground: In Fire Red, the earliest easy experience for Ground Types is Cerulean, where you can kill Ekans for fun. Once you've learned Surf, you can hunt Electric-types in the Power Plant.Fighting: Normal types abound for Mankey, your first Fighting type, to gain power with. Plenty of experience for him in Pewter City's Gym (use Low Kick on that Onix and Karate Chop on the Geodude). Later, you'll get to play with Hikers to bust their Rocks, and some Lasses with their Normal-types chosen for cuteness.Ice: By the time you get an Ice-type, you can Surf south of Pallet to power it up with Tangelas.Everything else, I recommend you stick an Exp. Share on it and level it up with something else. Some of them you'll have to, since Jigglypuff doesn't even know any offensive moves when you catch it.
