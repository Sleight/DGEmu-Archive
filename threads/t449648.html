So, if we aren't all "really real," I don't see any reason to change any sort of action that I would do anyway.Furthermore, I can demonstrate that I'm here. I can demonstrate that the internet exists. I can demonstrate what I perceive as reality. Since I have no way of demonstrating that this isn't "really real," I have no reason to believe that it isn't actually here, and because beliefs inform actions and actions have consequences, I choose to believe as many true things and as few false things as possible. One of the weak points in attempting to use colors as a means of signifying differences in perception is that there are quantifiable frequencies that light is emitted in to allow it to be perceived as differing colors in the first place. Absolutism and empirical observation trumps relativistic perception once again!And on that note, let's reintroduce George Berkeley's "Esse est percipi" ("To be is to be perceived"). We are vexed by the same issue posed in the "Schrödinger's cat" thought experiment or age old philosophical riddle: "If a tree falls in a forest and no one is around to hear it, does it make a sound?". Without an observer to receive the stimuli emanated by an event or object it is effectively inconsequential regardless of some stated reality. Thus, by extension, one can say that existence is vindicated simply by the state of being observed.Of course, this gives rise to some sort of twist into "any publicity is good publicity", but that's a tangent we'll save for later. One could also consider that an event's immediate significance is thus weighed in the value derived from its direct observers and that its gross value comes from the sum of its continued effect upon its observers and those that the observers effect in turn. One could extend this mechanic in the idea of causality as well.Regardless of all the fun things that these ideas entail, the general concept is still the same: the universe is a system of mutual observers and causally effective forces, so to be able to affect something else or be observed is effective in enacting existence in this respect.
