KKirie MotobaJul 12 2013, 12:41 PM

Well, ya

 

The file is 77.5MB I think.

How the GP calculation is done:

 

Take the filesize of it in bytes, divide once by 1024 to convert it to kilobytes, divide again by 1024 to turn it into megabyte.

Once there, round at that number to have no decimals, giving the normal GP cost.

Then, take the normal GP cost and apply your group membership rebate (sentinel is 50% off).

Then take that deducted total and apply any special promotion that is enabled (DGEmu Radio Launch is 50% off).

 

That's how you get 194GPs.

 

If you were a regular member and no special promotion, that album would cost 776GPs.
