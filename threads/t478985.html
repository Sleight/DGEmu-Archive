KKirie MotobaMar 24 2013, 06:54 AM

Just trying to at least have it reset:

 

Right-click your game in Steam, choose Properties.

In the General tab, click on "Set Launch Options..."

 

in the dialog, type something like "-w 1024 -h 768" without the quotes.

 

Ok, close, try to relaunch the game: theoretically, according to most people online, it forces the game to reset back to its 640x480 native resolution, which is perfect for you to reset your settings.
