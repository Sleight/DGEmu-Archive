this was probably the fourth SMT game i've played (first got hooked on them with the DS's Digital Devil Sagas and Persona Portable) and by far one of my favorites. the gameplay is magnificent now that you can control all members of your party, the lack of a "tiredness" meter made exploration, and beating the extra bosses much easier, and the bosses *SPOILER FREE* were quite well done for how they were supposed to be portrayed.

Bravo on such a stellar game SMT team
