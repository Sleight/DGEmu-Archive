I mean, using Lucky Egg may increase your exp (add that to a traded Pokemon and you get even more exp), HOWEVER, leveling quickly comes at a price. Have you ever wondered why a Charizard at 100 who trained with no Lucky Egg and belongs to you is much more powerful than a traded Lucky Egg Charizard at level 100? It's because of the IV gains that they obtain by battling. The more Pokemon you beat, the more IV's of a stat that the Pokemon gives you will obtain. Like Weepingbell in Emerald (I think it's Emerald but anyway). Everytime you defeat a Weepingbell, you obtain 3 IV points for Special Attack and you get maybe 1/20 of the exp needed to level. Then there's the traded Lucky Egg Pokemon who gets the same amount of IV points but gets maybe 1/4 of it's exp needed to level. Then you see that the Pokemon is going to obtain less stats after it levels compared to the one training at a slower pace.
