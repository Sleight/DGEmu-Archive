GGaldion the wiseNov 28 2011, 10:17 PM
If your going to write manga or comics, I would recommend for you to get Sandman book 3: Dream Country by Neil Gaiman. It's not an action novel nor is it closer to that genre, but what's great about this book is that they also printed Neil Gaiman's manuscript for the first comic in the book. It should let you know what you would write when you want to start making your own manga. Action novels that I will suggest is the Percy Jackson series. When I read this series, it reminds me more of the shonen mangas like Katekyo Hitman. It has everything from character development, interesting plots and epic battles (Which i also posted in a previous thread). If you got a DS and your also here to download DS roms. You can read the entire series through this rom here: http://www.dgemu.com/nds-details-5069.html?/Flips-Percy-Jackson
