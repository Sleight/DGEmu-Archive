It's quite the innovative piece in terms of animations compared to the previous generations. I like how they modified a few things in here and just in case...

***MINOR SPOILERS***

You have been warned after this line of text.

Unicorn Gundam, w/ Banagher piloting it, every time you defeat an enemy w/ the Beam Saber attack (not in NT-D mode), Unicorn goes into NT-D mode in the animation and Banagher recites an entire dialogue from a certain episode. I forgot which one, I think it was from episode 4?

00 Raiser's Trans-AM attack is sorta dynamic. When you use the multi-lock system w/ the skill, 00 Raiser uses Raiser Sword whereas you target 1 enemy w/ the skill, 00 Raiser will go into Trans-AM and use its GN Sword III while quantizing.

God Gundam's Exploding God Finger has a kill dynamic change as well. God Gundam no longer enters Hyper Mode at max tension but when using this attack, if it results into a kill, God Gundam goes into Hyper Mode during the animation.

00 Qan[T] w/ Setsuna F. Seiei (Trailblazer ver.) when using Trans-AM attack and resulting a killing blow will cause him to recite the dialogue he says when entering the main ELS body. You also hear Tiera as well.

***END MINOR SPOILERS***

This is all the interesting tibits I've found thus far.

This is how they made cuts and quite honestly, it is a great idea in my opinion. There's also the character drop list and some other mechs removed such as the Three Kingdoms from World.

The thing is though they implemented a great new system, Custom BGM. Ever hated the BGM on a certain character you liked? Well w/ the Custom BGM system, you can change it into something you like... In fact, you can pick whichever character and change it! It's awesome.
