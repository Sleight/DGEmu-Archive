I apologize if I'm repeating something that has been said already but there are so many replies, I'm going dizzy from trying to go through them all!My answer is not simply one over the other. I consider the Simpsons to be something that should be viewed in more than one light. The show has been around for over a decade and has gone through a few "phases". To put it simply, the early incarnation of the Simpsons was more about satire while the present incarnation is more about "craziness". They've "done it all" and seem to lean more towards going nuts in the last few seasons. Basically, the writers seem to be on a trend of creating stories simply for the reason that they're running out of things to do and so the show ends up funny but for a sort of "What the hell just happened?" factor. Don't get me wrong, I'm a fan of Simpsons and there are times when satire works its way into the recent shows but it's a rarity rather than the norm these days. So basically, between "early satire oriented Simpsons" and "current crazy Simpsons", I vote "early Simpsons" over Family Guy but vote for Family Guy over "current Simpsons".Family Guy is a great show. It is sharply satirical while being crazy-funny at times. For me, "early Simpsons" gets the edge in part due to nostalgia. I grew up with the show so it has a special place in my heart. Even so, it's a close race between "early Simpsons" and Family Guy. Family Guy, since day one, is what the "early Simpsons" writers used to be. Writers who were hungry to make comedic statements with the edge of intelligence.The core of comedy is intelligence. We laugh at comedy because when we think about the humour presented, there is a logical pattern that leads to bringing out a laugh. When we "get" the punchline, we can't help but laugh. The humour "makes sense". Silliness, on the other hand, is more of a "reactional" humour in that we laugh because the humour is goofy/silly/non-sensical. Both comedy and silliness makes us laugh but for different reasons. Much of the current Simpsons humour is rooted in silliness, which is why I vote Family Guy over it.
