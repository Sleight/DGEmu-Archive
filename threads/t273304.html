YyonisJul 04 2007, 06:51 PM
For emulation use no$GBA. It works 100% aside from no cel-shading and slow play. You'll have to mess with the settings to get it to work correctly. Here are the settings that I'm using. If it starts going way too fast like Khao said, change Emulation Speed to 16.78 MHz. Go to Options>Emulation Setup. Emu Speed: Ulimited MHz Disaster, 100%, Sound output: Your preference, no sound will give a slight speed boost depending on your computer. Reset/startup: Directly, Sound sample rate: Your preference again... Skip down to NDS Cartridge backup media, and set that as FLASH 512KBytes. That should get it playing. You can mess around with the emulation speed during play without resetting, and depending on the speed of your computer and the game/scenario you'll want to use slowdown x2 or x10 or 16.78 MHz. Make sure to, after finding working settings, go to Options>Save Options or you'll have to set it up every time you play. For the microphone puzzles on the volcano island, use a computer microphone. Note though, that saving can be a bit dodgy.... I saved before entering the first dungeon and saved all throughout it, played through it in one sitting, but the game crashed and it reverted to the save before the dungeon... Save states don't work for me either. I think it only saves your saves when you normally close out of the program, but not if it crashes. I haven't tested that yet, don't hold me to it.Also note that when playing, if the game's running at 50% speed, you'll have to make your touchscreen motions half as quickly because it needs to register the motion over enough frames to know that you're sliding forward to run, and not to stab.Last piece of advice: USE A GUIDE. There's one up on GameFAQs that I used. It's a lot more frustrating to be stuck in the fortune-teller's basement when you don't know that you're supposed to blow on the map in the corner for some reason. Some of the puzzle solutions like that will leave you groaning if you don't use a guide.
