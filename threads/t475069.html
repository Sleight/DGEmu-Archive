KKobato HasegawaORIGINAL POSTFeb 03 2012, 03:13 AM
I'm pretty sure that most of you play your classic console roms on your PC using a standard emulator.There are others that prefer a more genuine feeling: to experience their classic games on the real console.Unfortunately, finding and buying old games is not always easy, and they can easily add up to something very expensive.Most people know of the existence of flash carts thanks to the GBA and DS, but does everyone know about the existence of these carts for retro consoles also?Letting you guys know about 2 great sites for you to acquire those said flash carts, though be ready to pay premium prices for them... but for true retro gamers, it is an investment worth while.Retrozone: http://www.retrousb.com/Specializing in old game reproduction, they also have 2 very interesting products: the NES PowerPak and the SNES PowerPak, two products that will let you load roms into a compact flash and play them directly on your home console.Stone Age Gamer: http://stoneagegamer.com/A very fascinating website for anyone into retro gaming. Their products of interest: Everdrive 64 (N64 flash cart), Everdrive GG (Game Gear flash cart), Everdrive MD (Sega Genesis flash cart), Master Everdrive (Sega Master System flash cart) and the Super Everdrive (SNES flash cart).Asides from flash carts, they also sell used games, Portable NES consoles, and other specialized old school gaming products.There are, of course, several other flash carts available on the market, but I am only bringing up the most popular ones for you guys to see. :fun: ~Meru(I personally own the 2 PowerPaks, planning on getting the Everdrives, but saving the money for a PS Vita first :) )
