The cannon shoots electrons to a Phosphorous Screen which is excited and glows in the right colors.If it is an LCD TV it has a backlight which glows, two polarizers (often perpendicular) and LC between them, divided in pixels. Stokes general theorem states that the integral of a form in the boundary of a manifold equals the integral of its outer derivative in the manifold.There is a simplified stokes theorem, which is to apply this to a bounded surface (in R3), being the form a field. To integrate this inside the surface is the same as integrating the field along its boundary.Gauss Law is the generalized stokes theorem applied to a 3 - manifold (a volume) and using a vector field. Then, the integral of the field along the boundary (a surface) is equal to the integral of its divergence in the volume.
