Well, with the early dump of the Japanese version, let's begin discussing this game.The official timeline puts this game during the period when the Belmonts can't touch the Vampire Killer, for reasons that haven't really been explained. You play a chick (ZOMG NO WAYS) who's been sent by the church to destroy Dracula.From initial play, I'm a bit mixed about this game. Audio wise, it's very nice. Michiro Yamane does a great job once again with music. Level design, on the other hand, SUCKS. I find the design to make Lament of Innocence look good. Graphically, it's very nice, with some interesting bosses (but one early one is a piss poor boss fight).Controls are ok, but could have been done better. I don't see any options for changing button layouts, which would help since Y & X are attack, but trying to switch between them (for the fastest attacking) would be easier if it was Y/A (ala SOTN). Edit: Looks like there are control changes, and just like POR, you can switch between English/Japanese voices.I'm also not fond of being told "you can only use your normal attack if you have MP". Wait, NORMAL ATTACK? Seriously? Yep, your normal attack is completely dependent on if you have MP. Specials use hearts. Which I think is a very dumb move, but at least your MP refills fairly quick, if you do nothing. But the idea is still stupid, IMO.More playing is needed for a full opinion, but initially, I found Portrait of Ruin and even Dawn of Sorrow to be better then this. It's better then some other Castlevania games, but not tops. Sorry.
