Is there anything special needed to play Japanese games on my US system? I don't feel like modding anything, and I do not have a gameshark.
KKirie MotobaJul 01 2012, 03:04 AM
No, the Dreamcast is region free.

The only issue you might encounter is playing European releases with PAL standard, but even then, many games either have a NTSC selector or are patched for NTSC TV sets.

KKirie MotobaJul 01 2012, 08:00 PM
Naw, the only disc swapping to be done is on discs that are not self-bootable.
For those, you will need the Utopia Boot disc to be able to load them properly.

All the games I've uploaded have been played on my Dreamcast and tested to be working.
