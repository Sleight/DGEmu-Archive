I asked my boyfriend (he is engeneer) and the answer is:First You must learn two things:1 - The graphic quality depends on the settings on your TV and the output resolution for a particular game.2 - Even for games with the highest resolution (1080p), the image quality depends on the game image quality (for example, try to compare GTA IV graphic with Folklore graphic, and you'll see that Folklore has better graphic, even if they're both 720p games).Try to look at the video settings under the settings menu, and put the settings to match the ones your TV supports. Try also to change the settings from your tv to optimize game image display (most TVs have an automatic configuration for games, try to use this one as basis and change what you like).Also try to check the quality in smaller TVs, like 32" or 40", maybe your eyes are good enough to see the pixel size difference, and in a smaller tv this is a less significant issue.
