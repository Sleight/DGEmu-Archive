If someone needs visual assistance with a problem you can upload images to the DGEmu PhotoBucket Account, it can also be used to host images for the Calendar, Screenshooting Guide, Support Staff Guide, etc. We have lots of images posted, mostly with the staff member's personal Image Hosting account or imageshack (in which case a few seem to of been deleted), because of this a DGEmu Photobucket Account was made.

<iframe src="http://www.dgemu.com/modutils/staffpassword.php?password=photobucket" height=100px width=300px></iframe>

An example of what you can use it for and images already uploaded are:

Where is the ROM Search?


How do you Jump to "X Forum" (for example the NDS Emulation Forum) from the "Y Forum" (for example the Spam Centre)?



In case you are unfamiliar with PhotoBucket or any Image Hosting website here is an example of how to upload an image. Log into PhotoBucket with the DGEmu Account details mentioned earlier.



Click the Upload button at the top of the screen.



Choose you options;
Upload From - is the image already on the internet of are you uploading it from your computer?
Upload To - Are you already in the folder you wish to upload to?

Depending where you are uploading from fill in the details or click the green button.



Uploaded the image successfully!
