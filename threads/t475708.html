[font=Tahoma, sans-serif]The old era of the early nineties where the boom of the first teenage mutant ninja turtles sky rocketed from across the globe. The fab four became a household name and konami did it right with their first game on the nintendo. [/font][font=Tahoma, sans-serif]Though at first it may appear as a zelda clone, teenage mutant ninja turtles the game is pure old gaming galore. The plot is as simple as an episode of the animated series. Shredder kidnaps april and then kidnaps splinter after rescuing april as plans in making turtle soup of those pesky shell heads. [/font][font=Tahoma, sans-serif]The turtles you control is what makes this game much better than the future installments of the game. You can choose any of the four turtles any time in the game. Leonardo has a wide range of attack with his sword, mike has a limited range attack but his attack speed is faster than leonardo, Rafael has the fastes attack but is the only turtle with the shortest range while donatelo has a much wider attack range than the three but has the slowest attack speed. Along with the turtles primary weapons, you also get the chance to acquire special weapons like the shuriken, three stars, boomerang and other meaner ninja toys. When your turtles life is down to zero, you can also find them in certain dungeons being held prisoner and able to use them again. The life restoring pizza comes in quarter, half and whole which is found in some parts of the dungeons.[/font][font=Tahoma, sans-serif]The game is split into exploration and dungeon platform stages. [/font][font=Tahoma, sans-serif]The exploration stage has you in an over map view of the area, dodging tanks and fending off foot soldiers with your turtles. This will later be added depth as you gain access to your turtle mobile than can destroy those tanks and fire missiles to unlock areas in the map that are inaccesible. And in a certain stage, in metal gear solid europhia, you dodge spotlights at night to avoid detection. [/font][font=Tahoma, sans-serif]The platform stage is where the game shines as you make it to the opposite side of the dungeon or explore them further. Enemies comes in different variety as fire monsters, flying kamikaze bugs to robots with automative flying heads. Boss fights are as simple as dodging and attacking them, which requires of course some skill to really hit the mark without being damaged by their attacks. Certain areas of the dungeons will require you to find an item like the missiles for your turtle mobile and the cool ninja rope for the roof top area. There's also an under water stage race against time to find all the bombs (Which is where the challenge starts kicking in).[/font][font=Tahoma, sans-serif]Overall. This game is not just pure nostalgia, but it's quite possibly the best ninja turtles game ever other than the turtles in time on the SNES. The presentation is outstanding for it's era and the lifetime value is high because of the vast areas of the stages. Its not just a novelty game but a hardcore pit of skills game. [/font]
The Genesis turtles game is(while shorter) better than Turtles in Time. Just sayin.
