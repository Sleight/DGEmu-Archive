My personal favorite Craymel setup:Keele: Celsius, Efreet, Rem, Sekundes, & SylphMeredy: Maxwell, Shadow, Volt, Undine, & GnomePutting Celsius & Volt in this setup lets Keele use Absolute. Keele can also use Explode in this set up. Rem and Maxwell in this setup will also allow Keele to use Holy Lance. Sekundes obviously lets him use Distortion. Sylph & Shadow will also give Keele Cyclone. Just turn summon Rem off so he won't be summoning her when you don't need it.With Meredy, she'll have Meteor Swarm, and since Sekundes is in Keele's CC, she can use Shooting Stars, thus allowing her to use Summon Arche under the right conditions (Maxwell vitality max, use Shooting Stars). she'll also have Heal, as well as Nurse, and obviously Spread (Don't bother with Restore. It's just Nurse in a set spot). She'll also get Resurrection. Putting Celsius in Keele's cage lets Volt give her Indignation. She also gets Dark Force and Bloody Howl. Gnome just gives her Stalagmite and a few support skills. She'll also have Charge, from Volt, but it's useless pretty much. Since all her other attack spells are pretty useless, you'll want her to have Meteor Swarm & Shooting Stars so she can get some good damage in when she's not healing.(You could switch Sylph & Shadow around, to get Blizzard for Keele, but you'll miss out on the best Wind spell if you do that)If I may state my opinion, you don't want to give one of them ALL of the strongest attack spells. Otherwise your battles will go slower than they need too. Celsius will reach Max Vitality frequently in this CC setup as well, since Keele's AI seems to like spamming Freeze Lancer & Absolute. Undine will also get Max Vitality alot because of all the Healing Meredy will be doing. Maxwell won't get Max Vitality a whole lot, but when he does, make sure you ahve a Mental Ring equipped, and take control of Meredy and summon him to do the Dual the Sol extensions.
