Oopark86May 11 2011, 10:27 AM
I played it for 2 months before cancelling my subscription.Leveling from 1-50I had a ton of fun here.The scenery is fantastic and most zones have their own flavor (snow, desert, mountainous, barren, etc).The gameplay starts out pretty basic but get more and more complex as you get higher leveled (macro's are pretty handy here).They throw you three souls (subclasses) at you pretty quick but you should be ok if you just focus on one soul (and one offsoul) until you get used to the game.At 50, there are a ton of soul combinations (I respecced my warrior a good 50+ times even with 4 roles).You can go from quest hub to quest hub but you can also level purely off pvp (starting at lvl10) and also rifting (closing rifts and participating in zone events).I found rifting enjoyable because I really enjoyed exploring the landscape.They reward exploring with collectibles called artifacts as well as achievements and experience.EndgameWhen my guild downed Greenscale (dragon raid boss), my hands were shaking, guildmates were screaming in vent, euphoria all around, etc.I don't remember experiencing that kind of thing since downing Ragnaros from vanilla WoW.River of Souls (new death rift raid) is a bit bland for me.Downed the 1st 2 bosses but I didn't feel as excited as when we downed Greenscale for the first time.But there is plenty of endgame content for sure.These raids I mentioned are instanced but there are also outdoor raid rifts you can do.Faction grinding is a bit dull, and crafting is stereotypical (i got to 300 weaponsmithing/armorsmithing but none of epic gear i could craft came close to raid loot).There is also a very questline called the saga quests.You start the quest at lvl 10 and continue on this storyline questline all the way up to lvl 50.The saga quests usually have to be done with other players due to difficulty.There's a lot of lore and story in here if you enjoy that aspect.I ended up doing the entire questline at lvl 50 so I could solo most of it.But the final 10 or so steps require an entire raid of people to finish the objectives.The rewards along the way is definitely worth it though.I didn't really do sub50 PvP so I don't know what that's like.Endgame PvP was not very balanced before I left.Pyro mages and Sab Rogues pretty much dominated every other subclass.It can still be fun though.Overall, I think it is a worthwhile game to try to out.It is more "themepark" than "sandbox" but the game is pretty solid (relatively bugless, great visuals, good class system).The game doesn't revolutionize MMORPG's but it takes all the good parts of previous fantasy MMORPG's and adds a few bells and whistles (mainly calling/soul class system and rifts).I think I sank about 500 hours into the game before I decided to move on.
