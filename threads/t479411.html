KKirie MotobaJul 08 2013, 04:53 AM

But that's what often happens though.

 

On a laptop, as well as a Macbook, any of those USB ports, power charger port, eSATA, and whatever you have on the sides are all directly integrated into the motherboard (or circuit board for any of you fancy Mac people).

On only a few models that some of those are on seperate boards, and usually, they're bulkier / uglier / older devices.

 

But then again, a motherboard for a laptop ranges between 100 to 300, depending on the age and the model, Mac circuit boards start at 400+ no matter how old they are.

 

One more fancy thing to note: Do not, ever screw up the touchpad on your Mac. In most cases, to replace that "touchpad", you'll be replacing the whole palm rest + keyboard + touchpad as 1 piece, and that also costs a freaking fortune.
