KKiruria StrigaOct 13 2015, 02:00 AM

My favourite is Gen 5.  Black/White and its sequels had the best storylines of any main-series Pokemon game to boot, by a landslide.  I used to think Team Rocket was hands-down the best Pokemon crime syndicate ever, but now Team Plasma is a good contender as well.  Awesome battle music, PETA-like agenda, very interesting characters (especially N and Ghetsis, but Colress and the Shadow Triad are interesting too), also interesting battles (especially with N in the first games - where else do you get to battle a trainer with a legendary Pokemon?)... Plus at least in my opinion, the region itself had the most places to explore, and I like how much they expanded the region in the second installments.

 

I also liked the changing seasons - not only did it affect the types of Pokemon you can find, but also some scenery changes a la Oracle of Seasons, and subtle changes in the route music as well.  I really like the music in those games in general actually - partially because of the ton of variable mixes in there (a few towns give you the option of adding instruments to the music), partially because of awesome town themes (especially those of Floccesy Town, Driftveil City, and Icirrus City), and partially because of the awesome final battle themes from the first games.  (It's obvious that Ghetsis took battle theme advice from Sephiroth, what with his name being heard and all.)

 

Those games were also the first to make TMs reusable, something I considered a godsend.  I also liked the rustling patches of grass mechanic, especially since the high-EXP-giving Audino appeared very commonly in those - very useful for grinding.  Also the first games to have trainers (doctors and nurses) that heal your Pokemon - very useful in areas like Twist Mountain where there are trainers everywhere.

 

I have mixed opinions about the side games in those:

 

Battle Subway: Kind of meh compared to the Battle Frontier in previous games.  The only thing I ever used it for was to get to Anville Town.

 

Pokemon Musical: AWFUL.  The Pokemon contests from the 3rd gen were way better.  There's like no skill involved in the musical - you just wave props around and hope for the best.  I really didn't like how certain Pokemon couldn't hold certain props either.

 

Join Avenue: THE best thing to happen to Black 2/White 2.  I used to love getting all the shops and buying stuff from them.  Great way to get level ups, friendship increases, and EVs as well, even more so than the Secret Base pals from ORAS.

 

Dream World: I'm mixed on this one.  On one hand, I considered it a great way to get Pokemon with hidden abilities and egg moves, and also rare berries (check other people's shelves).  On the other, I didn't like that that was the only way to grow berries, and that since it was an online thing, they had to discontinue it after a while.

 

Dream Radar: It was kind of interesting, but I certainly did look like a fool while waving my 3DS around in front of other people who asked what I was doing.  At least it was a good way to get Riolus besides finding rare ones in Floccesy Ranch.

 

PWT: OMG this was awesome.  Battling gym leaders and Elite 4 members from previous games?  Huge nostalgia trip.  Only thing I don't like about it is that it uses Battle Frontier-esque rules, meaning I can't sweep the Johto Elite 4 with Kyurem.

 

Pokestar Studios: This was a pretty neat concept.  What I really liked about it was that it forced you to think of specific strategies for certain battles, against foes that weren't actual Pokemon, and sometimes with weird type and ability combinations.

 

Black Tower/White Tree Hollow: What really surprised me about this is that you can get EXP from the trainer battles.  Great for raising high-level Pokemon.
