Holy crap, I very vaguely remember making this thread earlier in the year. I'm actually sad this didn't get more replies/arguments, I think it's a somewhat thought provoking subject.

 

Anyways, recently I helped my brother get an (Wii) Atari emulator and a copy of every Atari game because for some weird ass reason he wanted to play some Atari games. Doing this took me about 10 minutes, I found a working Wii Atari emulator and a batch torrent of every Atari game (conveniently in under 9MB). I'm not going to say it's right or wrong, but I just want to list some Pros and Cons:

 

Pros:

-Very convenient, downloading nearly 800 games took less than 5 minutes and only took up the physical space of 1 SD card VS a room full of cartridges

-Free (durr)

-Helped my brother relive childhood + share the games he grew up with with his kids

-Finding physical copies all those Atari games this year (2013) would be expensive, difficult and take up a lot of space (assuming it's even possible).

-Finding a working Atari would also be somewhat difficult and more of a hassle than necessary.

 

Cons:

-Some person(s) out there missed out on the money that would have gone into buying the games + console

-Non-authentic copies of games (if you care about having authentic games/collect games)

-Illegal as we don't own a physical Atari or physical copies of the "backups" (ROMs)

-Not much of a DIRECT con, but the emulator was a Wii homebrew which required a modded Wii (is it illegal to mod consoles yet? I know pirating games is illegal and all that but not sure if they've made modding consoles illegal)

 

So yeah, I had some trouble coming up with Cons, if you want, feel free to add some.

Anyways, here's just a view on the subject. We technically did something technically wrong, but was it that big of a deal? I realize this is less relevant than say, modding an Xbox 360 and pirating games for it, but nonetheless it's still piracy and still illegal.

 

Also, on the matter of what piracy is, it's exactly what the name says, stealing. How is downloading ROMs/movies stealing? Stealing is getting/taking something that costs money without paying for it. Simple as that. Downloading ROMs is stealing from the game devs/companies, downloading music from websites or from music is stealing from the Artist/companies behind them, etc.
