Back when I was really young (I think I was like 9 or 10) and when YGO first started becoming popular (I'm talking like, when Pharoah's Servant JUST came out and fat neckbeards were stockpiling the booster crates) my older brother (about 24-ish), his somewhat new wife and her little brother (who was 2 years older than me) used to go to this one comic shop every saturday to play in $5 tournaments (mostly against like fucking 30yo adults and we usually always lost in the first round) and just socialize and try to trade cuards. The one time I DIDN'T go, my sister in law's brother was talking to 2 older kids and they said they wanted to trade cards with him (we used to keep our cards for trade in those binders with the pyramid on them in the first picture) but they left their cards in the trunk of their card, so he went outside with them alone. They punched him and he fell down, while he was down they nabbed his binder and ran off. He called my older brother and the friend my brother had made at the same comic store that was in the Navy and they chased the kids down and got his cards back (turns out the kids didn't even have a car, they were running to home base). It turned into a whole big thing with court and the kids getting probation for assault and petty theft and shit.

 

Man, when YGO was still fresh kids were going APESHIT. It was nuts. When I was in middle school (around the time YGO's popularity was dying out and only nerds liked them) I used to trade with some of the other nerds at school because they didn't know shit about cuard values and I used to rip them off. I used to have to pay this big black kid I knew to be my body guard while trading in case the nerds tried ganging up on me and stealing my shit.
