You can take on the role of either a Terrorist or a Counter Terrorist. Each with its own objective. There are 2 different types of missions that you can undertake while playing in offline mode, 1)Hostage Situation, where the Counter Terrorist will either try and kill all the terrorist or rescue and bring back the hostages to the hostage safe zone. Whilst the terrorist objective is to guard the hostages and kill off any Counter Terrorist forces.
2)Bomb Situation, where the Counter Terrorist will need to diffuse the bomb before it goes off, even if you kill all Terrorist units you will still need to defuse the bomb or it will still be mission failure. Whilst the Terrorist need to plant and protect the bomb to see that it either successfully goes off, or kill all Counter Terrorist forces.

There is not much difference between Counter Strike Source and that of Condition Zero(aka 1.6) besides the fact that the graphics quality has a huge improvement along with a few changes to the maps (ie Assault map has changed). This is a standalone game and DOES NOT require half life, and YES multiplayer works, although you can only play in the non steam servers if you do not wish to play multilayer VIA steam.
