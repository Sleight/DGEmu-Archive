[topic="208070"]I posted already in the other, wrong topic..[/topic] Is it worth it? Yes, but don't spend your last penny (it's not worrying-a-little-about-rent good), and if you don't like rpgs, puzzle games, or shooter/stealth games I'm not sure how much else there is. There are some really good games, and it's a really good system, but the best thing to say is that it's not an innovative system. The most fun you'll have with it is in playing the kind of thing you already like, not (like on a DS) suddenly discovering something bizarre, like a really funny lawyer sim?!BUT I FORGOT ABOUT DUNGEON SIEGE! And it's really a pretty good game, definitely the best hack-and-slash on the PSP. I've mostly played as the rogue type character, an elf named Serin, because you have to love a character who can become a ninja, assassin, or ranger (not so much a happy tree-loving ranger, more a blind maelstrom of ranged destruction.) Graphics are pretty good, and there's even sort of a plot, at least for that character. I've yet to finish the game with the mage (as a necromancer, his White Wizard path was too Gandalf-y) but little bits of the game seem different for him, which is a nice touch in hack-and-slash. And if you've played a Dungeon Siege before, you'll be pleased to know that your party can include a mule!
