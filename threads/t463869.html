EEsrayelDec 09 2010, 11:46 PM
Had a bit of experience with other Pokemon games (and numerous other non-Pokemon games) that were GB/GBC/GBA and I'm just echoing what the person above me has said because it fits with my experience. Most of the time when you buy games that are more than 5 years old, you'll confront issues with the internal battery dying at some point (or if you still play your games from WAAAY long ago). A lot of websites specialize in replacing these batteries, and so it is rarely much trouble to buy a copy of the game already modified to last for another 10 or so years, or you can sometimes send off your cartridge to some guy to have its battery replaced(googling would probably find a number of people offering this service, I would expect). The battery, from what I understand, causes 90% of the problems that these old games have with saving, and you generally cannot play the game in any reasonable way if the battery is indeed drained. Sadly it is a fact of the old technology, but certainly not something that is irreparable.
