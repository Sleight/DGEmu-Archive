Just a reply about the "not joining teamfight" comments, split pushing top/bot constantly is actually a legit strategy, its often used when when your team is behind and unable to win teamfights and you want to get farm while avoiding fights

And some champs don't contribute much in a teamfight so they want to be split pushing all the time anyways...also having teleport/global ult allows you to split push and still be in fights.   

 

In any case, the point of split pushing is so that you get strong enough that the enemy team can't push you out of lane with one champ so they have to send 2 or more people to kill you, which gives your team a timeframe where they can take other objectives uncontested such as dragon, mid towers, enemy jungle, or force a 4v3 fight in another lane.  So when you see someone always split pushing and not willing to join fights, sure they might be selfish or a dick but instead of blaming them, you should keep aware of enemy positions and take objectives when you see their team congregating towards the solo push, that's how split pushing contributes to the team.  In the case where the enemy ignores the pusher and forces teamfights anyways, the team's job is to disengage and stall so that the pusher can take many objectives themselves.  This will eventually lead to a backdoor where the rest of the team's job is just to keep the enemy from recalling, this is also strategy that you can implement (often when you're losing teamfights anyways)

 

Also some champs that can push and run away well even when underfarmed (Singed for example) can split push nonstop and "feed" the enemy team to the point where their KDA is ruined but their own bounty is really low, and the enemy team still has to constantly send people on long chases to get trivial kill gold while again taking the pressure off the rest of your team. 

 

 

So yeah, being solo all the time doesn't necessarily mean you're not contributing to your team, its to become a distraction that the enemy team can't ignore.  Another thing to consider is that maybe you shouldn't be grouping up all the time and hovering around non-base turrets that aren't going down anytime soon, consider farming your own lanes more often and forcing the enemy team to split up and come to you (I noticed this because our CS is often really bad in long games)

Tag along, you should quickly get enough for a Philosopher's stone, which then I complete with Shurelya's Reverie.

Second item I focus on getting is Boots of mobility, then move to a locket of the iron Solari.

After that, you decide, Runic Bulwark, Morellonom, Rylal, or whatever. You can also get the "Captain" enchantment for your boots to get your teammates faster into a battle you're supporting.

 

As for my leveling up, I go in this order:

W, E, W, E, Q, R, W, W, W, E, R, E, E, Q, Q, R, Q, Q

 

Your goal is to support your team as much as you can: W to heal the person you're laning with, E to silence the enemy when your team is going in for the kill, E to restore mana when you're around someone that uses too much mana.

Q is very useless in most cases, and the ult, well, that's quite easy to use, just roam the map and check on the level of health on your teammates before ulting to heal. Also a good thing to do is when you're quiet on your lane, but that in the mini map you see your team is engaging the enemy or getting ganked, just pay attention to their health bars, and ult when their near death. You always save 1 or 2 with it.

 

Boots of mobility, you pretty much run as fast as a Miss Fortune with boots outside of combat, making your escapes slightly easier and stuff.

 

And obvious rule of thumb when being support: Attack the minions in a timely fashion to bring them down near death, and let the person you're supporting get the kill. I do it all the time with TM, and I think we coordinate pretty darn well when I'm supporting his Caitlyn.
