nikrocks1 wrote:
Humans are animals, neh? It is our own ingenuity that we bring upon ourselves that helps us to develop our concepts of good or bad. A person that grows up with NO intelligent direction, no sway, no preconcieved notions about how to live, will not be unlike a wild animal. Now, of course we have to say that the child would survive, for our own purposes :yess: But my problem is that a child wouldn't ever be able to be raised without human influence, seeing as how we're made to be with other humans from birth; we're social beasts. I'm being roundabout, but my point is that while this forum would like to know what an unguided child would turn out to be, I say that "good" or "bad" doesn't really describe the direction it would take. Because we are so intelligent, even from birth we take in signs and learn from everything we sense. A developing human would take in whatever was around it and become that, or try to. If a baby somehow survived without human influence it would be a feral child, and act like whatever animal (or robot) raised it or was around it. So if one considers acting like an animal and using less intelligence than instinct "bad" then I guess I'd have to agree that that's how the baby'd turn out.
