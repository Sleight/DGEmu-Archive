0.1 NotesEven though this is only supposed to be a FAQ, I feel like there are several things I should remind you of.- The Rules & Guidelines are very important. You should go take a look if you haven't done so yet.- If you do not know what GPs/DPs are, read this.- For ROM troubleshooting, read this thread. - When you start a topic, make sure you precisely point out in the title what will be discussed in the thread itself. This is important for searching purposes. (Also for the fact that it's just plain annoying to see a thread titled "Hello")- We won't change our compressing method.Even though there are more efficiente ones (like RAR, 7Zip, UHARC, etc), those methods are not as "user friendly" as Zip is. Also, most emulators already have a built-in zip support; this does not happens to the other ones.If we stored our ROMs in those formats, members would need to download, decompress (un-rar, un-7z, un-UHARC, etc) then recompress (zip, optional) in order to play their favorite games into their emulators.Also, not many people know how to deal with other compressing formats.- Do not ask the forum to be translated into another language. We won't do this since this would require a lot of work and our Staff is already very busy.If you can't speak English, check an online translator, ask help from a friend or take some English classes.We have members from all around the World, and if we were to add language support to every existing one we would end up with a very fragmented board, with regional discussion threads, most of which would only mind people who speak that specific language.Only place where foreign language is accept is Spam Center. So, please, make sure you only post in English elsewhere.0.2 RulesRules for the Support & Suggestions section. If you have 50 Global DPs at the end of the day, it will reset to 12.5. If you have 2 Global DPs at the end of the day, it will still reset to 12.5.Q: I registered today. Why do I only have 10 DPs?A: At the first 24 hours, new members' DP will reset to 10 instead of 12.5. Just wait until next day, and you'll get 12.5 like everyone else.Q: When do global DPs reset? A: It is supposed to be 12AM GMT+2. Might take some time to happen (mainly if the server is busy).Q: How can I download expensive ROMs if I get no more than 12.5 DPs a day?A: You can convert to DPs the GPs you have gained from posting. Here's a list of all the DPs and what they stand for:- Global DPs: Those are used for any kind of ROM. However, they reset to 12.5 each day.- NCDPs: Those are used for NES, SNES and N64 ROMs only.- NDS/GBx DPs: Those are used for GBA, GBC, Gameboy and NDS ROMs only.- Atari DPs: Those are used for Atari Jaguar/Lynx ROMs only- Retro DPs: Those are used for Sam Coupe and Wonderswan ROMs only.- Sega DPs: Those are used for SMS, Genesis and Gamegear ROMs only.Note: Specific DPs are used prior to Global DPs.Note: You cannot convert decimals. Don't create a topic complaining about this, cause it won't be changed.*NOTE: You might also want to take a look at the DP Loan feature.Q: I want to download bigger ROMs, but global DPs keep reseting daily. Can I convert my GPs into specific DPs and combine these with global DPs?A: Yes. Nowadays seems you can use both Specific & Global DPs together to download bigger ROMs. Just keep in mind that Specific DPs are used prior to Global DPs.So, if you have, say, 13 NDS/GBx GP + 5 NCDP + 12.5 global DP and want to download a 20MB GBA ROM and a 8MB SNES ROM, you'll use 13 NDS/GBx + 7 global DPs and 5 NCDP + 3 global DPs, having 0 NDS/GBx + 0 NCDP + 2.5 global DPs left at the end of the process. Note that no matter in what order you download the ROMs, you'll have the same result (if Global DPs were used prior to Specific DPs, you would not be able to get both ROMs).Q: How come some members have negative GPs?A: Mods can deduct GPs for posts that are considered redundant/useless.Just follow the Rules, read our Warn FAQ, use our Search Engine and read pinned topics prior to posting and you should have no similar problem.BTW, posts considered to be excellent might be awarded GPs as well.Q: I tried to download a game only to find out I don't have enough DP's. I could have sworn I had more than enough. What's the deal?A: DP's are used prior to download. If you clicked the download button and cancel for whatever reason, your DP's have been used even though you didn't start the download at all. Also, if you double clicked the download button, your DP's have been used twice instead of once. Just be patient and everything will work properly.Q: Is it just me or do I get more points than usual when I post?A: The points you get for posting are now 10 for a new topic and 6 for a reply. Q: I have converted XX GPs into DPs, but they aren't showing on my Download page: it keeps saying I'm too poor to download a ROM; what should I do? A: Most likely, your browser is showing you cached content. Reload the page and you should be able to see the correct value. Q: ROMs are too expensive! Can't they get cheaper? A: 1DP = 1MB. Therefore, the cost is proprotional to our bandwhich usage. That means we won't lower it.Q: A few days after the forum software upgrade, I did not receive GPs at all for my posts! Can you refund me those GPs?A: No, we will not reimburse those GPs that were not given, as it would be extremely hard to determine the number of GPs that we should reimburse, should we decide to do so. Anyway, you don't have to worry, as all GPs functions work now, including the awarding of GPs after making posts, and as the breakdown of the GPs function only lasted for a few days. Please do not request for the reimbursement of GPs anymore.Q: Can you convert DPs to GPs / console DPs to global DPs?A: There is no way to convert unless you have donated. Console DPs cannot be converted to Global DPs under any circumstance, however console DPs can be converted to other console DPs if you have donated.1.3 ROM downloading/Main site related questionsQ: Is there an easier way to get larger ROMs?A: Once you reach 50 posts, try our new DP Loan system. Disable or uninstall the software that's causing the problem, then try again.A3: Are you using an INTERNET CONNECTION accelerator (Not to be mistaken with download accelerators)? Certain ISP's use accelerators for their dial-up clients. If that's the case, you should either manually turn it off or contact your ISP about it.A4: If you are using Internet Explorer, a yellow bar or a security message may appear at the top of the screen. If you're a guest or somesuch, you may not be allowed to install/download software.A7: While downloading ROMs on DGEmu, right-click (instead of left-clicking) the Download button, and select 'Save Target As' (or 'Save Link As' if you use Firefox).A8: Before you download a ROM of DGEmu, ensure that you have at least two times as much free space as the file size of what you're going to be downloading in your hard drive, more than that if you're doing something like browsing the web while the files are downloading.Otherwise, your download might fail, and your DPs will be deducted even though you will not get any ROMs from the download.Q: I downloaded a ROM, but too many DP's were deducted.
