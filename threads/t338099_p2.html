Hi, I'd like to join in the fun. Light acts like a particle in the case of reflection(particles bouncing off a surface), blackbody radiation(variable frequency of radiation emitted) and refraction(deviation of light due to presence of optical media molecules). It shows wave character in diffraction(it deviates due to curving of the surface media) and in the Young's double slit experiment(destructive and constructive interference.As for the cannonball problem, it's velocity becomes zero after 3 seconds(assuming g=10) and using the displacement formula we get the max height to be around 45m (30*3 - 5*9). The cannonball will fall right back into the cannon because there's no repulsive force in this case offered by air. As for the force exerted by the explostion, the mass of the ball is 2kg and the instantaneous acceleration is 30m/s^2 when it is shot, F=ma yields 60 as the force due to the explosion
