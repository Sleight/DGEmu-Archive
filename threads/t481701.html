Well, changing from those old Core 2 Duo boards to newer boards = need to change your RAM. You<re going from DDR2 to DDR3, so definitely need to keep RAM in your budget right away. 

Also, Biostar motherboards... Litterally stay away from those. They are the low end of low ends. Even going with a MSI board is better than that. I'd recommend to stick with a basic Gigabyte board, those are usually far more reliable than low end MSI and Biostar boards.

 

Processor itself is ok, no matter what procs you choose, it'll outperform that old Core 2 Duo.

Just at the price you're spending, if you really save up and put some more, you can get far better processors.
