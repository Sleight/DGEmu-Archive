Welcome, everybody, to the Screenshotters Mini-Blitz 2! I'd like to thank everybody who's participating for taking a little time away from your school work to participate.First, I invite everybody to read the FAQ thread which will cover most of your questions.Make sure you read the assignment thread, as that thread contains your (and everybody else's) assignment. You are to do YOUR assignment and YOUR assignment only. When you complete it, please post here saying that your assignment is complete. A completed assignment consists of screenshotting all of your games, and uploading these images to the server, along with adding genre and Emulator listings. (Note: If the ROM is unplayable, the use of our substitute placeholder images will be accepted in its place for the title image. Please make a note of this in the report that you'll make).If you have any questions, concerns, ideas, or whatever, it goes in THIS THREAD ONLY. Do not make a new thread, do not make a thread outside this forum (as most of the other staff can't help you anyways), and don't post in any other thread in this forum. Non-report threads in this forum will be blasted to the 15th dimension.Some games may already have screenshots, but possibly other information is missing.First, check to see what is needed, whether it's genre or emulator info. Then also check to see if the title and in-game shots are both uploaded and are good screenshots (the right game, good quality). If there's a problem with the present images, let us know and we can remove them for you.I've created some sample threads for you from work I've done while getting ready for our contest. Check them out for an idea on how to make your own.---OK. Most of you will be assigned to sets that will have duplicate games, some may be bad dumps, some may be good dumps, pirates, and other such alternates. You will be required to take a screenshot for each game regardless of the number of duplicates. You are also required to take a screenshot of each duplicate as well. You must use a new shot for the duplicate for it to be counted. Below will be an example.GB: fake - Smash Bros.
