BBossmanAug 04 2012, 12:07 AM
Well, is there a way to transfer your save files from an emulator (desmume) to your nds?
MMo­lh.Aug 04 2012, 12:31 AM
Yup. You'll need a flashcart, though! Just "Export Backup Memory" to obtain a raw savefile, convert the file to the appropriate savetype for the flashcart, and place it in the flashcart's SD card. Make sure that it has the same name as the ROM (except for the file extension, of course), and then you're done! :)
