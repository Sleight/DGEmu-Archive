SshadowmanwkpMay 24 2012, 12:21 PM
Yep you can still use slot-2 (gba game slot) when playing ds games when using a flashcart, it doesn't matter one bit. What you simply have to do is putting your gba game in and start up the ds game and get to the point that requires the gba game. However, you can't do this if you only have a slot 1 cart (like your R4), you will need a slot-2 flashcart as well, capable of playing ds games. I know there's a guide floating around somewhere explaining it in much more detail than I can, but I have to do some digging around.
