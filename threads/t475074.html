KKobato HasegawaFeb 04 2012, 07:53 PM
To be honest, I don't think it's necessary for you to perform the fix as the 3DS flash carts seems to be in perfect working order.Go directly to their download page here: http://www.r4i-sdhc.com/downloade.aspDownload their latest English firmware, version 1.52bOnce you load up the cart on the DSi, you should get your R4i 3DS menu.Click on the icon on the right side.Then in the menu, click on "Upgrade", and at the confirmation screen, click "Start"Make sure your DSi is fully charged, the upgrade can take about 2 minutes or so from my experience with a R4.After that, make sure to apply a skin offered by the card.If all that is done, your card is good to go.If you load a rom and get a white screen, that's actually not an issue with the card, but with the rom that needs patching. That's a completely different issue from the fix suggested on their site above.
