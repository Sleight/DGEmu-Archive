YWE' post='908499 wrote on Dec 13 2005, 05:29 PM:
Of course compared to today's standards the early DW games suck.But if you compare them to the games that came out around the same times, they were excellent games.
Hmm.DW1: 1986. FF1: 1987. DW2: 1987. FF2: 1988. DW3: 1988. Now, I liked the Dragon Warrior series, don't think I'm just here to pump Final Fantasy and down DW.. .. but if you compare the DW series to the games that came out around the same time, you find that the DW series was 1) not as consistent or well defined from a graphical style point of view, 2) had poorer coding both for gameplay and pushing the sound engine (which game's music you like better from an aesthetic point of view is a different matter), 3) had a less cohesive plot (and I'm not just referring to translations; the first couple DW games really had less storytelling going on), 4) had less complex gameplay. My conclusion is that they were still fun for their time, but 'excellent' is not the term I would use. 'decent' is more fitting.
