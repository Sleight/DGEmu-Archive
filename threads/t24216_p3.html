Star Ocean 2 is pretty rightfully regarded as one of the best RPGs for the PSX, wouldn't call it underrated. Chrono Cross is fairly underrated too, I'd say. Street Fighter III: Third Strike is regarded by hardcore fans as one of, if not the best 2D fighting game of all time... But the general public hated everything SF3.
