KKirie MotobaJan 05 2012, 04:50 AM
Ya, the firmware and operating system is not the same thing.It's like the bios on your computer vs the operating system. Basically, the firmware will upgrade the hardware chip on the flash cart itself, as the operating system (in your case, AKAIO) is what is running on the card. The best recommended console for upgrading a flash card firmware is usually the DS Lite, and the only real reason is because if you updated the DSi or 3DS to a newer firmware, the card won't be able to load to update itself... whereas the DS Lite doesn't have those issues to worry about.
