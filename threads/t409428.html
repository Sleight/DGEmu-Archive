YYungTerrorM1Mar 24 2009, 06:13 PM
What you have to do is make sure u have full armor, full health and your Carbine Rifle and then when the shooting part starts take cover between the doors and blow up the cars. As soon as Packie and Derrick start running follow them and make sure you always stay with them. Whenever Packie and Derrick stop to start shooting watch the opposite side to make sure nobody is shooting from behind and then help them kill the rest when your rear is clear. You will see a cut scene soon enough where they enter the underground subway. At this point you will have to follow them down there and kill those N. :( .S.E. army guys. Take cover behind a pillar and start shooting and a little while after the train comes Packie and Derrick will jump down on the tracks and start running, follow them. When your on the tracks its best to stay in the middle to avoid getting run down by and train. Run a little ahead of Packie and Derrick, turn around and start shooting at the cops that come down on the tracks. Run down the tracks, hop up on the next platform you see, make your way up the stairs. The best thing to do is to equip your shotgun at this point because when you get to the last stairs there is most likely going to be 4 or 5 cops near the exit. Just make sure you don't stall at this part, trust me and just run right out there and kill them with a couple pumpshot. (If you don't run right out the station they will be right up against the door and you will get never be able to make it outside trust me on this.) So now that your outside, you will notice that your wanted level went down from 5 stars to 3. Grab the first taxi or 4-door car you see and evade them. Then just go home and your done.When your complete this mission, you'll get $250,000 and your Full Exploration Achievement.
