Call of Duty 3 was... Well... Not so good. Call of Duty 2 was far better and more entertaining of a game than COD 3. Now Call of Duty 4 comes along with far better multiplayer mechanics. The game's multiplayer mode has far more depth and requires more skill both in choosing a loadout and in playing the actual game modes.Plus Call of Duty 3 just plain sucks.
