1) All snapshots must be good quality snaps. We reserve the right to reject snaps that are not of good quality.
2) All snapshots must follow the screenshotting standard. This standard will be listed at the beginning of the contest. Please read it thoroughly. Any snaps not following this standard will be rejected.
3) In order to classify as a “runner-up”, 1 full set must be completed. If this is not done, a runners-up award will not be awarded, and a penalty will apply if the penalty rules are in effect.
4) Judges reserve the right to kick anybody out of the contest for purposely making a mess of the screenshotting project, whether it’s uploading screenshots improperly, breaking other rules, etc.
5) All decisions by the judges are FINAL. Anybody contesting, complaining, or whining to the judges or anywhere on DGEmu Forum will automatically be disqualified from receiving any award, and possibly other penalties for breaking forum rules.
6) Those disqualified from the contest run the risk of being barred from future Screenshotting contests.
7) At the mid-point in the month, we run a check on progress. While we specify that you have a month to do the set, we expect to see some progress towards completing that minimum. For more details, see the Penalty notice section at the bottom of this thread.
8) Judges can amend the rules if necessary at any time.

Image Rules
1) All images posted MUST be from the game you're screenshotting!
Exception: There are special circumstances when you can use a placeholder image. For instance, pornographic games, games that do not work properly, etc. Use our official placeholder images.
2) Images must be under 30KB. Any images larger are automatically rejected by the system.
3) Images must be of "decent" quality. Try to make the images as close to 30KB as you can without going over. Don't post some 1KB image that is completely blurred to the max. We WILL make you redo the image(s).
4) Images must be properly labeled, including extension. So 1019.jpg is a good file name, while banana.jpg and 1019.JPG are not and are automatically rejected by the system. Title shots are #.jpg and in-game shots are #_1.jpg, where # is the game id # you see in the browser's address bar. Box art does NOT need to be uploaded. If you want to, that's fine, but this is NOT REQUIRED. For those that want to, the image naming scheme is #_b.jpg.
5) While we don't expect you to play each game for an hour, don't take an in-game shot from 5 milliseconds into the game. Try to show something to the game that makes it clear to the person who's gonna download it later.
6) Title shots should (of course) clearly show the title.
7) If you're not sure what exactly to snap (some games have confusing locations of titles, may not show a good title image, or may have multiple title screens), please contact one of the judges, or post in the discussion thread. We will help you out with your question.
8) Images should meed the size specifications listed under the emulator listing on the assignment page. In the case of MAME, if an image is over 350 pixels on both height and width, resizing by 50% on both dimensions is required (also, please note any games where you resized images in your report). Resizing Sam Coupe screenshots by 50% on both dimensions is REQUIRED. Any questions about this, please ask a judge.

Special Rule in regards to Atari 2600 Sets:
Most Atari 2600 games didn't have a title screen. The best thing to take is a screen with a © Copyright on it, or the very first thing you see. If you still have questions on what to take, please tell us which game (give the ID#) via posting it in the discussion thread and we will help you as best as possible.

Prize Rules
At the end of the month (August 6th 11:59PM CST), the contest will end and tallies totaled. Here's what the winners get!
First Place: 500 DPs (1 DP system of your choice) or 20,000 GPs.
Second Place: 250 DPs (1 DP system of your choice) or 10,000 GPs.
Third Place: 125 DPs (1 DP system of your choice) or 5,000 GPs.
Fourth Place: 75 DPs (1 DP system of your choice) or 3,000 GPs.
All runners up: 50 DPs (To NDS/GBx DPs only).

In the event of a draw, each person in the tie will split even the prize for each of the positions.
Ex: 3 people qualify for 1st place. Thus, they will split the 500, 250, and 125 prizes equally amongst them (each will get 291 points)
Ex: 2 people qualify for 2nd place. Thus, they will split the 250 and 125 prizes equally amongst them (each will get 187 points)
Ex: 5 people quality for 1st place. Thus, they will split the 500, 250, 125, and 75 prizes (as well as 1 50-point runner up prize) (each will get 190 points, as well as 10 additional Nintendo Handheld points)
(Note: In case of partial points from these prizes, DP totals are rounded down. So the 2nd example would not give 187.5, but 187.0 for the sake of simplicity.)

Note: Added starting in February 2008, the top 4 places will have a choice on DPs or GPs, but not both. However, if you have a split prize (see examples above) the GP total you earn will be based upon the DPs you would earn. So if example 1 applied (3 in first place) you would get either 291 DPs or 11,640 GPs. If your prize involves a runner up prize (such as in example 3), if you want GPs, the total would only be based upon the top 4 prizes.Those that get runner up places do not have a choice - they will get NDS/GBx DPs.
