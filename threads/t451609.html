I am beginning an info thread on Batman: Arkham Asylum 2. I will be posting regularly whenever I discover new information about the already anticipated sequel to one of the best games of 2009. We knew it was inevitable, but who knew it would be so soon.So far all they've given us is the trailer. My thoughts and ideas after the link.Viewable here:http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Nm99CGKLZUIs anyone else as excited as I am?!?!?!I thought the first game was absolutely amazing and here is my thoughts on what we see in the trailer:- The game will not be in the Asylum, as hinted by the tagline and website domain, the game will take place within Gotham City, but how big a playing and exploration area is questionable. I feel the developers would try to keep the size of the available area small, roughly same area size as Arkham Island, and have everything else blocked off by story related incidents.- Hinted Villains:- As we pan through the city we see two "Uncle Sam - We Want You" posters, this poster is viewable on the website as well arkhamhasmoved.com, but the face is torn away, look closely at the website poster with the torn away face and examine the chin, slight discolouration on the left-hand side of the chin hints that Two-Face will be one of the featured villains.- During the cityscape pan we see the Iceberg Lounge, The Penguin's social nightclub.- As we go inside the carnival funhouse with the bumper car you can see a sign that says "Sionis." Roman Sionis in the comics was better known as the Black Mask.- Definite Villains:- The trailer ends with Harley Quinn and the Joker watching through a window as the city is torn to pieces. The Joker appears ill, possible effect from recovery after the ending of the first game. So it will be a definite thing that both Joker and Harley will be back (Yay! My favourites!! Especially Harley, in some ways I think shes crazier and more dangerous than the Joker because of her constant attempts to win his approval).- Story Idea:- Ok, so from what I have seen in the trailer, it appears that Joker and several other Arkham inmates have escaped since the conclusion of the last game. Joker's goons are running free tearing the city to pieces. It could be an all out war against Gotham with Batman's greatest villains teaming up together to bring down the city and most importantly bringing down the Dark Knight.- Other villains I'd like to see:- I'd like to see a return of The Riddler, preferably I would love to actually have him as one of the central villains while also collecting his riddle trophies. To have him only talking to you and making you solve his riddles would be repetitive, fans will actually want to see him in this game.- Catwoman, friend or foe, she does not work for the Joker she simply takes advantage of his scheme to get what she wants, in the end possibly hindering or aiding Batman through the story (No multiplayer though, multiplayer I feel cannot be incorporated into what is essentially an epic single player experience, besides I hate Robin with a vengeance. I feel that he makes the Dark Knight seem weaker)- A return of Scarecrow wouldn't hurt, his hallucinogenic mind trips in the first game were absolutely brilliant and somewhat terrifying at the same time. Would love to see this applied to a Gotham pulled apart by mob war.- Depending on whether or not the Titan formula from the first game has any continuation into the story of the second, Bane or Killer Croc could always return, and even possibly Poison Ivy. Ivy's plants could definitely wreck havoc in Gotham.- Mr. Freeze, in the Collector's Edition for B:AA, the Doctors Journal listed Mr. Freeze as having escaped Arkham. He could be a potentially dangerous enemy, especially if in the story it involves the Joker having possession of Freeze's wife therefore getting Freeze to do his bidding in exchange for his wife. Could also provide explanation as to why the majority of the city cannot be explored, Freeze has frozen most of Gotham.Other than that, with the villains that we can already guess that are featured: Two-Face, Penguin and Black Mask along with Joker and Harley, perhaps two, maybe three, of the above villains could be added. Villains like the Penguin I can't imagine being bosses but rather minor characters (ie. Zsasz in the first game, not a major boss, but a major Batman villain).A location I would love to see available is Wayne Manor and access to the Bat Cave. The Cave on Arkham was awesome, but because we're no longer on Arkham, Batman needs to have his base where he can do his research and upgrade his gadgets.Holler out with your ideas and praise. Am currently hoping for a late 2010 release but more realistically we probably won't see it released til 2011, especially if we're waiting for a game that will exceed the first in every way imaginable (though there isn't much they could improve on. I thought the first game was damn near perfect). I'll come back and post regularly when I find out more, but don't expect too many posts from me this early, it'll probably be several months before we get another trailer or any news at all.

Ok, another minor "update" with clues as to the story of the next game.In the trailer we start off by seeing the gates of Arkham Asylum, but when the image pans up we see that rather than Arkham Island appearing behind the gates it appears as if the city is beyond the gates, with armed guards and police guarding the gates. It is possible that due to the takeover of Arkham Island, time needs to be taken to restore the damage caused by the Joker's takeover (especially with the damage caused by Ivy's plants), so a small section of the city has temporarily been quarantined and converted into a holding zone for all of Arkham's residents until the island is completely restored, and once again the Joker and his goons are attempting to take complete control of the quarantine zone in an attempt to escape into Gotham City.This idea would likely support the idea that producers working on the game did not want to turn Gotham City into a complete sandbox city involving travelling vast distances. The walls of the quarantined zone would reduce the size of explorable area to a size equivalent to the original Arkham Island.Post any ideas you've got and I'll be back with more news, updates and ideas later.See ya!
