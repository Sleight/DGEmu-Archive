This has been suggested time and time again. The site takes a lot of bandwidth strain from the downloads as-is, so they cannot refund DPs if your download stops midway or it's canceled. It spends the DP as soon as you click download.Also, send to email? You're going to have a lot of problems with email providers due to the amount of data being transferred over their servers, plus, most email providers only allow up to ~20mb attachments (correct me if I'm wrong), so that's also a problem. Lastly, putting them on filesharing sites would defeat the purpose of hosting the ROMs in the first place, and from what I understand, Filefactory is the only one that allows 1GB+, which means you're going to have some hideously slow downloads (and for those providing them, uploads) when it comes to ISOs. On top of that, you have sites such as uploaded.to blocking US IP addresses and such. It's just not feasible.
