It depends on what you are looking for to be honest.  If you are looking for SRPGs then I would recommend the following:

01) Advance Wars

02) Advance Wars 2

03) Final Fantasy Tactics Advance

04) Tactics Ogre

05) Fire Emblem

06) Fire Emblem - The Sacred Stones

07) Yu Yu Hakusho Tournament Tactics

08) Rebelstar - Tactical Command

09) Super Robot Taisen - Original Generation

10) Super Robot Taisen - Original Generation 2

 

For turn-based RPGs I would recommend the following

 

01) Shaman King - Legacy of the Spirits - Soaring Hawk/Sprinting Wolf

02) Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald

03) Golden Sun

04) Golden Sun - The Lost Age

05) Demikids Dark Version/Light Version

06) Medabots Metabee Version/Rokusho Version

07) Phantasy Star Collection

08) Ropopon 2 - Cross Version/Ring Version

09) Zoids Legacy

10) Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

 

And for Real-Time RPGs I would offer up the following options:

 

1) Summon Night

2) Summon Night 2

3) Shining Soul

4) Shining Soul II

5) Tales of Phantasia

 

Please, take note that not all of these games are listed in order and that I haven't played through all of them either.  A few of these are still sitting in my backlog for later completion along with 500 other games.
