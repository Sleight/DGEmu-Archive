Myself, paraphrased wrote:

As opposed to Pokemon, Digimon has better monster design graphically and better overarching plots/drama. However, horrible franchise management has always led to weak and uninteresting game design for the series. That and they really should have avoided making every Digimon end with "-mon" for their name (personal gripe).

What they should do is break off from other monster raising games and shift to an ARPG style game and steal the element from the SMT series where the humans actually fight. It's not like a human hadn't clocked a Digimon before. It was just a kid though I think. Anyway, human as the Player controlled character, the Digimon as an assisting AI that is controlled through a gambit (a la FFXII) style action presetting system.

They should also make use of every evolution type that ever appeared in the animated series and overhaul the exp types' effects on evolution to be more consistent and logical. This implies temporarily being able to Biomerge Digivolve with the human PC character or temporarily DNA Digivolve with another player's digimon or even a combination of the two, etc etc. Hell, those are the digivolutions we really signed up for anyway. The variety of one Digimon to be reused for its own power or for what it can achieve in tandem with other elements is what gives Digimon a greater set of fun possibilities.

Then they should use the overarching animated series plots with Yggdrasil and all that jazz, open it up a la MMO style or just go MMO altogether (not the crappy ones that exist for Digimon already), and have a full open world type deal and give it the ARPG gameplay smoothness of KH while taking up the maturity factor just a notch (only one though, it doesn't need to go full SMT dark).

They could also steal some .hack (same publishing company of Bandai anyway), where they have areas generated by code. Heck they could do a lot with the digital world front idea and data, hacking, viruses, anti-virals, etc. The modularity that could be achieved with digital DNA would make all other games in the genre seem uninteresting and repetitive. Not that they aren't, it's just that they'd be able to move beyond the cookie cutter models of whoring a series out for $.

Anyways... /soapbox.
