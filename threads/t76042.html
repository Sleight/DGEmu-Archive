ZzsiswickNov 23 2006, 08:15 PM
I've been gaming for a long time now, I started playing CS Source when it first launched and was immediately taken to it and considered it my favorite game. For me, what makes the game so successful is that the gameplay is fairly simple - the controls are no more complex than Doom - and noobs can easily get up and running without reading a manual. The game objectives are simple, you play as a terrorist or counterstrike teams - either trying to plant a bomb or rescue hostages on a given map. You can win by either completing the objective or elimiting the opposing team. There's a ton of unique weapons to choose from and your playing style can be modified to use any of them effectively - sometimes you'll want a specific weapon over another on any given map, (i.e. rifles for wide open maps, subs or shotguns for smaller confined spaces.) What I like best about the game is a player's skill is immediately recognizable, you can't easily run and gun in this game and succeed. Players that use their heads and change up their playing style usually do better in this game.Similarly, the CS network is rock solid and there are literally tens of thousands of networks to support online play. You can easily add friends to connect with online and the game also supports VIOP adding another layer to the online fun. The interface is very slick in design and it only takes seconds to get connected to a game and start playing. Valve automatically updates the game so you never really have to worry about having the latest patch.Anyhow, I've tried a ton of other FPS games since CS Source launched but I always seem to come back to it and fall in love all over again.
