Another vote for PC here, albeit a reluctant one. I would love to use a Mac since I like (most of) their design choices, durability, the way the hardware perfectly interacts with the OS, the better user interface and the possibility to use a fully functional CLI when I feel like it (certain things can simply be done faster in a terminal).

That being said, there are just too many downsides to Mac:
- A lot of software still isn't ported to Mac, including professional programs such as Studiomax 3D, whereas most professional programs that are used on Mac (Ableton, Photoshop and other Adobe programs,...) have perfectly fine Windows versions.
- Security will soon be worse than on PC (at least one that is running Windows 7 with UAC); just look at the recent Flashback Trojan (http://mashable.com/2012/04/11/mac-flashback-trojan-effects/) that infected 600,000 systems. As soon as an OS or any software gets popular enough someone will find a way to crack it. Mac is rapidly gaining popularity, so it won't be long until there will be more of these trojans floating around. Whereas Windows and Windows users have years of experience securing against this type of malware, Mac has not. Worse, a lot of Mac users still believe the mantra that you can't get a virus on Mac and will not update or properly secure their systems, turning them into easy targets.
- The hardware is just way too expensive. Every once in a while I get the urge to go check the latest Macbook Pro configurations and I'm always amazed by the ridiculous prices. For $2500 I can easily build or buy a PC laptop that's three times as fast. This is actually the only point that keeps turning me away from Mac.

In my experience, the slogan "It just works!" applies much more to Windows 7 than it does to Mac. However, I am definitely not a big Windows or Microsoft fan. I'm currently using Ubuntu for everything besides work and gaming and I eagerly await the day some miracle will occur that causes Macbooks to drop in price. ;)
