RRin ItoshikiMar 20 2012, 01:40 PM
No problem.FYI Pan:Open WinRAROptions > SettingsCompression tab > Create Default...Archive Format: RARCompression method: BestSplit to volumes, size: 200 MBUpdate mode: Add and replace filesThen ok out of there, that should have you settled for all your compression for Mediafire.Next time you want to RAR something and have it auto-split in 200mb parts, just grab whatever it is in Windows Explorer, right click, "Add to archive...", give it the name of the RAR archive you want, and press ok and it'll do the splitting for you. Saves you both the trouble of loading HJSplit to split your files + time.Also, HJSplit does not keep a check for the file integrity, so if ever it gets corrupted while uploading, you'll never know what is broken, vs splitting with WinRAR, if a part is corrupt, it will tell you which one it is.
