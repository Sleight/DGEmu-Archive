Charizard, on the other hand, has high Sp Att and Speed, plus has a sweet moveset [Dragon Claw, anyone?], so it does a better job at offense than Blaziken. As a side note, it seems that the Fire starter has consistently become one of the better choices.

JJered CainAug 04 2006, 08:09 PM
If you're looking for the easiest starter to get started on with the game, it's Bulbasaur, Cyndaquil, and either Treecko or Mudkip. I can't comment on who's best overall, but here's my little (or not so little) analysis on the first generation starters, to start with.Bulbasaur becomes a status effect machine as it evolves, and the Grass type is nice against the first couple of gyms in the first generation. You'll start running into trouble later on, though, if it's the only one you use, as Sabrina and Blaine may very well take the Bulbasaur line out with one hit.Squirtle's a solid choice, with a nice mix of special and physical moves, as well as protective moves. You'll run into problems as early as Misty and Lt. Surge, however, as Water type attacks will have less effect with Misty, and the Squirtle line'll be shocked easily by Surge. And don't get me started on Erika.Charmander's a rough choice to start out with, with type disadvantages to Brock and Misty, but if you're going for a challenge or prefer to balance out your team's levels instead of focusing on your starter, Charmander will help with a nice Steel-type attack in the first-gen remakes, Fire Red and Leaf Green, as well as the usual fire-type attacks you come to expect from the fire lizard.If I'm allowed, I'll post twice more here, to analyze the other two trios.

JJered CainAug 05 2006, 02:55 PM
Second generation analysis time!First off, we have Chikorita, who's a lot less of a status effect monster, and is a lot more defensive with the moves it learns, with Safeguard, Reflect, and Light Screen, as well as the healing Synthesis. Starting with Chikorita will give you problems, as Faulkner will tear you to shreds, and Buggsy's not much different.Next, Totodile. An offensive master, with moves designed to cause damage and increase damage dealt. Only three special attacks available through levelling up, however. Totodile and its evolutions will have no problems, but no easy time in the beginning, either, though you'll probably have a little easier time against Whitney and her Rollout. Pryce, near the end, will give you some problems.Finally, Cyndaquil. First off, Cyndaquil's more suited to Special Attacks, as three of the five damaging attacks it'll learn by levelling up are Fire-type attacks. Cyndaquil's also the easiest one to start with, although you'll only have an advantage with Buggsy in the start. Whitney and her Normal-types with Rollout will cause problems, but Jasmine and Pryce nearing the end will fall easily, and you won't have too many problems type-wise with either Morty or Faulkner.When I get the time (and enough posts have passed), I'll give my judgement on the final trio, the Hoenn three.
