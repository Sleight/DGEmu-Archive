DDarth RemielNov 28 2011, 08:03 PM
I can't have one single favourite, so I'll name several: Cave Story: It's been a long, long time since a game knocked my socks off, like Cave Story did. It takes gameplay of Castlevania: Symphony of the Night, with a healthy dose of Contra, and a surprisingly deep story. It's great, and it's free. You have no excuse to NOT play it. Amnesia: The Dark Descent: In a games industry that's seemingly forgotten how to make a truly scary game (aside from games like Ju-On: the Grudge, which is rife with dark rooms and cheap peek-a-boo scares), Amnesia is just an honestly scary game. The atmosphere this game has will seep into your very being, making something as simple as a door blowing open in the wind be scary. This, coupled with an awesome physics engine, and a cruel sanity meter akin to Eternal Darkness: Sanity's Requiem. It's a horrifying game, with a twisted, intriguing story. And since it's an indie game, it's cheap, too! Touhou Project: I like how all my best games ever are indie games. Touhou Project is a game made entirely by one man, who goes by the pseudonym "ZUN". It follows the fantasy world of Gensokyo, and usually a shrine maiden battling bizarre, evil forces. It's a tough-as-nails shooter game, which just takes utter joy in filling every inch of the screen in insta-kill bullets. Playing the game even on normal difficulty is a rush, and I can't reccomend enough the game's thirteen odd titles to any SHMUP fan. Also this may be a more personal one, but I salute ZUN for having a game series with an all girl cast, and not going the Capcom route of filling female roles with utterly shameless fanservice. DOOM: See, I have one non-indie game here! I have to give DOOM tribute, since it's really what got FPS-ing off the ground (because seriously? Who really cares about Wolfenstein 3D?). A wide variety of weapons, an array of monsters to use them on, and a large series of huge levels make DOOM into just a pleasure to play.Surely you all have heard this before, so I'll elaborate by saying the BEST way to play DOOM: Skulltag. Skulltag is a source port of DOOM based off the zDOOM engine, giving DOOM a nice openGL filter, and many advanced features, allowing modders to make some of the biggest, baddest DOOM mods ever. Even the Vanilla multiplayer is a delight, with gameplay akin to Quake 3: Arena. New DM levels, gametype archtypes like CTF and Juggernaut, and even new weapons (a devestating minigun, a grenade launcher that bounces off walls, and the murderous BFG10k). Skulltag is just the best way to play DOOM, and I suggest you get it first after buying a copy of DOOM. Painkiller: Last but not least: Painkiller. Painkiller is a shooter game made in 2004, which was a sort of homage to older FPS' like Serious Sam and DOOM. And it is a wonderfully great homage: In the first ten minutes of the game, you'll literally be killing hundreds of monsters, while a wicked heavy-metal background music amplifies your carnage. The weapons also follow this formula, and are possibly the strangest, yet satisfying, in any FPS I have ever played. Use a RPG Rocket with a minigun attached to it, or a long-range railgun that nails monsters to walls with entire trees (which, perplexingly enough, has a grenade launcher attached to it), or even the good 'ol shotgun, which can also freeze monsters.It also has online gameplay, akin to good old DOOM deathmatching. I cannot stress this enough. Painkiller will be the most fun you'll have with your PC ever. Unless Serious Sam 3 ends up not sucking. Or you just don't know what fun is. EDIT:
