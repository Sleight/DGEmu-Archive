Generally no there is no way to find out. You will either see you have been awarded 10DPs (Accepted and awarded), you will see you Review has been posted (only accepted, not good enough for a reward), nothing (it was rejected), or you have -10DPs (it was rejected a punished for being spam, copied from elsewhere, etc)

Just for the record I gave the award but only just .... if it included something like;
Gameplay: 1
Sound: 9
(etc)
Then it would of definitely of been an award, but I couldn't say no to the fact that you did a brilliant job or grammar, paragraphs, etc. To many just don't bother with grammar, capitalisation, etc
