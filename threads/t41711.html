Earthbound was a brilliantly written game. Instead of adding in another fantasy story to a market saturated with elves and magic spells and evil empires, the decided to make it start a kid in the suburbs, who uses a baseball bat instead of a sword. And he quested all over the planet to find key places that weren't meant to seal some great evil, but to remind him of his own memories and psychic power. The game was eccentric, and focused more on the road less traveled by. I feel it was a whole lot more heartfelt and lovingly put together than most RPGs today, even if the graphics were terrible, even for their own time. To this very day, Magicant in the game still strikes me as one of the most powerful moments of gaming to me.If they make a brand new Earthbound game, I would pay a king's ransom for it.
