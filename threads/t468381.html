Delphine Collection

This pack is a fun one. Flashback is one of my top ten favorite games of all time. It is the epitome of 16-bit platformers. Fade to Black, though by EA and not Delphine, is supposed to be a sequel, but the graphics are so atrocious that I personally never got around to playing it much. Out of This World, or Another World in Europe, is another amazing platformer that plays similarly to Flashback. There are quite a few other games in this pack. Check the wiki to learn more about the rest.
Also, there are french and English versions of some of the DOS ROMs in here. I don't think the french ones are too useful, no offense, but, they are tiny files so might as well include all of them.
Let me know how much you love Flashback. It and Out of This World are also available on Genesis/SNES.
