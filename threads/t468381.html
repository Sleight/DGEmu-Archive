Delphine Collection - (another compilation of games from hackerx's DOS post)

This pack is a fun one. Flashback is one of my top ten favorite games of all time. It is the epitome of 16-bit platformers. Fade to Black, though by EA and not Delphine, is supposed to be a sequel, but the graphics are so atrocious that I personally never got around to playing it much. Out of This World, or Another World in Europe, is another amazing platformer that plays similarly to Flashback. Cruise for a Corpse... never heard of it. Didn't know they made it, can't wait to try it, but since it is by Delphine and the files are tiny, I figured why not include it.
Also, there are french and English versions in here. Don't think the french ones are too useful, no offense, but, once again, tiny files so might as well include all of them.
Let me know how much you love Flashback. It and Out of This World are also available on Genesis/SNES.
