Summary:The Dreamcast is a wonderful console that doesn't require any modifications to it in order to play backups. The only downfall is that Dreamcast games are located on 1GB GD-Rom discs, and to be able to fit them on a 700MB CD-rom, people in the Dreamcast scene will need to rip / down sample audio or video / split games on multiple discs. On top of that, once the backup has been created, it was never really obvious as to how to burn them onto a disc in order to play them on your console.
