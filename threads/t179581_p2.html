I've played the game and to my surprise, find it quite fun. It's like a combination of all three Digimon World from the PSone but now you get to play it on a handheld. The background (during battle) changes unlike in Digimon World 2 and you actually get to have farms to raise your team of digimons. There's training to be done, food to be supplied, and even quests that you can do for your digimons. However, it lacks digimons, you only get to use a handfull of digimons and it gets repetitive sometimes, since you'll be controlling the same mob of digimons over and over again, not to mention the constant mobs of the same digimon, you'll hate it when you keep meeting mobs of weak digimons, in which you can't get anything good out of them, and unlike in pokemon, you don't get a repel here, lucky, eh? :D Also, it lacks freedom during battle, you only have 5 squares to hit/move and it gets repetitive to see the digimons act like they're marching <3 The fun part is, the bosses can be quite challenging, even if you have the ultimate set of equipments, especially at when fighting against the last boss, even my gang of megas can hardly fight it if not for the constant Revive T and Soma T. Overall, I'd say it's quite enjoying of a game and quite for the digimon fans out there.
