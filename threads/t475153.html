No$GBA... It's like the super famous emulator out there that can run many things.To answer your question, it is as stated by people...Get yourself VBA-M instead, it's an updated version of the old outdated VisualBoy Advance.To get the best settings you would be looking for, make sure these are checked:Options > Gameboy > Border AutomaticOptions > Gameboy > AutomaticOptions > Gameboy > Gameboy ColorsThen, go into this option:Options > Gameboy > Colors...In the new window, click on the drop-down box and choose: Real 'GB on GBASP' ColorsBy putting all those settings in, you will achieve this:- Playing on classic monochrome Game Boy games will appears in "color" as it would on a GBA SP- Playing Game Boy games that has Super Game Boy support will have their border displayed (ie: Mega Man V)- Playing Game Boy Color games will appear like they should on a GBC.- Playing Game Boy Advance games will appear like they should on a GBA.It's a one time setting, not per-game setting.
