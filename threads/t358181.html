I can tell you that getting a digimon in tokomons line is extremely tough, but as long as you keep the discipline at a higher-than-normal level and his happiness above the middle, with no care mistakes and weight at about 8 pounds, you should have no problem getting a tokomon. From there, it is increasingly difficult to get, for instance, an Angemon and even harder to get MagnaAngemon. You CANNOT make any mistakes when you raise your digimon if you start out with Poyomon, because the first mistake you make will result in a Greymon or other digimon from Agumon's line. Plus, your strength, wisdom, intelligence and so on play an important role in which champion form you will end up with. Focusing on speed and intelligence may or may not result in a Leomon, depending on what mistakes you have made in the time you've had him and what his weight is at the time. It's all dependant on a lot of factors, when you look at it. My suggestion to you is to start a new game and use the digivolves you get as references to help you in your current game - take some notes on that new game so that you'll be able to raise them right. Good luck!
