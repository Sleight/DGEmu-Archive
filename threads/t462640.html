someone wrote:
A question: Is it true that for the HGSS Pokesav's, there are a lot of grayed out buttons which limits my editing? Unlike the Platinum one?
I don't know about the Platinum one, but yes, as I recall there are many greyed-out areas on the HGSS Pokesav, representing values that Pokesav does not have the power to modify. You can't edit the Events, Pokedex, Accessories, PokeGear, Seals, Poffins or Pal Park. However you can still edit your Pokemon and Items fully, as well as things like Playtime and Multiplayer Avatar and Name and Money and even your ID and Secret ID.
