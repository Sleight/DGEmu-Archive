KKirie MotobaORIGINAL POSTJul 09 2013, 09:10 PM

Mainly concerns DM for his hosting, but will be taking input for all members as well.

 

While waiting for the possibly download slot based version of the site, I would propose the following:

- Completely eliminate any form of DP as a currency and only keeps GPs for everything download related.

 

Of course, it's something easy to say, but it's something huge to change as that affects many of the site's functions:

- Rom download costs should be adjusted accordingly as users must have more GPs than DPs.

- Many pages must be recoded to reflect the changes (I am unaware of what pages need this, but I know for sure we must now show rom costs)

- Eliminate any kind of reset, DP Loan, daily DP, etc.

- Offer more banking options so that users can invest to gain GPs (will replace the daily DPs and stuff)

 

What this will bring:

- Offer 1 standard currency to lower confusion amongst new members.

- Use DG Economy ACP tools to actively reward members with GPs during special events.

- If coded correctly, can set global promotions (same concepts as freeleech on torrent sites), etc.

- Can be coded to reflect member groups paying half price or less for roms (Power Members, etc)

- GPs can also be used in a new system I am currently conceiving in my head... more news to come at a later time. :)

 

This will also bring:

- Possibly higher bandwidth usage

- Lots of time consumption for the programming and debugging aspect. We do not need to change the look of the site.

- The need to have more people that know PHP that want to help. This is purely the coding aspect of the side we're changing, not the design.

 

Let me know what you think.
