AatomicknightNov 15 2009, 05:07 PM
Hmm, the other place you might try looking is C:\Users\<user>\AppData, where <user> is your username. Of course, searching the entire drive might be easier than guessing at where it might be.In any case, I wouldn't be so sure that having recent modified dates on the files translates to actually having newer save files. It could be that you or VBA modified the file at some point, but not necessarily when you were trying to save under the old configuration. Unless you were able to load newer save data under the old configuration, there's no guarantee that any new data was actually written to the files.
