I've reviewed quite a few games, but not an actual site though. I'm really impressed with this site. It's well-organized, has little spam, and also has its own rewards system, and that happens to be DP for downloading ROMs and GP for getting features here on the forums. I came from another community known as NSider (if that's enough for advertising, I'll remove this). This community seems to run similar to NSider: an off-topic board, boards about different systems, fan art boards, and all that nice stuff. The only difference is the strictness of the moderators, deleting posts, applying warnings, and docking GP for bad behavior. NSider does have several of the rules here, but they are a bit more lenient with the rules. While dupe threads are let go at NSider in one point of time, several are being delete there. Here, however, the moderators lock them on sight and dock GPs more frequently and even an initial thread to stop dupe threads. This helps keep the forums more organized as well as to help prevent the boards from being flooded with these posts. As for content, it must have taken a lot of effort to gather so many ROMs to reward people with for just being good members here. It must also cost a lot of money to make them free of any monetary charge (not counting DPs and GPs). You're doing a great job here managing this site and keeping these good rewards for people who behave on the forums. Overall, I'm very impressed with what you've done with this place. You have a great list of games to download as a reward for being an active, positive member of these forums and also gives members a motivation for being here. NSider has some rewards for members, but not as much as here. Great job here and keep up the good work <_< .
