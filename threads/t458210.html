Topic. I was just wondering if anyone else got this and what they think about it. I've played it some, and so far, I think it's pretty good and done justice to the franchise. The controls are good, but there are some problems, such as using the L stick button to transform. During heated battles, it's easy to click it and transform when you don't want to. They could have moved it to one of the d-pad buttons, since they only use the down d-pad button for looking at mission objectives and such. But that's the only BIG gripe I have with it right now. The overall game is quite good, and online multilayer has been hard, but fun. All in all, a good game.
