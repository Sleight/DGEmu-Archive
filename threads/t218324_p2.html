Llobo_the_sacredAug 18 2013, 10:34 PM

Well, I only ever sat down and watched the first three seasons of digimon so my decision well be based on my feelings about those series.  And, I feel torn between season one and three to be honest.  The first season introduces us to the concept and a young team that grows through growth as the story progresses.  On the other hand it suffers from repeated animation, speed lines, and other cost saving measures rather frequently, but the show had a lot of heart despite it's animated short comings.  On the other side of things season 3 had a more dark feeling to it and ends with a rather bitter sweet, but hopeful ending.  The third season also expands upon the universe as well and much like season 2 has tie ins to the digimon video games that were only released in Japan for the WonderSwan.

 

On the dark side of things I find season 2 to be a proverbial mess.  It suffers from having many ideas brought up but never expands upon the bulk of them properly.  The best example of this being the world of darkness and Kari's connection to it.  The show just seems to bring it up out of nowhere in episode 13 and doesn't talk about until numerous episodes later only to tie it up as quickly and neatly as possible.

 

Raivin wrote on Mar 18 2007, 12:22 PM:

In others humans had no need... Only for holding a certain item...

 

That isn't exactly true.  In the first three seasons the digimon get the required energy to digivolve from their tamers.  The digimon literally feed on their emotions in order to gain the power to digivolve for short bursts of time in order to protect them.  It offers an interesting symbiotic relationship between humans and digimon and shows the strong bonds necessary for a digimon to reach it's ultimate potential.  And, the first and third season also had fairly rounded cast of main characters for a show geared towards a younger audience.
