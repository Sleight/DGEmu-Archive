WRONG!The numbers of cores doesn't matter that much. You however need a good CPU >.<
Actually you would need both. Having a strong single processor is good and all but PCSX2 does fully support and utilize dual core systems by assigning the two cores to perform different tasks (I would guess to emulate the CPU and perhaps some other major component like the Floating Point Multiply Accumulator (FPU) and I/O Processor). Two powerful processors and a modern video card is all you need with the current version of PCSX2 to achieve the best possible performance.
