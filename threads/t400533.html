Molh: Also, note the part about "adding new content".
If you post in full sentences, but contribute nothing new to the topic, you can expect to get points deductions as well.So, for example, don't just do this:
someone wrote:
My favourite word is pie, full stop.
At the very least, say something like,
someone wrote:
My favourite word is pie, because when I was young my family always used to go out and have pie together. Whenever I read or hear the word 'pie', it brings back those fond memories of my childhood.
The person doing that is adding in stuff about why his favourite word is 'pie'. Unless that was a repetition of previous posts in the topic, this is considered 'new content', and so he'll get to keep his points. Got it?
