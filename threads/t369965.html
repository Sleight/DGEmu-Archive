How to Change version.txt on a PSP Running CFW

Many CFWs like to fool stuff that interacts with it (for example, ISOs) that it is at firmware v9.90. That way these programs think that the CFW is up-to-date. This poses a problem, however, when you want to upgrade your firmware, because the official PSP updaters might tell you, "Firmware is already 9.90. There's no need to upgrade."

This can be fixed by editing the PSP's version.txt, the file in its internal memory that's doing all the fooling:
1) Press SELECT on your PSP, the VSH menu will appear.
2) Look for "USB Device", change it from "Memory Stick" to "Flash 0".
3) Plug your PSP in your computer, open it, and look for a notepad file called "version.txt" in one of the folders. Find the "9.90" line inside the file, change it to "5.50", and then save it. DO NOT TOUCH ANY OTHER FILES INSIDE, JUST THE version.txt!
4) Unplug your PSP, press SELECT again to return the USB Device from "Flash 0" to "Memory Stick". And you're done!

If your VSH menu does not appear, enable it through the Recovery Menu. Shut down your PSP fully (hold the slider until it powers off), then turn it on and IMMEDIATELY hold the R bumper until it appears. Go to Configuration -> Use VSH menu, then set it to "VSH Menu". Then select Back -> Exit, wait for your PSP to load fully, and follow the instructions above.
