Over annalyzing this game is stupid, it wasn't made to be some brilliant, mind boggling story with beautiful cutscenes, it was made for people who want to rock zombies until their fingers get sore. If you're a diehard fan of zombie movies like I am, it's easy to overlook the imperfections of this game because for an endless hack and slash survival game its pretty much as good as they get. You can use pretty much anything you can find to kill any brain dead bastard that comes your way and you leanr moves like jujitsu throws and disembowelment that you can do with your bare hands. The game is totally beatable, and yes you won't be able to collect everything or save everyone but who cares, go play zelda then. What you can do is play through the entire game (if you're good enough) rack up your stats and new moves, and then start over again, play an entirely different story because of all the options you have, but this time you get to keep the stats you had from the last one and just keep on building on your own experience, while you kill zombies with frying pans and lawn darts. Basically if you want to just kill zombies or work out some agression, then you can do it endlessly, whether you play through the 72 hour story over and over again or you just play the infinite mode that you do earn after beating it. It rocks, and if you don't like it then go play viva pinada.
