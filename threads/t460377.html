Okay. This is the first quiz I’ve run in a while, so you guys might have to bear with me.Very basic rules:1. No spamming, copying answers, or repeating answers.2. Each participant is allowed one guess per question. However, this is not always the case, and will be explained later.3. The difficulty of the questions will be spanned over three tiers: Easy, Medium, Hard. 4. Easy questions = 100 pts, Medium questions = 250 pts , Hard questions = 500 pts.Now that we’ve got the rules down, I’ll explain how gps are earned. In this quiz, points accumulate and I keep track of them for each member (like in most quizzes) but the interesting feature is that they can be “traded in”. Any time you want to do a "trade in", just make a post and state what you'd like to trade for in your post. 50 points ---> 10 gps 100 points ---> (1) 1-upYou can buy as many 1-ups and gps as your points allow.Like in Scott Pilgrim (and many videogames) you can earn 1-ups. They are extra lives in the sense that you can use them to make another guess if you already answered incorrectly. On hard questions, I will give the participant who answers the question correctly 1-3 1-ups. The amount of 1-ups given will be determined before anyone answers the question. The question will have a "(1) (2) or (3) 1-ups" next to it.You can also spend points on 1-ups . (As seen above)Now, for the really fun part. Participants may want to save their points instead of spending them. This is because once a member reaches a certain number of points, they will receive a TON of gps.10,000 pts ---> 1,000 gps20,000 pts ---> 1,500 gps40,000 pts ---> 2,000 gpsThe numbers may go higher as the quiz progresses, but that’s all for now.Now that everything’s been explained, let’s get started!
