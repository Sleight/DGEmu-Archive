*Moved to DS section*I guess it depends on how they are broken. If they are just sticking or not making a good connection, you could try and clean them out (I read that spraying compressed air can work wonders for this). However, if they are physically damaged or worn out, there is little you can do at this point other than replace the entire unit. Either that or find someone good with electronics whom can replace the buttons for you (although that may prove difficult).Cleaning out the the control pad may help too. However, what often happens is that the rubber piece underneath that gives the buttons their "springy" sensation breaks down over time. Replacing this should make things good as new again. However, this also requires dismantling the system.
