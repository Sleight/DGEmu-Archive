Also there is a option of having you LED's reacting to the music your playing. After building a music library you can choose to play all songs, Show by artist, Album or Genre.Heres the controls for the music playerL = previous songR = next songA or Touching the round icon = Pause/PlayB = Back to the main screen (song stops playing BUT when you press A again the song continues from where it left)CompatibilityCheck out this page for rom compatibility http://wiki.scorpei.com/index.php/DSX_compatibility_listWell thats all the info i got about this nice little flashcart.
