To catch mew in pokemon red and blue, all you have to do is start the game over and play until you have three badges and hm02 *DO NOT BATTLE THE YOUNGSTER WITH A SLOWPOKE OR THE GAMBLER WITH TWO POLIWAGS AND A POLIWHIRL!!!* The youngster will be after the patch of grass between ceurelean city and Bill's house and the gambler is just outside of saffron city going to lavender town. Then you have to get a pokemon that knows fly, then on route 8, save in the underground path and save. Go outside and walk down one step and immediately press start. The gambler should have seen you, but the start menu should come up. Do not battle the gambler, and if he walks over to battle, shut the game off. Fly to ceurelean city. Your Start/A buttons will not work temporarily. Go battle the youngster. *DO NOT WALK DIRECTLY IN FRONT OF HIM OR YOUR GAME WILL FREEZE!!!* walk in front of him one step out and battle him. Defeat him and fly to lavender town. Walk to route 8 and your menue should pop up. Press B and a level 7 mew will appear. Its attack is pound.This is tested and proven by me. Everything must have perfect timing and must be just right. This is the only way I know of to catch mew. Reply with any other ways please.
