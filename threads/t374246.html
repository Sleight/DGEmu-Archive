[font="Arial Narrow"]Do you agree with embryonic stem cell research?General info:Embryonic stem cells are taken from a blastocyst, a 4-6 day old embryo. During this process, the embryo is destroyed.These cells are completely undifferentiated, meaning that, given the right condition, they can turn into any cell in the human body.Given this fact, stem cells have unlimited potential in medical sciences, from helping heart attack patients to treating various types of cancer. With the right environment, one group of stem cells can multiply indefinitely without differentiating. This is called a stem cell line.I am completely in agreement with all types of stem cell research. The good that can be gained from this technology outweighs all the negatives. The limits of stem cells haven't yet been found, a testament to the importance and scope of this technology. All blocks on funding and grants for this research should be removed, and it should be allowed to flourish so we may all reap the benefits.[/font]
