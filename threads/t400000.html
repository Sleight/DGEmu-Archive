So, yeah, had an idea earlier.Was thinking that topics in spam tend to evolve as members bounce conversation off each other, and can, for a little while stay on topic. If it's a semi-serious topic, anyway. I'm sure you all get the point, and have seen it, so I'll move on.Imo, people can't do that outside of spam because of:
someone wrote:
# Stay on topic. That is, do not post content that is irrelevant to the thread's current topic of discussion. If you wish to discuss something else, post in or start another thread.# Only the thread starter may alter the topic of discussion, granted it is done explicitly.
those two rules.What I'm suggesting, is to back off a little and let the members have discussions that aren't directly related to, purely for example, "What's better, Final Fantasy 7 or 12?". So rather than having twenty pages of "12 is better for sure", we could have discussions on characters, plots, quests, battle systems, etc.Naturally, it's not going to go as smoothly as I've put here, members aren't just going to launch into technical debates around the topic matter, but as it is right now, they're pretty much told not to. And (watch your step, hypocrisy ahead) people pounce on them for the littlest things, and they see off topic posts getting deductions and warns all over the place.If it's going to work, mods, and possibly some 'informed' members will want to steer conversation into more positive directions and probably change topic titles as and when they feel necessary.Could make the topics more interesting, and more fun to post in. That and you've got something other than the same borderline rule breaking posts to read while modding ... hopefully.
