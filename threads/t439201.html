If you want to run NTSC games on a PAL Gamecube you're going to need a freeloader. It's a special disc you insert before the game disc which allows you to load and play games from anywhere.Look around eBay or Play-Asia for a PAL freeloader. Otherwise, just sell the game you got on eBay and look for one from the correct region.
