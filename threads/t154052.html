GgeneralchickenFeb 22 2007, 05:50 PM
"A strong online game, will keep your attention."I write this review due to the fact that I am saddened by lack of an accurate one yet for such an up-and-coming game. The others listed are alright, but touch too much on the game's bad points, and not enough on the new update.Ah, the new update. Before it, the game was a potion spam fast to claim superiority, with everyone wearing the same armor.The update added a new upgrading system, the addition of rare armors\weapons, and the opening of an open PK server. More on these later.Story: 7\10.People who have played the game and read this high story tag rating may look at it and say "...What? There isn't a story!" but that's when I'll stop them and assure them that they are wrong. If you can sift through the mild engrish, there's a very apparent story within the game. Within the world, was peace at a time. The gods watched over the blessed kingdom of Madrigal.. until needing to move on to mold a new kingdom. They let their secrets with the race of the Dwarves (Known as Dwarpets), and trusted them to watch over the world.Also, they created a separate entity known as the "Clown." However, the Clown did not like his duty of guarding humanity, and had a personality split. Little is truly known about this "Clown" to this day, but regardless, back to the good parts..With this power, the Dwarpet race built machines, some good, most corrupt. The father of all the machines is known as the Clockworks. It's so powerful, that some of the inhabitants of the land even worship and make their own faction to honor this entity. The Dwarpets realize their wrongdoing in creating this machine, as it's sucking up the resources of the earth, much like what happened in Final Fantasy Seven. Yet still, some go on following it.On the other side, you have the player-race, Humans. Humans follow a sentient and normal "god". In the game, and are driven and encouraged to restore peace to the god's once blessed kingdom by ridding the land of corrupt monsters, Clockworks worshippers, and other corrupted machines. There's many quests involving this "holy war" referred to in-game as Scenario type quests.Enter FlyFF..(This story is what I've personally gathered. There may be more, there may be less, this is for you to find out yourself!)Graphics: 7\10.I was actually quite surprised at the graphics FlyFF can obtain. For being a free on-line game by fairly unknown Korean developers, there's numerous places in game that have made me go "Wow.. so pretty." the world is complimented by a working day\night and weather system. The graphics in this game are comparable to Ragnarok On-line, only fully retouched and brought into vibrant 3d. Don't misunderstand, this game is no title that will require you to shell out a few hundred for a new graphics card, but it's nothing to gawk at either. I recommend a GeForce FX5500++ card to play this, enhances the experience greatly.Game play: 9\10.Ah, this is where Fly for fun truly shines. It's simple, original, and addictive game play. In game you're met with an easy to understand combat interface, point and click to do pretty much anything, very easy to jump into! You're also offered special and unique things such as Combo Slots, Party Skills, the ability to cheer someone up, boosting their exp, and much much more. There are a few game play flaws however, such as well, none are too apparent in my mind right now, but a few things might catch your attention. Nothing really worth mentioning, though!Also, don't forget about the Flying itself, a pivotal part of the game play. You can fly all around the world, getting to your destinations much easier, seeing the sights, and even participating in aerial combat. Nothing like being about ready to die and making a dash for the sky!Social Aspect+Community: 7\10.This may seem like a weird category rating, but I find it quite needed for a review of an MMO game. The community, for the most part, is quite nice and helpful. The game however; is international, so you may run into a few rude\foreign players. I myself have had a few problems with rude players, but they're usually taken care of by the GMs rather instantly.You're very encouraged to socialize. There's a party system that actually REWARDS you for partying with others, with skills gained by the party such as Exp boosts, Drop rate boosts, attack power up, x2 regen speed, and an assortment of other delightful attributes.The guild system is also quite well developed. You can contribute money, exp, and quest drops to the guild to make it stronger. As it levels, it gains the ability to get more people in it, picking a logo and a cape, and an ability to participate in guild versus guild combat! The maximum player allotment in a guild is actually 40. Quite impressive.In-game support: 10\10.Absolutely perfect in-game support. There's ALWAYS a GM that you can talk to, and they will ALWAYS answer you. I've played World of Warcraft to level 60, and Final Fantasy 11 to 60+ as well. Their support was AWFUL compared to this gem of a free game. The GMs are even willing to come out and have a chat with you, show off the newest content to the game, and etc. Great group of people.Quest System: 5\10Again, another odd category I admit. I found this needed to be included to warn certain people. If you don't like "go fetch!" quests with no real story backing to them, then this game may not be for you. Basically, they give you an excuse to level up with "go get me blah blah" quests for "blah blah" reasons, like 35 Clock Hearts to make an artificial heart for a dying boy that you don't get to meet.However, it's better than grinding without reason, ala many MMOs on the market today. Sound: 6\10.Ah, the sound. It's solid, but repetitive. The combat sounds themselves are very nice and make up a solid 9\10 rating, but my gripe is the music repeating itself a good bit. Also the monsters making the same noises over and over, and the skill noises repeating. Oh well though, guess it's to be expected.PvP 6\10.I also included this due to the game being an MMO, and a lot of avid MMO'ers are PvP junkies at heart. This game has PvP rankings for your class, example: Mages get the ranks of "Apprentice, Mage, War-mage" and etc, there's a good deal for each class. In the future, having a PvP rank like this will allow you to access certain areas of the game where you need to be recognized as a good PvP'er to enter. The areas will offer harder monsters, unique quests, and all that kind of thing. The PvP fighting itself is actually quite balanced since the new patch. Every class can hold it's own. Though I will tell you -- do not expect a 5 minute PvP duel in FlyFF. The average fight is 20-30 seconds, till whoever dies. To the winner go a few rank points, and after a certain number of rank points, you get a new PvP title which you can lose if you lose too many fights.There's also great guild vs guild sieges, with no reward currently aside from fame on a ranking list. In the future updates however, you can gain access to guild halls, and special stat boosting equipment\items for having certain guild records or accomplishing certain things. Also, having a bad record in the future will make it much harder to level your guild.Re-playability: 6\10.It's always fun to make a new character, but grinding the same mobs over again can get a bit tedious, hence the 6\10 I'm giving this.-------Overall: 8\10.-------My closing statement is that FlyFF is one of the better Korean MMOs ever to exist, has a strong and rapidly growing player base, has great GMs, and a lot going for it. There's a few minor drawbacks, but they're looked into by the caring staff. The only real downsides here are the slight lag issues, the cash shop items being a bit expensive, and repetitive sound, along with the quests being a bit bland. For those issues I give the game a 9 out of 10, and since I believe no game is perfect, I take that rating down to 8\10. I hope you've found this review helpful and that you try out the game!
