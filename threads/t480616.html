DDaft PunkMar 07 2014, 12:17 AM
Bee Are wrote on Mar 06 2014, 08:28 PM:

Far as I understand, it's just a way to cook meat (I think youc an do other things, but mostly meat) all the way through perfectly. And, since it keeps the temperature at an exact number, you can supposedly leave it in there for as long as you want, and it'll never overcook.

 

Not true. It just takes all the guesswork out as you're maintaining an exact temperature for a predetermined amount of time. With red meats you have a decent amount of wiggle-room with the time before anything goes weird, but with fish and chicken (probably duck, too) it can go weird quickly. Honestly it's a real pain if you're just doing it on a stovetop, you need to helicopter around constantly turning the stove up and down to maintain the water temp. Restaurants use waterbaths that regulate themselves. That said, when you get it right it's pretty damn delicious.
