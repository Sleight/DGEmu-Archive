Alright, green port (also, it's pink, not red the third one)

Start menu > Control Panel
Top right, the View by:, set it to small icons instead of category.
Now click on sound.

In the new dialog, on the Playback tab, look for an item called "Speakers"
It should have a green checkmark on it. Select that item, and click on "Properties"
In the Speaker Properties window, move to "Enhancements" tab and put a checkmark into "Disable all enhancements"
Now move to "Advanced" tab and make sure both checkmarks are checked. in the Exclusive Mode section.
Apply, OK.

If you have a microphone attached to your computer, move to the "Communications" tab.
Default check is at "Reduce the volume of other sounds by 80%".
Change that to "Do nothing"
Apply OK.

Now test away.
