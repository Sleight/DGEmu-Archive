So I was looking for some new material to read recently...and decided to get up to speed with light novel translations, on stuff that I'd been reading ages ago.

Pleasantly surprised

 

Haruhi - vols 10 and 11 finally published, and TL'd too

FMP - series finished, completely TL'd (and the terrible old translations have been redone)

 

Rehosted for convenience, because they're licensed and were kinda hard to find:

Haruhi

FMP

 

 

Besides that, Baka-Tsuki has been massively updating its list of LN translation projects lately.

Some notables:

 

Baccano - up to vol16

Bakemonogatari - Kizu and Nekomonogatari

Infinite Stratos - all released volumes

Sword Art Online - all released volumes

To Aru Majutsu no Index - all 27 released volumes + a whole bunch of side stories.  Awesome

Hidan no Aria - up to vol9

Chrome Shelled Regios - up to vol15 (omitting some side stories)

Densetsu no Yuusha no Densetsu - 4 volumes of DaiDenYuuDen.  One of my favorite stories ever, unfortunately TL is rather slow

 

+ lots of other stuff, check it out!
