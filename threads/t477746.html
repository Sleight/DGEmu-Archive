Oh God yes, Chris Houlihan's Secret Room is an awesome easter egg. I am gonna have to go for the first one ever though.

I think in the game Adventure for the Atari 2600 there is a secret room that one of the programmers made and stuck in the game. Apparently he was fed up with how the programmers and staff that actually made the games weren't getting credit for their work. so this gentleman put a secret room in a maze that could only be found by touching a certain pixel. After that is achieved you enter the room and see the words "Created by Warren Robinett."

Now back in the day, this was a huge deal since every bit of space counted when making a game.
