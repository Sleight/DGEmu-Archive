Hi Auzzie Wingman, basically the EzFlash 3in1 is a slot 2 cart which can work as a standalone slot 2 cart. This means it can play GBA roms without the assistance of a slot 1 card, so it is compatible with any slot 1 card. It is called 3in1 because it acts as a rumble card, ram expansion (for browser and other things), and it's GBA compatibility. This card is only for DS lite, so make sure you have that. The way you put the games on is a method called flashing where you use the onboard 256Mbit NOR memory, it does not have removable storage, but the 256Mbit is enough to store any GBA out. I hope this answers some of the questions you may have, I personally have a stand alone slot 2 cart, and don't like to associate it with a slot 1 card, but if you are interested in that/ or have further questions about this cart, I can refer you to this great website: http://wiki.gbatemp.net/wiki/index.php?tit..._put_my_ROMs.3FI wish you luck in your search for a slot 2 cart ^^
