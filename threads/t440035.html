Cup of Cheer, featured only in Pearls of Lutra, is an extremely fizzy drink with a very sweet flavor, resembling strawberry fizz. It was brewed and invented by Ambrose Spike during the Winter of the Deepest Snow and was made of rhubarb harvested shortly before the snow fell, mixed with honey. One of the Tears of all Oceans was hidden by Fermald the Ancient in a firkin of Cup o' Cheer.
