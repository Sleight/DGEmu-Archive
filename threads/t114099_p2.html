It really was a good game. I'm a big fan of TCGs, games and actual cards (the only cards I've collected were Pokemon cards when I was young and Yu-Gi-Oh cards when I was a little older and they were still fresh). The things that I like about the Pokemon TCG is that it's fun (which some TCG games aren't), the gameplay has a relaxed feel (Compared to games such as Yu-Gi-Oh which tend to be more serious), you don't have to be an expert to be able to play (It teaches you the basics in the beginning which is all you need to know to play. I never even played it in real life, but learned from the game and was pretty good at it. It's a simple and fun game). It has sort of an RPG feel to it, meaning that you do more than just fight random people, get cards, build decks, and repeat. There are certain people you have to beat and several side quests/secrets that you can do to get special cards, like someone else said, it's a lot cheaper and easier to play than to play a physical game with another person. The level of customization is great (something that's common with TCG based games and the like), it's fun for all ages (it might be a little tricky for young children to understandhow to play since it's a bit more complex than traditional Pokemon games due to the amount of customization and strategy that is recquired in the game, but for the same reasons older players will like it better since it recquires more thought and feels less like a child's game).
