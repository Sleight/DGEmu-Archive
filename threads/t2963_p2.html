WwavehawkMay 13 2006, 11:57 AM
It's not just stereotypes. It also has a lot to do with the group of people who call you that. For example, if you're an anime fan and you're called an Otaku by other anime fans (outside of Japan, that is), it's generally an acceptable or positive thing. But if a Japanese person calls you an Otaku, that carries negative connotations with it--mainly because of a lot of cultural inferences and experiences with otaku in Japan.Or, to be more blunt, it's the difference between a fellow gamer calling you a geek and a Macho Football star calling you a geek. Both might mean it positively (or negatively), but the source plays for a lot when it comes to it being a term of endearment or name-calling.And by the way, being a nerd or geek isn't necessarily bad. It's how you take it.
