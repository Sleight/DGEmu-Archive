[center]

For those of you who have been living under a rock these are third person shooters with the ability to temporarily slow down time so you can leap through the air while firing and dodging bullets. Also unique is that instead of a health meter you have a pain meter. The more you get hurt the more it fills up. Suffer to much pain and you die, take pain killers (conveniently found all over the place if you look around for them) and you meter goes back down.
Oh, and the games also feature a rather unique story telling method. Instead of traditional cut scenes the story is presented primarily as comic book panels with voice overs. Here are a couple of videos to give you an idea of what to expect.
