As I said in another topic, the idea isn't to limit tournaments to only free games. The idea was to make a list of free games that could be used for tournaments because they are usually easier to obtain and set up. And lots of multiplayer online games that are commercial use CD keys and check against a master server and other things like that to prevent illegal copies from being used for online play. The easier it is for people to grab, install, and configure a game so that it works online the more likely the are to do so and compete in a tournament. A really fun MMOFPS I occasionally play is Combat Arms. It was developed by the highly acclaimed Nexon Corporation who has created hits such as Maplestory and Audition. I suggest we have a competition in this game. It's relatively easy to get used to and it happens to be a very fast-paced game. You can customize your weapon (limited though, future updates on this are coming out) as well as your character (It's a great and easy to use feature.) The game itself is free, but you can pay to buy items from the so called "Black Market." The Black Market is a section, promoted by Nexon, in which you can buy aesthetically pleasing items. The weapons you can buy in the Black Market are generally "cooler-looking" than their original counterparts to promote balance. There are many successful players who haven't spent a dime in the Black Market. It's all up to you, it really doesn't give you any advantage. The great part is, is that we can start our own clan and have our own private rooms (password blocked) to play in. It's a really fun game overall and I urge you all to check it out.
