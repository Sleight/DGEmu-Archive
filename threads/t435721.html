All of this I got out of a copy of PC Powerplay (an Aussie PC gaming magazine) that I got today (issue 164 for anyone who reads it or wants to look at the article themselves). I was actually surprised by some of the stuff being implemented in this one. I won't give my opinion of the ideas, simply detail them here for anyone who was wanting a sequel to Bioshock. Just a head's up; there will possibly be spoilers for Bioshock 1, so if you haven't finished the game, don't read.First of all; it's set in Rapture ten years after the first game, but you're not playing as an outsider this time: you're a Big Daddy now. Not just any BD, but the prototype; which still retains some humanity and self-awareness. The prototype also has access to plasmids and all of the weapons the Daddies used against you in the first game. The prototype is far more agile than the final designs and will be able to tear through regular splicers with ease.The Little Sisters are back, along with the option to harvest them. However, you can now bring them with you and search for corpses to harvest Adam from. This doesn't turn the game into an escort mission, though (artwork of the prototype show it with a spherical cage on its back with a Little Sister inside it), however when she starts extracting Adam, large numbers of splicers will attack from multiple angles; though you will have plenty of time to set up defences and other measures against them. Once the Sister is done, she'll climb back into the cage and will give you a portion of the Adam harvested (the more Adam she harvested, the more you get). Or alternatively, you can harvest her right after she's finished and get all of the Adam.An announcement trailer showed a Little Sister (presumably one of the ones Jack saved, but who was left behind in Rapture) who had grown up. She is known as the Big Sister in the game and was the one responsible for recreating the Little Sisters after Jack's work down there. If you do anything that de-stables the ecosystem of Rapture (presumably harvesting Little Sisters, but there may be other ways) she will start hunting you. Similar to how the player hunted Big Daddies in the first game, she will hunt the player and will attack you whether you are ready or not.There are also underwater sections of the game where the player will be able to wander around on the ocean floor outside Rapture, being able to look in on areas they will visit later. While there are some compulsory underwater areas, many are optional and the whole game world has been set up to allow more freedom of exploration for the player.The only character from the first game so far confirmed to be returning is Tennenbaum, though some concept art suggests that Andrew Ryan will return (possibly using a Vita-Chamber to avoid his grisly end in the first game). Vita-Chambers have been reworked and can also be switched off entirely.There was also a lot of info on the general feel of the game, however I'm not very good at summarising and copying the article word for word would take an eternity (I don't have anywhere I can sit the magazine where I can read and type at the same time), so you'll have to wait for other sources for those.Also, there will be multiplayer.
