KKaojiORIGINAL POSTNov 24 2008, 05:11 PM
In my short tenure here at DGEmu I have noticed one clear opinion which many folks around here share ... Pink Floyd was, is, and will forever be the King of Progressive Rock. Not only were his lyrics and sounds some of the most influential music in the history of rock and roll, it continues today that his songs spark some sort of revolution within people; a revolution devoted to peace, clarity and serenity. It is for these reasons, also that I am just a huge fan, that I'd like to hear about some of your experiences with Pink Floyd's music. Anything he's made you feel, or changed your feelings about. Times in your life where the soft lullaby of Comfortably Numb or On the Turning away made you peaceful, or times when Another Brick in the Wall, Have a Cigar, or Dogs of War touched you in some way.It's easy to say that Pink Floyd is revolutionary, it's not so easy to examine why his music made you feel that way. I remember the day that my grandpa was diagnosed with Alzheimer's and we found out his mind was slowly slipping away from him. I remember going home turning on On The Turning Away and crying alot about losing someone who was so special to me. I could have heard that song a thousand times that night and never stopped listening."On the turning away, from the pale and downtrodden. And the words they say which we won't understand. Don't accept that what's happening is just a case of all the sufferring. All you'll find is your joining in, the turning away. It's a sin that somehow, light is changing to shadow. and casting it's shroud over all we have known. Unaware how the ranks have grown, driven on by a heart of stone. We could find that we're all alone in the dream of the proud."Those are two verses which will always hold a special place inside me. Those two verses made me think long and hard about how my grandpa lived his life. Whether his mind was slipping away or not, he was alive. And it gave me more peace than I had ever had; and I found myself to be very serene.Your turn guys.Lol... I guess I'm the only one, eh? >.<
