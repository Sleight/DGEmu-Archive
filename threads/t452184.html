Isn't the rule of thumb usually your processor needs 10x the power of the console's processor or something. So you'd need a processor over 2ghz minimum?
Approximately. There is some variance depending on the target processor architecture, host architecture, efficiency of the host platform (OS, background software), and general efficiency of the emulator. The Wii has a primary CPU that operates below 750 MHz but there are emulators that run on it for nearly everything that came before it. Not all of it runs full speed yet but it is amazing that some of it runs at all. That includes the playstation and the saturn.These days emulators focus on compatibility over speed and optimized code. They count on being able to just throw more processing power at it and telling people to buy a new CPU. Thus the more mature the emulator the less processing power you need.
