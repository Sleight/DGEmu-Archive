If you ask me folks, I think that it should work out like this, though feel free to ignore me as you wish or heed me.Race: What do I mean? I mean as in whether you are a human, a dragon or a gnome, etc. Too many RPGs that I have seen that are based solely on a limited amount of player races.Sub-race: Again, quite simple... say you're bitten by a werewolf, you'd be a werewolf too. Say you have a dragon's curse that makes you become a humanoid hawk during the day, that's also a subrace (while it applies).Elements: No, we are NOT having this as something you choose at start, you hear me? *strangles those who think otherwise* Elements should be able to be chosen and changed via specific weapons, armour and accessory stones (e.g. if you are a saurian dragon, equipping a thunder stone will now give you lightning breath and claw/weapon attacks as well as respective weaknesses and strengths). And I swear, if I hear two elements being missed out one more time, I am going to smack someone into the next century. The elements are, and have ALWAYS been: Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Thunder, Light, Dark and Spirit. Honestly people... research, not just sticking to stereo-types set by other RPGs...Weapon powers: Before you all start spamming 'fireballs, lol'... No. Just... no. Look, you need to have items that have special powers to help a person. For example, a 'Moonlight sword', a holy sword, will allow you to break barriers of darkness, attack with divine(element) lightning(effect/appearance) waves or open up seals placed around. Equally, a 'Dark slayer', a dark sword, will allow you to purge barriers of light, attack with dark(element) meteors(effect/appearance) and open up other seals that a 'Moonlight sword' can't open.Curses: Yes, we have to respect that curses DO exist, and they do affect people. Say a griffin gets mad at you and curses you to be unable to touch solid matter, that's a curse. Curses can be bad or good depending on the use, and with certain items, players should be able to put a specific curse on themselves when they please or remove it. Who knows? People might like being a humanoid hawk during the day or being unable to touch anything solid. Equally, a item to use to cure them would be needed.Diseases: Yes, we do get sick, or get bitten... as such, we should be affected by diseases. Some can be bad in the terms of being actually sick, and being weakened... wheras some can be bad and good, such as being a werewolf (increased strength, agility, increased senses but instincts affect actions, and speech is greatly impaired, sounding more like a growl than actual speech). Again, a cure would be needed... as well as a means to infect yourself as you please.Legendary items: These items would be unlocked by hidden tasks done during your gameplay. Let's take say... the game Sparkster for example. In that, you had to collect 9 seal swords to get the golden armour. In this case, certain things done or obtained by the player would lead to the unlocking of other items. Say for example... to get a Dark slayer, you would need to obtain a dark crystal, a black dragon claw and Apothurite. Once you have these, the option to get a Dark slayer is open to you.Item durability: No. Just... no. This is the BANE of good RPGs, people get put off when they have a really good item they like and suddenly... *smash* Oops, it's broken... bye bye all the effort to get it. Seriously... please DM, do NOT add item durability, it WILL kill your RPG faster than you can say Role Playing Game.Alignment: Ok, let's get this straight... all people are neutral to begin with, it's the actions that decide how they are. They will NEVER be strictly evil or strictly good all their life. It should change with how the player acts.Interaction: Please, PLEASE let it NOT be one of those point and click things... they are seriously a piece of trash. Please try to make it something where you can control it with the keyboard and mouse.Item weight: A very noble idea here, but you need to make it NOT BASED ON STRENGTH. It should go up by level, as the character grows more mature and more capable as a adventurer.Stamina: Big no no. Stamina just ruins RPGs. We don't want to be waiting hours to be getting back enough stamina to do things again... we want to be able to act whenever and where-ever we please.Food and sleep: Here's the one that would be most fun... before a person sleeps, they MUST eat three meals (three meals a day, restores HP or MP depending on meal) and then must rest either at a hotel or by using camping gear.
