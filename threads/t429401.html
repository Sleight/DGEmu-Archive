Ok, I'm throwing the choices down to be betweenPrince Of Persia Sands Of Time Trilogy,Assassins Creed 1 & 2Prince Of Persia (2008)For all the games I say that they all have brilliant storylines, can't wait to see how Assassins Creed and Prince Of Persia '08 continue/conclude.Now personally I say that Assassins Creed is the best. To improve on the sequels, keep the same formula but improve on the combat. And.... Introduce difficulty levels. I found the game was too easy considering that you could never die, Elika would always save you. Again, not much incentive to play it more than once unless you're trophy collecting, though close to all the trophies can be earned in a single play-through.So in conclusion I say that though the POP games are vastly fun, but Assassins Creed has the best formula for the best gameplay. Free roam, with a good balance of combat, freedom to explore different ways to complete a mission allowing for non-repititive play-throughs and dozens of side missions to give that extra bit of freedom.
