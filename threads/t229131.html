KKuisanagiORIGINAL POSTApr 19 2007, 11:17 PM
Before the fall season starts, I want to make a big...very big update on the Anime Recom. list. Props to Ken-san for the reminder...Anyways, reading through it, I once again must state that it is way waaaay too long, and the majority of the anime on there is too out-of-date, too mediocre, or too unknown to deserve to be on any kind of Anime Recom. list. Additionally, the anime recommendations thread is flooded with people posting repeats over and over...and I for one lack the patience to read through it. Since Mrcn hasn't made an update in well over a year and the last attempt at voting for Kick-outs pretty much flopped, I would like approval to make a big update to the list to actually make it readable and useful rather than just a giant list that nobody wants to read through.Changes I plan to make [open to suggestions and alterations]:-A mass genocide against mediocre and out-dated series that have been on the list for too long. And that means...most of the stuff on there now is probably going to have to go. The animation quality of more recent series has jumped so much compared to a decade ago, that unless some oldie has an amazing plot, it really can't be called "recommended" anymore. And instead of going through the long process of voting on it, I think its much more efficient that I just gather together as many of the DGE Anime Commitee as I can [and volunteers well-versed in anime are welcome too] and systematically cut down the list...cutting 'too much' isn't really a problem at all, since what we're pursuing is a list of only the best-of-the-best, and so, the smaller the better.-The second big thing will be to add on the new outstanding series that have aired in the past 5-6 seasons since the list was last updated. Again...I propose not to use the recommendations thread at all, as from my experience, most posters there simply post all the anime they've watched, or have not really watched enough anime to have a valid opinion of what is 'best'. Therefore, the opinion of the DGEAC and any trustworthy volunteers I get here [hence why this is posted in the Staff Forum] should hold more weight, and we can have a mini-vote to determine what to add to the list. Though that shouldn't be very hard since I can name a dozen outstanding series in the last few seasons spontaneously...-The layout and notation of the list should be improved...Mrcn's current system is quite good IMO, but several things are a little buggy. Firstly, instead of making a new series notation, all series should have their airing year(s) listed ...and the "must watch" thing should be ousted since all the anime listed should be must watch [:)]. Also, if we cut down enough the list enough, I thought it might be useful to write short plot synopsis and maybe even a mini-rating system...or short of that, links to ANN, AniNFO, AniDB, etc. I'd like to hear any other suggestions for layout revision as well... -Lastly, I think this update is going to be a extended process, not simply one quick revision on a single day. After all...as many might know, I have a bit of difficutly being very active at this time, especially with exam season coming up. Probably the complete revision won't be done until the end of April or perhaps even May, but slowly and surely...it will be done. And I certainly can't do all this myself, so I'd like to humbly request [lol] help, mostly in making decisions on which series to add/remove and perhaps help in the actual revisions. Being a B.Mod, there's not exactly much I can do for reward except giving credits and such, but at least you get the warm feeling of having contributed to the Anime community. [:P]Well...guess that's all. So please post suggestions, discussion, and maybe a blurb about your otaku-ness. ~.^
