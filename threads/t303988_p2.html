ZZeroMk5Mar 11 2010, 12:32 PM
I much prefer this game over the first.Simply because unlike the first game, FFTA2 was not heavily focused on the main story. In fact, a heavy portion of its weight was occupied by side quests, which I personally think SE did a very creative job with.The main story itself isn't so deep, but the character development has much improved over the first FFTA, and even the numbered Final Fantasy games. Luso himself did not seem very interesting, simply because YOU are Luso, and from his perspective, you are looking at many other interesting characters, such as Cid, Adelle and Frimelda, whom all have shadowy pasts, and eventually they tackle their problems and move on with life.The character development was almost seinen anime level. It kind of felt like watching Cowboy Bebop (one of the best anime out there), and when it comes to side quests stories, Adelle and Frimelda immediately come to mind.The gameplay itself has become much generous. Laws and Judges still exist, however, breaking laws only results in the loss of "Clan Privileges", and the ability to revive your party members. However, this IS a strategy game, and with a nicely balanced team (eg. a tank, ranger, healer) you wouldn't let anyone get KO'd, would you? :P Difficulty really depends on how you play. The "Hard" difficulty itself is recommended even for beginners, because it is fairly easy, and becomes ridiculously easy if you decide to use certain overpowered setups. But giving yourself handicaps, or using the characters that you truly feel fun playing with, can add a certain degree of challenge.Content is incredible, you will spend hours and hours on this game. If you're a fan of SRPGs, I highly recommend giving this game a go.I rate FFTA2 a 8/10
