[font="Arial"]Signatures can be seen as art, actually they are art of a smaller form. Thus the reason why many call them "Small Art". Though, what "art" is, is decided by you and what you want to recognize art as.The way I see it is:If you've made something completely random (mind you, sometimes this can be the best art), render slapped on, some pretty little effects, and no underlying concept, or a concept behind it, then I don't see it as art. Regardless of if it's a signature, or a large piece, I just don't see it as art.If you've made something with a concept behind it, something to express emotion or to cause deep though, then I see this as art. Again, regardless if it's a signature or large piece, that doesn't matter.In the end though, it's what you want to see as "art", and what's not.[/font]
