DDJ Night Force 9ORIGINAL POSTMay 16 2006, 03:09 AM
I'm not sure if this is possible to pull off but it'd make life way easier if it could be done. Basically my suggestion is to allow moderators and up to be able to edit posts within closed threads (without having to open them first). Why? Suppose someone wants to frequently edit a pinned topic that always remains locked. It'd be really annoying for that person to always have to re-open the thread each and every time, do their edit, and close it again. Then Suppose that same person then realized they had forgotten something. Guess what? They'd have to re-open, edit, and close yet again. Naturally the applications of this idea would extend beyond pinned topics (e.g. making spelling/contextual corrections to a "why this was closed" reply in a rule-breaking thread).So what do you make of this idea?
