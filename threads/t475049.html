Last year I did a Let's Play of Lufia II: Rise of the Sinistrals, one of my favorite SNES games of all time (in fact, probably THE favorite of all time). It's the game that got me into RPGs lo those many years ago and it's a game I keep coming back to year after year. Rather than linking a few random videos I'll just put up the playlist for the LP. It's 38 parts in total, including intro, ending and a random bonus episode I recorded before the turn of the year.A Short IntroIn my Let's Play I basically just go through the game after not having played it for a few years (for shame!), providing live commentary along the way, mostly comments about whatever I'm doing in-game. I get most of the stuff in the game, including fighting all of the optional bosses in the main quest. The only place I skip is the Ancient Cave because it only consists of opening chests and random battles. The reason I chose to LP this game was mostly just for fun, and this is the result:Let's Play Lufia II: Rise of the Sinistrals PlaylistHope you enjoy it!And I guess I can embed a random video from the LP.
