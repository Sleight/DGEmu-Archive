Most movie tie-in games are like that though. They figure that the title of the movie will draw the player and compel them to buy it, so they put in the minimum amount of effort and time to this and what they release is garbage. For instance, back in 1995 I bought a game called Batman Forever for the SNES. I though the movie was pretty good, but in my defense, I was 9 years old, so yeah. I thought to myself, oh hey, this game can't be that bad. But it was. It truly was. First of all, the story follows some parts of the movie that got cut from the final release. I assume that they just gave the script to the first game company that walked through the door. That company happened to be Acclaim. The game is more like Mortal Kombat than it is a Batman game. Also, they did something to the SNES version since it still has to load after every screen. It sucks. The controls are unresponsive, and the graphics are ugly.
