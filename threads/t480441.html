KKHRSJan 18 2014, 08:04 AM

it's like playing tennis, except nothing like playing tennis.

 

-try to stay in the middle height of your field, don't get too close to either wall. This way you can better position yourself in relation to where the disk is going

 

-know when to use your dash. you don't need to spam it because more likely than not you'll end up in a place you didn't want to be. dashing is the best way to clear large gaps, but don't do it if you barely need to move

 

-don't stick too close to the net. like I said earlier your opponent can easily lob it way over your head for easy points. Only get close to the net if you're trying to make big plays.

 

-in relation to the last tip, try to stay in the back, kind of like a soccer/hockey goalie. You'll have more time to react to enemy shots, especially specials (heh).

 

-practice dashing diagonally. it's harder than dashing in cardinal directions but allows for big plays and big saves.

 

-try to focus on the middle of the map instead of one specific side. If you focus on the center you'll be able to see your opponent's positioning, where the disk is flying to and your positioning.

 

everything else you just get from playing the game and practicing.

 

Everything I said minus the parts about dashing is stuff I learned when I used to play tennis in high school.
