GGuitarbizarreJan 22 2007, 11:21 PM
Given the OP description, I'm guessing whats happened is he has a 52X CD-ROM drive (Or come other hi-speed drive, maybe 46X)Hi speed drives often have the side effect of making the disc extremely warm from having intense laser light passed over it so much more than in a slower drive. What may have happened is that through extended use the CD has been heated and expanded, then cooled, repeatedly, making the disc develop hairline fractures and crazing because of it. For whatever reason, maybe random chance and maybe through the cracks being perfectly vertical for whatever reason, the disc has kept working because the crazing hasn't disrupted the path of the laser.Eventually, the hairline cracks are just too much, it heats up and expands for he last time (widening the cracks as it does so and robbing the CD of structural integrity. The spinning of the disc in such a high speed drive is just too much for the weakened disc to take, and so it shatters.
