Overview:
This guide will talk about the 3 main and most known flashcards that will run DS games on the 3DS. It will explain where you can go to obtain the latest firmware and how to update your card to the latest firmware.

Note:
As of the writing of this guide, no flashcards can play 3DS roms. Please refrain from asking for flashcards that can play 3DS roms.

Acekard2i


R4i 3DS


Supercard DSTWO
This card is the most expensive out of all choices, but has a lot more features than any other flashcards. Most notably, this is the only flashcard capable of playing GameBoy Advance roms from slot-1. It is also the card that usually gets the quickest updates to support the latest firmwares released by Nintendo.
