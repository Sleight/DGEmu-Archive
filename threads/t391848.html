[Tutorial] How to format your 4gig memory stick to FAT

22 posts · page 1 of 1 · 551 views
DDon't Dream It's OverORIGINAL POSTMar 27 2008, 01:57 AM
1. Backup ALL the contents of your Memory Stick (e.g. Games, Music, Videos).2. Format your Memory Stick using your PSP. That would be (System Settings > Format Memory Stick™).3. Connect your PSP to your PC using USB.4. Go to the Command Prompt. (Start > Run > Type "cmd" > Ok)5. Type the command "format x: /FS:fat" where x is the drive letter of your PSP.6. When you are asked about 64kb block size, just choose yes.7. Copy ALL the original contents back into the Memory Stick.8. And, you're done. You now have a faster PSP formatted into a FAT16 file system.This will make transferring files from your PC to your PSP go faster, also it will help ISO's run faster
