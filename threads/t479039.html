cgfreak wrote on May 15 2013, 04:54 PM:

please explain why this album is not a boring slab of watered down 70s disco

 

It's a pretty eclectic album, sure about half of it is late 70's/early 80's inspired but I think comparing it to traditional disco is the wrong way of going about it. I think anyone that had been following The Collaborators videos had the album hyped to hell and back and were probably expecting some sort of huge revolution, instead what it is is unmistakeably more Daft Punk - disappointing for some maybe? All of the songs have that Daft Punk thing that I couldn't possibly describe with words and can't help but enjoy.

 

The best way to enjoy the album is to put it on, stop thinking about it and listen while doing something else. Little pieces really stand out and draw me back in, can't help but grin. RAM certainly isn't going to dethrone Discovery but it's close competition for second place vs Homework (probably not a popular opinion, I think people usually place Alive 2007 above that), and I have a feeling it's gonna grow on me.
