CCertosDec 23 2010, 09:22 PM
The two most overrated series in anime history, huh? I've watched both, tossed them aside, then picked them up again. For Naruto, I guess one of its finer aspects would be character development. Indeed, the relationships between the characters are shown very clearly, and mostly in the form of flashbacks (which are probably overused though, and getting old.) The character development in Bleach is sub par. By that, I do not mean that the characters do not develop, rather, they do develop frighteningly fast. However, the way they portray it is lacking in more ways than one. Like Naruto, flashbacks occur wildly during mid-battle, they somehow find their inner determination sleeping within them, and they power up, shocking both their opponent who was stronger than them by leap and bounds, and us, who are aware of that fact. The action in both of them are excellent, though repetitive sometimes, and the sure way to finding out if the character is going to win is the number of flashbacks they've had during the battle. Oh, it doesn't count if it's the enemy's flashbacks. Which brings me to another point, which I like about both Naruto and Bleach. Unlike many other series that stars the main character killing off opponents targeting himwith no apparent motive, they do actually delve into the pasts of the enemies, making us sympathise with them and what-not. Well conclusively, as both series are so similar, I prefer Bleach as... the art is better. Simple as that. Well I guess it's understandable (Naruto was adapted in 2002 and Bleach in 2004), so there's a 2-year gap in between. However! Look at the character design! Really! Call me superficial, but there's to be said about looking at Naruto (who was incidentally voiced by the same VA as Lambo in Kateikyoushi Hitman Reborn).
