At this point, pretty much every GBA game that will ever be made is dumped and available on our site. If the game you want to see, whether a new release or a translated release doesn't exist right now, it pretty much doesn't exist. Developers have moved on to the DS in Nintendo handheld development.

Anybody asking about releases that don't exist on our site will receive an automatic warning.

Anybody asking for releases that do exist on our site will get a slap in the face for failing to use the ROM search function, which is on the front page of the website. Oh, and your points will be deducted too and/or receive a warn.

Failure to read and make note of this topic is not an excuse, and will not excuse the violator from a warning.
