RRickstilwellJun 01 2011, 08:17 AM
I actually bought this and used it to export my Chao from Sonic Advance 1 to Sonic Adventure DX.It started with Sonic Advance which was one of my first GBA titles. In Sonic Advance it was easy to raise a Chao in the garden because all you had to do was run through the levels in time trial mode and collect rings many times. Sonic Advance had fun easy stages so the rings added up very quickly and before I knew it my Chao was level 99. Back then I had no idea what use the Chao could be so I leveled it up as much as I could.Sonic Adventure DX came out and after playing a while I realized that all 12 Sonic related games for Game Gear were unlockable. I didn't know about roms yet and loved the idea of playing Game Gear favorites on the TV. Sonic Mega Collection Plus and Sonic Gems Collection were not out yet either. By this time I only had 6 real Game Gear Sonic games so it was also like getting new harder to find games for free with some effort. I got most of the emblems but realized I needed to win some Chao competitions in order to get them all. Then I saw you could transport Chao from a GBA Sonic game to this one. There I remembered my max level Chao from Sonic Advance. I went to the store and got the GBA to Gamecube cable right away, easily won the competitions, and finally unlocked the remaining games.I have also tried using the cable to use my GBA as a controller when playing Game Boy games on the Gamecube via the Game Boy Player. It felt cool, but I stopped doing it because I got used to the Gamecube's d-pad location and the original GBA took batteries.
