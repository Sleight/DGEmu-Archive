MMilo-Jan 16 2015, 09:37 AM

Headsets really really REALLY come down to taste. A few tips though:

 

1.- If you are concerned about mic quality, forget about getting a headset with mic and buy a standalone mic. Even a cheap one will do better than the ones in 95% of the headsets out there.

 

2.- You WILL have to decide whether to prioritize headphones that are good for gaming or headphones that are good for music. Also whether you prefer open back (better soundstage at the expense of everyone being able to listen to them while you use them) or closed back for better sound isolation and bass.

 

3.- No you don't need LEDs to look edgy as fuck. Also, regardless of whether you want headphones for gaming or not, stay away from most "Gamer" headphones.

 

 

I haven't gone looking for laptops in a long while so I can't help you with that. Personally, the only brand of laptops I would trust is Asus though I've never taken a look at their laptops, but everything else they make is top notch.
