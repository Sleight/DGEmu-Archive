Ok, first we need to clear some stuff up here.1) Gamecube games do NOT spin backwards. They spin the same direction as any other optical disc on the face of the planet.2) Gamecube games, while not able to be read on computers straight out, ARE mini-DVDs. They just use a special press.3) If your cube is modded, you can use ordinary mini-DVDs in the system.4) If you have a special case top, you can use full DVDs. HOWEVER, only 1.4GB worth (the inner portion, the size of mini-DVDs) is read. So no, you can't burn 4+GB and expect your GC to read it.5) Without a modchip, you can only use the PSO exploit to boot stuff, however it's an elaborate process. Using a modchip such as the Qoob Pro is a much better way to go.http://modthatcube.pxn-os.com/main.htm - Has great information about Gamecube stuff.http://www.tehskeen.com/modules.php?name=D...egory&cid=1 - Mod That Cube's download section at TehSkeen.
