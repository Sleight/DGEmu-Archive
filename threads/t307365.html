Why do large breed of dogs have shorter lifespan than the smaller breeds? (1 point)
DDorian LightsbaneOct 16 2007, 01:43 AM
Through the generations, the outer size of the dogs have changed alot, but the size of organs takes much longer to change. That means that, generally, the larger breeds organs are too small and the heart has to pump much more to circulate the blood. That means a shorter life. The smaller breeds have organs that are larger than they need to be, thus the heart doesn't have to pump as many times to circulate the blood so it doesn't wear down as fast.
