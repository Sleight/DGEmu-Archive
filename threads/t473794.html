It's hard for me to pin down all of the franchises I would like to see have a sequal, but since this topic is in the current handheld section I'll try to limit myself to games that are centered in the handheld market.

 

1)  Lost Magic:  This little title on the DS invokes highly mixed feelings on my part.  It combines the elements of a RTS with those of action RPG and tends to be alright in terms of combat mechanics, but suffers from several major flaws that hurt the game overall.  I feel like this game does deserve a follow-up or prequel at the very least that hammers out the flaws of the original and improves upon it in every way.

 

2) My World My Way:  This title on the DS follows the adventures of Elise a spoiled princess who goes on adventures in order to impress a boy in order to become his girlfriend.  The game is just a lot of silly laughs in my opinion, and I'd love to see a follow-up that follows her continued adventures.

 

3) Summon Night:  While there was a DS game for this franchise it deserves far more recgonition and attention that it has received over the years.  Though, the DS game in my opinion is nowhere near as good as the GBA titles.

 

4) Bleach the 3rd Phantom:  While Bleach has received a lot of attention over the years this spin-off title follows the adventures of two original characters and is tied into the main plot of the series, but what makes me interested in a sequel is that to my knowledge this game is the only SRPG based off of the Bleach franchise.  I'd also hope that a sequel could address some of the balance issues that existed in the original game as well as balance out certain characters so that they weren't entirely useless.
