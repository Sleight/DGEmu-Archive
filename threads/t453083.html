BBISH! >:(Jan 14 2010, 09:23 AM
This is generally agreed upon to be the best Smeargle moveset, mainly because it's considered *almost* foolproof.EndureSporeEndeavorQuick Attack/Mach PunchHowever, you NEED a Salac Berry, which will increase your speed in a pinch. The idea here is go get a Smeargle with a nature that promotes Speed growth, and that has high IVs in speed. Then EV train him in only the speed category, if anything. Also, if you can, make sure you have LOW IVs in both of the defensive stats, as well as HP. Do NOT dump a single EV into any of those categories. That is the most crucial part of this moveset.Since Smeargle is so weak, it generally takes one hit to kill him. In fact, you WANT him to die in one hit. The plan is to use the move Endure, and pray that the opponent does any damaging attack, which will guarantee a kill, except that you're using Endure. This leaves you with 1 HP left, and the Salac Berry activates, making your speed ridiculously high. You'll need this to land the next attack FIRST. Now let loose with Spore, which has 100% accuracy, and guarantees the opponent falls asleep. This gives you at least one free move, and in most cases, at least two, depending on how unlucky you are.Here comes the fun part, use Endeavor which brings the opponent's HP down to yours, 1! Sometimes they'll wake up here if you're lucky, but more often than not, they'll be fast asleep after you use this. Next move, a simple Quick Attack/Mach Punch to finish them off!Next opponent comes out, rinse and repeat, without the Enduring, just put them to sleep, Endeavor, Quick Attack!

There are countless effective movesets for Smeargle. There are also separate effective movesets for Battle Frontier and competitive play.For the Battle Frontier, I've always run a set of Spore/Lock-On/Sheer Cold/Skill Swap to guarantee a kill in three turns.For competitive play, there are a lot more options. I'll be listing a few of the better ones:-Belly Drum Passer--Belly Drum--Substitute--Baton Pass--SporeUsually a lead, this set works wonders. Simply Spore, Substitute, Belly Drum and Baton Pass to something. It works even better with a Salac Berry to increase its Speed so it doesn't lose that Substitute while Baton Passing.-Baton Pass Support--Baton Pass--Spider Web--Ingrain--Spore/SubstituteThis set is great for supporting Baton Pass teams. The idea is to switch into something that can't really harm your team, then Spider Web, Ingrain and Baton Pass. Spore if neccessary.There's also the Endeavor set which has already been mentioned.
