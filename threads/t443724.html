iD has Linux ports of most of their games available and I believe there are Linux installers for every game in the Unreal series (not sure about Unreal 2) as well as Prey. There are also installers for the Serious Sam games if you want tons of mindless killing.Most of the open source FPS games for Linux are focused almost entirely on multiplayer. The good news is if you like multiplayer there are plenty of good ones with different themes and differing levels of prettiness/system requirements.http://icculus.org/alienarena/rpa/http://assault.cubers.net/http://alientrap.org/nexuiz/http://www.sauerbraten.org/http://tremulous.net/http://www.warsow.net/http://padworld.myexp.de/index.php?newshttp://www.bloodfrontier.com/http://legendsthegame.net/http://www.smokin-guns.net/When it comes to RPGs there aren't many open source ones with both a single player plot and 3D graphics. There are lots of classic 2D graphics RPGs and many of them are fun, there are several decent MMORPGs with Linux clients, and there are even some fun RPGs that don't technically have graphics at all. Nethack comes to mind for that last option.http://www.nethack.org/
