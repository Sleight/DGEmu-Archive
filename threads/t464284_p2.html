It's not over 9000. XD Flash cards don't have a measure of "power" per se. What he actually means is access time & read speed.C:PoR contains a timing bug that can actually freeze the official game card if you get enough going on at once and are unlucky. It's rare there, though. That bug becomes especially prominent when being played off flash cards because they tend to be slower than the memory inside an official card. Pulling up the start menu or the special effects used to display Wind are good examples of the sort of thing that can cause a freeze.Sandisk cards are known to be very slow, especially their standard 2GB model (even their "Ultra" models tend to be slower than their competitors' standard models). They're so slow in fact that they can cause problems with a lot of games such as you've been experiencing. One thing that may help a little bit is to format the card FAT16 w/64bit cluster size, which makes it a little easier for the DS to read.added: You have to be careful with the card ratings over on the M3 wiki. They've gotten kind of jumbled because the people contributing don't know the difference between the "Castlevania Test" and the "C:PoR" test. The former is actually a test of the intro movie to Castlevania: Dawn of Sorrow checking for skips or stuttering, especially during Dario's fire blast. The PoR crash test is not the same thing. eg: the "A-Data Super, 2GB" contributor got it wrong.
