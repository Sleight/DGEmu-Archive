PPyro StickMay 20 2012, 10:19 AM
These games are helpful but if its the only thing you are using to learn Japanese then apart from a small understanding of Hiragana you probably wont learn much. I suggest you buy the Japanese From Zero textbooks and work through them and the game at the same time or use the game for revision. The games on My Japanese Coach are probably the most helpful things out of the whole thing. It moves pretty fast as well so thats why i suggest you work through the books as well or else nothing will have a chance to stick. I stopped using the games when it started trying to teach me Katakana so ill go back to it once ive learnt Katakana through the books.

edit: I forgot to mention the price.
