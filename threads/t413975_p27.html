I have a modem that is a single port router plugged into a normal port on my WRT54G. The modem is set to forward all the internet traffic from the outside to the WRT and then I use the routing tables on it to send it on to which ever machine I want. If it still won't work open a command prompt and type "ipconfig /release *" and hit enter and then do "ipconfig /renew *" OR you can right click on network places and hit properties find your LAN connection right click on it and disable it and then right click on it and enable it again. If your STILL can't connect to the linksys to access the config page unplug it from the PC, reboot the PC, reset the router, and then plug the router back into the PC. Pick the linksys in the little drop down box, click enable then hit OK on the pop up window and the modem will reset. Then reset the linksys. After that things should act like the linksys it directly attached to the internet.The other option is Static NAT. Setup is similar to DMZ except you pick the static NAT option on the left instead of DMZ. The router won't act like it is directly attached to the internet but instead anything the westell doesn't think was requested traffic (AKA web pages and such) will be forwarded to the linksys and from there you can configure port forwarding on the linksys and send it where it really needs to go.
