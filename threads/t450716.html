While I was away at college I used my laptop as a wireless adapter. For those who don't know this little trick it's pretty simple:1. Take an ethernet cord and connect your 360 to your laptop.2. Turn on your xbox and your laptop.3. Make your way to the Network Connections menu via Control Panel.4. Highlight the Local Area Connection (your xbox) and the Wireless Connection.5. Right Click and hit Bridge Connection.Now this worked for me while I was away, but now that I'm home for a long break it's not working. I don't know if it's because of the slower internet speed or what... any suggestions?
