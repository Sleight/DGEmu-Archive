Touhou didn't even start as a shmup, but this weird arkanoid style game where you control Reimu bouncing a ball up.
This was the original Touhou on PC-98
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=duIPehrTbO0
 
Later, it came as a shmup, traditional top-down.
Then came the competitive VS shmup.
After that, it moved from PC-98 computers to Windows platform, and the fighting games were born.
 
It became popular because of the story, the music, and the characters, so it spawned this humongus following all around Japan, and soon after, all around the world, so there's doujin games and stuff based on Touhou EVERYWHERE, going turn based RPG, Castlevania, Megaman, Mario, soccer, puzzlers, rail shooters, etc. And people all get into Touhou, not through games, but mainly through music and then the characters.
 
And when I was saying with Kui that other night that Touhou remixes > anything, it litterally is, any style of music is there, classical, death metal, orchestrated, jazz, rock, pop, electro, you name it. It went to the point that it even has it's dedicated convention for music releases.
