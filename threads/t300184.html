Yup, that's all correct. It's a Squeenix game, created by the same creators of the Kingdom Hearts series, and only this time there's no Disney affiliation, so rejoice for that. This game takes a bit of an emphasis on urban lifestyle and for the most part, urban music. The inclusion of the touch screen control really is amazing once you get used to it.It seems that Nomura is having a bit of trouble localizing the title to the U.S., since there is a movie licensed under the same name. It's an extremely lame excuse, but it's legal, and that's all that seems to matter in the whole issue. I for one would love to see this translated, but if it happens that there's no localization, let's set our hopes on skilled fan translators.

AArchaicSentinelAug 08 2007, 10:13 AM
There's 8 buttons. The top right one is badge modification, the one left of that is equipment/item management. The one with the animals on it is the beastiary. The bottom right one is the Save option (usually available a day or two into the story). I have yet to find much use for the other options as they deal with the Wireless Communications and it's pointless as nobody else around me has the game.The game is very fun with a lot of replayability. Fan translation for this game looks highly plausible. Overall I'd say that this game is pretty enjoyable with the new dual screen battle system (it's hard at first but you get the hang of it eventually). The only downside is the language barrier and the fact that this is an RPG does not make it very import friendly.
