- Crossup = switching sides so the opponent has to switch their block direction. Air Trick always teleports on the opposite side, so the j.H will be a crossup.- BnB = Bread & Butter = an optimized combo, generally max damage while still being practical, that is meant to be used in a real match. Every character has a couple of BnBs off common starters that you should learn.- Bold Cancel = Using an installed (meaning you don't actually jump) Bold Move to cancel something, usually cancelling Stinger into teleport or a special. In the corner, you can also do the full (jumping) Bold Move after Stinger and followup with j.S/Hammer, but otherwise you have to input the cancelled move very quickly after the Bold Move, before Stinger's active frames end and Dante jumps. It does take some practice to do, at first try Stinger BC Volcano-Charge Shots is the upward gunshots style cancel, input is H after st.S-Anywhere you can do Crazy Dance (S style cancel), you can also do Fireworks (H style cancel). Just do Fireworks. A 2 frame link is technically very hard (in Marvel anyways lol) If you really want to do Crazy Dance, trying piano-ing your fingers on S to have more chances to hit the just frame.-Instant Air-Dash = IAD = air-dashing at the earliest jump frame.
