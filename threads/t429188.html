As an art student, I'll throw in my two cents.Flow and Movement are two different things.IIRC, flow is how your eyes moves around the piece, like a line moving from what side to the other.Movement is synonymous with the word flow, but movement generally means that it looks like something is moving. Flow and Movement are very important to one another, pieces that show movement almost always show flow and vice versa.I agree that most of your work does not show movement or flow, but thats how they were made, and they are still interesting.
