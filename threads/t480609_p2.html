As a beginner I would say the best things to start with would be:

 

-getting a good understanding of your anatomy

-gauging your initial strength

before you go through all of the trouble of actually developing some in depth routine that you might just drop altogether, do some test runs, see what your body is capable of. See which exercises seem easy with various weights and which seem hard. It might be hard with your current set up, but explore compound exercises in addition to isolation exercises (isolation = exercises that mainly target a single muscle such as a bicep curl and most of the stuff I listed. compound = exercises which uses multiple muscles or an entire muscle group such as a push/pull up).

Fiber is a hard thing to get sometimes.  Why raw green beans though?  There are tastier ways to eat them.  There are green bean chip things.  Or, even better, get some vegetable stock and some spices along with a lean turkey sausage, then get like every kind of legume you can find and make a stew.  High fiber, high protein, middle of the road calories, and effing delicious.  Plus, leftovers for days that warm up great in the microwave.

If you really want some fiber though, just get some lentils and make a simple lentil soup.  Takes like 20 minutes, has plenty of leftovers, and lentils are way higher in fiber than green beans.
