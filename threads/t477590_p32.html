I spent today playing Pokemon Uranium with my niece. It's an RPG Maker fangame that's actually pretty good. It's on a MUCH smaller scale than current gen pokemon games, but it has a lot of cool stuff like:

 

-almost an entirely original roster of pokemon to choose from (including made up evolutions for a few pre-existing pokemon)

-modern features such as online battling, GTS, and wonder trade

-smart innovations (i.e. certain NPC trainers you can keep fighting to grind money/exp without having to fight wild pokemon)

-almost everything is completely original, just injected into the cookie cutter pokemon formula of going from town to town and beating gyms

-lots of really cool hidden tricks and secrets such as secret criteria for certain evolutions (one of the junky pokemon you can pick up at the start can evolve into a legendary)

-A new mechanic called "nuclear pokemon" which is supposed to be when pokemon go feral and stop listening to commands and can potentially attack the trainer (haven't tried it yet but it sounds cool)

 

Cons:
-It's a fan game and is naturally sort of awkward and cringy

-some of the custom pokemon are straight up ripped off of some deviantart designs

-it's made in RPG Maker, not a hacked game, so all of the familiar pokemon mechanics are recreated and therefore aren't EXACTLY as they are in real pokemon games (i.e. poison sting is that shitty move most bug pokemon start off with that does no damage and has a low chance to poison but in this game it has a really high poison chance)

-has somewhat weird difficulty pacing making you need to grind in certain spots

-lack of animations as the animations have to be hand-made in rpg maker

 

We only got through the first gym after about 2 hours of playing but we've been having a lot of fun with it. I'd recommend it if any of you have actually bothered buying any of the 3DS games or just out of curiosity. It's free and pretty simple to get working on pc, it's just kind of finicky.
