GGnark-Aug 23 2007, 04:15 AM
I'm suprised there wasn't more boomshot talk, oh well. I'll give my analysis of each weapon, and then tell my favorite.Lancer - Mid-range combat. It's all right for most players, allowing for a constant flow of damage and a one hit, melee range kill. But one shot from a pistol can render the chainsaw useless, often getting the bayonet wielder killed himself. But, it is a necessity for cover-fire, especially on maps like Rooftops and Canals.Hammerburst - Mid-range combat. Just a faster version of the lancer without the chainsaw. Really only good for cover-fire and the occasional down.Gnasher Shotgun - Close-range combat. Given the officila speculation of a one hit kill from a scaled three meters away. Very good, especially if you have luck, even the most skilled players can be torn apart by a new player with a little luck. Often a bad choice against any special weapon (excluding the sniper). Most commonly used in multiplayer due to the one hit kill effect.Pistol - Mid-range combat. Nice zoom in feature, with a response trigger. Definitely the most intelligent pistol design in any shooter game on the market. The pistol whip is often executed, where an opponet is shot twice by the pistol, and then beat down. Very useful when trying to conserve ammo for the powerful guns and looking to kill a downed opponent.Boltok Revolver - Short-range combat. Not all that accurate, but very powerful for it's size. Mostly recommended for those who have very honed accuracy, because of the possible two hit kill (i.e. one shot to the chest, then one to the head). Something that puts the stop on this being a great weapon is it's very slow speed.Boomshot - Short/Mid-range combat. Very powerful, and capable of killing multiple targets. Often inaccurate becuase of the heavy projectile, causing it to arc. In a last effort, a player can kill himself and an enemy if his team is desperate for a win. Also comes with a powerful melee.Torque Bow - All-range combat. Definitely the most diverse weapon. Long ranged shots can be pulled off with this explosive weapon, with a very fast projectile. After death kills and multiple kills are also possible. There is also a blade on the end of it, leading to another powerful melee attack. Only setback is the time for charging it.Longshot Sniper Rifle - Mid/Long-range combat. One bullet clip, meh. It's always easy to pull off a headshot with proper patience and skill. I myself have personally gotten at least 500 headshots with it and only have 5000 ranked kills. Slow reload, but one shot downs are guaranteed when shot in the knees and above the waist with an active shot. Also a very nice melee. Not good at shotgun range.Hammer of the Dawn - All-range combat. D: I hate this thing. Slow, more annoying to get killed by than frag tags, dumb beeping noise. Massive damage, but no skill is involved at all. There is also no way to tell whose team is dropping the shot either. Suicide is very possible.In conclusion - I typed a lot, but the Torque Bow is the best weapon. I don't mind having to charge it up, fast shot and explosive projectile. It's a boomshot/sniper/sword hybrid, and can combat pretty much every weapon efficiently, except a shotgun to the face.
