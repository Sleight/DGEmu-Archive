CCoffeeConeORIGINAL POSTAug 24 2013, 05:41 AM
So I just finished Summon Night 1 on GBA. I happily got Summon Night 2 but I was kind of disappointed with it. For some reason it doesn't have the same feel I got with the first. I can't pinpoint what exactly. It could be the soundtrack? It could be the story? I'm not sure

I also missed the seemingly random guardian beast allotment on Summon Night where you have to answer a few questions to determine who you get as opposed to 2's vague description selection.

I also noticed that some enemies have been nerfed down to beginner level. Like the birds and that smiling fish thing that sends lightning bolts. I encountered them on the first forest map and I was surprised I could easily take them on with my beginner sword.

I also don't get the new crafting thing. There seems to be no more forging of random things I pick up and I can only use Iron Ore.

I want to enjoy Summon Night 2 but for some reason, I can't. Probably it's because I just finished Summon Night and am till on the point where I compare each sequel I play to the original.

Thanks for reading! If you have insights, please do reply.
