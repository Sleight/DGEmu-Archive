Bit of a Google, but a few random sites (including GBATemp) seemed to keep coming to the one solution.Apparently that game's not actually freezing, it's just that the dialogue box thingo just isn't showing up on the emulator. Except since you can't move during conversations/events, you're just standing there with no control over your character. So basically, just keep pressing 'A' to move past all the unviewable text, and crank up your volume to see if you can hear little beep noises indicating you're moving through the text.Those random sites I mentioned:http://answers.yahoo.com/question/index?qi...10181014AAZn3BAhttp://gbatemp.net/index.php?showtopic=146912http://warezvb.org/tutorials/42814-pokemon...lar-solved.html
