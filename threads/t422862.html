MMahjixNov 02 2008, 12:12 PM
Even though critique can be gained through this site, I think if you were really serious about the kind of art that your're doing and really wanted some critique, a good suggestion would be to join conceptart.org. You can learn many things from there. If you don't get a comment here, then either the forum is dead or people just don't feel like commenting. You can't make people comment by whining and threatening. That just makes people even more compelled to not comment at all.I'll let you sit on that.On topic, I'm not going to bluntly say "this is crap" because I believe that's a terrible thing to say to someone who's not well developed in their art abilities and everyone has to start from somewhere. It's not bad but keep trying. You seem to have some general idea of the structure of the face. I would say to work on the proportions of the face a little bit. I think you were going for an Anime style but the eyes are a bit huge in proportion to her age (judging that she's in her twenties). You will notice that in Anime the younger the characters are the larger the eyes are going to be and the older the characters are the smaller the eyes will be. Take Roy Mustang and Edward from Full Metal Alchemist for example: Their eye sizes are matched by their age. There are many different eye styles out there; try fooling around with different eye styles that fit a particular character's age. The chin is very rounded for an Anime character, especially for a female. Her bottom lip is really big in comparison to her top lip. The hair looks like it's on top of her "head line." When drawing hair, give your character a top head; the hair needs too look like it's lifted off the head... not be it. Bring the hair up (lol hair-lift). For the line quality... it's a bit more complex to explain and I don't want to flood you with more critique than needed but I would definitely add an outline on that hair. It can become quite tricky when trying to exclude a hair outline on an Anime character. Eyebrows are thicker than just one hair! Stylize it and make it look more suggestive or something. Give it some weight. On the coloring, try not to use gradients this way on hair. It's not the most appealing thing. Just make it a solid color and call it a day.Are you working with a tablet or are you doing this all by mouse? Are you sketching things out before drawing them or are you just drawing them as you go? If you're not sketching, I would advise to do so.
