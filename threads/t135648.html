Almost always watches Subs and not dubs because by the time they dub it, you've already finished the series. Exception is if you haven't heard of the series before hand.Lower mid-level: You know and have watched a few series Adult Swim hasn't shown. Prefer Subs over Dubs, but will watch dubs regularly. You've downloaded a few series, usually only of the ones on the bottom or a series that a higher leveled fanatic recommends to you.Lowest levels: you're favorite or only animes are Bleach/Naruto/DBZ/Inuyasha/One Piece.
