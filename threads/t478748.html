Well it seems that everyone has recommended some great games so far so I won't be redundant. I will however recommend Resident Evil Revelations, it's arguably the best 3DS game out there (maybe not so much with the recent release of Fire Emblem: Awakening) It was the first 3DS game I purchased and I still pick it up to play on a regular basis.

 

You mentioned you are an RPG fan so I'll also recommend Tales of the Abyss, it's a great port from PS2 and a pretty solid RPG.

Check out Kingdom Hearts: Dream Drop Distance and Code of Princess if you're into the action RPGs.

 

I don't want to overwhelm you but I'll just throw out a short list of some of my 3DS games just to give you some ideas.

 

Sakura Samurai: Art of the Sword (E-Shop, Action)

Mutant Mudds (E-Shop, Platformer)

Gunman Clive (E-Shop, Platformer)

Adventure Time: Hey Ice King! Why'd You Steal Our Garbage!?? (Retail, Platformer, Action RPG)

 

Check Out the E-Shop! There are tons of great games to download.

Some games on my E-Shop wishlist are Unchained Blades a hardcore first person dungeon crawler and Crimson Shroud a tribute to table top RPGs and an overall good RPG.
