KKirie MotobaMay 11 2012, 05:49 PM
Best practice:

I seperate my files into 800MB chunks. Most file hosts allow free users to download files up to 1GB each.
The exceptions to that rule that I know about are Mediafire and Crocko. Both of them, if I recall, is a 200MB chunk max.

I personally use WinRAR with maximum compression + 800MB separation. You would preferably stay with formats that are popular (zip or rar), and not use the lesser known (7z or hjsplit) as you don't want to bother people installing too many things to get their roms opened. You can at your discretion, as I know Pan does, and it's not the end of the world if you do it.

Registering free accounts will keep the files on servers for 90 days instead of 30.
Registering for Mediafire gives you no advantage, and makes you more prone to actually get your files removed because of copyright, same goes for Rapidshare, though Pan has his share of good luck and isn't getting his things torn down.

Registering to sites such as Uploaded.to, Depositfiles.com, and FileFactory.com earns you money as people download your files (don't expect a fortune though, and it also explains why DM uses said services).

Don't forget about the naming convention, the game title and region in between parentheses.
I can safely assume you will be uploading PAL games? If so, (E).
