The amount of lag depends on how fast your computer is. There are also some settings for no$gba to go faster, to change them load a rom with no$gba and go to options--> emulation setup. A new window will pop up and you can change the settings, the upper-left drop-down box that says "Emulation Speed, LCD Refresh has some speed settings for the emulator, meddle a bit around with it and see the results, but you can also change the Sound Output mode to Digital mono, or even none, another sound setting that improves performance is Sound Desired Sample Rate, which will reduce the sound quality at lower Hz but will also increase performance.If you want to use features that use the microphone there's a really easy way to do this (without getting audacity like the first post says, I'll change that later on). Just go to your taskbar and double-click the speaker, you'll get into volume control. Now go to options--> properties. A new window will open and you will see two round buttons, playback and recording. Select recording and make sure that stereo mix is checked in the list below and hit ok. You'll return to the window with the options changed, go to stereo mix and check the box select at the bottom, this way every sound your computer generates will be recorded, but no$gba will also see it as a microphone. Now start up no$gba start up some music, or something else and see if it works.Edit: The method I mentioned only works up to XP without installing software, if you have vista or up, you may need to update the driver of your sound card
