I've been playing Unite for a bit, and I can honestly say it's well worth it. One good thing about it is the "character transfer" option so you can pick up where you left off on Freedom 2.They tweaked quite a few things here, from Monster move-sets, Monster stats (i.e. more HP), modified weapons and armour, etc.I"m pretty sure they amped up the AI...Take the Silver Rathalos, for example. In Freedom 2, it usually takes me less than 15 mins to farm him with Onslaught Hammer. In Unite, it takes me nearly a half hour (and a few Mega Potions) to bring him down using the same techniques Then there's the Felyne Comrades. To be honest, that's the only thing that feels gimmicky, but they're actually quite fun to Hunt with depending if you set up their skills creatively. (Affinity Attack, Attack Up, Fall 7x/Get up 8x= one ass-kicking cat XD )Overall, it's pretty good. If I had to give a rating...7.5 or 8 out of 10. Despite all the new stuff they added, it's still technically the same game as Freedom 2. Side-note: I'm not sure if it's in Freedom 2, but I personally love the "Quick-stand Technique" in Unite. After being knocked down you can instantly dodge or guard AS you get back up. You don't get combo-ed to death that way :facepalm:
