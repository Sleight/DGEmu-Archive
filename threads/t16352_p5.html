Perhaps people should ask themselves what 'easy' means. If 'easy' means 'win 99%, and maybe more, of the battles in-game' then Pokemon is certainly easy. Most, if not all, games are biased towards the player succeeding, in order to make that player happy and therefore keep them playing. This is certainly the case with Pokemon - defeat in anything other than a Gym match is from my experience extraordinarily rare. However, many of these battles are pitched so that they're a challenge to the player, albeit one in which the odds favor them. I think it's a testament to Game Freak's skills that they usually get things right; battling, along with collecting, has always formed the meat of the Pokemon games, and if it were, as some say, 'too easy' then by definition many people would stop playing. Personally, I found Ruby/Sapphire too easy - this was to my mind a result of the dilution which that generation underwent when compared to the vastly GS series which preceded it. This was also a problem for many of my friends, who were disappointed by the ease at which the game could be cleared; many of them, like myself, are hoping for drastic improvements to Diamond/Pearl when our pre-orders come in in a couple weeks.
