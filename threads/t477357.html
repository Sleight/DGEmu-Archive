In a world inhabited by goblins and corrupt magicians, someone's gotta set it right! Courtney moves in to her uncles house-a huge mansion in a town populated by wizards, with her parents,who seem to care more about social status than their kid! As she tries to navigate through middle school life by day, her nights are filled with magic lessons from her uncle, Aloysius Crumrin, and team ups with the goblins waging wars in the town's backyard.
