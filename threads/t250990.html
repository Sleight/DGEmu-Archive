RRickstilwellJul 21 2009, 11:07 PM
I have a Virtual Boy and four games. They are Wario Land, Mario Clash, Mario's Tennis and Red Alarm (which plays kind of like SNES Star Fox). Wario Land is unique on this system, completely different from the Game Boy version and is just as fun as any Mario or Wario platformer.Mario Clash is an arcade style game.Virtual Boy never hurts my eyes or anything, but also I don't actually put my eyes right up to the screen. I give them some space where I can still see perfectly fine into the visor and adjust the screen position with the knob on the top of the unit until it is at a comfortable angle.Myself, I think it would be worth it to put more work into a Virtual Boy emulator. If they did it right, it could turn out to run just like a Game Boy emulator with custom color palette changing. I think the 3D effect is the least important aspect of the Virtual Boy to emulate. The most important aspect is the pure gameplay.The games themselves are not bad and should all be made playable on newer hardware in some form. If the games were to be remade on any system 32-bit or higher, background to foreground interaction is still possible. Keep in mind how the two Oddworld games for Playstation have sections where Abe goes into the background of the area and you can still control him there.I think at least two games, Wario Land (one of Wario's best adventures) and Mario Clash (a fun arcade style Mario) should be remade for play on a newer system rather than forgotten.As for the Virtual Boy emulators, all of the ones I have seen so far have lag issues, no remappable PC controller support, or no sound. Fix these three problems and we would have a good Virtual Boy emulator.
