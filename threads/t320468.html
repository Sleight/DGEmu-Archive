RRevolving DoorORIGINAL POSTAug 29 2007, 02:55 PM
The three transformations in these games are Berserk X Shinobi ,Thunder Berserker, and Berserk X Shinobi. Apparently there was supposed to be a third version called "Thunder Berserker" but, the developers decided that they should add TB to both Berserk X Shinobi and Beserk X Dinosaur versions.

Also using a technique called "Double Tribe", the three transformations can be combined to create three more: Shinobi Dinosaur, Dinosaur Berserker, and Berserker Shinobi. The Japan release date is in November 22, 2007, but there hasn't been any info on when it's going to be released in other countries.
