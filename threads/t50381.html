DreamCast was one of the best systems ever made, IMHO. It was the first of a generation of consoles that includes the PS2, XBox and GameCube. DC was (and is) known for quality arcade-style games and fluid graphics. I love DC games, because most of them have an "Arcade Mode" you can play anytime. Load times were extremely fast and the graphics are just a little below what you'd expect on a PS2 or GameCube, depending on the game.What I really love about the DC is all the innovative extras. It was the first console to feature on-line gameplay. It has a built-in 56K modem, so you could challenge opponents in multi-player games or even surf the Web! It also had an optional microphone, a reel for fishing games and a light gun. You could also play mini-games on the memory cards!I think DreamCast's biggest downfall was it couldn't play DVDs. Also, Sony generated so much hype for the PS2 that consumers (and game developers) flocked to it, leaving the DC in the dust. Now you can pick up a used DC for around $25 (US).I love my DC, even more than my PS2 and XBox. DC is easy to hack...you can download and burn CDRs, then pop them into your DC. You can play SMS, NES, Super NES and Genesis games right from your DC without a mod chip! (Not that anyone here would do such a thing.) :whistle2:
