EEndymion_MallornOct 04 2014, 03:56 AM

Let's start with the "Low DPs" thing.  If you like Sudoku?  There seem to be plenty.

 

Now, let's talk about quality, shall we?

Pokemon Mystery Dungeon: Blue Rescue Team - if you're looking to save on DPs, get Red Rescue Team on this one.  But here's the thing - Pokemon + Roguelike.  Dungeon crawling with Pokemon alone should grab you.  The fact that it just happens to have the highest number of "preview" pokemon in any game might help too.

Pokemon Ranger, Pokemon Conquest - Twists on the Pokemon formula.

Nine Hours, Nine Persons, Nine Doors; Ace Attorney Series; Trauma Center: Under The Knife Series: All of these are "light novels", and I enjoy playing through the story.  The game is pretty darn good too.

Advance Wars: They're great.  Pick up Days of Ruin if you like post-apocalyptic stories, or Dual Strike if you've played 1 & 2 (and why wouldn't you?  Just download 'em!)

 

 

I have more suggestions, but those should get you going.  PM me if you want some details!!
