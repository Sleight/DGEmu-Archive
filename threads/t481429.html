First of all welcome to DG. Second, first you need to make sure that you have enough Download points to make the download start. If you don't I suggest looking for a cheaper download. Now If you did have enough DP, and the download started, check your browser to see where downloaded files go (unless you are using IE in which case it likely put it in the downloads folder). Depending what you were trying to download, you will find the release number, the title of the game and then .zip or other archive type. From there make sure you have an emulator that will play the game you downloaded. After that it's a matter of what you are trying to do exactly. If you feel like elaborating on your problem further feel free and either I or someone else will gladly help.
