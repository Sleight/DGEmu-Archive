JJCAM-CTZOGFeb 28 2009, 11:12 PM
Played FF Origins, 1 got boring fast and I wasn't crazy about 2's leveling system. Plus, 2 got stale halfway through, never beat either game.I played FF3 (NES version) and again, got old fast.FF4 was great, I've beaten it 3 times over the past 8 years since I first played it.I got about 1/3 of the way in FF5 then lost interest.I've beaten FF6 twice, one of my favorite SNES games, even though I played the PSX version. Same thing.FF7, as overrated as it is, was a great game back when it was released and is still good in its own right. Fanboys kinda ruin it though. I've beaten it 3 or 4 times since first playing it back in 1998, almost got the PAL version for the extra stuff (no ruby/emerald weapon in that version as a trade-off).I thought FF8 was kinda dumb. The story was complete nonsense, and leveling up the enemies as you leveled up your characters made the game's difficulty kinda wacky. Some enemies you needed to be at a high level so you could draw better magic, but once you got to 100 you're at a serious disadvantage, makes beating the game nearly impossible.It wasn't awful, but I didn't find a whole lot about that game that I liked. Beat it once.FF9 was pretty fun, and was semi-interesting. I wasn't a fan of the limit-breaks though, most of them were useless, and Quina and Freya felt like random characters that didn't offer much to the story at all. Beat it twice.FF10, I loved it when it first came out, but over time I've slowly begun to hate its battle system, meh voice acting, tripe plot and cookie-cutter characters. Yeah, it definitely has its good points, like Blitzball and a good soundtrack, along with a few interesting pieces here and there, but I dunno, the FF 'magic' seemed to be missing from this one. ;/ Also, it was way too easy and only had a few side-extras.If you can get past all the estrogen pumping through FF10-2, it's actually kinda of fun and can be quite addicting. I hated 80% of the soundtrack and I thought the 'good' endings made the 60+ hours of gameplay seem hardly worth it. I didn't mind, actually kind of liked the battle system, and the graphics were purdy and sparkly. I beat it at.....97% I believe, never bothered with the chocobos or Via Infinito.I'm currently playing FF12 and am about 60% through it and loving it. Though there's so much stuff that getting 100% the first time around without a guide is going to be impossible. Still, I look at that as a testament to how large the game really is. 12 just might be my favorite one so far.And I guess these count too...FFT for PSX, it was good, but I actually didn't beat it on PSX, I got auto-saved at the first in a series of 'last' battles and was a bit under-leveled, and I didn't feel like putting an additional 50 hours into it. The PSP remake made the game twice as good, and the re-translation was very much welcome (and needed imo). I beat that once, but never did alot of the extras, mostly because I had no idea just how many were in it, and it's alot.FFTA wasn't as bad as most people say. Sure the story was kinda lame but I don't think a GBA game is going to be directed at people looking for a big epic war story anyhow, they were just playing to their *target* audience. I beat it once, then forgot about it afterward.
