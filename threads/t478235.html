It's time for everyone's favorite segment, Underrated Games and Undiscovered Gems! I don't remember what number this is since the old threads were deleted. I think there was like 1 or 2 of them but I don't remember what the games were. Anyways, today's game is Lost Magic for the DS. I don't really remember what the game is about but it's generic good guys VS bad guys stuff (the story doesn't matter, it's all about dat gameplay), here's wikipedia's synopsis:
someone wrote:
The storyline of the game centers on a young mage named Isaac whose father Russell, has left Isaac in the possession of the Wand of Light, one of seven powerful wands which allows him to cast damaging, healing, and other spells, and also capture and control opposing monsters. Isaac got separated from his parents when he was little, and was raised by a forest witch in the arts of magic.


Anyways, this is a game I actually bought many years ago and played on my chunky, original Nintendo DS and loved it. I first saw it in Nintendo Power (those pre-Internet days). There was only like 2 pages about it but the pictures looked awesome. The first thing that came to mind was FullMetal Alchemist (FMA used to air on Adult Swim so I was really into it at the time). The pages showed that you drew symbols on what looked to be like Transmutation Circles and it just looked awesome. I bought it and loved the game.

The gameplay is unique, it's a touch screen heavy game that requires you to draw symbols on your spell circle-thing. Various symbols correspond with certain elements and can be mixed and matched to make all kinds of combos. You lead your character around the map strategy-style shooting spells at your enemies. You can later recruit a horde of monsters to follow you around and fuck things up. The spells you can use and how many times you can use them are based on your MP. There are times when you'll have to duck out and hide until your MP replenishes itself enough for another raid. The gameplay is pretty involving, requires you to be alert and to think ahead. However, the game manages to still be very fun despite the hectic battles. I've never really seen anyone mention this game before so I figured it would be perfect for this segment as it's a really fun game for people that like strategy games or just want to play a DS game that legitimately uses touch screen controls. There's not much else I can say about the gameplay since I haven't played this in like 7 years (holy crap) but all it takes is a minute or 2 to look at the gameplay video above to see if the game is appealing to you. Give it a watch or try the game yourself, I don't really give a **** (this one might require an actual DS but I think playing it on an emu with a mouse or a drawing pad might be possible but won't be as easy as the real thing), have fun and good night!

There's this GBA game I really liked, but it has an absurdly, almost implausibly low amount of recognition from the internet.

Metalgun Slinger.

You play as this anime cowboy with a laser revolver, and it basically plays like Contra or Megaman, except you have this agility meter you can use to dodge bullets, do high jumps, barrel roll, chain shots together, and use spread attacks. It was one of the only GBA games I've played where the character has such a complex moveset, and all of it unlocked right from the get-go.

There's more, like smart bomb-like attacks and ability boosting medals, but that's nowhere near as fun to mess around with.

Level design's decent, music is decent, but the enemies are kind of bland. Samey bandits and robots and stuff, and all of them are so weak 50 of them couldn't take you on.

On the other hand, not much stopping you from turning each level into a bullet-riddled parkour course.
