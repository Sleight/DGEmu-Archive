THE THING I'M GOING TO DO

This weekend, a group of 6 friends myself including, aged between 18 and...27 I think, 50% male and 50% female, will be individually travelling towards a certain so called bungalow park (AKA, apparently, a holiday village). There, we will be spending two nights in a bungalow. That is, a one-story building with limited facilities that somehow still manages to be somewhat comfortable, if a lot less than my own place. While staying the aforementioned weekend in the aforementioned bungalow park, we will be doing...I have absolutely no idea what. But it's probably going to be fun. At night, when we're not going to be leaving the bungalow anyway, we will play games and watch movies (hence the request) and talk and stuff until deep in the night, or until some of the inhabitants of neighboring bungalows start complaining. After the weekend, each of us will be going their own way.

 

THE THING LOTS OF DUTCH PEOPLE DO

During summer holidays, lots and lots of Dutch families grab their caravan AKA trailer houses AKA sleurhut (meaning quite literally haulhut) and drive approximately 1000-2000km southwards towards France, Italy, or Spain. Travelling with such a caravan/trailer house/sleurhut takes a pretty long time since officially you're only allowed to go 80 km/h. Hence, many a Dutch family takes one or more stops at campings on the way. Some people reserve those camping spots beforehand, others simply check random campings, hoping there will still be a spot free. The latter are stupid people.

Once the Dutch family has finally reached their destination, everyone is pissed and tired and angry, and they check in at their actual main camping, where they will spend the major part of their holiday/vacation. Depending on facilities available on said camping, prices may vary between... fuck if I know, â‚¬20 and â‚¬200 a night? So, robbed of a lot of their money the family will spend approximately 1 to 3 weeks on said camping, varying per family, and they will enjoy it despite not having access to a decent toilet, a decent bed, and a lot of other decent things.

I have done this myself with my parents and siblings...three times I think, and I enjoyed all of it. Mostly.

After those approximately 1 to 3 weeks, the family will return to the Netherlands, using the same route as before but possibly with less stops, because "we're going home anyway". When they arrive home at last, everyone will be incredibly glad to be home again.

 

ANOTHER THING A LOT OF DUTCH PEOPLE DO BUT PROBABLY LESS BECAUSE IT'S MORE EXPENSIVE

Pretty much the same as in previous paragraph, except instead of driving the whole bloody distance with a caravan/trailer house/sleurhut they just go by car alone, and at each camping waiting for them will be a so-called stacaravan (which basically means standing caravan i.e. standing trailer house which sounds retarded), that is, a caravan that happens to be a bit more house-ish, with a bit more comfort and facilities, and is all in all rather similar to a bungalow, except it's still possible to somehow move it without needing...whatever you need to move a house. It is, however, not as mobile as an ordinary caravan/trailer house/sleurhut. The better comfort also means you're going to pay more. No idea how much, but probably twice as much or something.

I have done this with my parents and siblings as well. About.... 7 times, I guess. I also liked this.

 

ANOTHER THING A LOT OF DUTCH PEOPLE DO THAT I ALMOST FORGOT BECAUSE IT'S FOR POOR PEOPLE AND TRYHARD CAMPERS

However, you do get to drive a bit faster on the way towards your destination.
