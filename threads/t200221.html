A bluray drive doesn't weigh that much, actually. Big things that are different include the more massive cooling system and the fact that it has a large (16 cm?) fan and a series of large heatsinks all sharing the airflow capacity of that one fan, and the fact that its power supply is internal. If you consider the 360 including its external power brick, it's technically heavier than the PS3. That said, the difference that the internal power supply with PFC really makes is that Sony doesn't need to make separate SKUs for separate regions based on 110/120 V vs. 220/240 V power. Of course, they have a different model for the EU where they decided to transition to entirely software emulation of PS2/PS1 which hurt compatibility a bit, but it's the better choice in the long run (not that gamers have the ability to give a damn about the long run), and it saves on some manufacturing costs. That's technically a new revision, not a new SKU. Other regions will get the same at some point if they haven't already.
