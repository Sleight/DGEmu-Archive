It's fairly simple, and I got my own methods to reach the max quality nowadays without breaking my head:

 

On PC: Record with Fraps, stick files together with VirtualDub using the direct copy option to preserve quality, use Any Video Converter and upscale it to 1080p mp4, upload mp4 to Youtube.

 

On console: Record all games in 720p with component cables, use Any Video Converter and upscale it to 1080p mp4, upload mp4 to Youtube.
