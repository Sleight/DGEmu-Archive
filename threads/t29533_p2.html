SSir VGFeb 07 2008, 06:50 AM
Often times, an ISO is compressed and split into multiple parts to allow for smaller downloads. Once you get all of the parts from a website, put them all together into one folder and open up one file with WinRAR or 7-Zip (001, 002, etc in your case, you need 7-Zip). Then you'll see your files to extract, so extract them. Now you have an ISO.(Generally with 7-zip splitting, you have to extract it twice. Once will give you a single 7-zip file, then open that up to be able to extract your ISO)If you have a set of files named .r00 or .partxx.rar, then you use WinRAR.
