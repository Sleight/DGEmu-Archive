Three extra characters, in addition to the 15 or so from Scarlet Weather Rhapsody (10.5)


Btw, the Touhou fightans are not much like normal fightans because of the movement and projectile mechanics...neutral game is about filling the screen with a bajillion projectiles while dodging/grazing through your opponenents, then the rushdown game is freaking endless pressure for days. Some characters have setups for infinite unescapable blockstrings lol...well you can escape with the guard cancel which you have to use, unlike in other anime fighters. Also although blockstrings are long, if you block wrong you don't get hit, you just get more blockstun so you can up-back for a loooong time...




Anyways...the game is easy. Basic melee dial-a-combo is mash A a bunch of times, which you can then cancel with a special/spellcard. Spellcards are like supers and EX moves, I guess if you're lazy you can use cards instead of actually inputting specials, though you can only use max 3 of each skillcard. Otherwise its very much the same as actually playing Touhou, just spam projectiles (both B and C are projectiles) and fly through your opponents, note that flight time is limited by orbs and you can go through projectiles with dash/flight. Once you get in, the basic gist is you connect gaps between A moves with projectiles which you then dash/airdash in under and continue rushdown. For more info on individual characters, go here

Also I don't know how you have a problem inputting specials on a keyboard, its super easy (I started playing fighters on a keyboard and found other control systems much harder...)
Quarter circle is roll down=>right
DP is press right=>down=>right
