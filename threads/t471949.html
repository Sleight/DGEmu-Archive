CCat in the HatMay 12 2011, 09:22 PM
If this is just some home setup, then all you need to do is configure the router with something (laptop, desktop, whatever has an ethernet port and a screen) then it's done. Make sure router is hooked up to modem and walk away. If you get locked out of your router and you don't know the IP address/password of the router, you will have to hook something into it, but it doesn't matter what. Point is, after you configure it, it doesn't have to be hooked up to anything other than your modem.
