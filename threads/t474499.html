SshadowmanwkpNov 30 2011, 08:53 AM
DPs are download points, you accumulate 10 of them every day until you have 100 of them. You can also convert GPs, which you get by posting in forum sections that give you GPs and various other things. Your conversion rate is tied to your warn status, the more warning you have, the worse your conversion rate will be. There are also other ways to get DPs, like donating to the site. You can read all about it up here: http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?/topic/417167-forum-rules-and-guidelines/page__p__4329776#entry4329776 As for the quality question, you can indeed increase the video quality. Some emulators come with graphic filters, good examples are visual boy advance and DeSmuME. Graphic filters will enhance the picture quality, but some details might be dropped, because the technique ain't perfect (especially with older games like the ones for the original gameboy). Do note that it takes more processing power to use graphic filters, so if your computer can't handle the emulation, you shouldn't use graphic filters on top of that. By far the best of the filters is the hq#x filter, which makes the graphics much more smoother.
