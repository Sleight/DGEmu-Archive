Well somehow I get the feeling my topic description does not clarify my intentions for this topic, but rather mystifies it more. Essentially what I am interested in discussion here is, what should a country be based upon. There have been numerous theories as to what constitutes the best make up. Conservative Nationalism holds for instance that social stability can only be maintained (or is best maintained) if a country is based on a single culture. This draws from the view that differences in cultural views can lead to conflict which causes instability. Whereas Liberal Nationalism, holds that it is not the culture which maintains social stability but rather common rule of law. By this it is of little problem if there are people with different cultures living in the same country, so long as they all follow the same governing law. A few notes:1-While I recognize that there are numerous theories in regards to Multiculturalism societies (i.e cosmopolitan, Pluralist), I would rather this thread stuck to a central theme of whether or not a hegemonic culture is needed in order to maintain stability. Although by all means if you feel that such an argument can aid your stance in this discussion feel free to draw on it.2- I have constructed this thread drawing on political ideologies. This is why I have opted to use the term 'countires', so I could try and avoid confusion. Although naturally if you have reasoning from other fields which aid you point, I encourage you to draw on it.
