Anti-virus programs typically do not work well when more than one is present and operating. Pick one.Norton-good, solid program with good support.McAfee-Very very aggressive. I do not recommend it. Never get a virus program that acts without your input, you may loose files you want.AVG-haven't used it, but I've heard decent enough things about it. A program that always asks your permission before actingI'm sure someone who is more involved in network security can add more, but this should keep you safe as long as you download and surf the web intelligently.
