Wrestlemania XXX Review: Part I

 

Finally got around to watching Wrestlemania 30.  My first WM was WM12, that was...  Wow! I'm old, I'd say, but then I see wrestlers who were already old in 1996 are still wrestling today! Ok, so on to my extended WM30 review while learning how to make .webm files.

 
After some high production jaz-y intro(WM30 is probably WWE's biggest production ever) the show try to kick off with a nostalgia trigger, opening with Hulk Hogan coming into the ring with barely any reaction from the crowd, good thing they have a backup plan though, Austin walked in forgetting(lol) that the show is PG-13 now, the director improvise to save the day. The crowd chant "what" for a couple of minutes, and if that wasn't enough fanservice, The Rock walk into the ring, again, with a bigger reaction from the crowd than Hogan. Some more fanservice, they talk about the past 30 years of wrestlemania before they leave the ring to kick start the show.
 
 
Triple H against Daniel Bryan
 
First match of the night, and probably the most anticipated one, is fans favorite Daniel Bryan, who made a name for himself with his extraordinary in ring performance and the "yes" chant, against Triple H who became a main-eventer by marrying into the McMahons. The story here is that the Authority(HHH and wife Stephanie McMahon) don't think Bryan has what it takes to be the face of the company(ie champion) because he's short, not exactly muscular and straight up look like a hobo goat. Therefor, for the past few months they have been abusing their power to prevent him from becoming the WWE champion, but now that it's WM time, they have been forced to answer the fans demand(mostly by smark chants and boos), they're doing so with a twist though. HHH face Bryan and the winner goes to play in the main event match between (C)Randy Orton and (Royal Rumble winner)Batista for the WWE championship belt.
 
Triple H is announced by his milf wife(who continue to look better the older she get!) as he make his a glorified(Berserk themed?!) entrance. Fans go crazy for Bryan, who walk in with bandages around his arm, as if it was hurt, or something! Stephanie try to stare him to death but it doesn't work. The crowd really love doing this "Yes!" thing. The match start energetically, and that's carried over through most the match. Main theme here is Bryan dominating with Triple H getting cheap shots by concentrating on Bryan's hurt arm. Both wrestlers were doing a nice job trading moves, and Stephanie filled up the ringside role convincingly. Later on, HHH managed to land a pedigree, which Bryan kicked out of, He tried to do it again several time, but Bryan kep reversing/getting out of it. Triple H survived Bryan's submission moves few time before the match ending with Bryan's trademark knee strike after escaping a back drop.

 

Bryan celebrate his win, which means he will now take part in the main even for a title shot, and the crow is hyped as fuck about it. Stephanie just can't let it go, she jump into the ring to give Bryan some feminist slaps(I really hope this tuxedo top/short pants combo Stephanie is wearing catches on) while Triple H takes him by surprise from behind, drag him to the corner and give him a nice chair strike to break his arm even more!!! 

 

Overall it was a great match and an excellent start to the show, probably one of Triple H's best matches ever. How will Daniel Bryan overcome the odds, now with his arm more borken?! Stay tuned.
