If you're planning to build your own computer, I'd make a list of the parts you want (and also a few options for each of the parts). Then I'd wait for sales on certain parts (especially RAM, the case, fans, hard drives). The other parts come down in price due to newly released hardware (CPU, GPU, motherboard to a lesser extent). The only problem with buying the parts separately is if something is incompatible, you might be wasting more money than you save having to return an item, and buy one that works.Otherwise, if you're buying from a manufacturer, any time they have a sale is usually good. This route cuts down on the time you'll have to spend on research and shopping. Let me know if you're interested in building your own computer. I can provide a couple links to help you get started.
