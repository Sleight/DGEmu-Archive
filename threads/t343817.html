I've got a 40gb PS3 that I've had since March last year and I just got the YLOD yesterday. Spoke to Sony. Apparently what has happened is that due to the system overheating the seal around the CPU and/or GPU has cracked and separated from the main board. I have seen guys on Youtube fix it with a solder job, and Sony will fix it under warranty or may even replace it with a new console. If out of warranty, it'll cost you about $150US, but some stores offer a trade in on a dead console. EB Games in Australia offers $100AU for dead console trade-ins so I'll more than likely upgrade to the 120gb slim. But it is a rather annoying problem, especially when you don't have backups of any of your saves, despite having two PS3's in one house (mine and my brothers, his is only 3 months old, same 40gb model), and after resynching my trophies to his PS3, I've lost trophies!!! Trophies that I synced to the server maybe a week and a half before the YLOD, and now they're gone!!! I was pissed when I saw a stack of my trophies missing after resynching. I lost both of my platinums for Assassins Creed 2 and Borderlands, and am incredibly unhappy because I was 5 trophies away from the Bioshock Platinum (the 4 hard and survivor trophies plus platinum) and now I've lost all my progress on survivor mode.My new rule, and a suggestion to anyone else with a PS3 - back up your save games to an external USB device regularly and keep it safe, unfortunately games like Guitar Hero won't let you copy the save files, and synch your trophies regularly!!!! I reckon I've lost at least 20 trophies thanks to the YLOD.
