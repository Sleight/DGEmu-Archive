SShadowORIGINAL POSTJan 07 2011, 11:36 PM
http://www.stevetarzia.com/sonar/For laptops with a microphone. The speakers emit ultrasound which is then detected by the microphone. Based on how the ultrasound waves reflect off of whatever's in front of them and back to the microphone, it tries to determine whether or not you're at your computer and shuts the screen off if it thinks you're not there. Obviously, results will vary wildly between different quality speakers and mics. It keeps shutting the screen off when I'm at my computer, but that might be because the microphone on my laptop is really low-quality. Anyone else with a laptop want to try it out and see if it works well for them?
