A) What are the three broad types of unemployment? (easy)Frictional, structural and cyclical unemployment.B) Give a brief description as to what the cause and cost of each is? (hard)Frictional unemployment occurs when job seekers require time to search for new jobs, either because they have just entered the job market (just left school) or have just left their previous job. This type of unemployment is compatible with full employment, and is unavoidable. It doesn't impose much costs, and is not usually a concern of the government, except to ensure that there are sufficient jobs available in the economy to absorb these job seekers.Structural unemployment is caused when there is a mismatch between the skills of the job seeker and the skills needed for jobs with vaccancies. This type of unemployment is typically caused by the phenomenon of globalization; the rise of China and India has reduced the comparative advantages of many countries, hence causing many industries, such as manufacturing, to face a fall in demand in other countries. The most pressing problem with this type of unemployment is that it is a long term type of unemployment that requires reskilling and retraining of structurally unemployed workers in order for them to gain the necessary skills to find new jobs. It takes a substantial amount of time and funding, either from the government or companies, to carry out this retraining. New industries on the rise will also suffer as they will not be able to hire sufficient labour, due to the lack of skilled labour, causing insufficient economic growth of the indutries and the economy overall.Cyclical (demand-deficit) unemployment is caused by a lack of demand for goods and services in an industry / economy overall, leading to producers retrenching workers or not renewing employee contracts in order to cut costs of production. The increase in unemployment will cause the national income level of the country to fall, hence causing further falls in demand as disposable incomes decrease. This causes a drop in economic growth and overall standard of living in the country.Hope this answers the questions. =)
