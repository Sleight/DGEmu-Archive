Kidmojo, Here's something out of the FAQ to help you.
someone wrote:

1. If you have a pop-up blocker/anti script/firewall/proxy that doesn’t have DGEmu allowed then add DGEmu to it.
2. If you are using a download manager or internet accelerator then disable or uninstall it. This includes extensions like Downthemall, Flash Got, and Fasterfox.
3. If you are using Internet Explorer, a yellow bar or message may appear. Just click the link to start your download.
4. Do NOT pause your download. You won't be able to resume it afterwards.
5. Make sure that you have admin rights on your computer.
6. Try right clicking the download button and select "Save Target As" ("Save Link As" for Firefox)
7. Make sure that you have at least twice as much free space as the ROM that you are downloading. You need more than that if you are browsing or downloading other things. 8. If you get an HTML file (if you click your file and it takes you to an internet page) simply change the .html to .zip.
