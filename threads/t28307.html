MMr HamNov 03 2006, 10:39 AM
All the races are well balanced when it comes to speed, power and defence. While there is no ' Best Race', each race is, however, better at certain maps and scenarios. The Terran are very versatile and can settle very quickly into almost an area. They have the perfect balance of speed, power and defence. The Zerg are much easier to use, having only 3/4 of the number of buildings as the Protoss. This means they have acess to their full tech-tree extremely quickly. While they have a lot of speed, they lose in power and defence. Their defence is offset by the amount of defensive buildings they have. Having a whole base guided by Sunken and Spore colonies is extremely expensive and most units have very little HP. They make up for this in build speed. They can easily raise a large army in time to stop an incoming force, although most of the time, if they're up against a large force, they'll sustain large casualties. The Protoss are the exact opposite. They have the largest amount of buildings of any race. This means they must commit a lot of time and resources before their whole tech-tree is available. They make up for this in pure power. One Zealot can easily take on up to 4 zerglings, and with the right micro-management, even a small force of Zealots and Dragoons can cause heavy damage. Their defence is both high and low, compared to the circumstance. If the enemy has access to Science Vessels, even the largest of Protoss forces can easily be wiped out. Although, if the enemy doesn't have Science vessels, the shields can sway the tide of battle quite a lot. It all depeneds on what type of player you are.
