And now I will tell you why you are wrong..Yes, blowing into the DS works as a temporary solution, but it actually makes it worse over time. Eventually resulting in the buttons not working at all. Use a needle to clean out around them and inside them, and use compressed air after that on it.Next, clean all the dirt out of the other places where the buttons contact, and on the buttons themselves.Then reattach the spring and buttons... but don't put the DS back together just yet. Turn it on with the casing off to test your fix and to make sure you loaded the springs properly. If all is in working order...Proceed to put the DS back together, knowing it will never get any better than this without replacing the internal components.edit: Also, make sure to align your power switch and volume slider before snapping it all back in place.
