AAsheraMay 10 2012, 03:48 AM
Make sure the cooling pad fans work with your laptop's fan layout. If your laptop sucks in air from the bottom, the cooling pad should blow air into it, not away. And make sure the pad's fans line up with the laptop's fans and vents, or get a pad with risers so there's room between the laptop and the pad.

Something you might not have thought of is taking some veggies out of the freezer, put them in a ziplock bag and put that by either the fan intake or outflow. Even though the veggies are already sealed you put the ziploc around them because of condensation.

Another minor help is to open the bedroom door etc. so that there is air flow in what ever room your in. Using a big box fan to help with that works wonders. However if you do that you will HAVE to clean laptop insides sooner.
