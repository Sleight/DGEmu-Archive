Calvin Luther and the Deadly Doppelganger

CCalvin LutherORIGINAL POSTMar 19 2012, 02:25 AM
He woke to the cold and dark.The building had lost power over the night. He checked his cell phone, and saw that the network had gone down.He went to the west window of his apartment, peeking through the blinds. The sky was burnt. Everything had been bathed in red light. In the distance, he saw pillars of smoke. Across the street, in a window in the apartment building facing his, he saw something glint in the dying sunlight.The bullet shattered the window instantly. It sounded like a freight train as it pass over the man's shoulder. The blind smacked him on the mouth, and the glass lacerated uncovered skin and cut the t-shirt he'd worn to bed. He stood in shock, unable to comprehend what had happened. From across the street, he heard a swear, and metallic clicks. A basic understanding of the danger hit him, and he dove to the side. A second later, a bullet bored into the far wall.Outside, he heard a voice amplified by megaphone. "Tango on the fourth floor! Corner apartment! Move your asses!""Oh crap," the man said. He crawled to the side of the bed, where he'd left the duffel bag the night before, half-packed with clothes. He didn't have much to work with. The stores had already been looted of any heavy winter gear for days, and he'd always assumed that the problem would take care of itself."Get him! Blood for blood! Blood for blood!" the megaphone screeched.He threw on a pair of jeans and a sweater when the search team reached his door. He flinched as they began to smash at it, and began to pull his boots over his bare feet. He looked at the other window in his bedroom, facing north. It opened to the fire escape. He would be open, partially to the sniper to the west, assuming there were none on that side of the street. The fire escape led down to the alley, which had a dead end to the east and emptied into the street to the west. The wood of the door was beginning to crack. He didn't have time to come up with anything else."Tear you apart! Fuckin tear you apart!"He slipped on a jacket, and zipped the bag shut. He opened the northern window, flinching at the burst of cold air. He tried to remember how long the sniper took between shots. Maybe five seconds. Maybe more with a distraction.He threw the duffel bag through the open window. A shot. One-Mississippi. He vaulted over the window sill onto the metal grating. He turned towards the steep stairs. Two-Mississippi. Down a floor. The sniper was hanging out of the window to make the shot. Three-Mississippi. Turn, and slide down a floor. Four-Mississi--A bullet tore the air over his head. He ducked, slipped, fell. Over head, he heard his door crash to the floor."No escape! Tango in the alley! No escape! Blood for blood!"He launched himself over the handrail, and fell to the ground in a heap. He struggled to get up, reaching for the duffel bag next to him. A face appeared in the window above him. He scrambled, pumping his feet, head down, fighting down the pain."Tango's running! After him!"He ran hard, blood pounding and chest heaving. The sky was bright enough that his eyes couldn't adjust to the dark street. The sun cast dark shadows, the city's silhouette black against the red sky. The sound of the search team had fallen far away. He stopped, panting, staring back to where he had come.He'd lost them.
