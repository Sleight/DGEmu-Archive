NnatzorOct 22 2008, 07:57 PM
I killed the skeleton using only the knives: Hang on to the left node when you're shooting him. once he gets close, shoot yourself across to the right, he'll go backwards and when he's under you, shoot yourself to the middle. Attack him, doing the dodge (L) every so often until you're to the far left again. Hang on the left node, and repeat.The crab is was quite hard. I used only lightning to beat him. At level 12(?) you should be able to pull off four attacks in a row. Get full hearts, and on his last mode (where you only have one node to hang from, I think, and two platforms on the left) use your lightning special move (while standing in the top-left). For the rest, just figure out his attack pattern (it repeats) and learn how to dodge by jumping/falling between the two platforms. Took me 6-7 tries to get beat this guy.Anyways, very nice game. It strayed from the previous ones, but I find this one has a nice twist to the attack system, though at some times the levels/bosses are quite unforgiving.
