asoundmind' post='2121439 wrote on Jan 30 2007, 01:02 AM:
I don't understand why Square is being experimental to their Final Fantasy since FFX-2. It's like their trademark or something to make us, loyal fans as guinea pig or something >.<
That goes with about any game company. Just look back at some other series and you'll see much of the same thing.Legend of Zelda - Experimented with games like Zelda 2 where the gameplay was completely different. They still made a great game but it wasn't received so well in the end, so they returned to the old formula.Super Mario - Looks at all the spin offs we see today. This is because Nintendo established a good base character and tried different formulas with their games. In the end, majority of them are succesful and so they keep experimenting to see what people will like.Final Fantasy has been doing much of the same thing. Their biggest problem is that they change characters each time, so they don't really have a base character to work off like Nintendo did. This is why they tried to milk Final Fantasy VII, due to the well received characters it had. There's also games like Chocobo Racing that were done to also use some common monsters/creatures to experiment. It's hard to change everything completely with a game, since people are coming into something new each time. This is what happened with games like FFXI and FFXII, as opposed to what Nintendo did with Mario. People identified with Mario and even though the gameplay was different, they still enjoyed having a familiar face in the game. You'll also see that Dirge of Cerberus was a decent seller since people liked Vincent, despite the fact the gameplay was completely different from any other Final Fantasy game. It's not uncommon for series to do this, several more could be listed, but you'll see that Square-Enix is playing with two things at once. By changing gameplay, story, and characters all at once they are resetting that familiarity process just like when people popped in Final Fantasy I. Not only does this effect the gamer but also the fact that the company has to do that much more extra work on the game and can mess up that many more things.
