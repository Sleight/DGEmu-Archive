Introduction

No-Intro Preservation Society is a group of people that gathered to create DAT files to be used with ROM managers. Their goal is to collect ROM files of different video games in order to preserve them as closely to their original state as possible. Basically, these collections of ROM files do not contain any hacks, bad dumps, etc, only clean accurate ROM files. When this society was born, its initial goal was to remove the intros of pirate groups that were added to the Game Boy Advance ROM files. This is the origin of the name "No-Intro". You can find out more about them here: http://www.no-intro.org/

 

Why No-Intro?

As many of you may be familiar on the scene, other popular sets such as "Goodsets" exist. The focus of each standard is different: "Goodsets" tries to collect as many known ROM dumps as possible into one set, meaning all the hacks, bad dumps, over dumps, etc. are gathered together into it. The "No-Intro", however, collects only a known good ROM from each region or revision of a game, thus being much more faithful to the original games.

 

About This Set
