OK.. Here's the deal....At first you will definitely play offline games/single players. That is the usual learning curve. RPGs, Action, Adventure, you name it! Then multiplayer will be introduced to you, and gaming will never be the same. Lastly, you will engage in ONLINE games that will revolutionize your gaming experience. Those three are very separate experiences. The first one gives you the "inner gaming peace" as I would like to call it that is required in some games such as the epic award-winning RPG Final Fantasy Series! The other two kinda plays with your "social gaming needs" which is healthy. You don't wanna grow up being the guy on the corner of the classroom but you also don't wanna be the super-geekisque type who beats the hell out of everyone.... in your own virtual world.To answer your question, those three aforementioned gaming options give different authentic experiences. So there is no best way.
