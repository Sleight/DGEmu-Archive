I've been on this introvert/extrovert kick lately, possibly because of the inevitable onset of spring. I started reading this book that talks about how introversion isn't supposed to be a bad word, and a random conversation on a blog post got me interested in this anime, "Kuragehime", in which an extrovert infiltrates a home of introverts. It kind of reminds me of "Tsuritama", not because of the prominence of marine life but because the latter featured an alien who got two different types of introverts (one easily overwhelmed by outer stimulation, the other interested more in a solitary hobby than in the company of others) to open up and be a little more outgoing.

 

So anyway, I'm interested if anyone knows of a series with similar themes. Something slice-of-lifey would be best, as long as it has some kind of driving narrative (not just one-offs like "Lucky Star", for example). If it's quirky or has supernatural/sci-fi/fantasy elements that are not the main focus, all the better. Also, comedy is a must. I haven't been keeping up with the medium, so anything fairly recent (preferably already completed) or under-the-radar is ideal.

 

Thanks!

Man you have some weird tastes...

But quirky is a genre too

 

Mawaru Penguindrum - quirky galore, with an introvert/extrovert MC pair, also tonz of dramazzz and utterly convoluted 2deep4u story

Denpa Onna to Seishun Otoko - introvert FeMC, also aliens. Haven't seen much of this, heard it was decent

Boku wa Tomodachi ga Sukunai (Haganai) - bunch of friendless people make a club for finding friends.  Comedy-harem and fanservicey...also one of the few SoLs I find actually funny.  Sidenote, its currently the best selling light novel series

Steins;Gate - quirky and sciency characters, humurous character interactions, boss MC.  Also great story about timetravel hijinks (starts comedic, eventually gets totally srs), AotY 2012

Sakurasou no Pet na Kanojo - introvert FeMC, comedy-harem. Heard this was good, haven't watched it yet

Chuunibyou demo Koi ga Shitai (Chuu2) - introvert cast if you consider chuunibyou (google it) a type of social skills deficiency.  Standard KyoAni fare, pretty decent 

Kotoura-san - introvert FeMC who can reads minds and gets alienated by (mostly) everyone.  Currently airing

Hyouka - doesn't exactly satisfy all your requirements, but is one of the best in the genre of "make a club, SoL adventures", this one is about mystery solving and has the typical introvert=>warming-up growth of MC.  One of my favorites, highly recommended 

AnoHana

Kami Nomi zo Shiru Sekai
