Righty ho people, I'm afraid Ocarina of Time wins this one hands down.First, we need to look through the history of the Zelda series to understand why. Now are you sitting comfortably?Ocarina of Time is considered by many and most the Jewel in the Nintendo 64's crown. As many believe that Mario 64 was the first 3D platformer to arrive in the mainstream, it was good old OoT which perfected the 3D formula. For the first time, a Full expansive 3D world was available, and it indoctrinated a new wave of gamers.Nintendo made a smart decision at this point. realizing the sheer acclaim and success the big 'N' decided not to follow OoT with more of the same. So they created Majora's Mask. The wonderfully surreal fight against time. Realize at this point even Nintendo didn't think they could surpass Ocarina just Yet.And so then we have the dawn of the Gamecube, and with it, a new Zelda to boot. The Wind Waker. The Wind Waker was (comparatively) a much easier game than it's forefathers. It also showed a slight problem, a worrying indicator as for what is to come. The "Developer Override"Nintendo was hard cut for deadlines, and the Wind Waker was nowhere near as complete as they wanted it to be. But rather than give them time to complete Wind Waker, thus allowing the gamers the best experience, they created a fatal flaw...The Triforce HuntThe Triforce Hunt wasn't initially intended. And wasn't (however amusing to outsiders) any fun. A grueling test of determination and sheer RPG level grinding for cash. It remains one of Zelda's darkest hours, and one of Nintendo's as well. It was the start of the Developer Override.So...Zoom on to Twilight Princess. Nintendo has long got used to letting game quality suffer for deadlines. And then they decide they could surpass their own masterpiece. But several mistakes were made.They decided halfway through to port to the Wii, leaving TP an ugly half-caste, with better than GC graphics but Hugely Sub Wii ones.They still however, had the development of the TP continue, leading to the coders being majorly overstretched.As is the fashion, the coders had to meet unrealistic deadlines, meaning they had to cut corners.The main problem was none of these those, but a much more Damning Fate (tm). It was the ease of play which killed TP. It took the OoT formula and streamlined it, making it in effect just another game. Missing the intricacies which used to define the Nintendo product. Twilight Princess is not only the same game, but half the game of Ocarina of Time as well.Read well my friends and learn that just because a game in new, it doesn't make it better. Just because it's hyped, it doesn't guarantee greatness and finally.Play the Game before making an Opinion
