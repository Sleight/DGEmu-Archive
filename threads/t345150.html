My main reason for choosing Suikoden II was that it has more choices you can make that actually affect the storyline. It also has more minigames like the cooking contest and rope-climbing game. The appearances of many characters from the old game make this game great because it actually links the first one to this one. It also means you get some very respectable characters on your team because you know what they accomplished in the first game together if you already played it.The graphics are more polished, you can run with O at any time making traveling easier, and you can even find a few extra characters that are not required to get the 108 stars. Gameshark makes it even cooler because you can actually get Jowy back on your team permanently with that device. Suikoden 1 doesn't let you do that with Ted and Odessa. The only disappointing thing about the game is that a couple characters cannot be brought back from the dead like in the first one unless you use Gameshark.This game also has more alternate character choices and using Gameshark can let you have all of them at once.The only thing that I like less in this game is that you don't have infinite storage in the warehouse like in Suikoden 1. That makes it harder to play this game trying to collect all the items you find in chests and in battles. This is one of those games where you have to keep only what you need and sell what you don't need.
