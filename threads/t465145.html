You can force install it manually if you have the driver installer unpacked into the NVidia folder in C:\.First go into safe mode, go to device manager, and then remove any devices that fall under the unknown section and remove the current display adapter under the relevant entry for your display adapter.Now reboot, and when the add new hardware wizard, you can choose the manual type options (or try for the automatic options again), and then when you get to select the device manually from a list, pick the "Have Disk" button. Browse to where the drivers are within the NVidia folder and then click OK and browse the list for your card model. You will need to ignore the warnings that tell you that the device may not be compatible and other crap. Be SURE it's the right video card model you select.IF the wizard doesn't come up, go to device manager and add the hardware manually.Either way, it's possible to force feed a driver into Windows.
