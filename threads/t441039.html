So, I was thinking about a way to make the old idea of having free ROMs by using offsite links AND keeping our sad small userbase with us work, and I think I've finally come up with a decent idea.First of all, I think the main problem was a lack of enough man power. Having a small group of uploaders gets inefficient quickly, especially when we try to tackle the big ROMs, so, I thought of a system where we can encourage the average member to help us with it all. Here's my plan:We could encourage members to upload ROMs by rewarding them with juicy, big DPs rewards. They'd have to upload ROMs from DGemu ONLY, requiring them to keep all the files that came with the RAR (It'd include the DGemu.htm file) and their name intact (With the Dgemu.com prefix ;D), this would prevent abuse of the system and even if other sites stole our links, it'd be cheap publicity for us. So, how "Big" would these rewards be? I'm thinking of a complete DPs refund + a 20% bonus. The bigger the upload, the bigger the reward. This may seem like too much of a reward, but think about it, we'd be saving a lot of bandwidth from the people that would be downloading from the offsite links instead, so, with two guys choosing to download from the offsite link instead of downloading the file directly from DGemu, we'd already have justified the big DPs reward we gave to the uploader. So, even if someone decides to go on a "Download, upload, get reward, upload, download..." spree, we still win. :D The subforum would need topics to be approved first, and us mods would be in charge of downloading and checking that everything is in order, then approving the topic and listing the topic in an index topic for easy access, then the sents/admins/Bish would take care of rewarding the members. We could also have the uploading team with access to a premium account, if the idea takes off, it'd be nice if it could be funded by the site's donations pool, and we could invite any member that contributes often (Hopefully, with such a good reward, there would be plenty of them) so their uploads don't run the risk of getting deleted.Oh, and we obviously could use the few working links from Mono's playground to get us started. Well, that was long winded, and now that I look at it, it's not much different from the original idea, so let me resume it all: The key aspect of this is that we wouldn't have an uploading team "working" on the project, this would be a way of making members that just spent all their DPs in a game contribute to the site AND making it easier for them to get more ROMs from the site.
