I think it's admirable that you want to encourage people to post on the forums, but I'm not sure if offering GPs for every post (on the correct boards) is the best way to go about it. Most of the posts end up being one-liners or less. For example, on a "favorite game" thread, people list their favorite games with no explanation.I know that the moderators have the ability to deduct GPs for junk posts, but with a site that gets as much traffic as DGEmu.com, they can't possibly get to them all. Perhaps it would be more effective not to give automatic GPs for posting but to allow the moderators to use their discretion to award points for the best posts. (Empirical studies have demonstrated that intermittent reinforcement is more effective than constant reinforcement. Hence the popularity of lotteries and other games where the odds of winning are very low.) There would still be an incentive to post, but there would no longer be an incentive for hundreds of users to litter the boards with posts like "ff7 wuz the bset game ever!!!!!!!"Your ROM coverage is excellent.
