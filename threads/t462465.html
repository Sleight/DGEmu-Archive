They must have fallen out... what's going on out there?”“I don't know.”“Why are these pictures so important?”“I don't... it's complicated.”“What's on the other side of that door?”“Something we don't want to meet.”“So... what do we do?”“We get the hell out of this room. Yet.”“What do you mean?”“I'm... Just wait until later. I'll explain then. What we're going to do is this. It's going to knock down that door. When it does that, I smash through the mirror. Where's your desk?”“It's down the hall, up the stairs on the right two floors, a left and down the hall.”“Okay. We go through that mirror, we book it. You lead. How are you loaded?”“I don't-”“Gun.
