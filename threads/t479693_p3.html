DDaft PunkOct 25 2013, 07:16 AM

Post game spot for catching Pokemans. Each friend on your 3DS is assigned a type and will have 2 or 3 Pokemon of that type in their safari, it looks like it will assign any friend a type and safari even if they don't play Pokemon, but they'll only have 2 available to catch. If the friend does play and has beaten the champion and been online at the same time as you since it'll unlock the 3rd 'mon. Basically you talk to the reception lady, pick the safari you want to go on and she'll send you out in to a small patch of grass where you can only encounter the 2 or 3 Pokemon that have been assigned. The neat thing about it is any Pokemon you catch in here is guaranteed to have at least two max IVs, Pokemon may have their Dreamworld/hidden ability and the shiny encounter rate is (apparently) higher than normal. There are also some Pokemon you can only catch at the Safari at the moment.
