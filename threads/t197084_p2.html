WwymoreAug 28 2009, 09:54 PM
It appears from a lot of the posts here that people don't understand the Wiiconnect 24 concept. If you have your Wii set to Standby, then when you turn it off the light on the power button will be orange not red. It is not completely off and will continue to receive stuff online for the news and weather channels as well as certain games like Animal Crossing. Unfortunately, it has a tendency to overheat in this state and damage the GPU. I found this out after a couple of months when I started seeing black pixels flickering on the screen during certain games. It is therefore best to turn the Standby mode off where the power button is red not orange.
