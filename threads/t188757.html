This guide is intended for all Support Staff (SS). It covers the description of the current work for SS as well as new missions. Please read carefully so as to avoid making mistakes.1. Primary Mission: Answering Emailsa. BasicsAddress: http://webmail.dgemu.com/Login: supportPassword: dR@g0nzOnce you get in the mailbox, click on 'Mailbox" and a list of unanswered emails should be displayed. Remember to check the mailbox AT LEAST once a day: don't let a mail stay unanswered more than 24 hours!b. Answer FormatThe general attitude when replying an email is to be as proper and polite as possible. Remember that you are speaking in the name of Dgemu and therefore, you should NEVER insult or make fun of the person who sent the email. When a member begins to rant and insult you, just ignore it. Do not reply to provocation.Suggested format for a reply:
someone wrote:
Dear Member,[Content of the email]RegardsDGEmu Support
c. Common Questions and AnswersFor all question covered by the site FAQ: redirect them to the site FAQ (So read it first)For all question covered by the "Support and Suggestion" FAQ: redirect them to the SS FAQ (So read it aslo)The member forgot his/her username: retrieve it here by using his/her email addressThe member wishes to change his/her email address: do it thereThe member forgot his/her password tell him/her to go on the login page and retrieve it by himself/herself.The member cannot validate himself/herself: after verifying that it is not a dupe account, do it for him/her hereFor ROM problems: if you know the solution, answer it directly, otherwise, either leave it to other SS or suggest the member to post on the forum after making a search for similar topics.For donation questions: if you know the solution, answer. Otherwise PM PlaySwiss.Other common questions can be found here.These points covered the primary job of an SS and should be followed by ALL SS without exception.2. Moderating SupportSince SS are the most likely to become the baby moderators in the future, you should begin to support the moderation of this forum (if you are not doing this already). Here is how you can help:Report rule breaking posts.Find the dupe threads on the forum and post the link to the original topic.Provide technical support to members on the forum.Report mass spammers and dupe accounts for banning.When replying in a dupe/pointless thread, don't hesitate to put something like:- Don't reply after me or you will be warned- If you are not the topic starter or a staff member, don't reply or warns will be issued.3. Othersa. Inspecting ReviewsAs part of the staff, SS have check the Review Center regularly to decide whether the reviews submitted by members are worth being accepted. The Review Center is here.b. Welcoming MembersSince members that take time to introduce themselves are generally willing to become part of this community, it is a really good idea to answer their good will. The rule of thumb is to ensure every thread has at least one welcoming reply. When doing so, do not forget to remind them to read and follow the rules (providing a link would be helpful too).4. Support Staff PowersThrough the descriptions of your job above, you can already see all your powers. Here is the summary list of all what you can use. Retrieve Someone's Username by Inputting their Email or their email by entering their user ID.Change Someone's Email Address.Validate Members Manually.Check for dupe accounts through users' IPsValidate Reviews.To access your powers, click here.Thanks for your attentionDGEmu AdministrationCredits: Ody, Molh, Dragons Master, qcarachato, DJ Night Force 9
