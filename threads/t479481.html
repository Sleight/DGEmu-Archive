I play mainly old school games so it's nice to have a RELIABLE ROM source (somewhat of a rarity these days).

 

The point of this is that even though there's some downsides to DG, it's one of the most reliable and safest sites to get older games from and I really appreciate the effort made my Dragon's Master and the upper class staff members (Not me, I don't do crap.
