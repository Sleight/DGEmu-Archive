Kkuser77Jul 01 2010, 01:23 AM
It all depends on wether or not you've seen the original material.If you have nothing to base it on and are viewing the material first through the movie, then they seem original and unique.However, if you've seen the material in its original format then it becomes more difficult to enjoy.Liberties are the changes in the original story that the director took to make it more interesting. These liberties usually upset hardcore fans, and pleasingly suprise everyone else. However, there are some liberties that everyone likes, such as Throne of Blood, liberties that everyone dislikes, like the ending of Watchmen, and those that don't really matter, such as those in the Series of Unfortunate Events movie. I'll now break down into the specifics.Comics: Kickass, Spiderman, Watchmen: A few major liberties are always taken, but usually faithfully recreates them. Very appealing.Video Game: Super Mario Brothers, Advent Children, Doom: Many liberties are taken, their faithfulness and appeal vary. (I recomend watching Doom)Anime: Cartoon- Digimon, Pokemon: If they're canon then they are usually good. Be wary of noncanon, there's a better chance that they'll suck. Live Action- Deathnote, Dragonball: Few/ no liberties. Vary in appeal (I recomend Deathnote)TV series: read anime, it's usually the same type of thing.
