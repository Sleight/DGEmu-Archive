KKirie MotobaDec 14 2008, 07:10 AM
The Blaster360 is basically an external switch to the 360 that allows you to easily re-flash your DVD drive firmware without hassle.Basically, as long as you know how to tear down a 360, replug a few cables, and put everything back up, that's all you need to install it. No soldering needed.The Blaster 360 is basically stealth, when you switch it to 360 mode, it's basically not seen by the system, so you can play on live. Just be aware that playing copied games on live might be risky to getting your system banned.Just keep in mind, the Blaster360 is not a tool for you to expand the storage on a 360.

KKirie MotobaDec 18 2008, 06:37 AM
you can get ISOs online without a problem. Just bare in mind that all 360 games will only fit on dual layer DVDs, so be ready to stash out a bit more for blank DVD+R DL discs without hopefully making bad burns.Usually, a 360 ISO comes with 2 files, a .dvd file and a .iso file. Very important that you use the .dvd file to copy as it tells the burning program how to format ur data to be read by the 360. If not, you'll end up having an unusable game.As for the account, it doesn't matter. Microsoft usually only check people playing on Live for whether its a copy or not. As long as either your game copy is patched for stealth, or you just stay away from live with the burnt copies, it's fine.
