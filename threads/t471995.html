CCat in the HatMay 16 2011, 11:53 PM
Technically speaking, long division of polynomials is the same thing, and it is used to find oblique asymptotes or rational zeroes.As for straight up integer division, it's totally useful in measurements. It's just that because of the gradations on the measuring instruments we use today, the math is already done for us. I worked at a paper mill where people definitely couldn't use a tape measurer though.Oh, and integer division is extremely important on modular arithmetic used in kryptoanalysis and kryptography. The remainder is the only part that even matters. If you use numbers modulo 25 you can translate any sequence of numbers into letter equivalents, or you can use a different modulo and get ASCII values. Binary is also a form of integer division in which you have a number modulo 2 to give a series of 1's and 0's. Also, non-military time, as in 13:00 = 1:00, is integer division modulo 12.It's all over the place.
