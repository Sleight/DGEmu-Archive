This thread is going to be used to express my goals for this forum.Myself and the rest of the staff at DGEMU.COM, like to see this site as a community. I intend to host semi regular official tournaments which will have various potential rewards depending upon what the winners want. I am fairly open minded when it comes to potential rewards. In order to help in this massive endeavor I will be looking for a group of members to help me run official tournaments as well as facilitate members in any that they wish to run themselves. All non staff organized tournaments will be considered unofficial tournaments and will be done purely for fun.If there is a game which you would like to see us host a tournament for don't be afraid to make a thread asking for it. If we have enough of a demand then I will see what I can do to organize a tournament. The big thing to remember is that all of this is for you the members. It is my goal and my desire to see everyone get to know each other better and possibly form friendships which can transcend a forum. General Guidelines for tournaments-Do not exploit glitches in games.-Please show the other people participating in the tournament respect. (Essentially what I mean by this is don't whine or name call if someone else beats you)-Just have fun.That is it for my guidelines. The guideline section of this thread might be amended at anytime once I get a better picture of what our tournaments will be like.Note-I am still looking for someone to help me with Wii and PS3 tournaments, if you might be interested please send me a pm.
