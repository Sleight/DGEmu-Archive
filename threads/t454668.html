I really tried to like this game, especially with some of the major battle scenes once you hit disc 2, but several decisive factors condemned it for me. I'm not a fan of major war battles that can take upwards to an hour even if you're over leveled (well actually there's no base levels in the game, but a general one after each battle) and then facing a very challenging boss, only to die and have to begin all over again. It's got great music and great characters, but an amazingly bad story with zero plot twists. I'm sorry, but I can't recommend this. There's other reasons why, but it's better to find out for yourself. If you do enjoy it, then by all means, praise it, because I really tried and perhaps unconsciously still DO try to enjoy it :gbasp:
