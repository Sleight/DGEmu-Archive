SShidou MariyaDec 03 2008, 07:47 AM
A laptop cooler would be simply pushing some air onto the case of the 360, which will have little to no effect on it.What you could use though, is a cooler that you install to the back side of the 360 to aid pull out some hot air from the back (and believe me, it helps alot)Careful though, there's 2 different types of those: 1 that uses the AC to power it, the other uses 1 USB port to power it. Take the USB version, as the AC version has had multiple horrendous stories about them damaging the 360 permanently.

SShidou MariyaDec 05 2008, 05:07 AM
Ya, whether you choose a model to takes electricity from the wall outlet or the USB port, it's ok, as long as its power source is not the same AC outlet as the 360 intakes.The USB ports provided on the 360 are there to provide power for USB peripherals, and a fan plugged onto USB will never consume enough power to short circuit the port as many rumors have been passing around.I have 2 coolers installed on my 360. One pushing air in from the bottom of my 360, and one pulling air out from the back (My 360 is standing)And all I can say is that the one pulling air out from the back is much more efficient than the one pushing air in from the bottom.
