SshadowmanwkpNov 29 2012, 07:02 AM
The one flashcart that blows others out of the water would be the supercard DSTWO. It has consistent updates, an on-board cpu, to do stuff like play gba games without accessories and some other features. It is very expensive, about $40,- or so, but if money ain't an issue, this is the best flashcart money can buy.

Little note: Paypal has been blocking paypal accounts of flashcart retailers, thus locking out a safe payment method. Please use great care when ordering flashcarts online.

You can also look into patching your games if you aren't keen on spending your money. A very user friendly tool is retrogamefan's rom tool which can be downloaded here: http://filetrip.net/nds-downloads/rom-hacks/download-retrogamefan-ds-scene-rom-tool-v10-build-1205-f25536.html

Keep in mind that the tool only accepts clean roms, this means that you can't use a patched, trimmed, or otherwise modified rom.
