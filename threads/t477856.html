Heya!!

Hmm, usually, to load games off the HD, you will need the HD loader. I personally would recommend just sticking with copies of games, as the compatibility of games with the HD Loader isn't at 100%, though it works for most. The biggest advantage of putting games on the HD Loader is that your games will load up much faster than on DVD. The great downfall is that it's not compatible with PS1 games, as your modchip should be able to load all of that without issues.

The modchip and hard disk are seperate entities, they will not affect each other negatively. Same concept as my Wii that has a Wiikey and a USB loader for my games.

Hope that helps.
