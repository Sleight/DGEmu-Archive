Mononon' post='4870823 wrote on Sep 27 2010, 12:35 AM:

unless it was REALLY tiny that would be several thousand dollars.Titanium should work nicely and be rather durable. Or you could make your own mold and cast one from melted aluminum cans. Make the shape you want out of balsa wood or something else cheap and easy to work with. Make a plaster cast of it with a paper divider all the way around it one way so that when the plaster hardens you have a mold that is in two pieces and you can take it off the form you made a cast of. Drill a small hole in the bottom of your new mold. Then melt some aluminum and pour it in using a funnel (obviously not a plastic one since it would melt before you were done) and wait for it to cool. Either pull the mold apart or just break it if you don't want to make another one. Use a grinder to remove any extra metal on the bottom where the hole in the mold was and then polish it.
