I really like OpenArena. It's a free version of Quake 3 Arena (since the game was released under GPL by ID software). In order to comply with the GPL license of Quake 3 Arena, the graphics were slightly changed (mostly player skins, really). However, all the weapons and levels are intact, allowing classic Quake 3 online play for no money at all. Also, there are some pretty epic mods for this game (for instance, a gigantic kitchen, and all the players are about the size of a mouse).Another great online fragfest is Skulltag, which takes the already epic multiplayer of Doom, and makes it even better, with team-based games, and new deathmatch modes (Like instagib, where attacks cause other players to blow up, or "Gib"). This requires you to buy/pirate Doom or Doom II, but it's worth itI also played the Halo: Combat Evolved demo a lot. Even though I could only play on Blood Gulch, online Halo was a hoot (mind you, this was before I got an XBOX live gold account). Even at that, I almost want to buy the full game, because some mods of this game look incredible
