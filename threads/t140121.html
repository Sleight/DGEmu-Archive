Link goes to rescue Sages and awaken their power. Link goes after Ganon and epic fight ensues with Master Sword and some magical arrows he found.OoT's story was merely a rehashing of ALttP, made to resemble the backstory to ALttP. It was nothing new, save for the fact that in OoT you actually felt for the people who were being oppressed.In MM, however, you can feel more for the people you're fighting for. You get to know them better. This game gives you a better sense of how much the people are suffering.MM is much better than OoT. I wish that the limit was shorter, myself.
