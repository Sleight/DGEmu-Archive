DPs are used to download these.This is however no argument to prefer DPs over GPs, because, as everybody else here in this thread has mentioned, GPs can be converted into DPs. I will try to put this argument down by saying that DPs is a direct currency to downloading games, while GPs is an indirect currency. Because people are looking for a way to directly download more roms, having DPs as a reward will lure more newcomers to the debating forums than GPs. I will try to illustrate this, because it sounds quite silly. I mean, with GPs you can do so much more, can't you? Well the bigger part of consumers are looking for direct ways to consume. I will take the rise in buying games through the Iphone store or through Steam as an example. This is one of the purest forms of direct consuming: you want a game or mp3? Fill in your credit card number or use your pay pall account, accept the terms of agreement, et voila: you can download the game/mp3 and have immediate acces to it.Indirect consuming is having to go to a store to buy your music or games or having bol.com or ebay.com to ship the game before you have acces to it. The same mechanism is seen with DPs and GPs. DPs is direct consuming: you won't have to convert the points you win by spending hours on the forums so you can download more. Having DPs as a currency will attract more people to contribute to the Debating forum. Some will say that having to make posts on a debating forum to get more DPs is just as much work as converting GPs. This is not completely true. You see, with converting there are a couple of things a user has to be aware of. Users who spend a lot on the forums and who obtain a good reputation have a lower conversion rate than newcomers. A lot of time and effort is required to get a good reputation. The work you have to do to make full use of your GPs is way more than when you get DPs. To make my point short, having DPs as the reward currency will attract newcomers more to the Debating forums than having GPs as the reward currency.
