I understand why, but I very rarely connect to characters in an RPG because they're not my characters. D&D's Eye of the Beholder series was one of very few game series that embodied an effective dungeon crawl feeling while also making the characters yours. I want something else from Final Fantasy 6. I want to see Squall rock the Leonheart again. But, I know I won't, because it's FF. And part of my problem is putting such time into a game, 40+ hours, and what do I get in return? Heartache. Questions without answers. In any good strategy game, the end of the campaign is simple: You win. You rule the world/galaxy/universe/etc. That's why I don't like RPGs as much anymore.
