Try copying this line into game150.txtms0:/seplugins/FuSa_SD.prx 1(If game150.txt doesn't exist, Create it and paste in the line). A bit more about it for if you feel the need to read:'ms0:/seplugins/FuSa_SD.prx' gives the path of the plugin and the following 1 indicates it is enabled ( So 0 obviously means disabled ). vsh.txt is for the plugins you want enabled in your XMB.game.txt is for your ISOs and maybe the newer homebrew apps.game150.txt is for your homebrewpops.txt is for your PSX games.
