Parallel Universes / Multiverses. The idea is much like The Time Machine where the guy's girlfriend dies and he goes back in time trying to save her, thats the spoiler free synopsis of the film. I have the same thing happen, but I have my reasons why travelling back in time to change something doesn't work, so rather than time travel, travel to a different universe to a universe where she didn't die. But then there's the moral question, there would be another you, and could you kill yourself to take your own place =P I think its rather interesting.But theres a conversation in my story that constantly rattles through my head when the main character is trying to find a way to save his wife. The scientist explains the impossibility of changing the past, and presents the multiverse theory. And the way I tell it in my story idea is that every decision, every action and not just of humans, but animals and insects, etc. creates a new universe. To simplify, say for example I flip a coin to decide what movie to watch. In this universe I watch 300, but that decision spawned another universe where the result of the flip was different and I watch Transformers. That's my take on the multiverse theory, but the only question I have is, how is it possible for these multiverses to exist? Or even for that matter, a single parallel universe?Everything in our universe is made of matter. We take up physical space, so how can two universes occupy the same space? One might think that the universes don't sit atop one another, but rather side by side, but in that idea imagine you had a spaceship that could travel from one end of the universe to the other within an hour, if the parallel universes sat side by side, wouldn't you eventually pass through into the next universe? I think not. I think that for parallel universes to exist they would have to sit on top of one another and as stated above, how would you pass through into another.I disagree with the black hole theory, considering that a black hole is essentially a star, but the gravity has become so intense that light cannot escape, by trying to pass through a black hole you would not find something on the other side, you would probably be crushed and your body condensed to the size of an atom.My theory, for which will be used in my story, is about tachyon theory. A particle that can be created by nuclear explosions and can travel faster than the speed of light. The theory about tachyons is that (using telephones as an example) is the telephone signal moves at or slower than the speed of light, then it is obvious that the sent message indeed occured before the receiver heard it. But, if the signal moved faster than the speed of light, it is possible that the receiver could hear the message before it was transmitted. The idea came to mind after seeing Watchmen, where Dr. Manhattan describes tachyons as particles that travel backwards through what we see as time.So if tachyons can move through time and space, imagine building a machine, much like a fax machine for humans, but rather than being broken down into electrons, protons and neutrons, your entire body would have to be broken down into tachyons for you to be able to move faster than light through time and space. This is completely theory, and I admit that I'm not that smart, but if tachyons can move through time and space then what stops them from traversing multiverses?

Well, the theory of parallel universes or the 'multiverse' as put forth by Everett is not just science fiction, but is currently a theory which has quite a legion of physicists in support of it. This is because it purportedly helps to explain away some quantum phenomenon without resulting to (wavefunction) collapse and whatnot. There have been many thought experiments proposed to explore the possible existence of these parallel universes, and while many are still not possible to be performed satisfactorily in reality and may perhaps be rather speculative in nature, perhaps someday they will be refined. The book Schrodinger's Rabbits explores the historical background and explains the multiverse theory in a rather satisfactory manner for the layman.Feynman actually proposed that antimatter is simply ordinary matter travelling backwards in time. Speculation about tachyons is unfortunately not very feasible nor likely for nothing of that sort has been detected and such matter would violate the principle of relativity.
