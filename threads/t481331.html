KKirie MotobaSep 22 2014, 03:00 AM

You wouldn't dare do a crossfire setup in that HP though:

- HP motherboards are more than often customized and dumbed down Asus motherboards with proprietary bios, limiting functions.

- HP usually uses the minimum and cheapest China made HP branded power supplies possible. Those things usually don't cope well with demanding video cards.

- HP towers are not meant for efficient cooling. Most cases, you can end up overheating the inside of your case.

 

Also, to do a crossfire, you need to check with which video card it can cross-up. It gives some games a boost, but not that much.

Most important it: APU processors uses your system RAM to feed your video: Focus on better speed RAM to gain the most. Adding a video card is secondary, and not necessary.

 

That's mainly why I chose my A10: Cheaper option than buying another AMD processor and a dedicated video card, more power efficient, more quiet. All important for a nice HTPC.
