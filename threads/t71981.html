SShadow WolfOct 02 2005, 01:50 PM
Wow... this place really does lack Suikoden information. Well being a devoted fan and well informed in regards to the series, I'll try and keep everyone here as informed as possible.For starters the three characters on the poster are The Hero (bottom-left), who is the eldest son of Queen Arshetat Falanas and Queen’s Knight Commander Felid. His weapon is in fact the Falenean national treasure called "nagamaki" and is actually a single rod (like Tir McDohl or Jowy Atredies/Blight), but it apparently splits into a three piece rod like Nanami's. You can see this in the trailer. Oh and it is a guy and he is not gay... or at least we've received no such information from Konami yet. XPRion (bottom-right) is the hero's bodyguard and apparently a very talkative and loyal person. She'll probably account for a lot of the character development. An interesting fact about her is that unlike in any other Suikoden up to date where you can only see the main character, Rion is visible on the screen running behind the hero (only Rion though).Finally the woman that could make Jeane blush as far as boob size goes (top-right) is none other than Arshetat Falanas, the Queen of Falena. If you look carefully, you may notice the True Sun Rune attached to her forehead.You've probably realized by now that the game will be taking place in "The Queendom of Falena" (not a Kingdom), where only women are allowed to rule, so the main hero cannot inherit the throne, but rather his younger sister Limslair, who happens to love her older brother to bits, looks to become the future queen.The only other returning character that has been confirmed is Georg Prime (he is the one with a black eye-patch... much younger than in Suiko II). There have been no confirmations, but it does appear that Viki will also be making a comeback (it sounds like her in the trailer even though there is no visual of the scene). Killey is also rumored to appear although Konami hasn't shown a scent of him (but he should technically be there around this timeframe and interact with Georg). Short of that, no returning characters have been announced.The timeframe is also unknown, although judging by Georg's appearance and the fact that he was in Falena AFTER the Succession Wars (when Barbarossa became Emperor of the Scarlet Moon Empire) and before the Dunan Unification Wars, a logical timeframe would be about 14 - 0 years BEFORE the events in Suikoden II took place. More likely earlier, based on Georg's appearance. This has yet to be confirmed however and as such is only an educated guess made by myself and others alike in certain suikoden communities.The game should more than make up for Suikoden IV and I personally expect it to be the greatest thing to hit the PS2. No word of Suikoden IV or Suikoden Rhapsodia data uploads has been mentioned yet (you can upload from IV to Rhapsodia though), but the good news is that the six member parties are back. What's more they come in the standard 3 by 2 formation, but to add to the strategy further FORMATIONS have been added. Thus far I've seen the old school 3 x 2, a 1-2-3, six in a line (Suiko IV style) and a donut shaped formation (two up front, two spread at the sides and two at the back). It is uncertain if there are more, but I personally wouldn't be surprised.In addition to the 6 member parties, the skill system will also return and better then ever I might add. The skill points are now gained for the entire party rather than for each individual, which makes it much better as you won't have characters like Chris, with loads of useless skill points, which many of your other characters could use.Finally... and perhaps most importantly, THREE NEW True Runes have been announced for Suikoden V. One of them was The True Sun Rune which is in the possession of Arshetat Falanas, the Queen of Falena. The second is The True Rune of the Dawn and the final hasn't been revealed yet. It isn't known who has the other two runes, but the hero will no doubt get one of the three at some point. Hope this gives everyone a little insight into the story.@ Akito Tenkawa: You should look into getting Suikoden 1 and 2 on PSP (they're coming out soon) as the games are exactly the same as the ones on Playstation, but will probably be easier to find. Definitely worth it.@ Ninjahop: You always have the original four Suikodens and it won't be long before Suikoden Rhapsodia (Suikoden Tactics so to speak) hits the English shores. It has already been released in Japan for a while now.@ derekbrenden: You aren't the only one, believe me. Suikoden III was definitely a good game although an acquired taste for sure. Personally I cherish II above all else (fave game of all time that is). In the saga I'd rate them in this order: 2 > 1 > 3 > 4, but with the release of 5 I'm suspecting it'll end up being more like: 2 > 5 > 1 > 3 > 4 from the looks of things.Oh... forgot to mention... there will be sailing although the ships look far cooler than the Suikoden IV ones and for the most part the thing seems to be set on land, but you will see dragon horses (well... cavalry) swim through the water at the same speed that they run across the fields. If you found what I said interesting at all you may want to check out this site for further information. It's in Japanese, but it is the official Suikoden V web-site and you can get images of some of the characters as well as screenshots and a couple of VERY nice videos!!! Have fun.

It does appear however that work on Suikoden IV and Suikoden V was started by two different teams at around the same time.
