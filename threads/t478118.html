CChannel28Oct 26 2012, 08:44 AM
Occasionally when 3DS is updated it will effectively "brick" your 3DS if you use a flashcart, however soon after the 3DS is updated the 3DS compatible Flashcarts release an update themselves, which "cures" the "brick" effect.

So it is okay to update the 3DS but if you use your flashcart all the time you may want to download the 3DS update a few days after the release or after the Flashcart is also updated .... up to you.
