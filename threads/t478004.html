HHuang Fei HongOct 28 2012, 03:21 PM
Spicy/salty/savory food, sweet drinks, bitter beer, semi-sweet desserts...etc. It really all depends on the specifics of what I'm eating. Like uni for example, as minute as the sweetness is, the more the better.
However, sweet does have its limit of tastefulness...and then there's the aftertaste that often comes to ruin the experience. Sweets are an art requiring moderation.
Spiciness is a matter of tolerance. Some people can't take it at all...others can take so much of it, you have to question their sanity or humanity. Some like the thrill and the feeling of conquering something and going beyond logical limits.
Spiciness is also not a quality that stands alone, unlike how sweetness can (though sweet and bitter tend to marry well). The kind of heat needs to have a bit of a sour tang and of course some saltiness to be enhanced by the burn (and certainly not the other way around). Spiciness tends to compliment something that already tastes good, so it's more like a bonus with zing.
