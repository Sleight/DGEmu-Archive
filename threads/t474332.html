KKirie MotobaNov 20 2011, 07:53 AM
I'm assuming that the specs you provided are what you have currently on your system? If so, then there isn't that much you can improve upon.Also, you failed to mention your graphics card. Optical drive isn't an issue. If you're trying to be picky on brands, I would highly recommend Pioneer or Samsung drives. Reliability on those are top notch for burning and reading. Stay away from LG drives. Hard disk wise, I personally recommend Western Digital Caviar Black drives. Stay away from Seagate, their failure rates are still quite high even today, and any eco drive such as Western Digital Caviar Green drives are slower as you will notice spin up wait times. The suggestion of setting up in RAID0 is a good idea, but you have to keep in mind that if ever 1 drive fails, your whole setup is lost... Unless you're willing to spend even more for a third drive and set it up in RAID5, which then you're on for smooth sailing. Memory wish, going to 8GB is not really necessary, but if you really want, for budget but really reliable and cheap RAM, I highly recommend the Mushkin Silverline Stiletto: http://ncix.com/search/?categoryid=0&q=mushkinIf you want better clocks, go with G.Skill ram, prices are right, and quality is beyond exceptional. The other brand recommended: Corsair. CPU and mobo you have are fine as they are, no point in spending more. Cooling, you do not need liquid cooling, but please do buy an after-market cooler, the new AMD stock coolers really don't cut it. I've built 2 systems recently for people of which their stock cooler gives them overheating issues, so I had to go back and revise my builds for them... AMD really isn't what they used to be... As long as you have a mid-tower case, you should be fine. I've always been a fan of the Antec 902, if you want to be on the cheaper side, the Antec 300 offers really nice features for its price. Then you also have the Zalman Z7 and Z9 that are nice cases also. Power supplies isn't all about just the wattage, but the brand also. Generic brands tend to not supply the power rated, might have power spikes that can damage your components in the long run, etc. If you do have the money, the best power supplies out there are Seasonic. Premium pricing on those, but their build quality is exceptional. Second off, Corsair HX power supplies are highly recommended, those are made by Seasonic also, and offer really good reliability. On the more economic side, I've built systems with OCZ ModXstream power supplies, and they do hold out ok, but not as well as Corsair. Stay away from Antec power supplies, they don't work as well as what they used to before.
