In this tutorial I will show you how to make a full abstract, and hopefully you will all learn from the methods I use in my signatures. So lets get started.Step 1. -- Open up a New document, Mine will be 400x125Step 2. -- Now this will basically determin if you want your sig to be more on the light side or more on the dark side. If you want your sig to be on the lighter side, then leave your background white, and Brush with black. If you want you sig to be on the darker side, make your background black, and brush with white.Step 3. -- Make several layers of brushing, not too many, probably about 2-4 for begginers, (the more your have, the more complicated, and the more depth your brushing will have.)Here is What I have so far: (**NOTE: I was going for the lighter look, so I brushed with black**) Step 4. -- After you are done all your brushing, make a Brightness/Contrast Mask layer. (at the bottom of your layers pallette)This will help bring out your brushing and give it some life. Step 5. -- Adjust The Sliders until you happy with what you have.Step 6. -- This is Where you add your Render/Stock. if you want it to be more blended with your signature, then Desaturate it (Image->Apply->Deaturate.) otherwise Leave it how it is. (**NOTE: If you haveing Trouble cutting out an image, Read this tutorial for help**) Click Here For Cutting TutorialStep 7. -- This is what my Image looks like with the stock in place, I decided to have a Mech Assault one. Step 8. -- Now make a Color Balance layer. Via the layers Pallette, (The same Plavce as the Brightness/Contrast Option. (adjustment Layers Menu)Step 9. -- Here Are the Color Settings I used. (Make Sure to Use all three Options.) - - Step 10. -- Repeat Step 9. (the above step) Here are the settings I used: (be sure to make your previous Color Balance Layer invisible while you do this, so you know what color your using.) - - Step 11. -- Now to add an awesome multi color effect, go to your uppermost Color Balance Layer and go to Filter->Render->CloudsHere Is what I have so far: Step 12. -- Almost done! now its only for the finishing touches! For this step your going to add your border. The border ill be doing is a 2px simplicity. Make a new layer, (Make sure its all the way at the top of all your other layers.) and press CTRL+A to select the whole signature then go to Select->Modify->Border then Put 2px Then Go to Edit->Fill and set the color to blackNow with that done. go to Layer->Layer Style->and put a Stroke on your border. and Put these settings:Step 13. -- now for the text layer. Im going to leave this step to you. im just going to put a simple font and make it a little low on opacity. But you can do yours different...Put your text layer under your border layer and write what you please... then go to Layer->Layer Style->Blending Options and put a 1px black stroke on it, and then click ok. once that is done put the text's layer opacity down to around 50-75%. Here Is my finished Product: Now your Done!
