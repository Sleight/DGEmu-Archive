SsurfdirkApr 28 2012, 03:03 PM
We have this game. Mickey Mouse stars in it -- I kow what you're thinking -- it is a little kids game, but you would be wrong. Mickey goes into a mirror world and see the sorcerer Yensid creating Disney World. A bit of "green glop" escapes and ruins the parks in Disney World. Mickey Mouse comes to the rescue the parks by defeating the glop and the bad guys. Mickey paints or repaints stuff through the various levels to win. It kinda remind me of De Blob in some ways, but a little bit more "Epic".

The cool thing is -- if you are familiar with Disney World parks you will see various rides/attraction that were sued in the game. We live near Orlando and have season passes to the park, so it was kinda cool to see Mickey fighting his way through the theme parks rides/attractions.

I would reccomend renting this one to see if you like it --
