(thanks DM -Ling999rp)Purpose:The purpose for this competition is to make advertisements for the SOTW, hopefully by doing this more members will enter and better outcomes will emerge for a winner. Below are some different aspects of this competition. :-)Sizes, File formats, etc Requirements: - 468x60, 300x250, 160x600, 120x600, 125x125 it's all WIDTHxHEIGHT in case u wondered ;) - PNG, GIF, JPG, SWF are all allowed - try not to cross 50KB tho - and if possible make it far below lol. - Our dragon should be featured in some way, or you can simply display text on the ad saying DGemu so members know the ads belong to this site. :-)How to submit your entry?Simply post at least one submission that fits the requirements in this topic. :-) It's all right to edit your post if you want to and multiple entries are welcomed.
