There could be two possible interpretations of the question and therefore two different answers:1) The graviational force has spherical symmetry - it doesn't matter which direction you are relative to an object, the direction and magnitude of the force depends only on the mass of the object, your mass and the distance between them.2) The gravitational force has two-fold symmetry as the force due to gravity on you by an object is exactly equal and opposite to the force due to gravity on the object by you.I'm guessing you wanted the first answer though.
