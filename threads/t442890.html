I go the George Carlin route on this one--the planet was here billions of years before we came around, and now, 100 years of heavy industry is a threat? We can't deny that we've made an impact, and sure, after centuries maybe of heavy civilization, then we may have a problem, but think about it: natural events destroy as much as we do.
