RRuniteORIGINAL POSTJun 15 2008, 05:07 PM
The game will unite both protagonists and antagonists from installments of the main Final Fantasy series. Chaos will also make an appearance as the god of darkness, while a new character, Cosmos, will appear as the goddess of light. It is unknown, however, if these two will be playable or not. Recent articles released in Japanese magazines have stated that the game will have a storyline that requires playing through all of the characters to complete. There will be a minimum of ten main characters (not including their villain counterparts), and hidden characters are a possibility. Characters can gain customizable equipment, and will also be able to earn experience and gil. Tetsuya Nomura is responsible for the character designs, which will retain much of the look and style of Yoshitaka Amano's illustrations. Acutally, Chrissie, I can assure it plays nothing like Dirge of Cerberus (I played it at my cousin's).The system is this:Use O button attacks to lower your opponent's brave points(giant number above lifebar).Use square button attacks to deal damage. The more brave points you have than your opponent, the better.Use triangle to perform matrix-esque actions such as rail grinding, wall running, etc.X to jump.L or R is to guard(I forget which), but pin-point timing is needed. If successful, your opponent becomes unbalanced.the other of L or R is to dodge, and L+R is EX mode.In EX mode, hit your opponent with a square attack, and when prompted, press square again. It will activate an overdrive attack, which you can increase the damage of in varying ways (For Squall, press R when the line enters the bar at the left. For FF4's hero [dunno name of], you need to press the buttons in the left and right boxes at the same time, in order).Sometimes, during a square attack, if you have a summon equiped, it will activate and do something, usually messing with brave points.You can also equip other people's weapons at some point by buying it from one of the menus. This is also where you get evil characters from.The story mode is where you move along a grid until you face an obsticle. Destroy, try to get to the glowing red symbol as quickly as possible. Quicker you do it, the better rewards you get.Final word: It's awesome.
