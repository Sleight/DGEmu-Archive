OK so I played the boss like 10 times and finally sorta understand how you're supposed to play this game ...

Doing a just parry on the boss gives you a counter swipe which looks like nothing special, but puts them in a stun state which allows you to actually combo (basically launch) them and do all kinds of fancy stuff...so far I find that the Triangle - Triangle+Square move gives you a bunch of time to buffer launcher so its easier to do. After launch you can do stuff like aerial Square x 4 - Triangle x 2 (helm breaker-like thing) - Palm thrust - Stinger which is 33% damage on the Bladewolf (you can probably do more, but after 33% he goes to the next cutscene)

So I guess just parry is how you're supposed to approach enemies with more HP (you still can't launch the metal gears though)


Oh yeah for blade mode, you're supposed to cut in the red box, can't believe it was so obvious lol
