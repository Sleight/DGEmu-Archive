The Legend of Zelda series has left a lasting impact of the video game world on nearly every system. On the Nintendo, The Legend of Zelda brought a unique and lasting look; it revolutionized the idea of an adventure RPG style of game. Leaving the world a 3-D enviroment where the character was not limited to a 2-Dimensional direction became the series call to fame. The Ocarina of Time for the Nintendo 64 was perhaps one of the most visually stunning and memorable games on the system, while the Wind Waker was tremendous in it's own rights. Once again, The Legend of Zelda simply amazes in all of it's video game glory. But what makes the game series so memorable. In my own opinion, one of the most fascinating aspects of The Legend of Zelda is the boss system. Especially with the stunning graphics of the time, each boss was immersive and captivating. The Ocarina of Time, and the games that follow would involve a boss system that was so beautiful and stunning, it comes as no suprise that fans still love and admire the game. Noone can forget the way Link will enter the boss' lair, only to have the game enter a cutscene that reveals the fearsome and terrible boss that seems impossible to beat. Yet somehow, Link will always seem to figure out a way to defeat the greatest of foes that both uses your brain and uses a significant amount of skill at the same time. For this reason, among various others, The Legend of Zelda continues to be one of the both dominant series of video games out there. The Scholar
