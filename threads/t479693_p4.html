KKuisanagiNov 02 2013, 06:10 PM
I tested out the RNG method thoroughly, it really does work.

What you need:
-A male and female Magikarp (or a female Magikarp and something that breeds with it)
-Preferably the two Magikarp should have the same nature, but its not required.
-Check the stats of both Magikarps in the Battle Institute (this auto-levels them to 50), write them down. If they have different natures, multiply or divide by 1.1 to equalize the stats out to the nature of the Magikarp you plan on giving the Everstone to

How to do it:
1) Put the two Magikarp in the daycare (with Everstone and Destiny Knot)
2) Save
3) Bike around until the Daycare guy gives you the egg
4) Bike around more until the egg hatches (Magikarp is the fastest hatching egg, this usually takes 20 seconds with Flame Body)
5) Fly to Lumiose North Boulevard, go to Battle Institute
6) Enter a test battle (you'll need to carry an extra pokemon around since you need 3 unique pokemon to enter a battle)
7) Check out stats of the hatched Magikarp, find out which parent it inherited each stat from and which stat was not inherited

-If the uninherited stat was undesirable, soft-reset and reject the egg. Then repeat steps 2-7.
-If it was desirable (Atk or Sp.Atk) just soft-reset

8) Go into Daycare, take out the Magikarp. Replace them with the parents with the appropriate IVs, since you know which parent each IV is going to be inherited from. Remember to transfer over the Everstone and Destiny Knot
9) Generate the egg and hatch it, it should have the IVs you want

This method is mostly useful when you already have a bunch of 3-4 IV pokemon from the initial generation of breeding, it makes getting that first 5IV much less luck-based. Its also allows you to quickly generate more 5IV pokemon when you already have a 5IV parent, paricurarly when you have options for both a special and physical attacker (so 1/3 of the time the inheritance set will be useful)

Given the nerf to crit damage, the increased crit rate is a fair looking change (on paper)
Crits ignoring stat changes has always been the way they worked, as a mechanic to counter pokemon that raise defenses indefinitely, but now its just alot more relevant with the possibilty of 100% crit rate. Not to mention that it ignores dual screens too.

Thing is, this setup (Focus Energy+Scope Lens) means you give up a hold item, for the majority of Focus Energy users, its not very useful since they'd get more damage from an Swords Dance or Nasty Plot. Its only worth it if there are other factors to make crits worthwhile, such as a stat-lowering move or Sniper (and Kingdra is the only one with Sniper and Focus Energy)

Baton passing Focus Energy with Blaziken is a good alternative, though most of the pokemon with Sniper suck anyways (and Barbarcle is better off with Shell Smash), so you'd be looking instead for pokemon with Super Luck (Togekiss and non-Mega Absol) or high crit ratio moves. In most of these cases though, passing an SD would still be superior, and Blaziken can do that too.

So in the end, 100% crit will still be a niche set, but its certainly quite good on Kingdra. I did try it out yesterday and Sniper boosted Draco Meteor OHKOs just about anything, and Kingdra also has Flash Cannon to deal with Fairy. The problem is that Kingdra isn't fast enough to sweep without Swift Swim and is very easily revenged so IMO its not OP, just a pretty decent set.
