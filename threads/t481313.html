The flashcart I use on my 3DS is a SuperCard DSTwo and it works perfectly, if an update prevents it from working there's a patch for it in about 24 hours so it can work again, it only runs DS games though (and GBA and SNES if you get the plugins, and NES and NeoGeo if you get the right homebrew).

 

You avoid updates by never enabling wireless communications or accepting to install the updates.

 

Also, there's a Mexican magazine called Club Nintendo, but not an actual Mexican Club Nintendo?  :psy:


Edit: You can't use the eShop if you don't update though, and you need a Nintendo Network account as well, think well about whether you set your country to Mexico or USA (in your account) since you can't change that later, and depending on that you won't be able to buy some games (yes yes that's retarded you don't need to mention it). Also, you can use only one Nintendo Network account on a 3DS, so if you ever want to use a different account you either "System Transfer" everything (settings, downloaded games, saves of downloaded games, DLC) to another 3DS or... format the one you have and lose all of that stuff.

Erm, just a clarification: You do need to avoid the 3DS updates for at least a few days until you update your DSTWO card.

 

Reason why: If you update the 3DS before updating the DSTWO, you probably just blocked the DSTWO from loading anymore on the 3DS, so you can't update its firmware with said 3DS and require another 3DS or DS.

But once the Supercard is updated, go ahead and update the 3DS firmware, all is well.

 

Telling you this since I myself have no issues since I have a shit ton of 3DS and DS lying around the house now, but for those that only have 1 console, you don't want to block yourself.
