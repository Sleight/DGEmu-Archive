I plan on practicing medicine in the not-so-far future, but eventually I want to have a more administrative role than just treating patients.

Also, I somewhat disagree with the question's phrasing. It's not about what you want to "be" when you "grow up."

It's about what you're doing right now to get what you want to have later. I don't want "to be a doctor." I can just call myself that and see who bites. I want a medical degree and a well-paying job. After all, you will never just up and grow up. The day that you wake up and just say "Well, today I am an adult. Time to do adult things." will never come.

It's only years later that you'll realize roughly when you grew up. Make your plans and start working. Your entire life is defined by what you do, and whatever you're going to do, you're more than likely going to try and make a living doing it. Ergo, you're going to work your entire life. It's time to quit being scared of becoming some corporation's slave in a suit. The system is going nowhere, because no matter what you do, so long as you're alive, you support it.

Excuse my tangent, though. I just had to get that off my chest.
