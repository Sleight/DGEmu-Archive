Lending is giving a book to your friend with full expectations that he will return it. You can't "lend" a file. You can't send a file to someone with the full expectation that that someone will return it back. What are they going to do, attach it to their email and email it to you? The internet's a system of sharing, not a system of lending.Now, that file you sent out. What did you do to create that file? Drag and drop? Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V? Imagine that you were the creator of whatever you just spammed the world with. Imagine how you would feel. You'd probably be grateful that people are trying your product, but more importantly, outraged because you are being robbed of income that you should have rightfully gotten. All because of that guy who shared it with the world.I haven't explicitly stated this yet. The reason why it's stealing is because you, the recipient of the file, are getting something of value for free when it wasn't meant to be free. Stealing is wrong, because you wouldn't like it to happen to you. Moral of the story: filesharing = wrong.
