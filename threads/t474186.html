Humble Indie Bundle is charitable site that, every once in a while and for a limited time, offers independently developed games for, well, whatever you feel like paying. You can give a penny to $2000 (yes some people do that). With each update you gain access to a new set of games, most of which give you codes to unlock them on Steam, Desura, Android, etc. They come with the Mac, PC, and Linux versions when available. You can download it as many times as you like from however many computers because it is all DRM-free. You often even get a soundtrack for your trouble and you can download via torrent if you prefer.

We do offer most of the games they have posted here on this website. Since the games are release DRM-free, pirating them isn't exactly difficult. If you like any of the games or soundtracks posted here, please do support the site. When you purchase a bundle, you can choose how much of your price goes towards the site, the game developers, and/or your choice of two separate charities in whatever division you prefer:
[center]http://www.humblebundle.com[/center]
