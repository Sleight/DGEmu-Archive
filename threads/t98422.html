VviciouswhispersJan 15 2006, 01:37 AM
First of all, there have been 4 official in continuity Robins.The first was Dick Grayson. He is the one in the movies, the first one in the Batman cartoon, he also has appeared in the Teen Titans. He left Batman and became the leader of a group of sidekicks known as the Teen Titans. The Teen Titans briefly disbanded, then were reformed by a strange woman known as Raven to help fight her father, Trigon. Over the course of the reformed Teen Titans, titled New Teen Titans (and the basis for the Cartoon, though so way off), Dick realized that it was time for him to grow up and pass the mantle down, so he became Nightwing and gave the mantle to Jason Todd. Nightwing has been leader of the Teen Titans, then later the Titans, and finally after the death of a good friend of him, became the leader of the Outsiders. He was the former hero of Bludhaven before giving it up.Jason Todd was Bruce Wayne's adopted son. Basically, Dick felt bad cause Bruce adopted Jason and never adopted him. Some drama there. Jason Todd actually dyed his hair black (he was a natural blond) to look like Dick, so that people would believe there was still a young Robin (i.e. never aging). He was, basically, an extremely annoying punk. He was so hated that DC opened up a pay-for-call to vote to decide if Jason Todd was to be killed. The voters chose to off him, so Jason Todd was hit with a lead pipe by the Joker and blown up good. He caused Bruce to go into a deep depression and swear off partners for a while at least. Jason Todd has suddenly reappeared as the evil and rather childish villain, Red Hood.Tim Drake discovered that Dick Grayson was Robin by watching Dick preform his famous flip against the Penguin. Tim used his amazing deductive logic to figure out that if Dick Grayson was Robin, then Bruce Wayne was Batman. He started out trying to convince Dick to come back as Robin after Jason Todd's death, but both Dick and Bruce turned down that idea. Eventually Tim showed the compassion and capabilities to become the new Robin after Jason Todd, teaching Bruce to accept a partner and stop trying to commit suicide via bad guys. Tim was trained by the greatest martial artist in the DC Universe, Lady Shiva, and showed great potential to be a martial artist, but he always believed that being Robin would be temporary. He slowly pulled away from Batman and became a hero in his own right. He even had a sidekick/girlfriend, Stephanie Brown, who at the time was known as Spoiler. Tim Drake eventually retires as Robin, but comes back only to have his father murdered, to become a member of the newly formed Teen Titans and discover that one day he may become a gun totting psycho-Batman, and then given his own city, well shared with Cassandra Cain as Batgirl, named Bludhaven, former city of Nightwing. While Tim was retired, Stephanie Brown saw him flirting with another girl. She decided to get back at him by sewing her own Robin costume, then breaking into the Bat-Cave. While Stephanie Brown was Spoiler, many other heroes (except Tim) told her she was unqualified even to be Spoiler, but Bruce seemed to have an attack of senality at his young age, and hired her anyhow. She was eventually forced to resign as Robin and was thankfully killed during a massive Gang War which she was partly responsible for causing.As for the daughter of Selina and Bruce, she was from Earth-2 and her name was Helena Wayne aka Huntress II. She died during the massive Crisis of Infinite Earths back in the 80's along with most of the Earth-2 heroes. A version of her appeared in the short-lived Birds of Prey TV show as Helena Kyle.Helena Wayne has been replaced by the current Huntress, Huntress III, Helena Bertinelli. She's a mobster's daughter and she's been bashed by Batman several times to be less violent. Anyhow, Yes there are 3 Huntresses, so don't just say Huntress. Oh and for the person who wants to claim that Spider-Man is somehow better than Batman. You've got some issues. Spider-man and Batman go about things in entirely different fashions. Spider-Man wears a flashy, garish costume, fires off webbing, uses his super-powers and scientific knowledge to figure things out. He has a strange rogues gallery, most of which have super-powers just like Spiderman. He is not a world class detective and never will be. He's flashy, fun loving, and well-loved by his city. He also has yet to inspire a single side-kick or legacy.. The best he has is an alternate reality daughter and Arana who just got the Spider-Clan powers he rejected.Batman, however, uses his mind and a series of gadgets plus his years of training in martial arts to do his job. He's dark, broody, and doesn't trust anybody. He has a complete set of protocols to take out any and every super-hero in the DCU. He alternates between being an urban legend and well known super-hero. He's inspired several heroes including 4 Robins, 4 Batgirls, 1 Batwoman, Oracle, Nightwing, Catwoman, and 2 Huntresses to say the least.
