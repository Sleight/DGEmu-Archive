Yes, it's hilarious, but it also deconstructs race relations. It is specifically meant to confront race by exposing the viewpoint of many blacks and point out the contradictions that many people (both black and nonblack) hold in their personal philosophy. Each Character is an archetype, pointing out differing aspects of black culture as well as highlighting certain aspects of white culture. Aaron McGruder, who is personified by the 12-year-old Huey is angry at blacks and whites and isn't afraid to show it. Overall, it points out how ridiulous race conceptions even are, and I think the show deserves much more credit than it recieves, even if it already recieves a lot of credit.
