[font=Georgia]I would say that Pokemon Soul Silver is the most underrated game of all time. Everyone placed expectations upon the game based upon the first game. Yet rather than use the same combat system, they created a new one that required a great deal of strategy in how you utilized and prepared moves.[/font]
[font=Georgia][/font]
[font=Georgia]Next, the story was a perfect compliment to the story of the original Pokemon Silver. Rather than sticking with another Johto story, they went a different route and used alternate regions. The changes made created many different worlds, with a section of the world being caught as a sort of dump of all changes made to the original. It was a complex but rewarding setting in the end, and tied up the only real loose end from Pokemon Silver.[/font]
[font=Georgia]My only complaint with the game would be the cast. They used way too many Pokemons and many of them are severely underdeveloped. However, the core Pokemons are all strong in their own right.[/font]
[font=Georgia]In the end, the game may never be appreciated as much as it should be, but I loved it all the same.[/font]
