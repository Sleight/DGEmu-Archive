Recently I've been playing this on a friends PS3, and I love it. The new octocamo makes it so you can blend in almost anywhere, and take out the enemies in stealth. You can also upgrade your weapons, add modifications and even more ammo for them as you progress throughout the game. The game takes a real long time to get through mainly because of the cutscenes, it's almost like watching a movie at most parts. While you can skip ahead through all of them, one may want to watch them to understand the deep storyline they have created. I'd recommend this game to anyone who has played the other metal gear series games, even if you haven't it's a must play if you own a PS3! Who knows, you may go out and get the previous games as well. >.<
