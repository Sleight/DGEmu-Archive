Like Will Ferrell. 'Flat' things don't inspire nearly as much controversy as FFVII does. Now, FFV, there was a game with a 'flat' plot. But yes, FFVII has to have one of the worst stories I've ever seen in an RPG. It's just a mess.FFVII, really, is a game I feel will never get juuust the right amount of credit it deserves. The "hardcores" give it way too little, and condemn it as being the worst game ever (which is not even close)... The unwashed masses, on the other hand, hail it as the best thing since sliced bread (which is also way off)... It's somewhere in the middle. Which makes it the perfect representative for the series as a whole. Neither as great, nor as awful, as everyone says. People just need to realize that not only is there a middle ground, but it's okay to be part of it. Not every Final Fantasy has to be hailed as the best game ever, or the worst game ever.To get back on topic, though... The game is fun. After a few years thinking it was mostly underserving of its reputation, I forced myself to replay it a year or so ago, and once I got past just how awful the graphics were (and fixed up the TV's contrast and brightness and whatnot), I was presently surprised at how much better it was that I was remembering it. Yuffie's awesome in a snotty, Rutee Katrea sort of way, Cid's an awesomely crotchety old man, and Red XIII, when he's actually relevant (like, one scene), is pretty awesome too... The materia system is just fun. Yes, there's the whole problem with all the characters being basically the same, but that's pretty much a Final Fantasy standard now, and has been since FFV. But there's just a huge level of customization you can do with it, and I, personally, get a deal of pleasure out of beating Emerald Weapon in a matter of minutes using counter attacks alone.It's also the only game in the series with a decent chocobo sidequest. So that counts for something as well, I figure. At least there's nothing as soul-crushing as Chocobo Hot and Cold to drag it down....That... Was a lot bigger than I planned. Yeesh.

I love the fact that you can spend an insane amount of time at the Golden Saucer and various other side quests, or go for the straight-shot win.
