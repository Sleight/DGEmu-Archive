Post the 3 games you played this year that you enjoyed the most or what you thought were the best/most well made. Not limited to games that were released in 2014.

 

3. Sleeping Dogs

Holy shit I love this game. It had everything; the atmosphere, the story, the grafficks, literally everything about it was great EXCEPT for the gameplay. The gameplay is pretty fun at first but the more you play the more repetitive and boring it feels. I wouldn't say that it played bad, it was mainly the combat being 80% of the gameplay and also being extremely bland and repetitive. The other 20% though, the car segments, the parkour, doing shit like jumping from car to car, all of that was really fun. It's more than worth it to play it just for the plot alone or hell, just driving around and admiring the attention to detail in all of the scenery.

 

2. Fire Emblem Awakening

Replaced FE7 as my favorite FE. I just really liked everything about this one. The plot, the characters, the crapton of supports, the class mechanics, etc. It was overall a really good and enjoyable game. I also like how accessible they made it, you don't have to be COST EFFECTIVE just to play it for the first time.

 

1. Hotline Miami

I honestly can't remember the last time I enjoyed a game this much. This is by far the best indie game I've ever played. The gameplay is the epitome of fun, not the bland mindless fun that comes from playing something like MYF or the pure bullshit challenge of playing something like Dark Souls. It's the perfect balance of recklessness and strategy. You can't just rush in and at the same time you can't move at a snail's pace, it forces you to think and react as fast as possible to each unique situation. Gameplay aside I just loved everything else about it, the style/atmosphere, the art style, the soundtrack especially, and... pretty much everything. I honestly can't think of anything bad to say about this game, for what it is it's amazing.

 

Honorable mentions:

DmC - If you don't take it seriously it's pretty goddamn hilarious and actually fun despite the gameplay feeling repetitive by the end.

 

CV Lords of Shadows - Loved the atmosphere, the plot, the characters, extremely well done overall except for the goddamn gameplay. This isn't even on the same level as Sleeping Dogs, if you're not a CV fan and this is your first entry into the series then I honestly can't see you finishing it because of how goddamn boring and long it is. This is the definition of a "just watch it on youtube" game.

 

Far Cry 3 - The best worst game I've ever played.
