I do not really know what you plan on using your psp for so I will just throw some ideas out there.PSPVC is so far the best converter I have found for converting videos into PSP format.Remote Joy is both software for the computer was well as for your psp. If you install the plugin for the psp then connect it to your comp you can have the psp screen on your comp and play it threw there.Fraps is a great program for recording gaming, so if you get Remote Joy working, I suggest frags to record it.UMDGen is very helpful for editing ISO/CSO files. You can rip them, taking out stuff you may like what like the Update, or videos to make it smaller.Another plugin I suggest for the psp is CWCheat, which is a memory editor and basicly a game shark for psp.
VvwhooliganJun 09 2009, 03:45 PM
I've had mine for just over a year now and have found a few good programs too. I agree with Bob that PSPVC is a great tool to use. I have also had a lot of success with Netforce Beta 4 (don't know if there is a newer one out or not.) It is a browser similar to one included with the PSP, however is much better in that it allows use of all available RAM and makes surfing faster. (IIRC, 32 Mb for phat and 64Mb for slim.) Larger pages will lag the system down of course, but I've had no troubles at all with accessing hotmail mobile, my school e-mail, my bank account (really handy when I forgot to make a payment...) and I've even been able to view web-based class grade book. Awesome tool!Another video converter that I have used with AVI files is something called a 3gpp converter. I used it when I was playing around with my phone videos, but was surprised to find out that it actually converts videos to PSP and Ipod format too. Don't know how well it works with Vista though, since I used XP Pro.Also, POPS Loader is an awesome emulator for PSX games too. There are also other emulators for the GBA, the SNES and the NES that I use. There are others, but those are the ones I stuck with.You'll soon find out that a 1Gb stick may be too small. ;) Mine came with a 4 and I've since upgraded that for extra space with movies, music and the like. Kinda' along the same lines as Bob, it's all in what you want to use it for. I love mine to death since it has become so versatile. Of course, many of the programs I've mentioned require the use of a custom firmware. If your PSP does not have one, search around and there are ways to load one. Since yours is a phat model it is a snap to do.Hope this helps,Chance*EDIT* - D'oh! Maybe I should read your sig. Then I would know you already have a CFW... ;)
