gamer1258' post='4875259 wrote on Oct 8 2010, 06:22 PM:
Time travel isn't possible and if it was I'd probably go back to several important events like inventions and stuff.
The theory of Relativity doesn't rule it out, ever hear of gravitational/relative velocity time dilation?As far as backwards time travel goes, there's nothing that indisputably rules that out either, though you'd have to wonder why nobody has ever seen a time traveler from what we perceive to be the future. But then again, if parallel universes do exist in this particular form (if at all), and they are in fact made up of every possible variation of every possible action, then a person traveling back (or forward) in time would, in theory, open up a whole new reality/universe. This begs the question of how somebody would be able to get back to their own time/reality, assuming that was possible (and that these particular theories are correct). In short; it's not impossible until it's been proven to be, and even then scientists make a business out of proving each other wrong.Yeah, it all sounds like science fiction and for now it is. But remember; at one point laser guns, nanotechnology, cloaking devices, and teleportation were all once thought of as science fiction. We have a long way to go before we can effectively apply the latter two technologies, but both have been proven to be possible.
