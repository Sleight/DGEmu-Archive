Just curious though, is the error message more in this format:

My speculation is that this game may not require a a CHD, but in fact, the romset we carry might be outdated for that version of Battletoads.

Arcade roms work differently from normal console roms since they were are a single zip that contains multiple files inside that are simply memory dumps from several chips on the cartridge board.

And between every version of MAME, that same romset that worked with a previous version might stop working with the newer one due to an update to the romset or having new files to increase compatibility.


I just checked. Our rom is outdated, I think it was a dump meant to work on MAME 1.41.

I've just finished fixing a rom for you and am done uploading.

 

I personally recommend using MAME64 (formerly known as MAME32) instead of the official build.

 

Download the emulator here: http://www.mameui.info/ (look for "MAMEUI64 .149.1" link, requires 7-zip to extract).

 

Once the emulator extracted, download the rom found here and put it into the "ROMs" folder of the emulator. Make sure to NOT rename nor extract the content of the zip.

Rom download: http://www.filefactory.com/file/4sfnrnflms6j/n/btoads.zip

 

Once done, open up the emulator by loading "Mameui64.exe".

In the user interface, on the left hand side, click on "Available".

You'll notice that your "Game" list will be empty. On your keyboard, press "F5". this scan operation will take about 5 minutes to complete.

Once complete, "Battletoads" should appear with a green icon next to it. Double-click and enjoy. :)

KKirie MotobaAug 22 2013, 05:40 AM

Well, just follow my steps and download of the new rom, and it should all work out! :)

 

Arcade emulation is a bit more complicated than a regular console since the roms themselves consists of a zip with multiple rom dumps for video, audio, content, etc.

Everytime a new version of MAME or other arcade emulator comes out, there's always a list of rom changes that will break compatibility with your current ones.

 

For those cases, it's important that you maintain your roms with a rom manager such as Romcenter so that they can scan your sets, rename and fix them, etc.

I can explain in more detail next time if you're interested. :)
