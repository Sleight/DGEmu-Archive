I don't know why, but for some reason I remembered a show that used to air on Nickolodeon called As Told By Ginger. It's basically about a ginger haired middle school girl. I have no idea why I watched this (I was like 8?). I looked up the episode I remembered on youtube and lo and behold it was actually on there. Watching a show like this now I realized how many jokes and stuff went way over my head as a kid. What shows or cartoons did you watch when you were growing up? When we got cable I used to watch a lot of Cartoonnetwork. Whatever was on, Dexter's Lab, Powerpuff Girls... other things I can't remember. Then one day I came home from school and turned on the tv and saw a show I never saw before. It was fucking crazy. Instead of doing random dumb shit these guys were screaming and punching each other into mountains. Instead of going to the grocery store they were training and punching each other in the face. It was Dragon Ball Z, it was the first anime I ever saw and what got my brother and I into anime. I THINK this was before Toonami aired (I wanna say around '96-'97) but when it did we were hooked. We used to watch DBZ, Tenchi Muyo, Reboot(?) and Gundam Wing (I never liked mecha but my brother loved it so I watched it anyways). Tthen a few years later they started airing things like Rurouni Kenshin and Yu Yu Hakusho even though I used to watch YYH on Adult Swim late at night because I never had a bedtime.

6 to 10 years old, Japanese cartoons:

Future Boy Conan
One of Miyazaki's earlier stuff, believe it of not, this is Miyazaki's most popular work over here!

The Mysterious Cities of Gold
Awesome adventure story, with aztecs shiit and a huge golden eagle thingy.

Kimba the White Lion
This anime inspired Lion King! Actual, Lion King ripped off whole scenes for it.

Chibi Maruko Chan <3 <3 <3
My favourite anime ever, EVARRR!!!

Captain Tsubasa
Anyone who live in a country where Football is the main sport should know this, the reason we played football as kids was to imitate captain Tsubasa. Also, the show's video game(first one on the NES) was my first ever RPG(it played like FFX's Blitz ball, so not a real RPG!). Finished it with my older brother, even though it was entirely in Japanese, we memorized moves name, knew where to check for passwords(no saves!), we even knew players by looking at their Japanese names, at a time when we were barely able to write in Arabic, that's how much of a Captain Tsubasa fanboys we were.
