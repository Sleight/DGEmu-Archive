Oh. The tube coming out is attached to the green sponge on one end and the machine on the other. The pic is just a close up of the dressing. The actual vacuum isn't pictured. The green sponge is compressed under the clear tape, which is basically very sturdy cling wrap. The vac keeps a constant pressure of 125mmHG on there, which forces the wound to heal from the bottom up. The pressure on the sponge also sucks in any blood or discharge as well as any sanguinous (scar) tissue that might form, keeping the tissue fresh on the sides and top as the bottom heals.

It is a very slow process, but much faster with the vacuum. It could still take 6 months or more though.
