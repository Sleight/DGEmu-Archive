SStonecycleDec 26 2010, 09:18 PM
When it comes to games, graphics should not be the important part, but instead, as a minor bonus. It's the content and fun that counts.They each have their specialties to me. The N64 version (SSB) was very lacking in content with only twelve (if I recall correctly) characters, excluding the four unlockables. There are also very few stages to compete on, one for each series except for Mario's, which had two (Peach's Castle and an NES-themed SMB one). What people may consider cheap on there would be easy mistakes to learn with their drawbacks, like Captain Falcon's Falcon Punch. It's very powerful, but easy to dodge.Super Smash Bros. Brawl has been ridiculed over the major unbalancing of the different characters, and even more so with the Final Smash feature. It's tough to go against a powerhouse such as Ike as a weakling like Lucas, but with enough practice, one can learn to take advantage of a certain character using the weakest in a player's arsenal (which would be Jigglypuff to many new fighters). Some people may have been surprised at the inclusion of third-party characters Sonic and Solid Snake. The latter was originally intended to be in Super Smash Bros. Melee, but development was too far along to add another character. It's really all about perspective on what you want: Content or balance.
