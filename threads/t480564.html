KKirie MotobaFeb 17 2014, 05:02 PM

You better check how advanced your company is.

 

Speaking of experience from my environment (bank) and my fiancee's environment (law firm):

 

On the SCCM Admin side :

I personally can easily pull out all the names of workstations and the users on them where they installed torrent, P2P clients, etc. Hardware inventory (installed applications) and software inventory (registry keys, files on computers) are all visible to me. We didn't configure ours to take into account portable devices, but it can be done to track tablets, Windows Phones, Apple devices, Blackberries, etc.

 

On the Security Admin side:

Site filtering: I'm sure Piratebay falls under hacking, which should be filtered out by default.

At my fiancee's workplace, they even have something that monitors executables/scripts that are being run that are not part of the approved software lists, warns the users, and logs it to their IT admins. That blocks off any portable apps and anything that's not approved.

 

On the Telecom Admin side:

Most ports are blocked by default by their firewall. Only certain ports are opened for what they need.

Any sort of bandwidth is monitored. High usage / connections to unknown hosts constantly jump out like a sore thumb in their activity consoles.
