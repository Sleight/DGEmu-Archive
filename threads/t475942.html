I have discovered a nifty hack to change your tune on your VBA to play "8bit" chiptunes.

1: Go to I/O viewer and go to 0x4000CC-DM2DAD_L
2: Just check every box on the list (It will say FFFF)If you want to return to normal sounds just uncheck everything but boxes 7,5,and 2.

Note: Some music and sounds will not play with this trick.

PS:Try it on pokemon. :gs:
