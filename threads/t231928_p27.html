I love Lucas, Ness, Marth and Game & Watch.There's still doubts over the difference between the 13-year old psychic boys and whether they're clones, but they're not. Lucas has amazing ground attacks which come out obscenely fast with nearly no lag whatsoever. Forward Tilt is a good move with nice range to interrupt dashes, etc. Up Tilt is also good out of a dash, whereas if Down tilt seems useless, try using it when the opponent is lying down; 6% per hit is nice for damage-racking when the opponent can't do anything. Lucas' Fsmash is **** fast and KOs starting around 95%. Upsmash is a marvellous punisher that kills heavyweights at 80%, so is Dsmash.Ness' Fsmash may seem identical to Lucas', but you need to tip it for it to be a killer, else it won't kill even at 130%. His Usmash is good for combos and is versatile overall, but is really weak, so is Dsmash. His tilts also come out fast and have really good priority, but doesn't have the range that Lucas offers.In terms of recovery options, Lucas can use is grab in the air to grab the ledge, he can PK Thunder normally, or he can do a Zap Jump (search on youtube what this is), while Ness is still stuck with hitting himself in the butt with a ball of thunder (which, by the way, can be eaten by opponents just by jumping on it, killing Ness). However, Lucas' grabs come out reaalllllly slow and is easily punishable, giving Ness another advantage onstage.So. The difference?Lucas has an excellent ground game with an average aerial game. While Ness lives in the air and doesn't do as well on the ground. Lucas has A-tier recovery, while Ness has mediocre, easily-gimped recovery. Lucas' grab game sucks (due to his grab alone! He has the most damaging pummel in the game and excellent throws, but his grab. Sigh.), while Ness' grab game is really good.And I haven't even gone to the differences between their special attacks yet.Stop calling them clones.
