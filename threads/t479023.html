KKirie MotobaApr 09 2013, 05:40 AM

Well, to at least give your laptop some life: 2009 is considered old, and if you never actually maintained the fans, and that you live in a "dirty" environment (surrounded by pets, smokers, dust, etc), then it is most likely that the fans are clogged and cooling is not proper.

 

Overheating is highly probably, especially with the symptoms you're describing. Just feel the laptop when it goes off, surely a spot is hotter than the rest.

 

A first step to take is to at least try to clear out the vents with compressed air cans or something.

If that doesn't work and you feel adventurous, you can take apart your laptop, really go in and manually clean the fans, apply a new thermal paste, and put it back together. Take my warning though, I've done that for a good 4 years, and you definitely need to know what you're doing, and you have to be meticulous and ordered, or else, you'll end up closing up your laptop and scratching your head why there's so many spare screws all of the sudden.

KKirie MotobaApr 09 2013, 12:12 PM

Good news then.

 

Did your friend make sure to do an extended SMART test on your hard disk?

4 years of use is considered a lot for a laptop, and the symptom of a hard drive failing can start by simple file corruptions and such.

 

Loose Hard disk wiring doesn't happen often, though I wouldn't necessarily rule it out from the list of issues,

 

As for hinge issues, no matter the laptop, after many years to use, the hinge will become loose since they're usually held down with 3 to 5 screws on each side, and as they loosen up, they augment the risk of actually cracking the hinge. Those issues are especially prominent on HP dv9000, dv6000, and dv2000 series from back in the days, and many Dell Inspiron 5000 and Alienware M11x where the hinge design was just horrible and you would feel like punching the person that designed the hardware.
