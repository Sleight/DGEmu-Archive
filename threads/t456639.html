In order for the common member to get to know Staff better we have made this lovely forum! If you have a question that you would like to ask a Staff Member (that is NOT about why you got GPs deducted, warned, etc), or would simply like to get to know a Staff Member, then post in the topic that corresponds with that Staff Member.Staff Members will make their OWN topic, if they wish to take part! Staff members have the right to refuse to not answer a question if they feel it is too personal, rude, inappropriate, etc. They will also give GP deductions and warnings if required. If you have a question that is general that any staff member may be able to answer (Who is responsible for the Support Emails?, etc) then you can post such questions in the General Staff Questions topic.
