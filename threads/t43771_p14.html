EEnhasaDec 29 2006, 09:21 PM
My top 3 GBA games:1. Tactics Ogre: The Knight of Lodis: This is an excellent tactical RPG that provides challenge, depth, and a decent story (with multiple endings). The battle engine is much more complicated than that of the more popular Final Fantasy Tactics Advance, incorporating factors such as elemental affinity, alignment, terrain, and the performance of specific deeds (to enable the advanced classes). The only thing lacking in this game is the music, which is primitive and grainy. But given that this is a fairly early GBA game, that is forgiven.2. Final Fantasy Tactics Advance: This tactical RPG is less challenging, less complex, and more light-hearted than its predecessor, but it's still worth playing. As long as you think of it as a standalone game and not in comparison to the masterpiece that is Final Fantasy Tactics, you probably won't be disappointed.3. Riviera: The Promised Land: This turn-based RPG has not received the widespread recognition that it deserves. It has a great story, beautiful graphics, and good sound for a GBA game. Many of the dialogue scenes allow you to choose your own path, which is refreshing and contributes to the replay value. One thing that is annoying (or increases difficulty, depending on how you look at it) is the limited number of items that you can carry. The only thing that really bothered me about this game was the need to rapidly press a combination of buttons to avoid traps.I am not including ports of old games in this list. Although some ports have additional bonus features, they are generally superfluous and not enough to make it a "new game" per se.
