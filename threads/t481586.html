To be honest I just don't have the patience or the experience to fill out all of that. Twenty-one years of gaming is a lot and I was poor. I was 8 in 1994 and I really wanted a lot of things since I was a kid, but we couldn't only get a few things so I had to be selective with what I played.

 

It is an impressive list to be sure, but I never played any new games in 2007 say. I still play Dragon Quest IX: Sentinels of the Starry Skies and that game came out in 2009. So my record for gaming is far too spotty. I never played those Batman games from 2007 for instance and I feel pretty bad about it. Nothing I can really do though unless I play the XKCD Card and merely buy old games, play them and keep up a constant stream of gaming experience that is 6 months too late. Then I could quote memes from 6 months ago and seem really anachronistic.

 

As for my favorite gaming years, I suppose the N64 years were pretty good. I couldn't afford a Playstation, which seems rather myopic now, but I never had the prescience to know that Sony would become a gaming giant. I admit, I was a fanboy of Nintendo and didn't see any originality in the design of the controllers for the Playstation(Oh, so they added more shoulder buttons? Bah). I also hated the idea of a CD based gaming system since it meant you had to buy Memory Cards and wait for the freakin game to load all the time. I felt that the technology wasn't there yet. The only game I really thought was cool was Symphony of the Night, but I don't remember how I heard of it. In any case, the N64 years brought us The Legend of Zelda Ocarina of Time, The Legend of Zelda Majora's Mask, Banjo Kazooie, Diddy Kong Racing, Super Mario 64, Mischief Makers, Legend of the Mystical Ninja Starring Goemon, Goldeneye and more. So I was pretty happy with that console and I still have it somewhere in my basement.

 

Though I suppose 1994 was also pretty good since that kicked off the Golden Age of Rare. I mean, you had Donkey Kong Country, which was totally awesome. Then you had the sequels, Diddy's Kong Quest and Dixie's Double Trouble which were also good. I think the second one is far and away considered the best though because of it's tone perhaps.

 

Anyway, this post is way too long already, so that is the end of that.
