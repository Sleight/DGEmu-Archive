EElohimbkAug 30 2010, 01:09 PM
I have a tribal tattoo on my right shoulder. I would recommend to anyone thinking about tattoos that you make sure it is something you care deeply about or will last the test of time. Choosing an intricate detailed full color tattoo may seem like a great idea at first, but just like painting a house, it will have to be up kept to stay pretty. Over time lines with merge and colors will fade causing you to have to go back into the chair to get it touched up. If you do go straight black then make sure they have good inks and use the liner needle for the entire thing. It will hurt a little more but will stay looking great a lot longer. Also always put sunblock on your art! sun fades everything fast!
