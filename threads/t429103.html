So I'm not sure why this topic got resurrected, but I guess there are still people interested in the game, so here's my two cents.(For readers just picking up on the thread, Ragnarok DS was released in North America in February. No need for patches, and you can buy it in the store! :) )So this game really gives me mixed feelings. I used to play the original RO for hours on end. Even went for a marathon, plying for about 26 hours once! (that was pretty fun hehe) And then, unfortunately, my beloved server went down after the owner had a little hissy-fit. I tried some other servers, but none of them really had the same "feel" so I just kinda stopped...Then Ragnarok DS came out and I was like "Hoooo yeah, a chance to get back into RO!" But the game itself...man, it's just not very good. :P Sure it has the same characters, towns, visual style, and equipment as the original...but the actual battle system just doesn't work too well on the DS. Pair that with the awkward movement and it becomes really tough to deal with being out in the field.And then there are the obligatory dialogue scenes. You have to watch them. You can't skip through them. You can't even fast-forward them!Overall, it just loses the fast-paced excitement of the original RO and replaces it with hours of frustration. Oh well, at least it's got bunny ears! :)
