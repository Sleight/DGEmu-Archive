Time for a debate on an ultimately meaningless subject, yet one that is still interesting to millions of people: professional American football.

What are your picks for the upcoming league champion? What are your picks for divisional and conference champs? And for the two Superbowl teams, why?

Here are mine:
AFC North: I'm going to go out on a limb and pick the Cincinnati Bengals. I don't believe in the Steelers (can't as a Seahawks fan), and I think Flacco and the Ravens will slump, causing much hilarity over the exorbitant salary the 'elite' QB is making.
AFC South: Houston Texans here; the Colts might give them some trouble, and Schaub is not very impressive, but their defense should hold out.

AFC East: Ugh, the Patriots because they have no competition here.
AFC West: The Broncos, in much the same situation as the Patriots, but without losing any of their offensive weapons (just, you know, their starting C recently).

NFC North: Green Bay Packers.
NFC South: Atlanta Falcons; not much competition.
NFC East: Tough division to call, because of the parity. I'd pick the New York Giants of Football, because 
NFC West: The media is finally giving attention to the Seahawks, and I think their QB Russell Wilson will keep them grounded enough to upset the 49ers. When the teams last met, it was a 42-13 romp for the Seahawks, so any 49ers fans thinking their team will roll the Seahawks is delusional and arrogant. Interestingly enough, the all-time series for these two teams is tied, but the Seahawks have a massive point advantage.

 

NFC Conference: Seattle Seahawks.

AFC Conference: Denver Broncos.

Superbowl winner: This one is tough. I called this match-up the very weekend the two teams made their exits from last year's playoffs, but I couldn't pick a winner. Right now, I'm going to be biased and say the Seattle Seahawks. But it sure will be exciting.
