::::::::::UPDATE::::::::::SotW Info-Entry topics will be posted monthly with the theme or any conditions in the subtitle/topic description.-SotW winners will receive a GP prize. The prize will be 500GPs multiplied by the number of entries from now on. e.g. If 10 people enter the prize will be 10*500=5000GPs, if only one person enters the prize will be 1*500=500Gps.-The winner is decided by a group of SotW judges. The judges pick a 1st (3pts), 2nd (2pts) and 3rd (1pt). Whoever has the most points wins SotW and will be awarded GPs. They will also have their entry uploaded to show at the bottom of the forums for one week. The current judges are:-Finalage-stock-Night Terror SP (downshift)-Daft Punk (me)-pengu (when he has time to vote)-Judges may enter SotW, but will not be allowed to vote for the week that they entered.-The SotW winner may also to choose to be a guest judge for the week following their win.-Each week when the judges winner is announced a public vote thread will also be posted. This will be a poll so anyone can anonymously vote for who they think should win. The public vote winner will not receive any GPs.-Entry threads will close between 8pm-Midnight GMT+12 (New Zealand time). Use this website to compare it to your own timezone.-Any general SotW discussion should be posted in this thread.-If you wish to suggest a theme or condition for SotW it should be posted here.-If you don't have any image editing software you can download Photoshop here (scroll down to the CS2 link, the CS3 beta is dead), or GIMP here.SotW Rules-All posts in the entry thread must contain an entry (signature image), it must be in the post NOT your actual sig. If you are not posting a sig, DO NOT POST. -You may only enter once per week.-Entries must not exceed DGEmu's set dimensions of 500*400 pixels. -There is no filesize limit for entries, however, if the winner's sig is too large (in kb) it may be compressed/have the file-type changed before uploading.-Entries may not contain porn or nudity.-Entries may be in *.gif, *.jpg and *.png format.-You can now edit your post and/or entry if you wish up until the entry thread is closed, unless you have been disqualified.-Animation is not allowed unless specified in the theme or conditions.-Ripping or blatantly copying someone else's sig will not be tolerated and will result in a permanent ban from SotW. If you suspect an entries is ripped/copied send me a PM, do not post in the entry thread or you risk being warned.-Should an administrator feel the need to interrupt SotW their decision is final.-All other DGEmu rules must be followed (short posts are fine for entries).- As of today and onwards, contestants are expected to include a base image if they used one for comparison reasons. Breaking any of these rules will result in a disqualification for the week (or permanently), and may also result in point deductions or a positive warn.-Amended on the 18th of August, 2010. ~Finalage.-Amended on the 18th of March, 2011. ~Finalage.
