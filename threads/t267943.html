well, they are very different games in different genres. They both have the best elements in their respective genres and so they are hard to compare. Spyro on the one hand is an adventure game, so it is necessary to have open worlds and various quests to complete that use a variety of skills. Some people may like this more because certain people enjoy doing a variety of things, some of which may be challenging to them while others aren't. Crash on the other hand is a platformer game, which requires linear maps and progressively harder gameplay. Crash succeeds because of the unique "sonic's donkey" perspective (name given by the developers), which takes the standard side view of platformers and instead shows the back side, which allows for 3d environments. On top of that, Crash features many varied challenges, like breaking all the boxes in a level to earn gems, or, in warped, to run through the level in certain amount of time. The people who like this game better prefer to do something requiring one skill, and then master that skill, rather than test a bunch of different skills.My personal tastes attract me more to Crash, but i do enjoy playing spyro almost as much.
