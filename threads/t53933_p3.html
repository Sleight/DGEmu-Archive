I own both a 360 and a PS3 (I bought the 360 first). Graphics wise the PS3 is slightly better, but the 360 has a larger hard drive and has a wider selection of games (since several PS3 exclusive titles are no longer exclusive). There have been more must-have games out on the 360 than the PS3 in 2008 and I believe that trend will continue with games such as Halo OSTD and Halo Wars coming out in 2009. The online play for 360 is superb, with voice chat well-integrated. With most 360 bundles containing a headset a larger portion of the players have voice online, which is a plus when having to work together. Thus I find myself choosing 360 when a game is multi-console. However when it comes to multimedia I find myself using my PS3 more for movies and music. Movies play smoothly and with excellent controls (I have the PS3 BD remote, but have not tried the 360 remote). The 360 plays movies as well but makes it more difficult to access any movies you've saved to your hard drive as well as not supporting blu-ray. The XMB menu is superbly organized when compared to the 360's original menu or even its New Experience. The few Blu-rays I buy (which are still too expensive for everyday movie purchases imo) look astounding on a high definition tv. In addition the ability to natively partition and install an OS on the PS3 is a bonus since I can and have turned it into a computer (when my desktop was being repaired).So, to sum up my views on the two: if you are looking for a gaming console, especially FPS or other co-op/competitive games that require voice communication, go with the 360, with its wider selection of top-notch games. If you are looking to play Hi-def movies, music, and/or browse the internet in addition to play games, go with the PS3. The PS3 will cost you more but you can center your home entertainment center around it since it plays blu-ray, games, and music all on the same device.PS. I currently have 4 PS3 games (MGS4, Resistance 1&2, and LBP) I have 20 games for the 360 (like I said above I did purchase the 360 first so some prejudice)
