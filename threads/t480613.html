Barraco Barner wrote on Apr 29 2014, 03:55 AM:

Hey, can I use Showbox through my phone with chromecast?

You should be able to (that's how I use it), though I have seen people say they have trouble with it.  My cousin couldn't get it to work, but it works like a charm for me on 3mb/s internet.

 

Download the app

http://media-apps.cc/android/

 

Download some casting application.  I use LocalCast because it's free, but AllCast is good if you want to pay.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.stefanpledl.localcast

 

 

Open Localcast and click the cast icon to start the casting server in the app.

Go to Showbox and open up a movie or show you're interested in

Uncheck the "Play in Internal Player" box that is checked by default

When you click play, it will show your list of possible player apps, choose LocalCast and the show will cast.

 

In my experience, it takes 2 tries to get something to cast.  I'm not sure why.  I've noticed that, if I click an episode of a show or a movie I'm interested in, and it doesn't show how how big the file is, then it won't cast.  For me, I click cast, it takes me to local cast, I press back to go back to ShowBox, and click it again, and it works fine.  Not sure why.  It's a minor issue for me because the app does work reliably on the 2nd time every time.

Barraco Barner wrote on Apr 29 2014, 03:07 PM:

Any way to cast Chrome Tabs through phone/table?

Not tabs, but videos, yes.

 

First you'll need the Chrome Beta (which you should use anyways)

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.chrome.beta

 

Then you'll need to go to here and enable the flag.

chrome://flags#enable-cast

 

After that, any video you play, will have the cast icon at the top.  Keep in mind, you'll have to actually get the video working in full screen mode for it to cast.  So if you're doing porn for example.  Start the video playing, and it will then offer the icon.  If it has a full-screen button, you'll have to press that first.

 

Alternatively, you could just open the video link and share it with LocalCast, because it supports that as well.

 

I also suggest these flags, for speeding up scrolling and performance on mobile

http://www.androidauthority.com/chrome-android-scroll-lagging-363080/
