The 2010 one... that's the Reverse of Arcadia, isn't that right? I've got the 2011 one but the only difference is the number of available cards and the main storyline in the campaign mode. For me, I've always like the original series the best, so that style of dueling will always be close to my heart, but the new 5D's games incorporate a 3D engine implementing the graphics for the DS and use Synchron monsters... and there's also Turbo duels which is another form of dueling. But I guess the RPG part of the game isn't too bad in itself, in my opinion.
