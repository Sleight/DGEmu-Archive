There are other reasons as to why you would want to purchase the game.1) Unlike Reign of Chaos, In custom and BNet games, after your hero reaches level 5, you can't level anymore with regular creep killing. This helps noobs who ae just starting to play BNet and gives them clues as to how they set up their strategy.2) Upkeep limit was raised(i.e. 50 for Low Upkeep, 80 for High, 100 for max limit). This allows you to build a stronger attacking force without losing too many resources to upkeep.3) A Tavern system was set up for neutral heroes that can be hired by either side. So in the scenario where you an all out offensive strategy you can get three strength characters. Same applies for the cunning and mage heroes. Also, the tavern system allows you to revive heroes if your altar gets destroyed for a price of course.4) The new heroes implemented are more useful as well. For Night Elves, the Warden is awesome because of her ultimate spell. there have been many times where I find myself in the recieving end of a Demon Hunter spamming Serpent Ward as a rush offensive while they build up forces. The Crypt Lord is used many time for the Strength and spamming units in battle. Finally, the Blood Mage is useful with siege units due to Fire Strike, Phoenix, and Siphon Mana. Although there are many other reasons that make the game really good, such as storyline, new maps and the such, the four reasons above should pretty much cement your decision to purchase this game.Have fun!~Sparda
