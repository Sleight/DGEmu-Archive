I'm gonna have to recommend Dead Space. The atmosphere seemed a lot more creepy than Silent Hill. Something about being in space and having this fear of being sucked out into the vacuum or the isolation of it all seemed to really get to me when combined with enemies that were once human. If you want something fresh, go with Dead Space.
