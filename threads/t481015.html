KKirie MotobaMay 17 2014, 09:11 PM

Rooting is not the same as custom firmwares on PSP. Cyamodgen is though.

 

Basically, think of Linux: To be an admin to make changes to your system, you need to be the root administrator.

Same thing with Android: To make certain system changes, you need to be root administrator, but by default, that is disabled for obvious reasons. Basically, without root, you can install tons of stuff without problems (my current Xperia SP is not rooted).

But then when you want to install more advanced things that needs to dive deeper into your system (AdAway needs to modify HOSTS file to block things, any Firewall needs to have access to the network components to do filtering, Titanium Backup needs access to certain files to make copies of them, removing bloatware installed on devices since they are installed by default), then you need to be a root administrator.

 

So the process of rooting is basically patching your Android to be able to access these things, even if Google doesn't want you to. Rooting takes advantage of security holes to be able to pass the patch through. Once its done, you gain access, and you're fine until you update your Android OS with the newer version, that will reput everything back to default and probably fix the holes you used to root. You won't lose any data unless you wipe your system (required for some sorts of root, which is why it's important you need to have Titanium Backup in order to restore your stuff).

 

Basically, one thing you can do once you're root is that you're able to modify manufacturer installed applications. That means you can uninstall crap that let's say Sony puts on your phone when you buy a Sony phone, etc. What's cool is that with Lucky Patcher, you can patch the "Google Play" store to ignore license verification on some games, and also trick their refund system (when you buy an app, you download it to your phone, then you have x amount of time, and if you ask a refund, Google refunds you the purchase and uninstalls the app from your phone, but with cracked Google Play, they refund you, but you keep the app on the phone!) Doesn't work with all apps, but works with some (ie.: Dodonpachi Ressurection and Espgaluda I have in the DLC works with cracked Google Play, but they don't work on my Xperia SP. I bought them anyways now to support the company).

 

Also, Anti-virus is useless. I had one on my old phone, only thing it does is just slow down the phone when downloading stuff... As long as you don't go into the Google Play store and downloads random crap left and right (because yes, some Google Apps from the store are trojans), you're fine.
