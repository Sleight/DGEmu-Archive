MMo­lh.ORIGINAL POSTAug 15 2013, 02:43 PM

The Gateway 3DS, which can play all known 3DS ROMs on all 3DS models, has been released! While 3DS-mode (which the 3DS uses to play 3DS games) has not been hacked, the Gateway uses an exploit to play the ROMs on the 3DS. It is currently the only available way to play 3DS ROMs.

 

Here is a video of the Gateway 3DS playing 3DS ROMs on the 3DS:

[youtube]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uW3LxuDvHeI[/youtube]

 

Currently, the Gateway 3DS can only work on 3DS firmwares up to 4.5, and is unable to play ROMs outside the region of the 3DS used, but new units with 'firmware spoofing' (that will allow you to run ROMs with higher firmware requirements on the Gateway) and region-free features are rolling out soon. The makers are also working on Gateways which can work on firmwares beyond 4.5, up to 6.20.

 

In the meantime, please DO NOT UPDATE YOUR 3DS FIRMWARE if you want to use the Gateway on it. You can disable automatic updates by turning on parental control. In particular, do not update beyond firmware 6.2.0, whatever you do, as the exploit it uses will probably be patched after that.
