DGEMU COMICS

(W) C LUTHER

(A) R R JOHNSON

 

Hey, Richard!

 

It's going to be a pleasure working with you on this reboot. Druglordman and Shadow Luther have always been my personal favorites of the old DGC line, and I really think this is our chance to update these characters into something relevant, hip, and contemporary. I saw the work you were doing on Puma Man, and I really think you and me are going to mesh.

 

The idea of this reboot is that we're going back to basics: no more multi-year arcs that tie continuity into a knot and alienate new readers. At the same time, we want to use the past to enhance the present.

 

For character designs, I'll only provide the broad strokes; I'll leave the details to you.

 

For Druglordman, it's the basic, original idea from the Thirties: Zorro, with the cape and the hat, everything dark with brightly colored designs on the edges, and the swashbuckling attitude. In addition, the luchadore mask they gave him in the 70's redesign. It shouldn't be too elaborate or distracting: we don't want a redo of the cyberzerk mask that Liefeld gave him. It doesn't have any bells and whistles, it doesn't grant super-powers and it doesn't have a gun that folds out of it; it's just intimidating.

 

Shadow Luther, we want him to be very much the vigilante archetype. Black boots, black pants, black trench coat, black domino mask, and silver guns. I'm thinking Colt 1911's, but we can work out the details. The important thin is his stance: he's always aggressive, always slouching like he's about to leap at you. Druglordman can be chill; Shadow can't. We definitely want these two to play off each other. So in a fight, Shadow Luther just goes straight at the enemy, while Druglordman gets more strategic. Shadow destroys people, Druglordman gets people to destroy themselves.

 

Again, I'll leave the details to you.

 

Okay, enough talk: let's dig into this!
