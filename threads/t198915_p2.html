Q8Primary structure which is just the straight chain sequence of amino acids (polypeptide) bonded together via peptide bonds/links after the process of translation has occurred at the ribosomes.Secondary structure is where the protein chain begins to fold via hydrogen bonding. Some sections will form alpha helixes/helices or beta pleated sheets.Tertiary structure is where further folding of the protein occurs and gives the protein it's distinct 3D Shape. These can occur again due to hydrogen bonding or other types of forces such as disulfide bridges, ionic interactions, forces due to the hydrophobic and hydrophilic nature of some of the amino acids sometimes referred to as hydrophobic packing AND I guess Van der Waals forces too. They form a fibrous structure (such as collagen) or a globular structure (such as an enzyme).Quarternary structure where proteins in the tertiary structure will combine to form a larger combined protein (such as 4 protein subunits joining to form haemoglobin or the subunits making up an ion channel.)
