In ClosingWhy Report?Reporting quickly informs moderators of rule-breaking posts so that they don't need to search the forums to find them. When done correctly, reporting posts can make a moderator's job significantly easier than it would otherwise be. However, when done incorrectly, reporting can actually hinder the staff in effectively enforcing the rules. Respectable members in the community report rule-breaking posts/topics because they want to have a peaceful, productive message board just as much as the staff does. Members who respect the rules and the community are justly rewarded, and people who help out with quality reporting are no exception.Bad Reasons to ReportYou think that it shows the staff that you can be a moderator. It doesn't. It only shows that you know the rules and are willing to make an effort to have them enforced.You think that you are better than other members when you report a lot. Well, you aren't. Everyone that is in the group called "Members" is just a member, and how much they report does not change this. In fact, if you report for this reason, then you are displaying one of the exact attitudes that the staff does not like to see around the forums.You think that lots of reports directly leads to negs/a promotion. Negs (negative warns) are given at a moderator's discretion for a variety of reasons. Reporting may be a reason why one earns a neg, but that is only if the moderator appreciates your work and feels you should be rewarded for it. Several factors play into the promotions that are decided by the higher staff, and reporting is just one of them and not even the most important.Things to consider before reporting and/or replying to a rule-breaking thread:1. The Moderators don't care about your petty arguments. They wouldn't break out if you all would stop spamming in the wrong places anyway2. If you want to ask the staff a question, use the Support & Suggestions Section (be sure to search first though in case your question has already been asked). Your questions will always be answered there by either the board staff or a dedicated member.3. Don't simply reply just to say that a thread is a dupe. This does not help anything and does not contribute to the topic at all. It just makes you look bad.4. Also, if you choose to post in a dupe thread, remember to include the link to where you found the original topic. Most moderators will not close a topic until they know that the topic has actually been started before.5. If a topic is posted by a new member don't just criticize them for not knowing what to do or putting it in the wrong place. Instead, give them some advice so they know better or a link to the Rules and Regulations topic so they can learn what to do to not repeat the problem in the future.6. If a topic does not break any rules then do not report it. Reporting is intended to inform the staff that a rule was actually broken. This function is not to be used for alternative purposes such as trying to get members in trouble when they have done nothing wrong. Basically YOU could just end up getting warned. 7. Do not report any banned members. They are already gone so why do you need to bother the moderators with a report? It's not making you look good.8. Do not report posts older than 1 week, unless they are repeat topics: Many seem to like reporting old rulebreaking posts without realising that by one week, the posts would have been acted upon by a mod (1 week's the time limit given by TM24 in a support and suggestions thread sometime back).9. If you see a member breaking the same rule over and over again, please do not report every individual post. Instead, just submit one report and state in your report that the member has broken many rules in his/her posts (you can also provide links to those offending posts in your report).10. If you reported something a long while ago and there has still been no action taken by a moderator, then there is no need to report it again. The reports only disappear from the mod center when someone clears them. Either no one has been able to get to it yet or they did not agree with the report.11. You CAN report multiple posts within a single report under one or more of the following conditions: a. The posts in question are close together within the same thread (e.g multiple "overly short posts" in a poll topic) b. A member is flooding the board whereby you can report one of the threads/posts and claim that this member has been flooding. c. A member double/triple/etc posted their thread or reply.How To Use the Report Function:To report a rule-breaking post, simply click the Report button in the bottom left corner under the post and then a new window will open up. Check the appropriate report flag and type a BRIEF description of the rule being broken if necessary. Click "Submit Report" and it will go to the mod center. Always try and make your reports as concise as possible while still conveying the rule that was broken. The moderators don't need to be reading an essay on top of doing their job. Also, remember that the moderators were promoted to their position for a reason; they know how to do their job. You don't need to add additional things like "member should be warned" or "topic should be closed" in the reports. You only need to show what rule was broken and the rest will be taken care of by the staff.The latest improvement in the reporting system is the report flags feature. This makes it easier for both reporters and staff members, and even speeds things up for most people. Here is a picture of all the different report flags in the order you will see them:Here is a description of each flag and what you should consider when choosing to check it while reporting:Flooder: Use this check box for members who are continually posting spam that shows no effort in contributing to any conversation/topic and is only being made to gain points. In the text box, you may either put the name of the forum being flooded, or just say the entire board is under attack if that is the case. Also make sure to report flooders in the "Official Mass Spammer Report Thread" so that in case a moderator passes by there first, they can get to work quickly.Spam: Check this box for any topic/post that provides no discussion or makes no sense whatsoever. You can also use this to report "random vs. topics", which are topics that compare two things which have no relation or no business being compared. If you wish, you can use the text box to classify the type of spam at hand.Short Post: Generally, if a post is less than 5 or 6 words in length and is not a complete thought/sentence, it is considered too short for a point reward. Also keep in mind that if the post is merely a single noun phrase, like a game title, then it is considered a short post, even if the title is 7 words long. You generally do not need to use the text box for this report, but you can if you wish to report multiple short posts on a page.Other Language: If there is a post written in a language other than English that does NOT provide an English translation to go with it, then you may report it with this box checked. You most likely do not have not have to fill out the text form.All Caps: This is used to report posts made in all caps. No need to use the text box here.Wrong Forum: You use this to report a topic that is misplaced and should be moved. You may use the text box to post the name of the forum it should be moved to.Multiple Posts: Whenever you or someone else makes consecutive posts (double or triple posts, for instance), it is mainly due to lag. Check this box to report when this happens so that moderators can know to delete the extra posts and bring it back to a single post.Duplicate Topic: Use this to report a topic that is a duplicate of one previously done before. When you check this box, a new text line appears. Paste the URL of the ORIGINAL topic in this line so that the moderators can easily obtain it. You may use the text box on the bottom if you feel there needs to be further explanation as to why it is a duplicate topic.Oversized Image: Use this to report an image placed in a post with the img tags that exceeds the 50kb size limit stated in the rules. No need to use the text box here.Oversized Signature: Use this on any post (preferably in the General Discussions forum if possible so that all the moderators can find it) by a member to show their signature does not fit within the limits stated in the Profile Rules. As a reminder, the limits are: 450 pixels wide, 200 pixels tall and no more than 80 kb in filesize when combining it with the user's avatar. This also applies for multiple images all together.Advertising or Linking to other ROM sites: If a member is advertising a product or their site and does not appear to have permission, or if they are linking to sites that have ROMs besides DGEmu, then use this check box when reporting them.Offensive post of flame: If a member flames another or posts any obscene material in the post (including using cuss words that dodge the word filter and are clearly visible to everyone), then use this check box when reporting them.Off Topic Post: If a post has no relevance to the topic at hand, then report it while making sure to check this box.Asking for ROM or other item on the Rules: If a member asks for a ROM file or any other item listed in the "Stop Asking for the Following" thread, then report it with this box checked.Other: If there are any other rules broken in the post that are not listed in the other flags, check this box and use the text box to explain. You may also reported inappropriate usernames, avatars, or even profile pics with this. Please refer to the main forum rules to make sure you understand what else is eligible to be reported.ADDITIONAL NOTES:Remember that posting rules do not apply in the Spam Center, but all other rules do. If there are instances of advertising or another violation of the general rules in the Spam Center, they should still be reported.Posts may not count in the Hello/Goodbye, Praise the Dragon, and IBRPG sections, but significant spamming should still be reported there. Short posts don't need to be fretted about in those sections, though.There's no need to post in spam topics, especially when a member is flooding a forum. Just report one of the topics, and if they are flooding then point this out ONCE. Chances are that the member knows they are breaking rules anyway and you don't need to add further spam by posting in the topics.If there is a thread that you are certain is a dupe, but the original topic is in the Dump already, then there is no need to report it. Topics in the Dump are not eligible for such a case unless they are moved out of there.If a member is flooding a forum and you've reported that but there is no action taken by a mod right away, then feel free to make a post in the "Official Mass Spammer Report Thread" just in case a moderator capable of taking action happens to be viewing the Spam Center instead of working in the mod center. Don't worry if you made a wrong report by accident and do not PM the staff and/or make another report to apologize for that. From what I know, many members think that they somehow have to apologize if they have reported a non-rulebreaking post by accident. However, do not worry for even the best of members make errors sometimes and I assure you that the DGEmu staff will certainly forgive honest mistakes.How To Post In Dupe Threads And Topics:One thing to always keep in mind is that the safest bet for you as a member is to not post in a rule-breaking topic at all, but instead just click the report button and let the moderators do their jobs. If you are ever uncertain about a duplicate topic, don't even consider posting in it, because if your link turns out to be wrong, then you are only hurting yourself more.1. If you are going to post a dupe thread make sure it applies to the topic that you think it is the same as. If there is any doubt as to whether the topics are identical, do not post in the topic. The best bets for posting are in topics that you are 100% certain are duplicates and have not been replied to in a long time. Other safe instances are in common, reoccurring topics, like "Best [random console] Game" threads.2. If it is a new member that posted, tell them that it is wrong and why. Don't make them feel bad about themselves, because you were there once too3. It's a good idea to give the link to the Search Function anytime you post in a dupe thread. Then that member can find the answer to their problem/question without having to be further confused. Here is the search function link:Search: http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?act=Search4. Another good link to give to a new member is the link to the Rules & Regulations.Rules Link: http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=1602205. The best thing to remember about posting Dupe Threads is to be kind to people that don't know any better and to be thorough in your posts. Moderators will always pick up on that and it will make you look a lot better on the website.6. Do not threaten members with point deductions or warnings. It is not your place to act as a pseudo-moderator, so leave that job to the staff.7. Most importantly, ALWAYS follow up with a report after you post in these types of threads if for some reason you didn't before posting.In Closing:Always remember that quality beats quantity. Don't flood the mod center when it isn't necessary, but instead try to get as much work done as you can in as few reports as needed. Also, refrain from all instances of "mini-modding". Don't shove the rules down people's throats in posts, but instead just try to be as helpful and courteous as possible when you feel you need to make a post. Again, in most cases, sending a good report is sufficient, and an additional post is not needed. Doing a great job of reporting usually makes a member stand out and rewarded by the staff in some way or another. In many cases, these rewards lead to better conversion rates of GP->DP, but not always. Several poor quality reports or spamming the mod center usually leads to warns, and in extreme cases, a ban. Therefore, you should always focus on having good reports, not just "many" reports. It will pay off in the end. :) Credits:DJ Night Force 9 (for the idea of the guide and for revising it)Red Anvil, AWRyder, mohl394, Ody, and Falconer (for contributing some of the major points and doing some of the post help)Hadel (Rewriting the guide to handle "mini-modding" and the report form updates)</font>
