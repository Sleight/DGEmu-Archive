Wouldn't want to be the company that had the tech and then sat on it to leave the current system in the limelight, then have another company come out with a new system using tech very similar to what you were sitting on. Not only would the other company be stealing your thunder, you'd look like a copycat when you release your system.So, to the question, obviously I prefer the 3DS. If you already have a DS, then great, just sit and wait. If you don't, pick up a DS Lite if you want a current Nintendo handheld. I wouldn't spend the money on the DSi or DSi XL now that the 3DS has been announced and is expected within a year.
