I don't believe its the fan that has stopped the red ring of death, but actually an improved GPU. The original GPUs were notorious for overheating, and with the advent of newer, smaller GPUs, the 360 has fewer issues with overheating. The elite has the newer GPU's, which means they (theoretically) should not get the RROD. However, since its only been out for a year or so, we can only speculate on whether it will be an issue. And to Kickinthepants, I already noted earlier that intercoolers may actually make the RROD even more of a problem. Intercoolers may void the warranty, and may continue to fry the internal components even more (at least that is what the Nyko intercooler does). For all the people that think they have quick cures for the RROD: it's best to just send in the 360. Within the 3 year warranty, its free to fix, and its not exactly that long of a wait. All these "fixes" all have the potential to damage your system even more, and any other damages may not be under warranty.
