DdidgeballOct 14 2011, 12:58 AM
I prefer home-cooked food because, for the cost, 5-star cuisine really doesn't offer that much more. Sure, maybe the ingredients are rarer and the cooking quality (and occasionally recipes) better, but at a certain point, I just feel that I'm not getting my money's worth in basic volume of food. If both were offered to me for the same cost, I would likely still prefer home-cooked meal, partly because my parents and I both tend to under-season/under-salt food we prepare, and I like it that way more than I like over-seasoned food--which I find to be distressingly common for professionally-prepared food. Not to mention that under-seasoned food means that I can add random stuff to it, such as sprinkling parmesan and oregano on top of fried rice. I like trying out odd combinations. And if it's a meal that I've made myself, I get the satisfying feeling of accomplishment. Even if my food isn't as good. But then I know what I did, I know how it turned out, and I've learned something from the experience too. For example, I once learned that it's a bad idea to cook asparagus in soup, and I think that was a good learning experience even though the asparagus turned out to be extremely fibrous and hard to eat.
