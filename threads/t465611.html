How do you unlock more characters? I want people like snake and sonic. I tried almost everything but can't seem to unlock them.
BBash the GovernmentJan 21 2011, 03:43 AM
There are two ways I know of:1. Special Tasks. You can fight Ness to unlock him if you deflect 10 projectiles, with something like Fox's Reflector, or beat Classic Mode on different difficulties.2. Subspace Emissary. You unlock characters as you move along the line of the story. Once you beat Tabuu, you can go to the Forest as Link to get a door to appear early in the stage. This will lead to a fight with Toon Link, and unlock him upon defeat.In the Ruins level (the one where you go inside), there is a part where an elevator will take you down a shaft, and crystal jump out from the side walls. At the bottom, there will be a door. Fight Wolf O'Donnell and win to unlock him.In the Swamp level, after beating the big Diddy Kong shadow, you will be moving along a river. You will see the door very easily as you travel not to far down the river. Somewhere after the first fight where the screen locks. Fight Jigglypuff inside to unlock.As for Ganondorf, Wario and Bowser, make sure you pick up their trophies when playing Ness/Luigi/Dedede or Kirby.The rest should be automatic.REMEMBER: Toon Link, Jigglypuff and Wolf can't be unlocked in SE until Tabuu is defeated.
