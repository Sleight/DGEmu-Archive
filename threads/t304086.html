BBishAug 25 2007, 01:13 AM
I think it's alright. It's a fair enough SRPG, taking the battle system from FFTA and Tactics Ogre Knight of Lodis (and also Hoshigami, I hear). Character options do feel very restricted though; you can't change class and you can't recruit any randoms to your small private army. But everyone has a use.... except maybe Pollon. But of the 15 selectable characters, the other 14 are fairly well balanced.The story's... meh. You can see what's coming from some of the starting scenes. Although I thought there was a reasonable amount of character development, unlike something like FFTA. Oh, and there's alot of text. Alot. And there's no way to skip over all the dialogue, except to mash 'A'. =\
