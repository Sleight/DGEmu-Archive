For the Final Fantasy X thing, I suggest you get all of your overdrive gauges filled, for both the party members and the aeons. Then, save your data before you fight Seymour, in case you die, so you don't have to get their gauges up again. Anyway, start out with Yuna, Tidus, and Wakka, if he has Attack Reels, in your party. Otherwise, use Lulu or Auron. and summon an Aeon, and use the Aeon's Overdrive, which should be available. You should probably use Bahamut's Overdrive first, because with a Mega Flare, the Mortis should be gone. Then, Seymour will probably kill Bahamut. After that, just summon another Aeon with Yuna, and if it's Overdrive bar is full, which it should be, it should be able to use it's Overdrive and do some good damage. Repeat this method until Seymour is dead, or until the Aeons are all dead. In that case, Your two characters besides Yuna should have their Overdrives ready to use, and if you use those, Seymour should die. If not, you seriously need to level up your characters and get them stronger, because you won't get much farther. Basically, the trick is to use your Aeons as shields, and it works well. I hope I was able to help.
