Hey kumiko1. To uninstall NIS, you can simply go into your control panel and run the "Add/Remove Programs" in Windows XP or "Programs and Features" in Windows Vista. As an alternative, you can also download the Norton Removal Tool from Symantec's website and run it. You can get it from here:http://service1.symantec.com/SUPPORT/norto...&view=docid2. Depends if you want to purchase an AV or use one of the free ones on the Internet. I've installed many different AVs for clients at work, and surprisingly enough, this year, Norton Internet Security 2009 takes the crown on being a well balanced software, being the most lightweight Internet Security suite available on the market now and efficient. I was one of those that dislike Norton's software, but this year's version left me a great impression for them.If you're looking for a free one, you can either get Avast! or Avira AntiVir. They both work alright as basic protection, but be very wary of spyware and online threats, as they aren't too great in the protection department (false alarms, or detection rates not that efficient, etc)Hope that fills your needs.
