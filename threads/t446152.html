*Moved to "GameBoy Advance" forum*Your best bet is to get it in GSF (Gameboy sound file) format in order to ensure you have the absolute most complete and highest quality version of the soundtrack. Here is what you need to play it:1. Winamp (www.winamp.com)2. "Highly Advanced" Winamp plugin (http://www.caitsith2.com/gsf/)http://www.caitsith2.com/gsf/)3. The Mother 3 gsf soundtrack itself: (also found on http://www.caitsith2.com/gsf/)http://www.caitsith2.com/gsf/)4. Now you said you wanted to listen to it "on the go". In order to do that, you need to convert the gsf files to mp3 in order to put them on your portable music device. You can do that using winamp's "Disk Writer" output plugin which comes with Winamp itself (just be sure to configure it to create .mp3 files as it is set up to generate .wav by default). You may also use Jasper's file writer plugin too in order to create variable bit-rate (VBR) encoded mp3s for higher quality.Enjoy and feel free to ask questions if you have trouble setting up Winamp to play and convert these files (especially the conversion portion if you're not already familiar with it). Also, feel free to browse that same site for other GBA soundtracks you may want/like. There are tons of them.
