This list is incredibly hard to make especially since I haven't catch all of 2011 anime release (especially ones I'm having trouble watching). Still, here is what I came with. BEST ONGOING ANIME - Mobile Suit Gundam Unicorn For a series whom only released 4 episodes since last year, so many gundam fanatics and new comer to gundam is always hyped with waiting for the next episode. Best New Anime - Tiger and bunny This could be a bit biased for me who watches tokusatsu series such as the sentai series (magiranger, dekaranger,) and kamen rider series (kabuto, kiba, decade...)
