MmouseNov 07 2012, 02:02 AM
Sure.

They're dungeon crawlers, with randomized dungeons. Bit of a learning curve. Rather than leveling up your characters, you level up weapons. They have various attributes that make them stronger against certain types of monsters or certain elements, and you boost their stats by putting skill attachment things in them that get absorbed with each level-up. You can also break down weapons and put them into other weapons, giving a fraction of the broken-down weapon's stats.

There are various annoying things in the first one - weapons degrade over time and have to be repaired with special items, and your characters get thirsty over time and have to be given water. Like Hentai says, start with the first one. The plots are unrelated for the most part, but playing the first one makes you appreciate the second one more.

MmouseNov 07 2012, 02:07 AM
Oh, and the first game doesn't tell you a lot of things that might be useful to know. For instance...

You can find useful items scattered throughout towns as you advance through the dungeon. Every few levels, it's not a bad idea to look around the town and in people's homes for little chests.
There are a few cases where the order in which you place things in town make a big difference. For instance, in the first town, if you finish the old gaffer's store before you place your one friend's dad, the old man falls off his cart while putting on the sign and hurts himself, thus resulting in his store having a smaller inventory than what it would otherwise.
Not all upgrade paths are good, and only a few of them will take you to the highest tier of weapons. It's not a bad idea to look up a FAQ or something with a chart of the upgrade paths before choosing which course to take.


Also, yeah, if you focus on Xiao, she becomes so OP.
