Hum, a game that is only played in Brazil called "Truco", in which players receive three cards per round and with pairs (then 4 people) and they show their cards. The one with the biggest value wins (in a scale, from the weakest to the strongest, that is 4,5,6,7,Q,J,K,A,2,3 and the special cards seven of diamonds, ace of spades, seven of hearts and the Zap (4 of clubs). The cards 8,9,10 don't take part of the game). The big deal is that the player may say "Truco", and then the round pass to be worth three points. The members of the other pair may choose whether they accept or not. If they run, the pair that said "Truco" wins a point. If they accept, whoever wins the round gains three points. As nobody knows the cards of the other pair, it's not possible to know if the one who said Truco really has cards or is just bluffing. Another thing, after one pair says truco the other may say "Seis" (Six, in Portuguese), doubling the truco (making the round be worth six). Furthermore, the other may say "Nove" (nine) and finally "Doze" (twelve). The game ends when any pair scores twelve points.Well, pretty well explained, I think you can even try it out in USA, it's a really nice and funny game, maybe if you get interested you can search out on the internet for further explanations.
