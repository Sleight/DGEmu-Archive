I would compare them to Maxwell's equations: a pure beauty but trying to design a communication device yourself with just these four equations in mind is another matter...Oh, last thing before beginning to write: there will probably be some redundancy with the WARN FAQ, I'll deal with it later (just did it) and rewrite it completely (later).[center]-------------------------------------[/center]PRACTICAL GUIDE TO POSTING RULESDue to the fact that many members fail to fully understand and interpret correctly our rules, we decided to give you an understandable and practical version of what you can and what you can't do while posting in the forum and the consequences. Have in mind that rules are just COMMON SENSE so when you are in the doubt, just use some common sense of yours and everything would be clear.1. Starting a New TopicForbiddenSpam TopicsThis point is quite obvious. If your topic is like "lkgfgmfsjfghlk" with similar content, you WILL automatically be warned and your points deducted. Do it more than once, and ban will occur.Duplicate TopicsOne of the most recurrent mistakes in the forum. Many members fail to understand that we are a very big forum and that the topics they want to post are, most of the time, already posted by many others. So consider everything topic you want to post as done by default, especially if what you want to talk about is a popular subject. Then use the search functions and if you find nothing, you may consider posting it. Please read this topic for a tutorial on the search functions.Similar TopicsQuite close to the previous point, consider similar topics as duplicate topics. We don't need subtle variations on the same subject. For example, don't ever post a "Top 10 games on GBA": we have already a "Favorite GBA Games" thread where people can post they most, two most.... ten most favorite games. It's just pointless to have another of this kind. If you think your topic brings about an original point, please justify it so that we (moderators) can judge, otherwise, it will be closed automatically.Random Comparison TopicsEver read "Pokemon or Yu-Gi-Oh" thread on the forum? Yes, this kind of topics are to be avoided. Consider all VS topics are forbidden unless you can find an excellent reason to compare them and if so, please do post the reason, otherwise same consequences.Troubleshooting TopicsIf you have a technical issue with an emulator for example, don't post a topic until you read all the relevant pinned thread in the correct section of the forum. In most cases, the answer is already there. If not, post in a relevant existing topic. We don't need thread like "Help with X!" all over the place.Steps to FollowHere is the steps you should follow if a sudden urge of posting a new topic happens to you:Browse quickly in the correct forum to see whether a similar topic has been done (TIP: if the subject is popular, consider sorting the topics by replies or views).If nothing came, see if your topic belong to one of the previous categories above-mentioned.If still nothing came, then you may go ahead.If you are not sure, send a Private Message to a moderator.Choose CAREFULLY the title: it has to be meaningful and with no spelling errors. Titles like "help wit' da game!" should be thrown out from your mind.Write a meaningful message inside explaning why you think this topic is original and can bring about discussion.2. Posting a ReplyComparatively, it's much easier so that I recommend to any new member to start by just posting replies instead of new topics. Here is the list of recommendations:Always start by reading the first post.Highly recommended: read the other replies as well if the topic is not to big.When posting: make yourself clear, give as much details as possible and develop your point of view.Never post short replies.Never post if you don't add anything to the discussion.This list pretty short and should be easy to remember. If you failed to follow these recommendations, point deductions and warn will occur.Please keep in mind that moderators have the final word and their interpretation shouldn't be doubted. If however, you think you have been unfairly treated, just write a Private Message to the concerned moderator (or one of the moderators leading the section if you don't know) to explain politely and carefully your point of view. Ranting/Insulting/Flaming leads to warn and ban eventually.[center]-------------------------------------[/center]So any thought? Useful? Pointless? At least it should be more edible than the Forum Rules.
