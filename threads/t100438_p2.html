This is sort of like One Piece in that it's debatable as to who is the strongest due to the existance of so many candidates and groups, and a lack of conclusive evidence. So for now, I'd say the candidates for strongest are:1) Yamamoto Genryuusai:The 1st division captain and head of the Gotei 13, he was one of its founders more than 10,000 years earlier. Besides training, age naturally increases a Shinigami's soul power, so that automatically tells you that Genryuusai's a powerhouse. This is even more apparent when even before releasing his zanpakutou, his spirit power crushes Nanao (a vice-captain) and renders her unable to breath. Such a display of raw spirit power is unmatched by even Zaraki Kenpachi, even when both haven't released a thing and so are on even terms.From the way he took on Shunsui and Juushirou (two of the first captains who are ranked above the rest, especially when they fight together with their "complementary abilities", and the fact that both have paired zanpakutou) at the same time and overpowered them show how far above the other captains he is.His zanpakutou, Ryuujin Jakka, has been stated to be the strongest fire elemental, and the overall strongest attack-oriented Zanpakutou. We've seen the Shikai form so far, which looks more impressive than most of the Bankais we've seen, so you've got to wonder what his Bankai is like. 0_o;2) Aizen Sousuke:The extent of Aizen's displayed perfection so far is almost too stunning. He apparently has a vast wealth of spirit power, which clear when he can block Ichigo's bankai with his finger when all he's done is draw his zanpakutou. That's more than what we've seen from Kenpachi (always the benchmark for raw spirit power).His shikai, Kyouka Suigetsu, is very impressive. So far no weakness has been revealed. As long as Aizen releases it to his opponent once, that opponent would be trapped in "complete hypnosis" whenever it's released. The only thing is that the opponent has to be looking at it directly to be caught, but that's not an issue since practically all his opponents use their eyes. :) ; One possible weakness I can think about (not confirmed) is that the hypnosis is only related to how the opponent sees the zanpakuto itself, and not everything. The reason I think that's the case is because so far Aizen has always created the illusion out of the sword itself, and hasn't done any Tsukiyomi-like location switches. It keeps you wondering about his bankai, though. Will it be another support form, or will it be an attacking water form?
