PPaladin_AORIGINAL POSTApr 11 2007, 06:31 PM
I've noticed that if you change your GPs to console-specific DPs and then download a ROM from the console you just bought the DPs for, the system automatically deducts from your console-specific DPs first, instead of your Global DPs. For instance, if I have all 12.5 of my global DPs, plus 5 GBA DPs and I download a ROM that costs 6 DPs, the system automatically takes all 5 of my GBA DPs and only 1 of my global DPs. That might not bother a lot of people, but it does bother me a bit. What I'm suggesting is giving the members an option that they can set to choose whether the system takes points away from global DPs first or console-specific DPs first. This way members can build up DPs to download the game they want that has a 30 DP cost, while continuing to be able to download other games they want from the same console.
