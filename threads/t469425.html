Welcome to DGEmu! We hope you have a great stay here. To best enjoy the place there are a few things you should take note of:

1. Read and follow the Rules: And not just the Rules, but all the Forum Rules & Guidelines too. This is one of those forums where following the rules is actually important. It takes some time to read through, but it will keep you out of "trouble" and will answer the questions you'll invariably have about this place, including stuff about DPs.

2. Post in the correct (sub) forum. Read the pinned topics before you post. Make sure you don't post a topic unless it hasn't been done before. Look around the place a bit before you post, to get a feel for what sort of things belong where. Once you get to the correct place read the announcements and pinned topics, for the same reasons as point #1. Don't make a new topic if there's already an existing topic for it. Just post in the old topic, even if it's quite old ("necro-posting" is okay for discussion threads). Find old topics using Ctrl+F (the Search function is down) and search a few pages back before making your topic.

3. You might get GPs deducted, or even get Warned, for things you didn't know about. Especially if you didn't read the rules/FAQs/pinned topics. This is normal. When I was new I got Warned too, until I figured out how the rules work. Just learn from your mistakes and move on. Nothing will happen to you, unless you don't listen to the warnings made by the mods. If you break the rules repeatedly despite being warned about it you're gone, of course. Otherwise you still can stick around and enjoy yourself.

4. And that's it. Have fun!
