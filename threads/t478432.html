KKula DiamondDec 11 2012, 07:56 AM

There are some semi universal stuff, down forward + left punch is a launcher for most characters and LP+RP+Tag is a uneversal bound, but it's shitty and auto tag on hit, so yea, each has his own launchers/pounds moves. You can check each character's pound moves(green box). tag launchers(red box) and homing attacks(blue box) from the command list, but for regular launchers and wall break stuff you'd have to check yourself, training mode has everything you'll ever need.

 

If you want easy to use characters, Kuni is pretty straight forward, and after 10 minutes of practice you will never drop her BnBs. Leo, Jun/Unknown and Fing are easy to learn too.
