"Psyche" wrote:
The general definition of the term 'n00b' is ignorant fool. In the computer world, members in an online community are ranked by experience and activity in that community. The highest rank is Administrator (Admin), usually the owner of the website. There are many of these rankings, one of which is the rank 'n00b'. This rank is given the new members of the computer community, who become hated incredibly. Don't get me wrong, experienced members do not discriminate against the new, Every new person can have one of two ranks. They can either be a 'newbie' *someone that is new to something), or they can be the dreaded 'n00b' who is new to something, but also completely up themselves in everyway possible.Generally when you join an online community, you will join up with a forum. A forum is where you post your thoughts on topics and things to do with what your community is based upon, for all to see. Below I have made some guidelines for you to follow to successfully avoid becoming a n00b.1. Do not, at any point in time think of yourself as 'the best'. Not only will this lead people to think that you're up yourself, but they'll also realise that you're an annoying tool. Believe it or not, it is actually blatantly obvious to any logically minded person that no one is the best at something, especially if they're new to it.2. Experienced members are not your slaves. They will not do everything you command them to do every time, all the time. Hassling someone and having expectations of him or her to complete something just for you should never happen.3. All forum members need to understand what you are posting. You cannot post incoherent topics and replies. You do not need to be articulate and know long words' however, you have to post in English, with actual English words that should be spelt correctly (or close to it).Example:Incorrect, stupid n00b post:"omrg ur a lamo i dn lk yu i thk ur lame11111!one!!!!11!"Normal post:"I don't think you know what you're talking about"4. If you are part of a community where presentations are shown, such as an online art community, you have to tolerate criticism. There have been cases where n00bs have actually declared war against people who don't like their work (which was fatal to the naked eye). Not only is the n00b hated more, but also other members start to nag the admins for the n00b to have his or her account deleted instantly.5. If you have a disagreement with someone within the community, you can freely speak your mind. Most forums are there for you to post your opinions on things. However if you accuse the innocent for the wrong reasons, members of the forum will come down on you like ten thousand beavers on a wooden ruler. You be proclaimed a n00b and the admins will kick you the hell out.6. Questions are allowed; in most forums there is even a help section. Before you start asking questions however you must read the FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions) section. Posting questions already asked proves you to be ignorant and therefore very bad person. You should always make sure you ask relevant questions. Do not asks stupid and broad questions such as "How do I make a movie" It's obvious that to answer that a textbooks worth of words is required.7. All in all, if you're a n00b, people hate you for being disrespectful, ignorant and constantly looking for trouble. I leave you to go on your, hopefully, joyful experience with your online community with a final warning:Don't ever mess with Admins!
