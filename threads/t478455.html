From my new book series of fantasy, here's an except from Book 1, The Thrones Game.

 

Brun

 

Brun very much enjoyed being alive and having working legs. He would think about using them constantly. Walking, running, climbing, having sensory awareness of them. His favorite thing to do was to step in paint and make large, foot-printed posters that carried positive messages, like "I love my mommy and daddy and standing on my toes" or "Nothing is better than being able to stand".

 

Brun liked to climb, an activity that required his legs, which he loved dearly. As he climbed, he thought, If I couldn't use my legs, my life would be meaningless! And because he felt so strongly about it, he said it aloud! "If I couldn't use my legs," he said, while climbing a hundred feet high on a sheer tower wall, "my life would be meaningless!"

 

Suddenly, he came to the door, and heard something. It looked like the Queen and her twin brother! And they were having sex!

 

"Incest?" said Brun. "More like WINcest! Fuck you, Tolkein, you sexless jerkoff!"

 

"I know, right?" said the Queen's twin brother, whom she was having sex with. He kicked Brun off the wall.

 

"How tragically ironic!" said Brun, before he hit the floor and was instantly paralyzed.
