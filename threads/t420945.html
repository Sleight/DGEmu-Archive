READ BEFORE MAKING A REQUEST (KHRS 10/7/08)

KKHRSORIGINAL POSTOct 07 2008, 11:50 PM
Before making a request in here, make sure you read the Request Guidelines first. Any requests that do not comply with these rules will lead to point deductions and possible +10% warnings.1. Before making your request, Make sure it isn't in any of these topics: All NDS Roms Popular NDS Roms Nintendo DS Demos/Misc ROMs2. When asking for a ROM, PLEASE provide a link to the ROM that you want.3. When making your request, please adhere to the Forum Rules. Overly short posts or any posts that do not adhere to the rules will lead to a point deduction.4. Only request NINTENDO DS ROM's. This is for DS ROM's only. Don't ask for ROM's or ISO's from any other console/hand held.5. Be patient, if a person is taking a while to upload your ROM, it probably means they are busy or have better things to do. Just wait and someone will upload your request. DO NOT make any posts asking how long it will take.6. If somebody right before you requested the same ROM that you want to request, DO NOT make another post asking for the same ROM.7. Please be courteous. People are taking their time to do this, it's as simple as saying please. Please do not make any posts that say "Thanks" or anything like that. We don't need any extra posts cluttering things up.8. Only ask for DS ROM's that are ON DGEmu. If DGEmu doesn't have the ROM, then there's nothing we can do.9. Don't ask for emulation help. If you need to know how to set up an emulator, read this: NDS Guides and FAQs.10. Only request ROM's that are BIGGER than 15 DP's (15MB). We are here to help you with ROM's that are too hard to download. If it's something small and you can handle it yourself, then do it. We are here to help you, not be your slaves.11. Don't complain about how and where an uploader uploads the ROM. If you don't like the compressions format or the file-hosting site, too bad.12: DO NOT post links to other Rom sites. Doing so will receive an automatic point deduction/warning.Ok, after reading all of these, your request should look a little something like this:
someone wrote:
Can someone please upload Pokemon Diamond? http://www.dgemu.com/nds-details-1284.html Thank you.
And then an uploader will provide you with a link to your ROM.
someone wrote:
Here is your Pokemon Diamond ROM: http://rapidshare.com/files/132651038/_DGE...__U__Legacy_.7z
Anything that looks like this:
someone wrote:
Gimme Pokemon Diamond
or
someone wrote:
GIMME DAT GAME NOW!!
Will not be answered, and will result in point deductionsPOINTS were deducted for this post by KHRSPlease refer to the forum rules to find out why your points were deducted.Follow the rules and you will get your ROM.Thanks for reading! :smile1:EDIT by -Mario-: Added a rule, fixed some stuff.
