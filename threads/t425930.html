[center]For those with System Menu 3.0 - 4.1[/center]Tools Required:1. An SD card. It must NOT be an SDHC card, as the Wii doesn't support them.2. The BannerBomb install file.http://bannerbomb.qoid.us/index.old.php4. The HackMii install filehttp://bootmii.org/download/5. An SD card reader for your computer.Instructions1. If you have a private directory presently, rename it to something like "privateold".2. Extract BannerBomb onto your SD card, AS IS.3. Open up the HackMii install file and extract boot.elf and put it on the root of your SD card.4. Click the Circular Wii button in the lower left corner of the System Menu. Click Data Management, then Channels, Then SD. At this point, insert your SD card. At this point one of two things will happen. Either you'll get a dialog box ("Load boot.dol/elf?") or it'll freeze. If you get the dialog box, you win. Choose to load it and it'll run the HackMii install package.4a. If your Wii freezes, don't freak out. This means that the version of Bannerbomb you have doesn't work for your particular Wii. Try a different version from the website. Power down your Wii (hold Power for 5 seconds) to turn it off.5. When the HackMii installer runs, you'll see a scam warning message for several seconds (and no button presses work). THIS IS NORMAL. They installed it due to some scam artists. It'll tell you to press 1 in about 15 seconds.6. Choose what you wish to install, based upon what the program tells you you can. You do NOT have to install everything, but I highly recommend The Homebrew Channel and DVDX at a minimum. BootMii is secondary, but if you want to install it...go ahead.7. Reboot your Wii and remove BannerBomb from your SD card and restore the original private folder if you had one.

For those of you that don't wanna use BannerBomb for whatever reason.Remember to get The HackMii installer before doing these!http://bootmii.org/download/Indiana Pwns (Works on All System Menus):http://wiibrew.org/wiki/Indiana_PwnsInstructions taken from Wiibrew.org:Required materials * SD card (<= 2GB, not SDHC) formatted as FAT16 or FAT32. (The game, and therefore the exploit, can only read SD cards, not SDHC) * SD card reader * A LEGO Indiana Jones game that has been played at least once * Some homebrew to load (e.g. the Homebrew Channel installer) Step by step 1. Ensure your SD card is formatted as FAT. By default SD cards are formatted as FAT, so if you're not sure you can skip this step. 2. (Optional) If you have an existing Indiana save that you want to backup, do so before proceeding: 1. Put your SD card in your Wii and turn it on. 2. Go into Wii Options --> Data Management --> Save Data --> Wii 3. Find your Indiana save, click on it, click "Copy", and click Yes 4. Put your SD card in your computer, and copy the "private" folder from the card to a safe place. 3. Copy the "private" directory from the Indiana Pwns download to the root of your SD card. 4. Take your homebrew Wii executable (ELF file) and save it in the root directory of your SD card as "boot.elf". 5. Put your SD card in your Wii and turn it on. 6. Go into Wii Options --> Data Management --> Save Data --> Wii. 7. Find your Indiana save, click on it, click "Erase", and click Yes. 8. Open the SD card and select the "Indiana Pwns" save that corresponds to your game region. Note: Some people are having problems with the Wii not "seeing" the save file on the SD card. If you are experiencing this, try setting the archive bit for the data.bin file - in Windows this can be either be done from the file's properties dialog (right click on it in Windows Explorer and check the box) or from the command line using "attrib +a <path to data.bin>". More info at #wiihelp on Efnet. 9. Click copy and then yes. Now exit out of the menu. 10. Insert LEGO Indiana Jones disc and run the game. 11. Walk to the “art room” (find some walkthrough on the intertubes if you cannot find it), look at the second character. 12. Enjoy!Twilight Princess Hack (Works on System Menu 3.4 or less. Use LetterBomb to hack your Wii, in just a few minutes!

Instructions:
1. Find your Wii's MAC address: Select "Wii Settings" from the Wii Channel menu, then select "Internet," then "Console Settings."
The MAC address of the Wii console is displayed on the first line.

2. Go to the LetterBomb website, select your region under "System Menu Version", type in your MAC address, complete the captcha, then press either the "Cut the Red Wire" or "Cut the Blue Wire" button (doesn't matter which one). Download LetterBomb.zip.

3. Format your SD card using the panasonic formatter (format it to FAT32), then extract the contents of LetterBomb.zip into its root (i.e. not into any folder). Remove your SD card from your computer, and place it into your Wii.

4. Go to the Wii Message Center, and find the LetterBomb under either Yesterday or Today. It is a big red envelope with a bomb. Click on it.

5. If it hangs up, you might have selected the wrong region. Don't worry - just force-reboot it and start again. Otherwise the hackmii installer should run and install everything as per normal. I a scam warning message will appear for 15 seconds, and then the program will tell you to press 1.

6. Choose what you wish to install. You do NOT have to install everything, but The Homebrew Channel and BootMii are recommended.

7. Reboot your Wii, remove LetterBomb from your SD card, and restore the original private folder if you had one.
