Rrymu06Jan 07 2012, 11:21 PM
To be perfectly honest, Morrowind holds its own as far as games go. There are spells, like levitate for instance, that you CAN NOT acquire in Oblivion or Skyrim but are exclusive to Morrowind alone. The way the map used gridsquares to pinpoint locations was genius and the story was compelling. And there were almost no limitations whatsoever concerning enchantments; I could easily boost, say, my athletics skill to 100 on each piece of equipment I enchanted and leap from city to city, shore to shore (there is actually a set of scrolls that allow this!). Sure, Morrowind set the basis for Oblivion and Skyrim, but it is completely different in its own right, which makes it fun to play with still.
