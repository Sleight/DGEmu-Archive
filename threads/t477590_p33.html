Calvin Luther wrote on Aug 06 2015, 01:41 PM:

I know I'll regret asking, but what in the hell is a Danganronpa?

Visual novel on the Vita.  It's in the same vein as 999 and Virtue's Last Reward, but the ending isn't fucked.  In the first game, a group of students are invited to a high school for "Ultimates" (people that are the best at whatever they do) and one student (the MC) is the "Ultimate Luck" because he gets to go based on a drawing.  They show up, and end up getting forced to live there.  A talking robotic bear provides what they need to live and watches their moves.  Eventually he tells everyone they can either live in the school forever or one person can leave, but only if they kill one of their fellow students and manage to get away with it.  If they manage to kill someone and frame someone else for it, then they get to leave and everyone else dies.  If they get caught, then they die and the game continues.  And when everyone refuses to kill, different incentives are offered to speed things up (money, darkest secret being revealed, etc.)
