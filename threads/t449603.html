MmagpiefanJan 16 2010, 12:48 AM
From a fair bit of my own and others research, it seems a lot of the problems that occur with certain MAME roms is the compatibility between the rom and the version of MAME you use.I'm making the assumption that a particular rom that is downloaded is possibly developed using a certain MAME version. However when they are used on a newer MAME version some problems may be encountered. From my personal experience, there were a dozen roms that didn't work properly(some not at all) on the latest MAME, but when run through a 2003 version they work perfectly.If you have problems with any rom, I suggest you firstly try downloading it again as this is the simplest thing to try. THEN download an older version of MAME and see if the problem is fixed. You may have to try several before you get the right one. This is the only way you can almost guarantee a rom is definitely problematic.
