Calvin Luther concentrated on the ball. He had, to his knowledge, never played golf before, but the weight of the club felt familiar enough, and after only a few practice shots he found that he could put the ball just about anywhere he wanted it.He lined up the shot, glancing down at the target, then back to the ball. He went through the swing, slowly, letting the muscles guide themselves.Behind him, Shane coughed. "So, are we going to wait here all day, or are you..."He swung.The ball made a satisfying clink on contact, and an even more satisfying pmmph as it snapped the zombie's knee off. It toppled over, silently, and tried to drag its body towards Shane and Luther.Luther stepped towards the edge of the roof, and looked down into the streets. Groups of zombies lumbered around like particularly dumb cattle. He saw a little electric golfcart rolling through them, the occupants shoving the zombies out of the way with golf clubs.He heard Shane curse as she whiffed her shot. A second zombie was attempting to shuffle towards her. She whiffed again. "Hell with this," she growled. She walked up to the zombie, and swung her club at its waist. A cloud of dust poofed up into her face as the zombie fell in half."You know, maybe if you had a little patience, you wouldn't miss..." Luther said to her as she tossed the upper half of the corpse into the street."Maybe if you had my boot up your ass, we could've been done an hour ago," she snapped at him. He unceremoniously threw his zombie off, and chucked the rest of its leg with it."Just trying to have a little fun," he said, shrugging.Shane looked off into the distance. The island would have looked like a typical resort island to Luther if Luther had ever seen a resort island before. The resort compound stretched for a mile into the island, with swimming pools and water slides and golf courses visibile through the impeccably cared-for foliage. Behind some tasteful walls was the jungle, which Luther looked at with some trepidition. The jungle went on up to the volcano that sat in the middle of the island. Shane pointed at the top."So, how do we deal with that?"The mountaintop was shrouded in black smoke. Purple lightening crackled through out it. It was obviously evil.But more than that, Luther felt something up there. Something dark. Something heavy. He could feel it in the back of his mind, probing at him.He put away his club, and smiled at Shane. "How do we deal with that?""That's what I asked.""Simple. We find who's up there," he said, as he led her through the service entrance and down the stairs, "and we kill him." She didn't see him smile. "Simple as that."
