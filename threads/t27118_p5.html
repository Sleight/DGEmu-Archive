The game was ok. Honestly It kinda sucked because everybody built it up making it sound like the greatest thing since air and when I first played it was ok. but then it started going downhill. The story itself is new and good. The wolf concept I felt was WAY too boring. All you do as a wolf is run, bite, sniff, and dig they could have added some other things. But the thing that I thought were the worst was the dungeons. The first dungeon was VERY similar to the first dungeon in Majora's Mask. The second was similar to the fire dungeon from Ocarina. The third dungeon is 97% Similar to the water temple from OOT. After those you get 4 more dungeons. One of them is a big house in a blizzard like place. This was the most original dungeon in my opinion because you help the people IN the house instead of just going in the dungeon and start killing stuff. Another original dungeon was the last dungeon which was a place in the sky. It was HIGHLY original and the way you explore the dungeon is also original. You have 2 hookshot like items that you use to go around. you use the first hookshot to hold onto an oblect then use the 2nd hokshot to grab onto another object. Another dissapointment in this game was the items. The first item you get is the gale boomerang. That is an awesome weapon because you use it to fight,solve puzzles and you can use it inside and outside the dungeon at ANY TIME. However there are items you get that can only be used at certain places and certain times. One item is a spinner. this thing is dumb because you can only use it to go over certain surfaces and it has a "gear-like" design that u can use it to connect onto a track and scale certain walls and thats it. I only remember using it in the dungeons and once in the outside world to get a hidden item. another bad item was the dominion rod. It shoots an orb and if the orb hits a certain object it lets you control it. Now thats cool but you can only use it in like 3 or 4 places outside of dungeons. One thing I liked was that they brought back the fishing activity originally from OOT but now you actually use it for certain parts of the game. Another thing is that they had a technique system where you can learn sword techniques at certain parts of the game, similar to the one found in Minish Cap. There are alot of sidequests and extras in this game that can help you. There is a bug hunting side quest where you get paid to find bugs and a side quest where you can get a suit of armor that causes Link to not take any damage but eats up your rupees. Honestly I would give this game a 6/10 because I think they released this game too early and should have waited a little more and made it better.
