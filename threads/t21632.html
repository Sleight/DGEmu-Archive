Basic deck: I don't know what it is called but in Yukon Trail it was called Yukon. You draw five cards per player, randomly select first player. Player sets down one card and clockwise around the table everyone must place same suit or wildcard (jack). If a player does not have that suit he/she MUST use a jack. If that player does not have the suit or a jack, that player can put any card and lose all chance to win that round. Rankings of cards as follows: wrong suit, correct suit numerics, correct queen, correct king, correct ace, non-spade jack, jack of spades. Whoever wins that round picks up all of the cards and set in own winnings pile. Card values as follows: 2-9 = 0 pts, 10 = 10 pts, Q = 2 pts, K = 3 pts, A = 5 pts, ns jack = 10 pts, spade jack = 15 pts. After each round, each player draws another card.
