This topic is for sharing your opinion on the PSP. Please provide a full comment about it with some reason behind it. Otherwise, point deductions/warns will have to be administered._______________________________________I picked up my PSP today in the afternoon, came to about $350 as I bought the pack, a game, and an accessory kit. I set it up, and turned it on. Surprisingly, there were no defects as far as I could tell, and I did as much as I could to it in about an hour, without breaking it. Battery charges in about an hour from empty, and when playing a game at high screen intensity, it doesn't miss a beat.It comes with an intuitive OS, with 5 menus: Game, Video, Music, Photo, and Settings. If you load an UMD or media into it, an option will appear under each menu, and you can exit to the menu at any time, anywhere. You can also use its wireless to perform system updates, and it serves as a removable drive if you plug it in to a laptop, with a memory stick inserted.My only issue is that the 32 meg memory stick it came with is useful only for save games, and not for loading real music or media. I'll be picking up a 1 gig one later.Two more things:1. The instruction manual is *thick*. I was quite surprised, considering most of everything was intuitive, and the quick-reference card it came with sufficed.2. The earphones completely and utterly rip off iPod styling. No, seriously.
