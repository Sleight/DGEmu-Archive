Well, as long as you're doing it for fun and not really for profit (unless you're pretty much given the dead systems), then have fun with it.Here's a few things I can tell you about the system:1. The system's circuit board is entirely on the bottom half.2. You will need a special screwdriver, commonly referred to as a tri-wing. It's a screwdriver with a small "Y" tip.3. The top screen's connections go through the hinges to the bottom half.There are often a lot of parts for DSes out there, but they always seem to be so damn expensive. Look around on Google or eBay to see what you can run across. Oh, and make sure you have an exacto knife. That'll allow you to get to the screws that are under the bumpers on the top screen.
