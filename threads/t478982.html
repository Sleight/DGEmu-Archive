PPraecantatioMar 22 2013, 04:23 PM

The only way a DS lite can be connected to wifi is through the games that use wifi. 

If the game can be played online you'll probably find something like a "Nintendo WFC settings" button in the main menu where you'll find all the connection settings you need.

If you can't find a button like that the game probably can't be played online (or you just didn't search good enough  :P)

 

Anyway, if you're looking for a way to browse the internet on a DS lite, then I'm sorry to say that won't be possible. You'd need a DSi or 3DS for that...

Actually, to browse on the Internet on an original DS or DS Lite, it is possible: http://store.nintendo.com/webapp/wcs/stores/servlet/ProductDisplay?productId=129204¤cy=USD&catalogId=10001&tranId=0&lastAction=setCurr&storeId=10001&languageId=-1&categoryId=58694&ddkey=http:SetCurrencyPreference

 

But seriously, are you willing to fork over 30$ for a defunct web browser that can't even load Flash?

 

As for Wi-Fi, yes.

First, your home DSL should have a wireless router installed with it.

Your wireless connection needs to be secured with WEP as that's the only security supported by DS games.

 

If you really have no router, your other alternative is purchasing a Nintendo Wi-Fi USB connector and installing the software on PC here: http://www.nintendo.com/consumer/wfc/en_na/customersupport/downloadUSB.jsp

According to Nintendo, it's only workable on Windows XP and Vista, basically what it does is take your PC's Internet connection and emits a new wireless signal only for the DS consoles to connect to.

 

My opinion: Save yourself the trouble, go buy a wireless router, secure it WEP, and go. You'll be happy to know that spending 50$ for a router, you can also connect your laptops, ipods, phones, and whatever other wireless devices to the Internet.
