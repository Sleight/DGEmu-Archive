Sorry if this is construed as advertising. I'm trying to get a large pool of participants for this "event" to increase the fun exponentially for everyone. I'm not asking anyone to forsake a forum or whatever, you could participate in this only if you wanted. Yes, you would have to register at another forum (www.edgeofnowhere.cc) but, again, it's not like you'll have any commitment to it. This "event" is legitimately fun for any artist of any skill level. here is the original thread:http://www.edgeofnowhere.cc/viewtopic.php?t=385797And for those that may ask, I am Sabotage on that forum.Introduction:To keep in practice illustrators sometimes get together and create a blog, or contact each other in some other way, and do one sketch a week. They would rotate on picking the idea, so the first week one person would pick "pin-up girls" and everyone would draw their unique view of a pin-up girl. This could be anything from the standard sexy girl to a fat monstrous lady acting like a pin-up girl. The next week a different person would pick the topic and they would all draw that and so on. SketchAWeek works on the same principles. For this first week I will pick the subject. You're welcome to use any medium(Oil on cavas to MS Paint) to sketch your version of the subject. Complete the image to the best of your ability, use the full amount of time, PLEASE! There is no rush. You're welcome to post your work at 12PM(PST) the following Sunday. Similarly it doesn't matter if the work is not complete by that time, post what you have as it's better than nothing.There is no sign up. Participation is not required. Content:-Every week there will be a new SketchAWeek subject. The first weeks subject will be chosen by me, Sabotage. The weeks thereafter will be chosen by a user of my choice, at least until I find a better system for choosing or there are enough regulars that we can round robin.-Only final works (or as final as you can make them by the deadline) are accepted into the official SketchAWeek thread. WIP's and comments not pertaining to the final works, or otherwise inappropriate, posted in the official thread will be Imodded. This is up to my, Sabotage's, discretion. Most of you should know what type of banter I will and will not accept though I promise not to be overly heavy handed.-When posting your final work, though this isn't required, please provide some commentary with it. Where did your inspiration come from? What medium/tools did you use? How long did it take you? What do you like/dislike about it? What music did you listen to while making it? Why did you pick the colors you did? Things like that are very revealing and truly appreciated.CompetitionThough I'm not a competitive person myself I realize many others thrive on it. -At the end of every week the final works will be "judged". They will be judged for how well they get the subject matter across, presentation, use of the medium, etc. Who will judge?At first I, Sabotage, will judge. For this reason I will not include my own work in the judging, I'm happy just to do it. If I find anyone else who I think will do a good job in judging then they will help me. What do I have to do to become a judge?-You MUST participate REGULARLY. Even while judging.-You should have a generally good attitude, provide useful critiques, and be knowledgeable in at least one medium and at least know of others. -You must demonstrate fairnessThe decision to appoint a judge is completely up to me, Sabotage. A judge will get a tag and a special Avatar/userbar they can choose to wear. Rules:1) You're welcome to post your Works in Progress as separate topics but NOT in the official SketchAWeek thread. If you intend to regularly participate in the SketchAWeek, and thus regularly post your WIP, please limit them to one thread. A multitude of WIP threads from the same person will be trimmed.2) To reiterate the previous rule in other words: Only post your completed work in the official SketchAWeek thread. 3) Spamming and other extended conversation unrelated to the SketchAWeek will not be tolerated. Conversation can include the subject, the final works, and some general comments. FIRST SUBJECT:Robot of War- Why did you choose this subject?Everyone knows robots, especially robots used in war. You see them in Terminator, Mechs in the Matrix and other movies. I also wanted the first subject to be very open ended. the only stipulations are that it has to be robotic and the purpose of this robot must be war. You can create a myriad of things from that. An android with guns for hands, a mech with the firepower of 10 tanks, a battlefield janitor robot, the remote controlled APCs the army now uses. Anything robotic that could possibly be used in some theatre or facet of war. Not to mention it's easier for everyone to draw a mech if they are unfamiliar with figure drawing. Go nuts!SaW1 Start Date: Sunday, February 17th @ 12:00pm PST. SaW1 End Date: Sunday, February 24th @ 12:00pm PSTNOTE: This is the first SaW so there's almost 3 extra days for everyone to get started. You won't have this luxury for the rest of them so take advantage of it!________________________________SaW1: Robot of WarActive Judges: SabotageSubject Selector: SabotageTo be filled at end week:Best Creation:Honored Mention:Number of Participants:DO NOT POST ENTRIES OR OTHERWISE IN THIS THREAD. POST HERE OR IT WILL NOT BE COUNTED. Thank you and have fun!
