As I've said in a previous Guitar Hero vs. Rock Band thread, both have their ups and downs. Guitar Hero has the difficulty that whets the insatiable appetite of those who want it. Rock Band has the awesome group experience. My friends and I play both games, and we love them both. We don't really prefer one to the other. We just play Guitar Hero when we feel like a challenge, and we play Rock Band when we all feel like getting together. Sure, Rock Band's still kind of getting a slow start, but you guys try and get the licensing for all kinds of songs in such a short time. I've already been impressed with some of the stuff they've released, like the Boston Six Pack. I'll have to admit, though, alone, the Rock Band experience isn't as fun as it is with all your friends. That's when I turn to Guitar Hero. That's my opinion. Also; people complain too much about the $170 price tag with Rock Band. I'd say it's perfectly reasonable for the amount of hardware you get in there. Usually the guitars cost $40-$50 on their own. The drums already go for $80 (when they could easily charge at least $100 for them). The microphone, I'm not too sure...maybe $30 or $40? And the game itself, knowing the cost of new games in this generation, $60. Add all that together and you're looking at $210~$230. And now that the instruments have released as standalone accessories, there's even less reason to complain.
