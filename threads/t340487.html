Another fantastic way to get money is the clone glitch. I've tried this on several occasions it works trust me. If you exploit it too much the game has trouble handling it though and it tends to glitch and freeze. What you do is you get several of the same kind of magic scroll (doesn't matter what kind) but it has to be more than 1. Then press X two times on it and go to the item menu, then drop whatever item you want to clone. If you had 2 scrolls, and you dropped an iron sword, you will drop 2 iron swords instead of 1. If you had 10 scrolls, you'll drop 10 swords. See where this can come in handy? That 3000 gold enchanted ring you have is looking a lot more useful now isn't it? This also works for other scrolls so you can basically turn 3 scrolls into 300, and then use it on some alchemy ingredients or something to train with. Just don't do it to too many items because the game struggles sometimes. (it froze when I tried to make 3000 potatos ha ha ha) Hope this helped.
