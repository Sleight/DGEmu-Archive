Demon of Empire City' post='4958368 wrote on Jun 11 2011, 04:27 PM:
Ok, how the **** does blockign work?Why is my 9/11 creature keep being blocked by a 2/1 creature? My Spider is eing blocked by a Youthful Knight.He has first strike, but wy the **** does that matter?
If your opponent didn't attack with the creature before your turn, he has the option to use it to block your creature when attacking. In blocking, the enemy's creature will fully absorb the damage of your attacking monster. Unless that your monster has trample (the ability wherein if the creature attacking is blocked, the damage is dealt to the creature and the remaining damage is dealt to the player's life).Example, you have a 9/11 creature that is attacking. Then it is blocked by a 2/1 creature of the enemy. The result is the 2/1 creature will be sent to the graveyard.If your 9/11 creature has trample and is attacking, it deals 1 damage to the 2/1 creature, killing it and sending it to the graveyard, and you deal the rest damage (8 damage) to the player's life.
