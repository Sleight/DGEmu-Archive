In my opinion when it comes to choosing an RPG there is only one developer worth their salt and that is Bioware. Develoers of the classic Baldur,s gate its continuation into Neverwinter nights they realy know how to immerse the player within their world. Their only offering on the xbox however are the KOTOR series and Jade empire.KOTOR is and outstanding game still held highly in the opinion of game journalists and the like. With the recent release of mass effect the paralleles are constantly being drawn, if you feel you are missing out on one of the best RPGs of the year with mass effect then KOTOR is a must buy. In my opinion you cannot play the second before playing the first but others will say differently.Jade empire is far less in depth than Bioware's usual work but is still a beautiful game. Detailed leveling systems and heavy degrees of multipath and customisation are partially done away with in favour of a strong story telling experience. I thoroughly enjoyed my playthrough of the game but was not however compelled to replay though the game. If you are looking for a strong fantasy based game i would reccomend Jade Empire, if however you are looking for the best RPG on the Xbox i would go for KOTOR.
