[font="Book Antiqua"]Sauron, known in LOTR as The Dark Lord/The Enemy.etc, is a powerful and malignant spirit (called 'Maiar') who is the main antagonist of the series. It is he who forged the one ring in Mount Doom, and also he who exists in the books, only as the spirit tied to an eye of flame, peering over middle-earth from the top of his fortress in Barad-dur. He is destroyed with the One Ring, after it is dropped back into Mount Doom.Saruman is also a 'Maiar', a spirit of huge power, second only to the god-like 'Valar'. He came to Middle-Earth as the leader and most powerful of the Istari, human-like wizards who were sent by the Valar to fight Sauron. He was custodian of Isengard, and Lord of Orthanc (the black tower) for a long time, before the Ents defeated him; He refused to repent, and from Saruman the White, became Saruman of the Many-Colours. He was finally killed by his servant Grima Wormtongue.[/font]
