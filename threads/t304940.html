SsaifeemonAug 03 2008, 10:27 AM
It is better to give some time playing and retrying by yourself to achieve A-Class license. It is tough to get but i bet by each retry, you will discover a new driving skill. And of course, best drivers dont copy others' driving secrets. The most difficult thing is cornering and it will become easy after you manage to make good turning angles. It can be demonstrated like this: corner image:http://img3.orkut.com/images/milieu/1/1217...jpg?sig=1o1lynt You can follow path A->B->C->D.Regarding when to brake and when to accelerate, different people have different opinion but according to what i do both in game and in real it is like this:A= At this point apply brakes according to the deepness of the corner.B= Stop braking and dont accelerate until you turn and are about to reach position CC= When you are about to reach position C, start accelerating plus turning (for turning dont TAP directional buttons, just hold them) and if done correctly your car will reach D position without applying further braking and turning.D= Stop turning and start increasing your speed (as you are accelerating already)NOTE:-B* position is NOT CORRECT!! Many people think it is correct path but of course it is not. If you will follow B* path instead of B, the you will eventually get out of the track from outside region of D or you have to apply more brakes to manage to get to D. You can say that if two drivers are follow B* and B path then if both drivers manage to remain on the track, then driver using B path will go ahead.
