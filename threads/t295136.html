CchugbugSep 01 2007, 12:07 PM
On the under side of your Xbox there are some little rubber pads that act as feet. Peel these off to reveal some screws. there also some screws under the sticky labels, simply feel around untill you can feel a hole then stab through it with a screwdriver. Unscew all these screws completely and take them out. Turn your xbox upright again. The whole top should now lift clean off. Now disconect the CD reader and the hard drive (the to large boxes on top). This may require you to undo some more screws. You also need to diconnect the cables so that they are totally free from the rest of the xbox (this is a very wide thin cable, looks like a belt). Unplug what you must. Now either blow the dust away with an air jet or simply brush it off. Clean it as much as you cam, including the ports where you have just unpluged the cables from. Now reasemble it and you xbox should now load faster and recognise discs better.WARNING: If you do decide to dismantle your Xbox, it will void any waranties you have on it and you might have trouble when trading it in to a shop (if you do).
