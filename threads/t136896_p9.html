This set of Pokemon games right here is where I make my stand. I've been playing Pokemon since the card game came out and now it's just ridiculous. This franchise doesn't need better graphics or better animations or more Pokemon to waste my time pursuing 'cuz I'm a completist.IT NEEDS A PLOT! A STORYLINE! Hey, it could use a few characters!Look at the show! Every day Ash Ketchum has some new adventure, and even if it's not the height of entertainment, at least they try to make me give a crap about the persons and monsters involved! Ash collects friends, Monstrous and otherwise, and bonds with them in a semi-meaningful way!But the games have a story that can be summed up Thus: Wake up in tiny hometown. Procure a Pokemon. Battle Rival. Walk. Battle random meaningless passerby. Procure another couple of practically interchangeable and faceless Pokemon. Go into gym and challenge equally interchangeable and faceless Gym leader. Heal team when needed and repeat till you caught 'em all.Barely a paragraph.I love monster-catching\raising games. Digimon World and Monster Rancher are among my favorite games of all time because they have the one thing Pokemon lacks, the one thing that I think keeps it from DESERVING it's seat atop the throne of Pocket Monsteresque games. A soul. I cared about my little digital companions as I stomped around File Island in Digimon World, there was a time when I was young and I would cry just a bit when my Tigers or Golems or whatever died back on the original Monster Rancher. But you can't even come close to that in a Pokemon game. Your Pokemon are interchangeable, if you put your charmander in Bills PC for the entire game he won't care and neither will you. If he gets traded for another, stronger pokemon, there isn't any sentiment or emotion involved. You just go catch another or go trade for another or whatever.Heck, let's leave the Pokemon alone. Maybe it's best that they're so redundant, at least tiny chilluns ain't gotta worry about feeding them or anything. Maybe having a main character with more of a story would help. So he\she wants to be "the best". Why? Is their father "the best", and they want to surpass him? Is the current "best" an abusive tyrant, using his god-awfully strong Pokemon to subjugate and torment the helpless masses? Take your pick, cuz it's BYOS (Bring Your Own Storyline) day whenever you play these games. Normally, when games don't give you a particularly impressive backstory, it's because you're kinda creating it as you go. The unimpressive hero or heroine you start out with learns and grows and laughs and loves and has zany misadventures and stuff. Not so here. Hi, I'm Red\Blue\Ash\Gary\Whatever and I wanna beat the elite four or a similar organization and be the best. That's all the story you get. It's depressing, as with 500 or so lovable if somewhat stupid Pokemon you could really get something out of this game if only there was something to set my adventures apart from everyone elses. But there isn't. I know that every time I catch a Pokemon, someone out there has the same exact creature, same stats, same name, probably caught it in the same patch of Tall Grass.It wouldn't be hard to remedy these issues, really. I'm sure the boys and girls down at The Pokemon Company could write SOMETHING decent, in the way of a story at least. But they probably won't. And so I hereby swear off mainstream Pokemon titles until they at least make an attempt at giving the monsters in my pocket a personality beyond one word randomly decided at the point of capture (ooh my Charmander is CALM! Is your Charmander CALM??!?), or making the inevitably spikey-haired protagonist at least mildly interesting. In the meantime, Pokemon Mystery Dungeon (and any sequels to it, hopefully) will have to do. Hope you enjoy Pokemon Red & Blue V4 people.Good night, and fine gaming to you.
