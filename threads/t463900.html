I don't usually get sleeping problems during class as most of my professors are intriguing, active, and just overall fun.But, every semester there's always at least one boring person and here are the various ways I've found to keep myself awake.1: Eating beforehand. If a class is boring, it's a lot easier to remember if you haven't eaten in a while and then you'll be thinking about it,getting hungry, and getting distracted (and possibly sleepy).2. Writing notes. It can help if the teacher, not the subject itself, is boring. If the subject is boring, even taking notes might not help.3. Computer. Playing games on the computer, or watching subtitled videos, can definitely help. I had a music history course where I spent 80% of the time playing solitaire, minesweeper and sudoku.4. PSP. I had a professor that didn't allow computers so I'd put my notebook is such a way that it'd hide my psp and I'd just watch subtitled Death Note videos on it.5. Daydream. Class is boring...Time to daydream and pretend i'm someone else. Alternatively, thinking about a game strategy for a game I'll play when I get home or how to go about doing another homework works just as well.6. Not going. I know high schoolers can't do that, but here at university a lot of professors don't require attendance. Sadly, most boring professors I had did require it (because then no one'd come...), but sometimes I got lucky and one didn't care.7. Doing homework or some other written work (fanfiction perhaps?).
