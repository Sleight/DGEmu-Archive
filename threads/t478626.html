KKirie MotobaJan 16 2013, 05:55 PM

Just a Core i5 is like triple the price.

 

Also, between a Pentium G and Core i3, the i3 would've been a better choice for about double the price.

Celeron = 2 cores, 2 threads, no turboboost, low cache.

Pentium G = 2 cores, 2 threads, no turboboost.

i3 = 2 cores, 4 threads, no turboboost

i5 = 2 cores, 4 threads, with turboboost (and higher i5 models are 4 cores, 4 threads)

i7 = 4 cores, 8 threads, with turboboost (in sandy bridge, you also had i7 extreme with 6 cores and 12 threads, that's what I have on my PC)

 

G860 3.0GHz = 64.99 CAD

i3-3220 3.3GHz (On Ivy bridge, better than the Sandy bridge at same price) = 119.99CAD

i5-3470 3.2GHz turboboost to 3.6GHz (Ivy bridge) = 189.99CAD

 

Debate between G860 and a possible AMD Phenom II X4:

- Everyday tasks, the G860 takes it.

- Gaming, depending on which games, mostly G860 takes it (since most games aren't optimized for multi-core support)

- Encoding videos and audio, advanced Photoshop, other multi-threaded applications, AMD Phenom II X4 takes it.

Actually, Intel is worse.

 

You chose the LGA1155, which is ok for 2nd gen and 3rd gen core series of Intel.
