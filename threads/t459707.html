SshrmJul 28 2010, 02:46 AM
Are you going to be writing a snake game with some of these ideas?Maybe special items that could be picked up and held until the player decides to use it. Items could grant a slow-mode for, perhaps, 5-10 seconds, maybe a ghost ability or something that allows the player to pass through the snake's tale, maybe another type would allow the player to bounce off the next surface to be hit.Maybe place a 1x1 block permanent obstacle for each special item the player uses. A cost for the benefit of the item that the player will have to contend with even after the effect of each item has worn off.Here's another one: give the player 2 snakes to control, both controlled at the same time. Whenever the player makes a snake turn, they both turn the same way at the same time. This would only be challenging if there are 1 or more obstacles set up on the 2 halves of the field, but the 2 halves cannot be match. There has to be a difference to force the player to keep track of obstacles on both halves.
