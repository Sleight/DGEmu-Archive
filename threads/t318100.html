There's another phenomenon I would like to point out: the "MYST" one. When people think of adventure games, they think of monkey island and myst. Myst being a really complex adventure game with more complex puzzles that challenges the player to look for clues as to how to solve it or let the player figure it out themselves. What's bothering about myst is that it sometimes require you to read tons of short story length texts that cramps the momentum. Though awe inspiring in visuals and story, it will leave the average player slumped at the fifth crack at the first puzzles. I guess that's what people see in adventure games before, complicated. Before the gamefaqs era, most gamers want to progress through the game without any difficulty which is why the "doom" phenomenon was huge.
