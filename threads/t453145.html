For the last beta that ended on Jan 10th, I had an opportunity to play a couple days.I had a pretty fun time playing it! Although each game involves maximum of 64 players at one time, the server was almost free of lags.I didn't get to see any of the heavy military vehicles being used that I saw in the trailers, but just playing sabotage missions were pretty fun.In COD MW2 multiplayer modes, it just feels like a multiplayer for the sake of multiplayer. And leveling up does not mean much, really.In MAG, I felt compelled to level up as fast as I could, because the perks you get as you level up will obviously improve your performance, and spending 2 level up points (you only get 1 point to spend per single level up) to get a high-end sniping scope feels like real investment.Anyone else played MAG?
