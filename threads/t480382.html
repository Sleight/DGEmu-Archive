MBNA will start hassling you and offering you ton of promotions that are 90% of the time always around, especially during holidays:

6 months equal payment no interest, 1% transfer fee

1 year equal minimum payment with 0.9% interest, 1% transfer fee

 

Make all your necessary purchases on all high interest cards.

Once the time comes to pay them, call MBNA 1 week in advance to call in a balance transfer. Explain how you're a good client and they will offer you the 1 year payment at 0.9% interest and waver the transfer fee.

The balance to be transferred should be roughly the total spending of your card - the minimum payment.

Pay that minimum payment to your high interest card before the due date.

 

And that's it, not only have you had a nice month to accumulate on the card, you now transferred to another card with nice minimum payments for a year which you'll hopefully be diligent enough to focus on paying.

And if EVER by bad luck you can't pay (hopefully not), apply for a line of credit at your bank, they can easily offer you something with like 4 to 5% interest, so again, before your 1 year plan finishes, make the minimum payment and transfer the balance to your line of credit which will have a lifetime 4 to 5% interest for you to slowly pay it off.
