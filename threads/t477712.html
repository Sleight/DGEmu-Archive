Okay. I really liked Lord of the Rings, that was a good one, but I hated the cartoon version by...uh... Ralph Bakshi I think it was. That movie was terrible, seriously. All of the characters were Rotoscoped, which I don't have anything against if it is done well, but in this you could see through it and it was quite annoying in some cases. Then there is the fact that they stop the story at a really stupid part. Why would you stop the first movie halfway through the story of the second book? Seriously.

Then there was Charlie and the Chocolate Factory. I loved the book, but the movie adaptation was kind of meh. You know, the Gene Wilder one. I am prepared for flak with this one, but I didn't particularly like how they changed some of the things that happened. I'm really nitpicky when it comes to that, though I did like the Chocolate River ride for some reason. I just hated the Gobstopper subplot though. They looked really stupid. Why would you put such a thing in your mouth? It didn't look like it could actually fit in there. Also, it doesn't make any sense. I know it's supposed to be for children, but why couldn't that fake Snubnose or whatever his name was wait for the Gobstoppers to come out on sale?
