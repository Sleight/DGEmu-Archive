RRaymondMar 19 2011, 11:53 AM
The idea of having a PSP is so that it can be a PSP. Not a controller for the PS3 or an extra screen. Yes, it would save you some money on buying another controller, but its got its limitations. There is no R2, L2, R3, L3 and the whole left stick. Connecting the PSP to your PS3 and using it as an extra screen would cause much more problems on the PS3. Sending another video output to the PSP, with it being converted so that it looks viewable on the PSP would cause too much strain on the PS3 itself, hence creating frame rate issues. There is no point in playing PSP games with your PS3. You can use a second controller for that. Also most PSP games are different versions than the ones on PS3. Playing your PSP games through your PS3 would be ridiculous. You can already plug your PSP to your television, wiping out the factor that you need a PS3.
