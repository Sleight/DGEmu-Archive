This includes all of the US series except for the US GI Joe crossover. The movie adaptation is in continuity. "The Big Broadcast of 2006" is a story made up by Wreck-Gar.Cartoon (US): The four seasons of the original cartoon and the animated movie (which takes place between seasons 2 and 3). A 5th season that consisted of earlier episodes with new transition sequences featuring Powermaster Prime was also made.Cartoon (Japan): The first Japanese Transformers series: Fight Super Robot Lifeform Transformers, was dubs of the 1st two seasons of the American cartoon.
