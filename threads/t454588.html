GGaldion the wiseFeb 05 2012, 09:38 PM
For your zombie guide, zombieland has some really good pointers about surviving a zombie holocaust. Fortifying your house and live the rest of your life inside, may not be a good plan if the perimeter is covered by a large groups of zombies. It would be smart to have options of being able to make a fast escape if in case your fortified house has been invaded. Living beside a beach would be a good option. You'll have the option to escape by boat and have a steady supply of food. If you live near a lake, you'll have everything above including a steady supply of fresh water.Ammo in this world will be a highly coveted commodity. I would trade a carton of ammo for ten people who are very good with a gun (Think about it, what would a gun expert do against a room full of zombies without a GUN). Anyone who has functional fingers, eyes and ears can shoot. Unless the situation calls for it, I would then start shooting up zombies. Chainsaws, despite gaming and movie reference, is not something I would like to carry around. It eats up fuel which could be use for transportation or for running generators. Improvised weapons would be sufficient. A steel pipe with a sharpened end can be used as a makeshift javeline or be used for traps in hunting for game. Gathering survivors or doing the humane thing is without its risk. You will be adding up the mouths to feed which will shorten the span of your food supplies and you may risk a breach in your fortified base if one of them is infected (This is if the disease is spreadable via blood contact). Though added man power is very crucial when gathering for food, defending your base and other kinds of work. Medieval armors or steel armors........ is not a very smart commodity unless you have time to train yourself in moving around carrying all of that weight, even then though it's still not a smart commodity. When faced against a group of zombies, your best option is to be able to out run them and out maneuver them while taking them out one at time. Being in an armor destroys that option. Yes you will be able to withstand their bites and lashes, but they can easily overwhelm you while wearing the armor and pin you down (imagine being crushed by steel and 20 people) which will make it easier for them to remove your armor.
