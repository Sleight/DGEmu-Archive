SshadowmanwkpDec 21 2011, 01:15 PM
If you're more of the classic rpg fan, you can also look into final fantasy V and VI like dark dragon suggested. Here's a breakdown of both games: V is interesting because of the job system, it lets you build your own characters and it lets you choose their own abilities (like a knight that can use magic). The also features some very good music, however because of the job system, your characters are pretty much cardboard cutouts (however, the villain gilgamesh has some enjoyable quotes). VI on the other hand has a much better story and fleshed out characters, especially the villain Kefka is awesome. Personally I would recommend playing these remakes on a snes/psx emulator. The colors of the gba versions are very bright because a gba screen is very hard to see, the snes and psx versions of the game do not have this limitation. You can also try demikids (a shin megami tensei spin-off) and robopon (think pokémon with robots) these are monster training RPG's like pokémon and from what I've heard are very enjoyable (played half an hour each, so I can't really give a good opinion on the game). Beyblade doesn't really fit well into a genre >.>" I really liked g-revolution, but there isn't really a similar game. I however, dark dragon's recommendation of summon night swordcraft story is a good one. SN features an unique battle system in which you can carry three weapons into a battle, that are broken down in five categories, you make these weapons yourself, so you also need to find these materials in dungeons/the field. The second game is much better than the first, but both games are very enjoyable nevertheless.
