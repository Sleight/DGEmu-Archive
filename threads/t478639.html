The Opening

 

So, at first I was amazed.  The opening cutscene, though brief, is beautiful.  The voice acting is not all that great, but it's fine.  Nothing too bad.  The dialog seemed pretty standard stuff.  Seems more JRPG than FE at the beginning.  You start out by creating your avatar, and while I'm not a huge fan of creating a main character (I prefer someone with a bit more substance) the MC you make actually does speak and play a role (apparently).  He speaks quite often actually.  The character customization was locked though, so basically all you get to do is pick male or female.  Interestingly, you get to pick a strength and a weakness for you MC (he is a tactician as usual).  You can choose if he will have a buff to HP, Str, etc and which will be his weakness.  You cannot pick both to be the same thing to cancel the effect out though.  As I said earlier, the beginning cutscene is really lovely and the character models for in-game stuff are nice.  Nothing spectacular, but this is a very lovely looking game.  It won't blow your mind, but it pleases the eyes.  The 3D effect isn't useful in this opening part, but the depth added is actually pretty significant.  I guess if you want to spice up your FE, it's a nice addition.  Your also allowed to pick a difficulty (Easy, normal or Lunatic right off the bat).  You are not, however, allowed to play classic FE style.  The demo forces you to play with the option to revive fallen characters checked.  There will be a choice though, for those that want to go the old school route.  With the option that is used in the demo, defeated units will be revived and you can save at any time.

 

 

The Gameplay

 

So, it's basic FE fair in the first prologue mission.  One major difference this time around though is that the Tactician you make actually fights in battles.  I didn't know this beforehand (I didn't read much about FE because I didn't expect it to come overseas at first and then I kinda forgot about it until the other day).  The tactician can use magic and weapons and of course your buff and weakness you pick at the beginning will likely decide which you use the most.  The little battle sprites are nice, though the 3D effect makes it difficult to discern what class they are at a glance.  They are slightly blurred, so you will probably have to actually scroll over them if you use the 3D.  The sprites are tiny btw.  Like super mega tiny.  No nice big characters on the field like in the Wii FE games or even the GBA ones.  You start out with a Cleric, Great Night, Tactician, and a Lord.  I picked Str for my buff on my Tact, so his skill with a blade beat his magic abilities.

 

When you're playing the game it plays just like a regular FE game; however instead of Rescue, you can Pair Up, which allows 2 characters to occupy a single space and it seems that one character is basically "in charge" while the other is there to provide buffs and possibly block a hit if the main person can't take it.  For instance, if you select the Cleric and Pair up with the Great Knight, the Cleric will be in charge, but the Great Knight will provide buffs in battles and block hits for the Cleric.  If you Pair up the Tactician with the Lord, the Tactician will be in charge and he will do the attacking while the Lord sits tight and gives you buffs (like +10% hit).  However, you do not have to be paired up to receive these buffs, this is just the easiest way.  When characters are standing side-by-side, the same effect is received.  When a battle is initiated, the character that is adjacent to yours jumps in to the fight and provides backup as if you were paired.

 

My only problem so far is that I actually died in the first mission.  Twice.  There is a magic user that has a 2% chance of doing a critical and he managed to crit me twice in a row.  It is extremely frustrating because the demo restarts completely when you die. (I am playing on Hard mode for the record).

 

 

 

 

Conclusion

 

I'm really enjoying the game.  Besides the frustration of extremely lucky enemies (which would be solved if I just sent my Great Knight in (as he seems to be the Marcus of the bunch at this point in the game), but I don't want to.  I want my MC and the Lord to do the work, so I supposed it's just stubborness on my part.  Anyways, the game seems really spectacular and I am absolutely ecstatic to buy it when it's released.

 

EDIT:  It seems that when you are paired up you can actively switch which character is in charge.  I am having my Great Knight chauffeur my Cleric around; however, when I need to heal, I simply move them into place, select "Switch" and it puts my Cleric in charge and allows her to heal.  That being said, once you perform an action, that leaves your Cleric as the character that is open for an attack, so obviously there is a risk to de-shielding her.  Of course, the Great Knight will block attacks for her if they are paired together or adjacent, but I do not know if that happens 100% of the time as the enemies have only attacked her once and the Knight did step in that one time.

 

EDIT2:  Also, on the battlefield there are areas that are shiny.  They basically sparkle.  When you place a character on these spaces you seem to get random rewards.  I do not know if an enemy can steal them from you or not, but so far there are 2 in the demo chapter.  One raised my weapon level and another gave me 16 EXP.
