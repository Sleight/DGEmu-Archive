Here's the thing about Metal Gear Solid: the story is absolute shite. If you disagree, I'm sorry, but you're wrong. The stories in these games are convoluted, confusing, constantly retconned, and 100% bullshit. If you've ever wanted to play an MGS game because you want to immerse yourself, you're a fucking moron and you should write a post defending yourself so I can ignore it.

 

The story goes a little something like this: Chico and Pacifica Ocean (that's Paz's real name, MGS story is shite, get over it) have been captured by US Marines and are being held in a camp on Cuba. Jack Bauer must go in and rescue them. He does so, but not before choking out a panicked young boy so the young boy won't make too much fuss. Jack Bauer then, during the post game cutscene, cuts open Pacifica Ocean's stomach to remove a bomb to show off the cool things the Fox engine can do, but then he finds out they hid another bomb in Pacifica Ocean's nether-regions and she blows up and Jack Bauer goes into a coma. The end. Also there's this guy called Skullface or something, I don't know.

 

I don't have much to say about Jack Bauer as Snake, because Snake didn't do too much talking. I mean, yeah, I missed the old growl, but it wasn't distracting. He didn't have any memorable lines like, "What are those sticks in your head?" or "We managed to avoid drowning!" or "I killed my soul!" or "Meow meow meow," and as far as I know he never does the Metal... Gear schtick. But whatever.

 

The point of MGS is gameplay, because MGS3 let you throw snakes at people and that's great. Gameplay wise, it's pretty great. Snake does a lot of things, it's fun to dive out of sight. Guards are pretty observant this time around, and I could tell there was a difference in how far they could see between day and night. Unfortunately, the game removed most items like rations and the cardboard box, meaning 1. the game uses regenerating health, and 2. you can't hide in a box.

 

Graphics wise, it looks nice. I played it on the Xbox 360, and it was pretty neat to look at. I don't know, I'm not an arts guy. On the 360, there were some problems with frame rate and texture pop-in, and the game had a hard time keeping a lot of soldiers on the map at the same time, but once I got into the game it didn't bother me. If you think those are deal-breakers, it's because you're a bitch and a dumb bitch.

 

Lengthwise, it completely depends on how you feel about repeat gameplay. There's six missions all told, and there's some variety in between them. And the game gives you a lot of options when it comes to going through a mission. I've probably put 15 hours in, and I've enjoyed myself plenty.

 

Price: The game is worth $20 at least. And I mean, really. You fucks play JRPG's and fighters, so it's not like you've got anything better to spend your money on.

 

TL;DR: If you were thinking about buying, just fucking buy it. It's probably worth it.
