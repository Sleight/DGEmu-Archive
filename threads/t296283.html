RRyuukaMar 11 2009, 12:53 PM
4 is the worst title yet for many reasons. The inclusion of Star Wars characters feels completely out of place, but that's not what makes the game bad. I'm talking about some gameplay issues here.It's more than obvious that the game gives a huge advantage to playing purely offensively (and letting you stay on the offensive). The usefulness of guard impacts has been toned down quite a bit, as you can no longer GI throws. Also, the delay after you whiff a GI has been lengthened to the point where your opponent could react and punish it with just about anything.You can't guard "mid" attacks crouching. This effectively turns the entire game into 50-50s - which is not fun. You either block low and risk getting hit by 3/4 of the moves in the game, or you block high and risk getting thrown or hit low into KD and reset (then again, I guess you could always jump, which takes a couple frames to even leave the ground and once you're in the air, the smallest jab causes KD and reset). In the end, what's your reward for blocking successfully? Soul crush into critical finish, because the game hates you for blocking.If you don't want to get critical finished, you're forced to either poke out with a faster move (that usually doesn't result in damage) or GI something. The only way out of really good pressure or frame traps is to have a faster poke than they do. What happens if your character has no faster pokes? you're forced to run the gauntlet with GIs. If you fail then you simply eat a hit and die slower. Some moves even have ~3F of startup, which is probably faster than most normal human beings can react to. That's before mentioning that it takes about 1-2F for your character to even count as "standing up" in the first place. Add some netplay lag to that and it's even more fun.What a great game. I can't believe I spent money on this. I guess it could be fun to people who don't play fighting games seriously or who just want to mess around with friends, but to someone who wants to get into the raw mechanics and depth of these games, this game is a huge failure.
