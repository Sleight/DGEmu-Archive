RRyuukaApr 25 2007, 01:59 PM
Oh boy, my first real post in months... anyways, there are a few things that ruin anime for me.Botched Conversions - If it isn't broken, don't fix it (one of the reasons why Kyoto Animation is becoming my favorite studio of all time). I am a huge purist when it comes to conversions. I figure that if something was popular in its original form, then it shouldn't be changed (I'm looking at you, GONZO...). This is also why I disapprove of a lot of fanfics/doujinshi because the authors usually fail to understand the existing character development/relationships/subtleties and what made the source so great to begin with. In my opinion, anime conversions are hardest to succeed with visual novels because they have so much raw content and character development that fitting enough into a 12 or even a 26 episode time frame or even organizing the story chronologically becomes quite difficult (hence why all of Type-Moon's anime conversions disappoint me), but I digress. This somewhat applies to my subbing vs dubbing argument too.Untapped Potential or Bait and Switch - I could list a whole bunch of anime that fit here; where a series has something really going for it, but ultimately fails to deliver and thus ends up mediocre or worse. That, or the studio just blows their whole budget on the first few episodes and then cries when the whole thing blows up in their face. Nothing pains me more than going into a show that meets/surpasses my expectations at first and then the animators and/or directors get lazy and/or greedy and the whole thing just goes straight to hell.Popular Image - "Oh anime, you mean like Yu-gi-oh and Pokèmon?" or "Yeah, I've only seen [Naruto, DBZ, Bleach, FMA, and/or Death Note]; I consider myself a seasoned anime fan with tons of anime knowledge!" Whenever people say these things, I feel a growing urge to attack their life points directly.Ignorant Japanophiles - It is my honest opinion that people should learn Japanese to watch anime, and not the other way around. Too often have I heard people ignorantly throwing out random honorifics and quoting lines from anime when they have no idea what they mean.Yeah, there are definitely more pet peeves that I have regarding anime, but these are all the ones I can think of at the moment.

However, the thing that I hate most is dues ex machina, unless it's done in a humourous and/or self-criticizing manner. For those that don't know what a deus ex machina is, basically, it a situation in which things look hopeless for a character (i.e. they're about to be killed/defeated), and something completely random and (usually) unexplained happens that allows them to win and/or escape. A perfect example of this would be the fight between Rock Lee, Gaara, and Kimomaro, in which Kimomaru is about to win when, out of nowhere, he dies from a disease.Nine times out of ten, they're totally anti-climactic, and usually make little, if any, sense. Another example is when a character, at the last second, randomly busts out with a new attack or weapon, when there was no mention (or even the slightest hint) of it in the past, yet it's played off as though they've always known/had it.
