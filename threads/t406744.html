Yes, only if the games are intended to be real video games and not stupid merchandise tie-insThe reason why Scarface: The World Is Yours is the best movie-based game yet is because it was beyond a third-person shooter with Tony Montana's face on it. It was devoted to the original IP (copious amounts of gore and foul language are present in this game as it was in the movie) while making original content at the same time (the game doesn't follow the movie's plot; it depicts a "What If...?" scenario after the events of the film). Add that to smooth gameplay, great voice acting, and an awesome soundtrack and you've got an ideal movie-based game. The World Is Yours wouldn't have even needed the Scarface license to sell copies, it's an awesome game by itself and that's the kind of example that movie-based games should followAfter the game's release, it has been evident that no one has taken example, unfortunately; the amount of shovelware has only increased since the Wii's release. For now, we can rely only on Peter Wanat to make high-quality movie-based games for us
