The answer is already in the post..... A permutation means what order you pick something matters, e.g. pick from five people one person who becomes the president, one running mate and one lackey. A combination on the other hand what you pick doesn't matter, e.g. pick from five people three persons who will become the president. Permutations therefore have a much lower chance than combinations.
