Just to clear up a few things-1 - it's legal to install a Jailbroken firmware, however it's illegal to install cracked versions of App Store apps/games.2- yes, it will void your warranty, much like any unauthorised modification. So, to reiterate - you CAN mod it, but don't expect any repairs to be covered for by your warranty (or your Apple Care protection plan, if you have one).3- Usually, these will be semi-bricks, which are easily fixable by re-installing the Official firmware. Jailbroken firmware installation is pretty much the exact same process as installing original Apple Firmware, and the worst that comes from this is an incorrect installation requiring a restore.4- the iPhone dev team (the people responsible for jailbroken firmware) have managed to patch out that aspect of the 3.0 firmware betas. If they'll be able to do so with the final version remains to be seen.Yes, you can install emulators on a jailbroken iPod Touch. The most popular are gb4iphone (Gameboy Classic/color emu), NES.app (obvious), genesis 4 iphone, gpSPhone (GBA) and SNES 4 iPhone. Despite the "4 iphone" suffix, they work just fine on my 2nd gen iPod touch.
