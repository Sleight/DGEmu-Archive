Here at DGEmu, your security is important to us. This is just a friendly reminder that to tell you that the staff will never ask for your account password.

Having worked in the IT field for quite a few years now, I feel that security for users is important, thus why I wrote an article in the PC section of our forums to help users clean out viruses. Expect more of those kinds of articles to help you out. :)

On another note, to all our visitors, there was a new security bulletin published by Microsoft a few days ago regarding an exploit with Internet Explorer that could allow hackers to take full complete control of your computer. This does not affect DGEmu, but if you do visit other sites, you could be at risk. This issue affect all users of Internet Explorer versions 6 to 9, IE10 users are not affected (but seriously, how many of you are actually running Windows 8 right now?)

Microsoft has temporarily released a FixIt for the 32-bit version of Internet Explorer to prevent this issue. You can download it and apply it from here: http://support.micro....com/kb/2757760
Unfortunately, users of 64-bit Internet Explorer will be open to the attack until the next Windows Update is available.

My personal recommendation to all is to simply avoid Internet Explorer for now, and just use an alternative browser such as Firefox or Chrome.

To read more about the issue, here's Microsoft's security bulletin: http://technet.micro...dvisory/2757760
It might be a bit technical, but it is surely interesting for people working in IT. :)

If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to PM me or ask in the PC forums here:
