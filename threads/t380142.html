Little white lies, I interpret as being the tiny, fairly harmless ones. The very definition of a white lie is that this is a lie that won't hurt anybody at any point or rather isn't meant too. For example if somebody asks if a certain set of trousers makes them look fat, and they do, it's far more diplomatic to say something like, "well I like the colour but I don't think the style suits you as well as xxx." If you say no and they go out looking like a fool then ouch, if you tell the truth you'll hurt their feelings.So I don't think it's wrong to tell white lies, mainly small ones. Sometimes white lies can be hurtful but they weren't meant to be and are merely an example of somebody still learning what they should be diplomatic about and what they shouldn't. Obviously if a lie isn't needed then it shouldn't be said because of the risk of the problems it can cause except like certain white lies which are told everyday.
