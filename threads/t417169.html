Support and Suggestion Forum Rules Do not make any topic regarding any questions addressed in this FAQ or any of the other main FAQs or Rules forum threads in this site. Do not make a topic that does not have a suggestion in it. Do not make topics complaining about your warn level or point deductions. Do not ask for emulators. If you are going to make a topic suggesting a new skin for the site then make sure that you have one ready. You need to be able to present the code for it and everything. Our policy is: if you have one then we will consider it. If you make a topic please put your problem in the topic title or topic description. Do not make topics that just say “Help” or “Suggestion.” You must elaborate.Do not complain about the GP/DP System, your Global DP Reset, or the DP cost of ROMs. The reasons for these are to conserve bandwidth by limiting downloading. Each download uses up a bit of bandwidth. Without these, the site would shut down due to lack of bandwidth, since there are so many members downloading on a daily basis.Failure to follow any of these rules could lead to your points being deducted or a warn being issued.
