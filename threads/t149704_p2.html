The Final Fantasy series is the best RPG series hands down. Here's the breakdown:1. Stunning Graphics. Every Final Fantasy game ever created boasted the best known graphic engines at the time of their release.2. Epic story lines. Every Final Fantasy is a new game. It goes on its own story and its own characters. Each character (excluding the first couple where as they had no characters to speak of) has a personality all their own. The story lines are large and you can follow them and really get into them.3. New game play. Every Final Fantasy game has provided a new gaming experience. The Espers, the Guardian Forces, materia, the Sphere Grid. That's just to name a few of the game play additions we have seen over the years.4. Mini Games. Let face it, some of the most entertaining mini games on any platform have been hosted in the Final Fantasy games. Blitzball, Chocobo racing, that card game in which the name currently slips my mind. These are fun. I know several people that play Final Fantasy games over and over just to do better and better on the mini games.5. The Victory theme. The Final Fantasy victory theme is one of the most recognized victory theme is gaming today. It has also made appearance in other games.6. In-game reality. Let face it, most RPG's go a little crazy with the "reality" that's being weaved in front of us. Final Fantasy games take the time to explain to us the "reality" of this fantasy and we find ourselves able to believe it real in the confines of the game.7. Rememberable Enemies. Sephiroth, Kefa, the sorceresses. I remember them well and have fun fighting them again and again. Cain, the Elemental Fiends, Garland, and so many more!These are just some of the reasons I think the Final Fantasy series is the best RPG series ever.

OOutcomeOct 02 2007, 08:27 AM
Asking if there is a better RPG then FF is kind of an unfair question as FF has an entire library of different stories and game play mechanics to pick and choose from. While it is by no means the only series of RPG games which this is true, it's hard to say if FF is better or worse then an RPG that only encompasses one or two games instead of decades long multi-console games. Also since RPG games have been around for so long it is hard to draw comparisons between all the different types of RPGs that have existed, are the FF games better then Zork games? Graphically of course, for ease of game play most would say yes, but for story? That depends, do you like having a set path from beginning to end, one that plays the same each time you go back with richly developed plots and characters, or would you rather have a story that plays out differently each time you start a new quest? In the end asking if there are any better RPGs out there then FF is like asking what the best video game of all time is, the answer will vary greatly depending on what it is the player is looking to get out of it. The important thing is not if there is an all-incompessing best RPG but that you find games that you enjoy, regardless of what others may think of them.
