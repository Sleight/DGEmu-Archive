Alright thanks to bravepotato's suggestion, here goes, a brief answers guide to the questions u hear the most.=============== BEGIN CRAPPY TUTORIAL =============Q: I have the same IP as someone else, can you please register me?A: Well lad there's not much surprise in it - u may use the same proxy as someone else on the site or u guys try registering from the same school, whatever the reason is - if you're sure u never created an account in the past - we can register you - just reply to this email, change the title to "Register Me Plz" and include the username, password and email u want us to register u with.Q: I get a white screen when I open the game, what do I do?A: Well different ROMs (games) yield different outputs so I can't give you a 100% answer on why that happens - but we have the answers to the most common white-screen causes here:http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=22647If that still doesn't work it might be that the rom is in need of a patch or simply isn't playable - no matter what the cause is - we suggest u'll look in the answer in the GBA Emulation forum:http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showforum=31You'll surely find your answer there :D Q: There is no files in the winzip folder!A: Well I don't want to shatter your dream young lad but winzip sucks like hell! the program is so popular yet so shitty and that's a phenomena that I never really figured out. Fortunatly, there are some actually good packers out there which can really show the contents of a zip file - I suggest u'll take a look at WinAce (www.winace.com) and even better 7-Zip (www.7-zip.org).You can verify that the zip isn't empty by checking it's filesize (right click and Properties on Windows) - so unless the filesize is 0 bytes which in this case the download just didn't work, the problem is with your zip unpacker, and the answer would simply switching to a better one :D Q: I tried downloading it, but the dowload failed and I lost my money!A: We truly do our best to get every download u make launch and work without too many problems - but the huge traffic dgemu gets leads to certain data transfer problems - those problems are beyond our control and we can't really spot them because when the download request is sent we can't know if it safely got to your machine or not - so unfortunatly we can't even refund your dps. The only suggestion I can give u is to try again in times of less traffic (u can tell how busy the site is by the loading speed of the pages).Q: I get an error when Im trying to open the game page. It says the page is not available!A: Well I won't go into how the whole internet thing works but in short when a call for a page is sent from your browser there's a certain amount of time the browser will wait for it - now since dgemu is often bombed with download requests and users it happens that the server sometimes doesn't manage to send your browser data in time - so the page goes timeout and u get a page unavailable error, U just need to refresh - normally in the first or 2nd hit u'll see the page is loading again.Q: I get an error on the forums. It says that I'm not authorized to view it.A: 2 reasons I can see for that - the first one which is also the most common one is that your simply didn't validate your account - just use the resend validation email button on the forums' top and hit the link in your email - if u cant find the email just look for it in the bulk or spam folders - some mail server tend to throw it there. If u still can't validate your account just email webmaster@dgemu.com with your account name and validation request and he'll validate it for u - just make sure u email him from the email addy u registered with so he could be sure it's you.The second cause for that error is that you are trying to view a page u're not authorized to view lol - some forums and pages are made for moderators, administrators and various staff positions - If u don't belong to the group the page is intended for u'll get such an error.Q: Where are the emulators to play your ROMs?A: We don't have the emulators here, but a quick search can lead you to finding them without a problem. The latest versions of the emulators are usually found on www.zophar.net though if it's GBA emu you're looking we recommend VBA which can be downloaded from http://vba.ngemu.com============== END OF CRAPPY TUTORIAL =============If you guys got more question u don't know how to answer just reply here, me and another admin/sentinel/super-mod/whatever will edit the post and delete your reply.please don't reply anything irrelevant like "thank you DM you are my hero! I love you!" hehe - it's important we'll keep this topic in-topic ( :D ) so I won't have to search 10 pages and see if anyone has a new question :P Cheers, - Dragons Master-dualist passed by-
