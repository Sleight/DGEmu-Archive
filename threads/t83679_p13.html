I'm not gonna read through this thread, but suffice it to say, I'm pretty sure most of you have been spouting a bunch of **** about Digimon. I'll enlighten you:First of all, Digimon came out before Pokemon. So, it is not a ripoff.Second of all, the Digimon anime actually has a well-rounded plot, unlike the Pokemon anime.Lastly, the world of Pokemon is not such a great place, in my opinion. I mean, for chrissake, it is LEGAL in this world, to capture animals, and use them to fight other animals; they're basically your slaves. I understand that there is still a message of friendship in Pokemon - I understand this well. But, that doesn't undermine the fact that the trainers engage their Pokemon in battles for their own selfish gain, and desires. What does Ash want to become? He wants to become a Pokemon master; someone who is extremely skilled in the prospect of Pokemon battleing. So, basically, he does it for his own amusment; he doesn't want to do anything useful with his Pokemon training skills, like breed them, or research them, or provide medical aid for them. No, all he wants in life is to make Pokemon fight each other, and that's it. Wow, what a goal. . . And yes, there's lots of digimon fighting in the digital world, but at least there's a message of peace and unity in Digimon; the Digidestines, and the good Digimon, fight for the good of the digital world against the evil Digimon, who try to make the digital world into a society ruled by despotic leaders, who promote suffering; there's a positive message - a meaning - in there, amongst the fighting and violence. That's one major aspect I can appreciate from Digimon, but not so from Poke'mon.This is why, in my opinion, Digimon is better, as an anime, and as a series.
