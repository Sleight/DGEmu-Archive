Ok, time for some KoF projectiles talk, mainly King, but this work for any character who has a slow projectile with a fast recovery, ie, something they can throw, then run behind using it as cover.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 
 
At first glance, it appears that King's entire game plan is just running away and spamming projectiles. Thought that's how things look, it's not really that simple, there are way too many mobility options in KoF to navigate through projectiles, so a fireball spacing game like in Street Fighter doesn't actually work in KoF. Here I'll try to explain KoF's projectiles keep-away gameplan and how to fight someone who's playing like that.
 
First of all, King have two projectiles, LK and HK projectiles. Her HK projectile travel fast, but leave King stuck in the projectile throwing animation for a while, by the time King can move again the projectile have already traveled through the screen, more about how to use this later. Her other projectile, the LK one, is what make her so strong. It travels slowly enough for King to start running behind it, which give her a huge advantage, this is called the LK projectile trap(throw a slow moving projectile > run behind it). First lets look at the defending character's options for avoiding a normal projectile.
 
Let's say someone threw a projectile at you, you can:
1- Jump over it.
2- Roll through it. 
3- Block it.
 
See, avoiding projectiles is easy in KoF, what make the LK projectile trap interesting is that King will be behind the projectile, and with fast enough reaction, she should be able to punish whatever you do to avoid her projectile:
 
1- You jumped: King can anti-air with jumping CD(that's HK+HP), tornado kick, super, standing HP or HK depending on the spacing basically, you're at the mercy of the King player miss-timing his anti-air.
 
2- You rolled: this is the worst option(not taking lag into account), you basically roll toward King now, and she have all the time she need to punish your roll recovery with a full combo.
 
3- You blocked: while this might be your best option, you are now in block stun, and King is running toward you, congrats, you just gave her a free mix-up, is she going to do the slide attack, jumping attack, attempt a cross up or wait and grab you? So yea, your best option would still put you at a disadvantage.
 
- There's another option here, which is to jump back or back dash, this only work against King's LK projectile because it doesn't travel full stage, so when you back off enough distance, it will disappear before reaching you. This might appear as a good option to deal with King's LK projectile, but it's not, if you back off you just gave her an opportunity to through another LK fireball and you basically drove yourself closer to the corner and now have to deal with another LK projectile trap.
 
So WTF is this? Why is King so OP? How can she get so much advantage just by throwing a projectile? Well, that's not the whole story, the LK projectile trap only works when King is more than half a screen away from your character, that's the distance at which King is safe to throw a projectile(also for future usage, lets call this this "less than half a screen away from King" distance the Danger Zone!). So for King to be able to start her projectiles game, she first have to leave the danger zone, now this is where her other moves come into play. 
 
The moment the other player enter the danger zone, King is at a disadvantage and need to (option 1) push him out or (option 2) run away out of the danger zone, basically, this is where the "chase" part of the match up start, your character is trying to stay in the danger zone while King is trying to leave it.
 
Let's take a look at King's first option here, remember if you're chasing after King, you're not blocking and are very open to her ~long range attacks, these are her best option to prevent you from entering/staying in the danger zone:
 
Short Jump CD: travel a nice distance, gives King advantage on block and throw the other character out of the danger zone if it hit, it also can be used as an anti-air if the jump, the down side to this move is that it's kinda slow and people with fast reaction can block it.
 
Tornado Kick: This comes out faster than short jump CD, works as an anti-air and drive the other character away on hit too. But while it's harder to block than jump CD, it leave King at a disadvantage if blocked.
 
Slide Kick: slide kick have shorter range and wont push characters away from the danger zone unless King follow it with another move. Though it hits low which makes it harder to block.
 
Most of these moves come out relevantly fast and have a nice range, though there's another move that comes out fast, travel full screen, push them back if they get hit with it and leave King completely safe if blocked which is:
HK Projectile: this is where HK fireballs are used, especially the air version since King bounce back after doing it which help a lot when trying to leave the danger zone. And she can also use the stupidly fast ex projectile here. Though there's a big risk in using fireballs while in the danger zone, if the other player predict a ground fireball, he can jump and punish King heavily.
 
Now for the second option, which is running away instead of trying to push the other player out. Remember how in first option the player have to chase King so most of the time they wont be blocking? Well some players might notice that King is taking advantage of that by throwing some attacks to catch them, so they might stop for a second or two to block and bait something out of King, which gives King a chance to jump back and leave the danger zone.
King can't keep doing this forever though as she will eventually be cornered and have no room to jump back, that's why she need to use LK fireball trap as much as she can to try and gain ground/avoid ending up in the corner.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 
 
Okay, that's basically how King's projectiles game plan goes, it's a combination of LK fireball aggression and danger zone battle. If you're the other player, you want to constantly stay in the danger zone, over there King is just like any other character, it's very risky for her to throw fireballs and her other moves have low priority, well, none EX versions at least. 
 
Few notes to keep in mind while fighting King:
 
- Some characters can counter her LK projectile trap:
       - Negating it by throwing another Fireball, note that this might not be a safe option if done late.
       - Slow EX or Super fireballs, like Athena and Terry EX fireballs, or Saki's super.
       - Moves with invincibility that can go through projectiles, though realize that this is a gamble, those moves aren't safe, and you might get punished heavily if they're blocked(or not at all during lag).
       - Fast full screen neo-max/supers with projectiles invincibility. Kyo or Mai neo-max are fast enough to catch King during her fireball throwing animation, they work as an on reaction punish though it's a huge investment.
Not all characters have these option though, and generally, if King managed to start the LK projectile trap you're at a disadvantage.
 
- Never jump when King is jumping backward, if she throw an air fireball most characters have no way of avoiding it while in the air. The right thing to do when King jump backward is to close the distance with her by running on the ground. In offline you have to watch out for air fireballs that are close to the ground, but those are very hard to do in lag. Also characters with low profile running animations like Leona can just run under Kings air projectiles.
 
- You don't have to chase after King until you're in her face, long as you're in the danger zone, you're doing fine. Try to stay there and wait for her to do something stupid like a random Tornado Kick or jump on her if you predict that she's about to use a fireball. Almost everything King does at this range have a risk attached to it, just being in that range leave her at a disadvantage.
 
- Without meter, King have no invincible moves, so don't respect her wake-up, she have to just sit there and block or roll. 
 
That's about the general basic idea of how the King match up works, and remember other characters like Athena can do the slow projectile trap thing, so you can apply this gameplan to them, just figure out their good moves to use in the danger zone(hint low jump CD is always one of them). Also Athena's back dash is ridiculous if she want to run away. 
 
And finally, saying danger zone feels good for some reason! Also sorry if this ended up being too long, It could have been much, much shorter if I was writing it for someone who already play fighting games and understand fighting games terms, I avoided doing that to make it as clear as possible to everyone.
