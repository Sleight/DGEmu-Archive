athene's is 600 more which really isn't much  and I get 10 more AP, twice as much CDR, and much better Mana regen (mana font).

 

For spell vamp I'd just go for WoTa. The only reason is because I don't build spell vamp on all of my AP mids. I prioritize it for someone like Kennen for the infinite sustain. It's kind of pointless for Veigar since you shouldn't be getting hit since the stun should prevent you from getting fucked based on position (I've gotten away from a lot of ganks). For someone like Cassiopeia Spell vamp is optional but I prefer not to use it with her. She automatically loses a 1v2 and doesn't really get hurt in a 1v2. Karthus... well it's karthus,

 

It's not a bad item and it's totally viable, but I think it's more champ specific. It's pretty OP for Fiddlesticks jungle.
