There is always going to be those Die hard fans of the original formula... starting in a new region, as a young trainer with a choice of 3 starters, 8 badges, Elite 4, etc... But the new generation of gamers is not necessarily going to be all that interested in pixel-art and limited battle controls. I understand exactly why Nintendo doesn't want to stray from their original formula... it has made them gods! We are starting to see a move into 3D, with Diamond and Pearl, but that too is limited from what i have seen...I think it is time for an overhaul, as scary as that may be... and I don't think the NDS is a suitable platform... a full 3D game would be the way to go, but in saying that, I know I am asking too much...
