He's hardly doing it for just reasons. Zephiel's condemned all humanity as deserving of brutal death because he had an unhappy childhood. He's basically being an emo that needs to get a LiveJournal and listen to some Linkin Park. He admits to having no just reasons for wanting to kill everyone, he simply wants all of humanity to die.Now compare that to Arvis, who sacrificed hundreds of innocent lives, including his own younger brother, in a conspiracy to conquer Augustria (resulting in much death and forced Sigurd to fight Eltosian, his best friend) Isaac, and Verdane; allowed his prince to be assassinated and married his daughter (who is actually Arvis's long lost sister) to get access to the royal family; buys off Baron Reidrick to backstab King Lenster of Northern Thracia, and then executes Sigurd and Co, who unknowningly did all his dirty work, as traitors to Grandbell, after they just saved it from conspiracy. All so he can conquer all of Jugdral and extend Grandbell into a single continental empire, where he can enforce peace, justice, and equality to everyone- allowing freedoms of speech, religion, etc. And for many years, the land was in peace. Arvis then suffers from the wonderful irony and tragedy that befalls all good villains.His twisted son (and dark god reincarnate) Yurius murders his mother and tried to kill his sister. He then ursurps all power right out from under Arvis, making him essentially a lame duck, an emperor with no authority at all. The Freeges, the Dozels, even the Jungbys all answer to Yurius. And all of this was part of a conspiracy created by Manfroy, the one who helped Arvis with his scheme, to revive the evil Loput Empire. Yurius plunges the entire continent into a seemingly endless nightmare where familys are forced to turn over their children (ages 7-13) to be ritualistically tortured and sacrificed. Arvis is left powerless to do anything despite his efforts (being a full blooded descendant of Crusader Fala and all) and dies a miserable and meaningless death at the hands of his own step-son and the army formed to end the darkness he unknowingly unleashed.Now why did Arvis want to unite the continent like that? He's got an even worse childhood than Zephiel. His father, Duke Victor of Velthomer was a drunkard and womanizer with several mistresses. Sigyun, his favorite mistress was Arvis's mother. She had an affair with Prince Kult (eventually resulting in her becoming pregnant with Diadora); Victor drunkenly rapes one of his servants (resulting in Azel's conception). After finding out of the affair, Victor hangs himself, and Sigyun flees to Verdane and is never seen again- Azel is born on the day Victor commits suicide. Arvis is orphaned and positioned as Duke of Velthomer at age 7. He exiles all of Victor's mistresses and bastard children except Azel and his mother, who had been like a surrogate family to him; however Azel's mother falls ill and dies 10 years later. Since Azel's mother's death, Arvis can't stand being around women, seeing them as the reason his life was so miserable, and was never seen with a woman in his company.Let's see one childhood trauma results in emo boy wanting to kill all humanity, the other results in guy doing evil to allow him to protect humanity. Yeah, Arvis is by far better and more developed villain, Zepiel's a little pansy; hell, Elphin (Prince of Etruria) survived an assassination too, and he's not being all emo over it. By the Zephiel logic, Arvis should have crusaded to kill all women at the very least. I don't really like Zephiel, but I give him credit for sticking to his guns and not pulling a lame repentance at the last minute all for his precious half sister.
