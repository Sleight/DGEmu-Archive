AaioliJan 24 2013, 05:15 PM

it's actually not complicated at all. it's just for passive skills, stuff like +4 strength or +6% attack speed. you start at the middle of the grid and choose which path you take. one path focuses on strength, another on dexterity, and another on intelligence. every time you level up, you advance one step in the path of your choice. there are side paths here and there that provide extra perks if you think they're worth it.

 

it looks complex at first but so far I think it's a great system. if you've played final fantasy x, it's very similar to that.
