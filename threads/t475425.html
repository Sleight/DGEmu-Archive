It generally comes from incidents/situations with consequence(s) that is perceived to be negative and likely to be preventable. From experience, it would also be associated with resentment where one believes the outcome was unreasonable based on the thought-process that was decided upon. But more importantly, it is mainly a reflection of one's personality/attitude/opinion---leading to the interpretation of a given incident. But since the perception of one expressing hatred towards an outcome/incident does rarely representative of others (and other than him/her self), the most logical answer about the origin of hate would have to from within the perceiver's set of "instantanenous psychology"; note that a "set" here refers to the perceiver's holistic state (physical/mental/intellectual/spiritual/etc).
