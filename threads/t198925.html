HHadelJan 10 2007, 09:11 PM
Link's Awakening is the best original GB game. Period. There's a reason it was on top of the Nintendo Power Charts (yes, #1) from basically the time it came out until nearly the end of them even keeping charts anymore... one of the Pokemons finally passed it up.Final Fantasy Legend III is probably the best turn-based RPG (yes, it is the original Saga series).Final Fantasy Adventure is a great action RPG, but of course Sword of Mana for GBA, the remake, is a lot better.The Adventures of Lolo series was good on the NES, and its puzzles are about as good on the GB.Donkey Kong for the GB adds a lot to the original game and is truly a classic.Gargoyle's Quest is pretty cool, yea.Wario Blast was one that I always liked, but it's not necessarily a stellar classic.The Donkey Kong Land series is legit.Kirby's Dreamland 2 was pretty fun, and the original KDL I play just for nostalgic purposes.James Bond 007 was one of the later titles in B&W, and I found it pretty good.Wizardry games and Ultima games are all good too.The GB has some pretty decent titles, really. People don't give it enough credit. Hope that's a decent enough list for you! :D
