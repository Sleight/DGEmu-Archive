The internal memory of any phone or tablet is partitioned into 2 section:

- Internal memory

- sdcard memory

 

Both are part of the memory inside your phone.

For example, if you take my Ainol tablet: 16GB internal storage (reality of about 14GB).

1GB is reserved for system installation memory, 13GB are reserved for data. If you install too many things onto your system, you're basically eating up your 1GB reserved memory, even if you have like 10GB free, and your tablet will tell you it's full and can't install.

 

If you want to keep all your installed applications: Try going into settings, check each app, and if it says "Move to SDCard", click it. You'll notice that it'll take up space differently.

For example, I have Air Penguin installed from the Play Store which took up 19MB of internal memory on my cheap Xperia Phone. I had to force the data to "move to sdcard", and by doing that, it takes 2MB on my internal phone memory and 17MB on my "sdcard partition".

 

Since we're still talking about internal memory, this has no impact on performance.

If you actually did move it to a MicroSD card though, you'll notice a little suffering in performance when a game loads, and a little bit more battery drain.
