I posted this in another Etrian Odissey Thread but i think ill copy it hear so i can share my thoughts about this wonderful game:Ill be sincere, if you guys are diehard fans of OLD SCHOOL RPG then it is a must get, its a gem in it's class. It's a really traditional RPG. I've played both I and II and i must say both of em are very enjoyable and rewarding in the end. BUT! They are veeeeery hard, thats why they are rewarding. In the end EOII eats alive the first one they improved everything they needed to do.PROS:-Full customization of your characters. Specially on HOL (Heroes of Laagard) where you can custom skills and stats.-Very nice and diferent classes for you to choose. Total of 9 in the first one and 12 in the 2nd.-Some quests and a lot of side quests.-Mapping (love or hate kinda thing)-Tons of hours of game play (30 floors)-Nice anime style and monsters. Kinda get repetitive in the 1st one.CONS:-Extremely punishing and hard for the newbies (once you get used its a normal game).-Lack of visual effects (they implemented a lot more in HOL)-Tons of hours of game play (gets repetitive in the begining cause you have to grind a lot) And a lot of backtrack in the 1st one.-Mapping (love or hate kinda thing)-Did i mention hard? HARD HARD HARD!-Lame story.Anyway one more thing, Heroes of Laagard "EOII" Improved almost EVERYTHING and implemented new great stuff:-3 more classes.-Easier mapping and more options.-A lot more teleportation for decreasing the backtraking.-A lot more nice effects and a lot more monsters.-Easier way to get money, and exp.- A lot of minor tweaks that needed to be done (e.g. Selling repetitive items with 1 option, easier way of tracking quests, the exact loot needed for specific item etc)In the end the game is very rewarding. If i'm saying HOL is easier dont get fooled, its not easy at all it's just not EXTREMELY HARD LAME like the 1st one. The thing you need to know is, play it safe, always have potions and a way to get back to town, don't go to a new floor if your dont feel prepare, grind if you need to (money and exp) expend skill points in defence stuff and ways to help you get more moeny and exp (like DIVINITY AND SCAVENGE) etc etc. Anyway hope this helps you out
