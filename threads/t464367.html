MMo­lh.Dec 23 2010, 03:33 PM
Yes, but only if your "R4" is an "R4i". Your flashcart has to be specifically built to work on DSis. This means that the original R4, along with all R4 clones not called "R4i" won't work on the DSi (but they will work on a DS Lite or DS Phat). Popular flashcarts for the DSi include the Acekard 2i, M3i Zero, Supercard DSTwo and so on and so forth.How to get the flashcarts to work: http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=182458Where to buy a flashcart from (and more information on the respective recommended flashcarts): http://gbatemp.net/t217593-the-which-flash...get-info-threadOur general flashcarts faq: http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=363628

Yep, something like that. Most new flashcarts should have SDHC support by now. The original R4 doesn't of course, it's an old product. Well there isn't a comprehensive list or guide on micro SDs or anything, just get a good quality and reliable one. I do know that the AP bypass mode, which the acekard uses to play the latest ROMs, can't work properly on those cheap poor-quality micro SDs.
