OOpulent MaelstromORIGINAL POSTNov 26 2005, 12:50 PM
It's time for all of you GFXers to reunite again for a new event. Your job is to make the best team icon set for the forums. What is a team icon?The team icon is the image you see under most staff member's avatar stating their member group (Administrator, Sentinel, etc.):Requirements:- Width: 120 to 170 pixels- Height: 20 to 40 pixels- The DGEmu Dragon Logo must appear in the icons (other versions of this logo can be found by searching).- 4 team icons in total: Administrator, Sentinel, Super Moderator (or Super Mod), Moderator.Here's an image you can inspire your work from, courtesy of the dgscripts team:How to submit your entry?You can either post the set in this topic or send me a personal message. Your submission must contain the four individual team icon images in the following possible formats: PNG, GIF, BMP.You can always edit your post if you want to update your submission.If you have submitted your entry and it got disqualified, you may send a fixed one until submissions are no longer accepted.If you have sent an entry, but improved it since, you may send the improved version, but only until the poll is made.What will get your submission disqualified:- Not following the requirements.- Missing a team icon for a member group as specified.- A typo in the member group names.- If we judge your icon set to be a rip-off.Submissions will no longer be accepted after 15 entries are reached. The deadline is December 1st if we have 10 or more entries.After all the entries are sent, a poll will be made with all the contestants and their entries. The persons with most votes will be awarded as follows:Winner: 2000 GPs + 500 dedicated DPs of choice.Second place: 1000 GPsThird place: 500 GPsHaving at least two votes: 50 GPsIf we think your entry is good enough, we will use it in the forums, regardless of the results in the poll. The one whose entry is used may receive more GPs or DPs, additionally to the award for the poll results. Note that by enterring the contest you accept that we use your work as we will.Good luck, and don't forget to enjoy yourself. Together we will make DGEmu a better looking place. :drool: Entries:spleenmuffinzanzokenEricChizParkos-lKaYoSlReksVeksling999rpkanbeiBackBreakaFirebatFalconer
