Sometime last year, I sat for a whole weekend and tried to study SotN speed runs to see how quick I can go, by the end of the two/three days, I managed to complete the game(fake ending, no reverse castle) in under 30 minutes. I was also bored to death.

 

The fun part though wasn't the gameplay, it was understanding the glitches, you really get to see where the programmers fucked up and get a glimpse at the weird choices they made to implement some of the stuff. One simple example is the "mana regeneration glitch", mana regeneration isn't linked to a timer but to display frames, ie:

 

By timer: Generate 2 mana every 0.5 seconds.

What the game does(by display frames): Generate 2 mana every other 30 frames.

 

The results are, if you manage to freeze the game(by taking items like HP+/Mana+ or other important ability items) in the same frame that you were supposed to gain mana, the game will repeat that same display frame for about a second to simulate a "freeze" effect, and each time that frame is repeated you will gain mana.
