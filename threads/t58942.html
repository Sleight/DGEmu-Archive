Different elements of what make a character good have different victors.For favorite badass I go with Vile. The name alone sets him above the others, much like Vicious in Cowboy Bebop. Couple that with his mastery over the armorsuits, his bloodlust for vengeance on X and Zero, his ever changing and truly vicious array of weapons, and the fact that you never see him outside of his battle armor, and you've got the Boba Fett of Megaman.For best character growth, I have to go with X. Think of the ending of each of the X series. Every one ends with X questioning why the war always keeps going. The transformation from X to Cyber-elf X in Megaman Zero was a genius move by Capcom, because they expanded on the character growth they created in the previous series, although personally I think they should have ended the X series at 4. X started as nothing more than a battle droid and wound up being a digital Ghandi and choosing to sacrifice himself rather than keep fighting and getting so many people hurt.For most versatile I have to go with the MMZ form of Zero. He's adapted in every game, and having just finished MMZ4 I have to say he's far more adaptable than X and he is what Axl SHOULD have been. Some may say X is more versatile, but honestly, X has had essentially the same 8 powers in every game in the series, which gave you a specific path through the bosses in each one that you would only deviate from if you were curious what its like to not play them "in order". Zero doesn't even always get stuff from the bosses, so his playstyle is determined entirely by the player. He is adaptable to each different player.Favorite character design is a no-contest situation, Bass.EXE/Forte.EXE. Tiny bits of info across 4 games, the mysterious stranger persona, the raised collar obscuring his face, and the torn cloak covering him almost completely. That's a damn cool character design people. And when the glowing hand comes out, you can almost feel the menacing orchestral music course through you.But to consider everything together: versatility, character growth, design, and sheer badass-ness, I'm gonna have to go with Zero from MMZ. By the third game he really developed into a character completely separate from the Zero in the X series. But hey, that's just what I came up with off the top of my head, nothing says I'm right.
