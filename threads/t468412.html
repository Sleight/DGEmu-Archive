Anyone play it?It's kind of like Henry Hatsworth, except with a pet monster instead of a three-of-a-kind puzzle game... And without hilarious gibberish voiceovers.So, really, just the combat is like Henry Hatsworth, except there's no tea time. Instead, you pick up "pet items" that you feed to the monster to level it up and evolve it into various forms, each one with a different set of skills and traits. Otherwise, it's practically the same... except you play as a little girl instead of as a dapper gentleman who likes his tea.Okay, so the only real similarities are that it's a 2D platformer in which you unlock new abilities and moves (kind of like Super Metroid, actually, complete with a "roll" move), and you can rack up more points (to spend on upgrades and/or pet items) by juggling enemies and getting a high hit multiplier thing.Whatever the case, it's rather fun, at least in my opinion.

TTHE GRAND GALACTIC INQUISITORMar 21 2011, 06:17 AM
I think you have to be on an adjacent form. Also, if you want the evolution to unlock (faster?), give the monster the type of item that the particular evolution form (the one you're trying to unlock) likes best.Also, if you're having trouble getting a good amount of coins and items, here are a few tips if you haven't figured them out already.Melee every chance you can get. It's a lot easier to get coins and items that way. In most cases I can get a decent combo going (once you've gotten the five-hit combo and the upward attack) by doing four regular hits followed by an upward attack to juggle the enemy, then jump and hit them once while they're in the air, then land and repeat as quickly as you can, as many times as you can. If you think the monster is about to drop below your reach, try working in a punch-shot thing (the skill you get where you can punch and then immediately blast with the band), sometimes that gets the monster in the air again but possibly too far away for another upward strike. Also, after a certain number of hits, some monsters can't be juggled anymore.
