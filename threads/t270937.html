TtrancebamJun 20 2007, 05:36 PM
Out of all the card games I've actually seen and/or played, very few were based on anime. Of these, there were Fullmetal Alchemist, Naruto, Yu-Yu Hakusho, Pokemon, Digimon, Avatar, Monster Rancher, Zatchbell, Yu-Gi-Oh, and Bleach. Magination looks like a good one, but I'm not sure that it's based on an anime, and Duelmasters started as a card game (produced by Wizards...that's the company's name) and was turned into a really bad anime. The ones I haven't played are Bleach, Zatchbell, Monster Rancher, Avatar and Yu-Yu Hakusho, so I can't bag on any of those, but I have a lot of experience with the rest of them. The Fullmetal Alchemist TCG is a very in depth game that isn't much like the show unfortunately. I wasn't very fond of the game, though it's fun to play now and then. The Naruto TCG is actually based very closely on the show. You take groups of three shinobi at a time into battle against your opponent, and you have to spend chakra to do jutsus and all that jaz. It's a very unique card game, but once again I wouldn't particularly recommend it. The Digimon card game is a waste of time. It's nothing like the series, and it really isn't that fun to play. Basically each monster on their own card has three moves. You don't get to choose a move, your opponent's digimon type chooses that for you. Then you basically have a bunch of power-ups that you hope will end up making your move stronger than your opponents move. Not very fun. The Pokemon card game is quite good however. It's quite a bit like an actually Pokebattle, with one exception: you have more than 6 pokemon in your deck at the beginning of the game. Also, in order to have an evolved form of a pokemon, all you need is the card. No experience necessary. However, it is still a rather fun game. Although I did say Duelmasters came before it's anime, I will say that it's a great game. It plays a bit like Magic: The Gathering only a bit simpler. I rather like the way it's set up, and if you're a TCG fan, I would highly recommend this game. Yu-Gi-Oh is also rather well put together, although I don't like it much because the number of cards your allowed to play is limited to a number rather than how much energy you've built, and you can't play powerful monsters without sacrificing weaker ones depending on the star rating of the monsters. I actually forgot about the Shaman King TCG. This is by Upper Deck, same as Yu-Gi-Oh, but it's much funner. It goes by the same system as the Pirates of the Carribean TCG and the Avatar TCG. The system is fun to play and easy to learn. Also by upper deck, you have the World of Warcraft TCG and the Vs. System TCGs like Marvel, DC, and Hellboy, all very good games, I highly recommend them. So of the ones I've played, I highly recommend Shaman King, Avatar, Pokemon, and Duelmasters. Some fun ones to try, but not to spend too much on are Naruto, Fullmetal Alchemist, and Yu-Gi-Oh. Avoid the Digimon TCG. I've played many other TCGs, but since this is only for the anime based ones, that's all I have to say. Later days :br:
