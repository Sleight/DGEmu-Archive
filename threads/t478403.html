There are only a select few flashcarts that work with the 3ds, the anti-piracy has been updated quite frequently by nintendo and two out of three of the flashcarts you named are blocked already. You can see a list that is constantly updated here: http://gbatemp.net/threads/firmware-update-compilation-thread-list-of-flashcards-working-on-3ds.302773/

Personally I'd recommend the supercard dstwo out of the bunch by a longshot. It might be the most expensive out of the bunch, its onboard CPU improves speed of optimized homebrew and it has a good track record of 3ds compatability. As for shops, you might try these two:
