The reason most games come out in 720P on PS3 and they are in 180P on XBOX360 is because when making a game that is multiplatform you naturally make it for the system that has more restrictions. So instead of using all of the PS3's power in a title like Resident Evil 5 or Call Of Duty World At War, they make the game able to run on the 360 then port it over to the PS3 to ensure they can both play the title. This means that while the PS3 has the ability to far outshine the 360 it doesn't get the chance. What makes this especially hard for Sony is the fact that when DvD has a considerably less amount of space the maximum graphics the disc can support is only 720P and the actual XBOX does the upscaling to 1080P. So when the game is transfered to the PS3 it is only 720P and the system wasn't made to upscale. The pure size of Blu-Ray gives them the ability to not worry about disc usage so they can actually make the game in full 1080P. The only time you will see a incredible difference right now is on exclusive titles like Killzone 2, Resistance 2, and Gran Turismo 5. But you cannot compare the graphics between two totally different games. While some remakes for the PS3 have shown better graphics like Oblivion and Bioshock some people blame this on the period between the company making the game and releasing it on PS3.In the end it isn't so much about the graphics or else you would already have a PS3. The ability to watch Blu-ray movies and the exclusive titles maxing out the consoles abilities. Never mind all of the HD download titles that Sony has to offer.In most cases it is a selection of exclusive titles that convinces you to decide between PS3 and Microsoft.
