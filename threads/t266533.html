The Simpsons is a long running show as the years go by he never appears to age... I also think that American networks have a certain attitude towards programming; as long as the pilot episode proves to be popular, than they'll give the go-ahead to full production for a stipulated number of episodes. Writers of the show only seem to plan things from episode to episode. The show after all, can get cancelled anytime. If they're lucky, the writers struggle to finish up the series appropriately. I think this produces more variety and quantity in Japanese anime compared to American toons. I conclude that Japanese anime comes out top because of the variety and quantity. This doesn't mean I hate N. American cartoons either. But I do prefer the more alternative like Delta State [my fave], Daria, .....the Last Airbender, Samurai Jack, Grim Adventures of Billy & Mandy, and Skyland.
