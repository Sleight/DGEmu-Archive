1. Dream Theater. LaBrie and Petrucci are, quite possibly, the best vocal/guitar combination ever concocted. Add in an amazing bass player and a crazy keyboardist, and you have a combination almost impossible to beat. Now they just need a new drummer. Favorite songs: Learning to Live, Never Enough, This Dying Soul, To Live Forever.2.Jason Becker/Cacophony. Again, you have a match made in heaven. Only this time, it's two guitarists. Both of these guys didn't even need to try, they had a gift. Some of their stuff is incredibly hard to listen to, but if you listen to Speed Metal Symphony or End of the Beginning, you'll know what I mean. Favorite songs: Mabel's Fatal Fable, Speed Metal Symphony, The Ninja, End of the Beginning.3. Rush. These three guys don't even need an introduction. There has to be at least some song that they have that everyone likes. They have been working in perfect harmony since day one. Favorite songs: 2112, Working Man, The Spirit of Radio, Distant Early Warning.4. Negramaro. An Italian band. These guys are on my list because they are probably the best Italian band out there. The singer's voice can bring almost anyone to tears, plus the quick dynamic changes make these guys almost legendary. Favorite songs: Via Le Mani Dagli Occhi, La Distrazione, La Finestra, Un Passo Indietro.5. Led Zeppelin: Another band that doesn't need an introduction. Everyone, at some point in their life, has heard of Led Zeppelin, and I know of people who claim they hate classic rock, but still love these guys. Favorite songs: Since I've been Loving You, Black Dog, Achilles Last Stand, Rover6. Queensryche. One of the several high pitched crazy singer bands I love. These guys know what they can do, do it with authority, and plain kick as5. Most people seem to give Queensryche little chance because they hear Silent Lucidity and hate it. There's much more to them than just Silent Lucidity, believe me. Favorite songs: I Don't Believe in Love, Queen of the Reich, Jet City Woman, Another Rainy Night (Without You).7. Die Toten Hosen. Another foreign band, these guys named their band "The Dead Pants" for a reason unbeknownst to me. I love their style, and , as with Negramaro, the singer is crisp, clear, and on time. Favorite songs: Nur zu Beusch, Hier Kommt Alex, Meine Stadt, Zurueck zum Gluck.8. Scorpions. These guys are also German, but they sing in English. They have some very well known songs, as well as some not very many people know, that are very good. Unfortunately, they only seem to have one style of play, but that one style is amazing. Favorite songs: Rock you like a Hurricane, The Zoo, I Can't Explain, No one like You.9.Iron Maiden. These bros have several things going for them. They sound amazing live, they play fast, heavy, and in sync, and they just plain rock. Favorite songs: Fear of the Dark (Live), The Trooper, Hallowed be thy Name, Aces High.10. Journey. Who doesn't know Don't Stop Believing? Even more so, who can resist singing it whenever it comes on somewhere? That song is obviously their most popular, but there are several songs they have that also rock. Favorite songs: Seperate Ways (Worlds Apart), Anyway you Want It, Don't Stop Believing, Faithfully.
