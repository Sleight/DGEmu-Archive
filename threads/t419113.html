Abusive parents who took physical punishment into the realm of harmful beating has made the entire practice of spanking appear to be the tool of a dangerous and unreasonable individual. As a result of my myriad punishments, my children are very well behaved, in public and in private, and I am routinely complemented by other adults as a result.
