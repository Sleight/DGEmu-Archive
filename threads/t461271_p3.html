Soon, I will be moving, but once all that is worked out and squared away, I should be able to have the server running on a much more regular schedule.As such, here are some possible plugins I'd like to add. If any of you are still interested in playing, let me know what you think. Alternatively, if you want to scout out good plugins yourself, check out the Bukkit release subforum.iConomy - Establishes an economy on the server. Useful in that it adds functionality with other plugins. We could set it up so that players have the option to play such that commands and blocks cost currency, which can be gained from selling other blocks and such. Of course, players can opt out of this, but it would offer an alternative for anyone who gets bored with being able to generate whatever blocks they want.Minecart Mania - Kind of a given, at least for me. Adds boosters, speedbumps, directional tracks, etc. for minecarts.Towny - Players can establish chunks as towns, complete with town-specific permissions, chat, economy (upkeep, powered by iConomy), and even war if you feel like it. Also nations. It might not be as much fun with such a small server, but who knows.NPCTrader - So we establish an economy and individual towns - what about shops? This plugin lets a player create an NPC and have it sell the player's items to other players, even if the player is offline. Again, powered by iConomy.WorldEdit - We had this during the brief period when hMod was working on the server. It just offers a whole bunch of world editing options, from snow to pumpkin patches to spheres. It also works with MCEdit to allow for structures to be copied and pasted into the world.Dynmap - Assuming I can get this to work, it would create a web page with a dynamically created map of the server. Every time something is changed on the server, it would update that chunk on the map. You could also use it to find where other players are in-game.WarpGate - Remember when we had the RuneCraft portals? This is the same idea, except instead of using intricate floor patterns, it uses gates like the ones for the Nether.MonsterHunt - Kill monsters, get points. Every night, when the sun goes down, the monsters will come out. Try for a high score. Kills get rewards like items and (powered by iConomy) currency.So here's my vision for the server: Players can set up their own towns, shops, etc. We operate using the currency set up by iConomy - though I would have to configure costs for everything. At night, those brave souls who are interested can go out and fight monsters, earn points, and get money for their efforts. The money is used to buy blocks from others, or possibly from a "bank", and thus the economy is bolstered. Of course, players can also just go digging and then sell whatever they dig up.The big problem I see with all of this is that on a server with only eight people - and when only one or two players are on at a time - it wouldn't really be sustainable. We could open it to more players but have initial permissions be really restricted - even such that players cannot enter the towns of others, so they can't mess with stuff within those bounds. That way, town owners can let only those they want into town, and any griefing that occurs is their own fault.Doing so would also mean discarding McMyAdmin as a server wrapper, but I'm not really too bothered about that.Oh, and the MonsterHunt plugin would make the idea to re-enact L4D more fun, I think. It would keep score of our kills.
