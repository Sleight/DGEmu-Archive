Name: MechAssault: Phantom WarGenre: MechRelease dates: Japan Unknown, US 09/12/2006, EU UnknownDeveloper: Backbone Entertainment Company: Majesco Games__________I have scene many great Mech Warrior games in the past for the PC. The coolest being Mech Warrior Vengeance <3 . But I really hate the DS version. The Stylus is not very sensitive when you try to aim at the enemy with the mech and it always shifts back to the center. Since its so hard to aim moving and shooting the same target is extremely hard plus it takes too many shots to kill a simple tank. O and when the enemy strikes from the back it takes a real effort to make a U-turn so you would loose a great deal of health just like that. Graphics are quite nice I think everything is pretty smooth but its 130MB which is the biggest Rom file I have seen so far (when unzipped). I think graphics could be better if compared to FF3The buttons controls are not implemented too well when you use the stylus because when you use the stylus it’s impossible use the hover button comfortably as well. When you switch weapons you have to touch buttons on the touch pad, meaning you can't constantly enjoy the action on the top screen. There aren’t even enough buttons on the DS for you to switch between two weapons.The controls for the pc are great especially if u have the joy stick because the two weapons buttons are on the joy stick. BUT since Nintendo couldn't put buttons onto the stylus its not as fun as fans would expect it to be.Graphics 8/10Game Play 6/10I don't know how good the story is because its a pain in the donkey to finish just the first level. But its sure is fun STEPING on people (at least for the first few times)It’s my opinion anyway.
