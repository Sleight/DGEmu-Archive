Release DatesRelease dates for actual game cartridges can be found online at sites such as GameFAQs, GameStop, EBGAmes, and Nintendo.com. We do not dump GBA roms, so we have no means of determining when a particular rom will be released. Do not ask for ANY release dates because you can find them with a quick visit to any one of the sites mentioned above.--In-Game HelpSimilarly, in-game help can be found at sites such as GameFAQs and NeoSeeker. Chances are that everything you could possibly ask for is already included in the walkthroughs, FAQs, and message boards on those sites.If you can't find the information on those two sites, go search on Google for other gaming sites or other gaming resources. Unless you are playing a Japanese-only game, you will almost undoubtedly find a plethora of English material on that game.As a last resort, you can start a topic in the Spam Center. In-game help threads are not tolerated in this forum.--CheatsCheats can be found at sites like these:GameFAQs: http://www.gamefaqs.com/Codejunkies: http://www.codejunkies.com/Gameshark: http://www.gameshark.com/Codebreaker: http://www.codebreaker.com/If you still can't find them, there is a very handy invention called a search engine that you can use to find cheats online. Try Google or Yahoo! to start off.--Anyone who starts a topic on any one of these subjects will be penalized through point deductions and/or warnings in addition to having his or her topic closed.
