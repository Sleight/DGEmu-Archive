Don't get a Kinect.  Gen 1 Kinect was a travesty.  It barely functions.  But if you insist, then no, you don't need an AC Adapter with the newer versions of the 360.  The original version (20GB version) didn't have the port for power, but these models do.

 

The extra USB port isn't super useful, neither is the Digital Optical audio.  If you need those, you should probably know you need them before you purchase.

 

The E model was released alongside the Xbox One so both consoles looked relatively similar.  They are exactly the same functionality wise.  I'd go with the 500GB just to get the bigger HDD.  360s use proprietary HDDs, so it's unlikely you'd ever expand your storage.  But there was a firmware update to give them support for external HDDs a few months ago, so you could go that route if you needed more storage.
