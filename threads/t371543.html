Well, I think the key that a lot of people miss is that you have to go into it with a plan of some sorts. It doesn't help to just jump in without any idea of how you're going to do anything. Color scheme, placement, effects, all of this should at least cross your mind before you dedicate too much to the sig.But then again that's with most things you make =P It's not absolutely necessary of course, and the more you know the easier it'll be to wing it, but it's still just a good idea.
MMoon ChildJan 02 2008, 01:01 AM
It's almost identical to what I ask my clients to point out to me (what they want in their designs):- Color scheme- Width/height- Main image focus- What type of effects would benefit the main image- Contrasting colors usually don't work unless it's black and whiteEven if the said client were to give the same answers to two different designers they'd still have different outcomes, and that is because of what you said, imagination. Each designer has their own style and ways to go about things. I never plan anything, usually it's always a spur of the moment thing that I get, while others have to sort it out like a carefully laid plan. Everyone's methods are different, but the above points are what should be considered to do something successful.
