CCat in the HatOct 12 2012, 05:54 PM
JB has some nifty new features, like expandable notifications and quick reply to SMS. It's also so smooth and pretty. If you can root, do it and upgrade to a JB based rom (though CM10 is probably not the best). I have Resurrection Remix on mine, and I hear Thunderbolt is good. Only real problem with JB roms is RAM management. You'll be needing to reboot your phone every so often, but you should do that anyways. Also, if you're going to upgrade, make sure to get a kernel with BLN support, because it's awesome and very useful.

Also, I highly doubt old CM6/7 themes will be compatible with CM10 roms. If you want to try, just install and see what happens. Make a Nandroid backup before you do and you're set. Then it doesn't really matter if you up.

As for installing a newer version, no, you don't have to reset to factory (though after a few upgrades it's recommended). But you should Wipe cache and dalvik cache every upgrade, then you can just flash a new version and you're ready to go. Doesn't hurt to run a kernel cleaning script either (I'm sure there's one in zip form you can just flash real quick in your phone's xda forums).

JB > everything else
I've got it on my GSII, nook color, and Nexus 7 and enjoy it all around.

CCat in the HatOct 12 2012, 06:21 PM
Umm, well, I assume you have Clockwork Mod Recovery Installed (CWM) or TWRP?

This is where you install zips from your SD card.

Kernel Cleaning Script - gets rid of remnants of old kernels (useful if you flashed another)
BLN - back notification support (for if you want the LEDs on your phone to blink when you get a notification) It is only supported by certain kernels
Nandroid Backup - when you go to Backup/Restore in CWM or TWRP and you make a regular backup, this is a nandroid backup, it backs up everything but your personal information
Flash - when you install a new zip from CWM or TWRP
kernel - just determines what's supported by your phone. One is included in any custom rom, sometimes there are more and you have an option, and sometimes you have to go hunt down a zip file for yourself and flash it
Resurrection Remix and Thunderbolt - custom JellyBean based Roms for phones (may not be supported on your phone, idk)
Dalvik Cache - you wipe it when you install a new rom or upgrade an old one. It's under the Advanced tab on CWM
Cache - also wipe when you flash a new rom, it's just under "Wipe Cache partition" or something similar

That help?
