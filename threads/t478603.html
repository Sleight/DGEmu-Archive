KKula DiamondJan 11 2013, 02:06 AM

- It's made by Ninja Theory not Capcom's DMC team.

- The new character design is bad.

- Lots of the dialogues are just people screaming "Fuck You!" at each others.

 

 

But the real reasons are:

- It's looked at 30fps(original was 60) yet it can't keep it 30 all the time! It can be played at 60 on PC but that doesn't fix it as the animation were made for a 30fps game.

- Dumped down combat, and combat is the single most important thing in DMC game... So, yea, this is the main complaint here..

- The backgrounds look nice but almost everything else looks shit, the animation, character models and special effects looks way worse than DMC4 which was released in 2008 and runs at twice the frame rate!

- lots of unskippable cut-scenes(going by the demo), broken style system, bad enemy designs/types(going by the demo), and tones of minor annoying stuff.

 

Most of the hate comes from the thought that because of this game, we didn't get(and wont before two more years at earliest) a proper DMC game, and the fear that if this game sells well we might never get a proper DMC again, ever.

 

That being said, it's not that much of a bad game in it self, it's just a huge let down from DMC3 and 4. There's a PSN demo, try it if you're interested. If you end up liking it, pirate the game and buy DMC HD Collection.
