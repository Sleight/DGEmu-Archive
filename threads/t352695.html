Single Player RPG's own MMORPG's for the following reasons:1) Better Graphics: High end rpg's (TES 4: Oblivion) are sooo much better looking than their online counterparts (even WoW or EVE online). It's because the MMORPG's must work on everyone's computer and update regularly without lag.2) Better Sound: Online RPG's have the same crappy 2 min loop going forever...Most people end up listening to their own music instead. However on single player games, there are full soundtracks, performed by professional orchestras.3) Better Gameplay: MMORPG = Mindless Repetitive Killing for hours, A massive lack of interesting quests, and impossibly expensive high-level items.Single player game = Challenging and Puzzling Quests, Good Bosses, and a feasable leveling structure.Despite these three major reasons, millions of people still play MMORPG's because: - The Community: Interacting with fellow players, participating in PvP, guilds, shops, party quests, etc. is the drawcard of all MMORPG's. No player ever makes it far into a MMORPG without becoming involved in the community. Some people don't even play the game for the battles, they just want to talk to people with similar interests. - The achievements: Reaching the level cap of any online game is an achievement. It means you have a special kind of dedication, and your game peers will respect that. On a personal level, there is kudos for your determination to reach the pinnacle of months/years of work and fun. Congratulations, you have no life.Personally, I have really bad internet, and I don't want to have to go and play every day for my guild...I'd rather play a good single player RPG than an MMORPG !
