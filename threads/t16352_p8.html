FFloyd5642Sep 27 2015, 07:02 AM

It depends on what you want out of the game. If you just want to go and beat the final boss characters and your rival, then yeah. I would say that Pokemon is ridiculously easy. It's basically a glorified game of Rock-Paper-Scissors after all. If you know the Pokemon that are coming, you can construct a team that will beat them. Say you take Pokemon Yellow as an example (yeah, I'm old school). So I played it and had my team set up as follows; Pikachu, Venusaur, Charizard, Blastoise, Snorlax and hmmm I don't remember the last one, but I don't think it was Mewtwo...I think it was either an Alakazam or a Kadabra. So back to what I was saying, the Gyms, and most of the other trainers you encounter don't actually vary the type of Pokemon they use. So you can steamroll through them with one Pokemon type.

 

Getting all of the Pokemon is a bit of a challenge but it isn't impossible. All you need is time, and another version of the game in the same generation. Admittedly, it would take quite a while to catch all of them nowadays, isn't the total up to 718 Pokemon? Back when I was a kid you could get 150 in a snap.

 

Now I never did this myself, but I hear that playing Human Players is usually more of a challenge. Not because they all go to level 100 or so (that only takes time) but because once you have played that much you start to go into a psychological battle. You have to know the limits of each Pokemon; what TMs and HMs they can use, how high you can get their stats at level 100, how fast you can get them to be level 100, how much damage an attack does based on those stats. From what I have heard, all of that stuff is basic once you get to that level. Then again, I don't know. I never did get to that level.
