Downloading and Site Problems Forum Rules
Do Nots/Common Sense Stuff


Do not make any topic regarding any questions addressed in this FAQ or any of the other main FAQs or Rules forum threads in this site.

This includes any problems with any of the ROMs. If you encounter such problems, refer to this or these for help. If problems persist even after following those guides, then ask for help IN THE SPECIFIC EMULATION SUBFORUMS for the systems in which those ROMs belong to, NOT in this forum.
(for example, ask for help with GBA ROMs in the GBA Emulation forum, and not here)
Do not make a topic that does not have a question in it.
Do not post in a topic if you don’t know the answer to a question or if it has already been answered.
Do not make topics complaining about your warn level or point deductions.
Do not ask for emulators.
If you make a topic please put your problem in the topic title or topic description. Do not make topics that just say “Help” or “Suggestion.” You must elaborate.

Failure to follow any of these rules could lead to your points being deducted or a warn being issued.


Q: Are ROMs illegal and can I get into trouble for downloading them? Can DGEmu be shut down for hosting them?
A: There are places where ROMs are illegal. The United States is a good example. If you were caught with ROMs in North America then yes, you could get into legal trouble. However, DGEmu is based out of Israel and therefore the site itself it perfectly legal. ROMs are considered private property in Israel, so there is no direct threat to this site. Remember though, you download at your own risk.

Q: My download did not work. Can I have a DP refund?
A: Not unless the problem was caused by our server. 99.9% of the time it is a problem on your end so don't bother asking unless the server shuts down in the middle of your download.
(No, you won't get DP refunds due to IPS Driver Errors, I'm afraid.)

Q: I keep getting IPS Driver Errors. What should I do?
A: Refresh the page until it loads. If you can't the site might be down, please try again later. We apologise for the inconvenience caused.

Q: I clicked the download button but I didn't get my Rom/got a corrupt file. What happened?
A: Your download was incomplete. Download the ROM again. Take note of these:
1. If you have a pop-up blocker/anti script/firewall/proxy that doesn't have DGEmu allowed then add DGEmu to it.
2. If you are using a download manager or internet accelerator then disable or uninstall it. This includes extensions like Downthemall, Flash Got, and Fasterfox.
3. If you are using Internet Explorer, a yellow bar or message may appear. Just click the link to start your download.
4. Do NOT pause your download. You won't be able to resume it afterwards.
5. Make sure that you have admin rights on your computer.
6. Try right clicking the download button and select "Save Target As" ("Save Link As" for Firefox)
7. Make sure that you have at least twice as much free space as the ROM that you are downloading. You need more than that if you are browsing or downloading other things.
8. If you get an HTML file (if you click your file and it takes you to an internet page) simply change the .html to .zip.

Q: I downloaded a game and it didn't work. Can I have my DPs back?
A: No. Read the pinned FAQs and guides to figure out how to fix the game, or to figure out if the game is even playable. They are listed at the top of the emulation section of the system you are trying to use. DP refunds are only given for download errors caused by the server, and not for any client-side errors or problems with the ROM in an emulator.

Q: Why is there a point system?
A: To put it simply, we need it to limit downloading to conserve bandwidth. Each download uses up a bit of bandwidth. Without such limits, the site would shut down due to lack of bandwidth, as there are so many members downloading on a daily basis. 1DP=1MB of bandwidth.
