I have had bad experiences with physical storage, but the cloud has been good to me, and I absolutely love of for keeping ,y hard to find comic book runs. (Like my Brian Michael Bendis complete Daredevil run). So I thought I'd share what I knew and have learned recently if anyone is interested in these things and get some opinions or better options that maybe I don't k left about.

I've been looking into different cloud storage options so I can backup my comic book collection. Since I no longer use a computer (my tablet does well enough and is more portable) I am at the mercy of my tablets memory, which is lacking.

So far I've used dropbox and Google drive. Both are useful in their own way, but dropbox is the clear winner when it comes to mobile use.



Dropbox
+2GB of free space with opportunities for more
+batch uploading
+exporting to SD
-very expensive for more space


Google Drive
+5GB free space (no more)
+batch uploading
-no native way to export to SD
+solution for exporting ($0.99 for it)
+tons of extra functionality
+syncs with Google account
+cheap for more space

Skydrive
+7GB free space

Box
+5GB free space

I don't know much else about the last two. There is also Amazon storage,which is $1/GB/yr

As for other options

Mediafire and Mega
+50GB free space
-almost devoid of features or a good mobile app
-odd downloading options like CBR downloads as zip or text



Anyways, this is what I've learned about these things over the last day. What is your opinion? Do you like cloud storage? Do you use it often and what for? Which is your favorite?

Personally, I love dropbox, but the price for more space is top high. Like $10/month whereas Google Drive has a solution for it's only major flaw (that being the lack of a native option to export) though the fix is not especially nice, it works just fine and Drive is only $2.50/month for 50GB of additional space. Not the cheapest actually, but considering all the extras drive offers and the syncing with gmail and my android phones and such, its the best deal with the best features for me.

KKirie MotobaMay 09 2013, 12:15 PM

Cloud storage is really a nice thing nowadays. Just keep in mind though: Whatever you store in those clouds can be viewed and be used against you if you were ever suspected for anything related to piracy (so don't keep your apps and cracked stuff there). Where you store the data, the admins all have access to the files, and the encryption on these sites aren't top notch, even if they claim it to be. If you have a business and have client data that is sensitive, do NOT store them onto things like Dropbox and such. For home use however, it's great, especially if you can't afford a new hard disk or a server that shares the files to the Internet as you fit.

 

It's a nice option for you to store files and such, but if you were to use storage like me, having large HD videos and tons of disc image backups, then it won't save you much, especially if you want to recover all the files... Imagine redownloading 2TB of ISOs back when you need it.
