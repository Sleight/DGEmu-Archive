SShadowSep 22 2011, 11:08 PM
That sounds fine. And all retail CPUs come with heatsinks that have thermal paste on them, so you don't need any.Any Youtube guide should work for you. The process is extremely straightforward, I didn't even bother using a guide. The most important thing I can tell you is to MAKE ABSOLUTELY SURE that before you put your motherboard into the case, your case has the spacer screws on it so that the motherboard doesn't come in contact with the case (it rests on the spacer screws). If it touches the case, it could short out and damage the motherboard and possibly the CPU/RAM.
