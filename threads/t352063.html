Oh jeez...well, there's an animation window in imageready. That helps you see your frames. Then basically every layer can be added into an animation frame. So if you're using sprites, you just cut whatever sprite you want and add them into your document. When you select a frame, any visible layers will be added to that frame.It's more complicated than this, but...well that's a start.
Hharuna_13Nov 24 2007, 04:03 AM
[font="Verdana"]So example your using PS CS2 right? Pretend that the layers are like the frames of your animation. It may take a while if you're making a very detailed frame by frame animation but it'll be worth it. When you're done with it, save it as a PSD file or just import it to Photoshop Image Ready. You'll find the button for doing that a little bit at the bottom of the tools [last one in the tools thingy ^^;] Anyway, once imported, Windows>Animation and then, click the arrow in the circle and click "Make frames out of Layers" and just adjust the timing per frame. =D :D [/font]
