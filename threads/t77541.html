Bbooyah_boyORIGINAL POSTOct 12 2005, 04:27 AM
Below are some suggestions (as alluded to in the title of this thread) which I believe may help enliven the community, aid the site, and/or simply be worthwhile to try. Please take a moment to read them over, submit your own opinion, or even add something to them.As many of these would be exclusively hosted by the staff, or else, it would be best not to inform the general membership of such (were they to be enacted) I am posting this here, rather than in the Support & Suggestions forum.Daily/Weekly PollThe idea would be to append a poll (whose topic may vary, although forum or emulation based polls would naturally be preferable) to either the forum or portal homepage, in which each member may vote once. This poll would grant the member a set amount of GPs for their participation (five or so), and could be used to get immediate feedback from the membership on any one of a diverse number of subjects (or merely just for fun), while giving them some reason to do so. It might be changed (and thus, voted in) once each day, or once each week.Apart from giving the staff a quick means of gauging the appeal on any desired subject (say, present a poll on whether or not to institute some new forum feature), it would also prevent the buildup of spam posts in threads which might be used for the same goal (that is, acquiring user feedback).Calendar Based EventsThe following are all grouped under this header as each could be announced on the calendar; this would give the members some reason to check such, as well as notifying new members of upcoming events, thus, spurring them to remain active (furthermore, it would verify the existence of the calendar, which, as of now, does little more than list birthdays).DGEmu's Anniversary BashWell, it doesn't have to be a bash (or party, celebration, or whatever else you desire to call it), but something to celebrate DGEmu having grown a year older might be nice...perhaps even just listing this on the calendar alone.Holiday EventsThe following would be events listed on the calendar which revolve around one of the world-wide (or at least, widely celebrated) holiday events. I list but a few to give an example:Halloween (All Hallows Eve/The Day of the Dead) - Something like a trick-or-treat page hunt; wherein members could surf around from one page to another (or thread) and hit a button, recieving some GPs in return for such.Winter Holiday (Christmas) - We might give away a popular rom for free; for instance (just as a theoretical example), let's say they release of "Megaman BattleNetwork 6" occured around this time, you hold back putting such on the homepage until the specified day, then allow all members to download it free of charge.New Year's Day - Say some kind of giveaway, I cannot even think of what to do, but something to show that we appreciate the members for sticking with us through this year and into a new one.TournamentsLadder-tournaments - hosted by the staff (and apart from the Signature of the Week) - which would give the memebers a chance to compete on a greater scale. We might organize it a few weeks ahead of time, then hold a random drawing to see who will compete against whom, then judge the results to a final winner. The finalist could be presented with a set amount of GPs, access to a free rom of their choice, or some other forum-based prize, and (of course) presented a staff made trophy tag for their signature.We might hold:Grpahic-based tournaments for the creation of wallpaper, banners, buttons, or other items.Writing-based tournaments for the creation of prose (short-stories, and so on) or poetry.IB RPG tournaments involving arena battles, item collections, and so on.The idea could use some fleshing out, however, I believe it could be good fun.Member of the WeekEach week, the site administrators would each select one member (non-staff) who has distinguished themself in some way deemed recognizable (general posting activity, helpfulness, and so on) and nominate them in a poll; said poll would then be placed in the staff forum, wherein each member of the staff would vote, and the winner would be given the "Member of the Week" award; said award could come in any form, though the most obvious would be something like a trophy tag (designed by the staff and displaying them as the member of the week) and a set amount of GP (say 500), perhaps even some DP. If nothing else, it may be an added incentive to remain/become a good and active member of the forums.The way I see it, if each week the award was given at 9:00pm GMT on Saturday, and each administrator had their nomination in by the same time on Friday (giving the members almost a complete week to distinguish themselves), there would remain plenty of time for the staff members to get a say in who should recieve it.Sweepstakes/ContestsThere are currently twenty-one staff members total, which is the primary reason I bring this idea forth, as our numbers make it supportable...but only if each of us agrees to it.If each of us were to submit, say, $10 a month to a prize fund (hereafter referred to as "PF"), that would be $210 dollars per month, or $2520 per year, which amounts to more than enough to purchase tangible prizes for giveaways. It could even be only $5 a month, which would still work out to $105 a month, or a PF of $1260 a year.With that money (the details of who holds it and how to transfer it might be fully worked out later...although with PayPal, it might be done quickly, securely, and easily) we could then purchase real prizes for contests whose basis might vary from a simple puzzle (figure it out and send it to a specific email account with your name and address, and so on), or something more complex.We might host these sweepstakes at set intervals, accumulating a certain amount of money which would enable us to purchase different items; for instance were we to:* Host 1 giveaway every 1 month (which would leave us a PF of either $210 or $105), we would be able to giveaway something like a new game for a select system (NDS, GBA, PS2, etcetera). Either PF total would be more than enough to support the acquisition of a brand-new copy of a newly released hot title.* Host 1 giveaway every 3 months (which would leave us with a PF of either $630 or $315), and giveaway something like a console and game, or just a console (GBA SP/Micro, NDS, perhaps even a PS2).While the prizes might vary, or even be something radically different from what I suggested, surely we can all support it. After all, five (or even ten) dollars a month is little enough for anyone to be able to afford.DGEmu MerchandiseHaving read over PlaySwiss' thread appertaining to such, I was relatively disappointed to see that it had never been followed through on; thus, I would like to bring such to the attention of the staff once more...I know of a few places - much like CafePress, suggested by PlaySwiss - that do not charge you anything unless you sell something first, and then they just rake some stuff off the top. We might design and sell any sort of merchandise, from shirts and mugs, to mousepads and posters; they need not event conspicously bear the DGEmu label or text, but simply a dragon or small decal on top of the larger image (i.e. - the small dragon in a corner of the larger T-shirt design). Surely with so many talented artits in one place (and in one staff organization) coming up with something that would sell should not be too difficult.Furthermore, while I understand that overseas shipping might present a problem, we could overcome such given enough time/thought. Why, we might even host a competition wherein the members are allowed to design a label or graphic icon of some sort for one of the products.See PlaySwiss' thread: http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=30744&st=15Community NewsletterWe might send out a weekly or bi-weekly newsletter to all members via their supplied email addresses - or just to those who subscribe to it, which brings up the aspect of offering a newsletter subscription - in which we might include all sorts of site, forum, and general community news. For instance, we might include any of the following:* a list of the top ten newest roms* a list of upcoming rom releases offered at dgemu* a list of popular threads/topics of discussion* the member of the week (assuming we did such)* information on contests and other community events* site updates: such as staff changes, forum upgrades, system overhauls/additions* etcetera, etcetera, etceteraHopefully, this will not only keep our active members up-to-date, but also encourage those less active/inactive members to come and see what we have to offer.I for one would be willing to commit some time to it's creation, and surely we could find other staff members/hire new members to aid me or run it on their own.DGemu Emulators and TranslationsI understand how vastly the two subjects are separated from one another, however, for simplicities sake I am lumping them both together because certain points I wish to make regarding the incorporation of both, or either, into the site appertain to both.Obviously, as I am certain any one of us can attest to, the demand for emulators is great; sure, while for the most part we reply with smug, snide, or just rant-like replies to use a "search engine" or "try googling it", the fact is that many of the members who visit our site are new to the emulation community. Why, just answering a few letters in the Support Staff mailbox the other day led me to explaining what an emulator is, how it functions, and where to find them (as I have done in the past, and not only in response to contact emails, but also to pm's and threads) multiple times. Thus, the demand is present for emulators that are tried, true, tested, and proven to be the best. In fact, so great is/was the demand for them, that it led to the creation of the "Do Not Ask for the Following" thread in the Rules & Guidelines forum...surely that is a fair indicator of how often they are requested.As to translations, well, what's to be said: with the recent closure of The Whirlpool - the former premier compendium of translation news - and a shortage of other sites of it's calibur (sure, the Romhacking Repository, and Zophar's Domain make a passable effort at compiling the patches and keeping folks updated on their progress, but they are both bogged down with an overabundance of work and a shortage of dedicated staff members), there exists a greater need then ever for a good translation site; one that not only presents the pertinent information, but does such in a manner superior to any before it. The way I see it, the closure of The Whirlpool leaves a great chasm to be filled, one that someone with enough ambition to pursue could quickly fill. Thus - insofar as I can see - the DG-community has made no great strides in that direction...so why not seize the opportunity now to do so; truly, what better time could there be. A really good translation directory, presented in a professional manner (including none of those remarks that used to be posted in The Whirlpool - you know, the half-hearted attempts at humour that told the casual viewer absolutely nothing about the game or the translation/translation company's progress) could easily grab a great deal of attention, if not become the new preferred resource.In fact - insofar as both patches and emulators are concerned, and in an effort to conserve bandwidth - we need not even host a copy of either on the site itself, but merely display a link to the designer/creator's homepage, wherein it may be downloaded.Gamespot-like ROM Rating SystemI know a new rating system has been put forth before, however, I would like to add to such herein.I believe something like that in use on Gamespot.com (one rating by the staff/staff member who reviewed the game - or some chosen staff member - and one by the general body of members) would be best. It would allow the game to acquire an effectual rating that puts topics like the "Recommended GBA Games" thread and their like out of practial use, as - effectively - the staff rated score would reflect an honest (one can only hope) assessment of the game, and the membership concensus vote would reflect that which we attempt to do in said threads (the membership vote would of course reflect the average of all input votes).Games Listing by DeveloperAlong with the much discussed - and thus far, confirmed - entry of a new organization system of games (by genre), I would also like to suggest that we institute a "By Developer" organization system. This would make it easier for those folks who are interested in games produced by any single company (or group of companies) to track down titles manufactured by them; for instance, I know many people who will play a game merely because it was manufactured by Square-Enix, or Atlus, or Namco, and so on.I'm awaiting your input...
