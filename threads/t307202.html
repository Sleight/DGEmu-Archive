Single player story mode you do the same, but mixed in with some RPG elements (doing quests) to help push along the story.World Of Warcraft on the other hand is a MMORPG, you make a character, you focus on leveling that character, getting gear for it, and overall trying to improve the character. You do that by doing PvE content like quests, grouping to do instances (think dungeons), killing NPCs (monsters and whatnot) in the outdoor world or killing other player's characters in PvP combat, in the outdoor world, in battleground matches (maps designed with team based goals) and in rated arena matches with a small group of teammates.They may have the same name, with the same lore... but you can't compare them. One is always static, and you control and manage an army and base in full RTS style. The other is always growing and you control and manage one character in a full RPG style.Sorry for my rant, but whenever I see people trying to compare two games that shouldn't be compared to to game types, it can drive me nuts, hehe...
