DOWNLOAD POINTS (DPs)Now, firstly, DPs stand for 'Download Points' or something similar. These points are need for downloading roms. Logical, no?Ways to get DP:1. Convert your earned GPs from this board to DPs. Each post is 6gp, a new topic is 10gp. Its 30gps to 1 Global DP.Convert them in the GP->DP Converter, which can be found here: http://www.dgemu.com/Convert.html [link locations: main site/forums]This conversion rate will change depending on the rating you have. For every 1 rating you need 9% more GP to convert. However, with minus ratings, its -9% for every one minus rating you have.2. Global DPs will reset every 24 hours (at midnight, GMT+2) back to 12.5. If you have 3.05 DPs, when it resets, you will have 12.5. If you have 28 DPs, it will reset to 12.5DPs. They do not build up. And on a slightly related note, DPs converted from GPs (as from above) will not reset, and will stay until you use them up. However, these are used before your Global DPs, so it would be recommended that you only convert when you need the specified console DPs.3. Donate $10US, and you get 550 NDS/GBx DPs (which wont reset) you get, (PS, $20US = 1200 NDS/GBx DPs, the thorough donation conversions can be found here). The DP you get from donating can be specified if you want NCDP, Sega DP, etc instead of NDS/GBx DP. Apparently, once you've donated and don't want it all as NDS/GBx DP, the Donator's center allows you to convert them into other DP.You can't convert from Global DP to System DP nor from System DP to Global DP, though.4. Helping the site.There are certain ways in which you can add content to the site and be rewarded for it. You can submit a review of a game, and that'll earn you 10DPs only if it's of high quality (as judged by the staff. What they are looking for can be found in Reviews FAQ). Also, every now and then, there may be an opening in the screenshotters team. Check the main page for openings. Being a member of the screenshotters team is also a good way to contribute to the site and earn DP for doing so.5. DP LoanNot so much as a way to earn them, but there is a new DP Loan system which allows you to obtain more DPs which can be used over a set a period of time.Note that Each type of DP is used for specific roms. List as follows:NDS/GBx DPs for the NDS, GBA, GBC, GBNCDPs for N64, SNES, NESAtari for Atari Jaguar, Atari Lynx, Atari 2600,Sega for Sega Genesis, GameGear, Sega Master System (SMS)Retro for MAME, Neo Geo Pocket (Color), Wonderswan (Color), PC Engine - TurboGrafx-16, Sam CoupeGPsCredit towards Dgemu_NoobGPs are the currency of the forums and stand for Gold Pieces.But what are they for? Well, y'see, the many uses include:Converting into DPs to download hereBuying profile customizations at the DGStoreAnd... thats about it. GPs do not decrease or disappear unless you break forum rules.Yes, they are important, especially for downloading, so how to get them...?Posting a reply in a non-spam area will get you 6gpsPosting a topic in a non-spam area will get you 10gpsGlobal Moderators and up may award you gps for outstanding posts. However, it is up to their discernment so please do not bug them about it. Similarly, they may deduct GPs for spam posts or those of poor quality.Members who donate can convert their DPs into GPs here: http://www.dgemu.com/dp_convert.php(As per usual, got more ideas?
