What are some PC games you grew up with? What was some of your first games? What games do you sit back and say "damn I haven't played that in a long time"For me, PC gaming started in first grade, but it was at school. It started with The Oregon Trail for the Macintosh PCs, and SimTown. Which were on the computers in the library. There was also Treasure Mathstorm which I remember quite well. Also Number Munchers and so on. Yes these are educational games, but they started to get me into PC gaming. In 1998, my parents got are first PC. I remember the first 3 games I had was Incoming (which came with the PC) and the first 2 games I ever bought was NASCAR Racing and SODA Off-Road Racing. Then after that came the FPS games such as Half-Life, Quake II, Wolfenstein 3D, and so on. Then simulation games such as The Sims, Sim City 2000, and Rollercoaster Tycoon in the later years. I look back at alot of the PC games I own. Also games such as Need For Speed II, and III, Test Drive: Off-Road, Deus Ex, Lego Island, and Soldier of Fortune were PC games of the past I enjoy playing. There were even games that were ****ty that were fun, but looking back now, I think "damn, did I really enjoy this game that much?" I miss the days of PC games, between 1998 and 2004. Some awesome games. I do wish I would of had a PC before 1998 though, but luckily I had alot of friends who had computers and I would play games such as DooM, Duke 3D, Road Rash and so on.
