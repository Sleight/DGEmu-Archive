For numerical reference

I have an apartment in NJ, 15 mins from Manhattan by public transportation
Rent is ~$700/month, that's about average if you can get a 2-4 bedroom and a roommate (or several), studios will be at least $1k/month
Utilities + internet is ~$100/month
Food is ~$200/month, but that's mostly cheap branded groceries and cooking, and eating out rarely. If you eat out often, it'll be like double-triple that

So about $1k/month for expenses if living frugally, which is very doable
Cost of living is about the same in the other boroughs of NYC, probably a good bit higher in Manhattan
