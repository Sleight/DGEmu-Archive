Yeah, it does.

Generally resetting the auto attack timer is negating the animation that plays after you 'trigger' your AA (so Melee, it would be connecting with something, for ranged it would be once your projectile is in the air) before it plays into another loop.

As far as I know, some spells/skills won't actually negate the time it takes to play the animation, so you'll still have to wait. For example, I believe if you auto-attack, then use Rupture (which has a short cast animation), you'll just stand there until the next loop of your AA animation goes to play.

Off the top of my head, Vi's E, Nasus' Q, Teemo's Q, and Jax's Empower will all reset the attack timer.
