RrabbitwingsFeb 04 2014, 08:45 PM

I used to play Mineral Town a lot, and I enjoyed it, but like most people say it depends on what you like in a game.

 

It's a farm simulator, and a lot of the game revolves around nurturing your crops, and eventually animals, so that you can gain enough money to maintain the place and upgrade your farm and tools to be more efficient. I find the gameplay soothing; others might find it monotonous.

 

You can get married to certain members of the townsfolk and go around befriending everyone else, and throughout the game you'll be able to see various events and attend festivals. Much of this is optional, but it helps you become attached to the little community in the game.

 

Many versions of the game have some sort of ultimate goal, or at least end after a few years in-game, which might be reccommended because otherwise the game starts to feel a bit pointless after a while.
