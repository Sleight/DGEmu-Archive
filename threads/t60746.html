Despite its similarities in name, Sword of Mana isn't exactly a sequel to the classic SNES adventure, Secret of Mana. It's actually a rethinking and retelling of a past Game Boy classic RPG created by Square, Final Fantasy Adventure released a decade ago. Sword of Mana, while sharing the same storyline and characters, has been enhanced with improved graphics and sound, as well as much more impressive creatures and extended challenges. It's almost like an entirely new adventure. The game also features link-up support that will enhance the single-player adventure; the more friends that players link up to, the more powerful the special "Amigo" attack will become during the battles.

Well,Funny you should ask. I just happen to be playing it again as we speak. As you might be able to tell from my name, I'm a huge "Mana" fan. Actually, I should share a little history. The series is easier followed if you look at the Japanese market where there are similar titles.Seiken Densetsu, which I'm told from a number of sources (one of which being an excelent article on Wikipedia) translates as "Holy Sword Legend," was released in the United States of America as Final Fantasy Adventure.Seiken Densetsu II was released in the U.S.A. under the title Secret of Mana and for anyone who enjoys RPG's or Adventure games, this is a must-play! It essentially defines the series and is the reason for the recent development of new games for the series.Seiken Densetsu III was never released in the U.S.A., but was so popular (many say due to Secret of Mana's success) that there are good legal ROM's available that fans have translated into English. This game has a little more of a Final Fantasy feel than the rest of the games in the series if you ask me. Since I don't care for Final Fantasy games that much, I tend to not enjoy Seiken Densetsu III's game-play. The story is worth playing the game for, however.What many have referred to as the forth game in the canon, Legend of Mana, does not, in fact, have a number attached to it. This game is more of a gaiden, or side-kick to the series. Seiken Densetsu IV is actually in production now.All this leads me to say that Sword of Mana came out as a re-make of Final Fantasy Adventure which was really the first game in the Holy Sword Legend series. The best part of the re-make, in my estimation, is that the story is enhanced because of the power of the Game Boy Advance versus the Game Boy. If you are a long-time fan of the Holy Sword Legend series, this is a must-play. It will fill in cracks in your understanding of the story-line found in Secret of Mana. You may want to start with that game so that you can play the series in chronological order, or you may want to start with Secret of Mana. I'll leave that up to you. Either way, check out Sword of Mana. You won't be disappointed!Happy playing,treeofmana
