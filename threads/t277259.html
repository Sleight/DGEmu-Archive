Taken from a PM before Ody resigned:
someone wrote:
1. Creating "Promotion Points"It is common sense that to have an efficient staff, we have to give them the feeling that they are increasing in ranks, in other words, giving them the perspective of being promoted is the best mean to motivate them to do more job. Some people like Fal would say "if they don't have the patience to wait without longing to be promoted, they don't worth to be promoted. I waited for years and now I am here". I find these sort of comments quite misplaced. Afterall, modding is not like a noble cause that people have to carry sithout any second thoughts. Of course, it is not possible to promote everyone every month since there are only 5 ranks among the staff. Rather, we can create a sort of "Promotion Points", something like a warn log.Each month, the council will meet and each smod+ will give 3 points to bmods and SS. These points can be given together or separatly. Whenever someone reaches 50 points, he/she is promoted. That will certainly make bmods and SS more motivated to work more so as to get these points. Of course everyone has to justify why he/she gave these points. If a position is left open or if more staff is needed, then the person with the highest number of points is promoted.For smods, points should be given by sentinels and administrators only, not by themselves.Anyway, the idea behind is to avoid people behind demotivated: Amnesiac left once because he felt there was no real perspective. Dualist's last resignation was made after Fal's promotion and after PS denied his request to be promoted as well and then miraculously came back after PS promised him to make him sentinel... and other examples but you won't like them :oI think it won't hurt much if such system was built since at this rate, it would take months before someone is really promoted because he/she reached 50 points so that it won't be too different from now, it is just for staff to have a general idea of when then can be promoted.2. "Real" MAIt is a common saying that MA means nothing. Actually, it is only partly true. If we have few MAs, it means we do nothing. I was thinking about another MA scale. Instead of counting all the current actions such as point redection as MA, why not creating another count with only real MA? By real Ma, I mean:- Closing a thread by providing a reason- Issuing positive warnsWarn issuing count should be pretty easy to set up. For closing a thread by providing a reason, here is what I can think of:- editing either the first or last post.- post & close: in the mod log you showed me when you just become sentinel, I noticed that there was a difference between "closed" and "locked" (but I forgot which one is the appropriate). You can count the good one.- post & move: same as above.- set invisible/visible.The idea is to get rid of MA from point deduction and quick thread closing.Of course, there is way to cheat this count but you should keep this secret between administrators only. When a promotion is about to occur, you can check very quickly who is really doing his/her job.
Does anyone agree with any parts of these suggestions? I for one feel that we should have a slightly more scientific approach to promotions, but hey, maybe that's just me.

DDJ Night Force 9Jun 26 2007, 01:03 PM
It would seem that the points system is really no different than the current one:1. A promotions council thread opens2. Staff nominates potential candidates3. Staff votes4. Candidate with the most votes gets the positionYour description of how it should be just replaces "votes" with "points". I believe that if moderators or other staff want a promotion, they will already be motivated to work harder. Even then it may not be true though. Believe it or not, not everyone is aiming to make it to higher ranks. Some are perfectly content with where they are. Ody himself can relate to this when he said "I wish I could have been in support staff longer" and then there's Dualist. He really didn't like being a sentinel because he found the ACP a bit rather confusing and SMod suited him well. The same may go for others and in such a case, we could just choose the one with the "second most" points but again, we already do that anyway using the vote system.So yeah, to summarize, your idea seems to be a morphed version of the existing system with the delay of having to "accumulate points". Now if everyone feels that even if this does not really "add perspective" to the promotions process (or maybe you feel it does), it will satisfy more current staff as they will know how close they are to being bumped up (and not get the impression that the longer they are in their current position, the quicker they get promoted as some have mistakenly believed and asked me why person X got promoted when person X was on staff half as long), then by all means let us make it so. Since it is similar to the current system, then changing over should not be too difficult.As for "real" MAs: I'd just say stop including point deductions as MAs. These are very quick, arbitrary, and do not depict a quality moderator. Anyone can go into those huge topics like "Favourite NDS Game" and pick off all the overly short posts. It is those that go the extra step to provide links to the original thread in dupe topics or in warn logs, give some helpful advice if a member breaks a rule so they can avoid further infractions (not that everyone will follow it though) that should be considered for higher ranks. For support staff it's rather easy as all we'd have to find out is who is answering the most e-mails. So if this kind of stuff can be "counted", then great but I think it's something that moreso has to be "observed" though.Anyhow, that's my opinion on things.
