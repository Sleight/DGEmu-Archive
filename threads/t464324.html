I do agree with you on this, it seems like they pale in comparison to the old games. I am more of a PC gamer anymore, but it is like this no matter which platform you’re into.Every system had their hits and their bad games, but it is starting to be more bad games than good. The originality of them is starting to get lost, they all got this pre-processed plot feel to them.The most recent game I got was Final Fantasy XIV, I got suckered into it because it was one of my favorite childhood games on the NES. After I got started I forced myself to play it, I spent $50 on it and I was determined to try and get some satisfaction out of my money, but it didn’t get any better. In fact the more I played it the more I wished I was playing the original Final Fantasy game on Nintendo.But with game designers today they have a big problem with getting caught up in other parts of the game production. It seems like they get so wrapped up into making all the nice 3D models and fancy FX and graphics that they completely forget about making a good storyline and adding in good content and cram in a bunch of crap at the last minute so it has a storyline.And any of the good existing titles either have been canceled or died out due to the fact that they ruined them by going to generic or made too much of a change to it that it lost its originality.Zelda is a great title so let’s take that game for instance, rather than starting a new game and storyline, they would be far better off taking the original storyline and game and just beefing it up with today’s graphics.
