[heading]DGEMU reviews #1: Exerion (Nes)[/heading]
To be honest, donâ€™t like nes games all that much. Nes games on this console aren't as fleshed out as later generation games,especially the snes. The designers of snes really put some thought into making a good game. That doesn't mean however, that nes games themselves are bad. There is a good deal of games on this system that are worth playing. But, some great games never made it beyond Japan... Exerion is a fine example of such game. First released as an arcade game, exerion was ported to several consoles. At one point Jaleco considered selling the game in the US, but they didn't take the risk. Big BUT however, a US prototype was actually leaked and it can be downloaded on this site. It is even better than the Japanese nes release!

Exerion is a space ship shooter like space invaders and galaga. You control a space ship on the bottom of the screen, baddies come in from the top (or sometimes from the sides) and you have to shoot them down to score points. Like these games, you are limited on lives and can only get extras by scoring many points

If I were to stop the review here, you would think that exerion is a drab spaceship shooter with nothing much to offer. Youâ€™d be sorely wrong however, because unlike usual spaceship shooters, exerion actually offers a lot of fun to the player.

Gameplay
Wait didn't I just summarize the gameplay pretty well back there? Nope, exerion has a few tricks up its sleeves to keep the game fresh. Even though you have free movement of your ship and the game mostly plays like normal spaceship shooters do, exerion throws you a few curve balls.

First off is moving the ship, one of the very first things you'll do in this game. Your ship is pretty responsive when you press the d-pad, and it goes pretty much anywhere you like. However, when you release it, your ship needs to decelerate, which means that it won't make a dead stop immediately due to inertia. By doing this, Jaleco puts a lot of pressure on the player because you canâ€™t count on it that releasing your d-pad will stop your ship immediately as well.

Second off is shootingby pressing A, you will fire your standard shot. It's simple shot, but you can fire very rapidly, if you're using a turbo button, you will literally send out a stream of bullets. However, you must use them sparingly, because these shots have an ammo reserve. Sure, you can get more ammo by shooting your enemies, however you will most likely miss your target with those shots more often than that you will get your ammo back.

By pressing B, you will fire a double shot. Great! But it wouldnâ€™t be fun if there were no drawbacks. Unlike your rapid shot, the double shot takes awfully long to shoot again. The fire rate is comparable to that of space invaders, but the enemies are way less aggressive and you also have some barriers protecting you. In this game it's you and your enemies, nothing in between. That sounds awful doesnâ€™t it? Well, the only saving grace of this shot is that it doesn't consume ammo. Get ready to rely on that B button a lot in later levels.

Lastly, the enemies in this game are no slouch. There are several varieties with different attack patterns. Add in different kinds of projectiles that can be fired. If you add it all up,the game can become a complete mayhem in a matter of seconds. Luckily the game gives you a breather sometimes by throwing in a bonus stage.

A special mention goes to the two player game mode. You can compete against your friend for the highest score via the hot seat principle (first player one plays and then player two). However, this doesn't add anything to the game at all, and only serves for bragging rights.

Graphics
This game is an arcade port and it stays true to its coin-operated daddy. Your playing field is still as large as the arcade version, meaning that the screen is vertically oriented. In this port, you can scroll in any direction up to the game's boundaries, giving you quite some room to dodge. Of course, this also means that enemies can crawl out of these blind spots and get in for a cheap kill. It doesn't happen too often, if you watch out for it.

There's also good news, the scrolling background from the original has been preserved. If you fly close to the ground, you can actually see the ground and objects flying past you. It's a pretty neat effect and a big achievement, if you are aware how much (or little) the nes can do. The enemies you look down pretty generic and your ship's is also pretty generic. But while playing the game, it doesnâ€™t look too bad

Sound
No music here, only sound effects. But at least they're good sound effects. They aren't ear piercing and can be pretty neat to listen to. Once again, the sound effects are pretty standard and I could swear that I've heard some of the same sound effects in galaga.
Not much else to say about it here, other than that it does its job.

Summary
Exerion is one of the master pieces that never hit the United States or Europe, despite being one of the greatest games on the system. The gameplay mechanics adds a lot of thought to the game. The only things that I can think of that could've been done better are the scrolling of the screen and fleshing out the multi-player more. Iâ€™d recommend playing this game in short bursts if you have nothing to do, or if you are in old school shoot 'em ups.

Score: 9/10
