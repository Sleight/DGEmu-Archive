Brawl will last a lot longer, because The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword is due around the corner, and will effectively replace TP as the dominant Zelda game on the Wii. And considering that Nintendo is very passionate about this particular number, I can guarantee that it will outrun SSBB.
