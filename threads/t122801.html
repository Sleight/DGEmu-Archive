You can make an avatar from any picture off the internet (make sure it's not someone else's artwork, though). You just copy and paste it into an art program, crop the part of the picture you want down to the size you want it to be, save it into to the filetype you need, and upload it. This works best if the picture is small, or when you crop it there won't be any detail in the avatar.
