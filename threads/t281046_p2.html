I've been meaning to replay IX for awhile now. I was reading some FF retrospectives recently on gamespite.com (awesome website if you like games and or spite. I happen to like both), and it seems like, plot-wise, FFIX's story has held up the most out of the three Playstation Final Fantasies.Final Fantasy VII's story was cool at first, but it really took a turn for the insane near the end, what with Cloud being crazy and all. FFVIII I never liked, likely because I couldn't get my head around the junction system (at least at the young age of... 17). Owing to this, I never actually beat it, but from what I've heard, the plot gets very time-travely/parallel universy near the end, and while personally I find those things awesome, their sudden inclusion at the end of the game doesn't strike me as good storytelling. Also, I hated every single character in FFVIII. Every. single. one. Though Seifer gets honorable mention. Everything thing he says just makes me want to punch him in the face.FFIX ditches the insanity and time travel for... space aliens and darth vader look-a-likes. *cough*Yeah, there's that, but that stuff is ancillary to what FFIX is really about: death, and coming to terms with your own mortality. This guy explains it a lot more eloquently than I ever could, so check it out if you're so inclined:http://www.gamespite.net/toastywiki/index....yFinalFantasyIXWhat I think really makes FFIX great is its characters. They're endearing as hell. Vivi, I think, is one of the best written/most interesting characters in the entire FF series (and that's saying something in a series full of great characters) His tale is funny, charming, and heart-breaking all at the same time. Similarly, Zidane's cheerfulness and various failed attempts at hitting on the princess was a refreshing change of pace after Cloud and Squall's broodfests.Anyhoozles, yeah, FFIX holds a special place in my heart, so I certainly hope it's not the least popular in the FF series. I still think I may like VII more than IX, but that's mainly based on FFVII's music.
