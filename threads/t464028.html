A cache in computer terms is pretty much what the dictionary implies. A place where things get stored so they can be accessed quickly.As for Random Access. Hard drives use round platters. Think of it as being kinda like a vinyl album or a CD with the data stored in a big spiral only the storage is magnetic. Different pieces of data may be on different physical sections of the drive and while drives may be fast it still takes time to physically move the drive heads to the right section of the platters to read the data. Flash drives don't have moving parts so it doesn't take any extra time to read data regardless of where on the drive it is.All that said you are much better off forgetting readyboost and just buying a SSD for you OS and the software you use most often and a large normal drive for storing movies, music, ROMs, etc...
