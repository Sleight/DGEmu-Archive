Just a bit of info about the 'Super Sweeper' item, in the pre-release demo of FF7, you played through the reactor scene from the start of the game, there was one major difference though; the boss wasn't the Guard Scorpion, but was in fact the 'Super Sweeper'If you want to see a screenshot from the demo, here's a link; http://www.ffshrine.org/ff7/samp/30.jpgAlso, just so you know, the following items do nothing in game (there may be others)Super SweeperMasamune Replica1/32 SoldierTissueAutographAny rumours that these items do anything are FALSE, any rumours that you can revive Aerith/Aeris are FALSE (If you Gameshark her in, she remains invisible for many scenes, as she was never programmed to be in them, and she often can glitch the game), any rumours that you can recruit anyone extra other than Yuffie or Vincent are also FALSE (the game only has code for two others, Sephiroth and Young Cloud, and if you shark either of them into your party, the game generally glitches bigtime)To cut a long story short, most of these and other rumours have been disproven by looking at the games code and data through Gameshark or similar devices, and there is nothing surprising there (except a few non-working items and materia, which were started on, but never made it into the final cut)
