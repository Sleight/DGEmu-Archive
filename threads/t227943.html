WWISMay 03 2007, 02:53 AM
It really isn't a big problem. You just need to learn how to save things properly without losing any quality at all. For example, instead of saving the image as a PNG, you can save it as a JPEG. When the optimization window pops up, you can set it to a minimum of 10 without losing much quality, but setting it to 12 retains all quality and it has a small sigsize. (Note that this is NOT in the Save as Web, but simply Save-as.)PNG-8, in my opinion, isn't a very good file format. Seeing as how it is exactly the same as a GIF. PNG-8 limits the graphic to 256 colors. Unless your signature is greyscale, or completely monotone (I.e. color hued), I would advise against saving in PNG-8 or GIF if you want perfect quality.
