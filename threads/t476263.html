Well, for people buying used games, the money goes to the store (Gamestop) and not the game development company.
So yes, theoretically, the game dev company is making a loss per used game purchased as the profit gets wheeled in to them.

On the other note, to have a used game in the first place, you need someone what would have bought the game new. And the people that buy games new often trade in their games that they completed in order to get some cash to get more new games.

By disallowing resell of games, they are shooting themselves in the foot as those gamers that buy games new to resell once done might not be buying as many games anymore...
