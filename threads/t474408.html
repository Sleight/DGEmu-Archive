Finding a manga artist is going to be tough, especially one who will do it for free. Though there are two sites you might be able to find your artist: deviantart.comfanfiction.net What I could recommend for you to do is make a novel version or draft version of your manga and post it on fanfiction.net. You might get an artist interested in doing the art for your manga. My sister is also an artist and frequents this two places a lot. If you want to find an artist right away though, stop on by to deviantart and lookup some manga artist there and ask if they are willing to do the art for you for free. I'll check in with my sister for other info she might give to you.
