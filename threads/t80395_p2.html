UU238ShellJun 27 2010, 03:39 PM
A lot of people are either mis-informed, fanboys or don't know what they're talking about.AMD is more a budget processor, they've competely taken over the mid-price market with their processors. Their hex core processor is only around $300 which is amazing compared to Intel's hex core which is hovering around a grand. Very cheap per core and also cheap performance. AMDs processors also have no cold bug (processor gets so cold it gets unstable) and also can take a lot more voltage than Intel processors.Intel's processors are THE processors to get if you have a lot of money, want overclocking and higher performance. Intel's processors are amazing overclockers, you can hit 5GHz if you have the processor, right cooling, motherboard, luck and skill. Intel also can't take as much voltage as AMD but can run a lot hotter. It's max temperature is around 90 degrees where as with AMD, you don't want it above 65 degrees (celcius).Gaming. Probably the most important PC topic on these forums. You don't need an i5 or i7 to game. A Phenom II X4 955BE for around $140 is the most you'll ever need. Especially if you overclock it. Intel's are more for heavy multi-threaded workloads.It all depends on your budget and requirements. One is not better than the other.Just my 2 cents.
