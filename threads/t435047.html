The following should help you to get all of the characters in DragonballZ:Budokai 3Android 16-Beat Krillin's Dragon Universe once and play it a second time. When your on Namek you will see 2 red dots on the map at some point. Go to the dot that is more eastern and talk to the Captain Ginyu Frog. Proceed through the story until you get to the Android Saga and go to an area called "the plains" in south part of the map. You will find Android 16's mashed-up body there. After you retrive it, take it to Bulma and you will now have Android 16.Android 17-Beat Android 17 as Piccolo in Dragon Universe.Android 16-Beat Dragon Universe as Krillin.Bardock-Beat Goku's Dragon Universe twice and play it a third time. Before you fight Raditz look for a crashed spaceship that should say "Saiyan's Spaceship." Enter it to unlock him.Broly- Beat Goku's Dragon Universe twice and play it a third time. Eventually in the story, you will fight Broly. Beat him to unlock him.Captain Ginyu-Beat Captain Ginyu as Goku in Dragon Universe.Cell-Beat Cell as Teen Gohan in Dragon Universe.Cell Jr.-When you have Dragon Arena, fight level 30+ opponents until Piccolo breaks in and challenges you. Beat him to unlock Cell Jr.Cooler-Beat Vegeta's Dragon Universe once, beat Goku's twice and play Goku's a third time. After you beat Freiza, Super Saiyan Vegeta will appear where Frieza used to be. Beat him and then there will be one red dot on the map which will be Cooler. Beat Cooler to unlock him.Dabura-Beat Dabura as Adult Gohan in Dragon Universe.Dr. Gero-Beat Dr. Gero as Yamcha in Dragon Universe.Freiza-Beat Freiza as Goku in Dragon Universe.Adult Gohan-Beat Dragon Universe as Teen Gohan.Great Saiyaman-Beat Dragon Universe as Adult Gohan.Hercule-Play Piccolo's Dragon Universe and the southern area right after the "Central City" event before fighting Majin Buu. When you find Hercule, fly back to "Central City" and talk to Videl to unlock Hercule.Kid Buu-Beat Kid Buu as Goku in Dragon Universe.Kid Goku-Beat Dragon Universe as Broly.Kid Trunks-Beat Majin Buu as Vegeta in Dragon Universe.Majin Buu-Beat Majin Buu as Goku in Dragon Universe.Pan-When you have Dragon Arena, fight level 40+ opponents until Adult Gohan breaks in and challenges you. Beat him to unlock Pan.Recoome-Beat Recoome as Goku in Dragon Universe.Saibaman-When you have Dragon Arena, fight level 40+ opponents until Nappa breaks in and challenges you. Beat him to unlock Saibaman.Super Buu-Beat Super Buu as Goku in Dragon Universe.Supreme Kai-Beat Adult Gohan's Dragon Universe once, and play it a second time. After you have seen Videl twice and beat Goten, go to "Kami's Lookout" and beat Piccolo. When Dabura shows up look for Vegeta in the northeast and talk to him. After you beat Dabura, Gohan will decide to hunt for Vegeta. Beat Vegeta and head for the red dot on the map before you face Majin Buu. Then enter "the plains" and you will unlock Supreme Kai.Teen Gohan-Beat Dragon Universe as Kid GohanTrunks-Beat Dragon Universe as Vegeta.Uub-When fighting Kid Buu as Goku, finish him off with a Spirit Bomb and without being Super Saiyan.Vegeta-Beat Vegeta as Goku in Dragon Universe.Videl-Beat Adult Gohan's Dragon Universe once, and play it a second time. As soon as you start the story go to "the plains" south of "Master Roshi's House" and talk to Yajirobe, Videl, and Goten. After you talk to them go to "the plains" near "Goku's House" and Videl will be unlocked after the scene is finished.The following are special fusions, characters and transformations.Gogeta-Beat Vegeta's Dragon Universe once, and play it a second time. Obtain the Super Saiyan 4 capsule from Bulma and fly south to the red dot to fight Super Saiyan 4 Goku. Beat him to get the Gogeta fusion capsule.Goku Super Saiyain 4-Finish Kid Buu off with a Spirit Bomb and then search the map for a "?????" before you go to the tournament. It will be the Super Saiyan 4 capsule. NOTE:It might take a few tries before the capsule appears.Meta-Cooler-Beat Goku's Dragon Universe once, and play it a second time. Beat Freiza and go to the red dot at the top to fight Cooler. After you beat him Meta-Cooler will appear. Talk to him and will end up back on the map. Head a little bit more nore and you will find the Meta-Cooler capsule.Omega-Shenron-Beat Broly twice as Goku and then talk to Bulma in West City. After talking to her you'll get an invitaion from Gotenks to fight him in the tournament. After you beat him you'll have to fight Omega-Shenron. Beat him to unlock him. NOTE: By doin this you will also obtain Super Saiyan 2 and Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta.Kibitoshin-After you unlock the Supreme Kai and beat Kid Buu as Adult Gohan, go to the forest before fighting Broly and you will unlock the Kibitoshin Potara fusion.Vegito-As Vegeta/Goku, beat Super Buu with Goku/Vegeta in Super Buu's body in Dragon Universe.Vegeta Super Saiyan 4-Beat Vegeta's Dragon Universe once, and play it a second time. During the Buu Saga look for Bulma in "the plains" and she'll tell you to visit her later. Then fly to west city and she'll give the Super Saiyain 4 capsule.That is the most I can help you as far as I know.
