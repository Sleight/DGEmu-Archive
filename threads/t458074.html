Ttintin.92Jun 22 2010, 05:09 PM
PSX is in fact a different system. It was a product which never made it out of Japan. It was a PS2 with in-built DVR functions (coincidentally, the first device with the XMB). However, the PSX you're mentioning (and what most people on the internet say) refers to the original PlayStation. PSX on PSP means playing PlayStation games on a PlayStation Portable.As for how to play PSX on PSP (if anyone needs to know), there's two ways as of now.1) Officially. PlayStation games for use on the PS3 and PSP can be bought from the PlayStation Store, under the heading "PlayStation Classics". While i'm not too sure of the details, i'm kinda sure that you need a PS3 to be able to download them, then they have to be transferred to the PSP. Unfortunately, very few titles are available.2)Unofficially. Requires a hacked PSP. With an iso of the required game (which you can make yourself from the original PS cd), you can convert the iso to an eboot.pbp and then run it.
