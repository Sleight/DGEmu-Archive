Depends. What kinda music do you listen to, and what form factor do you want? I have Grado SR80i's and I love them. Basically, Grado SR headphones are amazing, but for anything else, there are usually better choices.The Shure SRH840 is around that price and is extremely comfortable, durable, and can be folded up to a more compact form for carrying around. They're true monitors, though; I returned mine because I thought they were too boring and analytical, and I was much happier with the more aggressive sound that Grados provide.
