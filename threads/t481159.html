My only knowledge of hostile takeovers comes from Yugioh. Don't they only need to buy 50% +1 of the company for a takeover? At 0.6b any japanese publisher can afford them, really. And Capcom is full of IPs that would be worth more than that without their idiotic management.

You're confusing a hostile takeover with a stock buyout.  Hostile takeovers are when a company buys another company that has measures in place to stop that.  They forcibly buy every pieces of them, basically.  The company ends up spending more than the company is worth, because the company didn't want to be bought.

 

In this situation, Capcom has taken away any defense of simply being bought out.  Any company could just waltz in and buy a controlling stake in the company.  There's nothing hostile about it.  They are admitting that they'd be alright if someone bought them.  What we don't know, is if they have a buyer in mind or if they're just testing the waters.
