DDragons MasterORIGINAL POSTJul 08 2010, 11:06 PM
Hey Guys,Every once in a while comes a crazy deadly query that stucks the DB for lots of minutes :channel: This is all because we have tons of members and when people search the "members" screen (for whatever reason!) the query there goes over all the members and finds their matching rows in 3 other member-tables which is a gigantic uber calculation which could take minutes for our ultra computer (oh!)So, I've found a good idea!Since absolutly no one cares about people who never post (right?) I've made the members category insearchable and created a new group -"Active Members" - of members with more than one post - this boosts up the members search a lot (though it is still slow, 10-15 seconds is way more reasonable than 150 which stucks the whole site and with the extra loads turns into 600 seconds!) A good idea that saves us a lot of trouble.I think there are more optimizations we can run - but for now we'll settle for that.Being awake and fixing the site again is fun! - Ben
