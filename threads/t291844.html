Some quick suggestions to (hopefully) solve, or at least lessen the spam problem here: Perhaps you should make the amount of Global download points that members get dependent on the amount of posts they have made (or the user group they are in). This would prevent people from simply registering and downloading. It would also encourage people to post because as they make more posts (or go into another user group), they get more Global download points each day. It would also mean that large roms would be out of reach for lurkers and would make it harder for people to abuse the Global reset each day. I don't much about Bandwidth, but wouldn't this put less of a strain on the forum's bandwidth by reducing the amount of downloads? If GP's were awarded all at a certain time, it would give moderators alot more time to punish rule-breakers. From what I've seen, most flooders simple don't care about the consequences because as soon as they download what they want, the moderators can't do anything except ban them which is useless anyway because they most likely won't come back. If all the GP's for the day were awarded at a single time, flooders like the ones i mentioned won't be able to download what they need and therefore won't get what they came for. But, like I said, I'm not quite sure about the technical part of it so bare with me if I'm wrong. After reading replying in some discussions, I've realised that most people don't bother reading what the topic is about and simply post their interpretation of the topic's title. When you actually care about the discussion, it gets very annoying when you have to refute a wave of people who don't even know what the topic is about. Perhaps if the default display mode was 'Linear+', people might find it easier to read the first post and find out what the topic is about. Placing the 'Search forum' bar at the top would make it a little more obvious to those who are oblivious to it. 'The Dump' is a really good idea and I think you should expand on it. Perhaps you should make an archive forum for all the deleted topics. This could set the standards and show members what bad topics are like. If people know the difference between a good and bad topic, they might think twice before posting the 5th 'What super-power do you want?' topic in GD or ask about a corrupt rom in the DS forum.I'm just throwing these out there. I don't know much about the technical part of the forums so I may have missed something blatantly obvious. I don't really care whether or not you implement these suggestions as long as you at least have a think about them.

1. Ok, we got the user groups thing covered. Regular members have a 12.5 reset, screenshotters I believe have about a 20 DP reset, then staff members start at 50 and climb to 200+ if they keep getting promoted. As for raising the reset as post count increases, that not only adds more bandwidth problems, but also is going to effectively make posting worthless if you kept doing that for high enough post counts. The post->GP system will stick, and it's better for both parties overall because it doesn't affect the bandwidth negatively and awards people for posting.2. If we only awarded all GP at a certain time, that actually wouldn't help things too much besides making people wait an entire day to download something. Moderators are doing a much better job of catching spammers these days, so we pretty much have that covered. Changing the GP distribution won't change flooders/mass spammers much, although it would certainly make some idiots realize they just wasted their time and won't be able to get away with it.3. Regardless of what mode it's in, people who are just spamming for GP aren't going to pay attention to what the posts say.4. I admit, the search button in the top right corner could stand to be bigger, along with the other buttons that go with it. Our old ones were pretty decent size in comparison to these. The 2nd search button below it is fine.5. Deleted topics are redirected to The Trash, which is a forum that only administrators can handle. We have no reason to have deleted topics be accessible by regular members, because then it would be pointless to have them deleted.Thanks for the suggestions, but for the most part, we have everything covered well for your ideas, although there is some good thought in a couple of these so I'll leave this open for other staff members to comment on if they wish.
