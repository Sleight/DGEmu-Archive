Although many of you might have been disappointed to hear that this week's Mortal Kombat clip was only a movie pitch, this news is sure to please MK fans and fighting fans from here to Timbuktu. Ahead of next week's trade show, E3, Warner Bros today announced the latest iteration in their Mortal Kombat franchise which they're calling the "most brutal" one to date - and judging by the trailer, that doesn't seem far off. "This game really is a response to what players have been demanding: mature presentation, reinvented 2D fighting mechanic and the best, most gruesome fatalities ever!" said Boon. Mortal Kombat is due for release in 2011. Now, get over here watch the trailer.
