Just to give you guys an idea, this is the concept we'd be aiming for:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z56bHNyV2LI

 

There's a lot that will go into the design first:

- Graphically, the presentation

- Programmatically, all the database entries and page generation and event handling

- Organizationally, the storage of all the data and such.

 

I was thinking of starting with the 8-bit generation first, so 2 types of cards: Master System and Nintendo Entertainment System.

 

The 2 starting cards would be either Alex Kidd or Mario, and as the story progresses, we'll move up to the 16-bit era introducing new cards and stuff.

 

As for card designs, I was aiming for something that looks like Pokemon cards.

These are the ones from Ayakashi, they're nice and all, but I find they lack the description text (in game, they fix it with a speech bubble above that you can close):

I kind of understand them since they're focusing on the artwork, but since we're dealing with sprites and such, we'll do it Pokemon or Yu-Gi-Oh style:

 

Basically, the card needs: A rarity and a type of card

What the card must NOT contain: The attack strength, the defense strength, and its level (all these information will be managed by a database since they will evolve with the player)
