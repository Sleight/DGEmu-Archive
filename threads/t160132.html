I know I will get killed for saying this but maybe the Advance Wars series are the best turn based games. Now just let me explain as briefly as possible why I think that. AW is not balanced at all, and it will never be balanced because the simple fact of having DIFFERENT "armies" (COs) makes them inherently broken. However, Intelligent Systems always stand out for breaking the mold (Paper Mario), and doing things with a different formula that always brings good results. In paper, AW sounds ridiculous: "Listen up guys, we are making a game were cities are just like a house that gives money and the HQ and producing bases are as big as them, units are also as big as those things. There will also be COs that alter the ways each army acts, HOW? Dont ask me just let the players use their imagination..." But in the end they got a game that plays like chess and can be balanced as long as players use rounded COs without powers. Let me ask you if anyone of has cared about AW being balanced.
