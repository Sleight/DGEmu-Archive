A masterpiece like Final Fantasy 13 has taken four years, imagine the next FF project on PS4, could take as long as 6 years or more for a game that exceeds the graphical quality of FF13. Just because the console is updated doesn't necessarily mean that the computer systems used to create the games have been updated. Ubisoft has already stated that they are working on games for the PS3 NOW. If they are already starting work on games now for a console that isn't to be released until prob 2015 at earliest, thats 6-7 years of work on a single game project.My only issue with this is sequels. One-off games will be fine but the issue comes with sequels. It takes two years to get a sequel to games as quality as Uncharted or Assassins Creed, and most sequels don't start production until the original game makes bank. A sequel to Batman Arkham Asylum wasn't decided upon until it was seen that the first game was a huge success and now we have to wait 2 years for the sequel. The same will go with the PS4 but it could be as long as 5 years between sequels.This is all just my own theories. Theres a lot of work that goes into games and as the games get bigger so does the production time. Developers will of course try to pound these games out as fast as they can but I can only imagine that this would lead to a lot of sloppily produced titles.
