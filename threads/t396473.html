OOysterSoupKitchenFloorWaxMuseumApr 21 2008, 04:40 PM
I wouldn't google tuts. Usually, people end up copying them word for word, and then they never get to develop their own style. Try to think of stuff yourself, and get some sigmaking techniques out of tuts, at most.As for the sig, the leftmost and rightmost render are good. I'd take out the middle two. A border would feel pretty meh for this sig. Try to avoid those big flashy text blending modes, I suggest blending it in a simpler way.But yeah, you don't have any background at all. Work on that. Don't follow the tuts, and go all random grunge brushing it. Brushes are good, but not as an entire bg-maker, only as extras - just try fixing in another stock and adding adjustments to it, making the entire thing a good, flowing whole.
