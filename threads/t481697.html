KKirie MotobaORIGINAL POSTAug 10 2015, 05:23 AM

So like last year, I attended the local anime convention here in Montreal, Otakuthon. This year has been really great.
 
So overall, how it went.
 
Day 0: Badge pickup.
Since I went to pickup my badge late, there was actually near no lineup. Issue though, is that I'm actually a panelist presenting Mega Man this year, but none of the staff members there on that day knew how I would claim my credit... Lack of training of staff members...
 
Day 1: Hectic Friday
So this year, the con started at 11 instead of 5... Well, I was stuck at work. But I managed to get out a bit early to go quickly snipe at the stores. Two things I noticed:
- Rare amiibos were ALL there, for RIDICULOUS prices.
- Funko Pop! Animation figures... Seriously, personally, I find them fugly, but they are so freaking popular it was in nearly every store. WTF.
 
After that, I moved on to Angela's concert. For those that don't know, she was most known for the opening of Stellvia, and sang I think all opening of Fafner. She definitely got the crowd going, making them all dance along with her. The guitarist, "onii-sama", was also fantastic on the guitar and drums. She opened her concert with Stellvia, and covered her Fafner songs, and ended with a cover of Cruel Angel's Thesis. Really nice.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZ0FP-1rEvY
 
After the concert, I went for her autograph session and bought the 2 albums she had on the spot. It was supposed to be an autograph on only 1 item per person, but I got totally lucky because Atsuko said it was fine!




 
Later that night, I went into the gaming room and met this cool girl cosplaying as Alice from Touhou. Suddenly got invited into the Touhou 3: Phantasmagoria of Dim.Dream tournament. I only played that game like twice in my life, but I decided to try anyways. First round, I completely stomped my opponent no issues. Second round, it was me against this girl... I just shouted: What?? I've just known you for 5 minutes, and now we have to be enemies???
So we played, and I lost both rounds, even though she was near losing... GRRR. In the end, she got third place and got away with a Touhou soundtrack!!
The person I lost to is this silly one here:



 
Day 2: Long Saturday
So firstly, I work up late... Really late... So by the time I made it to the convention, I had maybe 1 hour before our presentation on Mega Man...
And before I knew it, our panel came. We were given the biggest panel room available, and the room was like 80% filled. Holy crap, this is my second time ever presenting a panel, and I get this kind of crowd!?
So then we proceeded to present our panel, all the series of games, all the animes and cartoons, and lastly all about the Megamix/Gigamix mangas and Archie comics. It was great, people enjoyed my tinkering of the slides of Gutsman's ass, the girls presenting with me were awesome about the games and comics. I think that one line people will remember me for is this: "In Mega Man V on the GB, we get Mega Man fisting other robots. Litterally. And he doesn't let them go with one of the upgrades."
 
The day continued, I went to shop some more, and then joined up with a big part of the family of a girl I knew from the first time I went to this con. Had dinner with them, it was nice.
 
Once done, I came back to shop some more, and then went back to the video game lounge to participate to the tournament of Touhou 9: Phantasmagoria of Flower View. This time, Alice wasn't there to stand in my way, so I played best I could... I reached the finals, but got completely destroyed by Sedrife, the artist for the Touhou music circle èƒŒæˆ¸ (http://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/èƒŒæˆ¸).
What I won was an original copy of the game we just played the tournament of. quite happy to add this to my games collection. :)
 
To finish up the day, I attended the panel I helped out producing for Touhou songwriting. Very well presented. Both Sedrife and Yan Solo (from the Touhou music circle Lunatyc Records: http://lunatycrecords.bandcamp.com/releases) explained clearly the content that even a person like me that didn't study in that could understand. Overall, great panel that I stayed almost until the end when I realized I was about to be screwed with no more transportation back home if I didn't leave. XD
 
Day 3: The Most Hectic Sunday Ever
Again, I wanted to go earlier, but I only managed to get there 1 hour late. Lack of sleep really kills me...
Started my shopping spree, and got tons of awesome stuff. Ranging from little figures of Free and a plush of Hello Kitty for my wife, to a 5ft long poster of 25 years of Mega Man by an awesome artist, boxed and sealed Japanese Rockman games, and other stuff. Really a good grab.
 
Once my shopping spree was finally done I went to Flow's concert, which is in fact its last overseas destination for their 2015 Kiwami World Tour. Since it's Flow, they are really well known in the anime community, the concert hall was filled at 90%. For those that don't know, Flow did one of the openings, and the latest ending of Naruto, Code Geass opening, and much more. The whole crowd was going wild as they sang, it was really a great experience.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cZ7zQbMxm28
 
Once the concert was over, it was a bit hectic again, since I had to run to go get my badge partially reimbursed for the panel I presented, but then was stuck in a humongus lineup for Flow's autograph. I think I waited for 2 hours, but it was definitely worth the wait though. As I got a signature from each member at a time and thanked them for their wonderful concert and amazing music, the last one to sign was the guitarist of the band, Take. As I handed my badge to him, he took a closer look at my name on it and then said: Hmmmm... Megaman? In Japanese, it is Rockman. Woah, wasn't expecting him to know about Mega Man!!



 
And so as the autograph was done, I rushed to go get my reimbursement and encountered people I've previously went to cons with. Then I took a few more pictures, but not too many.
