Q29

First step, Cecil becomes paladin; you acquire the Sword of Legend/Mythgraven Blade (depending on game)

Second, get the rat tail from the lair of the summoned monsters in the underworld.

Third, go to the Adaman Isles (on surface) with the first airship (the one with the grapple for the hovercraft) and the hovercraft, and ride hovercraft to the little cave there.

Fourth, trade rat tail for adamantite; remakes will send you here later with other tails for more awesome items.

Fifth, take sword and stone, head back to the underground. South of the castle, against the southern wall of the underground, is a single village tile place. Inside is the legendary smith. Give him sword and stone.

Sixth, become the best at space! ... er, get the lunar whale, go to the moon, return.

Voila, Excalibur. One of Cecil's best swords.
