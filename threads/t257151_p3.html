Scrambled eggs- while cooking on a skillet or griddle, the egg is constantly stirred creating a fluffy yellow egg.Sunny side up- The egg is cracked onto the cooking surface and cooked, without moving it, until the white is thoroughly opaqueOver-easy- the egg is cooked like with sunny side up, but halfway through cooking is flipped over so there is white on both sides with liquid yolk in the center.Over-medium- the egg is cooked like over easy, but towards the end, the yolk is punctured so that is is cooked solid as wellPoached- the egg is cracked into boiling water and cooked until solidSoft-boiled- the egg is boiled with the shell still intact for a short time and served by breaking the top off and spooning out the semi-liquid eggHard-boiled- the egg is boiled like soft-boiled, but for a longer time so the egg is fully solid. These are cooled by running cold water over them until room temperature. Usually served in salads.Deviled eggs- a hard-boiled egg is cut in half, the yolk is removed and mixed with mustard and mayonnaise, put back in the whites and sprinkled with paprika.
