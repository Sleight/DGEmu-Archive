banditsin' post='2220306 wrote on Mar 1 2007, 05:21 PM:
Do you know any deadly moves that kirby uses and is feared by all the other characters? Other than the eating attack
Kirby is bottom-tier. Statistically, he's not a very good character. Still, he does have some strong moves. I think his best attack would be his B-air (backwards aerial attack). It's deceptively strong, and has some good combo potential at lower percentages. In order to use aerial attacks effectively in ground combat, you need to learn to SHFFL. SHFFL stands for "short hop, fast fall, L-cancel". Short hopping is done by pressing the jump button as fast as possible. How hard you press it doesn't matter, it's how fast you get on and off the button. You might not notice it much by playing as Kirby, so practice on someone with normal jumps. Fox is the hardest to short hop with, by the way, since his jump starts up really quickly. If you have trouble short hopping, you can try doing the "flick" method which is what I do. Use the tip of your thumb(nail) and flick the edge of the jump button. It's easier, but it might give you problems later on. Now, fast falling is simply done by pressing down on the control stick as you begin descending from a jump. L-cancelling is when you press the L, R, or even the Z button right before you land after doing an aerial attack. What that does is halve the delay that you would have from landing after using that attack. Now, the whole point of the SHFFL, as you may have deduced, is to provide a way of using aerial attacks in ground combat.By the way, I suggest using the C-stick to do aerial attacks. It may be frowned upon by others, but they're intentionally gimping themselves because they think it's "cheap". Thing is, the game doesn't know "cheap". It only knows win or lose. So don't worry about using the C-stick. Using the C-stick for aerial attacks means you can do things like use F-airs (forward aerial attack) while moving backwards and vice versa.

If you've been knocked off the stage, fly just under the ledge and use the cutter.
