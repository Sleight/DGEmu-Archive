Street Fighter X Megaman


For Megaman and Street Fighter's 25th anniversary, here's a free game that was sponsored by Capcom!

Controls:
W, A, S, D: Directional keys
K: Fire (Hold to charge)
L: Jump
S + L: Slide
I, O: Quick weapon change
Enter: Start, pause, and confirm

Esc: Quit game
F1: Change input device
F2: Configure controls
F3: Reset default controls
F4: Toggle window size
