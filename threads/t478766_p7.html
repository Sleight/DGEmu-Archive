Wards, general notes. Might be easier to explain in game when you can see their vision range.

 

1. Offensive wards to peek into their base. Don't plop em too near the turrets else they'll kill them.

 

2. Anti-gank wards. Not fool-proof, but will give you good vision of paths any would-be ganker would take to get to you.

 

3. This blue brush is very nice for peeking into the jungle. You can see 5 or so different paths converging on this point here.

 

4. Dragon/Baron wards. Actually you can also spread vision across two wards; one in the tiny river brush, and one on the other side of the pit.

 

5. Great spots for you to ward if you're paranoid they're coming in your jungle. These are all the main paths into your jungle that aren't from lane.
