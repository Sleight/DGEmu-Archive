Pphantoma4Jun 12 2011, 03:31 PM
I'll make a guess and say that being a Famous Musician isn't your only dream, but if I'm wrong then do pardon me.Dreams and Reality aren't always totally separate from another. The way I see it, you should always pursue your Dreams. Dreams give you a direction in life that provides motivation for a certain goal that you have. Be it fame or fortune, your Dream is just one avenue towards it. What's not to say that your Dreaming can't lead to your very own Reality? Who's saying that your "Reality" is the only way out?Learn to Dream big, but don't get trapped in your Dreams. Anchor them to Reality by doing a little goal-setting for yourself. Set little milestones to achieve. Give yourself realistic, measurable milestones to try and achieve by a certain timeframe. Don't simply throw out the end-point of "Famous Musician" and then say you'll find your way there somehow. Dreams will only stay that way if you simply dream of it.But never, ever throw out your alternative. Don't say it's impossible to reconcile a college education with your musical dreams. You just have to figure out how to do so. It'll be tough, what with time management and the need to spend valuable time on both your music and your education. You'll really have to plan your time spent with your college and your band properly.No college education can or will stop you from living your Dreams. And hey, even if your Dream doesn't work out, at least you've tried your best. Nobody can fault you for that. Good luck!
