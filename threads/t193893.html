1. Banning ConditionsAre eligible for ban the following cases ONLY:
(1) Mass spamming (or flooding): the definition of this term is given by atomicknight and we fix the number of spam topics/posts at 10. That means if a member posts 10 posts/topics with "dfgkjhk" like content, he/she is eligible for ban. However, 10 is just a suggested number to give an idea, use your own judgement.
(2) Dupe accounts: ban ONLY the dupe accounts and leave the original or main account valid. Comments on this item:- Give one positive one per account and report all warns from every dupe account.- Report also the GPs under 100 from all the other accounts (GPs over 100 can be left aside).- If there are many dupes, reporting all the warns should make the member eligible for ban under the rule (3).- If one of the accounts was banned under the rules (1) or (4), then ban all accounts.
(3) High warning level: generally, members with a warning level greater than 50% are eligible for ban. However, again use your judgment, this is just a suggested number.
(4) Other cases: for example, sometimes, there are members who registered just for advertizing. It can be easily recognized when you see a list of links leading to pornographic or pharmaceutical sites. Same policy for members posting pornographic pictures and Bugmenot accounts.

2. Unbanning ConditionsAre eligible for unban the following cases ONLY:
(1) Members banned under previous rules: this concerns mainly members banned for dupe accounts. If there is another rule update, this item should remain still valid.
(2) Special reason: any staff member may propose banned member for unban if he/she can provide some reasonable grounds for this.

3. Procedure- When banning or unbanning a member, always add a line in the warn log stating the reason why he/she is banned. If the warn log is full, delete a previous warn and add the last. It should help identifying why a member has been banned.- When you want to unban a member, open a poll in the banning subforum and state your reason. Every staff member is welcome to vote and post the reason of the choice. The majority decides the outcome.

4. Duties and Power- Support Staff and Forum Assistants: report for banning anyone who is eligible under the rules (1), (2), (3) and (4). Please include the link to the profile(s).- Forum Sentinels and Administrators: ban anyone eligible under the rules (1), (2), (3) and (4).- Everyone is authorized to open a poll for unban.

5. Report for Ban
Please use the following formats when reporting a member for ban:
- Topic title: member's display name
- Content: The post should include the following items.
Profile link (for a single user to ban) or IP finder link (for duplicate accounts)
Reason for ban
