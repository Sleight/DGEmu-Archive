Well you can get the game to work if you can tell us what flashcart you're using. The game is very popular and thus there are quite some fixes for the game.Anyways, 999 is a graphic novel, there's about 80% text and 20% puzzles, so you're going to read a lot. The writing however and the atmosphere it gives off is right on target, also due to the good localization. The game begins with a very rough start, and it only gets rougher from there (the game has a lot of death and also gore, the gore however is mostly off-screen though). If you're of the weak of heart, the game might not be good for you. the game does have some weaknesses, mostly the point-and-click syndrome. It's not as bad as most games from the point-and-click era, but you do have to tap quite a bit around before you eventually hit the desired spot. I'm not really disclosing much on the game, but personally I'd recommend playing the game if you don't mind reading (note: I actually hate reading, a lot, but I got sucked into this game)
