So my enthusiasm for England only a short few days ago has been subject to a very sharp dose of reality.Having spanked the English, the German team went on to give the Argentinians a bloody nose as well and a team which was regarded by many as too young to win the World Cup looks to be the form team of the tournament.Germany now have to play Spain in the semi-final, no mean feat when you consider that Spain were joint favourites before the tournament began. Paraguay certainly made Spain work hard for their victory in the quarter-finals and with a little more composure might well have scored. The German tacticians will have watched that game carefully and will feel they are able to press Spain high up the pitch as Paraguay did, but will have the composure and ability upfront to score a goal or two. This semi-final could be a classic.In the other match not many would look past the Dutch who have already knocked out Brazil (the other pre-tournament favourites), but Uruguay have far-exceeded everybody's expectations and Forlan seems to have his shooting boots on - a pity his strike partner Suarez is suspended after being red-carded for his handball against Ghana.So the Netherlands appear likely to be a shoe-in for the final next Sunday, but who will it be against Germany or Spain?Which of these three teams will have the honour of being the first European team to win a World Cup on another continent, or will Uruguay really surprise us all and go all the way?
