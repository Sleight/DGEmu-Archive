I would definitely start with Casuals to learn the ropes. You can always practice with bots right off.

 

Pistols:

The gun you start with, the P2000 and the Glock (semi-automatic, almost never set it to burst) work pretty well off the bat. The USP-S is more accurate than the P2000, but because of low ammo, it's not noob proof.

Like stock said, the CZ-279 is a great pistol, but again, very low ammo, so if you spray, chances are you will miss everything. The Five-Seven and Tec-9 are my favorites though. High ammo, ok accuracy, mid-tier almost run and gun proof pistols.

 

SMG:

Perfect for rushing, blindly shooting. Best here are P90 (too pro 90 for people, this gun reks all), followed by PP-Bizon. The other I love to use is the MP9 on CT side, but because of a low ammo cartridge, it's not as effective.

 

Heavy:

Totally not a shotgun user, but I've seen and have received the end of many great shottys that insta kill me. Don't ask me advice on this, I'm not too good with it.

There's also the M249 and Negev, 2 overly expensive guns that can be easily replaced by the more effective P90. But usually, I use those on last rounds of halves in Competitive to waste cash and have fun. NEGEV, just point the cursor down, and hold down that fire button~

 

Rifle:

M4A4 or AK-47, those are by far the most balanced guns. M4A1-S is even more effective than M4A4, but low ammo means you need to be extra preservative on your shots. These guns are really the point and shoot, reposition and shoot again. Spraying these will have almost no effect. AUG and SG 553 are a lot more expensive, but they have aim assists for aiming at semi long distance. They're my favorite guns to use in a lot of scenarios. Then you got sniper rifles, which the all-time favorite is the AWP: Almost always 1-shot kill on enemies, single shot, slow. If you master this gun, you will be hated. I'm not good at all with it, so forget me.

 

Also, learn up Dust II, like the corners, shortcuts, and locations you can hide on that map. It's the most played map in competitive.
