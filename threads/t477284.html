For those seeking a quick start up and application launch time, it is.
For those looking only for GB of storage, it isn't.

Also, just to let you guys know, prices of hard drives have dropped considerably, they are finally affordable again.
For those seeking storage, I now highly recommend Western Digital Red drives. Performance wise, faster than the Green series, and consumes less electricity.
Price wise, very affordable.

For those seeking to burn money into Western Digital Velociraptor X drives, don't, the black series are nearly at the same level of performance even for a 7200rpm drive vs a 10k rpm drive, and price range is similar for more GB on the Black.

And lastly, for those seeking average seek/write time to be faster than the rest on a computer that heavily does a lot of data transaction, a RAID 0 configuration with 2 drives is always welcomed, and if you have enough money, a RAID 5 with 3 drives is even better and safer for your precious data.
