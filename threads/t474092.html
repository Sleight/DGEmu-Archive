AA Love SupremeORIGINAL POSTOct 23 2011, 08:27 AM
Unlike most frequent members on this website, I’ve never posted anything saying what members I like and why. I’ve never voted for my favourite members or voted much in the competitions. It’s never been my thing. But now that everything seems to be really ending and slowing down now I just wanted to say something as a tribute to this site and the members that make this site. It’s not a farewell by me and I’ll not leaving just yet, but everybody including me is visiting and posting less. I’ve been a member of this website for nearly 9 years, nearly a decade. That’s a long time by any measure, but in internets years where everything seems to be in fastforward, it’s practically an eternity. I initially came here as an 11 year old, looking for the latest Harry Potter ROM. I sit here now as a 19 year old, not looking but in knowledge I had found a rich and unique community. This place has been a constant during a period of great change. This place holds so many memories… I remember posting 2 pages before the 4000 page of the BBB, it took 10 minutes to load and my post had ended up on page 4010. The Golden years came and went, and members started leaving. But this is all common knowledge, and the “Golden Years” and subsequent decline is oft-recited lore. But I remained. Along with other great members. And new members came and became part of DGEmu. But when the site design changed recently, I was genuinely a bit sad. That skin had been there during the Golden years and during most of my years here. When I saw the change I knew something had changed and it was all over. I can’t explain, but from the decline in posts, I’m sure other members understand me completely. But I’m drifting and rambling. What I really wanted to say and talk about are the members. Without them, this place was nothing and is nothing. And boy, we had some great members and that’s elevated us from just some IGN or another gaming forum. We were a community and unique, diverse members all bought something. It was fantastic. This place was a one in a life thing, I know it. We talked about everything and anything and I could reminisce for hours about the enjoyment and enriching experiences I gained from this experience. Just going on the archives makes me nostalgic and crazy, and seeing all the old member names makes me feel 13 again. People talk about how they’re leaving the forum for a real life. That’s bullshit, this place is a life unto itself and is as real as any physical object. This place will always be part me, and has definitely contributed to who I am now. All of us here are brothers, united forever by the fact we shared parlour. It sounds dramatic but for me, it’s true. I’ve made some great friends and I’ve made offers and received offers about being mailing games to other members. I meant it and I’m sure they meant it as well. You don’t need to look further than JTulip and Spleen to know this site is something special. Those that I made friends with, I truly care for and I wish them all the success and happiness in the world. And those I didn’t talk to much, that doesn’t diminish my affection for them. Deepest kisses, bear-tight hugs and lusty-stares to: Spleen, Bish, BR, 2BP, DM, Dhaos, Hatu, TM, Ammy, Dual, Milo, Shadow, Duce, Channel, Falcon, ‘Zam, Tomo, mohl, Kefka, Midi, Jennitulip, GBA, Shai, Ish, Pengu, Ryan, KHRS, and all the strange dudes that like anime and computers perhaps a tad too much. If I could, of course I would wish us back to ’05 with the BBB. But nothing lasts forever. I’ve had a great, great time and fantastic memories. I love this site and love the members more than I can ever express, and I wouldn’t trade this experience for anything in the world. Thank you to this website and always being there. “And we know what memories can bringThey bring Diamonds and Rust.” Jelly beans.
