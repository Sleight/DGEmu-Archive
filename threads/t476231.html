How to Hack Your PS3 (by installing Kmeaw's CFW)

1 posts · page 1 of 1 · closed · 52 views

'Hacking' your PS3 allows it to run programs that are not officially authorised by Sony. To do so, you first have to install custom firmware, or CFW, over the official firmware that your PS3 came in. Here is a simple guide for that.

Things You Will Need:
- A PS3 at or below official firmware (OFW) 3.55. PS3s at 3.56 or above can NOT be hacked.
- A USB drive that is formatted to FAT32 (with at least 200MB of free space)

Instructions:
First, update your firmware to OFW 3.55.
1. Download the OFW update from here, then rename it to "PS3UPDAT.PUP". Place it in the /PS3/UPDATE folder of your USB device.
2. Insert your USB device into the PS3, then select 'Settings' -> 'System Update' -> 'Update From Storage Device' on your PS3.

Once your PSP is at OFW 3.55...
1. Download the CFW Maker v1.5 (by Cyan from GBATemp)

2. Extract its contents (using 7-Zip or WinRAR) into another folder on the USB device. Let's call this folder /PS3/CFWM.

3. Place the OFW 3.55 file obtained earlier inside the /PS3/CFWM/Original_3.55_FW (make sure that it has been renamed to "PS3UPDAT.PUP")

4. Run the Kmeaw_3.55.bat file to produce the Kmeaw 3.55 CFW update file. Follow the instructions, and be sure to check the MD5 when prompted. If the MD5 is wrong, you have probably not followed all the steps correctly - please delete the KMEAW_3.55_CFW folder, and try again more carefully. Otherwise, this should produce a PS3UPDAT.PUP file inside the new /PSP/CFWM/KMEAW_3.55_CFW folder.

5. Place that PS3UPDAT.PUP file inside /PS3/UPDATE, overwriting any existing files if needed. Then insert your USB device into the PS3. Select 'Settings' -> 'System Update' -> 'Update From Storage Device' to update your PS3 to the Kmeaw 3.55 CFW!

A few things to take note:
- You cannot use PSN when you use CFW. PSN requires firmware of 3.60 or beyond. There is currently no CFW for anything more than 3.55.
- You cannot downgrade your PS3 to 3.55 from OFW 3.56 or above, without using "hardmods". But those methods are only recommended for those who really know what they are doing.

That's it for now. Cheers!
