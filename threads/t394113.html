OK, a few things...These are a little big, don't you think? I mean... it's like twice the size of the ones I'm used to seeing.The stocks needs to blend in with the area; I would suggest looking for some tutorials on how to blend in your stocks (not sure how easy this is but it wouldn't hurt to try).I can see you're trying to establish a colour scheme. That's good, do that.Add more effects and layers and layer adjustments, but don't overdo it.Try to accomplish a focal point (in this case it would obviously be the faces) using flow, color, lines, contrast anything to make the focal stand out or pop out at you.Add some sexy lighting too. Play around with some of the lighting effects to give it some emotion; Everyone wants a lil' somethin' somethin' with their package. ;}Oh and you always want to add some depth. You can accomplish this by sharpening some areas and lightly blurring out other areas in the picture, for example, you could sharpen the face but blur the background to make it seem like the background is actually behind the character.
