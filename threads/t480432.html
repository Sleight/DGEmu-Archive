found out after installing my brand new Gigabyte GTX 770 SC that metro last light will have some frame rate issues if you play it on max settings
couldn't figure out until I found this
http://www.geforce.com/whats-new/articles/metro-last-light-graphics-breakdown-and-performance-guide#last-light-anti-aliasing
turns out at 4x SSAA the GPU will process the image at 3840x2160 resolution and downscale it to 1920x1080, meaning it renders it at a 4K resolution...
I was surprised to see that games could do this at last gen of the NVidia line of cards... (ie the 600 series)
but not even a standalone GTX titan could do it without SLI safely without damaging the card or having frame rate issues.
I don't think im going to mess with anything near 4k any more...
