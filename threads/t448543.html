I personally thought this was a great game. Sure, it could have used some improvements, and it was pretty much a movie clone of Fight Night - but I really think if they made a Rocky Legends 3, added some extra modes, and made it a lot more realistic, it would have been a really good idea. Ubisoft did release "Rocky Balboa" on PSP, and it was a GREAT game. But they took out the training system where you go up in muscular strength, stamina, and such. That was a big minus - the battle system for Rocky Balboa was PERFECT. The side quests also were perfect. But they need to put the training games back in.
