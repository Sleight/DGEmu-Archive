Ttier2geekMar 11 2006, 03:45 AM
While the GameGear truly was much superior compared to the GameBoy, it had quite some disadvantages. First off is the most spoken of, bad battery life. It kinda sucks to have a handheld that eats up pricey little batteries. Also, the GameGear had a pretty poor marketing campaign, it also directly tried to make a mockery of the GameBoy which turned away Nintendo fans who were at first willing to accept the new handheld. Due to the initial lacking launch, game developers starting seeing little potential in creating games for the GG, and with less games on the GG, it became less attractive. As this chain of events continued, before you knew it the GG was found in bargain bins nation wide. Well....actually the GG wasn't THAT bad of a failure but it was enough to keep Sega from releasing another true Handheld.
