EEndymion_MallornSep 02 2006, 09:29 PM
To be honest, I can't say. They are wholly different gameplay experiences, for different types of gamers and different play styles. For my own part, I love the Zero series more than any other.Unlike Mega Man (the original) Zero has a real storyline and each game has a plot. I have been looking for RTRZ lately because of this very fact. Plus, I enjoy the merged genre that Mega Man was taken to by Zero, and I often think of the Zero games in the same vein as Castlevania: Harmony of Dissonance. They are products of a merged genre of gaming. They are the newer, purer breed of games, taking the simple sidescrolling fun and making it more than a simple "beat-em-up", though there is that element and someone not looking for more than that finds it with ease. But to a hardcore gamer, there's an RPG element as well. Between the chip system, EX Skills, Forms, and Bodies (not to mention the star system for the weapons and the acquirement of weaponry throughout the game), the hardcore gamer sees the beginnings of an RPG that take Mega Man Zero from being just another sidescroller with a ranking system and make it a true diamond in the rough. RockMan.EXE/MegaMan Battle Network, however, is only for harder-core gamers, like former Pokemon fans (from back when it was cool!). When you start MMBN, you know you're in for the long haul. The game starts and you're part Sherlock Holmes, part Han Solo. You can't just play for a little while, finish a stage and go on with your life. It takes time, random battling, and side quests...LOTS AND LOTS of both. It's not a game for the entry-level gamer, no matter what it might say. MMBN is the Final Fantasy of Mega Man. It takes someone who already knows what they're doing to play it. Plus, you really have to know Boktai to get the later ones and the Japanese ones.So it's a matter of what kind of a game you're looking for, and it's only personal opinion.
