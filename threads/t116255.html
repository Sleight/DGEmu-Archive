But is it really that good an RPG? The Overworld characters were blocks (the battles... ok, touché) and that hand (which thankfully you could get rid of) was too much of a giveaway. I felt that the music was fairly crap actually. Believe it or not, FF8's Boss tune was better than most of FF7's stuff (Aeris' death theme was the only tune I felt was good). FF8 had better graphics and music, but was generally nothing compared to FF7 and FF9 was probably the best out of the normal FF's for me.Why am I so critical of FF7, FF8 and indeed FF9? Because of the lack of value for other RPGs. Out of all the ones I've played, four really stand out and you may be surprised at one or two of them.Firstly is Star Ocean: 2nd Story. Around 5 mins into it, I knew I was in for something special and it delivered on a huge scale. I was almost overwhelmed with the quality of this title.Secondly is Castlevania: Symphony of the Night. Some people may not class this as an RPG, but it has a lot in common with an RPG:1. Theres HP/MP values2. Damage counters when you hit/get hit.3. Items and Equipable menus4. Cutscenes!I admit the voice acting is laughable (or terrible, whichever), but in 1997, that was a huge step. Only one other RPG had voice acting and its amongst my list!My Third Choice is Grandia! Yep, Game Arts' brainchild. The voice acting was fairly good and the story is more memorable than FF7 (imho). The battle system was ace and even though it wasn't fully 3D, it looked better than FF7. Another RPG took that kind of style (the final one in my list)Finally, my last choice is Dragon Warrior 7. I admit it takes a while to get started and you may get put off after a while, but I persevered and was rewarded hugely! I felt so good after beating the crap out of God (yes, God. He's dead!) with my team and I feel its Enix's 2nd Best RPG (Best being Star Ocean of course!)I do respect the others opinions, but feel that the majority haven't played anything but the crap RPGs and Final Fantasy.So all u FF homeboys out there, look around!
