So with the release of Super Mario Galaxy 2 around the corner, I thought I'd like to brief on more Wii Games due around the coming months of this year. So they are most likely to be:- The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword (Yes, that unnamed one that has been in the back of everyone's heads. It even has... a timeline?)- Kirby's Epic Yarn (Needs better title)- Epic Mickey (Needs better title. Anything that bears the word "Epic" as part of the titulary should be renamed or be pronounced illegal.)- Goldeneye 007 (YES. THE CLASSIC N64 JUGGERNAUT REVIVED FOR ONLINE!!!)- Super Mario Galaxy 2 (No biggie there)- Metroid Other M (They say it will be soon? How soon?)- And another one that I forgot to add, so feel free to do so.Breakdown- The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword is basically a graphical cross-fusion of Wind Waker and Twilight Princess. Kinda unique in terms of trying to relate the graphics to any other game. Link is one of the first Hylians, descended upon the ground from his home in the sky. The Oocca return as the people in the sky, of which I am sure the creature in the promotion art is one of them. The game will use the WiiMotionPlus accessory, which will allow for a larger array of slash work. You also get a sort of flying bombchu-boomerang majjig. The only Zelda so far with a timeline, to when Hyrule began.- Kirby's Epic Yarn looks like it came straight from a textile workshop, as that is the basis of the design. Kirby is a piece of string and uses his ever morphing string to traverse worlds of cloth, paper and zipper (rock, paper, scissors, geddit). While you may think this seems a bit wierd, it does play upon Kirby's colorful and playful feel and better yet, Kirby can turn into a tank! Or at least I think it was a tank.- Epic Mickey, not much to say here as much of it is still a mystery to me. It doesn't appear to be as violent as I first imagined though. Oh well. Research more if your keen.- Goldeneye 007. A semi revival of the N64 classic. Apparently, it is Daniel Craig's interpretation, so expect similar levels, as well as him lending his character to Bond. Can be co-oped, and to my understanding that is both offline and online. How? Apparently you get a munchkin to work alongside you. There is also a varied mode online (stuff like Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch and Goldengun) with what I believe is a simplistic level up system (Hopefully, more like Uncharted 2 than Call of Duty). Definitely one I'll be keeping my on, and will arrive sometime Novemeber.- Super Mario Galaxy 2... Check it yourself, it's released already (or on the brink thereof).- Metroid Other M was I think anticipated for August (next month) but I still haven't seen it in stores (a big title like this would normally have little to all the attention in the store... but no "nos"). Still, I think I would be conflicted with the side scrolling plus camera moving uncontrollably to the player. I hope it ain't jerky.So basically, look up what you want, and if you like what you see, get it! Goldeneye 007 and Metroid Other M are definitely on my list.
