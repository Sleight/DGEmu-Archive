A recently released and quite impressive rhythm game!
Trust me, watching the video might be a little puzzling at first (I actually went wtf when I first saw the promotional video to this game), but you will soon be addicted to this retro rhythm game with awesome music.

The premise is simple: You're going back in time, and you time your shots back at the enemies at the rhythm of the music, all while dodging enemy attacks.

It's also playable with a guitar peripheral! (After I checked the X-treme mode, I think it is quite IMPOSSIBLE without a guitar...)

No further waiting, please enjoy the video and the awesome music!
