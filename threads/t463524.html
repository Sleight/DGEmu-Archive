Reverse Ledge grab: Grabbing the edge while facing the other way (Although there are some situations in melee that allows this and usually you need to sweet spot the edge for this to come into effect)Multiple air dodges: Although more realistic, makes it easier for people to comeback on to the edge.Spot dodging: Ridiculously fast for some characters and makes it really cheap for characters like diddy kong.No momentum: This is something I really really hate about brawl but other people might not care.
