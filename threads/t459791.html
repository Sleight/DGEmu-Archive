After Germany has taken control of France and robs it of all its riches, Germany decides to have a Grand Prix in Saarbrücken in 1940. Sean Devlin an ordinary race car driver is cheated in the race when an important German colonel Kurt Dierker decides to shoot dead one of Sean's tires, of course the cheater always wins. Sean and his best friend Jules, want revenge for all the glory that the Kurt had won. So they decided to take his prized care and run it off a cliff, having been caught, Kurt mistaken the two for being a British spy. Using Dierker's disgusting ways, he uses a scalpel and re-arranges Jule's face, leaving all of his blood on Dierker's torture chair. As you take on the role of Sean, you sabotage various German petrol stations, power plants, rail road tracks, to inspire the French that there is still hope that justice will prevail.
