Nazi Zombies ( if you don't know already) is a never-ending mission on Call of Duty: World At War. After you complete the campaign, you unlock Nacht Der Untoten (Night Of The Undead). There are three more Nazi Zombie levels available. Each one of the other Nazi Zombie levels is found in one of the three Map Packs. You can play Nazi Zombies on Single Player, Two Player Splitscreen or up to four players on Xbox Live Gold.In Nacht Der Untoten, you start off in a room with five windows. You cannot leave this building. Each window is blocked off with planks of wood. Nazi Zombies tear down these planks in an attempt to reach and then eat you. You must kill the zombies using your knife or gun.You receive points for damaging/killing the zombies and for rebuilding the barricades. you can use these points to open either an extra room on the ground floor, an upstairs floor, or to upgrade your gun.You progress through different levels by killing Zombies. But there is an endless amount of levels.
