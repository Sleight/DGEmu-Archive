The staff reserves the right to increase your warning level, deduct points, or ban you for disregarding the rules.Posting RulesDo not spam. This includes nonsensical and illegible posts, obnoxious posting style (excessive capital letters, 1337-speak, etc.), random comparison threads, advertising in posts or personal messages, off-topic replies, and offensive posts or personal messages.Do not make duplicate (repeated) threads or posts. Use the search function or check pinned threads in the appropriate section to see if your desired topic has already been covered and never copy or repeat someone's post.Please post in English. Provide some sort of English translation if you do not know it very well.General RulesDo not post or link to any obscene, offensive or pornographic material.Do not attempt to bypass the profanity censorship filters.Do not display more than 100 kb of images in a post. If the images exceed this limit, use URL tags instead of IMG tags.Do not ask for the following: translation patches (search romhacking.net), ROMs and ISOs, game saves, DP refunds, higher global DP reset, and emulators. Links to several emulators can be found IN THE POPULAR EMULATORS THREAD.Do not make multiple accounts (unless authorized by the administration) or submit a working account to bugmenot. In either case, the accounts will be automatically banned.Profile RulesSignature images should not exceed the following size limits: 450 pixels wide, 400 pixels tall. This also applies for multiple images all together.Signatures and avatars shall not exceed a combined 80 kb file size.In addition to the image limits, you may not have more than 10 lines of text in a maximum window on 1024x768 resolution.Personal photos may not exceed a 100kb file size.No profile elements may have or link to any obscene, offensive or pornographic material.Do not abuse members through the personal message or comment systems.Members may advertise in their signatures as long as they follow the general rules and do not use referral links.Contacting the StaffIf you witness a member breaking a rule and wish to report him/her, please use the report feature under each post. A direct report to a staff member through a personal message or email will most likely be ignored.Posting only to say a certain post is violating the forum rules is unhelpful, considered spam, and will be treated accordingly.Do not email the site or send personal messages to the staff members asking questions which can be answered by reading the forums or the homepage.Do not PM or email a staff member requesting for GPs, DPs or ROMs.Do not PM or email a staff member to argue about your warn level. You will be warned a second time if it's proven that your warn was justified.The rules and regulations are subject to change without prior notification.Mods, do not edit this thread without permission. Thank you.~DGEmu Administration
