He was then hit in the back, but claims did not hear the second shot, the third shot of course was the fatal shot that (pardon for my terms) spread the President's brain over the back of the limo. Despite that Oswald was supposed to be shooting from behind the President, the Zapruder film clearly shows that the shot must of come from in front of the President.The Government claims that all 3 shots were made by Lee Harvey Oswald from the Book Depository Window in rapid succession, a theory which has been tested and no other person has been able to replicate the speed at which he was supposed to have fired, considering the reload speed of a bolt action rifle. Then there is the case of the magic bullet theory. A single bullet which entered kennedy's back, escaped through his neck, went into connelly's back, exited through his chest, went through connelly's wrist turning at impossible angles in mid-flight, and finally ending up in connelly's thigh. A single bullet, the first one fired. Though witness claim only 3 shots were heard, the impossible magic bullet theory suggest up to 7 or 8 bullets from different angles, meaning more than one shooter. Oswald, who was said to have fired the "3 shots" supposedly walked, not ran, but walked from the depository, killed an officer named Tippit and was captured in a movie theatre an hour and a half after the president was shot.
