Mystical Ninja (Hidden Gem)

Awesome, overlookded adventure game in the footsteps of Zelda

Lleon_autumnORIGINAL POSTMay 06 2011, 10:10 PM
Even though the Mystical Ninja series has been around for a long time (since the MSX) a lot of people have never played it, or even heard of it. Thats OK though, because Mystical Ninja for N64 isn't anything like its predesesors, which were side scrolling beat-em ups with some adventure style item collection mechanics. So you don't have to play any of the previous installments to enjoy this bad ass game. The production values for this game are a little lower, but its an amazing 3rd-person perspective adventure title for a system that needed more of them none the less. It has a very goofy and outlandish story plot that is so rediculous it makes fun of itself, but that's the genius in its intentional humor. Its the only game I have ever played that has its own LAUGH TRACK. All the story segments play like an intentionally bad sitcom, Kabuki style. The template for the game is carved right out of a chunk of Zelda (OoT/MM.) The levels are pretty big with plenty to explore. You can find and upgrade multiple weapons. There are even towns to explore, each with their own unique selection of items you can buy. From different sushi's for your health, to armor for you body. One thing that really reminded me of Zelda was in each town there is a fortune teller that can give you hints on what to do or where to go next, for a price of course. The boss battles are really imaginative and epic in scale. The only thing lacking in the game I felt was a lack of enemy diversity. Well anyways the best part about it is its dirt cheap, anywhere from $5-$10 usually. Have you ever played it? If so whats your favorite...anything about it?
