Actually Shipudden, it can take 2 people but it's very difficult. 3 people help though as 1 can control the super scoping, 1 can control both the 2 Foxes (or Falcos), and another can control Peach. For 2 people, 1 controls the 2 Reflectors and 1 can control the SSing. When the Super Scoper has gotten the pattern of shooting correct (so that green blobs are appearing between the Reflectors), the guy controlling the Reflectors can switch to Peach and the SSer can stop (before the game lags to death). Peach then proceeds to throw 14-16 veggies into the scope line between the Reflectors. By the way, something extremely useful to note about the Protect the Egg event is that the CPUs DO NOT intentionally attack the egg. Their goal is to beat Yoshi off the stage period. Fortunately, this event is treated as a timed melee match where you have infinite lives (I believe) and the only thing you really need to do is leave the egg in a safe spot where combat is not occuring (or will not go off the edge >_<). Kills are all part of scoring a record.
