There are three ways to choose which animes you are going to watch and actually get the whole season.1. Word of mouth - This is only if your sources (like your real live friend) watched that anime and went into the details about everything in that anime. It will mean that your friend or someone online has watched the anime more than once which will mean that it's actually worth watching.2. Watching the opening on youtube - Yeah, youtube will ban all anime episodes being uploaded there, but you can still catch the intros of the anime and make a passing judgement if that anime is worth watching. Though this will just make you decide whether to catch the first few episodes of the said anime, it's still worth the time to watch it on youtube or other websites, after all, it's only a few mb worth of downloading file than grabbing a whopping triple digit file size of an episode or buying a dvd of the anime.3. View the first episode - This is where you will be able to decide if the anime is all hype and no show or if it's one of those eye catching animes few has heard before. Although sometimes the first few episodes of an anime is great, was actually not that interesting in the middle of a season. But at least you'll make your mind much more easily with this option.An example of this was when I first heard of the new power puff girl Z a few years back. Yeah I watch PPG and the anime might seem... well... interesting. It wasn't when I downloaded the first episode is when I decided to even stop thinking about the awful anime and never even bothered checking if episode 2 was ready to be downloaded.
