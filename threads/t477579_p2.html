KKirie MotobaSep 17 2012, 03:58 AM
Forget the hard drive upgrade. Not needed.
If you were to do it, it's easy nowadays... Macrium Reflect Free version, install it, create an integral image of your hard disk onto an external hard drive, create a rescue cd.
Swap the hard drives, boot from rescue cd, restore the image from external back onto new drive, and tadah, working computer.
Last time I did it, it took me around 2 hours to do the whole backup, swap hard disk, and restore. Not a single data lost, all my configs, updates, etc all intact as they were.

As for video card, yes, budget tight = 6850 or 6870.
I've used that card on 3 builds for different people, and not a single complaint about not being able to run games at max or near max settings.

Not worth getting a 7850 or 7870 unless you feel like paying more for a card that offers slightly lower performance, but saves you electricity and heating.
