Okay! Here I come, rocking the world with sick beat... *smacks myself*Um, smart answers.1: Chang Qing: You're basically right. The Ranger class is only for Seeq; Rangers are the ones that can set traps everywhere, from the MP Draining leeches to the stabby-needle-from-the-floor trap, to the devilish Love Potion charm trap. They're one of the basic classes for Seeq. There's also Hunter, which needs 3 Archer a-abilities to unlock, or, in the case of Gria, I think it's Devastator abilities. Hunters are the ones with special attacks like Sidewinder, which deals major damage to monsters, and Ultima Shot, which deals massive damage to (almost) anything.2: Dead Lover:ChocoKnight is a hard class to work with, but here's what'cha gotta do. First! Weaken a chocobo! Get it into critical HP, and it'll kneel down.Then! Get your Choco Knight to stand next to the wounded birdie. If he can take it over, an action command will appear; if he can't, it won't appear. Hit the 'Mount' command, and he'll hop on its back, and TA-DA! You now have midgets riding dinosau...*smacks me twice*...Sorry. Moogles riding Chocobos.A warning to ya all: Mounted Choco Knights can only use their mount's abilities; any A-Abilities you have set on them are inaccessable until they dismount, and if you dismount, the chocobo runs away, never to be seen again.
