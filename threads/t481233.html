Try this:

 

1. Puncture about 8 small holes on your hot dog. 4 holes on both sides. 

 

2. Bring out a frying pan and pour a small amount of water onto the pan (The water level should be about half the height of the hot dogs. Or the water shouldn't completely cover your wieners)

 

3. Bring the water to a boil. Once bubbles are forming, place your wieners onto the boiling water.

 

4. Occassionally turn the wieners ever 3 minutes.

 

5. Once the water has almost evaporated (Like there is almost no water on the pan), transfer your dogs to a plate and drain the liquid from your pan.

 

6. Dry the pan (or use another one) and place it on high heat for 30 - 50 seconds.

 

7. Pour a small amount of oil and bring the heat down to medium.

 

8. Place the wieners onto the frying pan.

 

9. Occassionally turn the wieners every 1-2 minutes.

 

10. Let it cook till golden brown.

 

11. Serve with rice or hotdog buns.
