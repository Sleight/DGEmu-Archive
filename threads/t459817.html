Hey Guys,Today we've taken another step towards the improvement of DGEmu and it's magnificent downloads system :( Your DPs BonusFirst, we've set all your DPs to be 4 times your daily reset. For most of you, that's 100 Global DPs.Global Downloads Points AccumulationOh yeah you read it correctly - we now allow you to accumulate Global DPs to up to 4 times your daily reset.Everyday, your Global DPs will change to either your daily reset (25) or your current Global DPs plus 40% of your daily reset addition (that is, 10 DPs), the highest of both!For example, if you have 17 global dps today, tomorrow you'll have 27, a day after 37, etc, until it will reach 100 DPs.So now almost every game in DGEmu is accessible to every member, with little patience.The Accumulation FormulaOkay we've wanted the accumulation formula to acknowledge good members, so in the formula we're considering the member's warn level. Where the warn level is added to the calculation of daily points accumulation. That is, if a member has been good and has a -10% warn level, his extra daily DPs will be 11 instead of 10. A member with a +10% warn level will get 9 DPs daily, similarly.The accumulation formula is the following:[center]global_dps + (100 - warn_level) * 0.004 * global_dps_reset[/center]Without a lot of mathematics, For a regular member without warns, it means your current global DPs, plus 40% of your global dps reset, or 10 DPs :( What's Next..Well with DPs more reachable to members, we can enlarge the site and support more types of systems.We also intend to add more improvements to the download mechanism, such as resuming and DP refunds in cases of download failures.Obviously we still need money to keep the site up - so we're considering enabling power memberships again (that is, a small monthly payment for enhanced accounts - 10 times more global DPs reset, etc).. we'll touch these issues when they come.If everything works good economically, we'll start expanding to more servers, allowing higher speeds and bigger games (3DS anyone?)Well, enjoy! - Ben
