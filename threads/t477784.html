http://requiem.seraph-inn.com/
The artwork is beautiful. The story is masterful. Sarah Ellerton is a genius and I've never read a better webcomic in my life. There are 800 pages total. Be careful when you start it because you won't want to stop. It's set not on Earth but in a time similar to our Victorian era. The characters are all human, but some of the creatures are fictional. Each of the characters has a robust story that is vegged out well throughout the piece. I don't want to give anything away, so just read it!


http://dreamless.keenspot.com/d/20090105.html
This is drawn by the same woman who wrote and drew Phoenix Requiem, but is written by someone else. I read the whole thing in one sitting. It's approximately 200 pages and concludes very well. The plot is so amazingly unique. I could easily see this being an award winning film if webcomics ever made it to the big screen. Of course the artwork is gorgeous and throughout the whole piece you will be begging for a happy ending.


http://paranatural.net/
It's fairly new, but if you can get past the first few non-color pages, the art improves swiftly, the facial expressions are awesome and the story is both funny and damned entertaining.


http://thepunchlineismachismo.com/
This one is a brilliant concept, has beautiful art, updates consistently, and is hilarious. Sometime this is a comic about macho action heroes. Sometimes this is a slice of life comic about a time traveling Navy SEAL single dad from the nonspecific spacefuture. Really, it just depends on how things were going that day.


http://awkwardzombie.com/
It was started by a 15 year old girl who thought she was funny. Fortunately for us, she was right. She focuses mostly on video games she is currently playing and a lot of it is Super Smash Bros. This one is full of gems and, if you like gaming humor, you have to read it.


http://www.abominable.cc/
The Abominable Charles Christopher is a gorgeous epic. It's rofessionally done and consistently updated. You might even cry and you will definitely laugh. This is one I will not drop for a very long time.


http://supernormalstep.com/
This is an interesting sci-fi comic but with magic. I find it entertaining, it updates consistently, the art is smooth and one of the characters reminds me of Bruce Campbell. Give it a shot.


http://gigaville.com/comic.php?id=1
If you can get past the MSPaint art then you will love this one. It helps to be a fan of Metal Gear Solid, but even without having played the games this is still damn funny and so well written and planned out. The great thing is that it is finished at page 500 so you can read the whole thing through without having to catch up then wait week to week. I really wish this were canon for the game series.


http://limbero.org/jl8
JL8 is a gorgeously drawn and perfectly written webcomic. The premise is that many of DC's superheroes all go to school together as their super hero personalities at the age of eight. We all really love it. They post it on FaceBook, but a fan made this website to read it on.


There are more that I read, but I think that will do for now. I'll add more a little later.
