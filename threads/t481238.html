KKirie MotobaAug 01 2014, 08:01 AM

Many things can affect.

 

An R7 240 does heat up a bit.

The fan probably buzzes because it's turning at max speed to try to cool your card, but if the buzzing starts sounding like rattling, then you got a fan ball bearing issue.

 

Also, room ambient temperature does affect the computer temperature also.

I personally had to shut down my server and put my desktop to sleep during heat waves here when I didn't want to turn on AC to avoid overheating.

 

Basically, if your room is deadly hot, you're just feeding how air into the tower and pulling out even hotter air. Vicious cycle.

 

A temporary remedy would be to open the side panel, and throw a desk fan pointing inside, but that's really a workaround and not a permanent solution.
