Phoenix Wright is an awesome game! Basically, it's based on everyday logic. It's kind of like Hotel Dusk Room 215, only you have a courtroom, and you don't have to open all the doors and pick all the locks just to get your hands on evidence. The real action of this game is displayed in courtroom scenerios, so that's where most of your wits will be tested. It's better not to use a cheat guide on this, since you don't actually need one to make it past the entire game. Some cases are more challenging than others, but that's the fun part about it!Anyway, I think the art overall is pretty good as much as DS graphics can go. The plotlines are pretty funny, and sometimes you get totally unexpected turns and outcomes. I'd give this game a 8.2 if I had to critique, but it's pretty awesome.
