This is only a draft, so feel free to make/suggest any changes. The red text should be removed before the topic is made public.Q: What is the warning level?A: The warning level system is a way for staff members to see how well (or badly) members are behaving. Bad members will have warning levels higher than 0%; good members will have warning levels at or below 0%. According to the GP -> DP conversion page, it is possible to have less than a 0% warning level. If this isn't actually true, then I'll change this.Q: Why does my warning level matter?A: The warning level directly affects your GP -> DP conversion rate. To be exact, each warning level point is equal to a 9% increase in the exchange rate. For example, getting warned once will increase your warning level to 10%. This then causes your conversion rate to be 190% the normal rate.In addition, the warning level may influence a staff member's choice of any further disciplinary action. If multiple rules have been broken, you may be suspended from the forum for any amount of time. If you continue breaking the rules, you may be banned from the forums completely.Q: How can I see my warning level?A: At the top of the page, you should see a horizontal bar that contains the text "Logged in as: {insert username here}" By clicking on your username, you will be directed to your public profile. Directly underneath your join date is your warn level.You can also see your warn level under your GP count next to every post you make.Q: Why was I warned?A: Put quite simply, you were warned because you did not follow the rules. The topics in the Rules & Guidelines forum (http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showforum=91) state all the general rules that must be followed while participating in this forum. Furthermore, specific forums may have additional guidelines that are stated in the pinned topics at the top of the forum topic list. Any violation of these rules merits a warning.Q: How can I see what I was warned for or who warned me?A: Wherever you can see your warning level, you can click on the numerical warning level (e.g. 20%) to open your warning log. This allows you to see the time the warning was given, the staff member who gave the warning, and also why you were warned. You may also see a link to the post for which the warning was given.Q: How can I decrease my warning level?A: First of all, stop breaking the rules. Staff members are likely to become quite annoyed if you continually break the rules, and you may simply be banned if you choose not to follow them. If you wish to have any chance of lowering your warning level (or keeping it at its current level), make sure to read all the rules carefully to avoid making the same mistake again.Once you have successfully completed that task, you then have a chance to win back the good graces of staff members by being helpful to the community. In order to do so, you may choose to be especially helpful in the forums or to report any rule-breaking posts to staff members. If a staff member decides that you have been sufficiently helpful and have contributed adequately to the community, he or she may decide to lower your warn level.DO NOT EVER COMPLAIN ABOUT YOUR WARNING LEVEL TO A STAFF MEMBER. Maybe this should be extended to making new topics/posts on warning levels also? If you were warned, then you must have done something wrong. Go back and read all the rules more carefully. If you still feel that you were wrongly warned, you may elect to politely PM the staff member who gave you the warning. Note that you do so at your own risk. If the staff member decides that you are simply trying to worm your way out of something that you did wrong, you may face another warning and other disciplinary action. The S&S FAQ also expressly forbids PMs that apologize for breaking the rules. I personally don't mind receiving those kinds of PMs, but let me know if you would prefer to ban those kinds of PMs also.As mentioned earlier, this is only a draft, so there may be some awkward wordings or missing information. Also, I'm not completely sure if some of those stylistic modifications (i.e. caps, bold, italics, underline) are appropriate. Please give me some feedback on what to change, add, or remove. Thanks.
