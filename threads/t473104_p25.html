Your 2015 Indie Game Maker Contest Results

 

Hey KHRS!

Thank you for submitting Rosco VS The Aliens in the 2015 Indie Game Maker Contest. We've had an absolute blast hosting this contest, and we hope you've enjoyed being a participant.

We've received many amazing entries, each of which had our judges impressed with your tremendous creativity, commitment and drive. From great visuals to challenging levels and unusual themes, we've enjoyed seeing what you've realized in one short month.

Finishing a project is tough, but managing something in a month is applause-worthy! So, congratulations to you for being a step above the average Joe and accomplishing something most people only dream of.

 

Below, we've included the scores and comments from our judges. Each game was played a full hour by at least one judge. The more rounds a game passed, the more judges played it through.

 
Judge #1's Review Presentation 15 / 20 Gameplay 16 / 20 Engagement 19 / 20 Penalties -2 Theme 5 / 10 Total 53
Judge's Notes
Thank you for Rosco Vs The Aliens. I can safely say that this is the only game that made me laugh in the whole contest so far. And not only laugh, but a couple times, ROARING laughter. Like, from the gut. The bit how his wife says "Wait!" And then gives a tutorial... I completely lost it. And the reason he's captured is because he's a delicacy... hilarious. I did feel like his wise cracks jumped the shark many times, but really, it's worth it for the home runs he hits. I gotta say, I could do without "balls" and "dickbreath." Gave you minor penalties for relying on overt crudeness. Really minor in the long run considering your very high engagement and gameplay scores. Really good mapping too, so a strong presentation score. No real grasp of the growth theme, but also minor in the big picture. Some complaints: -I'd reduce the amount of slimes in that big room. Really slows down the pace. -It's not quite clear how to get that "Z" critical hit. I think the visual feedback is a bit misleading. -Game over shouldn't really be game over in a game like this. It's a comedy game, not a crappy grindy RPG. It should just send you back to a checkpoint while rosco says something hilarious. All in all, what a refreshing entry. Instead of banging my head against the wall, I just laughed, and laughed some more. Laughing is fun, and games are supposed to be fun, so you hit the mark on this one!
 
Judge #2's Review Presentation 16 / 20 Gameplay 14 / 20 Engagement 15 / 20 Penalties 0 Theme 4 / 10 Total 49
Judge's Notes
Overall, I think the game is quite successful at what it sets out to do. Some pretty decent jokes and writing propel the story, and some of the small touches make this game stand out. The mapping is fairly well done, with some decent attention to detail without becoming too cluttered. There is certainly a bit of a discrepancy between sprite styles but it's not the worst offense ever. The sound design is spot on, and the small "talking blurbs" that play when interacting with characters fit the game perfectly. While the story is pretty hollow overall, it doesn't need to have that much substance to be successful. The writing is fairly well done, but there are certainly a few instances where the jokes don't quite land, and the player is left with a "yeah I get it" feeling. The gameplay itself is what you'd expect from an RM game, and doesn't deviate terribly from that formula, but I must say the inclusion of a time based crit system is a very welcome change in an otherwise formulaic genre. Well done overall! The game us quite successful, and I look forward to seeing your future efforts!
 
Judge #3's Review Presentation 17 / 20 Gameplay 13 / 20 Engagement 16 / 20 Penalties 0 Theme 4 / 10 Total 50
Judge's Notes
While not a masterpiece, the writing really did make me laugh, and the gameplay is decent enough for what it is. I really really hope "A Johnson Emerged" a the beginning of that battle was an intentional joke, because it really was the thing that made me smile the most.
 
Judge #4's Review Presentation 13 / 20 Gameplay 13 / 20 Engagement 14 / 20 Penalties 0 Theme 0 / 10 Total 40
Judge's Notes
A science-fiction film noir with a duck. Pretty funny stuff, enjoyed the premise and the battle system was surprisingly good. Fights were well-balanced and the timed hits was a nice little addition. I couldn't help but wish the game was a little bit longer, even by the standards of this contest it seemed unusually brief.
Final Score: 48

 

Note: If your game says disqualified, but has a score from at least one judge, then it was NOT screened out in the screening process and was played for the full hour.

-The IGMC Judges
