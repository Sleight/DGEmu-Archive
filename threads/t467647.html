GGaldion the wiseORIGINAL POSTMar 05 2011, 10:22 PM
Your sitting out that saturday morning cartoon just waiting for your parents to let you use your current VG console when a 30 sec commercial just smacked you on your couch with it's awesomeness. You then almost forgot about going over another level of super mario world or sonic the hedgehog and can't wait to play that game on the tv. Of course seeing a video game commercial is rare in our country but we were able to see this commercial that we first saw in a gamepro (or was that Electronic gaming magazine?) magazine Rockman x 2 commercial . I'm a total megaman x after finishing that game for the 40th time (call it achievement but you'll be playing the same thing if you only have two games in your new console) so this commercial got me and my brother all hyped and started working on our school grade. By the way, we just saw this on our cousin's VHS tape that he used to record shows in his visit to japan which is how we were able to watch this.There is just something about commercials shown on TV that has more impact than ones shown on the internet today. Possibly because information was lacking back then that anyone who saw the commercial will start talking about how cool it was and how cool the game was and HOW COOL it was to play it. There was no one who would discuss about gameplay, presentation, replay value or even about the story except whether it was HOW COOL the game is or HOW NOT COOL the game is. Anyway, what's your favorite VG commercial before or after the internet?
