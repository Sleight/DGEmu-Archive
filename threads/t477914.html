KKirie MotobaORIGINAL POSTOct 02 2012, 12:33 AM


Hello to all!
If you currently have nothing planned for the long weekend that is coming up, here's something you can do!

Celebrating the release of the latest Pokemon Black 2 and White 2 games, Nintendo is holding an event in Toronto and Montreal!

For those living in Toronto, the event will be open on Saturday October 6th from 9:30AM to 6:00PM and on Sunday October 7th from 11:00AM to 6:00PM.
This will be held at Promenade Mall, 1 Promenade Circle, Thornhill, Ontario.

For the people living in Montreal (such as myself), the event is open only on Sunday October 7th from 11:00AM to 4:00PM.
This will be held at La Récréation, 404 Ontario E., Montreal, Quebec near metro Berri-Uqam.

They will be giving away gifts on a first come first serve basis.

A reminder for those that are getting the game: Starting on October 7th, a Wi-Fi event to claim your own Genesect will be live. Don't forget to connect and claim it!
Another reminder: Don't forget to claim your Keldeo in the original Black and White games before October 6th so that you can trade it in to your Black 2 and White 2 game, unveiling its resolute form!

For those attending the Montreal event, you might be crossing me in person. :)

~Meru Meru
