Mmystic17xNov 16 2005, 04:05 PM
Gameplay.I'll be honest, I went straight to hard mode. I figured I could always go back and do normal if I couldn't handle it. Hard is challenging and forces you to actually make use of most of the elements of the game to be successful. After grinding through the first few missions, I mainly focused on developing my techniques and learning the system. Surprisingly, the implementation comes off as being fairly deep, which allows you to feel good about taking out a room of people with automatic guns. You need to manage your focus bar which isn't a bad thing... because it forces you to work with the system and not just find a cheap way to fight. Once you have it down, you'll quite merrily whistle as you ooze with style between the enemies.... smashing anyone foolish enough to attack you. I had a Matrix moment where I took all the enemies out by the pillars of the post office. Once done, I surveyed the area and was quite giddy to see how the place had been ripped up rampaging through the scene. It is a nice feeling to be sure.Graphics.I gave the graphics a 9. While the texture system is fairly simplistic, the overall ambience and new take on combat deserves recognition in my opinion. Their attempt is a solid one and comes off nearly perfectly, with moments of "That didn't look quite right." However, it's nice to see a game actually attempts to make combat look correct, with things like localized blocking. Example, a person punches for your head, you could lean away from the punch, or raise an arm actually blocking the swing. It all adds to the experience. Also, the models are very nicely done, especially with the player models which receive some noticible extra attention... enough so that you never for a second think "Who is that?" It's obvious who it is... because well... it looks just like them.Sound.I gave this a 9. I'm not worrying about all the dynamics of if it's 5.1 bla bla... to be honest at the end of the day... do you feel like you're there and do you feel immersed. Except for the extremely loud car... the music is great... the sound effects are great and satisfying in a very true to Matrix delivery. The game supports in-game digital and therefore it's very crisp and satisfying.Value.A definite 10. Offering a slew of things I don't want to mention as to not spoil it for you guys who are reading this to find out if you should buy it... but the amount of goodies you get from hacking (and I'm not talking about the unsatisfying type of goodies either... like concept art... which while neat... won't send many into girly giggles). These are REAL bonuses... the kind that when you earn them you will literally say outloud "COOL!" Not to mention the tons of film footage you get, oozing out all the extra info about the Matrix plot that you just didn't know going in. You can't beat that.I put a high reviewers tilt on here to give justice to the fact that this game truly is attempting something that other games have not to this point. If a trend like this could start, the future for movie games would indeed be bright. I tip my hat to what they attempt, and any fan of the Matrix will be very satisfied by this game.If a gamespot guy were to review this, you may find most of the 9's turning to 8's and the 10's turning to a 9's. However, at a bare minimum I want to throw off the silly graders afterall. But for my buck, this game delivers... and if I was given the chance to play another I would happily place a preorder for it.
