Attack Damage Carries:
Master Yi - Too easy, for him; you can make him Hyper-Carry that can outsmart Tryndamere if you are genius. His previous rework has prevent us to do AP-Yi for gimmicks.

 

Tryndamere - Hypercarry for me. A well-fed Tryndamere can almost survive any 1V1 (Dueling) without the help of his ult.

 

Fiora - She's like female version of Master Yi. Need to say more?

 

Aatrox - My pick over Tryndamere because of his passive. His passive is annoying if nobody really kills him well. He can be tanky thanks from Life Steal. Annoying.

 

Nocturne - Nobody plays him so well, but I like his ultimate! I oftentimes enjoy backstabbing enemies from afar with that.

 

Pantheon - This Spartan guy is epic. The real Pantheon players should know when to make a gank with his ultimate.

 

Talon - Well, he's like Katarina without having a passive that does cooldown reduction. He's burst assassin on the job.

 

Zed - I had a hard time practicing him since his combo mechanics is tricky to use them.

 

Ability Power Carries

Ahri - When playing as her, I did work well on feeding her since she'll be slippery at late game.

 

Akali - Whatever the enemy Akali does, NEVER FEED HER.

 

Fizz - Same drill as Akali. NEVER FEED THAT GUY.

 

Kassadin - The royal AP kicker. Seriously, this guy works like a foil to almost all AP carry.

 

Katarina - No joke. Kat's fun if you understand her well.

 

Teemo - His AP build is everyone's worst nightmare. Mushrooms maybe?

Hybrids (Meaning they can be AP or AD)
Jax - His build tells you to build items with lifesteal and spell vamp equally to make him ruthless guy at late game.

Ezreal - I've been arguing how to build Ezreal for a long time. I use Trinity Force for him.

 

Attack Range Carry
Lucian - Personal favorite. He's mix of Ezreal's skillshot and Miss Fortune's ultimate. His difficulty is that positioning is the key to make most of his skills especially his ultimate which looks like Coyote Stark's attack from Bleach.

 

Kog' Maw - Yeesh, this guy outranges the hell out of all ranged champions (second is Caitlyn since she has the widest attack range in auto-attacks). The laugh trip is his Bio-Arcane Barrage. Laugh at it.

Vayne - I easily turn her into a frickin' one-woman army. Last time, it took me 200 creeps and few duels to make her almost dangerous and I scored ACE with the help of my team-mates by softening the whole force.

 

Varrus - Nothing appealing about him. He's just DPS type of ranged character who possess a good damaging steroid and light field control.

 

Graves - The shotgun he wears? Well, it tells you that he needs to get close and personal and deal burst damage.
