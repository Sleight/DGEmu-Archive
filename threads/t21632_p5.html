Stop The Bus!!! It's such an awesome game and we play for money at school. Stop The Bus Rules:In the game stop the bus you get three tokens/matches/symbols (we use matchsticks) and you get dealt three cards and the rest are set face down in a deck aside from the top one which is turned face up in another pile. On a players turn they may pick up the face up card or one off the top of the deck, they will then have four cards in their hand and therefore must discard one face up on to the seperate pile next to the deck. You can only have three cards in your hand and the first one to get a score of 31 from those three cards wins and every other play loses a matchstick. However all the cards in your hand must be of the same suit otherwise will not count to your score (, picture cards are worth ten, ace is worth eleven and every other card is worth the number it represents. Also at any one point during a players turn before they have picked up a card they may knock and they miss their turn, every other player then has one more turn and puts their cards down face up and whoever has the lowest score loses a matchstick. Once a player loses all their matchsticks they are then freeriding and if they were to lose another matchstick would be out for the rest of the game. The winner is obviously the final player in the game.Just for anyone that wanted to play but didn't know how.
