Bbooyah_boyMay 09 2005, 09:46 AM
I believe you mean "critiques accepted", not "critics". A critic is one who gives a critique, not the other way around...That much aside, I enjoy the background render on the signature image, however you should look in to giving it a border (it helps to emphasize the image itself, setting it apart from the remainder of the screen and placing a boundary on such), including further graphic elements and/or text, and replacing "Frozen" with either stylized text, or else a more bold color of such (white text is difficult to read, save you strike such with black). Also, while on the topic of text, you may wish to make use of a different font (that is Papyrus, correct?), or else, increase the size of such in order to place further emphasis upon it. Lastly, the majority of signature images include some byline or catchphrase in them which denotes either a characteristic of the image itself, or the owner...might I recommend you do such as well. The inclusion of such will not only draw the casual browser to spend further time admiring the siggy (to read the line), but also give some insight into your own personality.I would recommend you take some time to review those signatures of others whose work you admire, and thenafter strive to emulate such, with the added artistic influences you wish to bring to bear upon them.Overall, a nice start...I give it a 5 of 10.Best of luck...
