It won't screw you, but it's always good to prioritize either magic or strength so you can get the most damage out of your MC with either of those maxed, what I gather about stats:Str: Determines damage from normal attacks and physical skills, also helps reduce damage from normal attacks and physical skills. If you go this route make sure to get Pierce so your MC can take on endgame demons which have resistance, null or drain physical attacks. Reflect Phys can still bounce back physical attacks though, and a few enemies (and one boss) have that.Mag: Determines damage from spells and helps reduce damage from magical attacks, adds points to MP. Almighty magic is great since it can't be repelled or nulled and only some enemies at the very end of the game have resistance to it.Vit: Determines damage taken from both magical and physical attacks, adds points to HP.Agi: Determines when you act in battle, how often you dodge physical attacks, hit rate for physical attacks and I think it influences critical attacks.Hope that helps.
