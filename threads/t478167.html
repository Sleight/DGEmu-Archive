And we're back!

November's here (or nearly here, anyway), so it's time for another DGEmu Monthly Mixtape. And given that November is National Novel Writing Month, I thought this theme might be a great idea...

NOVEMBER'S THEME: stories

Sometimes, songs tell stories. Sometimes, they remind us of stories we've read or heard. And sometimes, we've got a personal story to tell about a song. Send us your story songs, and be sure to tell about their story-ness with a short description!

THE RULES:
Each member can submit up to two songs. Any additional songs will be ignored, unless you indicate which of your original submissions you wish to replace.
To submit songs, upload them using a file-sharing service (such as Mediafire) and send the link(s) to me (mouse) via PM. Song files posted as replies to this message will be ignored. (You might also find links to song files through Bee MP3 - be sure to check them first!)
Include a short description (preferably 1-2 sentences long) in your PM that explains how each song relates to the theme.
If more than 20 songs are submitted, the judges will make the decision on which songs to include and which songs to cut. The judges also reserve the right to cut any song for any reason.
Album art submissions must be 300px by 300px and formatted as JPEG images.

REWARDS:
Submit a song - 50 GP
Submit a chosen song - +200 GP
Submit winning album art design - 1000 GP
Rewards will be given out when the mixtape is completed.

The deadline for song and album art submissions is Sunday, Nov. 25, at 9 p.m. EST. The finished album will be released before the end of the month, and the theme for the next mixtape will be announced at the start of the next month.

And that's it! If you have any questions, send me a PM or reply to this topic. Also, we're still seeking judges if anyone is interested.
