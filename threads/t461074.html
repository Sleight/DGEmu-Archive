b. Answer Format

The general attitude when replying an email is to be as proper and polite as possible. Remember that you are speaking in the name of DGEmu and therefore, you should NEVER insult or make fun of the person who sent the email. When a member begins to rant and insult you, just ignore it. Do not reply to provocation.

Suggested format for a reply:
someone wrote:
Dear Member, [Content of the email] RegardsDGEmu Support


c. Common Questions and Answers
For all question covered by the Site FAQ: redirect them to the Site FAQ (So read it first)
For all question covered by the Downloading and Site Problems or Support FAQ: redirect them to the Downloading and Site FAQ and Downloading and Site Guidelines or the OLD S&S FAQ (So read it also)
The member forgot his/her username: retrieve it here by using his/her email address, if you have trouble finding the name you should also use the Member Search via the forums or the Member Search Staff Utility (the Staff Utility is limited to finding one name, but it allows you to search usernames, the Member Search function on the forums only allows you to search the Display Names but it comes with a list of names instead, so use both depending on the circumstances.
The member wishes to change his/her email address: do it here
The member forgot his/her password: tell him/her to go on the Login Page and retrieve it by himself/herself.
The member cannot validate himself/herself: after verifying that it is not a dupe account, send them an email saying you will do it for them in a few short minutes, send the email, go back to the Inbox, if there is no Mailer Daemon notice (refer to'What is Mailer Daemon?') do it for him/her here, if there an email from Mailer Daemon then it is most likely their Email Address does not exist, you should check it (ie some think it is like a webpage and put www. in front, if so send it without the "www.", if it works then change the Email Address using the SS Utilities. Other times it may occur as the Email Provider blocks us, this is the case with ComCast, if a member registers which ComCast there is nothing we can do, you can leave the account or validate it and send them a PM instantly telling them that ComCast does not accept emails from DGEmu and they should use an alternative Email Providers (such as Hotmail, GMail, Yahoo, etc).
For ROM problems: if you know the solution, answer it directly, otherwise, either leave it to other SS or suggest the member to post on the forum after making a search for similar topics.
For donation questions: if you know the solution, answer. Otherwise PM a Sentinel, probably Kiba is best, also be sure to look at the Easy Donation using PayPal thread.
For Power Members questions: if they want to know how long they have to donate before they can cancel the subscription, you can tell them it is up to them, but be aware that we record each of the transactions and when they cancel their subsciption. If they abuse the system they will be dealt with; if they want to cancel their Power Member subscription they must do so on PayPal (logging into PayPal and unsubscribe from DGEmu). *This has/is being added to the Easy Donation using PayPal thread.
During forum upgrade in October 2011, there was a pruning of members that had no post and not active for several years. If a members contact about missing account, let them know that there was pruning of inactive members.
Other common questions can be found at Forum FAQ, Forum Rules and Guidelines or Support Staff - Answers to FAQ.

These points covered the primary job of an SS and should be followed by ALL SS without exception.

3. Others

a. Inspecting Reviews
As part of the staff, SS have check the Review Center regularly to decide whether the reviews submitted by members are worth being accepted. The Review Center is here. Make sure the review you are inspecting meets the requirements that are in the Reviews FAQ/Old Reviews FAQ. For all reviews, except those that are just spam, be sure to Google to make sure the Review has not been plagiarized, you can do this most of the time by selecting one sentence that you think will be suitable (NOTE: Choose a sentence that has NO grammar or anything in it, as Google will not notice them, especially apostrophes (')), paste it in the Google search and the include quotation marks ("Example") at the beginning and end so it searches for the exact phrase, if there is a match then it has been plagiarized and you should select 'Decline This Review And Deduct 10 DPs for plagiarizing/spamming'. Before pressing this though you should report this review in the Review Inspection Notes thread. If the review is not plagiarized or is spam then, you may use the following as Guidelines when inspecting views (these are not exactly the same as the ones in Reviews FAQ/Old Reviews FAQ, they are just ones I personally do ... I have a high standard, it really is up to YOU, you can make your own requirements if you want, the decision is left for you o make:
If the review is only a few lines long, less then 100 words then simply 'Decline This Review!'.
If the review is over 100 words but isn't at least 4 paragraphs and doesn't contain a scoring (eg. 10/10) for different categories (eg. Sound, Graphics, Story, etc.) then chose 'Approve This Review!'.
If the review is well over 100 words is about or more then 4 paragraphs long and does include scoring and categories as mentioned above then chose 'Approve This Review And Reward Member with 10 DPs!'.

Remember the above is just a guideline, you don't have to follow it directly. Also be aware that SS are not the only staff who inspect reviews, all other staff members are permitted to inspect the reviews as well. If you come across any reviews that have already been submitted that are spam, plagiarized, etc. you can report them in the Accepted Plagiarized, Bad or Wrong Game Reviews thread.

5. Support Emails
Below is just some of the common tasks you do with an email: It automatically sends you to the InboxClick on an email to read it (at the top in the centre a Loading image can be seen).

In the email read what it says, if you can answer it click Reply.

When loaded this is where you type the response and then click the SEND button.

Once the message is sent or if it is an email which requires no response click Delete.

If the email is regarding something like a Donation problem then click Forward and email it to webmaster@dgemu.com OR as Ben (DM) is very busy you can try PMing Kiba or another Sentinel and see if they can rectify the problem.

Below are several key things you should know about when you are using the Email system. Also we have had a discussion in the Read/Unread emails thread and decided that if you can't answer the email then go back to the Inbox, select it and mark it as unseen.

Another thing we agreed on is when answering an email write your response at the top (the beginning of the email) so this will make it easy to read the conversation in the email. An example of this is shown below:

someone wrote:
Dear Member Please read the Rules and Guidelines Forum. Regards

DGEmu Support
*Insert Name*

Quoting newbie DGEmuNewbie@hotmail.com: What are DPs?


Unfortunately this email system does not have a filter, so you must delete all the Spam manually. An easy way to do this is to click the Subject column header. Move all the Spam emails to the Junk folder instead of the Trash. (Simply drag and drop)

Contact Form Emails or real emails in which the user has titled something else should all be moved to Trash though.

What is Mailer Daemon?Sometimes after you send a reply to a support email you may notice a new email from someone name Mailer Daemon (MAILER-DAEMON@air627.startdedicated.com) with a Subject title of, 'failure notice', Mailer Daemon errors only occur when you try to send an e-mail to a non-existent account. There's nothing that can be done about that apart from trying to find out the member's actual e-mail using the SS utilities. If that doesn't work, there's not much that can be done. There are a few other things that might help though, firstly look at the Email Service Provide (eg. Hotmail, Gmail, etc.), this is the underline part in, Test123@hotmail.com, some people stuff the typing up, in fact we even had one member register with, expet4life@dgemu.com, thinking perhaps we are a Email Service too, this was discussed here.

Another thing is that some members have registered their email address using 'www.' at the start of it, if you find this has happened then delete it and send again, this was also discussed here.

Other times it may occur as the Email Provider blocks us, this is the case with ComCast, if a member registers which ComCast there is nothing we can do, you can leave the account or validate it and send them a PM instantly telling them that ComCast does not accept emails from DGEmu and they should use an alternative Email Providers (such as Hotmail, GMail, Yahoo, etc).

If you find a email that just cracked you up laughing then give other SS a laugh to, once you have replied to the email instead of deleting it, move it to the 'Hilarity' folder, to view these funny emails click on the 'Hilarity' folder.

If a member emails us asking why they were banned check the IP, if there are dupe accounts then that is the reason, if none then check their posts, if none then use the Search Function, looking in the Bannings subforum using their Display Name, if still nothing then check the Official Mass Spammer Report Thread to see if someone reported them for spamming, this thread is now old but can still be used for anyone banned before the start of 2008. If still nothing then post in the SS Email Assistance thread.

If you need help with a Support Email that you don't know the answer to, a member has been banned and you can't find a reason (as stated above), anything at all, you can post in the SS Email Assistance thread and one of your fellow SS or a Senior Staff member (Admin or Super Mod most likely) will assist you in finding out the answer. If you have a suggestion that will affect SS and/or all staff (eg. Ask for a button to be added to a staff tool) then you should NO longer post it in the OLD Suggestion Thread, instead you should make a topic in the Active Staff Discussions.
