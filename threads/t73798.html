Iiheart80085Sep 30 2005, 03:56 AM
The masking tool allows you to extract a part of a photo like a person, animal, or whatever. The masking tool is at the bottom of the toolbar. It's the square with the circle inside it. You can just mouse over it to find it. Click on it to enter masking mode. While in mask mode, you paint over everything BUT the object you want. By default, what you paint will be translucent red. When you're done, just click the mask icon again in the toolbar. Now, the object you painted around will be surrounded with the "marching ants" border just as if you were to use the magic lasso. Now you can move, cut, paste or whatever you want with the object. I hope this makes sense.
