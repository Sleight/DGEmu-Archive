AatomicknightDec 07 2007, 01:17 AM
Yeah, it's turned off. It's separate because the information is two different tables. The information for "hot topics" is just read from the topics table, while individual replies are read from the posts table. Turning it on would require one additional query per topic per page load. Given the default setting of 40 topics/page, that's 40 additional queries every time someone views the topic listing in a forum. You can't do it just by loading replies in a forum since the posts table isn't cross-referenced with the forums table. Even if it were, you'd still have to cross-reference that with the topics table to mark the appropriate topic. Regardless of how you do it, you're adding a huge amount of overhead to every page request.
