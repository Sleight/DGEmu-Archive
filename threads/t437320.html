EEcho738Jun 23 2009, 02:54 AM
The PS3 comes packaged with the standard RYW cables, and will "work" on any TV with these ports. HDMI cables (RGB) will only work on matching ports, but are totally optional and only allow for higher resolution display. In short, yes the PS3 will work on your TV, and you don't need to buy anything extra for it to do so.
