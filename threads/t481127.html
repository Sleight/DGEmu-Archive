I'm sure if he's an avid fan of the Pokemon series' he would like any of the DS versions of Pokemon, as i myself do, of course. Well, the best one in my opinion is form Pokemon Black and White series generation. It's called Pokemon Black 2, or you could go with White 2 since either one is fine. The only difference is a certain Pokemon show up/appear more in one version instead of the other and the legendary Pokemon are different between the two. It was also the latest Pokemon video game they made for the Nintendo DS i believe (aside from a few others that play a little different like the Mystery Dungeon series and Pokemon Conquest).

Really, take your pick at any of the following and i'm sure he'll like it. There are, however, as few i did not include (Ones from the Mysery Dungeon series' and Pokemon Ranger), the reason being that with Mysery Dungeon you simply play as the Pokeomon itself and make friends with other Pokemon, where as the other games you can play as a character (boy or girl) and catch them. As far as Pokemon Ranger i'm not quite sure since i've never personally tried it. Also, if it's a 3DS then he'd really like one of the two newer additions to the series' like Pokemon X or Pokemon Y.
