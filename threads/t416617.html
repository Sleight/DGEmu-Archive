MMahjixSep 10 2008, 06:01 AM
Okay. I think I can see where you were going with this. I just think this needs a little more work to bring out some oomph. The first thing that comes to mind is flow. What direction is everything going? I can see there's a hint of vertical flow, but is it the best? Look at his cloth around his neck. Draw inspiration from that since it has such a dynamic flow. Here's an example an array of flow.Another thing that comes to mind is color. Are the colors good? I see you have taken colors from the sprite's palette and incorporated that in your design, which is fine, but you don't want it to clash with your focus. Try some techniques to emphasize the character such as muting some of the colors or using some form of lighting or anything that won't fight with your focus especially dealing with sprites. Also, when working with color ask yourself if the colors are over-saturated or not. An over-saturated piece could drive the eye away (eyesore). Color is good but it doesn't need to be over-saturated example.Depth is something that a lot of people look for in a sig. Find ways to accomplish this while keeping things consistent. King D's sigs utilizes great depth and you could draw inspiration from his sigs or many others.Lighting is one of the most important things in art and could make things a thousand times better, however it is not a must. A really good vector based sig could crap all over a sig with outstanding lighting. Here's a sig tutorial on how to do some really cool lighting effects and other cool stuff.It may sound too much "like right," but you could make a checklist to see if you've covered all or many of these things.Yay for organization! :)
