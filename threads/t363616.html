Do not open another thread about Flashing all discussions and questions go in this threadWhat is Flashing?Flashing is the process of changing the DS or DS lites firmware allowing it to boot slot 2 flashcarts in NDS mode without the use or a passcard, passkey etc. It also also you to directly boot a slot 1 or slot 2 flashcart in case of a malicious software overwrites part of your firmware causing your DS to be bricked to run flashme again to repair it. It also allows the DS to use "download play" and download and play games from DS's that are using the rom to play.How to flash your DSBefore starting to flash your DS download the flashme of your choice here, also make sure your battery is fully charged and have the ac adapter plugged in in case of battery running out. You'll also need a small metal object to short the SL1 contact.http://home.comcast.net/~olimar/flashme/The different types of flashme are:Normal - Removes the starting screen and Health and Safety screen. Hold Select when turning on the DS to boot normallyStealth - Like "normal" but does not remove the starting and health & safety screenNo auto boot - Does not automatically boot slot 2 device if detected. Hold "select" to boot slot 2 when turning on the DSNo auto boot stealth - Like "No auto boot" but does not remove the starting and health & safety screenOnce you have gotten everything you need then:1. Place the Flashme file on your flashcart2. Open the battery cover of your DS3. Plug in your flashcart and passcard if your are using a slot 2 flashcart.4. Turn on the DS and boot into the flashcart5. Run the flashme program6. If you receive the warning screen, then your firmware isn't recognized, this isn't a problem but if you continue with installing flashme then uninstalling flashme later may cause problems.7. The next screen will advise you that if you turn off(or the DS loses power) while installing flashme could harm your DS. Press the button combination X, B, X, B to continue.8. The installation will now start, you should see "0%" at the bottom of the screen in order to progress further you will have to short the SL1 contact.Also make sure that the metal oobject is insulated on the site, as next the the SL1 contact there is a VR. If the metal object comes into contact with both the SL1 contact and the VR then the DS will turn off Pictures of SL1 contact "DS lite" on the left. "DS" on the right.9. Once installing has started you can't turn off your DS or it may cause damage to it. Hold the metal object inplace to short the SL1 contact and also hold the Battery os it does not fall out. Once it reaches "100%" then it is safe to turn off your DS as it has finished flashing.Your DS is now flashed :o Functions of a flashed DSNow that your DS is flashed you can do some more things when you turn on your DS. You DS should boot up a slot 2 flashcart if it is detected.Holding Select will cause your DS to boot normallyHolding A+B+X+Y will force your DS to boot slot 2RecoveryIf the flashing was interrupted and you manage to a bit installed (Not sure maybe about 10% and higher) or your DS got bricked by a virus then hold START and SELECT to boot in recovery mode which will directly boot slot 1 or slot 2 without the DS firmware where you can run flashme again to repair the problems. If I have left anything out then feel free to PM me so I can update the post. XD
