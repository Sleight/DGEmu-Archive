DDarkmage2Sep 04 2010, 04:48 PM
This... is kinda a tough one to answer. No one method will work for everyone, nor will that method work on every person of the opposite gender. But first and foremost, you need to be yourself. I know, it's something that gets said so much that it's ridiculous. But trust me, it really does work. With that said, your weird or blurting out makes it sound as if you're too nervous when you try to disclose your feelings to someone else. If you can, find some way to calm yourself down. Spend what time you can with the person, get to know them, gradually work up your courage. Again, I know, a lot of this is obvious, so just bear with me. When you feel like the time's come to disclose the feelings you have, though, try to keep yourself calm. Being nervous often ends badly.Finally, a little bit of advice. A person's definition of 'smooth' or 'sexy' varies from one to the next, and more often than not, isn't something that has a large impact. What does is being who you are. Unless, of course, it's leading to other relations where being smooth/sexy would be expected. Anyway, I hope all of this helps you.
