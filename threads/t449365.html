Games can affect lives, depending on how high the addiction level is. Normally deceptively simple games like Tetris and Pokemon can become addictive quickly.For example...Take a look at most Facebook games like Farmville, Mafia Wars, Restaurant City, Happy Aquarium, and Pet Society. These games are fairly simple and heavily engaging. These games also revolve around time (when growing crops or fixing dishes) which is why they're called appointment games. As a result, they become highly addicting after a few short playthroughs and can definitely control people's lives. There are people who set timers for strange hours at night just top harvest crops or fix up dishes because there are penalties for not being on the game at the right time (even though most people fail to realize that it takes double the time for these negative effects to happen, for example, it takes four hours for strawberries to grow in Farmville, and another four hours for them to completely wilt). Achievements and levels add to the addiction by creating incentives to play longer and more frequently. Adding friends and neighbors allows players to connect with friends (and strangers), which increases the engagement factor.Compare this to a classic addicting game... Tetris...Once you've figured out how to play Tetris, it will literally consume a lot of your life, and you won't realize it's happening. The fact that it is simple makes it a highly engaging game. Although Tetris is far different than a game of Facebook, they are both addicting for the same reason.So a highly addicting, highly engaging game whether it's Farmville or Pokemon has the ability to seriously affect your life.
