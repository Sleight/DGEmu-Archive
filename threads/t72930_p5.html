Well, my obvious opinion here is, as a user who downloads roms and stuff, and with my previous ownership of a PSP before it got stolen:Rom Player: DS wins PSPDS roms are MUCH smaller in size and are easily available due to web sites hosting downloads. They range in size from 2mb (or smaller) up to 100mb or more. PSP isos on the other hand, can go up to a whooping 1-2 GB per game!That shows how much you can play in the event you wish to switch games in a long journey.Hardware support for ROMs/ISOs:DS has many 3rd party suppliers like Supercard, M3, G6 and whatnot, with Slot1 and Slot2 to decide over as well. The support for these can vary and if you are stuck with a lousy one, its considered bad luck. With the PSP however, it used to be bounded by firmware versions (although now I'm not sure if it is still) and not by different card providers. So that means that most homebrew and stuff out there will most likely work.Graphics:Erm, there is not much to decide on, its obvious.Gameplay:At the point of time when I lost my PSP last year May, there were very little RPGs and the first RPG they had sucked. Anyway, I can't comment much due to the amount of new games out for the year I missed action.Battery life:PSP about 4 hrs of gameplay (depends on brightness) and NDS about 11 (depends on your card, Compact flash eats up more power)Overall statement:PSP if you want graphics and quite a good screen for viewing movies, NDS if you want a good entertainment system with extra stuff like stylus, and an upcoming motion sensor pak.
