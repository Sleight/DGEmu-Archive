First off, allow me to warn you against ever using vincent in battles you cannot win on a walk (such as the battles against ruby and emmy). He may be the coolest character in the game (it's why I use him) but his limit breaks leave him at a sever disadvantage. They cause him to transform into a creature and madly pumps out his stats. However, it also causes him to lose control and attack blindly over and again till either he is dead or the enemy is dead. Actually, for most of the game, this is a good thing as vincent can dish out far more damege far more quickly than anyone else. Towards the end of the game though, as everyone begins to be able to hit the 10k damage cap with their normal attacks, vincent's limit breaks suddenly becomes far less appealing, especially against ruby and emmy who you definantly need to have control over your entire party to beat.Cat Saith I have to admit I have never used. For me though , it was tifa who carried the day against the emerald weapon for me. I didn't even have omnislash, I did not have knights of the round, I did not have final attack. I did, however, have two of my people with the 4-hit materia, 9999 hp, and plenty of megalixers. The fight generally progressed in that manner, him hitting me and occasionally killing people (hyper lowers your defense for the benefit of getting your limits quicker), me hammering into him with 4-hits, limit breaks, and comets and keeping my people in the fight and generally commanding the pace of the battle the entire time. In the end, I won on sheer luck.
