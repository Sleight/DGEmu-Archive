TTheFlameAlchemistORIGINAL POSTJul 13 2010, 09:30 PM
So I just got back into playing games, and I decided to give this game a try since I haven't touched my PSP in months... and I am very surprised. I had given this game a try on PS2, but never even got to the first battle for some reason, but now that I've played it, I'm glad I did!! I've heard only good things about Persona 3 and 4, and this is one of the best PSP games I have ever played. A lot is cut out from the PS2 version like the animated cut scenes and walking around the world with your character (through your dorm, and school). The awesome fighting and persona system is still there and they added the ability to control your teammates directly. This game is a turn based RPG. If you are an RPG fan, or a Persona fan, you should definitely check this game out. They even made a girl character playable, and that adds a lot of new story from what I've heard, so if you've played the game before, try it as the girl now! I think it'll surely become one of the best PSP games of the year, check out the reviews: http://www.metacritic.com/games/platforms/...ersona3portable
