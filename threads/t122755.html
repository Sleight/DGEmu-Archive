Put up the 'doesn't load' placeholder, and put 'Error - Battery Error 2' in your report.See, there are 2 ways of dumping the NES bios. There's a proper way (dumping it as a regular chip) and the improper way (dumping it as an NES game). Those 2 roms were the improper way. Thus, the emulator thinks it's loading up an NES game when BIOSes shouldn't be loaded that way, making the BIOS rom do stuff it's not suppose to do.By comparison, see the properly dumped BIOS in the BIOS pack in the FAQ. Not only is it smaller, but the extention is completely different (.rom instead of .nes or .unf). That and the inner workings are different, too.So yeah...There's a lot of improperly dumped BIOSes in the NES set, or any other set. Things like base unit (NES, Genesis) BIOSes, addon unit (EX: FDS, 32X) BIOSes, Cheat Devices (Game Genie, Action Replay, etc), PAL-NTSC converters, System-to-system converters, and other BIOSes to other devices. Just screenshot them like any other rom, and report errors on them just like any other rom.
