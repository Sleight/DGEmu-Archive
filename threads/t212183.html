Ok, to start off, let me explain Magic: the Gathering to those of you who haven't played before. This TCG consists of many different cards, abilities and rules. The basic rules are this:-There are two types of cards: land cards, and spell cards. If it's not a land card then it is a spell card.-there are many different types of spell cards, but the basic types are: Creature, Sorcery, Instant, Enchantment, Artifact-instants are the only card that can be played at any time, even during your opponents turn. All other spells cannot, unless the game text of the card states otherwise.I won't go over the playing rules for this competition, seeing as this is a competition to create the cards. To learn how to play, go to http://www.magicthegathering.comOther things you'll need to know to compete effectively in this competition:Every spell has a mana cost in order to have them put into play. The better the card is, the higher the mana cost will be.Every creature has a power and toughness. Creatures cannot have a toughness of 0 when they're created for this competition, unless an effect on the card would put them into play with a toughness higher than 0.There are 5 colors of mana: White, Blue, Black, Red, and Green. To note these colors in the create a card competition, simply put W, U, B, R, or G respectively.Some cards have a tapping ability. Tapping a card makes it unusable until it become untapped again, which normally happens at the beginning of each turn. To indicate that a card must be tapped for the cost of an activated ability, simply put T.Here's an example of a card.Cruel PunishmentCost: 2BBEnchantment - AuraRarity: CommonGame text:Enchant CreatureEnchanted creature cannot attack or block. Enchanted creature cannot use activated abilities unless they're mana abilities.Any card must have a rarity. The rarities are: Common, Uncommon, and Rare. Common cards aren't usually full of abilities, and aren't usually very powerful. Uncommon cards usually are somewhat powerful, and can have many interesting abilities. Rare cards can have a lot of power and a lot of abilities, but usually have the highest mana cost. Alright, go make some cards! If the card is unacceptable, I'll help you figure out why it's wrong, and help you fix it so that it's right. If you have a problem with a judgement I've made on your entry, pm me.Here are the guidelines for the first card:-must be a sorcery-must be uncommon-must be able to do damage to a creatureP.S. all entries will be judged by me. Please do not vote or give suggestions as to who should win.
