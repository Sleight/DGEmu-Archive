The reasoning for this has been provided in the rules thread for the longest time:
DG Announcements' post='4329778 wrote on Sep 3 2008, 02:38 AM:
Do not ask for a higher global DP reset
At the time of this post, DGEmu has approximately 1,500,000 members. Assuming that every one of these members has 25 MB worth of daily downloading bandwidth, that translates to 37,500,000 MB (~37.5 TB) of bandwidth PER DAY. Even an increase of 1 global DP would increase that amount by 1.5 TB, so don't ask. We've already doubled the DP reset over the last few months and offer DP free PC games, PSP ISOs, PS1 ISOs, Wii ISOs, etc. (!)

If you're not familiar with bandwidth, it's the thing that allows you to download from us. It is also expensive. Each download consumes a bit of bandwidth, proportionate to the size of the download (hence the DP cost of ROMs are also proportionate to filesize). No more bandwidth=no downloads for everybody. If you want more DPs, earn them yourself.
