Im fairly good at drawing, I usually draw pictures of sonic the hedgehog, or DBZ characters.But here are some helpfull tips I use.Before you start a drawing, make sure you know exactly what you want to draw, then burn the image you want to draw in the back of your mind. Invision the image on a piece of paper, and just follow the lines.I usually start at the top and work my way down and I shade the picture as I go.The more detail the picture has the better, it will keep things proportionate to each other.Try to hold the pencil or pen loose, so you dont tire out your hand, only hold it firmly when you are dark shading.Well, thats it for now, if I think of any other tips I will add them.
