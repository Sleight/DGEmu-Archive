For the most part, this has been ignored and left under the radar and for good reason: It was Microsoft attempting security. Who the hell in their right mind took that seriously, right? Between Windows Defender, Windows Firewall, Windows Live OneCare, etc., things were looking pretty bleak for Microsoft.However, much to even my surprise, beta reviews of MSE are positive nearly across the board. Today the final version dropped and it's looking good.Of course, although I don't recommend this as a replacement for a suite of any sort that includes a firewall (I'm sticking with my Comodo, regardless), this is a good supplemental anti-spyware program that could take the place of IOBit Security 360 (which I've recently touted in my guides) and definitely does take the place of AdAware. Even if you're using PC Tools Firewall Plus and Avast! (as per my updated recommendations which will need to be posted in the maintenance guide), this is still good to have in addition and not as a replacement by any means. Best of all it's free and still performs better than any attempt at a security program by Microsoft to date. Warning: your copy of Windows must be genuine.
