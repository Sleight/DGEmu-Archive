I'll try and compile a list of pros and consBattlefront IPros- Tank blasts are much stronger- Tank blasts have amazingly strong splash damage- The Jet Trooper's EMP shockwave actually have a decent area of effect radius- The tanks were tougher- Dogfight capabilities in land maps- Troop transport capabilities in land maps- Massive maps- Each planet had a few locations on which you could battle- The A.I. was considerably tough- The spawn point vehicles couldn't be re-deployed after they were destroyed.Cons- Invincible hero units- Not being able to be a hero unit- Not being able to sprint or roll- Mines were ineffective against vehicles- Vehicles are considerably weak- Battles could drag on for long periods of time- Not much variety in terms of vehicles- Maps can get really confusing- Absolute terrain advantages in terms of maps for certain sides- No true counter to air threatsBattlefront IIPros- Space battles, everything about them. The whole concept was amazing- The ability to play as a faction's hero- The timing/health feature on the hero- New classes such as the Clone Commando, IG-100 MagnaGuard, Bothan Spy, and the Imperial Officer- The ability to sprint and roll- New vehicles- FLAYMETHROWAZ!- The amazing alternate storyline, and how each mission is carried out- Everything is essentially more fast paced- The in game achievements allowing you to get bonuses- All the new modes (Hunt, Assault, CTF, etc.)- Slightly better graphicsCons- Space Battles can get tedious- Heroes are somewhat uber units compared to the rest of the the playable units, they far outperform them- Vehicles are super tough...- However, only 1 mine can kill them- Spawn point vehicles can re-spawn indefinitely- Small, boring maps compared to BFI- There is so much more room for improvement- Extremely stupid A.I.Overall, however, I would have to say I liked Battlefront IIbetter.
