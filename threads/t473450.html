There was never a point in having such a big forums anyway.It seemed like a brilliant idea at that time, to get members to participate actively in the community in return for downloads and other free stuff, but on hindsight all that that did was to artificially inflate the size of the forums. People who don't really want to post come in here, talk some nonsense, get what they want. Some of them make friends and stay. A few of us found something that we wanted to do here. Ultimately though most of the forums consisted of people who were just pretending that they wanted to hang out and talk about stuff here. This has finally collapsed upon itself.I am in favour of keeping the more technical and more helpful "support" forums, as I always have been, and of course you should keep a few forums here and there for chatting about stuff, but much of the rest is irrelevant already, and has been for some time (perhaps even from the start).If you want an actual, unforced community with true substance, then you will, well, need true substance:1. Timely, relevant, updated, helpful support, for example with roms and downloads. Some of you might have noted that this is what I had tried to provide. I saw for some time that it was essentially the only thing I was enpowered to do that would be truly, ultimately helpful. Even then, I quickly realised it was ultimately fruitless (more below).2. A few forums on the roms, consoles and stuff: Eventually, I suppose, every roms site will draw some curious people who do not mind talking about stuff that people who frequent roms sites will probably like. Give them a conducive environment to do this, and let a community grow naturally from that. This is the usual way such communities grow.3. A few other forums on general stuff: Occasionally people also like to talk about other things, naturally enough. Again give them a p nice and decent environment to do that. At the very least you have gotten rid of the source of the retards who clog those discussions with stupid useless responses, so this could actually work. #3 will probably happen after #1 and #2 though.4. Other stuff, once you get a decent and genuine community: The additional things. The Debates forum for better discussions. Hackerx's arcade. Extra stuff for people to stay and do. I guess the SC counts as one of these things already. It's essentially a haunt for people who already know each other, to chat in a familiar environment and chill out and so on.I don't know what else to add, really, but as I see it the above are what a real community has, or rather how it forms and develops.If you want to stick to the forums perhaps you might like to try following that, or something.Don't expect me to help out again though. I've burned much of my teenage life here already, trying to help to the best of my abilities. And all I achieved is, well, this. My life is more valuable than this, I'm afraid I have to say.Just my two cents, FWIW.
