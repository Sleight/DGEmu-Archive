To get curvy lines, Kaber, you - Set an anchor point, click somewhere else, hold and drag the mouse around to get a curve - shape.When the path is made, select a round brush of size equal to the thickness you want of the curvy line.Once you've done that, select the "Paths" tab from the Layers panel, and click the icon of "Stroke path with brush". (With the workpath and brush selected).[Screenie]
