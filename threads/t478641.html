MMutantXJan 21 2013, 04:15 AM

The way to sync up a remote to a Wii is by opening the SD card slot on the face of the Wii and then pushing the red button. Then wait for a couple of seconds and then push the red button that is located inside the Wii remote, right next to the battery slots. Just take off the back cover of the Wii remote, you'll see it.

When the blue lights stop flashing and then light up solid, it's synced to the Wii.
