Ganondorf, the Gerudo King of Evil Vs. the One Winged Angel, Sephiroth.Who would come out on top if they were to somehow cross pathes?WARNING: Don't read if you don't like spoilersGanondorf:Ganondorf hails from the Legend of Zelda Universe, and is the archnemesis of Link and most often than not final boss. He is an extremely powerful sorcerer and swordsman who has time and again laid waste to the land of Hyrule in a bid to claim the power of the gods known as the Triforce. With the Triforce of Power he was able to gain immortality and became nearly godlike in power which made him an even greater threat. He has killed many people including women and children alike in order to meet his goals. Although he is clever he is often overcome with his burning rage which often leads to his defeats. He is often also portrayed as a Blue Pig Demon with a giant Trident which is also very powerful. In this incarnation he is simply known as Ganon. Alot of time Ganondorf likes to manipulate others to do most of the dirty work for him as he waits on his throne at the end of the final dungeon. However, he is still a very powerful foe, even without the power of the Triforce and most of the time cannot be defeated without the power of the blade of evil's bane, the mastersword which can only be wielded by the chosen hero of the three goddesses who created the Triforce.Sephiroth:In the Final Fantasy universe, he hails from Final Fantasy VII and the archnemesis of Cloud. On this game's earthlike world the planet has a force inside it called the lifestream which is the source of life for the planet and is also where dead souls return to after death. Long ago a powerful alien life form known as Jenova fell to earth. This creature resembled a fallen deity, but it was far from it. It traveled the universe consuming world after world. After crashing to earth it made its way across the planet killing many people until an ancient race sealed it away into the crater it created when it arrived. It stayed dormant until scientists thousands of years later found it and began to experiment on it. They used the cells from its body to try and create super soldiers. Most of them transformed into vile monsters, but one met their expecatations. Sephiroth, while still in his mothers womb was injected with Jenova's cells and after being born looked like a normal human being. Through his life he grew strong and very powerful, almost god-like in strength. After learning of the terrible experiment that made him what he was he turned on humanity, believing they must be punished for their wicked ways of sealing Jenova away. With this simple misunderstanding Sephiroth became a seemingly unstoppable force, capable of creating clones of himself and contolling anyone with Jenova's cells inside them or even using them as a conduit to recreate his own image and mind inside of them. He attempted to create another impact crater in the earth by casting a meteor to fall to the planet in order to gain control of the planet by taking control of the planet's lifestream energy. He morphed his body into a creature with 7 wings, his image of a divine being. Even after death he came back in order to bring pain to the world and his enemies. His power is unreal.
