SShrapCatteFeb 20 2005, 06:26 AM
The quick version; I like cats.The long version;When I was younger I had an obsession with chickens. This grew into almost a need to buy everything that had anything to do with the animal. This obsession continued until one day in preprimary, when I discovered a small ceramic model of a cat buried under the sand, which, when I originally saw it, I thought was a boot. Of course, on closer inspection, I found what it truely was. This triggered a second obsession; that with cats. I had this for a long time, once again, collecting everything cat-like - soft toys etc. until I played a little game called 'Soldat' on the internet, which sparked my love of weapons and guns. I then made a series of MSPaint works to do with bullets and shrapnel. The alias I used at that time, was of course, "Shrapnel". After realizing that I still had a great love for cats, I combined the two, the picture of a bullet with the small ASCII image; ">>^-.-^<<". I then thought to myself that the picture of the bullet was stealing the show, so to speak, and removed it, ending up with simply the small cat-like symbol. Having done this, I decided that I should combine the name aswell, and so became the alias; "ShrapCatte". I've adapted this many times on many different art works, which I use mostly with the popular online IMer; "MSN Messenger" as "Display Pictures". Finally I created an artwork that was purely centered around the small symbol. It was made to look slightly like the yet-to-be-released-in-Australia; PSP, with the symbol on what looked a bit like an LCD screen. Since then I've always used the same alias and have adapted most avatars, pictures and signatures to suit it, and so, to suit me; ShrapCatte.That's how my signature represents me.Anyway, I'd love to continue debating but if I don't seriously get my homework done, I will no longer have a head to debate with tomorrow and further into the future. So off I go for now.
