someone wrote:
The serpent of Gaia Rock in Golden sun: The lost age (i'm not very sure of it's name) but when you haven't weakened him yet he's terrible, he recovers like 1000 hp each turn so in that shape he's invencible.
The serpent's name is Orochi, that whole scenario is based on a Japanese myth about the sea and storm god Susano-ou. He rescues the maiden, Kushinada, from being sacrificed to Orochi, a multi-headed serpent that was terrorizing the Izumo province by blocking the main river with its two huge tails. It demanded regular virgin sacrifices- Susano-ou agreed to slay Orochi to prevent Kushinada's death in exchange for marrying her. He offered Orochi 8 large barrels of pure sake (which has a very high alcohol content btw), one for each of its heads. After it consumed them, he attacked, and due to their drunken state, severed each head. While cutting up the tail to unblock the river, Susano-ou finds a magnificent sword, Kusanagi no Tsurugi, "grass cutting sword" (it is also called The Clouds of Heaven). He gives it as a gift to his sister, Amaterasu the sun goddess. Hint Hint from that story. If you didn't know about that, after you kill Orochi, use Sand and go behind the corpse, and check the tail by the water. You will find a strong sword (if you get it the moment you can), Clouds of Heaven. It has an unleash effect too. Do note the english version of Golden Sun: Lost Age changed the dialogue a bit to hide the alcohol use: sake becomes 'dragon bane'. The names weren't changed and still reference the famous myth, right down to Susa's son. Yamato Takeru was a son of Susano-ou.
