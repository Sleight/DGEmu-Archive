GGagaganMay 11 2008, 05:54 PM
Square-Enix and EA represent absolute opposites on the game development spectrum.EA is mostly schedule-driven, churning out installments of their sports franchises each year, with features taking a back seat. If the development team is ready with a new feature by the deadline, it goes in. If not, the game is released without it anyway. This ends in most games being just player updates with slight graphics and movement tweaks.S-E is mostly feature-driven, with a detailed set of game features being the top priority, often meaning that games get delays beyond the original deadline. The game is not released until at least most features are ready.Personally, I prefer Square-Enix's games by far, since it doesn't matter to me how long it took to make a game. Once it's released, you compare it to other games on an equal footing, and EA games generally can't compare to S-E's quality.
