KKHRSJul 27 2015, 08:30 PM

This was my first time making cheesecake so it came out a little... off. Also, I didn't use a recipe I just threw stuff together

 

crust = http://www.gotchocolate.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/51fDiRul3yL.jpg

Those grahams come in 2 sleeves and I used a whole sleeve for the crust. Crush the grahams into dust, mix with melted butter so it turns into beach sand, smash it into a cheesecake pan as tight as possible, bake at 350 degrees for 5 minutes.

 

cheesecake:

-2 packs of philadelphia cream cheese

-3 eggs

-X amount of nutella (however much you like)

-vanilla extract

-Sour cream (I didn't put this in but I should have. It makes the cheesecake less dense and mine came out really dense but it's k cuz I like dense cheesecake)

 

Cream the cheese by using either a blender or a whisk or some shit. Mix in 1 egg at a time so your shit doesn't get lumpy. Throw everything else in. Throw cheesecake filling in pan over the crust. Throw your crushed up nuts (optional but the nuts were my favorite part) on top of everything. Bake at 350 degrees for approx 50 minutes. Sides should be set but the center should be jiggly. Take it out and let it cool for a few hours. Put in fridge over night (don't eat hot cheesecake, it's fucking disgusting). Eat. Water bath is optional. I didn't bother with it.

 

Disclaimer: It doesn't actually taste like Ferrero Rocher, it just tastes like chocolate, cream cheese and hazelnuts, which is fucking delicious
