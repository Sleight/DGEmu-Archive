Oobsess5Sep 12 2010, 01:59 PM
Definitely aimed at children, this is a nice story structured as a cartoon slide show interspersed with simple puzzles. For example, clean the engine: here's soap, rub the soap all over the engine with the stylus; here's a water hose, wash the soap off the engine using the stylus; here's a rag, dry off the engine using the stylus. I'm in my 50s, but most 3- and 4-year-olds are probably better gamers than me; still, it only took a half-hour to finish the game and I doubt if many can best that time by much - after all you've got to watch and listen to the cartoon! The mini-games are just re-runs of the simple games in the story.The game is too easy with virtually no replay value, but I like trains and the novelty of this particular game made me glad I downloaded it.
