Absolutely fantastic series, especially the first two books. The first one dealt more with the sci-fi aspect of things, which was interesting, but then Speaker for the Dead turned out to be monstrous. Never before have I seen a book so artfully intertwine science fiction and human emotion to tightly together. That book goes far beyond your standard sci-fi novel and really expands upon the human condition and was a VERY powerful book. I finished it, laid it down on my table and just sat there in disbelief, wallowing in thought for a good 10 minutes. Really makes you think.As for what I got out of them? Well, nothing is sacred, and I really don't remember much more about them since it's been a few years, but I remember that it impacted me greatly. One of these days I'll reread it and get back to you with what that impact really was. My memory sucks.
