Yeah, you evolve.
You don't grow levels though, you grow links (friendships) and each percentage point you go up nets you a stat boost.
After a certain friendship level, you can evolve. (for pokemon that evolve by leveling at least).
There are stones. I have no idea how happiness and trading evolution go.

Also, Eevee is a fine starter. This game has nothing to do with the regular pokemon series. I'm sure there is technically a best team, but there are no pokemon that are useless (except Magikarp). Your pokemon grows based on it's relationship with the trainer. if you link with a bidoof and it's a golden link and you max it out, Bidoof will just as capable of taking out legendaries as any other pokemon out there. The link is what's important, not the pokemon. That being said, even if it's not a golden link, growing your pokemon will still net you strong results. You basically get to grow the pokemon you like. There are actually a total of five ways to evolve a pokemon in this game. :o You can just get your pokemon to a certain link% where they'll evolve. All you have to do is battle frequently. Then there are item evolutions, where you place an item on a pokemon and after a battle they'll evolve if there high enough (Ex: Evee + any stone) Next, there is an evolution that requires a specific area. For example: In order for Evee to evolve into an Umbreon or Espeon, get him to a certain link% then battle with him in that area to evolve. Espeon: Illusio, Umbreon: Yaksha. You can also evolve the pokemon by getting them to a certain stat. Like with Steelix. If you get an Onyx to a high enough defensive stat, he'll evolve. Lastly, there's warlord combat. Which means get your chosen pokemon to a certain link%, then battle and defeat a warlord with that pokemon... Easy. :)
