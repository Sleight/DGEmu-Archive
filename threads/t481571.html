Avoid Halo: The Master Chief Collection...

FFarsight XR-20ORIGINAL POSTFeb 18 2015, 10:57 AM

This game came out exclusively for the Xbox One in November. It was intended to contain upgraded ports of Halos 1 through 4 all on a single disc, but it was nowhere near finished when it launched, and 90+ days after, patches have STILL been unable to fix the worst problems.

 

Matchmaking is effectively broken. Long waits, uneven teams, failed map loads, buggy parties, and plenty of quitters thanks to only one playlist being ranked.

 

Campaign doesn't always save properly.

 

Hit detection issues in Halo 1 and 2, desynch issues in Halo 2 Anniversary.

 

Numerous bugs in Halo 1 stemming from the use of Gearbox's bad 2003 PC port.

 

Several control schemes have labeling and mapping issues.

 

Halo 3 and 4 are outdated builds compared to Xbox 360.

 

It's actually NOT all on a single disc - multiplayer requires a MASSIVE patch.

 

Dedicated servers, a heavily promoted feature, are a rare sight.

 

The developer, 343 Industries, has been very evasive since launch. The company's president, Dan Ayoub, has gone AWOL, and their community manager, Andy "Bravo" Dudynsky, never replies to questions with anything more detailed than "it's coming/we're working on it/an update is planned", et cetera. And the franchise development director, Frank "Frankie" O'Connor, INSISTS that the matchmaking problems caught them TOTALLY by surprise... :whistling:

 

Oh, and Halo 5 isn't going to look or play anything like the older Halos.

 

Good job, Microsoft! :D
