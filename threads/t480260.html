I don't get why so many people can't appreciate FFT"s story's complexity. NAH NIGGA FF7 WAS DEEP, FFT IS 4 BABBYS.

It might be a Shakespearean-esque biblical allegory, but I haven't seen many games that come anywhere near it in terms of complex character relationships and growth.

 

The only thing about FF4 is that you have to appreciate the time it came out. I'm not saying that NOW it's one of those GOTYAY FF games like 6 or 9 that deserve like 20 re-releases (though it did get way too many re-releases and ports), but at the time it came out it had a pretty good storyline and was the first in the series to do so. Honestly I think FF4's story is better and has more complexities compared to like FF5 and FF3 which were games that were about on the same scale (big overworld exploration, changing main characters, stuff you had to find on the side like jobs/classes/items, etc.) but each featured more generic characters and plot points. I know FF4 has it's generic Star Wars-esque moments, but more importantly you have to appreciate how the game progressed. Things like meeting Rydia as a kid and then seeing her again but grown up, the way the party switches in regards to the plot, little things like finding Yang passed out close to the land of Summoners after he tried to 1v1 Leviathan, upgrading the initial Paladin's sword into Excalibur (which they did again essentially in FFX), and lots of other things. I mean by today's standards the story probably doesn't seem that good but at the time it was great.
