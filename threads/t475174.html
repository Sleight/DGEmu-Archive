There's 2 different sets usually available online:

- GoodSet: Their goal is to classify as many available dumps of games available, meaning hacks, bad dumps, homebrew, etc. You'd choose this when you want to have a complete list of games every dumped (meaning tons of dupes in the process).

- No-Intro: Their goal is to have a single set of perfect dumps, meaning all hacks, bad dumps, homebrew, ROMs with intros are not inside this. You'd choose this if you care only about official non-modified ROMs.
