I can go both ways on liking/disliking a hanging ending. For me it comes down to what the overall tone of the movie was. Inception was a movie that tended to jerk you around in your perceptions of what reality was and should be. From that stand point, the somewhat unclear ending fits in with the tone of the movie by jerking the audience around one last time.It also reminded me just a bit of the Matrix. In that movie, after you left the theater, there was that general sort of uneasiness about thinking how it is we actually perceive reality, and whether or not our perceptions can lie to us and reality is something quite different than what we expect. Inception's ending had much that same flavor of disjointedness, and that questioning of what the differences between our perceptions and reality are even after you leave the theater, again driving home the point of the movie, which makes the ending a successful one.
