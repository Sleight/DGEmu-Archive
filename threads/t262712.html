Ok, the rule at hand:
5th posting rule wrote:
Do not post meaningless threads, one word (or short) nonsense posts, or the likes, and do not "bump" threads to keep them near the top of the list. Random comparison topics that do not provide any sort of reasoning for the comparison will be closed on sight.
I have received some PMs from members over the last couple weeks questioning my point deductions of their short posts, and I think it's time we make this its own separate rule, and clarify what we mean by short post, because we all have slightly different interpretations, and this makes the justification for point deduction rather unclear at times.Here's my interpretation for what should be deducted on the grounds of being a "short post":Incomplete thought/sentenceAnswering the topic without a reason behind it (example: "My favorite Pokemon is Zapdos", no reason given for that opinion)I deduct if it's less than 5 words posted, with almost no exceptions, because you can rarely follow the other 2 things I just said without doing that anyway and it's cheap and lazyIf it's just a game title or something, even if the title is long (example: "Moero - Nekketsu Rhythm Damashii Osu - Tatakae - Ouendan 2")I have seen other things that didn't fit this get deducted as short posts too, so it would be good if we could come to an agreement on what a short post is exactly, and if we could clarify this in the rules so that members have no excuse for complaining. I don't know what others necessarily interpret that rule to mean, and I don't know if you think I'm interpreting it wrong, so a standard on it would be nice. Any input whatsoever would be much appreciated. ;0
