RReinsolAug 15 2006, 03:11 AM
I'll break it down nicely for ya: (Remember, these are based on chronological achievements as well as stand alone gameplay.)The Shin Megami Tensei/ Persona Series: As good or better than Final Fantasy. The series itself deals greatly with the occult, and is geared towards a much more mature audience. Don't pass this one up. Dragon Warrior (Dragon Quest) Series:I'm surprised no one mentioned this, it's been around slightly longer than Final Fantasy, and has a following almost as dedicated. Truly great games, with a surprising amount of attention to detail. (Check out the battle engine on DW7, crazy stuff)Suikoden Series: These games are simply amazing. One of the best storylines ever to grace an RPG. 108 playable characters, none of which are boring or repetative, strategy war scenario's, and enough fun mini-games to bide the time. No RPG lover should EVER go without playing the masterpiece that is Suikoden 2.Breath of Fire Series: They all have their highpoints, and while the story remains much the same throughout, they all have great little innovations which heighten the gameplay. With every new release the series just get's better and more inventive.Shadow Hearts series: Kicks-donkey, with awesome occult themes, and a unique battle system. The characters are all memorable and the series ties together nicely.Xenogears/Xenosaga Series: Amazing, very convoluted story and unique battle system. A little hard for some to follow along with, but well worth the time. A cinematic masterpiece.Star Ocean Series: Indepth storyline, mixed genre's (Fantasy/Sci-Fi) awesome reaal-time battle system and a plethora of mini-games make it greatly worthwhile.Tales Series: I'll say this with almost complete asurity "Best Multiplayer System Ever in an RPG," the battles are fast paced and fun to play. Add in the multiplayer experience and deep storyline and you get a stellar series.Disgaea: (SRPG) A hilarious, easy to follow story, mixed with near unending gameplay. I've played this game for well over a hundred hours and am still no where close to completing everything. Your level cap is 9999, every item has a 100floor dungeon in it, and the game mechanics are amazing.Super Mario RPG/Paper Mario series: Mario isn't the king of gaming for nothing, anything with his name in it s gold, and this is no exception. Just as one would expect, very fun, memorable adventure.Valkyrie Profile: Any game that deals with Norse Mythology is bound to kick-donkey, but this game isn't just great because of that. A truly innovative 2D level design, with an amazingly intuitive (not to mention fun) battle system. This game is gold. The icing on the cake is the storyline and character developement, which is top-notch.Earthbound: This game has to be one of the first RPG's to gain a huge cult following. It's hilarious, yet very simple.Dark Cloud 2. I've spent many, many hours playing this game. The story is simple fare, but there's so much to do in the game that it's nearly unending. Beautiful graphics, great gameplay, a mass amount of weapons to choose from, multiple play styles, the ability to customize your own city, and tons of mini-games make this one a gem.Lufia 2: Interesting story, memorable characters, and a great puzzle system. Reminds me of why I loved the SNES era of RPG's.Grandia Series: Incredibly memorable characters, and amazing battle system. This series made RPG battles fun again. The storyline is easy to follow, yet complex, and the dungeon crawls are very interactive.Wild ARMS series: Western-themed RPG anyone? This series has a cult following for good reason. The characters are all very fleshed out. The dungeons are all full of unique puzzles, for which you need not only specific characters, but specific tools to pass through. Top it off with a great post-apocalyptic story and you've got a good contender.I've left out all Squaresoft games, (with the obvious exceptions of Breath of Fire 1, and Super Mario RPG) as it seems unfair to compare with the same company. As well as, I'm sure, a few notable games that I'm completly forgetting. I've also not added any PC RPG's to the list, but there's some amazing contenders there. Still, I think I've gotten the point across. Final Fantasy is, and shall remain to be an amazing series, but that doesn't give an RPG lover the excuse to pass up any of these titles.PS: Please forgive any glaring spelling errors, it's late and I'm sure I've made a few.
