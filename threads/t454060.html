TTheFlameAlchemistFeb 05 2010, 02:37 AM
Dear rhonnos, You can watch movies on the nds if you convert them to dpg format. A good program to encode dpg videos is called batchdpg. Batchdpg can be found here: http://www.linfoxdomain.com/nintendo/ds/ under the "Essential Utilities" section. To encode the video you can use this guide here: http://www.modchipstore.com/support/index....kbarticleid=182You can also go to youtube and type in batch dpg tutorial and learn in a video. From there you need to go to linfoxdomain again, and go under the Great Homebrew section and download the latest moonshell, which is required to play the .dpg videos. Maybe Acekard has it built it, and you can watch videos directly, but I'm not sure.
