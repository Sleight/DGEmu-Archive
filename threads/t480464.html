Just an FYI for people who don't know about AdFly.

 

When you click on the link to download files, you will be shown an Ad.  Do *not* download anything that shows up on this page!  This page might also pop-up another window or show you "Your download is now ready" type links.  Ignore them.  Things downloaded from this page may be bad for your PC.

 

Just wait 5 seconds and a "Skip Ad" button will show up in the upper right corner of the page.  Click on it.

 

This new page (or this "Save file as..." prompt) is what you want.  It might be a MediaFire page (or some other "download wrapper" page) or it might be a direct file linked from dropbox or some other cloud storage.

 

Safe downloading!
