Hitman 2 Silent Assassin
Agent 47 is back and balder than ever in the 2rd instalment of the hitman series. This series begins when you go back to your church that you prayed in to seek repent for your sins of killing. But after you have talked to your priest he tells you, "you are a good man, I have seen you donate a lot of money to the church, you should continue...." as soon as you think it'll be a happy ending, that's where everything just begins. The priest is taken as a hostage, and now it is time for you to find the man that gave you a place to live. In this game you are taking to different parts of the world to find him and to take out some targets for some few extra thousand. This game has improved on every glitch and everything that ever went wrong in the first instalment . There are new weapons this time, you can wield a katana and become a ninja, carry an M60 the loudness weapon you can get your hands on, an SP12 the best shotgun you'll find or the "custom rifle "the rarest sniper rifle anyone in the world can get their hands on. The aim of the game is to be as silent as possible when taking your targets life and not to get suspicious around guards. The more silent you be the more chance you'll have of gaining special weapons like "silence ballers" ( dual silenced pistols) or an "M4" just naming a few of the unlockable you can obtain. You can do that or, grab your enemies guns and blast your way through the mission leaving only blood all over the wall cause you thought "paint the town red" was taken literally. This game requires not skill but strategy, in a certain mission (tracking Hayamato) you may just want to point a gun at his head and pull the trigger or think outside the square and put poison in his meal.
