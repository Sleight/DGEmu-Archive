Hhershey16Nov 08 2009, 11:57 AM
My favorite GBA game is Advanced Wars 2.This is one of deepest and best designed turned based strategy games from the same people who made fire emblem. This game, for starters, is simple to grasp. Attacking is simple, moving is simple, money is simple, ect. However the nuances of the gameplay are very complex. It is a game that is easy to play, but hard to master. also, unlike fire emblem, you can let your units die and still play well. This is very liberating. I feel much more like the commander of an army than the leader of a rogue band of vigilantes. The battle animations are fantastic, and lend the intensity found in RTS to an already fantastic turn based game. The characters are well thought out and likable, and the art style is stylized and charming, sometimes verging on beautiful. The campaign mode is long and difficult, and when you beat it you unlock an even more challenging one. The story is good and the scenarios are well thought out. The multiplayer support is incredible. Four people can play on one GBA, this is a unique feature, and this game becomes frantic with other people. Also the game has an awesome map editor that is detailed yet easy to use, and also various one shot missions that are very well done. I would recommend this as a download for anyone. You can't go wrong here.
