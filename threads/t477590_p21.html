KKenNov 11 2014, 05:29 PM

So, I finally played Final Fantasy Type-0.

 

The bad:

- Using different items in battle is tough because you need to open the menu but the game doesn't pause while you do that, so you keep getting hit if not outright killed for being a sitting duck while looking for that elixir...

- Some enemies do absurd amounts of damage in some attacks, probably because you have twelve party members and whoever gets knocked out is replaced by another, but it still makes playing as you favorite characters pretty difficult

- The last chapter comes out of nowhere and the story goes off the rails

- Very limited number of abilities in battle, probably as a result of the amount of buttons on the PSP

- Final mission's enemies levels are based on the average of your party members, my weakest was lv49, my strongest was 60, so it was hellish...

- Only active characters gain EXP, so if you stick with your favorite characters everyone else will be unusable most of the game

- Lots of items can't be obtained without an SE account or cheating...

 

The good:

- So much FF goodness: Four crystals, evil empire, overworld map, chocobo riding, neat cut scenes involving summons, Gilgamesh (+Battle on the Big Bridge), Magitek weaponry, Shinryu, optional airship, there's a Cid (and he's the bad guy)...

- Nice designs for the main characters

- Variety of movesets

- Alright story up until the last chapter

- Good graphics and music for the PSP

- Cheesy final boss battle (yes this is a plus despite everything else about the final chapter).

- New storyline content available in NG+

 

Not sure:

- One-dimensional main characters, I can even list the characters by their main traits easily: the main character-looking one, the sweet and gentle one, the classy long-winded one, the feisty one, the weird child-like one, the tough girl, the aloof one, the always calm one, the delinquent one, the ever-cheerful one, the responsible class rep, the mean looking but actually nice one
- Pretty edgy for a FF, Bahamut gets shot down by a Magitek airship right at the start of the game and you have to walk around his bleeding corpse in the first mission, a chocobo and its rider die there as well, most named characters die as the game goes on too...
 
Personally I enjoyed the game a bunch, I'll play it a second time for the extra story and happy ending, but I'm cheating the hell out of it because screw the rules I want costumes and all the spiffy equipment.
 
If the bad things about it are fixed for the PS4 version I'll definitely get a PS4 with the HD version of the game next year.
