Schwarz Cougar wrote on Apr 10 2015, 05:39 PM:

Weeeell, Dissidia had some really bad mechanics that makes the gameplay unattractive. 

For example, dodging gives you a ton of invul frames and blocking is essentially a parry except with duration much longer than your average parry and not much recovery.  This results in a defensive and campy game since you can stay safe while spamming harassment moves with dodge cancelling and make melee approaches risky.  Like when two mages play, it'll be a very loooong boring game of projectile spam since closing space is poor risk/reward in comparison.   

Another example is EX-Burst/Revenge which is just a terrible idea for a fighting game...imagine in other games with combo-breakers (like Burst), if bursting your opponent's combo gave you a free combo of your own with no proration or anything and could potentially win the match off it...its just bad.  It also makes the EX meter too game-changing since once the opponent fills their bar you're basically forced to play passive keepaway, there's no point even trying to combo them. 

 

However I'm assuming an arcade version will revamp the battle system and fix alot of these problems.  Gundam VS for example is very successful in arcades (most played arcade game IIRC) and has a similar system, just more tuned for competetive play...for example dodging in that game has a short duration and is meter limited.  Other than that I like the gameplay alot, it sure is flashy...
