AatomicknightAug 20 2007, 02:53 PM
Carriage returns are "characters" created by pressing the "Return" (Enter) key. Newlines are "characters" delimiting the start of a new line. Unfortunately, every OS interprets them differently, so that's where the issue comes up.But yeah, NFO files are just plaintext, which is why they "work." They just don't recognize standalone newlines as actual newlines.I don't think it's especially necessary (but then again, people are lacking in common sense), but I guess there's no real harm to add it.
