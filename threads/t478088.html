So I've been catching up because I keep hearing good things and I haven't read since the Death of Spider-Man.

I read Ultimate Fallout, and teared up for like 3 issues straight, because that crap is so sad.
I also read all of Ultimate Hawkeye and I'm on issue 9 of Ultimates, Spider-Man, and X-Men.

I'd say the only thing to really blow me away so far, was the scene where Reed Richards was standing in front of the Hulk and that was like the whole page. That was pretty awesome. Reed is a pretty spectacular villain in this universe. It's really really interesting. Reading all of these at once, I would say that it's kind of exasperating for no one to ever catch a break in this universe. Everything everywhere is always going to hell. No one ever kicks back for a laugh.

It is really awesome though. I'm definitely enjoying Ultimates. All the antagonists are legitimately interesting. Spider-Man is good too, though it kind of feels slow after Death of Spider-man and what the Ultimates and the X-Men are dealing with.


Anything else anyone is reading these days that's been really good? I keep hearing great things about the Hawkeye comic and Waid's Daredevil, but I don't keep up with either.
