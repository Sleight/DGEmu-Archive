== Cheese filled meatballs ==

- 1 1/2 lbs of minced meat

- 2/3 clove of garlic

- 1 onion

- 1/2 cup of brown bread crumbs

- 1 large egg

- dash of black pepper

- dash of salt

- Fresh basil, oregano, and rosemary (didn't really count this)

- Kraft Cracker Barrel Extra Old cheese

 

1. Chop the shit out of onion and garlic

2. Beat the shit out of the egg (but not too much)

3. Throw the meat in large mix bowl, dump the bread crumbs in, merge onion, garlic, egg, basil, oregano, and rosemary

4. Using your hands, have fun going into that mess and mixing it all up until more or less combined.

5. Take cheese up and cut a bunch of small cubes.

6. Take some meat in 1 hand, shove cheese cube in the meat, roll it up into ball, repeat.

Gave me 24 meatballs.

 

To prep, I'll be doing this:

- Take a nice baking tray, glaze with some olive oil
- Place the meat balls on top, cook until golden at bottom

- Use cooking pincers, turn them around, cook other side until golden

- Top that off on top of your favorite pasta and pour a nice tomato based meatless sauce.

 

 

== Le hamburger meat ==

- 1 1/2 lbs of minced meat

- 2/3 clove of garlic

- 1 onion

- 1/2 cup of brown bread crumbs

- 1/3 cup of milk

- Fresh basil, oregano, and rosemary (didn't really count this)

 

1. Chop the shit out of onion and garlic

2. Throw the meat in large mix bowl, dump the bread crumbs in, merge onion, garlic, milk, basil, oregano, and rosemary

3. Using your hands, have fun going into that mess and mixing it all up until more or less combined.

4. Take out plates, make hand sized meat balls, throw, flatten on a side, turn it, flatten again until both surface is smooth. Fix edges as needed.

Gave me 4 large hamburger meat patties

 

To prep, I'll be doing either one of these:

Traditional Hamburger

- Prep pan with cooking oil, heated.
- Cook the meat, season as necessary during cooking (Worcestershire sauce is a must)

- Serve in hamburger with preference toppings per person.

 

Hamburger Steak

- Prep pan with cooking oil, heated.

- Cook the meat, season with stronger steak spices

- Pour gravy sauceon top when serving

- Serve with mashed potatoes and steamed veggies.
