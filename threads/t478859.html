Please, note that the below information comes from gamefaqs and is not my own work.  I do hope that this answers your question though.

 

FORMAT
month/day-month/day robopon
fortune

12/21-1/20 Train
You love nature and small animals. You can love sincerely. Should you really be collecting Robopon, though? They're not exactly snuggly. Anyway, you also work slowly but surely toward your goal. Be patient.
1/21-2/19 Crysty
You're very attractive. I didn't need the stars to tell me that. Hee hee. People find you easy to get along with. You also like to plan your purchases. Don't spend all your money on videogames, though. Your Robopon will pay the price.
2/20-3/20 GigaPon
Hm, I'm not sure if this is good or bad, but you depend on others for everything. Have a personality of your own. Here, I'll help you-- No... do it for yourself. Maybe I shouldn't have read your fortune. You nedd to make your own life.
3/21-4/20 Ping
You're a natural leader. Because of your cool head, you should guide others. You make decisions on short notice based on instinct. But you're often right. Give others a chance as well, and beware number 3. Luck comes on a whirlwind.
4/21-5/20 Train
You are a grounded and realistic person. You don't set lofty goals. You also cherish your memories and keepsakes. Don't be too serious... You must relax and take it easy. You'll never forget this Hindo festival!
5/21-6/20 Crysty
You love freedom and live life on your own terms. You think that star-reading is ridiculous--hey! Anyway. Yoou like to fight for a good purpose. You should remember to consider others, though.
6/21-7/20 GigaPon
You're calm and laid back. You also have a keen eye for details. The downside is that you rarely express an opinion of your own. Soon you may use your natural talents to comfort others.
7/21-8/20 Ping
You are very honest and, in fact, can't lie. This is good and bad. You may find many friends, but not everyone is as trustworthy as you are. Try not to be gullible. By the way, star-reading is an exact science.
8/21-9-20 Train
You can work hard at a single task for hours. This can be a bad thing... You rarely try new things, and remain in your comfort zone. Get out a bit. Meditate on pyramid power and things will become clear. Remove your mask.
9/21-10/20 Crysty
You rush out and try new things as soon as possible. When shopping, you make many impulse purchases. You're also good at making a lot of friends. But you wish you had more close friendships. Some may consider you fickle.
10/21-11/20 GigaPon
Most people think you're a quiet person, right? Aha! That's not all true! You are lively, and skilled at competing. Interesting. You should branch out so that others understand the real you. You should go far, I predict.
11/21-12/20 Ping
You're very curios. You like to jump intothings and explore. But remember what curiosity did to that cat, and... that little monkey. But' you're not a cat or a monkey, so I guess you're perfectly safe, for now.
