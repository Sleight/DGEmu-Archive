DDhaosJan 10 2006, 07:20 PM
It really depends what you look for in a fighting game. Marvel vs Capcom has extremely exaggerated super moves and regular moves, sometimes it can be hard to keep up if facing off against an experienced player, and everything is very fast. Capcom vs SNK, is nicer to look at in terms of graphics, it functions more in the classic fighting game style where its 1 on 1 rather than being able to call in your team maters at any time and the moves aren't too exaggerated. For Marvel vs Capcom, its easy to be amazing with any character whereas with Capcom vs SNK each character takes mastery of combos, moves and chains to be good at the game. In my opinion, Capcom vs SNK is A LOT better for fighting game fans.
