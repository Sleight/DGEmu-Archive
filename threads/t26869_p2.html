Out of the three (not including Young Link) featured in SSBM (I don't know that many others), I would say Marth. Link isn't purely a swordsman, and Marth's Falchion is superior to Roy's Sword of Seals.If we're talking from the SSBM stand point, Marth is definitely the top swordsman. Both Marth and Roy's attack range exceed Link, but unlike Roy, Marth's sweetspot on his sword is at the tip, meaning it is in his best interest to keep some space between him and his opponent. Roy, on the other hand, will want to stay close to his opponent because his sword's sweetspot is at the center of the blade, reducing the range benefit of having a sword. Also, when Marth hits with his sweetspot, it deals more damage than when Roy hits with his sweetspot. Marth is also much faster than Roy or Link, being able to do more than one attack in one short hop (ex. SHDF). Marth also falls slower than Roy and Link, making him harder to combo than Roy and Link. While Link wins out in terms of recovery, Roy is the loser because he cannot cover very much vertical distance with his up-B, coupled with his fast fall speed.It's reasons like this that put Marth in high-tier compared to Roy and Link who are in the low to bottom tiers.
