Make sure that all posts are relevant to the topic. Do not spam this topic or expect a warn. If you are not asking a question regarding the contest or submitting an entry, then don't post!If you have questions please PM me.[center]----------------------------------------------------------[/center]All right, so in case you didn't know, we're talking about these:[center]----[/center]Types of Entries Allowed-DG themed entries (entries that use the Dragon Logo (link is below) in some way-Video game themed - These entries must use a video game as a basis for the badge design (such as Mega Man, Mario, Sonic, etc.) The badges must reflect the game's natural hierarchy (weather it be bosses from strongest to weakest, or weakest to most powerful items). [Example- the Support Staff Badge might have a Goomba on it while the Admin badge would have Bowser]Entry Requirements-Width dimensions: 120 to 170 pixels- Height dimensions: 20 to 40 pixels- The DGEmu Dragon Logo (click to see it) must be used in entries that do not have a video game theme.- Make sure your entry is either in PNG or GIF format (PNG is preferable)- The entry may not be ripped off of someone else or from another forum.- Make sure that you submit images for all of the following staff positions: Administrator, Sentinel, Super Moderator (or Super Mod), Moderator, and Support StaffIf any of the above requirements are not met, you will most likely be disqualified.How to submit your entryYou must submit your entry by posting in this thread. That way we don't need to worry about whether the appropriate staff members see it or not. You are always welcome to edit your post if you update your entries. You may also edit your post if your entry does not fit the qualifications if the contest has not ended already. It would be easier for everyone if you use the img tags to show us all of your images in the post, like this:However, we are not going to complain too much if you just put the image's url in the post.How to WinUnlike some previous graphics contests, there will be no poll for members to vote in. A panel of staff members will decide which ones should be considered and which set will be used, if any. Of course, the staff reserves the right not to use any of the submissions. If your set is used, then you win the grand prize. If your set is considered, it is also eligible for a secondary prize.PrizesWinner: 5000 GP + 200 dedicated DP of choice; if you do not want GP/DP, alternative prizes may also be discussed (extra PM storage space and things of the like)Secondary prizes: anywhere from 500 to 1500 GP may be given to anyone else being considered.The submission deadline is September 25th.Good luck, everyone! We look forward to your entries. Have fun! :blink: Kudos to Hadel for writing all this stuff before ;o
