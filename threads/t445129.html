First off, do not create a bunch of threads asking questions. You already had a thread asking about a CFW. That is why I took GPs.Now, onto business. You need to be a little more patient.You have a white PSP PHAT on 4.05 OFW. You'll need either A.) A pandora battery or B. ChickHEN.A.) Pandora Battery: You can do this two ways. You can hardmod the battery as described in the pinned thread in this section. It involves opening the battery and soldering. So that is at your own risk. Or you can softmod one using a PSP that is already CFW enabled...but you said you don't know anyone that has one, so that is probably out. You can also buy what is called a tool battery from certain websites. (I bought mine from divineo).B.) You can use the Homebrew Enabler (or HEN) called ChickHEN. It takes a PSP on an OFW, and enables you to run homebrew and emulators. Since you can do this, you can load the CFW installer. So I suggest taking this path.I suggest using this on your PSP to install the chickHEN:http://pspcustomfirmware.com/hen/easy-inst...-units-only.htmThen you can use Hellcat's recovery flasher to install a CFW to your PSP:http://dl.qj.net/Recovery-Flasher-v1.60-PS...27143/catid/114
