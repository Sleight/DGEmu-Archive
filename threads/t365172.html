Yes, this was partially prompted by a S&S thread from an hour or two ago, but I've actually been thinking that the advertising rule/situation is pretty odd ever since I've been on the staff. We've certainly been unclear about who can advertise and who can't, even though the rule for it seemed pretty clear (we ended up making that pinned thread in staff and even then some members have had their sigs wrongfully removed). Therefore, I have some proposals to make regarding improvement for this so that moderators hardly have to do anything and all permissions will truly be controlled only by sentinels + admins:Change the rule slightly. Abolish advertising in signatures entirely and limit the ads to a single forum (possibly in Site Resources). Therefore, mods can remove any sigs which have ads without even having to second-guess.To be able to make new threads in the advertising forum (or post in it at all for that matter, but I don't see the point in allowing replies at all), we can keep the same essential guidelines: 1000 posts (or maybe drop it a bit to something else) and admin/sent approval.This would be set up by having a special permission mask applied to a secondary group which sents/admins can give to members they approve of advertising. Admins who volunteer to be in charge of advertising approval can have their names listed in the "forum rules" slot much like what is currently done with the Other Rom Sites section.Basic advertising guidelines can be added to a pinned thread in this section so that we can make sure no one is allowed to post pyramid schemes, porn sites, ROM sites, and other stuff like that. Mods can remove threads which violate these guidelines.This can nearly guarantee that people won't need to see ads unless they want to (by browsing the appropriate forum) and it will basically wipe out any confusion by moderators who don't bother checking the pinned thread in staff. Who's on board with a change like this?EDIT: one small point invoked from an exchange with another member is that Power Members essentially "sponsor" the site in a way, so if we wanted, we could also add a part to the rules that says they are allowed to advertise via signature if they wish (and moderators can tell them apart by their member group, of course).

DDJ Night Force 9Dec 11 2007, 03:44 PM
I really don't agree with "personal sites" where no benefit is present as a form of advertising at all. I think that should be fully allowed as long as there are no forums on that site. The reason I say this is because there is actually a field in everyone's profile that even states "personal site" so by considering it a form of advertising is like forbidding members to fill out something in their own profile. Now the reason why I say "no forums" in personal sites is because it's a security risk.Scenerio:1. There's a member who likes to use the same password in all web forums (and I'm sure many members do)2. Someone opens a web board that doesn't encrypt passwords and advertises it in their profile3. Some members see this and visit the site and join the forums4. Member who owns the site looks up their password in their database5. Member who owns the site now hijacks his members' dgemu accounts.And yes, this can happen and has been brought up before.So in summary, I say personal sites without forums (or anything else that requires user authentication) or any sort of E-Commerce or other forms of personal/financial gain should be fully allowed. Anything else (as depicted through #1-5in Bish's post) requires permission so that a sentinel or admin can investigate the site first.
