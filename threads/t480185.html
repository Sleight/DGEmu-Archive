I'm sorry that I have to bust the bubble, but if we're going to talk about DooM WADs, you've just walked into territory I'm very familiar with.

 

Let's start with something I want to point out here, and I think this deserves to be in big letters:
 

 

DOOM IS NOW ALMOST 22

 

Now, let's get back to other talk about some awesome WADs.  While I'm not going to disagree about the quality of Brutal Doom, there are a few others which I think are just a real cut above.  There's a lot to be said for things that shake up Doom without messing it up; and the wads Doom the Way id Did (and the creatively-named sequel, Doom 2 the Way id Did) definitely display that.  You can find info here: http://doomwiki.org/wiki/Doom_the_Way_id_Did

 

I think people have talked about the Aliens TC and Batman TC quite a bit; plus there's the Doom64 TC and Doom64 EX - both of which are awesome if you like D64 - hosted, btw, here on DGEmu.  I actually have to suggest EX on this one for all the benefits it provides over emulating 64.

 

FreeDoom isn't exactly a "Mod" or a WAD, but it is just as important to everyone who cares about Doom and in today's world.  http://doomwiki.org/wiki/FreeDoom.  While there's any number of places to download the original WADs, they are still under copyright.  The overall goal of the project is pretty simple: create an open-source IWAD (I will not get into IWAD vs. PWAD here, leave that to an engine thread).  They're still working like madmen on it, and I appreciate that effort.  I think it's important to comment on.

 

But my hands-down favorite WADpack yet has to be Action Doom 2: Urban Brawl.  A really nice 'cel-shaded' style to the game and the switch to 'beat-em-up' gameplay really interested me and I just love the way the community can turn out such great things.

 

I can't help but suggest a visit to last year's Cacowards - and a hope that 2014 maintains the tradition: http://www.doomworld.com/20years/index.php
