Karnov' timestamp='1344643288' post='5030029 wrote:

Also, any tips on handling Elizabeth with Kanji? The amtchup seems beyond retarded.

Its actually pretty even, though it will certainly look one-sided. If Liz keeps you out and outspaces Kanji, she wins in 2-3 confirms. If Kanji gets in and gets his throw mixup going...he wins in 2-3 guesses. So basically, block alot until you get in.

Liz is pretty much always going to be either spamming disruptor (block it, she wastes meter) or jumping and spacing with j.B and Kanji actually deals with the matchup pretty well because he has very high reward anti-airs, 236A/B, 236SB, 2B etc
Also one thing I noticed is that you can use Kanji's R-action to clash with Thanatos's 2C and not get CH'd by it...which means that it basically nullifies the 5D/2C mixup. Though I guess Liz can then stagger 2C, but then you can poke Thanatos away during the end of 5CC. So trying using R-action after blocking her 5CC =)

Once you do get momentum, Liz falls apart so fast its pretty funny...she will probably be mashing on R-action (I know I do) so bait it once and she's dead
