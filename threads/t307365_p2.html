Dogs detect sounds as low as the 16 to 20 Hz frequency range (compared to 20 to 70 Hz for humans) and above 45 kHz[27] (compared to 13 to 20 kHz for humans),[24] and in addition have a degree of ear mobility that helps them to rapidly pinpoint the exact location of a sound.
