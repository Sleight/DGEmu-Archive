Zoids Legacy is the first GBA game that is translated into English, since all versions of these franchises have only been published in the original language, Japanese.For fans of this series has been a wonder in a language we can understand more easily. Most only had the Japanese version, like me, we are hard to understand many things in the game, because it's RPG style, but despite that we struggled to understand and move on. Thanks to the English version can now understand the true history of the game and enjoy even more battles and adventures of Zeru and all the characters of the series and games of Zoids world.The game involves us in as Zeru strives to help at all costs to their friends, and obviously to do to get stronger, and that's where we help. The graphics are very good, excellent effects of attacks like the animations. It is noted that much improved from "Zoids Saga", although the battle system is the same, finding new Zoids and new weapons that can be attached gives us an experience like to be a real Zoids pilot.The truth is that this was my first Zoids game, of course in the Japanese version, and since I'm a big fan of the Zoids series, I was so excited that I did not mind the language that was the game to the point of finalizing with my own notes to the game.I can only say that this is an excellent game of GameboyAdvance and I'm glad that have taken the time to translate it into English.I hope all who play it carried with good memories of the game.Finally I apologize if my English is confused or does not make much sense, that is because I'm Hispanic, not much language domain, but I wanted to make a post that could be understood by visitors to the site.Thank you very much. o.O
