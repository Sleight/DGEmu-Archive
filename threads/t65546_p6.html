I definitely agree with this.  In practically any game, it's the one I most often have equipped.  Although in most cases, the Hookshot just stuns enemies rather than killing them.  This is especially useful in A Link to the Past, since those darn soldiers move around and have their sword/spear/etc. in the way.  I find the Boomerang rather useless in that game, since the Hookshot can also stun enemies and retrieve items, but can also cross gaps.  It's also very useful for killing Skulltulas in Ocarina of Time, and isn't a limited use like the bow is (with the latter I'm worried about running out of arrows).  The one game where I find the bow superior to the hookshot is A Link Between Worlds, since you essentially get unlimited arrows in that game (it runs off the same energy meter that all other items do, hookshot included).  Plus when upgraded, it shoots out three arrows at different directions, which is pretty neat.
