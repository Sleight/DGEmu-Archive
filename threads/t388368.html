DGEmu’s Design DegreeWelcome to DGEmu’s Design Degree, a design competition. This competition is purely focused on the applicable uses of graphic design. This means that you probably will not get to make signatures. :laugh: Instead, the challenges may consist of creating a logo for a company, or designing a movie poster. This requires a creative mind, an eye for design, and a set of skills very different than what is used for signature making. Without further ado, here are the contest details:Rounds:You will be given a new challenge roughly every week. You must complete the assignment by the due-date. In addition, you must also fulfill all of the requirements and/or restrictions given that round. In that post, I will provide the type of graphic you will be creating, the requirements and what you will be marked on, a few tips and examples, the submission format, and how many people will be eliminated that round.Elimination:At the end of the round, the graphics will all be posted within one post. You may change your graphic anytime, until all the graphics have been posted within that one big post. The judges will give a detailed critique for each graphic. Each round, a certain number (depending on the number of participants) of people will be eliminated. The judges will pick the bottom few entries of that round. The people who make the most “bottom” lists from the judges will be eliminated.Prizes:First Place: Will receive 2000GPs, and a first place banner.Runner Up: Will receive 500GPs, and a second place banner.Prizes are heavily dependent on how many people participate in this contest. Prizes definitely would have a high chance of bumping up if many people join and participate actively. Rules:1. Posting a graphic that you have not made is absolutely not tolerated. You will be disqualified from this competition and a moderator will be contacted. Also, you may not post Collaborations (works between more than one person), the work you post must be entirely yours.2. You may not use drawings/photographs/images from deviantArt unless you have explicit permission from the artist. OR it is clearly labeled as a "Stock" image, or a resource. It is recommended that you find images from free stock websites. 3. You must include the main stock images you used for your entries.4. Please be respectful of all other contestants and judges.5. If you do not complete your assignment on time, you will be eliminated.6. If you are going to be away for any period of time, let me know in advance so I can give you your assignment beforehand.7. If you have a problem with a judge(s)’s ratings, Please take it up privately through PM.8. Challenge One- Create an Album CoverYou have been asked by a record company to create an album cover for one of their new CDs. The company has given you several requirements:1. The album should be one of a released, in-production, or planned album. It must be one of an existing artist, and you must copy their track listings exactly. 2. The album must look professional.3. The album look must reflect the style or genre of the artist or album itself. 4. The album cover must be of a certain proportion (explained below).You are allowed to work with a stock image of the artist, or you may create a stockless album cover. Whatever you feel works the best. The proportions of the image should be as follows:The image should be submitted in the shape of a long rectangle, encompassing both the front and back covers, with an area of thin width in the middle to account for the thickness of the CD case. The front and back covers may be slightly longer in width, to make it look rectangular. Your submission should be at least 400 pixels in height.An example of how it should look like can be found by clicking Here. This is also a good reference for where other things on the album could go. The front cover should be on the right, and the back cover should be on the left. Tips- The album name should be clearly legible. As with the album artist, track listings, and any other information you feel should be important.- While you are not limited to what resources you may use, please try to make it look somewhat professional.- If you do not meet a few minor requirements for this assignment, it will reflect as part of your review as functionality will play a big part in this competition.- For track listings and albums, a good site for information is "www.allmusic.com". - It is encouraged to simulate all the information that will appear on a real-life album.
