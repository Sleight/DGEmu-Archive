Thanks for all the support fellas, it's hugely appreciated :( .I just got back from getting a bone-marrow biopsy - you really want to avoid this procedure at all costs. Basically, they use a cork-screw type thing to create a hole in the pelvic bone near the spine. Then, they drain off some of the fluid (painful); then they take a core of the marrow itself. That part hurt like a mother-****er - the same intensity of pain as when you put your back out, if anyone knows what that's like. I had to tell them to stop and give me some sedative, after which I was okay, but now that the sedative and local-anaesthetic has worn off, it feels like someone "cracked my buttocks with a hammer", to quote Sideshow Mel.So, yeah... just an update. Thanks again everybody.EDIT (Jan 31):I've had round 2 of the chemo, and things are looking pretty good at this point. Test results have shown that the cancer is localized to the neck and hasn't spread, and they've also shown that radio-therapy will be effective; (meaning that I'll only need 2 more sessions of chemo, and then probably 2 doses of radio). The chemo hasn't hit me nearly as hard as the stories I've heard lead me to believe it would - it's still pretty draining physically, but at least I haven't had the nausea which is the part I was most worried about. Anyhow, I'll give this dose a couple more days to get out of my system, and then I'll be active again.EDIT (Feb 16):Okay, so you've probably noticed that I've been MIA for the last couple of weeks. That means that sitting over a keyboard for any length of time is out of the question. Apart from that, I got a little burnt out after my last period of activity (averaging 50 or 60 mod actions a day or some ungodly number for a few days), and it was doing my head in, so I permitted myself some down-time.
