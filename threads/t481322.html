I don't know if there's a lot of people still around that knew him here on DG, it's been a while since he last visited. I'll remember Ryan as an enthousiastic and fun guy who loved to get into dumb arguments with dumb people about dumb shit. He loved beer just as much as I do. I didn't dig his music, it wasn't my style, but I have to admit he was a good musician.

It's very sad to see a young, talented, nice human being like this fade away, and it really got me thinking about how all these anonymous people we interact with over the Internet are all real people and not personalitybots spamming their whatever. It made me think about the conversations I'd had with Ryan, the real human behind the forum account, the laughs we'd had and the forum hijinks that happened when we were both still active members. It made me think about how all of that was virtual, and that despite that, in a way, our connection was real, and that all of these Internet friends we have can actually be really valuable. It made me think that this really felt like I lost someone I'd known.

His name was Ryanfromtheshire, Ryan Lee Hansson, he loved to play the banjo, read Tolkien, play some vidja on his PSP, drink beer and he just isn't here any more. Even though I never met him, I'm incredibly saddened by his passing, and I feel like I lost a friend. An internet friend, but that doesn't make it any less real.

Sleep tight Ryan.
