Never use a defragmenting tool on flash media- all the rewriting the tool does will shorten the card's lifespan. The best way to "defragment" your microSD is to copy the contents to HD, format the card and then copy the contents back all at once. They'll be loaded onto the card without fragmenting. It's only when you delete and add new files to the card that they may become fragmented.I'd recommend formatting as FAT16, 64k cluster size if games are freezing on you. There could be some speed problems with your microSD card (are you sure it's actually the Japanese model?), and the fat16/64k format may help due to lower overhead. Normally Windows' formatter can't choose the cluster size, but there's a computer management tool in the control panel under administrative tools that will let you do this via a disk management option.A Q-tip very slightly dampened with rubbing alcohol should do for cleaning the contacts. Be careful not to touch the circuit board above the pins, and let the alcohol evaporate completely before attempting to use the card.
