This is a guide for the ewin 2 flash cart, i did not make this guide all rights go to whoever made it.Official Site.http://www.ewinflash.cn/(warning in chinese)You will need a ewin2 flash cart, a sd card (mini, micro etc...), a sd card reader.1. First format your sd card to Fat (warning by default it is set to fat32 which is not supported)2.download the software. (Program to remain on your pc to transfer files over)http://darkforce.gbatemp.net/ewin2/EWinTools(15.09.06).zipdownload the firmware (goes on micro sd card)http://darkforce.gbatemp.net/ewin2/fw_release0930-2.raralternate download link.http://www.ds-scene.net/sub_section/downlo...amp;showfiles=1unzip the software onto your computer and unzip the firmware onto the sd card itself.3. now plug your sd card into the ewin cart and plug it into your ds, and turn it on while Pressing 'L', 'R' and 'Up' simultaneously it will update the firmware on the cart.4.After updating the firmware, you need to remove the sd card from the cart, and plug into your computer, then Load up the Ewin loader program, and choose a output directory (the output directory is your sd card)EWinTools also has a variety of skins you can choose from. They are all look very professional and there's everything from iTunes to Vista:On the left hand side is a autoupdater, even if you version of the loader is outdated you can simply update, click on it first.now add the roms you wish to your cart and start her up, you can even click and drag roms into your cart though this program.happy gaming.......................................................................................................Extra Knowledge.If you press 'R' the menu switches to a more detailed viewing mode and brings up information on each game such as the title, display icon, internal name and game code, and rom size.Game saves are saved onto SRAM and are automatically backed up to the SD card next time you boot a game.
