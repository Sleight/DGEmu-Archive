I think building a custom computer for your own home/gaming use is great. I don't think it's arrogant to list your specs though - in my opinion, half the reason why you would build your own computer is because you actually care about what goes into it. In this case, I think that the builder can be rightly proud of what they have in their box. Granted, it wouldn't be very nice to flaunt it and go and trash talk other people's computers. With the case lights and fancy stuff and all, that's just the other half of building a custom computer. And I think it's fine to be proud of your own custom case/design as well. But in my opinion, I have to admit that I think that many custom builders are more concerned with what goes into their computer, rather than how it looks on the outside. Like for me personally, my computer is custom built, but it uses a really ugly branded case that I have from the computer prior to building my custom one. For me, it was a matter of saving money. I had thought about getting a nicer case... but in the end, I deemed it an unnecessary expenditure, since the box would always be sitting under my desk, never to see the light of day. When it comes to laptops, I begin to care more about visual appeal, since I will be using it out and about (although, of course, excessive and fancy lighting is a little too much for me on a laptop). But then when it comes to laptops for me, building a custom laptop is not yet a very appropriate route... for me personally, small size and functionality is most important in a laptop. Not necessarily gaming power. So although I could buy a bare-bone laptop and customize the internal components to my liking, it wouldn't be nearly as small, portable and functional as going with a stock model would have allowed for my purposes. Anyway... those are my own thoughts and opinions. YMMV and I know everyone has a different view of this type of stuff, which, is perfectly fine, because, it's your computer and if you love it, then that's all that really matters..!
