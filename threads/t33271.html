I'm new to the site here (and loving the concept... very spiffy idea, admins!), and couldn't resist throwing in my two cents. Hopefully this doesn't get me flamed too bad :D ....Doom 3's the winner.I don't know how much technical knowledge the board members here have (seeing as it's a ROM site, I'm assuming you're comprised of more gamers than coders/artists/etc), so I won't fault anyone for picking Half-Life 2 :P . It does look spiffy to an uncaring eye. But, as a "programmer" myself, I view the whole graphics thing in a totally different light.While Half-Life 2 had considerably more detailed textures (and believable character animations), that's about all it has. Really, Doom's biggest "fault" is that it does the underlying shadowing/lighting/etc technology beautifully, at the expense of what most kiddies these days consider to be "visuals" (e.g. 2048x2048 photo-scanned textures :drool: ).Yeah, Half-Life 2 does initially look better on the surface... but when you realize that:- There are no mirrors (...stencil buffering has been around for ages, Valve...)- Most surfaces don't even have bumps, and the oldschool style relative-positioned environment map is used incorrectly almost everywhere (shiney concrete, anyone?)- It still uses precompiled lightmaps- BSPs still need compiling (no on-the-fly vis management)- The material shader implementation is not very intuitive...among a host of other things, it starts to look a lot less beautiful :o .Oh, and hi, by the way :o .
