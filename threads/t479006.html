This is my analysis of Infinite's ending... gathered from my own understanding of the ending, as well as other analyses. 
Obviously, there are many different dimensions and realities that exist, as proven by the ending.This is how I believe everything ties together.
 
Bear in mind that in all realities, Booker is a veteran who fought at Wounded Knee. Sometime soon after (though the date isn't specifically mentioned, this is presumably a good amount of time after Wounded Knee, which took place on December 29th, 1890), he was offered the chance for redemption, both for the murders, and the gambling/addiction afterwards by means of baptism. This is the fork in the road, so to speak, that splits the timeline.
 
Reality A - Part One: Booker becomes Comstock. 
Booker, having accumulated massive amounts of debt after a period of frequent gambling and alcohol troubles, decides to accept the baptism offered to him. This is how he becomes involved with religion, and the idea of grace and forgiveness, renaming himself as Zachary Comstock.
 
He does not have a child, but somehow, somewhere, along the way, he becomes the mayor/governor of Columbia (which at this time, is still not afloat). It is around this time when he meets Lutece, who possesses the ability to peek into the different realities and at different times, and shares with him the demonstrations (which is how he is able to make claims of the future, all of course, under the title of prophet). She soon discovers how to make atoms float (and how to hop dimensions, which ties in later), and before you know it, Comstock's vision of a heaven-like Columbia is born. 
 
As time goes on, Comstock's religious influence spreads - however, a problem arises. Exposure to Lutece's tests leaves him unable to produce children, and therefore, an heir to his throne. Hoping to maintain his image, and his legacy, Comstock works together with the Luteces to take the child of Reality B's Booker DeWitt. 
 
Reality B -  Booker remains.
Booker, still having accumulated massive amounts of debt and an addiction to alcohol (again, due to PTSD), is given the opportunity to be born again in Christ. However, in this reality, he declines the baptism - because he believes that dunking his head underwater won't resolve his sins in anyway. So, he leaves. A couple of years after, he becomes father to Anna DeWitt (who's mother is unknown - how and if she leaves is not said, though, it is presumable that she leaves Booker alone with Anna). Still, though, he is plagued by an immense amount of debt, and is unsure how to solve for it.
It is then when he is given an offer - "Give us the girl, and wipe away the debt."
 
An offer to which he accepts. He gives Anna to Comstock (not just in this reality, but in every reality that he denied the baptism). He lives on, though not muchis known past here. It is revealed that he brands AD on his hand (Anna DeWitt), but, that's it. Most likely, he lives in depression for having given away his only child. 
 
Reality A - Part Two - Comstock's hopes are fulfilled.
Comstock, now having a child of his own (sort of), begins to propagandize by claiming that she was a Miracle Child, grown in the womb of Lady Comstock in just seven days. Naturally, the people, all of whom are nitwits, believe this... however, Lady Comstock obviously does not. She believes that he is a cheat, and hates him for it - but if she were to tell the truth, it wouldn't be good for his reputation. 
 
So, Comstock has her assassinated, to ensure that nobody knows the truth - he then blames this on Fitzroy, the leader of the VOX. Everybody accepts this, and time moves on. 
 
Comstock, preparing for the event in which Booker hops dimensions, spreads word of a "false sheperd", and makes sure to prepare for the event. When he discovers that Anna, now called Elizabeth, can alter dimensions, he syphons her powers, again with the help of the Lutece twins.
 
Time goes on. If there were no interference, then, Elizabeth would have succeeded Comstock, and destroyed Earth (as we saw). However, plagued by guilt, the Lutece twins aim to fix the timeline, and go off once more to Booker DeWitt (though, in which reality, I do not know. To me, it doesn't make sense for it to be Reality B's Booker, since his monetary debt has been repaid. I wouldn't trust the same guy twice after what he did. This is why I believe it is a third reality.) 
 
Reality C - Bioshock Infinite Timeline
Bring us the girl, and wipe away the debt.
 
This is the offer. And it is given to Reality C's Booker before Anna is born (remember that, at the credits, he said there was no baby). To him, this is just a job. He doesn't know that Elizabeth is his daughter.  (I won't waste time by going through the plot of Infinite, because, most likely if you're reading this, you already know what happens.)
 
In this reality, Booker physically kills Comstock - however, only in one reality does he do so. There are infinitely many realities that have Comstock in them. The only way to truly kill Comstock is to kill him before he is born. 
And this is why Elizabeth brings Booker to the original reality where he became Comstock. To kill him in the cradle. To tear out the root. To fix things. By killing him here, she erases all realities where Comstock exists (which is why the many different versions of Elizabeth are erased). All that is left now is the reality where Booker denies baptism... and remains himself, to live his life with his daughter, Anna.)
 
This is the best I could come up with... and I know I'm not very good at explaining things, but.
