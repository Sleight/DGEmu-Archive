I posted a topic in the Off Topic section (Do you enjoy spmming topic after topic?) and mentioned this briefly, but to summarize - this board has a lot of meaningless posts. Meaningless as in numerous one word/two word comments that are spammed in multiple topics just to get one's daily download points or some other crap. I know there's a huge volume of users on here, but from what I've seen the forums have a very small handful of threads that are actually full of thoughtful discussion. So the change in the GP system would put more emphasis on if you make your posts a little lengthier, you get rewarded better. For example, the length of your comment/post times 2 or something of the sort. Starting a topic could net more points, also if you take the length into account. Adding reviews to games or supplying screenshots to roms is another possibility. Of course, it's not to say that every post needs to be a lengthy response. You could just post your one liner and you get the standard points. The other modification that would have to be done would be to raise the prices of the roms just a little bit to counter the inflation in point value. This would make the forum 1) More informational, with better quality posts and responses2) Lessen traffic that mods have to monitor - I assume that every time a post says POINTS HAVE BEEN DEDUCTED FOR THIS POST a mod had gone and personally deducted points from that person. Less of these posts - less times one has to deduct. 3) Weed out the people that just spam the forums just to get GP to get ROMs. They're basically just raping your bandwidth, and for all the work you've done to set up a token system - you're not getting much out of it (in the form of discussion or otherwise). I don't think you would suffer from lack of users simply because the ROMs you have here are plentiful, organized, updated and linked directly. I use torrents but I would prefer a direct download for one ROM over torrents right now. If anything, your user base will increase because the quality of your site is top notch, ROM-wise and forum-wise.
