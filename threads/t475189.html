KKirie MotobaFeb 09 2015, 08:17 PM

Well, to quickly explain on a GoodSet:

There are tons of duplicate files with bad dumps / over dumps.

 

Using 7-Zip has a huge advantage over any other archiving format: Rom files that share very similar information will be reduced to near 0 bytes in size when compressed with their comrades in 7-Zip. This effectively halves the size of full sets.

Then, people tend to package the whole set into larger split archives, such as RAR or ZIP at this point, just to let users click on less links.

 

Last time, to get a full working GoodSet on my computer, I downloaded something similar to this, extracted everything to unveil 7-Zip archives that I can't use with emulators, so I batch extracted that also to give me uncompressed ROMs. At this point, to save space, I needed to run another batch to recompress all ROMs back into seperate zip files. This is all for the sake of saving download bandwidth.
