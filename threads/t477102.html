KKula DiamondSep 12 2012, 07:53 PM
If you can't react fast enough or trying to block a string you didn't see before, block it all while standing, only few characters can do high damage combos starting from a fast low attack, usually you will just take one low hit and that's it, on the other hand, if you try to block low and end up getting hit with a mid attack or a launcher you will eat tones of damage.

Some characters like Kuma/Eddy have low(grounded!) stances in which they can do fast all low strings with a lot of damage, those are very problematic if you're a beginner, but characters can't block while in a special stance so attack them out of it the moment you see an opening.

You shouldn't block all day and wait for the opponent to finish his string to counter as most strings are relatively safe, what you want to do while under pressure is backing up and creating space for yourself to start your offence. Unlike most games, back dashing is safe in Tekken and very useful to get out of pressure, if you keep holding back after the back dash you will block any mid/high attack even if you're still in the middle of the back dash animation, so abuse it.

KKula DiamondSep 13 2012, 09:48 AM
Lili's mini beginners guid.

DF = Down Forward, UB = Up Back, LP = Left Punch RP = Right Kick and so on...

For close range Pokes:

LP>HP Her fastest attack, safe on block but both hits are high so they will wiff on crouching opponents, also don't do the follow up string(RK) as it can be blocked even if LP>HP hits and will leave you at a disadvantage. You can follow it up with LK for a low attack, but it will leave you on a disadvantage even on hit so don't go for it unless you need that last hit to take the round. So just do LP>HP then start your mix ups.

DF+LP ~Fast, and has more range that it appear to have, safe on block and it hit mid so there's no crouching under it, good to start your offence with. When blocked mix up between grabs or side step then launcher.

F+RP>LK fast and has a lot of reach, good for a start of the round offence and wiff punishes, but leave you on a disadvantage if blocked.


The ideal space between Lili and the opponent is "start of the round space + 1 back dash(~3 back dashes away from the opponent)", this is the distance you want to hover around most of the time as it gives Lili the space she need to function, from here on:

F+RK if you catch the other player trying to close in on you or if he wiffs something by mistake this move is very good to punish, it's very fast for a move with this much reach, on block it leave you on a disadvantage but away from the other player so it's hard to punish.

QCF>LK+RK this move is Lili's signature move and the reason why Lili is so good at this distance. It's fast, can close a lot of distance if you delay LK+RK a little after QCF, does nice damage, but most importantly it leave you at an advantage if blocked, so much that the other player's only option is to focus and react to(guess) your next mix up:
- LP>RP or F+RP>LK and the opponent wont even have time to side step.
- F+Grab(slower than normal grab but catches side steps), opponent will have to grab tech or crouch.
- Launcher(UF+LK or DF+RP) if your opponent is crouching.
- Low attack
etc...

Abuse this move, it's only disadvantage is that it hits high so it wiff on crouching characters, so once your opponent learn to crouch under it, here's what to do:

Starting from the distance we discussed earlier, do a QCF, Lili will do her special dash(Dew Glide) and look at the opponent, if he's standing go for LK+RK normally, but if he start to crouch follow the QCF with LP>RP this does tones of damage and a knock down.

Lili's biggest weakness is her low attacks, she can only deal high damage from lows on counter hit and that move is very risky... will continue after lunch...

Ok time to learn some stuff.

Bound Moves
During juggles, you can hit your opponent with a bound move to make him hit the ground and bounce upward allowing you few more hits. Basically they are juggles extenders.
Bound moves have green box in front of them in the command list.
Lili's F+LP+HP and DF+LK are bound moves. DF+LK does more damage but F+LP+HP is faster.
DF+LK is a bet slow, so to make it connect during a juggle hit them with LP then do DF+LK.

An easy example of a bound combo is:
Launcher >>> LP,RP string twice >>> bound >>> take a step forward then LK,RP,LK string
DF+RP,LP,RP,LP,RP,F+LP+HP,F,F,LK,RP,LK

Tag Assault
-Press the tag button right after landing a bound attack and your partner will jump in to help with the combo and you will be controlling him.
-Your partner will leave after preforming one attack, if you do a string he will leave after you complete the string and control will go back to your original character.
-The character you control during tag assault is the one with the whit aura around him/her.
-If the string end up with the character in a special stance, you get to do another one.
-If the string preformed by the incoming character takes too long, your original character will leave and you will continue controlling the incoming character, this will also happen if you don't attack with the incoming character.
-Incoming character will lose all his red health.
-The moment your incoming character attack the opponent, your opponent's partner Netsu(that red aura thing, does more damage while it's active) will activate.
-You can keep holding the tag button and your partener will automatically preform a pre-set move/string.

If Lili is the partner, you can do F+LK+HK, LK, HK it does nice damage and easy to follow up if you have an attack with long reach when Lili leave.

Normal Tag Combos
-Just press tag after a launcher, the character will leave and your partner will come to continue the combo.
-Doesn't work with all launchers, just the ones with a red box in front of them in the command list(D+LK+HK for Lili).
-If you preform a normal tag combo, your opponent lose all his red health.

Tag Grab
-RP+TAG, preform a tag team move, and the partner will stay in after it.
-Opponent lose all red health.
