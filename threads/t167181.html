Ok, to clarify a couple things before I start. Capcom DISSOLVED clover. That means everyone has their jobs, they're just back in the main capcom company. Anyway, Okami is awesome. It's gameplay rivals zelda, only instead of a million and one items you, have your trusty celestial brush, which makes it so you don't need a cluttered up inventory screen. There are a plethora of sidequests to do to earn praise, money, and other things, which super increase the replay value. It's also PACKED full of things to do normally. The artwork is amazing, and the story is quite incredible. The characters are well realized, quirky, and unique. The gameplay is very entertaining too. Most of the fun in Okami is actually figuring out how to use what you learn, because they don't hold your hand like they do in most games (like zelda), and the usefulness of the brush techniques is amazingly diverse. This should be a game every ps2 owner should have if they're not a closed minded gamer. It's just that good
