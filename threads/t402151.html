HHyou_FushichiouORIGINAL POSTMay 24 2008, 04:02 PM
Yes, these are a few suggestions I thought of, the reply to all of which are either “Who has the time?” or “Who effing cares?”. Still, I think they are at least worth thinking over for the people who don’t ignore me by default.I know, tl;dr, but please try.Upcoming Releases (Subsection of Multi-Console Gaming)This is based on a topic made a few days ago. Topics about unreleased or hypothetical games sometimes don’t have concrete evidence to support lengthy discussions. In fact, most people only speculate; even the idea of the discussed game may never come to fruition. Topics related to these often ask about when such-and-so game will be released. Normally, mods and SS direct them to GameFAQs or Wikipedia for the dates. What I was thinking is that all the topics could be moved to a new subsection of Multi-Console Gaming. It should be a non-for-profit area where members can discuss what they know, theorize, and share their enthusiasm. The topics then expire when the game is released in the last region (of Japan, North America, and Europe). Japan can almost always be ignored since it almost always seen releases first. Once the topic expires, a permanent topic may be made in the appropriate location by anyone. The purpose of this idea is just for the sake of organization.Interactive Help (Subsection of Support & Suggestions) I see this as a potential breeding ground for new staff members. Hey, stick with me for a second. I admit that I can’t solve every problem in the sections I mod. The point of that statement, other than my stupidity, is that the limited amount of staff members have limited knowledge, both individually and cumulatively. If you can’t tell, I’m pondering a successor to the Specialists group. There should be an official and voluntary group for this job. Anyone who is a self-proclaimed expert in Multi-Console gaming, Handhelds, Consoles, or Visual Arts may volunteer. As long as they give good info/help and receive very few complaints, they stay on the team. (I visualized a list of ‘approved’ helpers.) For benefits, I was going to suggest SS forum viewing, but seeing more of Channel isn’t exactly a plus. (I’m kidding.) Part of this idea is the demolition of all the Help, Hints & Tips subsections. All the Help topics can go in Support & Suggestions or an Interactive Help subsection of S&S. Excellent replies by helpers can warrant GP awards. Hint and tips are useless since GameFAQs walkthroughs are more useful and members may be fortunate enough to rely on good common sense to beat a game.Find-A-Friend (Subsection of Website/Forum Advertsiments) The internet is not a series of tubes, but I just-as-absurdly think of it as a massively multiplayer role-playing social network. Anywho (not a spelling error), I joined some sites just because people I know are on them. Yes, I give in to peer pressure at times, but the point is that this is meant to work like a friend code section, but for web sites and games. In the theoretical Find-A-Friend subsection, each topic would represent a site or game. Members can post their names, codes, and relevant information in the proper topic. If necessary, the section can also be used to get seeds for torrents. Then again, advertising may be fine just the way it is.SS Posting in Closed Topics Has this been put into effect yet or have I been missing all the obvious evidence? They won’t have many chances to upstage the mods when they can be shut out of potentially unanswered topics.Get Rid of the Other Rom Sites section It undermines what little authority the site had left. It also is the reason why no smart people show up anymore. At least close all the topics, since no one posts anything worth reading anymore.Off-site Rom Linking This could be done once a week or once a month based on the most wanted rom over a threshold of, say, 30 MBs. It doesn’t need to be a permanent link, but it should last three or four days at a time to be effective and should be announced on the main page.SOTW Announcement Can the SOTW winner have an announcement rather than that bottom frame? On dial-up, it is the last to load and on high-speed, the frame sometimes comes up as ‘connection expired’ or ‘timed out’. Anyway, SOTW is a side attraction of a side attraction. People will find it if they scroll down far enough. Give Spleen announcing powers!Topic Filters (NOT gate) One thing that bothered me was the filter for “Topics: I Replied”; there is no option for “Topics: I Haven’t Replied”. I don’t like posting twice in the same topic with the same basic information. It might be less exhaustive on the system than repeatedly checking each topic’s Javascript replies link.Tally Point Deductions (and Review Deductions) Is it possible to add on to the table(s) for point deductions? Staff can see warns received but not deductions, especially when done through Mod Utilities. Repeat offenders could get deduction after deduction without seeing a PM or warn. This also keeps track of people who don’t erratically post in enthusiastic bursts. On a different note, (A minor?... Screw you. It’s funnier when you’re delirious) do SS report repeat plagiarizers in Bannings after scouring the Review Inspection Notes?Different Submission Format Some reviews have great insight but terrible formatting and grammar. The problem is that we can’t edit someone else’s work. This could be solved with a Terms of Agreement statement and topic submission. The same forms and interface for review submission can be used, but the information can be redirected to a new subsection somewhere. Some magical script can turn each submission into an editable topic. If a staff person approves, but needs to edit spelling/grammar/format mistakes, he or she can do so. However, the review has to go back to the author for another approval. If the member is satisfied, the staff member then clicks the appropriate ‘approve’ link.DG Store V3 This kind of ties in with that new AK topic…. Would it be hard to add fonts, centers, and strikeouts to the store?The End.
