I'd take it a step further (and onto the soapbox) and say that the graphics separated Wind Waker from all other games in general. Graphics technology is always evolving and taking steps toward more visually realistic areas. And while we get used to the advances of our RE5s and GoW2s, our overall standards and expectations for graphics is raised to the point that all the stuff released on the older generations, is simply ugly and archaic. There are a few exceptions to this, of course, where the older stuff from back then still looks great today, and Wind Waker is one of those exceptions. It's phenomenal how well this game has stood the test of time: The animation would make Pixar blush, the lighting is dynamic as hell, and the sunsets. . . It's a damn shame Ninty didn't keep the cell-shaded work for TP; that game looked outdated the moment it was released.
