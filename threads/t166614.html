The following questions are also among the most asked so that it would be a good idea to clarify them. There are many answers to these questions that have been scattered everywhere. I intend to post the following guide that summarizes all these answers in the Rules & Guidelines forum. DM gave me his support for it and I would like the staff give me comments so that I can ameliorate it.The two questions are:- what do member groups represent?- how to join them? Especially, how to become a mod?_____________________Transversally to posting ranks, there are member groups that define your status on the forum. Your member group is displayed just above your post count. This guide is intended for member to both understand the meaning of each group as well as to see how to join them. Especially it covers in the last part the commonly asked question: how to become a moderator?So never ask this question again elsewhere on the forum: not only you'll throw away all your chances to become one but also receive a warn for not reading the rules.1. Members GroupsValidatingMembersPower MembersScreenshottersSupport StaffForum AssistantForum GuardsForum SentinelsAdminsHardcore MembersHall of FamerBanned2. What do they represent?ValidatingNon fully registered members are in this group. They must activate their account first via the confirmation e-mail or otherwise they are not allowed to post nor to download roms.MembersCommom registered members.Power MembersMembers who donate more than $10 a month.ScreenshottersMembers who take care of the screenshots that can been seen on rom pages. They have specific work to do and have higher daily DPs to carry their task.Support StaffMembers who answer e-mails and take care of validating, changing registering e-mails, etc. They also assist moderators by regularly reporting rule-breaking posts.Forum AssistantsModerators ensure that all posts and threads comply to the board rules. They can close, edit, move, and delete them, but those powers are only limited to their assigned section(s). Moderators can also warn and suspend any member as well as view warn logs.Forum GuardsGlobal moderators are the same as moderators except that their powers extend out to the entire board.Forum SentinelsThey have all the global moderator powers plus access to some of the admin controls. Hence they can be referred to as administrator assistants. Sentinels share some common responsibilties (such as changing member user/display names upon request) but their major ones are specific to the individual's skills. For example, some will primarly moderate and handle bannings while others build scripts to expand dgemu.AdminsThey have full power and take care of uploading roms and staff promotion. They will also make the major decisions for the site.Hardcore MembersEx-staff who retired for one reason or another.Hall of FamerEx-staff who used to be outstanding and contributed a lot to the site.BannedMembers who broke important and/or many rules. They can neither view, post , nor download roms.3. How to become a screeshotter?Every month, the screeshotting team makes an annoucement on the main site to recruit new screeshotters. You can apply, then you have to pass a test and if you pass if, you can join the team. However, a reform is under way so that joining the screenshotting team may be different in a few months.4. How to join the staff?If you are a member and wish to join the staff, then you must read the following.Staff rankingSupport Staff < Forum Assistants < Forum Guards < Forum Sentinels < AdminsWhen do promotions take place?It happens sometimes that a staff member retires or is demoted for long absence and/or major rule breaking. In this case, some spots may become available. Also, it can happen that we need more staff to handle the ever increasing number of members and posts. Promotions generally take place at these special periods.What positions are available for members?In most of the case, only support staff and forum assistant positions can be accessed directly from members. The higher staff can be reached by climbing the ladder once you joined you staff.RequirementsSo how to become a support staff or a forum assistant? The following is a general requirement list, which may not be necessary not sufficient. We are not machines, we judge with our feeling and base our judgement on what we observe on the forum. Ultimately it is the judgement and decision from the higher staff that will decide whether you can or not be promoted.Reporting a lot of rule breaking posts (please read the report FAQ to make sure you make the 'good' reports).Finding the dupe threads on the forums by posting the link to the original topics.Being helpful by answering the problem encountered by other members.Having a decent behavior by being courteous (at least not mean) toward other members and staff.Not required but always welcome: having an technical expertise in one or several main subjects discussed on the forum (eg being an expert of DS flash carts, PS emulation...)In short are promotable people who show without saying it that they are capable of modding and know the job's requirements. People promoted to support staff or forum assistants are mostly filled with many negative warns, which are a proof of their helpfulness on the forum so that beginning by seeking for negative warns is a good start (see the warn FAQ for more details). Please note that support staff is more easy of access than forum assistant position, the latter being most of the time by current support staff.Last word on promotion: NEVER ask for it or all your chances will be anihilated. We don't need to know who boasts the best but who actually do the best. In other words: "you don't ask us, we'll ask you".Credits:- Ody for writing it- hackerx for the description of different member groups- xenos219 for the information about the screenshotting team and pointing out some glitchs- atomicknight for fixing the links- DJ Night Force 9 for revisting the guide and correcting it- Teck for the last citation- Dragons Master for his support
