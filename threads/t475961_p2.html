Kula Diamond' timestamp='1334383222' post='5008415 wrote:

How the **** do you fight Vergil(I'm willing to learn a new character if it have real answers against him)?

Vergil's main strength is his dominant ground game, where he can hitconfirm stinger into a full combo from nearly 2/3 screen (much like Dante) and his normals are all huge and have fast startup. His main weakness is that his normals are also extremely unsafe on whiff because he can't cancel whiffed normals into specials. So the way to fight him is to make him whiff, if the Vergil is getting preditable with stingers and st.H, start jumping at him and punish the massive recovery. Also always pushblock him early since it often causes him to whiff in a blockstring (this frustrated me to no end when I first started using Vergil). His air game is actually also pretty good because of j.M's huge air-to-air hitbox and confirms into j.2H or j.S, but very very few Vergils know to use it, so its generally safe to approach from the air. Vergil's own jump-in options are bad, so you can safely anti-air him with airgrabs or st.S with most characters. Also bad Vergils will j.2H all the time, which is *not* punishable on block because he can teleport cancel it (unlike Dante's j.S), but easily punishable by airgrabbing him out of it. And he gets little damage off it unless he XFs so, don't be scared of it too much.

Also don't even try to contend with DT Vergil's stuff because his hitboxes get even bigger and he can cancel on whiff, so just wait it out.

As for mixups, Rapid Slash will always cross up (unlike Berserker Slash). Most people do it after a blockstring into stinger, look for the startup animation where he readies his sword and prepare to block crossup...once you can block it, you take away his best mixup option (since he has to XF or DT/Spiral Swords to hitconfirm from it, so he wastes resources). As for the teleport, its pretty unsafe like all teleports so just mash cr.L to beat whatever he tries afterwards. Most Vergils will also try to j.2H out of the air teleport, just punish with airgrab. With a good beam assist however its another story, properly timed it makes the teleport safe and the beam itself will crossup...the best way to avoid beam crossup setups in my experiences is to superjump when you predict it so the beam whiffs under you.

Also Vergil's damage is god awful if you aren't doing his high execution Round Trip loops, and he has paper HP, so you really shouldn't have too much trouble against bad Vergils.
