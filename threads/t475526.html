They make DLC characters to make more money, there's no conspiracy theory here. And there's two ways to make DLC:1- After getting the game ready, you gather a small part of the team which worked on the game to take care of making the new characters.2- Make them while you're still working on the game with main team, before the game is done.Two is a much more efficient way, cheaper and faster. It basically have better profit/cost ratio, which is all that matter from Capcom viewpoint.Now they could avoid putting those characters on the disc, but then people will have to download the whole characters from PSN/XBL instead of just downloading a 150kb unlock code.
