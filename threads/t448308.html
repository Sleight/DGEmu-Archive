I've been playing the game for like a month. It's challenging, but it's not as difficult as people claim. It definitely punishes people for rushing in without thinking, but it's difficulty is something normal people can handle for sure. I really enjoy the game, mainly because of the wonderfully morbid atmosphere throughout most of the game. But of course, the game has it's flaws, like stage design that has many entirely random places where you can fall and die just to "increase the difficulty", totally not a good way to do that, it's more frustrating and annoying than challenging. Also, the online community is mainly horrible, since people just love torturing new players by attacking them. But once you get past those flaws, you'll get a very enriching game experience
