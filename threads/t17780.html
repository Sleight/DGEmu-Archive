1a) This is a list of top-notch anime to satify anyone's animation needs, from otaku to newcomer. The entries are listed alphabetically for anime aired before 2005, and seasonally for everything afterwards. The list is also composed of anime recommended by forum members for forum members. New recommendations are added every season, so if you've been following the current season, feel free to post in the most current recommendations thread. Keep in mind that the list will only be adding anime from the most current season.1b) The condition is: You have watched the anime you are recommending. Don't post any anime that you have only heard/read are good or that you expect to be enjoyable. Only post anime that you consider to be the finest, meaning that you would not hesitate to recommend them to your friends or the likes.1c) To recommend an anime, post its title together with the corresponding information, meaning at least the number of episodes and a genre classification. Don't write an essay, though - short summaries may be helpful but are unnecessary because they won't be on the list anyway.1d) The topic to do this is the ARL addition topic.2a) Suggestions will be regularly reviewed by the DGEmu Anime Committee (DGEAC): mrcn, Kuisanagi, Leor, Fafner, Likkun, Ryuuka, viciouswhispers, Ichida Kirby.2b) Only anime this expert group composed of experienced anime watchers deem worthy will be placed on the list. Beyond content changes, several important format changes have been made as well:-The *new* list is now categorized by season instead of alphabetically. This is because airing year and recent-ness is a pretty big factor in determining animation quality, and also because its easier on the eyes than one big wall of text.-The [M>A] and all other denotations for anime origins have been removed. Honestly...they weren't all that useful to knowing general information about a series, and information like that can be found easily if someone really gets into an anime [and is pretty useless otherwise].
