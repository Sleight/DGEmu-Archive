Ah, I've been expecting this.Both have their obvious merits. Though Digimon was conceived first with a series of Tamagotchi-like toys in the mid to late 1990's, Pokemon is favored by many as having been the original, showing up on the Game Boy in 1998. The anime series followed shortly after and became a hit, with ten seasons ensuing.Digimon premiered in 1999 and unlike Pokemon, has a much more non-linear plot. While the obvious goal in Pokemon was to catch all of the monsters and defeat the Gym Leaders, the plot of Digimon more involved saving the Digital World. The first two seasons of Digimon followed the same group through their adventures, while the 3rd season, Digimon Tamers, re-invented the franchise into it's arguably most popular incarnation. This can possibly be attributed to the contribution of Chiaki J. Konaka, who had previously worked on Serial Experiments Lain.With Digimon back this year after a three-year hiatus for its fifth season and fourth incarnation, Digimon Savers, this battle will not end soon. Everyone has their own personal preferance, but there is not one that is 'better' than the other. Respecting others opinions will put this debate to rest.
