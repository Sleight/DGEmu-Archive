The Resident Evil series has always been pretty good, however the same could not be said of their voice acting in some of the earlier titles. The game is a survival horror where there are puzzles galore and saving ammo is a must. The storyline is very in-depth, and in the older games, you were always faced with the hard decision of what guns to leave behind in order to pick up the important items, as well as new guns. Some of the storyline would be projected through important files that would be lying around rooms in whatever building the walking monstrosities forced you to take shelter in. If you are a really big fan of the series, then you will want to find all the files in order to get a better idea of what had happened to the area the game takes place in, as well as to find clues on opening certain doors or where to go next in the game.
