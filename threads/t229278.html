Q : What is DGEmu?A : DGEmu is an acronym for Dragons Generation Emulation - the emulation site of the Dragons Generation network established by Dragons Master.Q : What are DPs / Download Points?A : Downloads Points (DPs) are points you use to download roms with. DPs can now be converted to GPs, provided you are a donor.Q : What are GPs?A : GPs or Gold Pieces are points your earn by posting in the forums, or converting from DPs (again, donor only). GPs can be converted to DPs using the GPs to DPs Converter, or be used to buy customizations from the DGstore.Q: What is this DGstore?A: DGstore is a sort of a shop, where you can spend your GPs to buy profile and post customizations for your account on the forums, such as Posting color, Member title, etc.It is here where you'll get to change your Display name and username too.http://www.dgemu.com/forums/dg_store.phpQ: How do you post?A: Go to the Forums(click on the button on the menu at your left), choose a forum and click on its title to enter it, choose a thread and click on its title to enter it, and click on the 'Post Reply' button to post. To post a topic, go to a forum using those steps already described, and then simply click on the 'New Topic' button. You also need to have validated your account after registering, to post.Please note: Before posting in the Forums, please read and follow everything in the Rules & Guidelines forum first.Q : How many GPs do I get when I post in the forums?A : You get 6 GPs for each post, 10 GPs for each topic and 10 GPs for each poll. However, there is an exception: You get NO GPs at all when you post in the Hello & Goodbye, Praise the Dragon, Spam, and any of the Visual Art forums.Q : Will I have to pay money to download these ROMs?A : No, our ROMs are free for downloading, you only have to pay DPs to download a ROM(DPs are automatically deducted whenever you click the Download button).If you wish to do so, you may donate money to DGEmu in exchange for more DPs to download more ROMs.If you wish to donate, thank you very much for your kind contribution, for more information please visit the Donate PagePlease note that all donations are strictly voluntary and optional.Q: How do you play a GBA ROM?A: Please refer to our "How to use VisualBoy Advance" guide:http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=198827Q: How many accounts is one user entitled to?A: Strictly one only. Users who register more than one account will have all their dupes banned, and they will not be able to use them to access the DGEmu Forums, or download any roms.Repeat offenders will get their IP addresses banned as well.Q: How do I get my warn level down?/What is 'warn level' in the first place?A: Please refer to our Warn FAQ:http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=149849Q: Why are there different kinds of DPs?A: Each type except for the Global DPs is used to download certain roms:- Global DPs: Those are used for any kind of ROM. However, they reset to 12.5 each day.- NCDPs: Those are used for NES, SNES and N64 ROMs only.- NDS/GBx DPs: Those are used for GBA, GBC, Gameboy and NDS ROMs only.- Atari DPs: Those are used for Atari Jaguar/Lynx ROMs only- Retro DPs: Those are used for Sam Coupe, MAME and Wonderswan ROMs only.- Sega DPs: Those are used for SMS, Genesis and Gamegear ROMs only.Q: How do I get more DPs?A: You can get DPs in the following ways :- Converting Gps to DPs using the convertor- Donating- Using the "DP Loan" Function- Writing an excellent review for a game - you get awarded 10 System specific DPs for it.Q: Exactly how many DPs do I get by donating?A: Please refer to the DPs and Donating topic:http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=143961Q: How do I convert one type of DPs into another?A: You can only do so if you are a Donator.For Donators, you can convert one type of DPs into another using the DPs to DPs Converter in the Donators' Center:http://www.dgemu.com/D_Center.htmlPlease note : Global DPs cannot be converted to any other type of DPs.Q: Can I donate in another person's name?A: Sure, you can customize your donation in any way you want through arrangement with our Webmaster. Please contact him by emailing webmaster(AT)dgemu(DOT)comQ: Can I donate ROMs to DGEmu?A: Unfortunately, DGEmu does not accept any donations or contributions in the form of ROMs, whether DGEmu has the ROMs or not. Please do not offer us ROMs, especially in exhange for DPs, as we will be unable to accept them.Q: I waited for a few days, and my DPs didn't go above 12.5!/I had 20 Global DPs yesterday, but I only have 12.5 today! What's happening?A: You do not earn any dps per day. Global dps reset to 12.5 everyday - Whether you have more, or less - at around 12AM GMT, they do not build up.Q: My Global DPs didn't reset today! Why?A: It may take some time for the Global DPs to reset, but do not worry, as they do reset everyday, so please just wait for them to reset. It may also be possible that your browser is displaying cached content. Try clearing your cache, or a forced refresh (Ctrl + F5).Q: I have converted XX GPs into DPs, but they aren't showing on my Download page: it keeps saying I'm too poor to download a ROM; what should I do?A: Most likely, your browser is showing you cached content, (again! tongue.gif ). Reload(Ctrl+R for Internet Explorer and Mozilla Firefox) the page or try a forced refresh (Ctrl + F5) and you should be able to see the correct value.Q: ROMs are too expensive! Can't they get cheaper?A: 1DP = 1MB. Therefore, the cost is proprotional to our bandwhich usage, therefore it will not be lowered.Q: I can't download a rom at all/Downloading a rom takes far too long! Can you help?A: This is due to your connection speed, thus we cannot help you. Please upgrade your connection (broadband is recommended) or come when there are less people on DGEmu.Also, please update your browser to the latest version, or change it entirely(we recommend Mozilla Firefox from http://www.mozilla.org/)Updating Internet Explorer: http://www.microsoft.com/windows/ie/downloads/default.mspxUpdating Mozilla Firefox(also the Add-ons page): https://addons.mozilla.org/?application=firefoxUpdating Opera : http://www.opera.com/download/Updating Netscape: http://browser.netscape.com/Q: I downloaded a ROM, but the .zip file was corrupted/empty/did not contain any ROM files/I didn't even get a .zip file! What should I do?A: When you download a ROM, please do not select 'Open' when the File Download window pops up.Better still, the next time you download a ROM, do not click the 'Download' or 'Click Here to Download' button, instead, right-click it and select 'Save Target As'.Do not use download managers, download accelerators, or anything that pauses/resumes downloads or alters the speed of downloading, as that will cause your download to become very messed up.Also, If you get a zip file that's 24 kb, report it here :http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=134747Q: I downloaded a ROM and it's not working. Can I get a refund?A: No. We only refund members who have been experiencing problems with downloaded ROMs when there are problems with the site that we are aware of.The Global DPs were created for this specific purpose. If a ROM is not working, try redownloading it when your Global DPs reset.Please do not request for a DPs refund under any circumstances.Q: I tried to download a game only to find out I don't have enough DP's. I could have sworn I had more than enough. What's the deal?A: DP's are used prior to download. If you clicked the download button and cancel for whatever reason, your DP's have been used even though you didn't start the download at all.Also, if you double clicked the download button, your DP's have been used twice instead of once. Just be patient and everything will work properly.Q: I have donated to the site, but can't access Donator Center/didn't get the DPs I paid for. What is going on?A: Please wait for your donation to be processed, it usually takes less than 24 hours from the time of your donation.However, if it has been more than 24 hours since your donation, and this problem still persists, please contact Dragons Master atwebmaster(AT)dgemu(DOT)com directly, or Playswiss, since they are the only people who can help you on that issue.Please keep in mind that they may be very busy, so it might take a little time before they reply to your message.Q: I donated to the site not long ago, and got some dps, but they're gone now! Where have they been to?!A: Please refer to http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=95800Q: Can I delete my acccount?A: No, you can't delete your own member account.Also, please do not request for your account to be deleted under any circumstances, as DGEmu does not delete member accounts in any case.If you wish to leave DGEmu, please just leave your account as it is.Q: I cannot login/I get logged out soon after logging in each time! What's happened?A: Please clear your cache, enable all cookies by DGEmu, and try again.There are about 2 possibilities:1) Your account has been banned. This will only happen if you have made more than one account on DGEmu, or if you have broken the Forum Rules too oftenly in the forums.2) You cannot remember your username correctly. If so, please contact us using the 'Contact Us' link on the menu to your left, stating your problem, and giving us your email address that you used to register your account with, and we'll be able to help you.Q: Some GBA ROMs do not work! What should I do?A: Please refer to our Emulation FAQ for more information:http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=186228If you are receiving a black screen, however, Please update your DirectX from Microsoft's DirectX Page.Q: The Classic NES ROMs don't work! What should I do to fix them?A: Nothing, as, unfortunately, they aren't supposed to work.Q: If they aren't supposed to work, then why are they on DGEmu?A: Those roms are mainly for rom collectors. Whatever the case, DGEmu uploads all the roms avalible for the types of roms it has, without much exception.Q: I had trouble downloading the Mario Tennis: Power Tour ROM! Can you help?A: Please download Mozilla Firefox from http://www.mozilla.org/ and use it to redownload that ROM.Q: The Pokemon Emerald ROM does not work! How do I make it work?A: Please follow the steps here EXACTLY AS WRITTEN: http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=21909Q: Some N64 ROMs do not work! Why?A: Actually, they do work, just follow the solutions in this topic: http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=22390 and all should be fine.Q: Where should I get emulators?A: For emulators except for Nintendo DS emulators, please refer to http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=61643For Nintendo DS emulators, please refer to http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=68847Nintendo DS emulation is still in its infancy, so many Nintendo DS ROMs do not work at all with any emulator, and some which work do not work well.This topic might help you find out which Nintendo DS ROMs work, and how well they work:http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=215270Q: When I went into DGEmu, there was spyware/trojans/viruses? Why?A: DGEmu does not put spyware/trojans/viruses etc.("malware") at all.
