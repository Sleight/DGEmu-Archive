VVibronaterJun 29 2007, 02:31 PM
To Smudge, duplicate your render leave one layer hidden, then on your other layers of the render take the smudge tool and pretty much get rid of your render. But you have to make sure it doesn't look too messed up, it's better to pull to the unblended parts of the render. Then unhide the layer you left alone, should be blended better. Takes a LOT of practice, I'm still not great at it.The main ideas is to make the render look like it's part of the BG, not just slapped on.And I really do reccomend that you get more brushes...
