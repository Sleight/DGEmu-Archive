Its an interesting idea, an RPG with crisp Anime style graphics rather than the same stuff you'd see in Guild Wars or World of Warcraft. Makes for an acceptable MMORPG I guess. The combat system is different too... Based on combat actions you choose rather than just blindly swinging, you can attack, defend, counter, smash etc. But the problem is that if you don't have a very fast internet connection you're going to have a VERY hard time hitting anything, or blocking any attack, because they will keep knocking you down. It can be interesting to play if you don't mind the EXTREMELY annoying in-game music (not what you hear in the trailers, that is good), and the fact that even though you can play the game for free, you need a members card to access all the cool features like pets, and the rest of the character looks.
