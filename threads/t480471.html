I decided to give all of you a little treat. This is the Mother 3 soundtrack. However, unlike the official or even the GSF (i.e. the Gameboy Advance Sound Format) version, this one was ripped using a tool called "GBA Mus Riper" (yes, it's actually Riper; not Ripper) converting it into a soundfont for the samples and a series of midi files. They were then converted into mp3's and properly named and tagged for easy access and listening. The result is that AWFUL background noise normally produced by GBA sound chip is eliminated completely as the samples are played directly without any quality loss or downsampling. Hope you enjoy this soundtrack the way it was meant to be heard!
