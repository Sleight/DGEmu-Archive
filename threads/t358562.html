Pokemon Red/BlueGlitch City:Go into the Safari Zone and say YES that you want to catch Pokemon. Go into the first rest house in front of you. Talk to the man with glasses. Leave the house and try to leave Safari Zone. When he asks you if you are leaving early, say NO. It will make you walk back into the Safari Zone. Leave again, and once again say NO. When you go back into the Safari Zone, save your game and then shut it off. Turn it back on. Go out of the Safari Zone. This is when the first glitch appears. Instead of asking you if you want to leave early, he asks you if you want to join the hunt. When he asks you this make sure to say NO again. Walk out of the house and FLY to Cinnabar Island. SURF up and down the coast until there is the gong you usually hear in the Safari Zone. The game then warps you back to the Safari Zone and the warden asks if you had a good haul and so forth. Leave the building, and when you go outside you are in a strange "Glitch City." It is very easy to get trapped in here, and the different glitches make it hard to move around. Some things to try are fishing, surfing, and trying to find new paths. Just remember, nothing is what it seems. This trick does not appear to ruin any other part of the game. If you get stuck in there and want to get out of the city, you must FLY out and save.For Yellow version,Invisible PC:Go to the people hotel in Celadon City. Go all the way to the center of the right wall. Go one step up and press A. You will log onto a PC just as if there were one there.Bicycle Music:While in Seafoam Islands, ride your bicycle in the cave. Fall down one of the holes. You will either land on another section of the cave or in water, but either way the bicycle music will still be playing even though you are no longer on the bike.Tested and 100% true, trust me...

I'll teach you the Mew Glitch. You can catch it with no problem. Nintendo said that there is no way to catch Mew, but they LIED...Here are the steps you need in order to catch Mew:This cheat requires the Red, Blue, or Yellow version. I only tested it on the Red and Blue versions, but I don't see any reason why it wouldn't work on Yellow. So, after you've fished your R/B/Y version out of your drawer and blown the dust out of it, first of all, you will most likely need to restart the game, seeing as, for this cheat to work there are certain trainers that you had to not battle along the way. Okay, for a while, just play the game as normal. Make sure that you do not battle the following trainers: the boy trainer facing north on the way to Bill's house, and the old man trainer facing north on the way between Lavender Town and Celadon City. In Celadon City, stop at the Department Store and pick up some Great Balls. Also make sure that you have enough room in your box on the PC for when you catch Mew. Alright, all set? Then cut through Celadon and get the Fly HM from the girl in the secret retreat over Cycling Road. Teach Fly to a Pokemon. Next, cut back through Celadon City and go into the underground passage. I recommend that you save inside the underground passage just in case you mess up, because if you do, you'll need to restart the game all over again. And that would be bad. Exit the Underground Passage house, but DON'T MOVE! Okay, you are just above the old man that I told you not to battle before. Get ready, this might take a few attempts. What you need to do is take one step south and hit the start button so that the menu pops up. If you did it right, you'll see the old man. He's just standing there for now, but if you didn't hit the start button right, he would have wanted to battle you. The thing is, YOU CANNOT battle him. So, hopefully if you did that right, you'll see the old man, but the menu will be open.Got it? Okay. Now you need to go to your Pokemon Menu on the Menu list, and select the Pokemon that you taught fly. Tell it to fly to Cerulean City. The old man will suddenly see you, but you'll be flying away before he can do anything about it. In Cerulean City, go up the bridge and head towards Bill's house. Make sure you don't battle anyone along the way. Stop just before you get to the boy I told you not to battle before.Make sure that you are far enough away from the boy, so that when he sees you, he'll have to walk up to get to you. If he doesn't walk up to get to you, your game supposedly will freeze and everything will be messed up. Battle and defeat the boy. He will use a Slowpoke. Fighting this Slowpoke will be what makes Mew appear later. It has something to do with that particular Slowpoke's Special Statistic being 21. I don't understand it, but it works. Immediately after defeating the trainer, fly to Lavender Town. Beneath the PokeCenter and to the west, there is a narrow path. Head along that path. Suddenly, you will be stopped and the Menu will pop up. Just hit B and it will go away. Then, out of nowhere, you will be attacked. It will be a level seven Mew. I don't think you need me to tell you this, but CATCH IT!!!And there you have it! A Mew! Your very own little Mew for you to nurture and train! And Nintendo said it couldn't be done! Oh and, just a note, this glitch will let you catch other Pokemon, too, by fighting Pokemon with different specials instead of the Slowpoke. For instance, to get a level 7 Mewtwo, you'll need to encounter a Pokemon with Special 131 (to make life easy, train one of your Pokemon to Special 131 and fight a Ditto in place of the Slowpoke trainer). Although it would be kinda neat to be able to train a baby Mewtwo from level 7, you can get all the other Pokemon naturally, so all I really care about using this cheat for Mew....

Clone Pokemon and itemsNote: This trick could corrupt your saved game file. Put the Pokemon you want cloned in an empty box on Bill's computer. Then switch to another empty box. When it asks you to save select "Yes". Once it says there is another file, select "Yes" and at the same time turn the power off on the Game Boy system.
