And so today I attended the Pokemon event here in Montreal, just to get a quick look of what they had to offer. The event was held in Montreal at a place called "La RÃ©crÃ©ation". (http://www.larecreation.ca/PAGES_ENG./eng_Concept.html)

Upon my arrival 15 minutes in advance, I was pleasantly surprised by the amount of people already in the lineup. Already possible around 40 people. When it came the time to start entering the event, the line pretty much quadrupled in length. The lineup was mostly full of people between the ages of 16 and 35 (mostly around 20), and the minority were children accompanied by parents. This just shows that the community following the Pokemon cult are not just simply kids.

The event was held in a small place. The activities offered were the following:
- Picture session with giant inflatable Pokemons.
- Pikachu origami
- Pokemon Black 2 and White 2 games on demo, all on Nintendo 3DS and Nintendo 3DS XL consoles.

They were giving a few goodies though, so I took the opportunity to take a picture of the stuff I got once I got back home. Unfortunately, I didn't take any pictures at the event as it was just WAY too crowded...


Basically, what I got was a promotional preview guide of the games, an event invitation card, Pokeball shaped cookies (one of them is missing something...), and an Oshawott figurine (you had the choice between a plastic Pokemon figurine or a Pokeball stress ball).

Overall, the event wasn't too grant, the activities were limited, and especially the place was too small to accommodate all the guests.

Since I had more time to kill, I decided to go over at the local EB Games to pick these things up:
- A collector's edition strategy guide
- Both versions of the game


And that's pretty much it.

I hope you guys liked this quick mini report. :)
