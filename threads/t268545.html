Skills that I reccomend:Glide lv.1 or higher: To help dodge meteorQuick run lv.2 or higher: To have a better chance to escape his attacksAerial dodge lv.1 or higher: this will be useful while trying to intterupt sinharvestRetaliating slash: When he slashes you in the air, right when he's visible press squareAerial finish: This will take out a good amount of HP if you do an aerial comboSlapshot: This will do an extra hit at the end of a combo, and if equipped with aerial finish, it can take out around 1/8- 1/4 of an HP barUpper slash: start a combo, then knock him in the air with upper slash and do an air comboslide dash: after you use guard on Sephiroth, you'll get a cchance to hit him faster if you have this equipped.Once more: This skill will help you a lot, considering Sephiroth does lot of combosLeaf Bracer: It'll help while casting cure, since you'll be invincible while using it.MP rage: Sephiroth will attack you a lot, so MP will recover faster during MP charge.Ultima Keyblade: You'll heed it for it's effect, and for the fact that it's the strongest key in the game.Elixers: use ONLY if he hits you with sin harvest. ( you can tell if you can interrupt it if you don't have a halo on your head)At the start of the battle, once the command menu is visible press triangle to guard ( He does this a few times), if you don't don't worry, if you're around lv. 70, you'll have enough hp to live, if you guarded, attack him once, then while the combo is still going, press square, then do an aerial combo to deal a fair amount of damage. The next part is easy, if you have retaliating slash. Sephiroth will knock you in the air (which he'll do a lot throughout the battle), once he does this, press square if he's visible, then do an air combo on him. Do this every time he does this.Once he loses around 4-5 bars of HP, he'll start using three new attacks: sin harvest and tow other which I don't know the name of. Sin harvest will bring your hp to 1 and take out all your MP, if you don't interrupt him. He will also use a move which will create dark balls, use magnet to stop these from hitting you. The other move is where he summons fire pillars that suck you up if you're not fast enough.Once he has four bars left he'll start glowing red and use one more new move: Meteor. If you have glide, glide around the arena to avoid the meteors. If you're almost dead use curaga or an elixir.I recommend being lv. 70 while battling Sephiroth.

SShifting ShadowsApr 19 2009, 05:28 AM
There's actually a way to get rid of the first 3 -5 bars and you take maybe only a bit of damage, if any, even in Proud mode. You equip both negative combo and one of the finishing moves (guard break, explosion)+slide dash then after guard you attack. Attack the 2-hit combo (negative) and do the combo one more time. This prevents him from warping. After the second combo, block when Sephiroth raises his sword and do (counterguard) then attack with the double 2-hit combo again. Continue until he warps away or does a different attack. If he warps, let him hit you into the air once then counterattack with square. The dark balls and fire pillar can be (mostly) stopped with reflega. I'd suggest magnet for the dark balls though. Thunder doesn't work with Sin Harvest. Try to get him before he finishes, and if you can't , use an elixir. Others have already given you the rest of what I know.
