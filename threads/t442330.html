I think the big thing that people in this thread are missing is that you can have power and still maintain peace. "Power" is a very broad term, after all, and there are other ways to get a rival nation to fall in line besides having a large military, such as economically or technologically. America is a great example of this. We're losing our status as the world's only superpower not because we don't possess a large and advanced army, but because our economy is spinning down the toilet. We don't hold the same influence over other nations as we did a decade ago, and I'm not even talking about public opinions, which are largely irrelevant.
