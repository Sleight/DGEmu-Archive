New Topic Rules for Linux OS Uploads

We welcome all contributions, but we do have certain standards and rules.

-Be sure that what you upload is fully tested and operational.
-All files must be archived in .zip, .rar, or .7z format.
-You may upload to any file-sharing site (Ex.: FileFactory, MediaFire, DepositFiles, etc.)
-We do not accept torrent files or links to other direct download sites. (Ex.: The Pirate Bay, EdgeEmu, etc.)
-We prefer you split the files for more convenient uploading/downloading into chunks of 800 MB where the files exceed 900 MB. If you plan to use MediaFire, you will want to split the files into chunks of less than 200 MB to fit their criteria. You can split the archives using WinRAR or HJSplit, though, if set up correctly, WinRAR is simpler.
-Do not use password protected files.
-Be sure that what you are posting is not already posted.
-The title of your posting topic must be the title of the game only.
-All new topics will be "Unapproved" or invisible until a moderator can test the game. This may take a few days.
-Please be patient, but feel free to PM Meru or Pan before or after posting with any questions or update requests.

Your topic should appear in this format or one very similar.

Posting template:

[center]


A quick description of your upload and/or a link to general information such as the gamefaqs page for the game or its wiki page

Size in MB or GB[/center]

Filefactory_link_002

[/ code] (with no space between the / and the word code)
