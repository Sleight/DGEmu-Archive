All ROMs for GBA, NDS, 3DS, etc hosted on DGEmu (not ones we link to in the Download Centre) are NOT patched, they are straight dumps, no alterations what so ever!

 

This is for a variety of reasons, the major ones of which are:

Even if the game does not work at all there are collectors who download ANY ROM (or US ROMs only, JP ROMs only, etc etc).

If a patch is released, say v1.1 and then a few months later a new patch is released (v1.2) then you will NEED a clean ROM (like what we host) to apply the new patch.  Patching over a already patched ROM will either not work or cause serious problems.

Same as above but including translation patches, etc.
