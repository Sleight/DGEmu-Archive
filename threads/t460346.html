RRayadragonNov 22 2010, 09:59 PM
I'll be the first dissenter and admit that I've been known to hold a grudge for years at times. I've managed to eventually get over most of them, although a few still give me twinges from time to time.For the most part, I do agree with the previous posters that holding a grudge for a long period of time can be damaging. After all, you're wasting your time on someone who really doesn't deserve your time or caring. I do think it's possible to gain some useful information about yourself with this process though. Most of the time, during the grudge I reexamine events that led to the grudge. Often times I realize that we were both to blame, though perhaps in different amounts, for the situation. By examining the causes for the grudge, I can learn how to avoid the situation in the future, by learning how to better deal with the actions of others or by learning how to better handle my own over reactions.
