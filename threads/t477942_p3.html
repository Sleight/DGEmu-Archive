KKirie MotobaApr 05 2013, 02:59 PM

I'm just curious, for many people experiencing issues:

 

I notice that most people only specify the size of MicroSD, but never the Class type.

 

People using Class 2 MicroSD can suffer from music that jumps, slow videos, slower load times, etc.

The base recommended for flashcarts is usually Class 4 at least.

If you got more money, you can always invest into a Class 10, but those aren't cheap.

 

To give an example to identify the class of your card, look for a number inside the letter "C".

 

As an example, in the image below, the Samsung 32GB MicroSD card is a Class 6.

Just an FYI for people who don't know about AdFly.

 

When you click on the link to DL these files, you will be shown an Ad.  Do *not* download anything that shows up on this page!  It is not going to be good for your PC.  Wait about 5 or 6 seconds (and close any pop-up windows that open in the meantime) and then click "Skip Ad" in the upper right corner of the page.

On the new page, click the Download button that colored orange.  This is the file you want.

 

Safe downloading!
