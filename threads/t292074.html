Ok, so I used to ask about this before I was on staff, and I never felt I got a sufficient answer from any staff members. Thoughts?

Personally, I think it is necessary to have a Help, Hints & Tips section. I don't believe GameFAQs (and all similar sites combined) can answer ALL questions about every game in existance. A brand new game comes out and therefore nobody has released a FAQ for it yet (because they have to play through it first). 2. Someone may be seeking a certain cheat which can't be found anymore. For example, try and find a gameshark/codebreaker code for Megaman - Battle Network 5 that makes charged shots from dark unisons always succeed in executing the associated darkchip and never summon Rockman DS when you let go of the B button. You won't find that cheat anywhere and therefore, finding such a code would require someone who knows what relevant memory values that get changed during this process.3. Some games a so obscure, nobody even bothers to write a FAQ yet some member may play one of these and ask for help. With the current system, a thread like that would be closed asking the member to go to gamefaqs just to find out that no FAQ even exists.So while this does keep repetitive topics out of the way, it also has its disadvantages as illustrated above. I agree with the idea to re-open the GBA help, hints, & tips but remove GP awards in it and all similar sections. That'll keep the spammers out and hopefully leave those that do intend to help.
