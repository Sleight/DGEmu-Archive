OOpulent MaelstromORIGINAL POSTMar 06 2005, 11:02 PM
Due to the fact that I feel like it, here are some new moderating instructions that I urge you to follow.Warn a member for every single thread or post that is in violation. I don't care if they are newbies or they didn't read the rules right, they still have to be warned, no excuse. Feel free to PM these members, tell them to read the forum rules and FAQs, etc.Don't cuss, make fun of, or insult members. Do your best not to offend them.Please rename the title of any thread if it doesn't indicate what's in them. That way members can find threads easier when searching.When you close a thread, state why you closed it, even if the reason is obvious. Do it whether it requires that you post or edit a post in order to do it. I don't want to recieve PMs asking " :D omg y waz my topic closed?!?!?!?! :D ".When a member sends successive spam posts/topics deliberately to gain GPs, don't bother to warn them and report them for a ban (suspend them from posting if you can, but still report them). Then, you may delete all those member's posts. There's a very useful tool called The Mod CP, ask me for help if you don't know how to use it. Do not, and I repeat DO NOT EVER close (or move) a thread that doesn't violate an official DGEmu Forum Rules rule. Even if it gets spammed like hell. In which case you must mod all the spammers one by one, ignoring the thread itself. Same goes for when a question has been answered, or the problem is solved, LEAVE IT OPEN.Topic starter can't help it if his topic gets spammed, and if he has another question then he shouldn't post it in another thread.And stop moving good threads to spam, it's really starting to get on my nerves.Of course, if you are a forum assistant, all of these apply only in your respective forums. And of course, all these apply only in GP and post counting forums.If you don't intend to do all of this when required, don't touch the thread, leave it for others to mod. Note: If any of these instructions clashes with something in Saady's topic or in the rules, my instructions take priority.I'm leaving this topic open for questions or comments. If your member group is Support Staff or GFX Team don't post in here because it will automatically count as spam.
