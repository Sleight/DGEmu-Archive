The purpose of The Dump is to give you members a chance to save your topics from being deleted. The other purpose of The Dump is for us staff members to be able to make the forums cleaner and easier to browse.For MembersRules and Explanation of the Idea of The DumpMembers can Not start new topics in The Dump.You may get your topic moved out of The Dump and into the Spam Center if it can accumulate 50 new posts that are not spam or are atleast remotely interesting and generate more posts from other members or staff.You may reply to topics in order to keep them alive longer. Keep in mind that any topic that has not been replied to within 60 days will be deleted. Do not repeatedly ask a Moderator or Admin to move your topic out of The Dump if it has already been denied. Doing so will lead to a warn at that staff members discretion.All Rules but Posting rules still apply to The DumpFor StaffUsage and InstructionsIt is up to the Moderator as to how long a topic has to wait to be moved into the dump. However no less than 30 days. Duplicate topics are the only exception to this stipulation.Topics with 20 replies or over should not be moved into The Dump unless they are duplicates of another topic and have less replies than that topic, and/or, would be too difficult to merge with the original topic. All topics in Support and Suggestion require an Admin's discretion.If a topic in The Dump reaches 50 or more replies while in The Dump, check to see if the topic is worthy of being moved into the Spam Center. If it is not leave it.If a Moderator feels a topic has been wrongly moved into The Dump when it could have been moved to Spam Center. They may feel free to move it there with the consent of a Higher Authority. (Smod through Admin)These rules will be updated as new situations prompting a change arise.- DGEmu Administration
