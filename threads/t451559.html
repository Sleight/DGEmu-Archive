Please make sure that your posts to this section follow these few rules:- When uploading a soundtrack, please use one of the following as your host: Megaupload, Mediafire, File Factory, Fileserve, or Hotfile- Do not post a link to another website's download page or download link- All soundtracks should be compressed as a zip or rar file- Do not upload ISOs in this section. Any lossless (APE, FLAC, etc.) format or regular media (MP3, OGG, etc.) format is allowed- Please do not upload protected media (M4P, WMA, etc.)- Posting a thread considered as spam will automatically result in a warn.Here's a template on how you should present your post:<Album Title><Album Cover><Track List><Download Links>If your post follows the rules and is approved, you'll get a little reward. :gs:Happy uploading! Sharing with the community is just the first step towards making this site even more awesome!
