What is it?Debate of the week, an idea which was proposed by 2bigpigs, is a weekly competition which will see the best among you being awarded with nice prizes.How does it work?A new thread will be created every Monday and remain open until the following Monday when the next thread is opened. As a thread is closed, Kiza19 and/ or myself Finalage will choose the top five posts. After these posts have been, a poll will be created so that you, the members, can vote for the reply which you believe is best. The lucky winner of this vote will get themselves a nice prize.But what is the prize?The prize for the winning post will be 250 GPs. If there is a tie for first place, then the 250 GPs will be split. The winner will also be given the option of creating the next topic.Debate rulesNow before you jump in a few words of caution, the rules of the Debates forum (found here) are in full effect here. As such we expect you to sufficiently develop your posts (guidelines on this here).- As of current, I Finalage, have taken the task of the debate of the week upon myself. Any questions and comments are to be directed to me. Thank you. The ammendment of Monday in place of Sunday was made offically as of March 7th, 2010.You may notice the format of this section changing a bit. That is my fault, as I am shifting things around here to make them, at least in my mind, more organized. Mainly, I am unpinning topics and pinning others. Ordinarily, I will unpin a topic once voting is over and pin up the new one, so as to minimize the amount of pinned topics around here. - Finalage.A note to MODS: As of now, I ordinarily will wait until the Debate Of The Week topics sink to the second page before placing them here in this section. I want to give debaters a chance to continue debating on them in the open. Please don't move them back here unless they are in the second page and I have not done so.
