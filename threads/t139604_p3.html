Looking at posts and topics, it's hard to say which RPG subtype everyone is looking for. Let me post some rarely heard RPG.DOKAPON KINGDOM. RPG/BOARDGAMEYou might be thinking, 'What's that?! final fantasy and mario party combined?!'. Yes it is what your thinking. DOKAPON KINGDOM is a boardgame multiplayer RPG set. Choose your avatar, set your stat and you take turns against CPU and human players going around the board, level grinding, shopping, boss fighting questing and everything. The board pieces are towns, empty spaces where the random encounters are found, dungeon entrances, magic shop and misc. types land types spreaded around. Players rolls the dice to explore the kingdom leveling up and doing everything you would do in an RPG... one turn at a time. I'll try at one point to go back to reviewing games and talk about DOKAPON KINGDOM in details, but I can tell you that this is a low memory sized multiplayer RPG not to be missed.

Well as an avid RPG fan myself, I have a bit of a list, but here are some of my favourites at the moment:*Fire Emblem: What can I say? It's a classic. Turn-based strategic action, with all your favourite classic classes. Besides, you feel real accomplishment after beating a tough map without losing any units.*Golden Sun Dark Dawn: Again, another classic, and if you've played the GBA versions, you'd know what you'll be getting. I love a good puzzle solving... and there's also bosses to beat after you're done with the main game.*Shin Megami Tensei Devil Survivor: It's the first of the series I've ever played, but the replayable value is very nice. With several endings, what battles you choose and paths you take can change your outcome. Battles are turn-based, and fusing demons is very addictive. XD *The World Ends With You: A real-time battle game; but collecting and leveling up pins has never been more fun! I don't usually play real-time RPGs but this one is a must for collection!*Final Fantasy III: If you're looking for some old-school RPG, this turn-based game definitely brings back some memories. :unsure: Though Final Fantasy IV is also nice, though the real-time yet turn-based battling is something that I had to get used to.*Dragon Quest series: The remakes are really nice, and are also reminiscent of old-school RPG.As you can see, I quite enjoy the classics. :oHope I helped.
