SSir VGJan 20 2008, 02:22 PM
The game overall was a wonderful treat, since Rondo is one of my favorite Castlevania games.There are some minor faults with it though:1 - The English subtitles are full of errors. Most of them are completely inexcusable. "English Adaptation" should never be misspelled by ZRO LIMIT PRODUCTIONS given that they've been writing that for the last decade. There's at least 1/2 dozen stupid spelling errors that could have been caught if they simply proofread.2 - Redubbed SOTN with weak voice actors. Yes, I understand the redubbing of the intro to make Rondo and SOTN have the same dialogue. But the rest of the game should have been untouched. Yes, people said that the writing was goofy, but I think it was actually more fitting for the time period rather than the new text. And the voice actors missed important cues to get the feeling across. Like when Alucard meets Maria for the first time, he's suppose to pause (as a hesitation) before giving out his name. Simple stuff like this was missed.3 - The port's cutscenes (most notably the intro) don't run properly compared to the original.4 - Instead of that Peke game, they should have put in Castlevania: Dracula X in instead (or put everything in). That would have created a full Castlevania X experience. Yes, the SNES game stank, but it would have made for a interesting comparison for people who have never played it. And there was plenty of room left over on the UMD to stuff it in.But the remake is so much fun to play. :D
