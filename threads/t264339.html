Please read the rules of this section before posting. Failure to abide by these rules will likely result in point deductions, warns, or both.

First of all, keep in mind that the Multi-Console Gaming forum is not the place to talk about anything related to gaming, but instead should be used to hold gaming discussions that do not fit into other console-specific sections located on the board. Topics about multiple consoles, multi-platform games, and cross-platform series should make up the bulk of the topics here, as the forum description indicates. To prevent spamming, just make sure you're following the rules listed below and report topics/posts that break these rules.

Do NOT Post the Following:
Do not post topics about one specific game that is for one specific gaming system. You can make a topic for it in one of the other gaming forums designated for its system.
Do not waste your time asking for cheats or making a topic about cheats. You can go to GameFAQs, Gameshark's site, CodeBreaker's site, or other such places for cheats and in-game help.
Do not ask for release dates of games. GameFAQs, IGN, Gamespot, and numerous other sources have that information.
Do not ask about emulators, ROMs, or anything in that field. Every other gaming section has areas designated for this type of discussion, so use them. Here are some topics to refer to if you want emulators or help with specific emulators:

Stop Asking for the Following (has an emulator list at the bottom)
GBA Emulation FAQ
NDS Emulation FAQ
Playstation Emulation Info
N64 Emulation Guide
Classic Console Emulation Guide
MAME User's Guide
Other help and discussion on emulation can be found in the sections where those topics are located.
Do not ask for help in any game. Again, that's what GameFAQs is for.
Do not create duplicate topics, of course, as stated in the main Forum Rules. To prevent this, you can either use the search function or take a look at the list of popular topics in this forum.

Keep in mind that all other universal forum rules apply here, too.

We Encourage You to Post the Following:
You can post topics about game series which have games for more multiple gaming systems (as long as all of the systems don't fit into another specific forum).
You can post topics about a single game that can be played on multiple systems, as long as you are talking about the game in general and not one specific version.
You can post topics about general gaming news that does not involve a single platform.
You can post topics about general gaming strategy, general discussion of a genre, or favorite games within a specific genre.
You can post topics comparing different gaming companies and their games, as long as you do not violate the "random versus topic" rule from the main forum rules.


Remember, you can only make a new topic if it has not been done before.
