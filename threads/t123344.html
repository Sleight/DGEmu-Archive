Thanks.Notes about the Atari 2600 screens:1. Unlike other systems, many Atari 2600 games lack a main title screen and therefore many screenshots will only contain in-game only.2. The Atari 2600 seems to be capable of about 4 different resolutions. However, I will use 320x250 sized screenshots for all roms (except the few that may require the larger sized 320x305 in which case I will scale down accordingly) to avoid images getting cut off.3. Many Atari games try to circumvent the sprite limitation by altering sprites between frames. This effect seems to work well with minimal flicker, but makes it very difficult to screenshot since there's no possibly way to screenshot 2 frames at once. Therefore, some screenshots may seem to be missing stuff and there is little I can do. However, for asteroids though, it altered between your ship and the asteroids so I could easily super impose the ship in the correct spot.
