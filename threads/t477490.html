KKirie MotobaAug 16 2012, 07:50 PM
None really.
I just follow the manufacturer's sites to get the latest drivers.

There is a very nifty tool called Ma-Config that scans your computers for all your drivers and tries to find the latest WHQL approved drivers, but the site to download those drivers is French only if I recall.
If you want to try anyways:
The software: http://www.ma-config.com/en
The site to treat the resutls: http://www.touslesdrivers.com/

If you want some lists:

For auto detect Intel integrated graphics, chipset, and ethernet: http://www.intel.com/p/en_US/support/detect?iid=dc_iduu
For AMD graphics drivers and chipsets: http://support.amd.com/us/Pages/AMDSupportHub.aspx
For nVidia graphics and chipsets: http://www.nvidia.com/Download/index.aspx?lang=en-us
Realtek audio and ethernet drivers: http://www.realtek.com.tw/downloads/

There's many more for component hardware drivers.

If you're looking for manufacturer based laptops and desktops (HP, Acer, Toshiba, Dell, Samsung, etc), each manufacturer's sites have their drivers, and some of them have proprietary modifications for their models (ie.: HP's nVidia chipset drivers for Pavilion dv9000 series are not the same as the ones on nVidia's official website).
