I haven't heard anybody talking about whether or not 3d works with your condition, don't you have a game shop near you with a 3ds demo stand to test it yourself? The 3ds stands are nearly in every game store I go (usually even two). Personally it barely works for me, I have average eyesight and it seems that I need to keep the screen ridiculously far to let the 3d take effect, even after adjusting the 3d focal point and the distance of the 3ds itself. If it is adjusted wrong by the tiniest bit, i will see a double image.edit: After a bit of googling, it seems that it differs on a per person basis, even for people with keratoconus. You might get lucky and see 3d, or you will see ghosting. you can however play 3ds games in 2d, the 3d is not necessary at all.
