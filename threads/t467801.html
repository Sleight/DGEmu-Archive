Before it became bastardized with a really poor hentai game, Valis was a Castlevania knock-off for the Sega Genesis that featured awkward and really bad play control, uninspired music and an extreme challenge due to lack of being able to do anything correctly (see: #$%@ play control). It starred a female student who becomes a scantily-clad woman warrior who is dragged into a fantasy world to rescue her real life friend/rival and stop an evil king bent on pilfering the world's life energy. It plays very similarly to Castlevania (only again, it's really shoddy) and actually has some cool character designs. Unfortunately it's very anime-y, right down to the cutscenes, but if you can look past this, you can find a decently entertaining story. The play control will outright kill the game for veteran gamers, yet I wanted to bring this series up because it brought back some memories of ultra-hard games (also see: Battletoads lolol). The series spawned three sequels (in the second game, heroine Yuko must battle a new evil king who's attempt to null the previous badguy's plans end up making him go evil, and in the third game, a dark force from another reality invades fantasy land), and then the controversial hentai game that for many fans, killed the series. Well I'm not talking about that POS, I'm talking about the original Sega versions of it.I have no "good" memories of this poor game series, but memories nonetheless, and I wanted to know if anyone else recalls/heard of this series that forever remained in the shadows.
