Actually, I don't think the older Pokemon games work with VBALink. The way the older gameboys link is not supported by the VBALink. You'll have to get TGB Dual, which is the only GameBoy emulator that will emulate the old link cable. It only works with Windows or DOS. You can find it on Zophar.netI'm not sure if your VBA saves will work with TGB Dual. I think they should, but if you encounter any problems, do some searching on the internet and you may find more help on the subject. I don't have any more direct experience to share.
