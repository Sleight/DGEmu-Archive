Basically all you will need (this is being literal) is:

-A computer
-7zip or a program that can extract compressed files
-A Micro SD card
-A MicroSD to USB card reader or any device that will allow you to connect your microSD to your comp (usually included when you buy a card, or you can buy a cheap one from Dealextreme, PM for details)
-GPs I guess to download ROMs
-Flaschcart System files aka Firmware
-A Nintendo DS

That should be about it.

-7zip = http://www.7-zip.org/download.html
- Micro SD cards and various readers = http://dx.com/s/micro+sd+32gb (some flashcarts will do bundles where they sell you the cart and a mSD which may be cheaper)
- firmware = http://www.linfoxdom...om/nintendo/ds/ (you simply find the firmware for your cart, extract it then put the SYSTEM folder on the root of the microSD. Also on the root make afolder called ROMs which is where your roms will go)

if you want more info make a thread and other people will help. don't rely on me because I haven't kept up with emulation info in years.
