OOpulent MaelstromApr 23 2007, 07:55 PM
The low framerate in the game is in most cases due to a limitation in the emulator. That's why having a dual-core CPU won't make much difference in speed, because No$ doesn't support it yet and only uses one core. In worst cases it even slows it down.That's also why there's nothing significant you can do to improve your speed. You can change your hardware but the improvement will still be random. Even lowering the emulation settings won't speed up things considerably and clearing up processes in my experience is useless. Most likely if you have a powerful CPU it will run comfortably, but it's not guaranteed.Unless you're in luck and the game constantly runs at a comfortable speed, all you can do is wait for the next version of the emulator, and hope it speeds up things, or of course buy a DS and the game, or a flash card. All things considered, if you don't want all the hassle the DS isn't that expensive and well worth buying.
