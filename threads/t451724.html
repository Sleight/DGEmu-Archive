Donkey Kong 64 was alright, but it didn't reach the awesomeness of the SNES games. Each stage had 25 Special Golden Bananas, 500 ordinary bananas and a massive amount of other things.

 

I never beat the game. I got up to Hideout Helm, which is the last stage, but I never felt like beating it. It was madness, and there was that one Minigame that always sucked ass for me. I hated that minigame with my whole entire soul. Then they forced you to beat the original Donkey Kong, which I must admit was rather fun. You had to do it twice too. First you had to beat the game, and then you had to go and beat the high score. That would get you some kind of Nintendo coin or the Rareware coin. Then of course you had to play Jetpac, which was really fun. Shooting aliens and meteors while building a space ship and getting the fuel.
