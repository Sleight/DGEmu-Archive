To answer TAM. Einhander is squaresoft's entry into the shooter gender. Unlike other shooter though, enihander boasts in it's 3 dimensional environment which emerges the player deep into the game and will feel like playing a 3-d flight simulation game. The game mechanics in this shooter is what fans love about the game. Your einhander has a standard gun which never runs out and the various weapons which you collect all over the game. You choose which einhander type you will use. There are three of them and each are for different player's preference. The einhander mk-1 carries two weapons (machineguns, cannons, spreader) which can be used simultaniously, preferred by players who wants more fire power at the same time. The einhander mk-2 carres three weapons, this einhander can only fire the selected weapon and the standard gun at the same time, this is preferred by players who wants to save the heavy firepower to boss battles. And finally the einhander mk-3 which only carries one weapon but can fire a double standard gun, preferred by players who doesn't rely on weapons. I really can't tell you how enjoyable this game is in detail, but I assure you that no other shooter has ever rivaled this one back in the PS1 era.
