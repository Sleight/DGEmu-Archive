What is it?
The DP loan system is a way of allowing members to download larger roms without needing to donate or make hundreds of posts on the forum. What the system does is give you a large global DP reset on a specified date in exchange for your global DPs in the following days for a specified period of time.

Who can use it?
Currently, the system is restricted to users with at least 50 posts due to possible abuse by members with multiple accounts. People who have donated at least $5.00 to the site also have access, regardless of post count. Banned and validating members cannot use the system, as they are not allowed to download roms at all.

How does it work?
First, visit the DP loan page: http://www.dgemu.com/dploan.html.
On the page, you can see your conversion rate, which represents the number of additional global DPs you can get for each day that you give up in exchange for the loan. Members with negative warning levels will have higher conversion rates while members with positive warning levels will have lower ones. The conversion rate is calculated as follows:Conversion rate = 7 - { warning level / 20 }

For example, my conversion rate is 12 DPs/day.
This means that if I take out a loan for 10 days, I will have 12 * 10 = 120 more global DPs than usual after the next global DP reset. After the following reset, my global DPs will drop to 0 until the restore date listed on that page. So this is what my global DPs would look like:

As you can see, I gave up 10 day's worth of global DPs in order to get 220 now. Even though I won't be able to download using global DPs until March 24, I can download 220 MB worth of roms on the 13th.

Please note that you can only have one loan at a time. Also, all times are GMT+2, so your local date may be different from the ones shown on the page. All DP resets take place at midnight GMT+2.
If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us. Enjoy!

Reviews FAQ
Q: How do I write a Review?
A: From the details page of every game click .
Look to the left and you will see a "Post Review" section. Click where is says "Click here" and begin your review. Please be sure to delete the "Write your review here.." text first or it'll appear in your review. When you're done, rate the game below and then simply click submit to send it for inspection

Q: Oops! I made an error. Is there any way to fix/edit my review?
A: Not at the moment. Maybe a future version will allow review inspectors to edit reviews and correct errors for you. Do not worry, nobody demands perfection so if it is a decent review, it will still be accepted.

Q: What is in it for me?
A: If you write a highly exceptional review, you will receive 10 DPs for your efforts. If you don't get DPs, then your only reward is the satisfaction of contributing something to DGEmu.

Q: How will I know if my review is approved?
A: Simple. It'll then appear in the reviews section of that particular game. Notice that you will not be informed on whether your review has been approved so check back periodically (typically if your review is not present after 48 hours, then it was probably declined).

Q: How can I check my review after submission?
A: If your review is accepted it'll appear amongst the others for that particular game. The only way to see if it's there is to navigate to the review page. Currently there is no system to track your reviews.

Q: My review was approved, but I did not get any DPs, why?
A: Not all approved reviews will earn you 10 DPs. As briefly mentioned above, only those which are of a highly professional standards are worthy of reward.

Q: I lost Dps!! :D
A: Both plagiarism and spamming will not be tolerated. If our review inspectors find out that you've copied from an official review site like GameFaqs or see you just sent garbage, your review will not only get declined, but you will be penalized 10 DPs as well. Please only submit original and meaningful material.

NOTE: If you created a review for another site that you then wish to submit here as well, it may be best to mention this at the top of review so you are not mistakenly accused of plagiarism.

Q: If writing reviews copied from places like Gamefaqs is not allowed, then why do I see some there?
A: Either they were missed or they were submitted before that rule was made.

Q: Is there a limit to the total number of DPs I can earn from submitting reviews?
A: No. As long as your reviews are legitimate, written by you, and of high quality, you may earn 10 DPs for every review you submit, regardless of how many you have submitted in total. Note that any spamming or plagiarizing (as interpreted by the review inspectors) will earn you a deduction for each review falling under those categories.

Q: Who are the review inspectors?
A: Anyone who is support staff or higher as well as a few selected members can inspect your reviews. Do not badger them if you think that you deserved a reward, but did not receive it because there is no way for anybody to know who saw your review. If however, you have other types of questions, then feel free to ask (but most of them should have already been answered in this guide)

Q: So how do I write a review that is guaranteed to earn me DPs?
A: Please read the following guide: (written by ling99rp):Tutorial for making a review that is of quality.**First of all, I'd like to re-emphasize that your review must be written by you. Bad Roms
Okay... so you've downloaded a rom that doesn't work. Bugger. You wasted DPs on it.

Problem: Trying to open the download box thingy, doesn't work, you click "Back", and lose your DPs
Solution: If this happens all the time, use a different browser. Mozilla, Firefox, IE6 work all fine.

Problem: You've downloaded it, except the .zip file says the archive is corrupted.
Explanation: Most likely your downloading process was interrupted and so therefore at some point, your download stopped and said it was done or you unchecked a box in your download so now the download disappears completely in the void of your hard drive never to be seen again.
Solution: Nothing to do but try again.

Problem: You accidently unchecked the box in your download window and now the familiar save box doesnt pop up. (Can't find where your file downloaded to)
Solution: Simply goto start menu if you have Win XP then settings and then control panel. While in control panel look for folder options. You may need to set control panel to classic mode to find it. In folder options click the filetypes tab and search for the program that opens your zip files. Click the advanced button and look for an unmarked box that says "confirm open after download" mark that box. Then click the OK button of course and then the close button. Now when you download it should once again bring up that oh so familiar box. - Credit to Mörder

Problem: Download didnt work and your DPs gone!
Solution: Just try again.

Problem: Downloaded the file, but theres nothing in it!Explanation: No idea. It works for the rest of us, why not you?
Solution: If you download the file again, have the same problem, try downloading another version of it. Eg. When theres a US version, theres usually a European version that supports UK-style English.

Problem: When solutions dont workExplanation: Most likely is something to do with the rom or site itself.
Solution: Report it! (In comments and suggestions)
Problem: While running a GBA Video ROM the screen turns all black during the video.
Explanation: You're using an emulator (or a version of one) that does not support GBA videos.
Solution: Download the latest version of VBA or use another emulator.

Problem: While running a Classic NES ROM the emulator says something like "gamepak error reset power".Explanation: It's not a problem actually, the Cassic NES series aren't supposed to work on emulators.
Solution: There might be a crack for the ROM, which you can find here.
