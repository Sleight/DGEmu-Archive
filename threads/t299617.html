Please keep your posts relevant to the topic! That means either submitting an entry or asking a question not already answered in this post! Essentially no exceptions to this rule will be allowed and members who fail to obey by this rule will have points removed!---All right, so in case you didn't know, we're talking about these:----Entry Requirements-Width dimensions: 120 to 170 pixels- Height dimensions: 20 to 40 pixels- The DGEmu Dragon Logo (click to see it) must be used in the submitted image.- Make sure your entry is either in PNG or GIF format (PNG is preferable)- The entry may not be ripped off of someone else or from another forum.- Make sure that you submit images for all of the following staff positions: Administrator, Sentinel, Super Moderator (or Super Mod), Moderator, and Support StaffIf any of the above requirements are not met, you will most likely be disqualified.How to submit your entryYou must submit your entry by posting in this thread. That way we don't need to worry about whether the appropriate staff members see it or not. You are always welcome to edit your post if you update your entries. You may also edit your post if your entry does not fit the qualifications if the contest has not ended already. It would be easier for everyone if you use the img tags to show us all of your images in the post, like this:However, we are not going to complain too much if you just put the image's url in the post.How to WinUnlike some previous graphics contests, there will be no poll for members to vote in. A panel of staff members will decide which ones should be considered and which set will be used, if any. Of course, the staff reserves the right not to use any of the submissions. If your set is used, then you win the grand prize. If your set is considered, it is also eligible for a secondary prize.PrizesWinner: 5000 GP + 25 dedicated DP of choice; if you do not want GP/DP, alternative prizes may also be discussed (extra PM storage space and things of the like)Secondary prizes: anywhere from 500 to 1500 GP may be given to anyone else being considered.The submission deadline is now August 25th.Good luck, everyone! We look forward to your entries. Have fun! ^_^

Breezy`' post='3172324 wrote on Aug 7 2007, 03:03 PM:
Is there any version of the DGEmu logo with a transparent background?
If you are using Photoshop there is no need for one with a transparent BG (assuming that you are using Photoshop).What you could do if you're using Photoshop (I'm using Photoshop CS2 but it can also work with Photoshop 7.0) is:1. open the file2. change the file to RGB by going to Image >> Mode>> RGB2. make a new layer3. there should be a Channels tab. If there is not you can view the tab by going to windows and choosing Channels4. in the Channels tab you should be four channel layers: RGB, Red, Green, Blue5. click on the Blue channel layer6. at the bottom the the Channels tab window there should be small icons at the bottom, one looking like a circle; click this one.7. go back to your regular layers tab and choose the layer you created8. click Select >> Inverse9. click on the Paint Bucket tool and fill in the selected area with black10. go to the first layer and delete the layer. You should have the logo on a transparent layer now.I hope that helps. :3--------------------------------------------------------I would like to share my two cents!!
