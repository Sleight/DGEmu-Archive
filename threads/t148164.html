DdylenSep 02 2007, 12:56 PM
You can get decent cards like Dark Magician (2500/2100), Gaia the Fierce (2300/2000), Skull Knight (2650/2150) and Meteor B. Dragon (3500/2000) from the Low Meadow Mage by getting S rank using a power strategy, which just means beating your opponent in 5 or less turns. Also If you're really having trouble you can duel Pegasus for the equip card Megamorph which powers any monster card up by 1000 atk and 1000 def, but this involves defeating him using a tech strategy, the easiest way of doing this is dueling until he has as little remaining cards in his deck as possible and then beat him, once he is down to around 1 card in his deck you have a good chance of getting a tech S rank. Beating Pegasus with a power S rank can win you a bright castle which can increase the power of any monster by 500 atk and 500 def.
