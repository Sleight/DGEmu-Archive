DDoctor CthulhuforaheadJan 15 2007, 03:05 AM
Most underrated game ever is a pretty huge amount of stuff to sort through, with no real, definitive answer, but a few games that really strike me as hugely underrated...Crystalis. Someone mentioned it earlier, and I agree completely. If SMB3 isn't the best game on the NES, then Crystalis is. It takes the Zelda formula and does it ten times better than it's ever been done before and, maybe even ever since. Easily the best RPG on the NES, easily.Plok. Three mentions in one topic. That's probably about half the people who've ever even played Plok. Very fun and unique game. It takes the standard platformer formula and, like Crystalis, does better than it's even been done before. ...Outside of a Mario or Kirby game, anyway. It's great, but it may not be SMB3 or Kirby Superstar great. Still, tons of fun.Tales of Destiny. Not Tales of Eternia (the US Tales of Destiny II), but rather, the first Tales of Destiny, for the PSX. The best game in the Tales series by a long shot, and, in my opinion, the unheralded gem of the PSX library. ToD crams more personality into a bunch of squat little chibi sprites than ToS could manage with bigger, CG characters, fully voiced cutscenes, and multiple disks.Space Megaforce. No other game on the SNES makes use of its capabilities like Space Megaforce. Most shmups on the SNES are nearly unbearable from the slowdown, but Space Megaforce, despite being the most chaotic game on the system (we're talking more than Gunstar Heroes here), as well as some of the prettiest graphics on it, has no slowdown at all.
