New Vegas has more options for particular builds, it seems. For instance, you could do a whole unarmed/melee build, with perks specific to that type of gameplay. You also get perks only like once every two levels.That said, strategies for Fallout 3 work pretty much as well. For that sort of build, I suppose you'd want a nice amount of agility, perception and intelligence over strength and endurance or something (though intelligence is always a nice one to boost anyway because it determines how many stat points you get each level). There's no bobbleheads in this one, but there are books (and the perk that boosts how many points books give you). There are also magazines that give you +20 or whatever in a stat for a limited amount of time, so it's useful to carry a bunch of those and just get stats up to 80.
