KKvotheFeb 10 2008, 04:41 AM
This is an abstract piece that I feel represents the emotional turmoil one experiences when forced to deal with the constantly shifting paradigms that modern urban life invariably brings about. The somewhat faded colours give the image a deceptively soft and amalgamated feel, which when contrasted with the asymmetry brought about by the straight, even lines gives the beholder an impartial view of the chaos and cultural clash that are so rampant all across today's world. Moreover, the increasing complexity and intricacy of the lines toward the center of the piece, as emphasised by the dichromatic 'frame' that invites the eye to delve further into the illustrious depths of the apparent chaos depicted by the image -- further accented by the gradual increase in intensity of the colours, culminating in a shade of red that is not quite unlike that of dried and congealed blood, which causes the beholder to contemplate further about what it might represent -- firmly brings the viewer along by the hand and forces him or her to consider more thoroughly the inherent disharmony of our environment.I hope you appreciate what I have created here.It's not often that ones such as you are graced with such artistic perfection.
