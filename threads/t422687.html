Well let us see here then. The theme is a bit of a tricky one.I guess I'll see if this WIP will work or not(likely it will never get finished, it has been shelved for nearly 40 hours now).When I saw the theme this signature instantly came to mind.First I need to explain the series it is from a bit. Pumpkin Scissors is a war drama. To be more precise it takes place in the aftermath of a war that has pretty much left society in ruins. Alice is the 2nd Lieutenant that leads missions for the 'War Relief' unit. The job is to basically hand out rations, fix transportation paths, and, pushed particularly hard by Alice, to uncover and punish corruption that is harming the War Relief efforts.Second to explain the symbolism. The wings are obviously to mimic the 'angelic' imagery to impress upon one her 'nobility'. However, at the same time the ruined city in the background represents that no matter how hard she tries, she can not save everyone. In addition her efforts to be 'noble' drive her away from everyone. The rest of the quite above her name goes along the lines "To be fair she cannot take any sides, and thus, no one can take her side". It then goes into the noble bit. I am bungling it a bit. But basically it goes, she constantly strives to be more, however, in the end she is still human and must rely on others to at least some extent. That is what the coat over her dress symbolizes. That is the coat of the Corporal of the Pumpkin Scissors. It plays an important part in the final battle scene of the anime. Long story short, no matter how hard she tries she is still human. The harder she tries to be something more the more lonely she becomes.Maybe not 'Human' in eyes of others, but when I saw the theme this is what came to my mind. Of course I have the advantage of having watched the series and having read the manga(which is strongly what I built the signature off, people without that advantage may not be able to see what I see).
