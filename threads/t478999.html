Not sure if this is the right forum for this but I will post it here until told otherwise. 

 

 

           So, with the news of a new Star Wars movie coming out in the near future, my head and heart are a-buzz with a cornicopia of emotional responses. While the idea of a new movie coming out does get my nerd genes pumping into overdrive, the news that it will be the first Star Wars without the touch of George Lucas finds me a tad bit worried and, honestly, more than scared.

 

          If that isn't enough to make the die hard Star Wars fans question the new film then the fact that Star Wars is now owned by Disney should. Now granted, Disney did a so-so job with Marvel (in my opinion Kevin Smith should have kept it for himself), but what worries me is this: Will the heart and soul of Star Wars be lost in this Disney-fuel mania to suck up all forms of media entertainment(sp?) or will it still be the same far, far away galaxy that we all know and love?

 

         I leave it up to you to decide.
