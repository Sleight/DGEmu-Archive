KKHRSJan 04 2014, 08:54 AM

Ok so I think I'm done with Terraria now. Like literally, I'm done, I've got and done most of the stuff in the game not counting some optional stuff and super rare drops. Overall it was really fun. I'm not a fan of the whole minecraft make houses shit so I'm glad that the building aspect of the game was optional and yet there's a good enough reason to dedicate some time to it. I focused on the RPG elements of it like fighting the bosses and working on my DPS rather than the free world. Everyone says it's 2-D minecraft but it's really nothing like that, it's basically an action RPG with randomly generated worlds that you have free control over. Really only the very beginning of the game is heavy on building and exploration. Once you've gotten a good amount of stuff you're ready to fight your first boss and then from there your progression is mainly based around the different bosses in the game.

 

I'd go as far as recommending it to anyone interested in a really good, cheap action RPG. It's a really fun and relatively short game with little to no replay value. If you REALLY like the game you can replay it with another new character but it'll just be the same objectives but just in a randomly generated world (which the worlds really aren't. All maps follow a strict format for generation, there's just a minimal amount of differences). I'd go as far as saying that this is similar to a metroidvania type game. While there's nothing other than bosses preventing you from moving on plot/story-wise, a lot of exploration is based on finding certain power ups (i.e. grappling hooks, wings, lava walking boots, etc.) and slight backtracking through the world you've developed.
