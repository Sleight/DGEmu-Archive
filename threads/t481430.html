KKirie MotobaDec 03 2014, 12:23 AM

Tons of decent displays out there.

 

Basics to consider:

- 1920x1080 display

- 5ms response time or lower

- LED display (or whatever equivalent that Samsung loves to give that basically means LED)

- DVI connector or HDMI

 

Nice additions:

- IPS display (nicer viewing angles)

- Adjustable stand (in height, tiltable, those are extremely nice for shmups)

- > 1920x1080 resolution (usually expensive as hell, but those give out nicest output)

- USB hub (sometimes convenient to have USB ports on the screen)

 

Useless features:

- Integrated speakers (holy shit, not only is the sound suck ass, but it adds extra weight to your screen)

- DisplayPort (unless your video card supports this dynamically, DisplayPort is not needed in nearly all instances)

KKirie MotobaDec 03 2014, 02:05 AM

VGA, analog output is quite fuzzy especially when trying to game. Any digital signal is clearer, so DVI, HDMI, or DisplayPort. The best out of all is DisplayPort (large data bandwidth, can send nearly any kind of resolution and signal), but it's not widely used (ATI and I think nVidia uses those for like 3 screen setups and more). If you were working in graphical processing (Look up ATI FireGL graphics cards, those are not for gaming), they usually rely on DVI and DisplayPorts if I recall.

 

USB ports on monitor is quite useful, especially for those that tend to hide away their desktop computer in a corner. Lots of people just pop their USB stick right there to transfer data, it's also nice to plug wireless receptors for mouse and keyboard for a closer range signal. I personally don't have those, but I've seen a lot of them put to good use. But as I said, nice to have, not really necessary.

 

Also, for the response time, I have 5ms 60hz screens, and they don't bother me at all when gaming.

Go for this if you can: http://www.ncix.com/detail/asus-ve247h-23-6in-widescreen-led-b6-57310-1029.htm

Same model, no audio: http://www.ncix.com/detail/asus-vs247h-p-23-6in-widescreen-led-20-64582-1029.htm

 

Yes, it has stupid integrated speakers, but they don't inflate the price for them at least.

I have 2 Asus 5ms LCD monitors and 1 Asus 5ms LED monitor as a triple monitor setup, and I've yet to have any issues with any gaming and whatsoever.
