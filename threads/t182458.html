NDS Flashcarts FAQ

Before opening a thread asking anything about Flashcarts or problems with them, please read the following and see if your question is answered. Any member making a post/thread which content is covered here will receive a WARN.
(Also read GBATemp's Flashcart FAQ, it's pretty good.)

Q: What is a flashcart?
A: Flashcarts are devices that are designed to play ROMs or run homebrew programs on the DS.

Q: Are there different types of flashcarts?
A: Yes they are basically two types of flashcarts: slot 1 and slot 2. Slot 1 flashcarts go into the slot for DS games and Slot 2 flashcarts goes in the slot for GBA games or DS option packs (Rumble, RAM, Camera etc.).
Most Slot 2 flashcarts are now tremendously outdated, with all current updated flashcarts which play DS ROMs being Slot-1s.

Q: Do Flashcarts work for multiplayer or Wifi?
A: Yes.

Q: Do they work on the 3DS?
A: Yes, some of them do. Please refer to our 3DS Emulation FAQ for more info.

Q: How do I use this flashcart?
A: Please refer to our flashcarts user guides for more information.

Q: Can I use cheats with flashcarts?
A: Yes some flashcarts have cheat functions built-in to the firmware, while others like slot 2 flashcarts might need to use dipstar to cheat.To get the latest Cheat database check this page: http://cheats.gbatemp.net/

Q: Where can I get the latest firmware for my flashcart?
A: This site should be up to date most of the time and has the firmware for lots of flashcarts and other files of interest.

Q: I used to play on a different flashcart or emulator, can I transfer my save? How?
A: First you need to convert the save files. You can use either Shunyweb's or UniqueGeek's converter.Then make sure the converted save file matches the requirements for your new flashcart (file name and location).
Use this topic for assistance if required: NDS SaveGame Converter Guide

Q: I don't have enough space for more roms, is there a way to squeeze more in?
A: You can use a roms trimmer to reduce the size of roms to get some more space, each flashcart most probably uses its own type of rom trimmer.

Q: How can I play GBA roms with a slot 1 flashcart?
A: Some flashcarts can emulate GBA ROMs on their own. For other slot 1 flashcarts use the Ezflash 3in1 Expansion pack to play GBA roms. If your flashcart isn't the EzFlash brand then you'll need GBA ExpLoader to get it to run. Some other flashcarts like the M3 REAL come with a GBA pack which you'll need to play GBA roms.
More info: GBATemp's Guide to Running GBA ROMs on the DS

Q: Somes games on the DS can unlocked extra things by having a GBA game in it, can flashcarts also get the extras?A: Yes although it may not work for all flashcarts. For slot 2 flashcarts, some of them like the M3 Perfect can load a GBA rom and then start a NDS rom so that the features are unlocked. For slot 2 flashcarts however if you are using the EzFlash 3in1, you'll need to have the GBA rom in NOR mode, more details here. For the M3 REAL you can do so if your read here under the GBA linkage section.

Q: Can I play NES, SNES, GBC etc roms with a flashcart?
A: Yes, although not all emulators are perfect. See Here for more information, or here for a more comprehensive list.

Q: Can I play music and videos on my DS?
A: Yes. To do so you need a homebrew program. The most popular one is Moonshell, which can read text files, open up images and play mp3 and converted video files. To convert video files read this topic: [FLASHCART] Converting videos for Moonshell

Q: Roms freeze a lot, or else they play very slowly. How can I fix this?
A: If you're sure it's not anti-piracy (i.e. the flashcart can play the roms fine), or if this happens on all roms even after updating your flashcart's OS ("firmware"), you can try reformatting the SD card. Backup the contents of your SD card to a separate location, use the official formatter to reformat it, then put them back.

Difference between Slot 1 and Slot 2
Slot 1 flashcarts goes into Slot 1 (where DS games are plugged into) of the DS. These flashcarts don't require any bypass (Passcard, Superkey, Flashme, etc.) to run DS roms. Download play for some roms work with Slot 1 flashcarts because the rom is read from Slot 1. The Slot 1 flashcarts however cannot play GBA roms (except for Supercard DSTWO)
Slot 2 flashcarts goes into Slot 2 (where GBA games go and certain DS accessories are plugged into) of the DS. These flashcarts can run without a bypass but will only be able to play GBA roms. If you wish to play NDS roms you will need a bypass. Download play will only work if the receiving DS is flashed with flashme because the rom is read from Slot 2. Slot 2 flashcarts should be able to run on any Gameboy advance system to play GBA roms. (Note: Some Slot-2 carts, such as the M3 Professional will NOT play GBA games.)
**Slot 2 cards which play NDS ROMs are now completely outdated. All current NDS flashcarts are Slot-1 devices. :o
