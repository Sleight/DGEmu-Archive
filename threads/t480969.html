Go for whole grain or high fiber noodles and mix in some meat and veggies with the sauce - I often use leftover taco beef or shredded chicken. Hentai's budget stir-fry pasta (healthy version)

 

1 and 1/2 cup cook pasta (add 1/2 cup more if you like)

1 cup sliced tomatoes

1/2 cup basil (option, though add in for taste)

1/2 cup chopped garlic

1/2 cup chopped onions

1 pinch salt and pepper for taste.

1 tablespoon of cooking oil

1 can of tuna (optional)

 

1. Pour oil onto frying pan and heat it over medium fire. Should take about two - three minutes. Dab or drop a few water onto the oil. If it sizzles, your good to go.

2. Add onions and garlic. Stir it until it becomes brown.

3. add in basil and tomatoes. Stir fry them for two minutes. This is also when you will add tuna if you have it.

4. Add cooked pasta and stir it for about three minutes.

5. Serve. Sprinkle a bit of Parmesan if desired.

 

This is so far one of my versatile recipe. You can add different kinds of vegetables apart from basil and tomatoes.
