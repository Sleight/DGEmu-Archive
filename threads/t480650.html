KKula DiamondApr 02 2014, 07:10 PM

Ok, so I played this enough by now, and I have to say, this is the best stealth system in any game I played. It's MGS3(subsistence)'s style of stealth taken to the next level, you really have to have complete awareness of the environment around you before making any move, at least while playing with the slow-motion thing turned off(I can totally see why that thing was implemented, the game would have been a nightmare for most people without it). My main complain about the stealth is something that would probably be fixed by time the main game comes out, which is the lack of stealth helping gadgets, no AP/heartbeat sensors, cardboard boxes, camouflage suites, masks, etc... You only get the tranq-gun and empty magazines to throw for distractions.

 

The control is so much better and a huge part of why this is the MGS game with the best gameplay, you can do way more CQC shit than ever before without having to hold down 3 or so buttons at once, I also love being able to run(and dive into baddies MGS2/3 style). Control is good enough for an open world game, chest high walls are no longer impossible obstacles, actually, you can climb to the roof of most buildings in the base. I really didn't feel restricted in term of what can and can't be done.
