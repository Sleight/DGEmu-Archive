does anyone know how long the batteries last (recharge cycle) before needing to be replaced?
GGrubbenDec 09 2006, 08:21 AM
They're Li-Ion batteries. It depends a lot on how they're treated.Recharge them every chance you get. Li-Ions do not like to be deep-cycled and will wear out faster if you keep waiting till they're low or dead before charging. They'll also wear out faster if they're exposed to high temperatures, such as being left in a hot car on a sunny day.If they're topped up often and kept cool, they should last about the equivalent of 500 full charges before they wear to the point where they need to be replaced.
