So, I played with it for like 5 minutes and then I quit. Not because I didn't like (I really liked it actually) but because that's all the time it took to cycle through all the features. This thing is so bare bones right now. It's depressing. It works pretty well as a mouse for IE9 on the console, and it's serviceable to move from app to app (it's actually easier to just search for the app you want to click it directly than use the app as a controller to find the app in the crazy new ad-dashboard).

Anyways, it's neat. If you have an android phone and an Xbox, I'd say give it a shot.

It is missing one key feature though. Turning on the Xbox. I don't know why that's so hard, but that would be such a great feature that's not there for some reason. Oh well.
