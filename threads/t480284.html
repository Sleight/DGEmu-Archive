What's the controller you're trying to use?


SysWOW64 is only available if you're running a 64-bit operating system.

 

First thing to do, can you also give me a screenshot of your list of application in Add/Remove Programs?

 

Also, libraries of Visual C++ Redistributables, there are many versions.

I don't remember which one is used by Sonic, but you should just install all of them. Since I do dev in Visual Studio on my comp, all those libraries are installed, probably a reason why I never had this issue...

 

<strike>Visual C++ 2005: http://www.microsoft.com/en-ca/download/details.aspx?id=26347</strike>

<strike>Visual C++ 2008: http://www.microsoft.com/en-ca/download/details.aspx?id=5582</strike>

Visual C++ 2010: http://www.microsoft.com/en-us/download/details.aspx?id=5555

 

<strike>Yes, you need all of them, each set contains their own subset of libraries that may be required for specific needs depending on the games.</strike>

 

Uninstall the Visual C++ 2010 pack you have installed on your system, reboot, and reinstall from the website.

I know Steam installs a copy itself when you install the game, but sometimes, it doesn't do a good job at it.


One last thing that could occur (though I doubt, or else, half your system would be complaining about it), check your system environment variables.

 

Open Start Menu

Right-click "Computer" and click on Properties.

In the new window, click on "Advanced system properties" in the list on the left.

In the new window, click on Environment Variables.

In the new window, at the second section called "System Variables", scroll down to the one called "Path", select it and click Modify.

In the small prompt box, copy the content of "Variable value" into notepad and do a find for "%SystemRoot%\system32" and "C:\Windows\system32".

 

If neither of them show up, you have a huge problem.

To fix it, just go the the end of the value, and add the following without the quotation marks: ";%SystemRoot%\system32"

 

Once done, restart your comp just to be sure it gets updated with the new info, and then try sanic again. Should work now.
