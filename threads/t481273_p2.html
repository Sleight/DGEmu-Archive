The many time on first round as CT, I use either my USP-S or P250, rush mid to tunnel stairs, and when they rush, 1 dink headshot on the first or second passer by, and run away, call out for whole team to rotate.

Usually, when they notice, they slow down the rush a bit and get distracted by me, so our team has time to rotate.

 

The best round I think I had for that was using a Five Seven, kills the first guy with headshot, kills a second guy passing by, lit up a third passer by, no bullets left, ran back down to lower tunns, and switch to knife, guy chases me, takes the corner (and obviously they all suspect the box), I rush out, take a few hits, but managed to stab him, another kill.

Last 2, I left it all to my teammates since by then, I took too much damage.

 

Plan fails badly though if they decide to rush up cat.

 

This really depends on my days though, some days, I do extremely horribly...

Some others, I'm carrying the team.

 

That's like the same with the AWP. Some days, I can hit every target, no matter how much cover they take with a small head poking out, some other days, it takes me like 5 shots just to kill a random running.

 

Also, one trick that works like 80% of the time on DUST II unless they have a really pro AWPer, you hold long by simply camping behind fire car with your cod gun (AUG) and take aim. As they come in, most of the time, they don't notice you, so you can start picking them off. By the time they notice, it's hard to aim at your since you're mostly shielded by car, and fire makes it harder to see you, but not for you to see them when zoomed.

The only weakness to that is that if they come from CAT, you're completely open for an easy headshot, so you need someone watching CAT for you.
