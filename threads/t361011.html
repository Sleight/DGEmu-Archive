There are plenty of "animeish" games out there for the PSP besides the major Naruto, Bleach related games. Here's some that i've played:Class of Heroes: A brand new PSP game with Anime-like Characters and fantasy based. It's a strategy RPG, where you create YOUR OWN team consisting of what race and class you want them to be. They have about 10 different races such as Demon, Fairy, Dragon, Human, Elf, etc. either male or female. There isn't really a deep storyline whoever, the customization of the characters is pretty fun.Popolocrois: Also an RPG with Anime-like scenes and play. I enjoy the story line as well as the animated cut scenes and battle. One of the older RPGs released earlier when the PSP came out, but a good must play!Crimson Gem Saga: I just got this game, but so far so good. Voice acting and beautiful artwork. So far an entertaining battle system, i'm looking forward to playing more.Final Fantasy Tactics: War of the Lions: I'm sure you've all heard of this by now, but if you haven't played it once, you HAVE to try. Amazing story and i love personally love RTS's :]Guilty Gear XX Accent Core: A 2D fighter that is a rare find! However, if you enjoy rock music with amazing looking character designs, you'll enjoy playing this game! As this game series serves as my all time favorite fighter, i am a bit biased but, do try it out! Great mashing fun and quick play! Also available for the PS2!Star Ocean: Second Evolution: First came out on the Playstation and remade on the PSP. One of the most valued of classic RPGs with remade events, scenes and voices. Although the story seems simple at first, it'll turn towards it's underlying story which is amazing for it's time. If you want to get into the classics, this one i would recommended! Project DIVA: A Vocoloid game released only in Japan, but if you can import it, it's a simple and great beat game. Unlike games such as DJ Max, all the music is Vocoloid related and is 10x more simple than most beat games. Another quick and fun game.Puyo Pop Fever: A puzzle game where you drop Puyo's ontop of one another and make long chains! An anime-ish game with adorable voice overs and characters. Quick and very simple to learn game AND in english!
