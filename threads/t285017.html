i discovered a glitch in Legend of Zelda, Link's Awakening. the first dungeon where you fight the Moldorm (big wormy guy >.>) and he knocks you down a hole, you fall into a chamber and have to climb back out and start over again. the hole at the very top right corner of the screen, fall into it. when the screen changes to the dungeon where you fall, immediately jump back up! youll come back to the moldorms lair, and it will be all glitchy. you can walk on walls, but some floor will be water or grass or marsh. be careful where you walk, and save BEFORE doing this glitch. the very first chest you open will be the power bracelet, and all the rest will be the power bracelet lv2. you keep walking and will eventually get to glitched versions of all the dungeons, except for the 7th and up. if you go down or up stairs, you will fix the glitch and be in the dungeon you were in when you went to the stairs. (ae, if you go in the stairs near the flippers in the water dungeon, you will go down those stairs in the normal water dungeon and go get the flippers)curious to see if anyone else saw this! i think its a back door for the game programmers. the following pics should help guide you through the first part, to get the flippers so that you wont get too stuck. NOTE: follow the arrow EXACTLY to the square, or you will get stuck somewhere. first, jump into the top right corner hole. you will fall into the dungeon, jump just as the screen is changing. it might take some practice to get the timing right. once you have it, youre in!some enemies are still there, and can still hurt you/be killed by you. theys all glitchy too ^^;some areas have nothing at all, but be careful! if you fall into this square, you wont be able to get back out D:follow the bottom arrow and walk along the statues, and get the first bracelet. then follow the top arrow to the next screen.more bracelet! and glitchy tektites! <_< defeat the enemies here, it should be easy as they cant move much. then go into the stairs to fix the glitch, and successfully teleport to the water dungeon.you will be on a ladder, just go right back up.stoopid gels >3 *slashey slash*walk in front of the hardhat beetles so that they run after you, and right into the water *kerplunk*>.> thars a gel in thah thar chest!backtrack until you are all the way back here, where the locked door was. use the key on this door.this boss is easy, just stand in this one spot and hit him when he goes up to the top, hell turn around and just before he touches the bottom hit him again. hell go up again, and you repeat the process till he dies.Hurry! those blocks close fast! go the red arrow route and you should miss the fizzy.Huzzah! now go back home and try again, see where else it takes you!EDIT: i think it only works in the original, not DX but i never got to play DX so im not sure if they fixed it in that one.
