RraindreamsNov 16 2006, 07:28 AM
Each battle system has its own pros and cons, in my opinion. Just examining two:Real time action- Pros: It makes battles a lot more exciting when you have complete control. Also, it feels good when you beat all the enemies very quickly or take 0 damage during the fight because it's your accomplishment, not the RNG's. It lends a more realistic feel to the game and causes you to learn new strategies to help you get through. It's fun to see which ideas work and which don't, and boss fights are way more fun. Cons: While fun at first, this battle system makes random battles tiresome very quickly because real time action requires a lot more work than most other systems. When you are navigating those convoluted final dungeons where a mistake can cost you several more minutes of effort to get back where you were (I'm looking at you, Summon Night), battles under this system can more easily make you lose your place. Also, your wrist starts hurting.Turn-based strategy- Pros: This battle type requires the most thought, and using your brain really pays off. This type usually involves little or no tedious leveling, meaning more of your time is devoted to the task at hand. In multiplayer modes, the person with the most talent wins, rather than the person with the most time. Building your team is a whole strategy in and of itself, taking conditions and your foe into account. Cons: Perfectionists beware- it's often difficult to keep every single character alive for the whole game. If your favorite character dies, or if too many characters die, resetting erases a lot of patient work, and sometimes a level can take you an extremely long time. In other battle systems, a major mistake might just make you lose a lot of hp, which you then just have to heal. In turn-based strategy games, a similar mistake is a lot more costly, often killing the character and then causing a breach in your defenses, endangering your chance of victory.
