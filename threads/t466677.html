I tried to find an article to help explain why some people can remember dreams well and others can't. This one seems to do a good job.Why Can't We Remember Our Dreams?Personally I don't sleep very well. Due to back troubles and frequent heartburn its very rare that I get through more than one sleep cycle in its entirety in one night. This results in a terrible ability to remember the dreams that I do have. When taking medications for coughing, however, I find that I dream quite vividly and remember my dreams fairly well upon waking.Perfects the best tip that I can give if you want to remember your dreams is to try and go over them as soon as you wake up. Lack of transference explains why we try and remember our dreams ten or fifteen minutes after we wake up, but just can't seem to do so.I hope this helps!
