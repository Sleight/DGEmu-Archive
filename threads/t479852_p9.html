Thoughts on the game so far:

-Combos are stupid easy but proration is strict, extended combos do require some timing and knowledge of what works at higher prorations

-Usually you cannot get hard knockdown unless you spend meter, meterless specials often allow air tech. Higher prorations require spending 2 bar on super to get oki.

-You can control movement during neutral jump like DI in smash. Allows for ambiguous crossups

-Trump card (kirifuda) is a pretty interesting mechanic. One of the things it univerally does is allow assist calls with no animation, allows you to setup some continous mixup with a lockdown assist or a covered high/low/crossup with a fast one.

-DPs (impact skill) in the game are pretty sucky, they're actually not DPs and just superarmor moves, you can react to the clash hitstop with your own superarmor move to beat it. Also loses to throws. Reversal trumps cards are reaaaally good though, they are safe on block and do huge damage, but not all chars have them and limited uses.

-Neutral in DFC is actually pretty fun, it has kinda an air footsies game like melty and what assist you choose changes neutral alot. I like it better than Under Night...the combo system definitely pales in comparison though.

-Meter management is also a big part of the game since damage and oki is heavily meter dependant. You get increased meter build for using powerup blast (offensive burst), trump card, or just for moving forward in neutral.
