Do Not Ask when a Game/ROM Will be Released

HhackerxORIGINAL POSTOct 22 2004, 07:17 PM
DGEmu cannot - nor will we enage in attempting to - determine when any game or games will be released.The answer to such queries is simple: They will be released when the manufacturer decides to release them.If you would like to know when a game will become commercially availabe for purchase in cartridge format in your area, check the release date with an online game-merchant, such as Gamestop or EBGames.You can also check release dates of titles for all systems at GameFAQs.com, by entering the games title into the search engine, and clicking the button labelled "Game Information". The release date will be listed in the middle of the concurrent page.Generally, DGEmu acquires a rom for newly released games within one week of it's commercial release; occassionally, we are able to obtain a copy beforehand, through use of kiosk copies - copies released for display and demonstration.As noted above, any member who posts a topic requesting the release date of any game will be given an automatic warning.The only exception to the above noted rule will be if a game had a set release date, and was - for whatever reason - delayed.Failure to read and make note of this topic is not an excuse, and will not excuse the violator from a warning.------------------------------------------------------------------------Edited or Added to By:booyah_boy
