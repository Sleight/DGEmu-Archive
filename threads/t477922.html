Hey there!

As a member of the DGEmu staff, I'm going to try something new by trying something old. During the month of October, I'd like to kick off the first instance of what I hope to be a recurring event: the monthly mixtape.

The setup is simple - each month, I'll post a topic containing a theme. Members can then send me songs to fit that theme, and I (along with a few other judges, most likely) will pick out up to 20 songs to compile a digital "mixtape" that will be released for everyone to download.

Each monthly mixtape will also (probably) be made available in a streaming radio format on the DG website.

So enough rambling, here are the details.


OCTOBER'S THEME: horror

What songs send shivers up your spine? What do you listen to that puts you in a spooky mood for Halloween? The music doesn't have to be Halloween-oriented (i.e. "Monster Mash"), but it certainly can be.

THE RULES:
Each member can submit up to two songs. Any additional songs will be ignored, unless you indicate which of your original submissions you wish to replace.
To submit songs, upload them using a file-sharing service (such as Mediafire) and send the link(s) to me (mouse) via PM. Song files posted as replies to this message will be ignored. (You might also find links to song files through Bee MP3 - be sure to check them first!)
Include a short description in your PM that explains how each song relates to the theme.
If more than 20 songs are submitted, the judges will make the decision on which songs to include and which songs to cut. The judges also reserve the right to cut any song for any reason.
Album art submissions must be 300px by 300px and formatted as JPEG images.

REWARDS:
Submit a song - 50 GP
Submit a chosen song - +200 GP
Submit winning album art design - 1000 GP
Rewards will be given out when the mixtape is completed.

The deadline for song and album art submissions is Friday, Oct. 26, at 9 p.m. EST. SUBMISSIONS ARE CLOSED. The finished album will be released before the end of the month, and the theme for the next mixtape will be announced at the start of the next month.

And that's it! If you have any questions, send me a PM or reply to this topic. Again, we're trying something new here, so this post will be updated as details are worked out.
