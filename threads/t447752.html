Isn't life technically the random movements of atoms and their chemical reactions? If so, isn't everything already planned out? This was the question posed to me by my chemisty teacher who wanted me to think outside the box. If life was already pre-planned, then is the value of choice an illusion? I think not, since we are usually in control of our behaviours and thoughts (unless you're a Freudian hardcore that thinks we're controlled primarily by our unconscious, which I do believe, just to not as great of an extent as a neo-Freudian would.) This prompts the question of if you believe in the realm of the supernatural, which I do to an extent. Now the social stigma associated with the supernatural is ghosts, goblins, witches and the like. I am not referring to the latter, but simply another plane of reality or existence that isn't dictated by the scientific laws that we like to apply to everything. In terms of a the supernatural, I do believe in the presence, or the element, of a soul in our bodies. Life would be to convenient, I think, if we were here to live, procreate if we wished to, and die. I believe that we are here for great purposes and to accomplish great deeds. While our physical realm may be controlled by the factors of phyics, chemistry, biology, and all other proven sciences, I believe that the supernatural plane often allows us to be the charismatic people that we can be. How come we are able to think, create, and actively live our lifestyles? If the world was only governed by the principles of sciences, wouldn't everything run like clockwork? I believe that by utilizing, or we being utilized by, the supernatural plane, we can effectively change the course of history and the future. It's a weird idea, not backed by much support, but it's just a thought. Hear me out.
