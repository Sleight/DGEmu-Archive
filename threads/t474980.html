Ok, it still doesn't have any logging function, but that should be fairly simple to implement.But for now, I want to let you guys test out the utility.It can be found here:http://www.dgemu.com/modutils/staffutils.phpYou must be logged in to access the page (obviously).Usage:1. Plug-in the box some information to search for, choose the type of search you're doing, and click "Query"2. If the member is found, you will be put into "edit" mode where all the functions are available to you.3. To edit the GPs, type in a number (max 5 digits). Note that if you type random characters into the box, they will be omitted. That said: type "wtf450" will be "450", type "2.1" will be "21", etc.4. You can copy paste the URL of a post (top right corner of a post, there's always a "#XXX". When you click on that, the link to the post will be displayed). If the URL is valid, the GP action will be added at the end of the post.5. All the rest of the buttons should be self explanatory... Note that you cannot apply flood control nor ban another staff member, for obvious reasons. :)6. To start working on a new member, click "New", that will put the utility back into search mode and disable the form until you pull up your next member.Known bugs, currently no known fix to them to my knowledge until further research:- When you put and URL to modify, you will notice that once the page reloads, the post appears to be a mashed up BBCode fest instead of being properly formatted. To fix it, just click "Edit" and right away "Save Changes". That will format the post correctly.- When visiting review inspection, when you click on any function to approve or decline a review, it will boot you out of the Staff Utilities. That's an issue related to the way those pages are coded. Unless we review and recode the page, that probably won't be solved.- There's a double scrollbar on the right hand side. Stupid HTML formatting is giving me a huge headache when trying to stretch the content to 100% the height of the browser...- When closing the staff utilities, it brings you back to the main forum page instead of where you were left off in the frame.Things that will be implemented after:- Logging. Currently, any action taken will not be logged.- A theme... It looks butt ugly, but hey, at least it works.- Formats: Left sidebar style (the current one), top sidebar (like old mod utilities), new window.I'll be writing a more thourough guide and properly adding comments into my script afterwards.
