SShine'Jul 23 2010, 11:37 PM
There are many games in the DS that are good, but it depends on what's your style and such...Pokemon, yeah the most basic and best. I got a DS in the first place to play Diamond you know?If you're tired of that or you're done with it, try the Digimon series. It's a little like Pokemon, but you can see a lot of differences. Good for Digimon Fans out there.If you want a classic dungeon crawler, try Summon Knight : Twin Age or Etrian Odyssey. Summon Knight uses the Touch Screen frequently, while Etrian Odyssey tends to hold on the D-Pad and others.For Digital Novels (aka people who like to read), get the Ace Attorney series, one of the greatest out there. Just play it by order, or you'll get lost easily. Time Hollow is a good game also, but it's short. I finished it in less than a day...I think.The World Ends With You is another game you can try. Mostly Touch Screen for the amazing battles and the storyline's good too.For Yu-gi-oh fans or for those interested, try Yu-gi-oh World Championship 2010. It's the latest so far, but if you want something easier, you can try the previous years first before you go to this one.And for music, get EBA, stands for Elite Beat Agents. It's an awesome tapping/rhythm that can get you hooked for hours. And if that's not enough, you can go to the japanese version Osu! Tatakae! Ouendan! and Taiko. You can even go further by playing it in the computer, there's a site to that. :gs:Hm...what else have I tried..?For the usual mini-games whatever, you can play Mario Party for the DS, which would be accompanied by Mario Kart too.Suikoden Tierkreis is also a game I got easily addicted to, but sadly I haven't finished it yet...(it's a Turn-Based RPG)Puzzle fans, grab Professor Layton at this moment, because it's puzzles will keep you trying again and again. The characters are nicely drawn and etc....(wooh, I'm getting lazy...) There are many more games that you can try, but these are just some of the games I've played and loved.
