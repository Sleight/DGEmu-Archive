EEriSubaraApr 17 2012, 02:53 PM
Paco: Despite the fact that the games feature the same 'adventure out and collect Pokemon, battle gyms, and then battle Elite 4' aspect, there are things that change. Mostly, the general storyline of each game that is present amongst the mon collecting. The storyline is something that they've developed and worked on since the first gen. Said first-gen's story plot was rather minimal, but the fifth gen definitely shows that they worked on the storyline to try and make it deeper.

There's also the mechanics, which (though it still contributes to the 'same-ness' as people would say) still help to expand on the games. First gen was incredibly cut and dry -- as well as terribly glitchy and unbalanced, -- but throughout the years, they've added the genders, various game mechanic improvements, and other aspects to improve it.

Yes, it is the same stuff for the most part, but keep in mind that the series is a franchise. In general, most game franchises tend to feel 'same-ish' in the long run when you stop to look at them. Do you notice how many people in the Zelda fandom complain that the franchise is pretty much the same with only small modifications to plot and items? How about people who complain about the Call of Duty franchise? The Mario franchise is mainly Mario having to save Peach from Bowser.

If you look at a game franchise, you can tend to see various similarities with the games in the main franchises themselves. They all have core underlying things that make them be a part of of that franchise, and those things won't change.

If you want different flavors to a franchise, there's always side-games that get made from time to time.
