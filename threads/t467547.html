EEndymion_MallornMay 20 2011, 05:58 AM
Okay, let's start with a few things. Firstly, let's compare between the original Final Fantasy, versus the original Pokemon.In Final Fantasy, you customized your characters before you set out, giving them names and classes. You then take your team through a story which, if followed, presents one of the greatest turnabouts in gaming. It makes you question the nature of heroics if you think about it too long. Your characters are silent cyphers in a world full of people.In Pokemon, your character is without a doubt a blank slate. It's an excuse to keep multiple Pokemon together, and that's really all. There is no significant action by the human character. The Pokemon take center stage.Plus, there's the whole fact that Pokemon is about somewhat mindless acquisition, where Final Fantasy is about character growth and following the story.If I wasn't half-asleep, I could go even farther.
