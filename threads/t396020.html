Use the following format when starting a new topic in the active staff discussions forum. - This will help maintaining organization.1st part of the Title CodeCurrent Month (3 Letters)Month names: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, DecExample ▼
someone wrote:
[Aug][XX][XX][XXX]Topic Title
2nd part of the Title CodeCurrent Day (2 Numbers)It is Obvious.Example ▼
someone wrote:
[Aug][23][XX][XXX]Topic Title
3rd part of the Title CodeCurrent Year (2 Numbers)Again it is Obvious. Example ▼
someone wrote:
[Aug][23][06][XXX]Topic Title
4th Part of the Title CodeDiscussion Category (3 Letters) -Support Staff related discussions -Example, Projects or new ability's for support staff.[GFX] -Graphics Team Related projects. -Example, Projects requiring the use of Gfx team, buttons and such.[RUL] -New Rules or Rule Changes -Self explanatory[Don] -Ads & Donations -Any discussion pertaining to Ads or Donations[Net] -DG-Network -Any discussion having to do with the DG-Network[Evn] -Event Planning -Staff Members hosting events that need authorization use this code. -Scripting Discussions -Need something that requires scripting, give detailed explanations.[Rep] -Reports of Progress and Issues on the site. -Self explanatory[GEN] -General ideas - Have an idea you would like to submit give detailed information.Example ▼
someone wrote:
[Aug][23][06][RUL]Topic Title
5th and last part of the Title CodeDiscussion TitleThis is the most important part of the title codes format. Make sure the title is informative. I cannot stress this enough. We MUST know what the topic of your discussion is about. Example ▼
