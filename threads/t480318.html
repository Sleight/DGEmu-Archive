The week has started, and once again, it's time for another batch of music! This is the before last one, a bunch of anime soundtracks. You may wonder why specifically these 3 animes? The answer is: These 3 animes were the first ones I have watched with Japanese voice overs and English subtitles. These are dating back from my high school days, quite a few years back. Vandread was my very first series, followed by Love Hina, and then Noir. My anime genre preference has long evolved since those days. For now, I'll share with you all my roots. Personal recommendation: the Noir albums. Yuki Kajiura composes great music and never fails to deliver.
