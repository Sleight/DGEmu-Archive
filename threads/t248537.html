KKiririn-shiORIGINAL POSTMay 24 2007, 03:51 AM
It seems that negative warns are sometimes handed too easily by some moderators (inlcuding myself in the past) for reporting. It was mentionned by several members and staff complaining about how easy they got their negs (see Shazzam and Hadel particularly). So why not define a maximum number of negative warns for each type of "good actions" members may do? Here is what I propose for illustration purpose:- 3 negative warns maximum for reporting (1).- 2 negative warns maximum for posting high quality posts (2).- 3 negative warns maximum for being helpful (3).- 1 negative warn maximum for reporting mass flooders (4).- 2 negative warns maximum for writing outstanding guides (5).- 1 negative warn maximum for overall behavior and quality (6).- 1 random negative warn maximum (7).A little explanation on each of the items:(1) Nothing to add except that reporting overly short posts should really be awarded with 1 negative warn maximum.(2) Since most of the posts made in the forum are bordering on spam, it is natural to reward high quality posts. I suggest that these two negative warns should be for members with 500+ and 1000+ posts so that we can judge on the first 500 posts' quality and then on the first 1000's quality. If after 1000, post quality begin to decrease, the possibility of removing this negative warn is also considered.(3) By helpful posts, I mean those who solve non trivial problem such as emulation answers. Answer to people asking how to get DP is not included...(4) Reporting a mass flooder is good but should not be rewarded more than 1 neg.(5) No comment.(6) Should be awarded to members who have been active for a very long time (more than one year for example).(7) Should be given by sentinels and up only.Of course, these are only intended as an informal guideline that moderators should try to enforce.So comments?

DDJ Night Force 9May 24 2007, 09:37 PM
Perhaps instead of making such a rigid format as to how negs should be dished out, maybe it could simply go by date. For example, if a member recieved a neg for reporting, then they can not recieve another until X amount of time has elasped (e.g. 3 or 4 months perhaps). However, they can still recieve negs for other contributions. This way, if a member does a ton of reporting, they recieve 1 neg but if someone does a ton of reporting and makes several very helpful posts (which get noticed), then that member is entitled to 2 negs (1 for each action type). It's essentially the same thing done for positive warns as well. With the exception of very serious offenses, it's unlikely a member would be warned twice for 2 posts (with similar dates) breaking the same rule. However, after some time if a member breaks the rule again, then that member would be warned again for repeating the same offense.Basically, everyone would just look at the member's warn log before issuing the neg to see if they have earned another. Hopefully that ends the more recent trend of certain members getting to -110% too quickly.Agreed - Strykr
