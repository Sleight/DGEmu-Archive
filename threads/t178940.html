Critique: Constructive criticism of the effectiveness of a work or the appropriateness of the choices made by a creator or performer, based on established criteria as appropriate for the given circumstances.Meaning? If people ask for "c'n'c", what they want is tips on how they could improve the artwork, preferably informed decisions too. That’s not to say they don’t want people to also say whether or not they like the art, but a post saying "I really like this" or "it sucks" is not sufficient [as well as being in violation of the rules for short posting, remember, VA is not spam]So what does that mean for you?If you are posting art in showcase, be ready to accept advanced criticisms, both good and bad. Don’t only post something for praise.If you are responding to posted art, try taking a little more time and thinking about it :) . as said before, saying "it’s nice" doesn’t cut it. Think of ways in which it could be improved, or aspects of it that you think were well done. That way next time the artist makes something, they know what they’ve done right and what needs changing.Of course, critique is highly subject to opinion, but if you combine a bunch of it, it should help.---------------------------------------------------------This is just a guideline on how to best help out artists here, it’s not against the rules to just say you like or dislike something, provided it’s a long enough post.Would be nice if more people did critique properly though :o
