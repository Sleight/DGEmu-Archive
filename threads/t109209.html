JJarethDeamethApr 18 2006, 11:41 PM
When picking people for your team you need to consider what style of fighting you prefer. If you prefer a fight you know you can't lose, you will want Generals like Gatrie and Brom on your side, Mages like Tormoid, Soren and Illyania. Never use the promoted versions because their stats end up like crap compared to those who you trained from day one. Rolf is also a good choice for fights you know you can win. Now if you want a more direct approach you would definatly want swordmasters, and you can never go wrong with Zihark, however I have found that Stephan is actually either just a bit further than Zihark or on Par with him, so pick whoever. Paladins you can never run out of, literally. I ended the game with all the paladins having different weapon combos. If you haven't figured out that Women get more Magical Resistance and Atk than the Males and that the males have more Defense and Attack, then you really need to check up on their stats. So when I was putting together teams I would have equal male and females, and stick the females with the magic weapons(Bolt Axe, Flame Lance, Wind Sword) and the males with Silver Weapons. I know that the magic weapons run out, but if you use the Action Replay to just get those weapons then its quite entertaining to see ANYONE get killed by the females :D . A good rule of thumb:1) TRAIN IKE2) TRAIN RHYS3) TRAIN MISTThese Three are very VERY good to have around. One you can't get rid of anyways, so make sure Ike gets his Occult scroll so he can just rip through enemies. Rhys is good to have around being the main healer and only light magic user. And despite what others may think, Mist can become your main offense for horses. If you think logically, what can a MAGIC user do with a SWORD? Hmm... Put the two words together and you get MAGIC SWORD, that means use the WIND SWORD. Put Sol on her just in case she gets hit too... even though its rare for a Valkyrie to get hit at all, it can still hurt so better heal up.
