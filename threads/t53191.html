Council ManagementThe purpose of the council is to Develop, and, Organize DGEMU and the DGNetwork. If you have an idea that is within reason, and, is something you could in some way help implement then by all means start a topic with the appropriate code. All new ideas must get the approval of the majority of the Admins.Council Rules and GuidelinesDo NOT go Off-topic. A little chicanery is ok so long as you don't over do it and stay on topic.Do NOT use foul language, flame another council members idea, or, ask any foolish questions. Show you are worthy of your status.Give informative titles. Do NOT make other council members guess what your topic pertains to.Do NOT duplicate topics. If you have something new to add or a change that needs to be made then Edit the topic. Check the Council at least once a week. Although it is better to check it daily. Keep this place productive and develop your ideas further.Read what other say, even if it's a bit long. Every Council members opinion counts. Unless everything you are going write is important, try making your replies shorter and more to the point.Do NOT pin your topic. Unless your topic is a truly important message. A topic which has received no replies for a week will count as a closed discussion and whoever is in charge of the implementation may start work on it.The Council may decide on a closure of a certain discussion when they wish to rush implementation due to importance, and, if they feel they have received enough feedback on the subject.Do NOT delete any council discussions. Omission of a council discussion is up to the discretion of an Admin.These rules and guidelines are subject to change with out notice. It would be wise to check them for changes regularly. Not acknowledging that the rules have changed is no excuse.- Dragons Master and the DGEmu Administration
