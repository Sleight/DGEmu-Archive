It's not too hard:

 

First turn: Starting with everything holding. Contract abs, hips, ankles. Relax knees. Extend pecs, neck. Raise shoulders.

Second turn: Extend hips, knees, abs.Contract neck.

Third turn: Extend ankles. Contract pecs.

Fourth turn: Lower shoulders. Contract abs, hips, knees., Extend neck.

 

You should smash beyond full force with your knees if any major part of your opponent flies at this area. Plus if you hold this pose you'll hang on to the bar and they can't hit you back and you won't dq.
