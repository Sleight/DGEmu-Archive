DdeelunaDec 27 2014, 10:22 PM
Well I hate to say it but sometimes the ground within the socket can go bad, which is what it sounds like you are describing. (the green wire) So unless you are brave about opening wall sockets and have a nice quality multimeter, and possibly willing to run a brand new wire through the walls, I'd get someone that is licensed to check that socket for you. Though sometimes it's not so much the ground wire but the ground in just the socket. but there is also the possiblity that the ground wire could have slipped off the screw and just needs to be reconnected. Either way, if you decide to try that, stay safe and stay alive, turn off the circuit breaker for that socket first. The last thing you want to do is get nailed by AC current, it's not fun.
