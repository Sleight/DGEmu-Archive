Survival of the Fittest, in my opinion, is the Universal Law that everything in this world answers to. This can range from a simple dandelion to a complex human being. Most people equate 'fit' to a physical aspect and thus envision Survival of the Fittest meaning Survival of the Physically Strongest. This is not the case, and it is highly unfortunate that many people see it this way. Survival of the Fittest refers to the ability to adapt and thus survive. My definition of adaptation involves three concepts as defined below. Perfect survivability can be considered to possess all three concepts in perfection. Perfect lack of survivability can be considered to possess none of the three concepts at all. Physical-refers to anything physically related. This could mean, but is not limited to, overall health, strength, bodily endurance, and agility.Mental-refers to anything related to intelligence. This could mean, but is not limited to, ability to use environment and use of ingenuity as opposed to physical application.Social*-refers to anything related to a social setting. This could mean, but is not limited to, the ability to manipulate** other living organisms. NOTE 1: I put Social in its own category, even though it could technically be placed in the Mental category, due to the fact that we have social and non-social organisms. Non-social organisms are immediately inferior to social organisms because of a complete lack of one of the main concepts I listed. NOTE 2: Please keep in mind that manipulation is a neutral concept and I am not implying that it is either negative or positive. Manipulate can mean gain loyalty, gain respect, trick, take advantage of, etc. Using these three concepts we can develop a spectrum that all living organisms fall under when defining inferiority (lack of survivability) and superiority (perfect survivability).This brings us to part two of the discussion. Justifiability. Justice, being the base word, means to administer a deserved punishment or reward. In the case of the law of survival, those that are superior will be rewarded while those that are inferior will be punished. Of course the ultimate reward is to live, and the ultimate punishment is to die. My honest opinion is that "survival of the fittest" is a corruption of scientific principles that are only meant to explain natural phenomena. The phrase's meaning is always based on the context. It doesn't seek out the perfect form of life. It's merely a process by which life continues on earth. Humanity generally has for a goal the well-being and happiness of as many individuals as possible. Therefore "survival of the fittest" means nothing to such a society. Our society's goal is to help as many people be as "fit" as possible.
