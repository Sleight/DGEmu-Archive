Many a night has passed where I would look at the clock and think "I've only got a few more quests before I finish this Guild's line. I'll finish and go to bed." Then I'd finish a couple more quests and realize that I was playing like I play Metal Gear Solid, and it took me two hours to get the current quest item. The detail in it is amazing, to the point where I actually jumped when the flail trap hit me in the back of the head... But, as with everything, the glitches can be pretty weird, to the point of breaking realism. The names bothered me, but when you see names like Jakben Imbel and Antoinetta Marie, you just know the creators were trying to make people like us (pun-haters) mad. The plane traveling is a cool feature, but nothing we haven't seen. Taking out so many of the weapons from Morrowind made me mad, but they kept M'aiq, so... Yeah. I'm done raving. Oblivion or Dead Rising.
