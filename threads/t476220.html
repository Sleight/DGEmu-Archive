2.- Run Maverick OTServerXML.exe then just wait til loads all the stuff.

3.- Go to Tibia client with IP changer directory and open Tibia.exe, then open the IP changer.exe, set the IP u put on the global.lua and press change IP.

4.- Go back to the Tibia.exe window and click on acces account, enter to the account manager with the account name: 111111 and the password: 111111 and create your account, password and character.

5.- Log out with the account manager and then click on acces account and enter your account number and password. (The one you created with the account manager).

You can start playing now, you can start quests by asking to the NPC's, or exploring the map. You can start a conversation with an NPC just by saying Hi, or hello and interacting with certain keywords like help, job, name, offer (for the seller and buyer npc's), etc, etc.
