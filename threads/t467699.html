I'm also Chinese and understand where you're coming from on the "marry only Chinese" restriction. My parents told me that preferably they would want me to marry someone in my own culture because it's easier for them to communicate with the other person. They aren't against me marrying someone non-Chinese, but they're just worried that there will be cultural barriers when I go to the other side's family gatherings and vice versa where you would be isolated, and it's difficult for both sides' parents to communicate with each other. But in the end after all considerations, who you want to marry is up to you, I think it's just as long as the person is true and good to you, that's all that matters.
