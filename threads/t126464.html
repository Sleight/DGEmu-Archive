HHound Of ChulainnApr 28 2006, 10:42 AM
There's been some discussion in this topic about how to combat spam more effectively, and the consensus is that warnings aren't enough.So I was thinking about ways to further discourage spam and it struck me that it should be something that has an instant impact on whoever's breaking the rules - they often don't know enough to even read their warn log and it usually doesn't matter if their topic gets closed, because they get the points and convert them anyway. So I figure "hit them in the back pocket", as it were.I know nothing about the required coding/scripting/whatever that would be needed to make this idea work, so bear with me:Put a button somewhere in a post (probably under the warn level, or next to the delete and edit buttons) that mods and up can see that says: "deduct GP for this post". If a mod sees an offending post, he clicks the button, and it removes a set amount of GP: either however many points are gained by posting, a fixed amount (maybe 10 GP), or better yet, an amount that increases with the warn level; (so if someone on 0% made a two word post, and a mod clicks the proposed button, he'd lose 10 GP, if he was on 30%, he'd lose 30 GP).It obviously has to be a one time only click, so that nobody has points removed twice, and like I said, I know nothing about the logistics.But I think that this would be an effective way of stopping spammers before they got out of control. If they know that they're trying to swim upstream, it might make them a bit more careful about following the rules and it'll stop the idiots who think: "I don't care if my topic gets closed - I'll take my points, convert them and run like hell".
