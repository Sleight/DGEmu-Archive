Here's everything you need to know about 3DS ROMs and Emulation. Anyone making posts or topics about anything covered in here will have points deducted and will be eligible for a warn.

Q: Where can I get 3DS ROMs?
A: NOTHING can play them yet, however, we do offer them for collection purposes.

Q: Where/When can I get a 3DS Emulator?
A: It will probably take a few years before a functioning 3DS emulator comes out. Till then they don't exist, and nobody knows when they will appear either.

Q: Which flashcarts can work on the 3DS?
A: Only DS ROMs can be played on flashcarts right now, even on the 3DS.
Here is the list of flashcarts currently working on the 3DS

Q: Which flashcarts can play 3DS ROMs on the 3DS?
A: None of them can. 3DS mode hasn't been hacked yet, and so no 3DS-mode flashcarts have been produced.

Q: Will current flashcarts which can play DS ROMs on the 3DS be able to play 3DS ROMs too someday?
A: Probably not, but nobody knows for sure. We'll just have to wait for 3DS mode to be hacked to find out.
