Well, the origin of video games began in the 30's and 40's, with mechanical, coin operated pinball machines that began the gaming saga. The first game, using electronics, may have been created as far back as 1946-1947, when Thomas T. Goldsmith Jr. and Estle Ray Mann created a small game, that allowed a "missle" to be fired out and controlled about using knobs and such. However, the targets were merely drawn onto the CRT, so it technically can't be classified as a video game.The first actual Electronic game, was Tic-tac-toe, created by A.S.Douglas, with a PhD degree at the University of Cambridge. At that time, the university had an EDSAC vaccuum-tube computer, which used a cathode ray tube to display the contents of one of the 32 mercury delay lines (which stored the programs and data). The display was organized as a matrix of 35 by 16 dots, hence a 35x16 pixels display. A.S. Douglas wrote his thesis on the Human-Computer interraction, and illustrated it with a graphic Tic-Tac-Toe game displayed on a cathode ray tube. This is the earliest graphical computer game known to exist. The game was played against the machine, which used special algorithms to win whenever possible.(http://www.pong-story.com/intro.htm)Willy Higginbotham in 1958 created Tennis For Two, on an oscilloscope.
