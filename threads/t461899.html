Possibly. They're quite outdated already anyway, to the point where I'm surprised they're actually still supported.For such flashcarts, it's best to use them mainly for old/older ROMs, as it'll always be a bit tough to play the latest, AP-heavy ROMs with them.The venom crack doesn't seem to be very good, try the patch by retrogamefan of GBATemp:http://filetrip.net/f21855-Open-Patch-Game-Fixes-1-01.html(patch it using the clean ROM from DGEmu)If that still doesn't work there isn't much I can do for you. :facepalm:As for recommendations, if you're conscious about price choose the Acekard 2i. It's extremely cheap, highly reliable despite the price, and good with regular updates that allow playing the latest ROMs. Of course it's also pretty no-frills, you can play ROMs with it well and that's it, but that's about the only drawback if you can call it one:http://shoptemp.com/products/Acekard-2.1-C...-Lite-p-31.htmlhttp://shoptemp.com/products/Acekard-2i-Ca...e-DSi-p-30.htmlhttp://www.dealextreme.com/forums/Forums.dx/Forum.11811http://www.dealextreme.com/forums/Forums.dx/Forum.18728If you're not concerned about price try the Supercard DSTWO. From what I've heard it's just the best. Plenty of features, an inbuilt CPU for emulation, extremely regular updates, an ability to naturally bypass AP, GBA and SNES emulation without slot-2 (not perfect yet of course, but getting there), etc etc. And it's great for playing ROMs too:http://shoptemp.com/products/Supercard-DST...e-DSi-p-97.html
