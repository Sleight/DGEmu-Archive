GP Giveaways

 

Meru and I are in discussions about having a monthly GP Giveaway Contest.  We are thinking about a quiz at the moment.

 

The quiz goes on for a specified week.  At the end, we compile the points of how many people got it right.

 

The ranking goes highest points win, if a lot of people are tied, first one to answer wins (time stamped posts).

 

We were unsure how to make this happen at first as comments and posts mean that everyone can see what others have guessed, but if we made at new forum and had it like the DLC (Download Centre) where members posts become invisible after posting meaning they can't change their answers (or even SEE their own posts however) and others can't see their post either ....

 

We are still deciding whether their should be a restriction (like my old Lottery which required members to have 50 posts or more) and if so what restriction.  However we are leaning towards no restriction as we can have a staff cheat sheet with the answers to the quiz and all staff can then participate in adding up the points, etc.

 

[hr]

 

No more DPs?!?!

 

We may be a LONG way off this but we are looking in eventually COMPLETELY eliminate any form of DP as a currency and only keeps GPs for everything download related. (We are also considering to have free downloads (NO GPs) and allow visitors to NOT sign up yet still download ROMs)

 

To assist with GPs been the main currency we are looking to offer more banking options so that users can invest to gain GPs.

 

This will bring:

- Offer 1 standard currency to lower confusion amongst new members.

- Use DG Economy ACP tools to actively reward members with GPs during special events.

- If coded correctly, can set global promotions (same concepts as freeleech on torrent sites), etc.

- Can be coded to reflect member groups paying half price or less for roms (Power Members, etc)
