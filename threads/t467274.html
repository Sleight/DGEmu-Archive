This is directed towards my generation (I am 18), although of course all ages are welcome to reply. Generally speaking (at least in Texas and most likely America, I can't speak for other nations), especially when we were in middle school and high school, the accepted way to initiate a relationship is to approach the person of interest and ask them something along the lines of "will you go out with me?" The reply will usually boil down to yes or no, if yes then the two are officially boyfriend and girlfriend and the relationship goes wherever it goes. With our parents generation, the accepted method is to approach the person of interest and ask them on a date. If yes, then you date. Perhaps a second, a third, and so on. At some point there is a first kiss, wherein abouts they probably begin considering themselves boyfriend and girlfriend, and the relationship goes where it goes from there.I now pose two questions:1. What is our generation going to do? Will we continue asking people to be our girlfriend or boyfriend, or will we "mature" and start dating?2. What do you prefer? Would you rather be asked to be a boyfriend or girlfriend, or be asked on a date?
