Then use Irvine's Fast Ammo with Strength 200 something by junctioning Ultima to it, then have Squall with 255 use the Renzokuken, and do your best with Zell's 200 something strength and Dual. I happen to be a superman when it comes to Dual and Zell; I execute those commands in a fraction of a second. If I'm given 10 seconds of command time, you can expect a lot of punch from Zell that can RIVAL Squall's Lionheart limit break. Don't use meteor strike alot though, it acts sorta like Demi; after a few hits, the damage goes down a little. I find it good for a lot of enemies with over 9999 health. :drool: Be fast on the trigger with Irvine and fast ammo. I unloaded about 50 shots on the Weapon the first limit break and I had to use demolition ammo after that so I could have a nice 9999 punch and not use my AP ammo (Saving it for Ultimecia). For Squall, just keep him going good with the Renzokuken Trigger hits. The Holy War will keep your people alive throughout the battle so just refine it off of the Gilgamesh Card. If you don't have that, you should just steal a Hero from Siefer or refine the Laguna Card for 100 heros. Otherwise, have Shell on and godspeed!
