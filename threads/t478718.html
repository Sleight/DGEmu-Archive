KKirie MotobaJan 29 2013, 10:45 PM

Answering quickly:

 

PS1 ISOs: Drop the Alcohol 120% thing, it's not needed. ImgBurn is your best friend at this age and time. The only console you might need another app to pirate properly is the Dreamcast where you might need DiscJuggler to burn your games.

As always, discs of choice: CD-R, burned at lowest speeds possible (I usually burn around 10x and they come out nice and clean, and make sure to use good media, not cheap stuff)

 

As for mounting discs for PC games: Daemon Tools as Kui suggested or Virtual CloneDrive. They both work wonders with games that have no disc based protections such as SecurDisc and other such.

 

Seeing how your friend is stubborn, here's a site to download your NoCD executables: http://www.gamecopyworld.com/games/pc_sw_knights_of_the_old_republic.shtml

Note 1: Make sure to patch the game to whatever version is required for the NoCD.

Note 2: Access the site with Firefox and AdBlock Plus (or any browser with a nice decent ad blocker). I've been using that site since my days in college, one of the most reliable sites for PC game cracks, just that it is flooding with ads.
