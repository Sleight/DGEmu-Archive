TTHE GRAND GALACTIC INQUISITORORIGINAL POSTSep 02 2011, 03:11 AM
So, it's that time again. I'm looking for an anime to watch while I eat my dinner each night, something to pass half an hour or so in amusement. I have a few criteria, for anyone who might have suggestions:- Quirky slice-of-life with a supernatural twist or two (and a good dose of humor) is welcome. What is not welcome is harem anime with an emotionally distant male lead and several females exhibiting one-dimensional/fetish-centric personalities.- Action and fights are all right, if used as a means to an end (a la HunterXHunter, perhaps, or the latest Birdy remake). What is not all right is any of a countless number of shonen-based shows in which whole episodes (or multiple episodes) are centered around a single fight, and the plot is simply a vehicle for pitting various individuals with specific special abilities against other individuals with specific special abilities. Total snoozefest.- I guess I enjoy revenge stories from time to time (Cowboy Bebop, Trigun, GunXSword), but only if the characters are actually appealing (I'm looking at you, Outlaw Star). Heck, I prefer any kind of story over vignette-based narrative (e.g. Kino's Journey, Mushishi).- I prefer my stories to have a definite beginning, middle and end, without a whole lot of filler crap stuffed in between. A couple seasons is all right - episode totals numbering in the hundreds are not.I guess I'm more interested in newer series, as I'm less likely to have heard of them and formed an opinion on them. The most recent shows I can remember watching and enjoying quite a bit are Dennou Coil, Beck: Mongolian Chop Squad and Working!!! (recent to me, not necessarily released recently). Of course, I'm also a big fan of innovative storytelling like Baccano! and Durarararararara (and also Haruhi, I guess that counts).The guidelines I mentioned aren't necessarily requirements, just some insight into the sort of anime that I usually watch and enjoy. Suggestions (that don't suck) are much appreciated.
