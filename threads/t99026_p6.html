The variation of allele frequency by the way, is the proper definition of evolution, in this way, evolution is fact because the change in the variation of allele frequency can be observed. The theory of evolution on the other hand, is a construct that is meant to explain the fact of evolution, the theory says that genetic drift and natural selection plays major roles in the change of allele frequency. A fact is essentially a tautology; it is true because it is true (though in the case of humans this is synonymous with: it is true because it is seen/heard/felt/tasted/smelled). The truth of a theory is evaluated differently; the current theory of evolution is not necessarily true; that is, natural selection does not necessarily account for changes in allele frequency, it is merely a logical conclusion that also happens to mesh with the observed results.
