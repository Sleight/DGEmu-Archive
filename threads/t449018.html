Their are many fake flash memory devices being sold on ebay, I myself used to have a fake one. An easy way to check is when on a PSP you go to your memory stick properties/info see if magic gate is enabled. They look the same at the official sandisk/sony/whatever brand memory sticks but the boxing is shady sometimes. I remember mine wasn't so bad, slow transfer speeds, but my friend got one that was so corrupt he could only place 2/8gb worth of data on it and a lot of other bad stuff was happening during reading the files off of it. Memory sticks are pretty cheap now a days though; just be sure to get a real one.
