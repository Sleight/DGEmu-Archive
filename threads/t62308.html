someone wrote:
What other way to increase profits?!
Profits probably won't increase in the slightest bit. It has more to do with costs of development going up. For every $50 the consumer pays for a game, the average game developer probably makes $2-5. That's how inefficient the distribution model is, but when you have current-gen titles that exceed $20 million in development costs alone (forget advertising), try and imagine how much next-gen development will cost. I can almost guarantee that at least one next-gen title at some point in time down the road will have a cost that exceeds that of an average Hollywood blockbuster film.Add to that that less than 5% of all games at any given time *EVER* turn a profit, and the financial risk to publishers increases, so there's basically no other way to offset the rising costs.
