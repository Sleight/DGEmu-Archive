After all, the way the game(s) was originally made there were only one or two ways of earning a Master Ball without cheating. Had Game Freaks intended that a certain Pokemon or a certain group of Pokemon couldn't be caught without using the Master Ball, then don't you think that there would be a way to acquire more Master Balls without cheating? The criteria, I believe, for successfully catching a Pokemon are that your lead Pokemon must have one or more attributes that are higher than the Pokemon that you wish to catch, the Pokemon that you wish to catch must be PARALYZED/SLEEP/WEAK or some condition that would render it impossible for the intended Pokemon to escape. The intended Pokemon must have a low evasion factor, etc. It is possible to catch a legendary Pokemon with an Ultra Ball or a Premier Ball, etc. However, the lower you get in quality of pokeball (towards the Poke Ball), the harder, if not difficult it gets to catch a legendary with one. If you really have that kind of time to do this, then go ahead and try. Most people prefer the more direct and quicker route.
