Done most of them, have a practice on the last few.Here's the basics:Click on 'Check IP' against one of the reports listed.They're auto sorted in ascending order according to join date in the IP finder, give it a quick look to check for similarities in the username / display name / email fields, if there's nothing there that matches, then make a decision based on join dates / active times. If there are four accounts on an IP, three of them clearly dupes, a fourth one that bears no similarity, but they all appeared in the space of a day, then it's probably worth banning. The top account in the list, as mentioned, is the oldest one, if that account is validated (if it doesn't say validating on the ban link, it's validated) then anything after it that looks dupe-ish qualifies for a warn. Even if there's one validated account and nine that aren't validated after it, they're still dupes because they're made after the original. Five warns, I ban the original, and generally IP ban aswell. In short, any dupes after a validated account constitute a warn on the oldest validated account there, anything prior to the oldest validated account I'll just ban without giving a warn.Keep an eye out for their warn level jumping up 20%, either you double clicked, or someone's posted about the same member twice. Oh, and about the whole oldest account thing, if there's a newer one that has a fair number of posts, I'll generally ban the rest of them and keep that one going.It's pretty much common sense for the most part, the intricacies you'll pick up in time.
