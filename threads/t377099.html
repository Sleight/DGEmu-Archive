Hi all,As most of you are aware, PSX ISOs were supposedly granted to dgemu's "Power Members". However, to date, there has been no means of giving them access. Therefore, I figured it'd be an awesome idea if us staff could pitch in to assemble the site ourselves.The site itself: What I was thinking is a site that only displays a login page at first with no additional information (much like what bitgamer.com has) whereby power members are granted access. Since this is going to exist on a separate server, I can't use any database information from dgemu so a login system will have to be built and managed independently. Once logged in, a power member will be free to download PSX ISOs within a certain limitation (like dgemu) to be determined later on in development. Placing a limit will not only keep the costs down on that server too but will also prevent power members from leaking their login info on public sites.Staff: Since I am in school, I will be generally quite busy, so the more help, the faster and better this gets done. Please do not apply unless you can see this all the way through. Like anything, it's obviously not expected that you work on this every chance you get especially since there is no specific deadline set, but obviously something needs to get done.Here's the kind of staff needed to get this done:Web Designers: I need someone who is proficient in designing site layouts and given that this is for PlayStation ISOs, would build something relative to that theme. All that's really needed is a prototype of how you think the site should look using some static examples when necessary. I can simply take that and convert it into separate HTML templates that will then be populated by values from the database (i.e. used dynamically).Web Programmers: This team will handle the backbone coding of the site as well as managing its database. I'm obviously one of those but anyone else who knows PHP, MySQL, and possibly AJAX is welcome to join me (e.g. the existing coding team for this site would be handy if they are available)ISO Testers: This is probably the most time consuming job ever but I strongly feel it is necessary especially if a limit is to be placed on the site. However, the reward to the individual (or individuals if multiple people want to divide this task) who accomplishes this will be that they will end up with every ISO on the site that they downloaded to do as they please afterwards. What I need done, is for someone to download each game and test to see if it works on the two emulator EPSXE and pSX as well as a modded PSX (if possible as I don't except anybody to have both an NTSC and PAL Playstation unit but if you do, then even better). Having a separate copy of the ISO will not do. It's the one on the server that needs testing and is the only one that counts. The main reason why I ask this is because these ISOs were uploaded by many different people over time and on top of that, some were done in a heck of a rush when there were several submissions at once. I want to ensure that they "work" and not corrupted during the conversion process to img/ccd/sub/cue. It'd be easiest to make a spreadsheet as a report on how well as game was working much like what the screenshotters do. Any ISOs that just flat out don't work will have to be discarded.Well I think I covered everything. If you have any ideas and feedback or you want to apply for a specific task, post in this thread or PM me. Let's hope we can get going what power members paid for and deserve to have.
