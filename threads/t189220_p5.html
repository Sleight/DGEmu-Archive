Let n be the total number of coins. Divide the coins into two groups: one consisting of 26 coins (group A) and the other consisting of n-26 coins (group B) (it doesn't matter how you split the coins, so you can do it without looking). Then turn upside down all coins from group A (this can also be done without looking). Problem is solved.This works because initially, there are k heads up coins in group A, 26 - k tails up coins in group A, and 26 - k heads up coins in group B. When you turn upside down all of group A, there are 26 - k heads up coins in group A (same as in group B).
