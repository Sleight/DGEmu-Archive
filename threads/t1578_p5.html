RrasufelleJun 03 2007, 10:12 PM
Best Game of All Time lists are so hard to do, if for no other reason than the number of qualifiers required to actually get anything resembling an accurate guess. It's one thing to say 'Best 2D Platformer of All Time' but quite a different animal when it's the 'Best FPS of All Time'. In order to shrink things down a bit, I'll do three or four broad genres rather than console-specific listings.Best 'classic' console RPG of All Time: While there are several obvious contenders for this title- Grandia, Dragon Warrior, Lunar- I have to go with Final Fantasy. Or, to be more specific, the first Final Fantasy. It introduced the console gamer to the classes we've all come to know and love, the massive overworld, the airships, and most importantly, one of the most epic musical scores in any game series ever released. Not only that, but for the time the graphics were decent, and the gameplay went without any truly major changes all the way through to the current day, not accounting for the addition of Summoning and ATB.Best RTS of all time: While this issue could be strongly debated, probably MY favorite RTS would be Age of Empires 2. Strong graphics, lots of options for creation of your armies, and easy to learn. You can sit down in five minutes and figure out how all the major play mechanics work, or sit at it for six hours straight being schooled on why the French never conquered the Japanese.Best FPS of all time: There are really only three options for this one, Quake, Doom, or Half Life. Personally, I'm going with Half Life. The streaming levels mean you can play the entire game in one sitting with virtually no load times, the weapons offer a lot of variety and many nifty ways of offing your opponents, and the AI doesn't suck like it does in most ID games, including the Quake series. While not necessarily the most innovative game of the genre, it did make full use of both computing power and graphics potential for the time, and even now there are thousands worldwide who gather online to deathmatch in TFC or any number of other mods available. Some may argue that Halo belongs here, but not only do I personally not care for Halo, but it doesn't really carry anything we haven't seen before in other shooters, and everything it offered had been done better before, if not in quite as well marketed a package.Best 2D Platformer: Super Metroid. Without this game, not only would we not have the modern Metroid games, but the Castlevania games would be missing many of the features that people have come to love as well (if you take the 'metroid-vania' phenomena to heart). Excellent graphics, a memorable main character, and overall gameplay that still holds your attention more than ten years after the game was released- how can you complain?While there are any number of genres/subgenres I could add, I'm going to leave the list here to keep from rambling any more.
