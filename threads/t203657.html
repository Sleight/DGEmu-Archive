I have always wondered what can explain the number of view people may get. Including some relevant variables, I found something interesting.Variables used:- Staff: being of having been staff- Promoted: it means the concerned member has been promoted since the profile views are enables- SOTW: number of weeks the SOTW winner had his/her sig displayed at the bottom of the page since...- Girl: dummy variable (1=yes, 0=no and comprise both male and unset)- Spam: a 2-point scale indicating how active a member is in the Spam Center since...- Posts: number of posts- Special: any special factor=1, no special factor=0 (ex: for DM, it's 1 since he is the site owner...)The results are displayed below:Regression Statistics R Square 0,609351306Adjusted R Square 0,556763982Observations 60Variable | Coefficients | P-valueIntercept | 154 | 5,46%Staff | 153 | 12,89%Promoted | 468 | 0,30%SOTW | 571 | 0,01%Girl | 296 | 3,06%Spam | 158 | 1,40%Posts | 0 | 2,64%Special | 667 | 0,22%For those who are new to statistics, the adjusted R-square is the best measure of the goodness to fit of the model. Here it is 54%, meaning that 54% of the variations is actually explained by the model, whoch is excellent in this sort of domain where many other unseen factors exist.For the coefficients, I removed all non significant variables such as ratin. The only limit case is Staff since its p-value is superior to the standard 5%. It means that being a staff member does not necessarily influence your number of views.Otherwise, all the other factors do influence at 95% confidence level the number of views. For example, being a girl brings about 296 additional views. Being newly promoted gives you 468 views, etc...The 0 you can see about Posts is due to the rounding. It was actually 0.06 however the stadard error was 0.03 making the coefficient still significant.Anyway, interesting, no?
