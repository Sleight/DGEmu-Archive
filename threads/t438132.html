Ascendancy is a rather unique turn base Space strategy game. There are a unusually large number of races to play as, each has their own special ability, and none of them are human. Thats right, this is one of the few space games where there simply are no humans.
Combine the fact that there are 21 races with the ability to play against 6 opponents at one in some very large randomly generated galaxies and you have a strategy game with virtually infinite replayability.
Also rather unique is the fact that most of the games content is presented as 3D and can be rotated. This includes the universe, the star systems, and even the research tree. And the research tree is almost better described as DNA or a molecule as the structure isn't so much branched as splitting rejoining and intertwined. It is almost impossible to describe or explain with a screenshot.
