KKirie MotobaMar 12 2015, 04:50 AM

@KH: Sure, you sack of potato. Don't make me come and peel you.

 

@Bish: Well, the holes are there to help with airflow, which is a great thing. The last thing you want is a comp that overheats.

But the 200R has all the good of the 300R also: PSU facing downwards out the case, with filter (very important), and the front has enough intakes.

 

On mine, I transferred the back 120mm fan to the front instead of the top just for the sake of having a fan blow cool air onto the SSDs and especially that WD Black, since those drives tend to act erratically when under too much heat.

The goods are that the case does come with plenty of options, and nearly screwless DVD drive install.

Caveats: top grill is REALLY flimsy. It feels like I was going to punch a hole through by holding it near there. That and the rubber feet, although large, are probably stuck with saliva... They came off like nothing as I was handling the tower, and won't stick back. Of, and the drive caddies are really plasticky. They work, but could've been better. That's pretty much it. A nice decent case, but for the price, I think I would go back to the Fractal Design Core cases instead. They have more features, and the case is even more solid than this. The drive trays are all at least in metal and solid.

 

And yes, as much as we're always in wintery weather (computers serve as a good source of heating), our summer are quite humid and deadly hot. Unless you run your computer in a room with AC, your computer will overheat.

I leave my server and desktop on all winter, but once summer hits, I shut down everything before leaving the house because my place gets seriously deadly hot indoors.

98% uptime, not bad. (2% downtime while I pulled this thing apart to swap the parts out)
