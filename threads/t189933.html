For clarification, Tales of Phantasia is the game in question here, it originally came out on the SNES in Japan, and later on the PSX in Japan, neither of which made it to the US. It was more recently ported to the GBA, and finally released in the US (but was a fairly poor port, imo). The most recent version is this PSP "full voice edition", which again, likely won't be released in the US.The other titles in the series include Tales of Destiny (released on PSX in US/Japan, and recently remade for the PS2 in Japan), Tales of Eternia (released on PSX in US/Japan, but called "Tales of Destiny 2" in the US, also later released on PSP, but not in the US, although you can import the EU version), Tales of Destiny 2 (released on PS2 in Japan, but never in the US), Tales of Rebirth (same deal), and then the more recent ones (Tales of Symphonia, Tales of Legendia, and Tales of the Abyss). Additionally, there've been several spin-off titles, which also never made it to the states.
