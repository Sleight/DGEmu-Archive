He was one of my issues with the story.

Let's talk about The Usual Suspects.

That's an example of an extremely well crafted character reveal. Whilst there are very scarce hints littered throughout, it still had so much impact because of the significance of the character.

One of the problems with AA5 is that

our Mr Assassin just seemingly suddenly shows up out of nowhere. His facade very rarely drops in any noticeable way, not at all hinting about Fulbright's true nature.
