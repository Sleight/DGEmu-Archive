Do not ask for translation patches If it isn't at http://www.romhacking.net, it doesn't exist.Never ask for ROMsApparently, most members fail to understand that DGEmu has every single commercial ROM in existence (for the systems we have). Do not ask for non-commercial ROMs DGEmu does not have.Do not ask for game savesIf you want game saves, go to www.gamefaqs.com and most of what you need should be there. If you absolutely have to create a topic about it, do it in the spam section.Do not ask for DP refundsWe only offer refunds in extreme cases - namely, when the server messes up and doesn't give anyone the file they requested. Since most download errors are client-side (i.e. your fault), asking for refunds will cause your topic to be closed.Do not ask for a higher global DP resetAt the time of this post, DGEmu has approximately 528,400 members. Assuming that every one of these members has 12.5 MB worth of daily downloading bandwidth, that translates to 6,605,000 MB (6.6 TB) of bandwidth PER DAY. Even an increase of 1 global DP would increase that amount by 0.5 TB, so it's not going to happen.Do not ask for ISOsMost ISOs can be 700MB or more in size, meaning that hosting them on a normal web server would burn loads of bandwidth in a matter of hours (exception will be the PSX ISOs but those are not up yet and will only be made available to power members). If you want ISOs, please use a search engine to find torrents or if you're lucky, someone who has uploaded the desired game to Rapidshre.Do not ask for emulatorsHere's a list (and links) of compatible and reliable emulators for most systems.
