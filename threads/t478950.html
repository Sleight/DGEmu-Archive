GPs are added to your account for posting on the forums and doing other social-type actions. For example, by posting this, I receive 10 GPs, which I can then convert into DPs. DPs are, of course, used for downloading things from the site. For a default user such as you or me, the conversion rate is 30 to 1 for daily DPs (which reset every day, as per the name) or 40 to 1 for user DPs (which stay with you until used).
