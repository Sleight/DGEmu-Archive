http://www.wiihacks.com/recommended-faqs-guides-tutorials-only/96886-guide-softmod-any-wii.html

Not understand everything in the sense of knowing every specific IOS number and what it does, but that you understand the gist of the guide. If it's too much reading for you, congratz, you are chizmad. Take some time out of your busy 9th grade life of playing video games and masturbating to read this several times to avoid any fuck ups. It's really straight forward. If you have any questions feel free to post in this thread or message me. If you need me to get on steam chat or whatever do the same as I usually never run steam.

 

when you're on part 1 click the spoiler that says "4.3 Letterbomb no game disc required"

there's a link in the spoiler to go to letter bomb. open it in a new tab, put in your wii's MAC address and you get a custom installer. Follow the rest of the instructions for part 1.

 

part 2 is easy and straight forward. Just be patient. It takes some time and you might get scared that it froze or whatever but its fine, just be patient.

 

part 3 actually is optional. I personally don't have a copy of my Wii's NAND (basically system files) and have been fine without it and even recovered from a partial brick thanks to priiloader. So you can skip this but if you can do it (I actually couldn't back in the day) it doesn't hurt to have it. Basically a NAND back up  will let you recover your wii from almost anything bad that could happen to it. So you can skip it, but if you like to be prepared for a rainy day it doesn't hurt to have around. Alternatively you can just be smart and not let your wii get fucked up. I usually make NAND backups for like parents who ask me to mod their kid's wiis and I'm like "Your kids are pretty fucking retarded so I'm going to make a back up in case they fuck things up."

 

That's chapter 1. It might seem like a lot of work but it really isn't. It's basically downloading a bunch of stuff onto an SD card and running them on your wii. That's the EASY part.

 

 

Chapter 2 is the actual modding of the wii's system files and where things can get screwed up if you're a retard about it.

 

Basically just download all the stuff they tell you to download (you can skip the verify checksums part).

Next, you're installing WADs. WADs are files you write to your wii. There are 2 kinds, internal WADs (used for modding) and Wii channels (such as virtual console or wiiware games) come in WAD format.

Ignore the part with the shop channel, you're not going to use that again ever (seriously, don't use shop channel again).

MOST IMPORTANT! Install priiloader. That's your failsafe. Like I said, priiloader saved my partially bricked wii after my retarded 6yo (at the time) niece decided to update the firmware.

Make sure you install the hacks they recommend especially the ones regarding updates. Firmware updates = bricked wii though there probably won't be any more of those now (lucky).

 

That's about it. The rest of the guide is in regards to loading games. Personally I recommend having a mass USB storage device. Wii games on average are about 4.3gb and can fill up an external HD pretty quickly if it's small. If you don't want to do that, you can use the DVD method. Just watch out for certain games like smash bros brawl that were originally written on dual layer DVDs and don't run on puny DVD-Rs.

 

That's about it. Like I said, it may seem like a lot of work but it's mainly just downloading files to an SD card, popping it into your wii, installing files to your wii, rinse and repeat. If you need to, feel free to contact me with any questions.
