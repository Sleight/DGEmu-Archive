This is a series I make using footage from the anime Guyver The Bio Boosted Armor.

 

If you don't know what an Abridged Series is, it takes a source material (anime, cartoon, videogame) and shortens it so that the basic plot comes across, packed full of jokes.

 

I do the majority of the voices, with help from two friends.  I do all of the writing and editing myself.  I'm managing the make each successive episode tighter, so expect future episodes to be under 7 minutes.
