I hope this is taken seriously. I'm quite serious.I would like to open the topic of a different type of relationship. I would like to say now that, for sake of the debate, the sex of the people involved doesn't matter. The people involved love each other equally.The "normal" type of relationship involves two people, be it two men, two women, a man and a woman, or any other combination with no gender assignment or in-between genders, but generally involves only two people.I believe that, if there are three people, whom of which love each other, could and should be in a relationship together, all mutual, with full knowledge of there being three people involved (this is entirely different than someone cheating on another, or one person seeing two others who know about the other but don't love the other).I will leave it at that, asking that people don't bring in the sex of the people involved (I've had someone tell me two women and a man is alright but anything else was wrong), they (the "triple(?)" have full consent and acknowledgement of all involved, and please, please don't make "threesome" sexual jokes or references; this is about the love and affection, not sex involving three people for fun.My view: I think it is alright if there's consent, full knowledge, etc. I am not in this type of relationship, but I can see that yes, it could work. Yes, the public may frown upon such a thing, but many other things have been frowned upon before and later accepted (homosexual couples, interracial couples, couples of different religions or wealth, and more). I wont say this is something that will be accepted right away, but it's a possibility in the future. Yes, jealousy could be a factor, but I believe that if all three really love each other, then it wouldn't be a real problem (in comparison to a couple where one is jealous of the other's friend, colleague, or otherwise)In short: Love is love, right? Why can't three work as well as two?
