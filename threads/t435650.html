AatomicknightORIGINAL POSTOct 29 2008, 05:14 AM
For those of you who don't already know, Dolphin is the premier GameCube and Wii emulator in existence, with decent compatibility and performance (namely, SSBM runs almost flawlessly as does New Super Mario Bros Wii).

You can get it from its official website here:
http://dolphin-emula...m/download.html

Do note that while it is generally recommended to use the latest versions, they may break some things that work in the previous versions (it is incremental development after all). If so, just try an older version.

How to install and use Dolphin: http://wiki.dolphin-...talling_Dolphin

That's it for now. Have fun!
