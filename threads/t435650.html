For those of you who don't already know, Dolphin is the premier GameCube and Wii emulator in existence, with decent compatibility and performance (namely, SSBM runs almost flawlessly as does New Super Mario Bros Wii).

Latest SVN (both x86 and x64) Here:

NOTICE: Some SVN's may break things that work in the previous versions (it is incremental development after all). If something doesn't work, roll back to an old version. Keep in mind that the site linked above only has the "newest" one so keep a SVN you know worked well just in case.
