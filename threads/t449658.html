Fferofax ( ° 3°)~ チュウJan 21 2010, 07:33 PM
dude, just rename the .NDS file everytime you want to play your own save. for example, to keep things simple, rename your .NDS and .SAV into AoE-Myth_1.nds and AoE-Myth_1.sav. When your brother wants to play, have him rename the .NDS file by changing the "1" into "2" (ie, AoE-Myth_2.nds). this way, the game will create a NEW SAVE for that game, then you just keep renaming back and forth to access your own save files. but remember to rename only the .NDS, and remember what number you use so you don't overwrite each other's save.oh yeah, you'll have to use something that can rename files from within your NintendoDS (DSOrganize works well enough).
