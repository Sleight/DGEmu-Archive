EeljorgeAug 05 2010, 11:19 AM
I know what you mean, I was very nervous about trying to interview till the point I just didn't do it for the same reasons as you. In all honesty, when I eventually had to do it, it wasn't as bad as you think it will be. I didn't have much confidence in myself, and that turns out to be the biggest obstacle. It's hard to believe in yourself, but you are most likely a lot better at what you want to do than you think. Also, most places will train you in the specifics, so the interview is more about what you have learned and who you are more than a simple "what do you know" test. Be honest with them, and let them know you will do your best at the job and you shouldn't have a problem.I would not continue studies at this point, because the issues you have with your fear will not go away. You may want to spend some of your spare time working on a few of your art works and test the waters with that (although finding work as an artist is going to be much more difficult unless you are extremely talented or find a niche market).In the end, the old phrase is still true. The first step is definitely the hardest.
