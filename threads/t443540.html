If you've read my post in the Hello & Goodbye forum, you already know this but I think it belongs here as well.During my senior year, a friend of mine was playing Earthbound for the SNES on the computer next to me in class. I asked him about it, and he told me it was part of an emulating package which was given to him by his own friend and asked me if I'd like a copy. I was both surprised and amazed by the few hundred games (along with the very impressive ZSNES emulator) which were inside the package, all of which were in excellent working order and many of which provided me hours of entertainment (I still play those original games today). Unfortunately, I had no idea where the ROMs came from, since my friend had no idea where his friend had gotten the original package. Fortunately, after exploring the folders included in the package, I found the original, zipped files of the games (somehow sorted by genre) which said that the ROMs were downloaded from DGEmu.com.And, several downloads later, the rest is history. Thanks for creating such an indescribably wonderful site!
