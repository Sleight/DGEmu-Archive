Switch. I don't know if it has a different name (or even exists in most other parts of the world!), but over here it's a game where everyone puts down a card, which has to follow a set of rules, of which they include; same suit, same number, or an Ace, which can be used at any time. There are many other rules, such as, if you manage to play a 7, then you can play the rest of the cards you have which are of the same suit as said 7. You can also play doubles, as long as your start with one of the same suit as the card which is currently in play. The winner is whoever manages to get rid of all their cards first.The rules are rather complicated, and I can't really be bothered typing them all out, but I think it's a great game. Easy enough to get the hang of, and it's fun, fast paced, and, to an extent, rather strategical.
