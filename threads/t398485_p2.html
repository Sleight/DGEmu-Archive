Minus XI (11) if you count that as you.Final Fantasy is more of a story-telling game. That is always how I have viewed it. You simply move the character, have fun battling--and sit down with a nice cup of tea and listen to the characters either whine about their problems--or save the world. Majority of the time, it is both rolled into one. I suppose most can find that annoying, but I somewhat enjoy it; after all it is interesting and different every time. Different classes, different spells, and probably a bit more focused on six characters over FE's 43+ or how many they friggin have. It's a lot more personal then Fire Emblem, focusing on the characters a tad bit more, if you catch my drift.In the end, I'll say FE and FF tie in my book.
