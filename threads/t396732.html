Equip all your party with energy shields. That should take most of the damage from the blaster fire. Also, if you're having an especially hard time, use stims for a small extra boost too.And try having all your party members attack the same person. That way you can finish off individal people quickly. I generally kill the normal mandolorians first, then the leader, then any remaining underlings.Also, try and get heavy hitting people like Zaalbar in the fight. If he's got an energy sheild to protect him from the blaster fire he should be fine. I would suggest not taking Mission Vao into that fight... she generally gets creamed.
