EEmessaiJun 21 2008, 09:44 PM
This game is definitely a tough-run for someone like me who's not really oriented towards these kinds of games, but I gotta' admit it grows on you. A lot of the eye-catching special effects in this game (mainly the OTs) are incredibly invigorating when that one ninja in the back of the room has been shooting you with fire arrows for the last half a minute and you just can't wait to run one your weapon through his... well which ever limbs are remaining when you actually get the OT. The difficulty of the bosses is, however, severely undermined by weapon choice. Killing certain bosses is incredibly easy with specific weapons, for what ever reason. I'm not sure if that was a design decision (or if it was a good or bad one) but it's definitely noticeable. Alexei the first time through was incredibly irritating (I used the uhh... crap, the claws, I forgot what they're called sorry) and by the time I died I was ready to feed my controller to a trash compactor, but I whipped out the Dragon Sword and voop~ Easy stuff. In any case, I'm definitely looking forward to beating this game... along with burning off all the stress it builds up! -.-x
