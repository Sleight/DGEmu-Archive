DDJ Night Force 9ORIGINAL POSTJun 06 2013, 02:20 PM

Not sure how many of you are a member of these but they have been disappearing rapidly lately due to the legal climate. I am guessing the people behind them got REALLY nervous of being found out by copyright vultures. First BitGamer goes down and now more recently, Undergound-gamer and GazelleGames closed in rapid succession all for the same reason. What probably hurt the most was losing Underground-gamer. The majority of BitGamer and GazelleGames's content can be found widely available on public trackers but Underground-gamer has a lot of very old and rare titles on there (including a fan translation of the first King's Field, countless roms of beta versions for several games, and many other things). That MAME set I uploaded to Dgemu a while back came from there too. I have also used it to keep my MAME roms up to date as well as find the latest MameUI x64 release complete with High score saving and the Cave drivers enabled. DasNamenlose from this site invited me there originally when I was looking for PSX ISO's to put up on Dgemu for power members to enjoy (sadly the project was never completed). Sir VG was on there too (invited by me) and would release his latest DS rom dumps and even full sets so that was really nice too.

 

Was anyone else here a member of those sites or perhaps others that have closed their doors out of fear of legal repercussion? Kudos to Ben / Dragons Master for keeping Dgemu open all this time and avoiding all the burning fires (figuratively speaking). I guess it comes down to how much it sucks that United States law (most notably the DMCA) is dictating what the rest of the world is allowed to access. That is VERY VERY wrong.

 

Any other sites like these been closing by the way?
