"RPG MAKER VX" is a software that enables you to create your original role playing game without any special knowledge. The process is very simple. First, create a map which becomes the stage of your adventure. Secondly, setup the hero, monsters, items, magic, etc,all the things you need to play the RPG. Finally you can complete your very own RPG by creating the events and placing them around the map.Minimun System Requirements:Microsoft® Windows® 2000 / XP / Vista PC with 1.0GHz Intel® Pentium® III equivalent or higher processor At least 256MB of system RAM 1024x768 or better video resolution 100MB available hard disk spaceRecommended System Requirements:Microsoft® Windows® XP / Vista PC with 2.0GHz Intel® Pentium®4 equivalent or higher processor At least 512MB of system RAMHow to get the Key Generator to work1. After you install RPG Maker XP and open it up, a window will pop up. Click 'Already Pai'2. Open up the Key Generator.3. On the next window in RPG Maker XP, click Generate Code.4. Copy and Paste the serial given from the Key Generator Piece by piece into the window. 5. Then click 'Print Web' 6. Copy the URL and paste it into the Key Generator Program.7. Click generate Activation Code 8. Copy the serial number that comes out in the 3rd box, then go back to RPG Maker VX9. Then click Submit Auth. Code (or W/e, closing THAT window only helps too.)10. Paste the serial number in the box where it asks you for it.11. Submit the number and enjoy.Distributing your GameRPG Maker VX allows you to distribute your game to other people so that they can play it on your computer. But after you've published your game using RPG Maker VX, you can send it/distribute it to other people. The other people do not need the RPG Maker Software for it to work, but they need the RGSS-RTP, that's the plugin/software that allows RPG Maker VX created games to work on their computer. So when you distribute it, it's best that you include the setup in there as well, and let them know that they need the RGSS-RTP to be installed. It doesn't take that much space anyway (only about 40-60MB). I personally prefer RPG Maker XP, but VX is good (I have both on my comp anyway) VX is a bit easier to use, so this is highly recommended for the new comers. And this will work on vista without having to go through the trouble of putting it in Compatibility Mode so that's one less step for vista users to do. And yes install the RGSS-RTP and RPG Maker VX.
