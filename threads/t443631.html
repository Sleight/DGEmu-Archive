Hey everyoneI've decided to make a thread about the Nazi zombie levels; they are great as they have unlimited number of waves. At the moment there is only three stages which are downloadable online; off course, the new one are not for free :P A summary on all of the nazi zombie level: In the world of nazi zombie, you are in a world of terror. A terror which is never forgotten, each stage consist of boarded up windows which can be repaired when destroyed by these zombies. Conversely, there is blocked paths which has to be unlocked through points; points which can be obtained through killing zombies. Weapons can be purchased on the wall or from a mystery ? box which cost 950 points. There is a wide array of ways from getting points, this includes killing zombies, repairing windows, and repairing broken walls. When the player get swiped by the nazi zombies, you will be on your last stand; this is when you can only use your knife or handgun (ray gun, magnum, pistol.etc) whats really interesting is that you can revive players which are on their last stand (please note that perks will be abolished once your back up - this only applies to the new nazi zombie level). Theres no need to worry if you can't save your team members because they will respawn on the new wave, despite losing their weapons and perks. Ultimately, surviving is what really matters, if your team mates are down then you are in trouble. Play tactical or get eatened up by the killer nazi zombies.The three explicit nazi zombie level is:Nacht der Untoten - A German name for "Night of the undead". This surely is an average zombie shooting level for starters. Verrückt - A German name for "insane", this level is totally fully of madness. Perks machines are now available and each has special ability which gives the player a boost; Sleight of Hand, Double Tap, Juggernaut, and quick revive. Sleight of hand makes the player reload much more faster, hence the name sleight of hand. Double tap makes the player shoot much more faster and this comes in handy when your using a machine gun. Juggernaut is one of the best perks to stay alive, this keeps your normal health gauge maximized, so instead of getting killed by 1 swipe you will get killed by 3 or 4 swipes. Quick revive is also needed so that you can save your team mates; without your team mates then you will be killing all of them yourself. Traps are also available, however you will need to turn on the electric beforehand. In return you will be able to electricute any nazi zombies passing through the doors of doom. Shi No Numa - A Japanese name for "Marsh of death", this particular stage includes hellhounds; fast running vicious hounds waiting to eat fresh meat from chosen people. This stage is the largest out of the others as you will be able to travel outside the house; the swamp. Though this map may sound huge, there is more traps like the spinning turbine "the flogger". It rotates at a speed that anything walking by it will be spiked to death, alternately will be flying in solo; to get through this, you will need to crouch down and walk to the other side. A zip-line can also be used to travel to different places. There is four huts in this place with four swamps in total; each hut contains one perk machine but will be at random.This post was written by me, so please do not copy it without any permission.
