I'm going to post a bit more 'meta' since its always better to have info than not. Also even some of the story bosses can get quite hard and you'll need a good setup to beat them.

IMO the two best skillsets that you'll definitely want somewhere on your team are Spiritmaster and Salve-maker.

-Spiritmaster's Enigma (null elemental damage) and Fairy's Ward (null status effects) are indispensible for many fights and Stillness (null all damage for 2 turns) is super useful even if you're not abusing it.

-Salve-maker is the only skillset that can inflict weaknesses, which is great at increasing damage for your attackers. It's also the best class at single target heals, buffs, revives, debuffs, and can do 9999 damage with just Attack Item Amp. Basically its the jack of all trades and master of all, only caveat being that you have to spend money...though there are various ways to make a ton of money so that's not really an issue.

-Vampire is also up there among the most useful of jobs. It has a party full heal with Blood Thirst + White Wind which requires no SP or M.Def investment like generic healers do. Genome skills are mostly multitarget physical attacks that also cause debuffs/status, cost no BP, and are quite strong as well, making it much superior to Valkyrie.

-Rangers aren't that great because most bosses are human and have no family weakness to exploit. Multiburst also isn't that good for its BP cost. However Precision and Hawkeye are vital supports to any attacker using autoattacks (Ninja, Pirate) so you'll definitely want to level Rangers regardless.

-A setup I like for Arcanist:
Swordmaster Sub
Pierce M.Def
Zero
Gloom (replace when you can hit damage cap w/o it)

Use Interment x4 to get your MP to 0 and trigger Zero, then cast Interment with Free Lunch. Easily hits 9999, basically is an improved Amp Strike Pirate that also hits multiple foes and ignores defense.
FYI about Arcanists, Exterminate does disgusting amounts of damage (I was hitting 9999s without any damage boosting supports) but its hard to get the Poison=>Exterminate combo off on bosses as most of them resist poison. Great for mobs though. If you do go that route, use Red Mage subclass so you can also groupcast Reflect and Protect/Shell.

-Conjurers are actually great at hitting huge magic damage, though you can't see what its actually capable of without Bravely Second. Susano-o Invocation is the only way to get to max crit without spending multiple turns/specials, and combined with Magic Crit, it effectively doubles your damage. Conjurer is also great with Phantom weapon as they have the best M.Atk (tied with Arcanist) and an A rank in swords, so you'll get high P.Atk and M.Atk. The last slot is a BP generator, which is important to have unless you're doing Hasten World + Stillness. The two methods of BP regen are Poison + BP Recovery and My Hero + Mimic. BP Recovery requires everyone to have the support (meaning you cant use Ninjas since they require all 5 SP), it also prevents you from using Fairy's Ward or Reflect... otherwise its the superior method. My Hero setup for fights where you need status immunity, it also requires usage of Low Leverage to work properly. With BP Recovery setup, you'll have extra BP that is used to cast Enigma and Shell/Protect, otherwise someone else on the team should have Spiritmaster sub. Either the Salve-Maker or the generator should have Slow World to cripple the enemy, since you'll still have plenty of BP.

For speed tiers, you want your damage dealers to be faster than the BP generator so you go into the negatives every turn and then get filled back up to +3.
