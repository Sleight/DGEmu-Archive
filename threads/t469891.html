It won't work. First of all, we're all in a stalemate. We all know how terrible the power of nuclear weapons are, and we all know that if one nation uses them, you better be sure there will be retaliation. We all fear a nuclear holocaust, and because we are sure that a worldwide nuclear war will result in that, we remain in a stalemate. As for actual disarmament, thats a no too. Take plain old gun laws. The idea of no one having guns is great, but we all know that will never happen. If guns are outlawed, the black market is still there. We have to be able to use guns in order to protect ourselves against malicious gun owners. If the malicious people know they will most likely get killed committing a crime, they probably won't do it. Same thing with nuclear weapons. Say the US, Russia, UK, France, etc destroy their nukes. We know there will be nations like North Korea that will try to secretly make the weapons to gain power. Only by remaining in this stalemate are we protected.
