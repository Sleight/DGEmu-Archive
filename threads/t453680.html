I must refer you to article here, which discusses the future of the English language.In it, they suggest that English is following in the footsteps of Latin. It's a powerful almost universal language, the language of a vast cultural empire that spans several nations. Eventually, it will split apart, as people of various cultures speak it differently. Eventually, these different dialects will become so different, they will evolve into new languages. This is what happened with Latin. In the article, it is stated that language historians believe that even in 300AD, the elite's classical Latin and the "vulgar" Latin of the masses.
