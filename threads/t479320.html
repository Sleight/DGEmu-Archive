So I don't know if anyone here is a fan of rooting and flashing android, but have any of you played with Halo yet?

It's probably one of the best things I've ever seen. It's basically a notification replacement that makes your notifications pop up in a small collapse able window on scree inside of a bubble (similar to facebooks chat heads but more fleshed out). Essentially, any time you get an alert, you can open a small floating instance of the app that notified you on top of what you are already doing. It is a full version of the app, not just a mini version or an interactive notification.

For instance, if you get an email the halo will light up and show the face of the person that emailed you. If you click it then a floating gmail will appear. The full app with all the options and functionality. You can use it as you please them tap it away and the Halo will hide until you get another notification.

Anyways, just thought this was awesome. I've been trying it and I like it. Anyone else?
