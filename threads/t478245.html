milo-' timestamp='1352855180' post='5049182 wrote:

I guess you go through the battles to grind for stickers, kind of like MMBN.

Well, you can find stickers in the worlds, you can make them, you can buy them, or you can get them from battles. You mostly battle for coins. Anytime you hit an enemy that is out of health (your jumps can hit many times, not just once or twice) you get a coin for your troubles. You can spend coins to buy stickers or to spin the battle wheel, which is basically a slot machine that lets you get bonuses and attack more times in a turn.

It's a bit strange to actually use items in an RPG. I'm a hoarder, generally, and just keep all my items throughout RPGs, so it takes a bit of getting used to realizing that everything you get is expendable because you will definitely get more. I'd say the lack of tutorials or visual cues or hints is somewhat annoying. Some people might like it because it's hardcore, but there have been a couple of instances, where I would not have known what to do if I hadn't read it in a review...
