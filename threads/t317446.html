Rrichaard16Sep 09 2007, 12:09 PM
aside from the fact that when compared to dino crisis 2 this game was very poor and a waste of money, i do happen to have itthe final boss (Cebalrai) is a very difficult fight however it can be doneAlways hang back a lot, don't try to get too close... keep your character moving between the middle and one of the sides too to help avoid being bitten. Instead of constantly blasting the dino all the time, rather wait for it to attack you... then your laser should focus on the attacking head and you can shoot it, causing it to stop trying to bite you... this helps a lot in saving your life.The Tempests are a huge advantage in this section. Keep using them... while you are taking care of the heads, the Tempests will help get its life down...Once the creature transforms, keep moving from side to side again. Try to stay fairly close to the monster this time, as then it will usually shoot it's shock attack over your character... you're not completely safe though, but try to stay off the walls except when the middle head fires...And once you get it's life down low enough, you're in for an extremely pleasant surprise. A little help that will make the rest of the battle a breeze... Hope this helps!good luck
