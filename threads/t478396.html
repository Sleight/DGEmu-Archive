KKula DiamondDec 07 2012, 09:37 AM

One of the best things about DMC4 combat are the enemy types, each class of enemies should be approached differently, there are no filler enemies that just look different to increase the number of enemy types without offering something different to game play.
 
Ok, now, for projectiles, lots of enemies have them and some are really fast, for me the most annoying one is Frost's Ice Machingun, but anyway, most enemies don't go for projectile attacks unless they're away from you. Don't know if you already knew this, but off-screen enemies don't attack you, so if you're fighting a group of enemies at close range and there are couple of Frosts watching from afar, turn the camera away from them before they start throwing Ice bullets, same thing for the dogs, the reptiles and Mega Scarecrows(their stupid spinning attacks are bullshit). Oh! And you're invincible during Devil Trigger activation, use that to as a last moment dodge.
 
Calibur is a stupid move, back then forward on an analogue is stupid, it has its uses, but you're not missing much by not using it.
 
Think of Table Hoper like a parry or Dante's Just Guard, you have to do the dodge just before you get hit(has to be sideways), Good for building style/meter and showing off but nowhere as useful as the Royal Guard stuff.
 
For other moves, hmm... Roulette Spin is really good for air combos, Exceed is pretty fun and an essential part of Nero moves, but to get a lot from it you have to buy the EX version of the other moves, so it's a huge investment, good thing you can unlearn moves to try new ones, see what you like, table hopper and calibur are some of the least useful stuff, unlearn that shiit.


Against the flying Death things, you can take off their invisibility using Devil Bringer too(range upgrade is pretty nice), but the smaller ones are no problem, the big death guy(Faust) though can be a pain, best way to deal with him is after taking off his invisibility, get close to him and activate Devil Trigger to bounce his body in the air then immediately do Showdown(have to buy it first), it's Nero's most damaging move and should kill him before he can turn invisible again.
