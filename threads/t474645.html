What is pokesav?
It is a modification tool that writes codes processed by a hardware system such as an Action Replay, or software program such as an Emulator. Here is a picture for a general idea of what pokesavlooks like:
http://larryhadalitt...les/Pokesav.jpg

You can create anything from money cheats to pokemon modifications of any kind to item cheats and various others. Note: Most pokemon modification cheats such as one I am going to paste below replace all in your party or particular boxes. Also you can make the stats of pokemon 999, but they will return to the IV/EV status once put in any box in your PC, so maxing out the IV/EV levels is probably the best way to have ridiculously strong pokemon. I will come back later with a more detailed explanation and tutorial with how pokesav is used. But for now I recommend watching tutorials on youtube as visual reference is much better and makes everything that much simpler.
