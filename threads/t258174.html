The thing plays disc-based videogames, and also has the ability to play digital video and audio, as well as make use of other technologies via its USB ports and Memory Stick port. Its wireless capabilities will allow free wi-fi.WHAT DOES IT LOOK LIKE?-Well, I’ll let IGN tell you since I’m too lazy to via this nice pic Looks damm nice don’t it? Capable of storing up to 1.8GB of data, the UMD is a disc format enclosed in a small cartridge.IT's not your normal disc. It's brand newWILL I BE ABLE TO GET THESE UMDS ANY TIME SOON?-Nope.Only Sony will be able to create UMD media.the company has no plans to make external UMD writers devices, and even third-party publishers will not be able to create their own discs without Sony's ability to press the data to UMD.Blame the pirates not Sony.SO, DO I NEED A MEMORY CARD OR SOMETHING?-The PSP uses Sony's Memory Stick format, small rewriteable cards. There are available in different sizes ranging from 32 mb to up to 512. The sticks aren't only used for games, but they are also used to view video files and used for the PSP's secondary feature, mp3 player. You can store your mp3s or A3TRAC (or something like that) on the stick and play em on your psp.These cards are already available since many Sony products use them. Check your local electronics store to see what they look like. The specific Memory Stick format to be supported by PSP is the Memory Stick PRO Duo format, which also makes it compatible with the standard Memory Stick Duo format. I think that the PRO has quicker file transfer or something like that compared to the regular DuoMP3 PLAYER. SWEET. WHAT ELSE DOES IT HAVE?Its other secondary feature is its ability to play UMD movies. IF companies feel like it. They can release their DVDs in UMD format. Only 2 movies are confirmed so far. FF7: Advent Children and a Gundam Wing Collection. Recently, Sony has bought MGM studios, so they have access to their movies (which I believe include the 007 films).Other then that, there's nothing much more known.SO WITH ALL THESE FEATURES, WHAT ABOUT THE BATTERY?-The PSP uses a recharable battery that gets 4-6 hours with the backlight set to max, volume at half max, headphones instead of speakers, and wireless LAN not in use. You get 4-5 watching movies. It all really matter on the games you play. More extensive powered games will waste it quicker but less power gives more battery lifemovie playing is about 5-8 depending on where you're streaming it from.
