Sorry to be a pain, but what is the difference between a bad dump and an over dump, and do either mean the rom doesn't work?
SSir VGOct 02 2007, 07:06 AM
A bad dump means that while the dump is the proper size, the contents are incorrect, and sometimes do not work (more often, they work, but something in game is really messed up...usually the graphics).Overdumps similar to good dumps, but the size of the ROM is too big for what it actually is. These games almost always work fine...they're just bigger then they need to be. (So in other words, if a ROM is 32Mbit, but it was dumped at 64Mbit, that's an overdump.)For those that need a more simplistic/graphic explanation, let's use this.Let's say I'm dumping a game called "Alphabet".A good dump turns up a rom with contents ABCDEFGHIJKLM.A bad dump would be (for example) ABCDEFHGIJKML. Same size, but contents are incorrect compared to the good dump.An overdump would be (for example) ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOP. The contents of Alphabet are there, but there's extra stuff after it that isn't needed, making the ROM larger than it needs to be.Hope that's a good explanation for you.
