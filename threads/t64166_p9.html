SShadow ZeroNov 14 2006, 10:29 AM
Seems that there's a lot of opinons here. Might as well add mine.My breakdown is as Follows:Treecko: Is one of the few pure-grass pokemon in the game, and the ONLY one available for the better half of it. Sadly, that's not saying much. Sure, he gets some decent grass moves early on, but as you actually get into the game it makes less of a difference because of the other part-grass types who can pick up the slack. Ovberall, ranks lowest of the three because while it doesn't have any TRULY Glaring weaknesses (IIRC, once you beat the First gym you're set until near the halfway point of the game), End game it just doesn't stack compared to the late game Supers/Dragons. One plus, though: with emerald's reorganizing of the E4, Treecko is the only one of the three that can Sweep the Finals now. worth a thought.Mudkip: Good overall choice. Will get you through the first gym with little effort (with water gun, of all things...), and has a decent typing to boot. So what's the problem? Mudkip's Later forms are basically Ground pokes with water tacked on for the hell of it. Past Water gun, Swampert and Marshtomp learn few (if any) water moves, forcing you to use precious TMs to assist. Granted, the ability to use surf makes up for this (somewhat). Battle-wise, Mudkip is hard to type-over because even though it has ONE glaring weakness (grass) there are very few pokes that can effectively take advantage of it (but once they do, it's over), as Mudkip is a freaking TANK. Of course, the problem with that is that it's also slow as all hell, so that needs to be taken into consideration. My Judgement ranks him in a tie for the top spot.Torchic: NOW we're talking. Torchic is easily the strongest of the three early-game, barring the first gym which owns the crap out of him. But otherwise, Torchic becomes a solid powerhouse Poke with excellent offensive potential....not to mention that barring Kyogre, he's pretty much the best fire Poke in the game, without trading (and that's saying something, when your choices are limited to Slugma, Vulpix/Ninetails, and Torkoal). A solid and reliable pick for anyone, with potential to have staying power up until the final four, at which point he gets utterly slaughtered (when before he could sweep the entire thing singlehandedly).Still, an overall powerful, and COOL poke that can add nicely to any team.My Verdict goes to Torchic, but Mudkip is a good choice anyway.
