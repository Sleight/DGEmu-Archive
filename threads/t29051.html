hatu wrote on Jan 9 2005, 04:24 PM:
EA's sport gamesFinal Fantasy 7
To say that FF7 is truly an over-rated game is hard to argue. It's success and true value lies not in it's gaming aspects, but rather in it's business and marketing aspects. Not that it's a bad game, in fact it really IS a gem. But the real deal behind the craze is that FF7 was the game that brought final fantasy into hi gear in the states. When it came out there weren't that many games for the original Playstation, and even fewer that were of it's calibur. That's why this game sticks out so much. This game was so far ahead of the competition that it simply wowed and wooed audiences. And because the PS lacked a quantity of games, many non-RPG players decided to take a chance and see what all the rave was about. The game had a incredibly deep and thorough storyline, something that many new RPGers had yet to experience, and brought them into this genre. This meant squaresoft now had a greater audience to sell their games to, and thus giving them the potential to push their limits and boundaries as a business. That is why FF7 truly IS a gem. It's just that when we look back into the past, with all the advanced the gaming industry has made it doesn't seem so big anymore. Now that we have moved on, it's luster doesn't attract us anymore. And that is why it doesn't seem so legendary anymore.
