I'm going to do a comparison with the GBA games:The strategy: Yugioh requires building a deck with low and high level monsters, magic and spell cards, effect cards and a good strategy to go with it. Pokemon you just need moves and strong monsters, so the point goes to YugiohThe graphics: Yugioh has one image for each card, Pokemon has two for each monster, and two back sprites as well [normal/shiny]. So this point to Pokemon.The attack styles: Pokemon have four ways of attacking, the four moves. Yugioh monsters can attack only one way, by attacking a card on the field/the opponent's life points. So this point also goes to Pokemon.The animation on the GBA games: Yugioh games feature no animation except a slight vibration of the cards when attacking, but have fast, clear animations [placing card on the field etc.] Pokemon has an animation for every attack, and as there are hundreds of attacks, this point is to Pokemon.Defending: Yugioh cards can be put in 'defense mode', Pokemon have more moves to defend themselves but can still take damage from some attacks. Point not awarded.Amount of monsters: You can have from 1-6 Pokemon in the team, and from 40+ in Yugioh. Point to Yugioh.Amount of monsters in play: You can only have one or two Pokemon in play, and five monsters in Yugioh. This point goes to Yugioh.Playing as RPG: In Yugioh, collecting all the cards is a relatively simple task: no trades needed, and you can even put the cards you have in the game via the password. In Pokemon, you need to trade with at least three different games to catch all 386 Pokemon currently available, there is a plot [not much of a plot, but it's there] to Pokemon as well, so this point to Pokemon.Making monsters stronger: Yugioh cards can be equipped with up to 5 cards to boost power, and Pokemon evolve up to twice. When Pokemon evolve ALL stats increase by a little, using magic cards on monsters in Yugioh sometimes increases one stat, sometimes both, but by 200-900. Point to Yugioh.TIEBREAKER: Google search.Pokemon: 48,000,000 resultsYugioh: 24,200,000 resultsThis point, and the match, to Pokemon.Yugioh: 4pts Pokemon: 5pts
