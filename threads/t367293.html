"250 man" means "250 lots of 10,000", i.e. 2.5 million."nin" means "people".Japanese persons' names very often contain Kanji (characters adopted from the Chinese). Apart from the commonly used ones, most characters are found only in names. Thus there is a need to have them in a dictionary.This package looks like a load of test/exam material on Kanji. It also holds a look-up of idiom phrases made up of 4 Kanji characters.
