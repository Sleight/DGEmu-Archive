KKirie MotobaFeb 18 2014, 05:04 PM

Eh, you can't even spend a little bit of money to replace it?

Need to find your serial number to get the correct part number, but in average in equivalent models:

http://www.ebay.com/bhp/hp-pavilion-g6-screen

 

55$ for a new screen, free shipping.

 

Pavilion G6 should be easy to replace:

- The 2 rubber pads at the bottom near the laptop itself where the hinges are, use a flat screwdriver to remove them. There should be 2 screws there holding it the hinge to the LCD case.

- The LCD bezel itself are all plastic clips around. To unclip, use your finger to pull from inside out (yes, you'll get tons of fingerprints on the broken LCD, who cares...)

- Once off, I think you should have 4 to 8 screws to remove on each side of the screen that holds it down to the brackets.

- Slowly pull it out, you'll notice a single flat cable for the monitor input, and possible a second cable from the inverter (though most models will bring both those together into 1 cable) all held down by tape. Untape, and unplug by pulling on it.

- Align new screen in, plug it, put the screws back on the brackets.

- Before closing up, turn on your laptop and make sure your screen is working fine, no dead pixels or anything.

- If all good, then just shut down your laptop, clip the bezel back, put the screws back with the rubber pads, and done!

 

Takes max 30 minutes to replace.

For the place I used to work at, we charged 120$ for the screen + 80$ to replace it.

That's how you cut it down from a 200$ budget to like 55$. :3
