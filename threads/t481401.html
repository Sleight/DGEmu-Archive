lol yeah but that means you are looking at the wrong brand of monitors. Look for an NEC Multisync. And yes I realize that there are still people that use composite (the NEC can take composite, component, HDMI, VGA, sometimes DVI, and coax (camera not tuner) and I've seen these as big as 70in. The only drawback to the NEC Multisync is the no tuner but for someone like me a tuner chip in a tv is damn near useless. and then there is price but this is a "professional grade" product we are talking about.

As for projectors...I love mine and my nice white wall in my living room. lovely setup really, but if you are worried about lamp life, look into the LED Lamp models. they may not have a lot of power (lumens wise), but LEDs go for 30,000 hours or more before burning out. the DLP color wheel is more likely to break before the lamp is on those... unless you get an LCD model, but then you get image ghosting which is not fun for gaming or watching sporting events. Can you tell that I worked in that industry yet?

As for Chromecast... My Roku 2 xs and Roku 3 both can do everything the chromecast can do and more. Not to mention without needing Google's input to function. I can send videos to it from firefox.
