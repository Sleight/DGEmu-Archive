So I got the capture card...turns out it doesn't do HDCP bypass either, but instead uses a D-terminal wire (provided) which plugs ino the SCART (component/composite) port. This apparently ouputs in 1080p anyways, and then the card converts it to HDMI out for passthrough to the TV...I'm assuming the Avermedia card works the same way.

This setup was actually bad for me since I use a monitor/projector which don't have audio and instead use the component wires to get audio out...now the D-terminal cable takes up the component slot so I could only capture audio and not hear it. So...I actually played for an extended period of time (Xillia) over the weekend with the capture card doing passthrough over D-terminal...and noticed that the game looked super jaggy and generally not as good as it used to.  Upon further research, D-terminal actually uses the exact same analog signal as component...in other words, outputting in 1080p is a lie, its totally upscaled and thinking it looks as good as HDMI is just a placebo effect lol.  After switching back to HDMI, I realized that it was, in actuality, much inferior.

 

So, if you wanted to play the game in 1080p with passthrough on a capture card, the only options are actually:

 

HDMI video out, component audio out

HDMI video out, optical audio out

HDMI video out, HDMI audio out (need to split the audio out of HDMI via an extractor such as this)

 

This also means that doing HDCP bypass is a requirement, not an option. 

 

So in summary...everyone who streams/records gameplay on PS3 is either also playing the game with crappy upscaled video, or using some clunky hardware workaround to do HDCP bypass - pretty much fail on Sony's part for implementing HDCP on all content.  This also could all be solved if the PS3 could output video through HDMI and component at the same time so you don't have to do passthrough...it even already has this feature for audio, just not video =/   ...another PS3 fail

And now, another "HDCP is dumb" post, now concerning next gen!

So next gen, supposedly you can do all your recording and streaming without a capture card because of XBone's dvr and PS4's share button. But in reality, in-system options for something like that which can consume massive system resources are bound to be extremely limited.
Most likely, both systems will be recording in low quality, highly compressed video and have their streaming options optimized for low bitrates...also Sony has announced that game devs can disable sharing on PS4 games, and the XBone has a time limit on recording length. And besides that, neither Microsoft or Sony is likely to let you record content to hdd and export it to PC, removing it from their system...which means you miss out on all the features recording/streaming through PC provides, such as editing, adding in commentary or other PC audio, all the fancy Xsplit stuff, etc

So in the end, you'd still want to use a capture card. Both systems now have Blu-ray drives and Netflix/Hulu/w/e and the XBone supposedly even does cable TV...so its expected that HDCP will be on everything, like it is now on the PS3. The difference however is that next-gen consoles no longer have component video out, only HDMI...so you literally have no option but go through HDCP (and cards that only capture component will become obsolete except on Wii U).
So that means having some means of HDCP bypass will be even more important.
