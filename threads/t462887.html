JJaghatNov 16 2010, 03:46 AM
Sorry.Don't mind the optic chiasm bit. It's just to mean that if you have something in only one eye, I think that means it's before the chiasm. The chiasm is a place in the middle of your head where the information from both eyes converge and then split again, but in different bundles. Between your eye and the chiasm, the info comes in from both nerves, each one carrying the information of its entire eye. After the chiasm, what goes to the left occipital is what you see on your left, etc.If you were to have blindness in only a section of your eye, or a section of your field a view (important difference), that would sometimes precisely indicate where the lesion occured. Though if we speak lesions, it's mostly long-term damage. Since your problem was over in two minutes, then I imagine it probably indicates local stress.Or something completely different and irrelevant. I don't know all possible causes for blindness. I just wouldn't delay.
