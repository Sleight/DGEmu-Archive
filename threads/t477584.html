LlaughingpiggyOct 05 2012, 04:46 AM
I recently finished 999. I got only 4 out of 6 endings, but I finished the true ending. It was pretty good, though the extra playthroughs were a bit tedious (luckily they have a speed-up button). It's not quite like pheonix wright, but it is still a puzzle game with a plot that makes you curious as to who the real plotter/murderer is.

I'm a big fan of pheonix wright, and my honest opinion is that 999 can't really match up to PW in terms of character consistency. The puzzle style is a little different, and the cut scenes in 999 take up most of the game. It also has a much darker tone, and murders are actually taken seriously in 999 (and maybe described with a few too many details). However, the plot is indeed very mystifying and I found myself really, really, wanting to find out what really happens in the true ending*. The plot is also very complicated, and those long dialogues that seem like tangents actually come back later to piece into one awesome story.

Over all, I'd say pretty confidently that you will enjoy 999 if you like pheonix wright and you like reading. Those cut scenes are really long!

*My enthusiasm may be biased, as I hit the true ending in my first go without guides, and was hit with a 'to be continued', as one of the other endings was a prerequisite for the full true ending, and that feeling of incompletion just kept gnawing at me.
