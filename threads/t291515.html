Ok, so that hustla guy with the ridiculously strange name that we had to ban gave me an idea. If mods can suspend people who post spam, why not also be able to take reporting privileges away from mod pool spammers? As I see it, posting privileges and reporting privileges are two entirely different realms. We have had some cases where people have "Kyn00bi" syndrome; that is, while their posting habits may be annoying at worst, their reporting habits are just downright wasteful and spammy. I don't feel that someone should be suspended from posting on the board if it is only their reporting habits that are getting them warned. That last guy to get banned may have survived if I had any inclination to suspend him, but since I couldn't suspend him from reporting, I just kept warning him, thinking he might just understand what the hell he's doing.GameFAQs doesn't even allow members to report until they reach a certain status anyway, but I don't think we need to go that far. We should be able to suspend it though from anyone spamming, confusing it with the reply button, or just generally wasting our time with reports that don't help us. Perhaps have the report button take suspended people to a different page that tells them they can't report until a certain date and to take some time to read the reporting guide. I have confidence that AK, Firebat or DJ could code something like this with relative ease, considering some of the other things I've seen them put together.The question is, do we want to be able to do this, and would we rather have all moderators be able to do it, or even go so far as to only allow people with ACP access to do it? Would you rather we just suspend them from posting as well? I personally am in favor of allowing all moderators to suspend people from reporting, because some of these people are generally ok posters but just have difficulty in reporting, so I see no reason why we can't make a clear distinction in the two privileges.So, what does everyone think? If you're a moderator, would this be helpful to you for some cases (I know I could have used it some), and if you're an administrator, would you be willing to allow us moderators to exercise such power? Some feedback would be nice. Thanks.
