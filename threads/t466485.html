I am not sure if I liked it or not. On the one hand, it is very well done, the acting is great, and the film goes into a deep exploration of the characters and their motivations.On the other hand, it is intensely violent and dark, possibly to the point of absurdity. I seriously doubt a film such as this would ever make its way into U.S. theaters. People would likely protest that it might encourage copycat behavior of acts such as electrocuting and/or drowning a child and/or animals, stabbing someone to death with a kitchen knife, bludgeoning someone to death or detonating a bomb at an assembly, all perpetrated by teenagers.I planned to write a review of the film, but now I am not so sure.
