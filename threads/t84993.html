At request this thread has been made for all of you who need help flashing ROMs on your DS. Since most emulators can't play ROMs this is the only to play them.There are several different ways:1. GBA flash cart with either the NDS rom patcher by ]{ain or the WRG rom patcher2. Supercard - which is similar to the GBA flash cart, just works a lot better with NDS roms and doesn't always need patching3. M3 - seems to be the best out there so far. Its a lot like the GBAMP but its for the DS and can do basically everything from ROMs to Media.4. Neo Flash kit - first DS flash cart released, mostly used by DS Dev teams since they get them for free. It is quite expensive but has the highest compatability.5. XG Flash 2 turbo plus - don't know much about abit, but seems similar to the supercard6. Flash 2 Advance - standard flash cart with built in memoryIn order to use these there is a key element needed. Either the firmwire or flashme update for your DS. In order to do this you either need:1. dspassme2. passkey/magickeyor something along these lines.Once you have this you can follow these instructions here.here are some other links that may help:NDS patcher threadhttp://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=74678NDS firmwire (flashme)http://ds.gcdev.com/dsfirmware/DS homebrew appzhttp://nintendo-ds.dcemu.co.uk/M3 Adapterhttp://www.m3adapter.com/M3 forumhttp://m3-forum.com/Supercardhttp://www.linker4u.com/pp/success.aspBrotherBeavis Patched the Flashme to .gba for you if you need it as well, here:http://www.freewebs.com/freakanimes/Freaka...%20Firmware.htmHow to flash:1. Use dspassme/passkey/magic key and follow the instructions for firmwire updated i linked above.- your DS is now ready for flashing roms, media and homebrew.2. patch roms (if necessary) and flash onto flash cart (which ever one you use its all relatively the same).3. Now you're ready to play.here is a list of ROMs that won't work when patched and flashed on a GBA cart:http://users.skynet.be/DuNe/NDS/list.htmlHere is some help in case you brick your DS (as in screens won't turn on, but the green light does)http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showtopic=94957***note: if you are completely new to the flash cart scene, i advise using google and doing as much research as possible. I just got my PassMe2, so I thought I'd pass this info along from my testing so far.I'm using that with the Flash2Advance Ultra, using a USB linker to update my cart.Software Needed:1) wrg-loadme.ziphttp://releases.pocketheaven.com/?section=toolsPurpose: Patch your NDS ROMs2) passme2_mb.gbahttp://pepsiman.pwp.blueyonder.co.ukPurpose: Sync the Flash Cart and the PassMe23) NDS ROM of choicehttp://www.dgemu.com/nds.htmlPurpose: Your ROM, stupid!Ok, here's what you need to do. First, you load just the passme2_mb.gba onto the cart. After you've loaded it, pop your cart into the NDS and load it up in GBA mode. You'll get a list of NDS Game serials. You'll need to match this to what the PassMe2 was setup for. So for instance, if you had your PassMe2 coded for Castlevania: Dawn of Sorrow (U), you'll need to find NTR-ACVE-USA. Line that up and hit A, which will write to the SRAM some data. You're done with this, shut down the DS.Now you'll need to patch your NDS game. Unzip the ROM and the files from WRG-LoadMe. Select your game, and in the bottom drop down box, change it from Normal to "New Firmware" if you have the v4 or v5 DS. If you're not sure which you have, there's a way to find out. I'll explain later. Click "Apply Loader" and you're done. You have a .nds.gba file.Now flash your cart with just this new NDS game that has been patched (this will usually take 10-15 minutes). Once you're done, stick the GBA cart in, and your PassMe2 with the game it's programmed for into the NDS. Boot your NDS and away you go!The "new firmware" selection only applies to NDS v4 or v5 firmware. To figure out which you have:1. Insert a DS game in NDS slot. 2. Turn up your DS (Hold START for boot menu if you have it to auto boot your NDS/GBA game) and open Pictochat, room A. 3. Now remove the NDS cartridge from the NDS slot.4. Both screens should turn in the same color. The color 'chart': v1: DS will freeze, when you remove the cart. v2: Grayish Blue (thanks, tepples) v3: Dark Greenv4 Yellow v5: MagentaiQue: Dark Green FlashME: Dark Green So if you have Yellow or Magenta screens, you need to use "New Firmware".Remember that PassMe2s require a specific game. They can be reprogrammed, but they only work with ONE GAME. Have the game and know the serial code!
