Hmm...so I played D/P for a bit, and also did some research on the new pokes of generation IV. Here to post a few notable ones and ones with potentially battle-worthy stats...just for others to take a look over, so...enjoy. ^_^ Btw, names are in Romanji, so they might bit different in the English version.#389: DodaitosuType: Grass/GroundAbility: OvergrowStats:HP: 95Attack: 109Defense: 105SpAtk: 75SpDef: 85Speed: 56The Grass type starter...and weirdly, this time around, the Grass type one is all physical. STABed EQ with that Atk power is nice, otherwise its pretty slow and Grass moves are still mostly special...alot like Swampert from R/S/E except with Water and Grass switched.#392: GoukazaruType: Fire/FightingAbility: BlazeStats:HP: 76Attack: 104Defense: 78SpAtk: 104SpDef: 78Speed: 108Meh...Blaziken rip-off, and its not really much better except for the Spd. It can sweep both physical and special, though better special since it gets better moves on that side...like the new Flare Drive. Oh, and instead of the Fried Chicken, we have Son Goku this time. ;) #395: EnperutoType: Water/SteelAbility: TorrentStats:HP: 84Attack: 86Defense: 88SpAtk: 111SpDef: 101Speed: 60Steel type...that's cool for a starter. Nice typage overall, this probably plays alot like a Gard, relying on special tanking and its high SA rather than sweeping. However, it also learns Agility, so feel free to go on a rampage with a giant penguin. ^_^ #398: MukuhawkType: FlyingAbility: IntimidateStats:HP: 85 Attack: 120 Defense: 70 SpAtk: 50 SpDef: 50 Speed: 100Those stats say it can sweep...and it sure can, with the new Flying move, Brave Bird [120 power, recoil], or the old Aerial Ace. Indimidate's nice too. Plus, you can get the first form [Mukkuru] very early in the game.#407: RozureidoType: GrassAbility: Natural Cure/Poison PointStats:HP: 60 Attack: 70 Defense: 55 SpAtk: 136 SpDef: 90 Speed: 105 Oooh...an evil-looking Roselia evolution with lots of sweeping potential...me likes. ^_^ #409: Rampard:Type: RockAbility: UnconventionalStats: HP: 97 Attack: 165 Defense: 60 SpAtk: 65 SpDef: 50 Speed: 58Just...look at that freakish Atk stat! Its actually higher than the attack of every legendary poke except FR Deoxys...just BP it some Spd, and this thing could rip teams apart. And its not legendary at all, one of the two fossil pokes that you can get in every game. The other one has the same stats excpet with Atk switched with Def, which makes it not nearly as good.#445: GaburaisuType: Dragon/GroundAbility: Sand VeilStats:HP: 108 Attack: 130 Defense: 95 SpAtk: 80 SpDef: 85 Speed: 102 nonOMG...its Salamence! Except minus the crappy Aerial Ace and non-STABED EQs...this thing has got STABed EQ, and Dragon Claw that runs off physical atk...uber. If only it didn't still have the 4x weak to Ice...#461: WeavileType: Dark/IceAbility: PressureStats: HP: 70 Attack: 120 Defense: 65 SpAtk: 45 SpDef: 85 Speed: 125 Sneasal evolution...and I like that Atk and Spd, it'll make a very good Dark type physical sweeper, especially since quite a few new attacking Dark moves were added in D/P. #462: JibacoilType: Electric/SteelAbility: Magnet Pull/SturdyStats: HP: 70 Attack: 70 Defense: 115 SpAtk: 130 SpDef: 90 Speed: 60 Magneton evolution...sweet SA. ^_^ #464: DosydonType: Ground/RockAbility: Lightning RodStats: HP: 115 Attack: 140 Defense: 130 SpAtk: 55 SpDef: 55 Speed: 40 Whoo~! Super-Rydon...once again, drool-inducing stats. #467: BooburnType: FireAbility: Flame BodyStats: HP: 75 Attack: 95 Defense: 67 SpAtk: 125 SpDef: 95 Speed: 83 Magmar evolution. There's one for Electabuzz too...but this one has much better stat distribution. #470: LeafiaType: GrassAbility: Leaf GuardStats:HP: 65 Attack: 110 Defense: 130 SpAtk: 60 SpDef: 65 Speed: 95Kawaii~! Another Evee-lution, this time for Grass...not-so-good stat distribution this time, but its cute. #471: GlaciaType: IceAbility: Snow HideStats: HP: 65 Attack: 60 Defense: 110 SpAtk: 130 SpDef: 95 Speed: 65And another! Again with the funky stat dist...but who cares, its cute. :P #473: ManmooType: Ice/GroundAblility: Oblivious/Snow HideStats: HP: 110 Attack: 130 Defense: 80 SpAtk: 70 SpDef: 60 Speed: 80Piloswine evolution...and eww, so ugly. >.< Those stats say that it could rip through stuff with EQ though.#475: ErladeType: Psychic/FightingAbility: Humble HeartStats: HP: 68 Attack: 125 Defense: 65 SpAtk: 65 SpDef: 115 Speed: 80 Its the male version of Gard...finally no more cross-dressing Gards. :D It gets Swordsdance and some new Fighting moves too, definitely worth trying out.#476: DiaonseType: Rock/SteelAbility: Sturdy/Magnet PullStats:HP: 60 Attack: 55 Defense: 145 SpAtk: 75 SpDef: 150 Speed: 40 What is...this...thing...However, with that typage and stats, this could potentially tank better than Dusclops. Annoying to look at and play against. >.< #477: YonoirType: GhostAbility: PressureStats:HP: 45 Attack: 100 Defense: 135 SpAtk: 65 SpDef: 135 Speed: 45 Or...maybe I spoke too soon. :P Its all of Dusclops's tankish goodness...'cept now with some freakish attack power on Shadow Ball. #480: YuxieType: PsychicAbility: LevitateStats: HP: 75 Attack: 75 Defense: 130 SpAtk: 75 SpDef: 130 Speed: 95 Kanto had the Bird Trio, Johto had the Dog Trio, Hoenn had the Regi Trio...Shinou has the Fairy Trio, and Yuxie is the first one.Extremely yummy tank stats, movepool is kinda meh though.#481: EmuritType: PsychicAbility: LevitateStats: HP: 80 Attack: 105 Defense: 105 SpAtk: 105 SpDef: 105 Speed: 80 Fairy number two is very Mew/Celebi-like...even all around, I guess you could do anything with it [even Heal Bell since it learns that], making it very unpredictable.#482: AgnomeType: PsychicAbility: LevitateStats:HP: 75 Attack: 125 Defense: 70 SpAtk: 125 SpDef: 70 Speed: 115 Yuxie for defense, Emurit for versatality...so guess what this is? A toned down offensive Deoxys...still very cool though. It can also Explode [yay! ^_^]. #483: Diaruga:Type: Steel/DragonAbility: PressureStats: HP: 100 Attack: 120 Defense: 120 SpAtk: 150 SpDef: 100 Speed: 90 Here's ultimate Legendary number one, the Time traveler. And...geez, Steel Dragon has just got to be the most awesome defensive typage ever. Weak to Fighting and Ground, normal damage from Ice, Dragon, and Fire, and resistance to everthing else. Unfortunately, Diaruga [Dialga] doesn't have tank stats, and even 100 base is kinda average for a super-legendary...its typage more than makes up for that though. 150 is pretty nice, especially since its ultimate move, Luster Cannon , is SA based, but EQ and Dragon Claw aren't anymore. #484: ParukiaType: Water/DragonAbility: PressureStats:HP: 100 Attack: 120 Defense: 120 SpAtk: 150 SpDef: 100 Speed: 90 And...ultimate legendary number two, the Space traveler. Water/Dragon is IMO not quite as good as Steel/Dragon; less weaknesses [only one to Dragon], but not nearly as many resistances. Nevertheless, they're basically the same...stats are nearly identical, as are movepools. Well...maybe not movepools, I read somewhere that Aqua Tail is physical-based...#485: HeatranType: Fire/SteelAbility: Flash FireStats: HP: 91 Attack: 90 Defense: 106 SpAtk: 130 SpDef: 106 Speed: 77 Another legendary...this one with the Fire moves, even getting its own signature one [Magma Storm]. Tankish and awesome SA with another very cool typage that has tons of resistances...but also a x4 weak to Ground which means EQ bait...which might just turn this into an Aggron rehash. #486: RegigasType: NormalAbility: Slow StartStats:HP: 110 Attack: 160 Defense: 110 SpAtk: 80 SpDef: 110 Speed: 100 A Normal type legendary...that's new. And the fourth Regi, most powerful one too. ^_^ #487: GiratinaType: Ghost/DragonAbility: PressureStats:HP: 150 Attack: 100 Defense: 120 SpAtk: 100 SpDef: 120 Speed: 90 Another Dragon combo type Tier 2 legendary...these seem to be real popular in D/P. This one's a defensive oriented, though with a decidedly offensive moveset and a typage isn't nearly as good as Parukia or Diaruga's...#488: KureceliaType: PsychicAbility: LevitateStats: HP: 120 Attack: 70 Defense: 120 SpAtk: 75 SpDef: 130 Speed: 85 Hmm...Psychic, Levitate, tank stats, where've we seen that before? :P Kurecelia learns Mighty Guard though, which may give it and advantage over Yuxie.#490: ManaphyType: WaterAbility: Moist BodyStats:HP: 100Attack: 100Defense: 100SpAtk: 100SpDef: 100Speed: 100Traditional non-in-game-obtainable poke with 100 in every stat...that's Manaphy. It learns Tail Glow [no BP though, darn], and some intetersting new moves like Heart Swap and Aqua Ring for which I'm too lazy to look up what they do...#491: DarkraiType: DarkAbility: NightmareStats:HP: 70 Attack: 90 Defense: 90 SpAtk: 135 SpDef: 90 Speed: 125 A Dark type legendary with perfect specials sweeper stats, and equiped with new Dark moves...sigh.Too bad its another one of those only-obtainable-at-obscure-Nintendo-event pokes. #492: ShiemiType: GrassAbility: Natural CureStats: HP: 100 Attack: 100 Defense: 100 SpAtk: 100 SpDef: 100 Speed: 100 A weird Celebi-like thingy obtained in the same way...I'm not even going to bother...#493: ArseusType: NormalAbility: Multi-typeStats:HP: 120 Attack: 120 Defense: 120 SpAtk: 120 SpDef: 120 Speed: 120 Simply awesome stats...and its ability allows it to change its typage by equiping it with a set of Plate items.Another event-obtained poke though...Gameshark anyone?Well...that's that.
