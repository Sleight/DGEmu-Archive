For everyone with the MISSING KEYS.PLIST ERROR
1. Exit redsn0w and turn off your phone and unplug it
2. Plug your phone back in and then enter DFU mode without opening redsn0w yet. DFU=(3 sec lock button, 10 sec home and lock button, 15 second just home button).

6. Exit redsn0w, unplug your device, plug it back in and put the phone into DFU mode! Open redsn0w!
7. In redsn0w go to extras>>select IPSW (6.0).

When you're jailbreaking an iPod/Phone it seems like such a good idea until you get it done and realize the benefits are almost negligible since the things don't do anything particularly well nor do you get any real benefits in terms of customization (at least nowhere near the level of Android customization) and the only apps that you would possible want to pirate are either a) exceedingly cheap 2) have been in a humble android/indie bundle at some point for even cheaper 3) you already own on a superior platform or 4) suck.

 

All jailbreaking your iProduct manages to do is instill stability problems in the firmware and tether you to your computer on the off chance the new instability makes you have to reset, not to mention updating is a pain and you have to go through the same unwieldy process when you finally get tired of dealing with the jailbroken product and you decide it would just be simpler to go back to stock (which you will if you use it long enough)

 

iPod/Phones are for simple folk, and there's nothing wrong with that, but after you've tried out Android (especially JellyBean or Ice Cream Sandwich) it's hard to imagine why you'd ever want to use something so hamstrung as iOS.
