The three games are just amazing, and while I played Justice For All first, I found it to be the most lacking (story-wise, and character-wise) besides for a few characters in JFA. Ace Attourney being the first game was amazing. It introduced Phoenix and his mentor (Mia), it covered alot of the background that I didn't know about in JFA, which made it more in depth imo, but then again, I played it out of order. AA also came with a DS only case, which used the DS' touch screen to allow you to "scientifically investigate" your evidence, utilizing the ability of the touch screen. Sadly, this was lost in JFA and TnT. Now on to Trials and Tribulations, the last game in the trilogy, this game, was probably the most epic out of the three, with the exception of one case, but that's just my opinion. With flashback cases, and the explanation of things that happened all the way back in Ace Attourney, the last cases of this game really tied together alot of loose ends in the whole trilogy. In the end, I found myself spending around 10 hours for each case, (5 cases in Ace Attourney, 4/5? I forgot in Justice For All, and 5 for Trials and Tribulations.) Although, for the long cases, I spent quite a few hours more (20 hours for 1-5, and around 30 for 2-2, etc.) Anyways, I hope others enjoyed the PW Trilogy as much as I have, because it's an amazing game, with an amazing cast, and an epic storyline.
