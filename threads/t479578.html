KKirie MotobaAug 16 2013, 06:56 PM

For that price, it should be a must buy.

 

I've own a X-530 and X-540 back in the days, and they were both really good speaker sets, perfect to annoy your neighbors with awesome bass output, the satellites themselves do a good job to keep you in your movie or game.

 

Z-506 seems to be its successor, with pretty much similar specs. My opinion is to get it, you can't go wrong at the price (that usually gets you the equivalent, but in 2.1 speakers).

 

Make sure the audio output from your desktop supports surround sound 5.1. If you're still running your old overclocked P4, I have a feeling your onboard audio chipset does not support HD Audio.

 

Just look in the back of your comp:

- 3 audio jacks = you're screwed and need a sound card

- 5 audio jacks = you're good to go, it's 5.1 ready.
