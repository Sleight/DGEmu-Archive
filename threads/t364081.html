You may have seen that one S&S thread which suggested adding a debates section. Well, we figured it's worth a try at least. Here is the section:http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showforum=159All forum assistants (bmods) are assigned to this section, as it is a General Discussions subforum. Here are the basic expectations of moderators in this section:Read the pinned thread in that section (click here for link) so that you know its basic rules.Don't step in and interfere with discussion unless something is getting out of hand (i.e. flaming or going way off-topic for a long time). Of course, you're always welcome to participate in the debates if you wish.Deduct points from short, stupid posts that don't contribute to the discussion and then make sure to delete the post. Make sure to only do this to posts made after December 8th, 2008. (or December 9 if you're in the other part of the world).You don't need to be constantly checking threads for bad posts, but at least always check the reports and maybe quickly browse for overly short statements.This subforum will be in a testing phase until the start of January, 2008. At that time, we can review how things are going over there and will be expecting feedback from the moderators that have been actively covering the section. If it's turned out to be a complete failure, the section will be deleted and all threads will be moved back to General Discussions. If it's going fine, obviously it has the potential to be a permanent forum. Have fun with this, everyone! :(
