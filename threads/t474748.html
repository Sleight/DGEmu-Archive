There is an alternative for DGemu if you are not bothered to earn points. The site is www.coolrom.com. I think it has more and better ds games, but unfortunately it doesn't have the option to download 3ds and dsi games. Coolrom.com is simple and easy to use. All you have to do is download and copy the file into your r4. When I first got an r4, I downloaded a ds game from megaupload, but it was too complicated because I had to convert the .rar file into .ds. Coolrom.com also gives you the option to download an emulator and play console games on your computer or laptop.
