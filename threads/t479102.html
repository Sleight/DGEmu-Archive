AaioliApr 22 2013, 04:07 PM

I like the Samsung phones personally, and with the Galaxy S4 coming out soon the S2's price should drop (and it's already mid-range). The S2 is still the phone I use and I don't see a reason to upgrade. It runs Ice Cream Sandwich and has plenty of storage, is very fast, runs games without a problem, and the screen is the perfect size for a phone. It's the perfect size in that you can watch video on it with superb quality, but the phone's size itself is still small enough that it'll fit in your pocket even if you're wearing skinny fit jeans. It has a very good camera + a decent front-facing camera. The sound is surprisingly good for a phone of this price range. The only downside I can think of is that some carriers sell the S2X only, which is a little more powerful but has super crappy buttons and no LED notifications. That's the one I have. It's not ideal but the rest of the phone features make up for it.

 

edit: I guess I should say that you can also go with the S3 since it's going to get a price drop as well I'm pretty sure. to be honest I don't see enough improvement to the S3 to justify the price jump from the S2 (I think it's mostly the super quality screen) but that depends how much money you have to spend.
