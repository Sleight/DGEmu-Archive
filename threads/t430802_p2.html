In my opinion, its not something any of us will be able to comprehend. The best way for me to explain my idea of it, though, is that "Of course there's something afterwards, otherwise whats the point?" and I don't mean it in a philosophical standpoint, but I just mean that, right now, we are an arbitrary group of atoms that function in a specific way (breath in, breath out, energy in, energy out) and we think about stuff. Later, when we are still the same thing (but potentially a different arbitrary group of atoms) why wouldn't we still think, still do *stuff* and still have an existence of some kind? Thing is, we'll never have any idea what kind of stuff it is or on what scope/scale the existence is, and by the time we get there, it won't matter! Anything from time and space to the relationship of energy to matter might be different at that point, and we'll never even get the chance to figure it out. To me this is the best part of existence, you don't HAVE to know what just happened or whats coming up next to enjoy the ride, cuz you can be damn sure that its just gonna keep happening whether you like it or not.
