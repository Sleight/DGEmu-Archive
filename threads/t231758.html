[center][/center]Name: Nintendo DS ('DS' can mean either 'Dual Screen' or 'Developers' System') [Left Image]Release Dates: North America- Sunday, November 21, 2004 Japan- Thursday, December 2, 2004 Europe- Friday, March 11, 2005 Australia/New Zealand- Thursday, February 24, 2005 China- Saturday, July 23, 2005Name: Nintendo DS Lite (known as iQue DS Lite in China) [Center Image]Release Dates: North America- Sunday, June 11, 2006 Japan- Thursday, March 2, 2006 Europe- Friday, June 23, 2006 Australia/New Zealand- Thursday, June 1, 2006 China- Saturday, July 29, 2006 South Korea- Thursday, January 18, 2007Name: Nintendo DSi (the 'i' is symbolic of the camera "eye"s and also of the subject "I" and its individuality) [Right Image]Release Dates: North America- Sunday, April 5, 2009 Japan- Saturday, November 1, 2008 Europe- Friday, April 3, 2009 Australia/New Zealand- Thursday, April 2, 2009 General SummaryThe Nintendo DS (NDS) is one of the latest handheld consoles and is part of the seventh generation. It is currently one of the best-selling systems out there with more units sold than the Wii, Playstation 3, or Xbox 360. The NDS is noted for its dual screens with one being a touch-sensitive screen, which allows more interaction in certain games. In addition, the use of 'Friend Codes' allows the use of playing others online as well as voice/text chatting. Something to note about the NDS is that it is region-free with the exceptions of Chinese version games because of a different firmware chip necessary for the Chinese character images. Chinese version games require the iQue DS. One of the features of the NDS is the DS Download Play, which lets users play multiplayer games using only one game card. The range for this is only about 60 feet where one of the handhelds downloads all of the necessary data from the other unit. The NDS is able to play Game Boy Advance (GBA) games through the Slot 2 on the system. The NDS games are inserted into Slot 1. Though the NDS can play GBA games, it cannot play the old Game Boy and Game Boy Color games. When the NDS plays a GBA game, there is the option of using either the top of bottom screens for display when playing.The NDS comes with a multitude of accessories: TV Tuner, Magnetic Stand, Stylus string, Opera Browser, NDS Headset, Rumble Pak, an MP3 player, and a Wi-Fi USB Connector. The TV Tuner is only available in Japan. The Magnetic Stand is also Japan-only. It connects to the unit's Slot 2 to hold the NDS up or stick it to a surface. The stylus string is another Japan-only accessory as it also comes with the magnetic stand. The NDS Browser is a variation of Opera's web browser specifically made for use on the DS. It uses the touchscreen for inputs and handwriting recognition. The NDS Headset has one earphone, a microphone, and can be used with all games that may require use of the handheld's internal microphone. The Rumble Pak goes into the unit's Slot 2 and adds vibration to simulate action in games. The MP3 player was announced by Nintedo of Europe and little else is currently known about it. The Wi-Fi USB Connector connects to a computer's USB port to make a mini-hotspot for a maximum of five NDS handhelds to access the Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection through the computer's internet connection. This only works when the computer in question is running Windows XP. Look online for other accessories.More than a year after the release of the original NDS, the Nintendo DS Lite was released. As can be expected, the NDS Lite is much lighter than the original NDS. Also, it has a much longer battery life than its predecessor. The screens are brighter and more durable, but they still get scratched as easily. Another difference is that the directional pad was reduced in size while the power button and internal microphone were relocated. The stylus that comes with the NDS Lite is also bigger for an easier grip. The NDS Lite comes in five colors: Onyx Black (Jet Black), Ice Blue, Coral Pink (Noble Pink), Enamel Navy, and Polar White (Crystal White).In 2008-9, the Nintendo DSi was released as the third iteration of the Nintendo DS handheld gaming console.Nintendo began development in late 2006, and unveiled the console in October 2008 during a Nintendo Conference in Tokyo. As a seventh-generation console, the DSi primarily shares the handheld gaming market with Sony's PlayStation Portable. Most of the DS Lite's attributes are retained with fewer aesthetic changes made compared to the first two iterations. A major feature that distinguishes the Nintendo DSi is its two interactive digital cameras that can be both incorporated into gameplay and used as a standalone camera feature. The DSi also includes connectivity with an online store (Nintendo DSi Shop) as well as internal and external storage of content.[center]More Information:Wikipedia: Nintendo DSWikipedia: Nintendo DS
