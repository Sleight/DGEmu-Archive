I'm a fan of Adventure Time. It does have a plot, though watching one episode of it won't tell you much. What's interesting about it (to me, at least) is that there is a lot going on in the show - past history, why everything is all crazy - but they never directly address it. They're living in a post-apocalyptic world. Finn is (arguably) the last human alive, raised by dog people.
