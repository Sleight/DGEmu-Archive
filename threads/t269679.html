(Picture may differ wildly, there are many versions of this card out)The N-Card and its clones are very well known and this guide is simply to help you install and update your N-Card.Step one: Download the N-Card Firmware.DownloadWARNING BACK UP GAME SAVES AND ALL FILES BEFORE APPLY UPGRADE.Step two:Place your N-Card inside your ds and plug in your slot 2 fireline (gba cartridge), and plug in your usb cable into it and your pc.Step three: Turn your ds on and click on the N-Card icon while holding down the following keys: UP+L+R+A+B (the text should be flashing red).Step four: Double click "NAND_ALL.bat"to run upgrade programStep Five: after updating you are required to format your N-Card, that is why you need to backup your saves and files.Turn your ds off and unplug your usb cable, turn your ds back on and click on the N-card icon while holding down the following button keys: Down+L+R+A+B (this will delete everything on your N-Card), afterwards turn your ds off.Step Six: Plug back in your usb cable and run the N-card program and select transfer data (may be data management), Copy "xmenu.nds" to your N-card.Step Seven: Turn your ds off and restart it, Enter "GAME MODE", so that a DSYSTEM folder will be set up.Congratulations you have just updated your N-Card Software.------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------Main Concerns:Battery and Corrupt SavesThe N-Card Line is well know for defective batteries, i highly recommend you download and run this battery checker, the battery inside the N-card maintains your save files without it, your saves will become corrupted.DownloadInstructions on how to use:Simply run it as you would a normal ds game, a screen will pop up and it will run a diagnostic test on your N-card, and will say simply Pass or fail, if it says Pass you are fine and your battery is good, if it says Fail then there is a problem with your N-card and your saves maybe corrupted there is no way to fix this.FormattingDo not format N-Card with windows you will destroy your N-Cards File System, use the quick format method mentioned above or use a full format, i recommend this if you screwed up and formatted the N-card with WindowsHow to FULL format(Full format need more than 10mins, because of writting test)1. Insert Gba slot Writer into slot 2 and insert N-Card into sloy 1.2. Hold the "UP" directionnal button and 3. still holding "Up", press "L+R+Select+START+UP", will enter FULL format program mode.

I have had my DS Linker now for a few days and have gone from hating it to loving it. This is because of the fact that while the operation is very simple, getting and installing and using the software can be very confusing. The first thing to know is a reliable URL for software download is at:http://www.dslinker.cn/soft/Note that they have just recently posted a XMENU upgrade (2.55) and if you don't have it as yet you should run and get it. The interface is much nicer looking and I believe fixes some problems I was experienceing with the older 2.53. Basically, I have found that the 2.55 version makes changing the size of the save file a lot easier and I do not seeing it ever resetting as the old menu did with me. The first hint I have found to share is do not ever, ever use the AUTO setting for memory save size. This fails constantly in both versions of XMENU. Instead, I have found that manually resetting (using the select and <- -> combo) to 4Mbit for EVERY game is the key. I understand some games may have 2Mbit but apparently the 4Mbit setting works with them as well and I guess you just lose a tiny bit of memory. But since I have manually set all game to the 4Mbit setting I have not had one failure is saving or startup So, avoid the AUTO setting like the plague.Anyone found anything else out about these that isn't very obvious?(Reposted this after a staffer mentioned this topic was alrerady available and locked my thread. Didn't see this topic when I looked as I did not know the proper name for this was N-Card as I only knew it as DS Linker.
