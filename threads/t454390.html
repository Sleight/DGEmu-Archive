SSir VGFeb 16 2010, 09:42 PM
To use the screenadmin page, you enter in a name that you need to give permissions to. It'll give a list of names that work. Click on the one you want. Click Promote.Once you return to the screenadmin page, you'll need to assign system permissions. If no permissions are assigned, they can't upload screenshots for that system. Click on the name under screenshotter column. Check any box for a system you want to assign permissions to. Click Save.If you ever want to demote somebody, you need to go to that page, scroll to the bottom and check all 3 boxes, then click demote.I never much used the other one.As for images you upload, remember that they have to be .jpg files under 30KB.P.S. Be careful when adding anybody to the list that has special permissions (like staff members) as that page overwrites them. This is why I never removed atomicknight, for instance.(VGHQ is my test account for standard user with screenshotting permissions. That's why that one is on the page, BTW.)
