KkarmakodeDec 18 2009, 04:38 AM
I have spent many long hours researching this very topic, and others like it, before i even became a hardcore gamer. There are actually a few more positive effects from playing video games than just enhanced memory and hand-eye coordination. Most games even make you smarter, even the mindless gore-fests that most people find unappealing. The most looked-over change is your cognitive ability, in other words, your ability to process, filter, and implement the massive amounts of data streaming through your brain. There was a test to prove this theory about two years ago, when a biologist found his 6 year old son playing on a Playstation, and he wondered if games made you smart. He got a few colleagues, hooked them up to an MRI, and had them play Tetris. The readings that he got showed activity that was off the charts from the people struggling to position the blocks. He made them play the game religiously for the next 6 months and performed the same test again. Not only was it much easier to play the game this time, but the brain activity had toned down dramatically, showing that their brains were having absolutely no problem filtering all of the information coming in! So yes, playing video games improves your memory, and makes you a hell of a lot smarter.
