Well there are various techniques that an individual can take in order to remember things better. For example after you finished studying for an exam it helps to take a nap; simply because when you memorize information the information gets stored into long term memory rather than short term mem. What helps me is not to study at all right before an exam, simply because when you quickly glance information after studying for an extend amount of time, quickly glancing at information will aid in you forgetting the information. Most people believe quickly glancing b4 a test will help them remember, but if you have tricky questions where it looks like 2 answer are correct the mind will tend to contradict between answers, resulting in wrong answers. Best thing to do, study over a few days; especially if it is a big test, and take breaks. -Bonilla
