Another game worth mentioning is the SNES game Radical Dreamers, which was actually released via Satellaview, and never made it to America officially(though there is an unnofficial translation available)The game itself is comparatively unique, as it isn't an RPG in the traditional sense. Truly it does not compare to any other game. While there are similar games for the PC, this game is unique among SNES games; a choose your own adventure game, where you choose your options(and sometimes, if you take too long, they're chosen for you), with drawings in the background, usually of the room you are in. The music in the game is mostly ambient, but fits the mood quite well, and there are some moments that just have beautiful music, which you almost have to stop just so you can listen. Some of you who have played Chrono Cross will definately recognize names, and probably recognize a few of the songs, as some of the songs from this game moved on to Chrono Cross(The battle theme, for example)
