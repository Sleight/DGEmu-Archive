But is all this bullying necessary? What could happen if these "targets" fought back? What are your opinions?

Bullying is one of the many social strategies used to establish a social pecking order, ensuring the survival of those at the top.

The stereotypes seen in movies embellish this point and are easily recognisable, with the victim being a social outcast. The nerd in glasses has barely any strength. The bully, say a young strong male asserts his dominance, often in front of friends (to warn of competition), in an attempt to impress a girl (to mate), often has first access to food and resources (the better quality and amount).

So what is the difference between bullying and say, when we see a group of animals, like a pride of lions? It’s in the fact that the assertion op power is unnecessary and often disproportional. It can lead to psychological and physical harm. Causing anything from a mild depression, to personality disorders.

However those that are able to handle and learn to deal with bullying are at an advantage, as they are better able to deal with disputes. Bullies themselves seem to enjoy the social perks associated with it. However it is important to realise that there often is an underlying reason people bully (which for example can be linked to childhood trauma’s or personal insecurities).

There are different methods to stop bullying. Key is, that the bully (and any bystanders) sees you as an equal or better. While some can turn it round with a smart comment or a well-placed argument, other appears to only to accept physical dominion. Key is the fact that the bully looses social standing (not just in his own eyes, but the other bystanders as well) and that the victim is no longer presenting itself as victim.

However the people that bullies or receives the bullying can change. Nowadays, the ‘geeks’ are ‘chique’. You could argue that they are fighting back, having managed to beat the stereotype and change what is considered social valuable. Thus the attributes someone needs to have, like physical strength (we don’t need to hunt as we nowadays can go to a supermarket or order pizza), might be less important the ability to handle computers.

While I cannot condone bullying, I do realise it is probably a necessary evil growing up. Most people at some point in their lives will have been victim, bystander or bully. It will be unrealistic to prevent bullying, however strategies taught at a young age to recognise it, teach people how to deal with it (bystanders who as a collective often have a greater power than the bully) and aid to victims (and bullies) so they can rise above it are improving all the time. Hopefully in this way, minimisation of damage can be done.
