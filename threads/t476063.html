The subject goes back as far as time would allow. The laws of nature, survival of the fittest, any and all of these used as reasoning to justify bullying. Wikipedia states:

Bullying is a form of agressive behavior manifested by the use of force or coercion to affect others, particularly when the behavior is habitual and involves an imbalance of power. It can include verbal harassment, physical assault or coercion and may be directed repeatedly towards particular victims, perhaps on grounds of race, religion, gender, sexuality, or ability. The "imbalance of power" may be social power and/or physical power. The victim of bullying is sometimes referred to as a "target".

The world has seen many types of bullying, elementary bullying or bullying in school, cyber-bullying, religious bullying where the church pushes belief by some forcful format, even political bullying such as useless wars. But is all this bullying neccesary? What could happen if these "targets" fought back? What are your opinions?
