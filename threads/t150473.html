In order to split it into a three-section staff, Ikkaku says 'Split!' (裂けろ sakero). His bankai Ryūmon Hōzukimaru is also composed of three sections. All of them oversized of course. One end is a large chinese broadsword (dadao) and the other a monk's spade. The center section, a large curved blade with a handle on its blunt side, usually floats behind Ikkaku while he uses his arms to hold the other ends. However, sometimes he just spins the whole thing around while holding the center blade. While in bankai Ryūmon Hōzukimaru Dragon Crest (located on the center blade) slowly fills up with crimson ink as Ikkaku's spiritual pressure rises, when completely filled Ryūmon Hōzukimaru is on full power.
