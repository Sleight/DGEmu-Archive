Here are some photos of the case that I took. I had purchased this product about 3-5 days ago and put it to the test 2 times when I was out with friends to see if it works as advertised and it does very well. I was out with my friends playing my 3DS system, getting multiple street pass tags playing games throughout the day and at about 8:27pm was when my battery got low and gave me a slim orange battery life. Exactly at that time of 8:27pm was when I flicked the switch on my 3DS Power Case and began charging it. At 9:06pm the case had a little more than 75% battery life. Then at 9:54pm when I checked it as I was about to get on the train to head home, the case battery had died and my 3DS was fully charged. I assume that my 3DS was fully charged a little bit before 9:54pm, I am going to say my 3DS was fully charged at around 9:35pm. The 3DS Power Case has very fast charge times and doubles the 3DS's Battery Capacity. Everything on the 3DS is easily accessible (headphone jack, stylus, sd card slot, all led indicators and so on). The case is very comfortable it feels like a hard rubber material. It is also just a tiny bit bulky but it is not that big of a deal since the comfort of the case makes up for it. The only things I wish the case had were a little more slim of a design and either some led's to notify how much of a charge the case has or a number led indicator on the case to let you know how much the case has charged. I rate this product a 4/5 stars and would highly recommend it for those who need extra battery life while on the go. You can purchase this case at various retailers below for around $24-$25 dollars.
