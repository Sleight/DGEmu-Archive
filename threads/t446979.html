Sigh... This idea's so old that even the Mod I asked(Shai) must've forgotten about it too. Pity. I was hoping that more people would be interested.I shall now explain what is RPing is. (How silly of me to forget to mention earlier)RP-ing, basically, is a topic where the creator of the topic creates a storyline. The Creator then begins the story, of which other members could continue. However, the RP is 'written' from a narrator's view. For example...C: The night was dark, and Dazzle(Creator's assumed name) flew through the trees, searching for the item he was looking for. The DarkeTome lay on the ground, with its unholy magic sealed in it. Dazzle spared a few seconds for a smug grin and reached for the item.Random Member: As Dazzle's hand reached for the Tome, Terrah(Member's assumed name) then let loose his horde of bees at the Necromancer. "Thou shalt not violate the Forest of Twilight with thy filth!" lamented Terrah.That was just an example. To put things simply, RP-ing is like creating a novel. It can be manipulated in many ways, and the best part would be manipulating the other members in your story. But different forums have different rules, so I'm not sure with this one.Support!
