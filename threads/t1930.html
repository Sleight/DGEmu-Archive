If you're into humor and content, and don't necessarily need the eye candy, Kingdom of Loathing is a great MMORPG to play. It's browser-based, so no real great graphics (your character is literally a stick figure), but the gameplay is downright hilarious at times. They have classes such as Turtle Tamer and Disco Bandit, equipment like a projectile icemaker and Ol Scratch's ashcan (a tiny trashcan fire :D ), and monsters like various types of hobos, gnollish crossdressers, even a mummy that refuses to die. Plus...what other game could you facestab a hippy? Plus, in about a little under a month, they should be doing their yearly holiday content, lovingly referred to as the Crimbo season, complete with free advent calendar. And the entire game is free. You can donate for access to whatever item of the month is currently in the store, but it's only for special equipment and familiars (like pets, they help you fight and stuff), but at no point is any content donation-locked. If an IotM familiar drops an item that unlocks content, it's always completely tradeable, and purchasable with in-game currency (which is Meat :D )And if you get a good clan, there's usually great conversation in /clan chat.
