Ok I couldn't find any threads about this so I decided to ask, myself...I've been a fan of Metroid since I first played it on NES when I was a little boy...I thought Metroid II (along with Zelda IV) was the best Gameboy game (even though it's a bit antiquated these days)...Super Metroid III is one of the best games EVER...and Metroid Fusion and Zero Mission have perfectly and elegantly carried on the Metroid series on GBA...But Metroid Prime came along and for the first time ever I had really mixed feelings about it...Sure, it looked gorgeous and had some really cool effects...But the decision to convert it to an FPS never sat well with me...after playing it a bit I knew that I just didn't care for it...I found it tedious (with all the scanning), I found it slow-paced (despite some intense shoot-outs)...And most of all, it didn't FEEL like a Metroid game to me...I got Metroid Prime Hunters for DS hoping it would be better, but everything I didn't like about Prime was actually WORSE on Hunters...And the stylus controls are just indescribably akward...I didn't play it very long before I found myself popping in Fusion again and happily playing through that for about the billionth time...I just don't feel Metroid transitioned into 3D well at all...It might be a different story if it was 3rd-person but to make it an FPS took away alot of what made Metroid cool to me...The problem is, it seems to have been pretty well recieved by most people and I'm afraid there won't be anymore 2D Metroids...I don't have to worry about Castlevania because NONE of the 3D incarnations of it have done very well so they'll keep making 2D ones (at least for handhelds) because they know they'll sell...But Metroid is a different case...I really hope we haven't seen the last 2D Metroid...Since I know most of you DO like the 3D Metroids, I'd like to hear your opinions of them versus the 2D ones...If you like them better, or worse, or about the same...
