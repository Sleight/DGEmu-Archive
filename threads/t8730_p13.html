Baldur's Gate 2 is (arguably) the best RPG, and I still haven't found another RPG whose battle system is anywhere near as effective as BG. I mean, your regular RPGs' battles are pretty well attack->confirm, attack->confirm, summon (with a few minutes pause), then attack->confirm again. Not to mention that the story is well-crafted and moves at a good clip, there is so much to do with all the sidequests, and the visuals are very nice to look at.
