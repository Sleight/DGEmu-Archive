Welcome to the "User Uploaded Videos" section.Please do read carefully before posting to avoid getting warned. :)This section is dedicated to members who upload their own original work on Youtube.You get to share your videos and have people discuss about them in your own thread.--- RULES ---1. The videos you post MUST be your own work. Do not post original work made by others.2. You may have a maximum of 5 videos in a new topic.3. If you have more than two videos (ex.: Let's Play Videos), you may link to your Youtube playlist or profile.4. Do not post multiple topics pertaining to the same playlist (ex.: Posting part 1 in one topic, and part 2 in a second topic).5. Do not post video responses.--- GUIDELINE ---To open a topic, make sure your post contains at least these information:1. A short description of what your video is about.2. Obviously your videos links. (Youtube videos get treated automatically as embedded videos)3. A link to your Youtube profile or playlist if needed.An example of a new post would be:These are my new videos of a speedrun I did in X game.<insert youtube url here><insert youtube url here>More videos can be found on my playlist here: <insert youtube playlist="" url here>Enjoy sharing your videos! ^^
