DDJ Night Force 9ORIGINAL POSTMar 28 2007, 10:30 PM
Given the recent episodes like the one between Ody and Hadel/Trashy/=-=/etc, I strongly feel that some preventative action needs to be taken. Rules like this have been proposed before but denied due to reasons like "then we'd have to then ban half the spam center" and "well some people can't take a joke". However, I have come up with another possible solution (a variation of what was suggested before) that should keep the "harmful" insults under control.Since it is quite subjective as to what is considered "offensive" (i.e. some people may find certain things hurtful while others laugh it off), we can let the targeted member decide. Basically, if someone says something potentially insulting about another member. That member (and ONLY that other member) can choose to "report that post" claiming that they were hurt by its contents. A moderator will then inspect the post in question and if it is clear that an "insult" was made, then action will be taken.For example, some member sees a post made by another member that offended him/her. He/she would then be able report that post and a moderator would have a look. Whether the "moderator" finds it offensive or not (because obviously that moderator would have a different perspective than the one who actually got offended), if there is anything in there that so much as "resembles" any sort of personal attack/insult/etc, then that moderator is obliged to dish out a warn increase to the offender. Of course if the moderator is unsure, he or she can always PM the member about it.Now although, warn increases mean nothing to certain people (e.g. some staff and even certain regular members), seeing something like "Edit: Warned for insulting <member's display name>" will at least send a message to the rest of the community that this type of behaviour is unwelcome. Also, another form of action to encompass ALL members (and since insults tend to only happen in the spam center as it is the only place members can currently get away with it) would be a suspension or ban JUST from the spam center after X number of warnings due to this type of offense. I'm sure being cut from one's favourite forum will likely make him or her think twice about what they say.So what does everyone think?
