Set mine up this morning.  Works really well.  There are some caveats though.  The controller can turn your system On, but that's the only thing the PS Button on the Dualshock 4 can do.  It can't turn the system off or get to the XMB when playing a game, so once you start a game, you're stuck there unless you have a PS3 controller handy.  Granted, it's not that big of a deal considering how much better it feels to play with the DS4, but I guess it's up to you if it's worth the trouble.  I happened to get a 2nd controller when I bought my PS4, so it's an easy setup for me.
