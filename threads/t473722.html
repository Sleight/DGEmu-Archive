http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?showforum=174The RULES there will explain everything you need to know. There are a few basic methods.1. Wait. Every day you get more DPs. But if you're impatient...2. Write a review. If you write a good game review you can get points, but most people think that's too much effort.3. Answer quizzes (http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?/forum/208-quizzes/). If you go to the QUIZ section some of the quizzes offer GPs which can be turned into DPs. Or if you go to one of my 3 personal quizzes, every 5 points you get a free DS ROM from me.4. Post. Once you hit 50 posts you can enter the Lottery(http://www.dgemu.com/forums/index.php?/forum/284-lottery/). Also after 50 posts you can get a DP loan.Hope that helps.
