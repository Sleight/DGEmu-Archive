I finally completed this (with only 65% accomplished). Brilliant game, but I definitely had a love/hate relationship with it:Fantastic game. I can't count the amount of times that I failed a mission due to the AI character dying; Nevertheless, for all of it's faults GTA IV is a spectacular game and certainly a contender (if not a sure bet) for game of the year. A truly breathtaking effort, in the end
