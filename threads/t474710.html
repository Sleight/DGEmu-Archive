[MH] Touhou Project - Collection 2011

Predicted release date: December 25 2011
Actual release date: December 24 2011

My 11th release to the Internet. Last year's collection was big, but this year, it's even bigger. Spanning accross 5 DVDs, this collection contains all of the Touhou Project and Seihou Project games, along with lots of doujin games and my creation, the Touhou Flash Project that attempts to assemble all the Flash games and videos of Touhou together.Like usual, there are custom cover and disc arts, along with a booklet art.
Special thanks goes out to all members of Hongfire and DGEmu that helped me during the Touhou Flash Project beta test.

Take note: Not for the feint of heart with a slow connection, this collection is 17GB worth of download.
