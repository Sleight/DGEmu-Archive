Notes:

A chronological collection of the event which ran in all of the Bat-family books (apart from Batwoman, Detective Comics and Batwing) from October 2012 to February 2013, featuring the return of the Joker after a year in hiding, as he tries to take apart the Bat-family.

These are only the issues which feature Joker himself and tie in directly to the event. Detective Comics #1 is included due to its importance to the storyline.
This is only a suggested order as there is no official order apart from Teen Titans and Red Hood And The Outlaws.
Each file is under 200MB.
