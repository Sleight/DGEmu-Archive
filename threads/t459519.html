Banjo-Kazooie mastered the formula, I think. I'm not talking about the latest game, but rather, the Nintendo 64's installments. The movement is fluid; you cause the movement, and are not bothered by skidding. You can actually jump around, whereas the Zelda titles use a cheap mechanic which does not allow the character (Link) to jump unless you approach a ledge. The first two Banjo-Kazooie games were masterpieces for their time, and they still are. It can make you feel like you are in control of your destiny. The gameplay is not monotonous. You can explore at your own whim. It's a perfect platformer, IMO.
