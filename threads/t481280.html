DdeelunaSep 05 2014, 04:11 PM
Well before you dive in, make sure that you have the needed tools.

1: a soldering iron with a small tip for the detail work
2: a camera (to take pictures of your steps as you go)
3: a triwing screw driver
4: a nice wide space to work.

The Camera is a rather important thing that many don't use when they do repairs, it's useful in making sure you don't forget what steps you used to take the device apart so you can more easily put it back together. Also, if you feel so kind, you could post your steps when you finish. Everyone loves a good tutorial.
