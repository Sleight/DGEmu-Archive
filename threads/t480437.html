I figured since everyone uses one or the other or both we should have a thread where we all recommend obscure stuff.
I'm gonna skip the obvious one like Star Trek, Firefly, BSG, and movies like Avengers and instead go for the odd ones you've probably never heard of. Also, if there's a show on both Hulu and NetFlix I'll post it under NetFlix since that seems to be more popular.

Hulu

Booth at the End - This is a Hulu exclusive. There are two seasons and it's such a great show. If you like story driven shows go for this one. There's a guy who sits in a booth at a diner. He can grant a wish if you ask him, but you must perform a task for it to work. The task is different for each person and each request. The stories often tie in together and it's amazing.
The Awesomes - It only got one season, but it's alright. If you like Robot Chicken this might appeal to you. It isn't always great, but I say it's worth a watch.
The Neighbors - A bit too quirky, but otherwise it is a real funny sitcom. Give it a shot.
@midnight - It's a late night comedy show. Comedians just make jokes about funny shit on the internet. It's a great half hour run by Chris Hardwick from Nerdist. Definitely worth the watch.
Of course, not everyone knows that Colbert show and Daily show are on Hulu. Unless you are a Republican, you'll enjoy those.
America's Most Smartest Model - Another odd reality show, but I loved this one, even called the winner from the first episode. They take models and give them intelligence tests. Hilarious.

NOTE Hulu is free. You can watch it in your browser, but if you want it on your phone or TV you gotta pay. Also, some shows are exclusive to paid accounts, but there's plenty for free.

NetFlix

Awake - A great ghost story with an amazing twist ending. A must watch. Also, there's a scene with boobs, if that's what it takes.
Oddities - It's like a mix of Pawn Stars, American Pickers, and some goth show. They buy and sell creepy shit like bones and such. I enjoy it and it's got 3 seasons with a west coast spin off that has one season. It's not for everyone, but I like it.
Leverage - This one most of you are probably aware of. It's on both sites, though it hit Hulu first. I'd say it's worth watching all five seasons. It's kind of like if Ocean's 11 was a tv show, but with five people.

There's a lot more, bu this is already tldr so lemme know what you like and maybe I'll find some cool new stuff.

Let's see

 

Archer - Love this show.  Satirical spy thriller thing with weird animation.  Plus Jessica Walters is endlessly funny.

 

Bob's Burgers - same voice actor as Archer for main character.  It's just a really weird family doing weird stuff in a burger shop.  It's better than I thought going in, so I was pleasantly surprised.

 

Parks and Recreation - weak first season, but luckily it is short.  Picks up considerably in the second season and the characters are really charming.

 

Sons of Anarchy - hardcore biker dudes doing hardcore stuff with Leela from Futurama hanging out.  It's good.

 

Arrow - Starts off relatively weak, but it gets better, and the second season is way better.

 

It's Always Sunny in Philidelphia - super hilarious, but definitely an acquired taste.  I've always found it really great, especailly Charlie (the crack episode is brilliant)

 

Raising Hope - starts off really well, but lags later in the first season.  I haven't watched all of it, but I thought it was good enough to watch.  It has a strong start at the very least.

 

Chuck - just one of those cult shows that's good.  It's interesting to say the least

 

The Following - Kevin Bacon in a suspense show.  It's good.  Good talent involved.

 

30 Rock - One of my favorite shows.  I think Tina Fey and Alec Baldwin are perfect together and the show gets so incredibly meta towards the end.  Another one with a weak first season that picks up considerably.

 

Revenge - An ABC drama that is way better than it should be.

 

Wilfred - Frodo and an anthrophmorphised dog get into shenanigans

 

Soul Eater - the only newer-ish anime I can stand to watch that's on Netflix.

 

The LEague - If you like lots of "man" humor, this is good.  I think it's a little heavy on the sex and dick jokes, but it's still enjoyable and definitely has some memorable moments.  It's worth a try.

 

The Tudors - factually inaccurate recreation of things that kinda happened.  It's definitely the best period show, but it is still entertaining and definitely worth looking into.

 

Fringe - another weird cult thing.  LIke all the others, it' san acquired taste and hard to recommend because you never know what people will think of it.

 

The IT Crowd - One of my new favorite comics.  The seasons are only 6 episodse long so it doesn't overstay it's welcome.  The only downside is that it has a laugh track, which sucks, because I think it's pretty brilliant.  The characters are really great and I think everyone should watch a few episodes. It's like what Big Bang Theory should be.

 

Louie - this is a weird show that is darkly funny.

 

Burn NOtice - spy show that's good.

 

Hell on Wheels - gritty war thingy that AMC is doing.

 

Lilyhammer - comedy mobster show.  It's humorous and entertaining.  Not sure why no one talks about it.

 

Nikita - hot chicks kicking ass

 

Jericho - post apocalyptic show that's interesting, but never finished

 

Luthor - kinda breaking bad-ish with a black guy

 

Transformers Prime - I hate the CG style, but the actual show is really good.  Good characters (except the humans obviously) and the bad guys are actually bad and entertaining.  it seems to be skewed towards adults

 

Damages - Basically a show with a woman that has batterd wife syndrome from her bitch boss, but it's good to watch.  Lots of suspense and intrigue.

 

Fry and Laurie - Stephen Fry and Hugh Laurie do very British things.

 

Awake - a fantastic show that got cancelled after one season.  Shame, it had a great premise.  Basically a guy had a wreck and suffered a head injury.  Now he shifts between two realities, one where his son died and another where his wife died.  He switches between them when he falls asleep and he can't tell which is a dream.  He has a therapist in both and they try to convince him which is real.  It's a complicated show that's really good.

 

Tron Legacy - the best thing to happen to Tron ever.

 

Batman Brave and the Bold - wacky Batman teams up with wacky people and does wacky things for wacky reasons
