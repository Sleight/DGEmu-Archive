Some try to argue that it is the theft of a sale since people would have had to buy a copy but that argument gets holes blown in it by libraries, rental companies, people lending their copies to each other, sales of used copies (which the publishers don't get any money from), and the willingness of people to just do without if they can't afford it or get a copy for free. On the other hand piracy (as in manufacturing and selling counterfeit copies of goods) is illegal pretty much everywhere. I fully support busting people that try to take games/movies/books/whatever that somebody else made and make fake copies and sell them to people that thought they were financially supporting those that legitimately made the original the copies were made from.
