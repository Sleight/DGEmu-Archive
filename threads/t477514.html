KKirie MotobaAug 20 2012, 12:33 PM
Oh alright, I thought you already had a game that was prepared with multiple games. Now I get it. :)

Unfortunately, what you are requesting for is impossible as MAME is rom loading based: 1 Rom at a time.
What you would want is to be able to emulate the Playchoice's game selection ability.

How the Playchoice worked was that it had a mainboard with 10 slots for a game each, just like at the following link:
http://web.archive.org/web/20100411064526/http://www.gamersgraveyard.com/repository/nes/history/nes_arcade.html

In order to achieve this on an emulator, you would need a specific emulator that is capable of loading said 10 roms at the same time and emulate the arcade board's switches. Unfortunately, this is not supported in MAME since it loads only 1 game at a time, and to my knowledge, there is no known emulator for this either.

Hope that clarifies things a bit for you.
