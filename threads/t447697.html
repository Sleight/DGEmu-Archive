It's not the same, at all. Most game, software, movie, etc. review websites have both reviewer ratings and average user ratings for a reason; each type of rating has its pros and cons. And those are professional websites where the reviewers are paid to review any mainstream game that is released and be unbiased and objective about it; what are the odds that the rating of a game selectively reviewed by a handful of random DGEmu members—who were probably just doing it for the DP reward—is going to be any accurate indication of how the game stacks up against other games that were reviewed by a different amount of different people? This is why the average user rating is much handier in a place like this; you are getting the opinion of the masses. Even the biased, extreme ratings are evened out by themselves, and generally speaking the more people like something (in proportion to the amount of people who have voiced an opinion on it), the higher the odds you'll like it. That doesn't mean I'm against tying one type of rating to the reviews, as you said, as long as you keep the average user rating. My first suggestion to restrict the list of ROMs sorted by rating to those with a minimum of X votes seems like a harmless and simple start, and it wouldn't be doing the same job.
