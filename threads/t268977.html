Here is a quickie reviewBioshock: When I played the demo and watched the trailers I was looking forward to Bioshock, but I really wasnt anticipating anything more than an above average thriller game. The game is so much more. From the moment you start the game is just a free ride of action, mystery, horror, corruption, fantasy, and plot. It has everything you could ever want in a game and more. The graphics are top notch and stay that way due to zero lag and great frame-rate. The sound is brillant, from the stomping of the Big Daddy to the sound of your body almost dead. The voice acting is one of the best I have ever seen because instead of getting celebrities they focused on sophisticated actors specifically tailored to their parts. I especially remember the artist as amazingly acted. Whats really nice about Bioshock is the fact that you have 3 different difficulty levels. If you wish you can go to the easiest setting at first, usually I dont recommend this because it feels like the satisfaction is gone from beating it, but the truth is you are really able to absorb the amazing storyline of this game without having to worry about dying. The highest level isnt the terribly difficult other than some big daddies and thats my only concern (though it is minor). Gameplay? Are you kidding? This game has some of the best gameplay I have ever seen in my life. With a nice arsenal of weapons at your disposal, as much as most good FPS's you would think that it would end there. No each gun has 3 separate bullets, 2 separate upgrades that make them look sexy, and some nice effects of shooting. Wait, there is more. You have these things called plasmids( I dont get into details because Im trying not to spoil anything) but basically they allow you to shoot fire with the snap of your fingers, shoot electricity, telekinesis, and a ton of other ones that make killing your enemy oh so very enjoyable. Its hard to tell you how good the plot is without spoiling it, but I can honestly say that it has the BEST storyline of any game I have ever seen or played. It is simply the best in terms of plot twists, character development, and wicked villians. No game forced me to think so deep about issues that always seemed so simple morally. I guess you just have to trust me on this but nothing is as good and deep as this plot. Graphics:9.5 Sound:10 Gameplay:10 Storyline:10 Replay Value:8 Overall:10, after playing this it is probably impossible to give it anything else.
