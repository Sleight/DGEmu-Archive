KKula DiamondMay 05 2014, 08:44 PM
Some important things to keep in mind:
- People fish for parry, don't use the weapons normal combos a lot, second/third attacks are easier to parry since they don't have to predict the timing.
- Watch out for roll traps, ie, casting a slow/weak spell followed by a powerful spell with the intent to catch you during the recovery frames of the roll if you try roll away from the first spell.
- Watch out for guard break, people will use this if you turtle behind a shield, if it catch you while holding a the shield up, you're dead. Though you can bait/react to it and punish hard, since it does nothing if you're not blocking.
- Bait things a lot, lots of people are very predictable, and if you know what they're going to do, punish it. The most obvious thing is rolling, many would roll to the side every time you run toward them to avoid your running attack, delay the attack and hit them during their roll recovery frames.
