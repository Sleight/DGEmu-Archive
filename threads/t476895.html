Ok, maybe it's not the most popular kind of music out there. And maybe we're not in the 90s anymore. But come on! Ska/Punk/Reggae bands are awesome! If you love 'em or hate 'em, you can't deny that there is, at least, one song you love. It's so energetic they make you wanna jump around and do crazy stuff all over the place.

I'm a big fan of these bands. Acts like Reel Big Fish (my favorite band), Streetlight Manifesto (the guys in my sig), Sublime, Less Than Jake, Mighty Mighty Bosstones, Planet Smashers, Rancid, Goldfinger, etc. shaped what Ska/Punk and Reggae was all about in the 90s.

If you know these bands, or any other bands, discuss them here. If you don't, well, I encourage you to give them a listen! Maybe you'll find something you like!
