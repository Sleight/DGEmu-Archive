No, they should not be treated like everyone else, because they're not like everyone else. I, like the OP, am also legally blind. Yes, I can see, but not well enough to be able to (legally) drive, and glasses don't do much so I don't bother with them. Also, I find it patronizing when people cheer mundane achievements made by disabled people. "Oh look, that deaf person told a funny joke! He's sooo brave!"However, regarding whether they should be held as less intelligent people, I gotta say, I don't recall ever hearing anyone call someone an idiot for say, being in a wheelchair. However, like I said above, it would seem that some people tend to go out of their way to be patronizing to the disabled, which could make the disabled person feel less intelligent, but that doesn't necessarily mean s/he's regarded as such by those who are being patronizing. Obviously, the retarded should be regarded as less intelligent, no matter how many hurt feelings it causes, because that's what their disability is all about.
