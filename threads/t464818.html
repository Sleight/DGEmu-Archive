Considering that virtually everyone we put on FC are adbots (with some flooders) as well as closing the topics, it's more important that the topics are removed out of sight of members which is why we dump/remove links/make invisible/delete etc. Now if the spammer/adbot has gone wild posting loads of stuff, it's pretty annoying for us mods to go round each topic/post making them invisible/deleting/removing links/dumping etc. And especially, if they are in a section where we mods don't have access to, it just stays lying around closed until an SMod comes.So what I was thinking, is that since Flood Control automatically closes all topics made by the flooders, can it not also be tweaked so that each and every single post of his is made invisible at least or so that it automatically places said spammer on mod-preview. This way, if there are too many posts to deal with, it can all be dealt with in one go yet the posts are still there (but just invisible) if the Sentinel/Admin decides to want to check the posts before making the decision on banning and then deleting all the posts.It's not a big issue if it can't be done. I guess we can just report it and wait for an Smod to do the rest but if this can be done all in one go, then I guess it makes things easier.
