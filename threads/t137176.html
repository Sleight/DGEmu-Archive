This question is the greatest question, for both Zelda and Paper Mario belong to different genre's of video games. Therefore the correct question should be which do you like better: Zelda or Paper Mario? Thus, in my own opinion, I enjoy The Legend of Zelda better, although Paper Mario is a great series as well. The Legend of Zelda is a more serious style of dungeon style of adventure game, while Paper Mario is a more humurous style of RPG. The graphics and soundtrack for The Legend of Zelda are by far the superior of the two, with a stunningly beautiful soundtrack that is memorable in itself and the graphics are superb as well. Paper Mario of course, does not focus on creating an intense soundtrack and visually stunning style of graphics. It rather focuses on creating an enjoyable, light and witty, family friendly game. The storyline is also more memorable in The Legend of Zelda series. Of course, some parts of it are cliche, such as the kidnapped Princess Zelda, yet other aspects of the storyline make up for it. Ocarina of Time and A Link to the Past are some of the most unique plots interwoven into the main storylines. In retrospect, The Legend of Zelda is a far superior game, but that is mainly because it is one of the greatest game series of all time. Paper Mario is a tremendous game in itself, but it will never compare to The Legend of Zelda. The Scholar
